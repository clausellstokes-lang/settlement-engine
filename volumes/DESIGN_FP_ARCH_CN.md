## §2 · THE LAWS THE FAMILY BINDS (operative readings)

1. **One principle (§126.1):** the tab is a PROJECTION of the belief plane plus a
   DISPATCHER of typed intents — zero new state, zero parallel command paths. Read side
   renders what THIS settlement BELIEVES, staleness and confidence visible; ground truth
   behind `includeGroundTruth` (C11); second-order via C8.
2. **Edit mode = typed ops only (§126.2):** every verb is a consented typed op through
   C3's `execute`, becoming a world-input event the next tick consumes —
   provenance-carried, undo-covered, honest per THE PROMISE (orders are events, never
   rewrites).
3. **One edit mode, war-gated (§126a):** the console is the war tab under the C12
   toggle; the command surface ACTIVATES only when `hasLiveWarState` is true for this
   settlement; at peace, edit mode on the war tab carries no command center — rendered
   as in-fiction absence, the C13 idiom.
4. **Dormancy-as-absence (§126.3):** sections mount on their feeder flags (C16) and
   render in-fiction absence until lit; ONE view model feeds tab AND PDF; the da-cured
   chokepoints (C15) are consumed from birth.
5. **The pin suppresses the resolution arm ONLY (§128.2):** everything else keeps
   simulating — the famine keeps starving, the siege keeps choking trade, beliefs keep
   spreading. Never a settlement freeze.
6. **Clamped floors (§128.3):** a pinned stressor's TERMINAL arms clamp at chair-derived
   floors — the pin preserves the suffering, never completes it.
7. **The dyadic clause (§128.4):** where a stressor has participants, the pin holds the
   WHOLE relationship in stasis — the attacker's commitment persists at its own clamped
   floor. Parties outside the stressor stay fully organic.
8. **The besieger union (§128a):** settlement-faction army OR external authored host —
   a typed record (name, strength band, disposition; setting-agnostic buckets, never a
   simulated character), no faction economy behind it. The besieged side simulates
   identically: the machinery reads THE SIEGE, not the besieger's pedigree. Unpinned
   external sieges resolve organically against the authored band; a pinned external
   besieger IS authored state — the dyadic clause holds it trivially.
9. **Exclusivity absolute + the conversion valve (§128b, §129.1):** a committed army is
   committed; the pin never exempts it. On a pinned two-party siege, edit mode offers
   ONE sanctioned action — COMMIT THE FORCE: the army converts to a §128a external host
   minted from its current strength band; the siege continues identically; the
   settlement books a recorded, provenance-carried loss; capacity frees. v1 converts the
   WHOLE force; valve available ONLY with the pin; unpinning a converted siege leaves
   the host authored. The valve GENERALIZES (§129.1) to any pin that locks a committed
   resource forever; pins that lock no resource (a famine) need no valve.
10. **The two-hat law (§129.2–3):** every console verb declares its hat. ORDER (default)
    moves through world physics — transit time, interception risk, news, exactly as if a
    faction willed it; order-class communications (supply requests, peace feelers)
    travel under the same message physics in non-omniscient worlds. AUTHOR is instant
    placement, provenance-marked AS AUTHORSHIP, no synthetic journey; the world
    discovers the placed thing through the normal belief plane. Both hats
    provenance-carried and visually distinct; AUTHOR-placed resources obey exclusivity
    FROM PLACEMENT ONWARD.
11. **Estate law:** §42/§43 — a value without an executed derivation is a promotion
    STOP; §49/§50 + §85.4 + §102.3 + §104.4 priced below; §77 premise maps at every
    compile; §45's SPV sweep and validator-topology simulation; census doors per the
    ceiling law (prefer re-points; name suites).
