With the car there, the owner's "before first advance" window closes on a world still at tick 0, and nothing reopens it. The cure costs no more lines: ink the sim clone.
2. **S-PA-4.** A confirmed fact is not pinned. Edit Mode's own `chooseOrPin` works because the pin IS the value. PA stores only a state word, and its stream label changes with every whole-realm re-roll. So the review's promise that "confirmed … facts stand" is false on the first realm re-roll, and the user's intent is silently replaced.
3. **S-PA-2.** The import lifecycle row is the opposite of the code. An account import builds `createNewCampaignWorldState` and re-mints every save id. Every choice and the whole inked past are lost, and PA's references could not survive the remint even if the record were carried.
