## The three I would defend hardest
1. **PS-r2-2 (the sea, the harbour, teleport).** The code was executed on the suite's own fixtures with the new-canon opt-ins: most march paths in a port world cross sea edges that have no gate. The convoy ledger is lit in PS's presets, the chair has already ruled the harbour to be PS's sea border gate (CHAIR-NOTES 14), and the owner says it is "already in the books". A builder cannot build PS2 as written on a lit world.
2. **PS-r2-1 (wartime tolerance).** The owner's sentence is unconditional: tolerance "is increased during wartime". PS makes it collapse to nothing at home, including when an enemy is merely passing through, and pins that with P13.
3. **PS-r2-3 / PS-r2-4 (the key and the skip).** Both are mechanical. PS keys the unit on the clock it shifts every held week. On the landed position law, the next-node test cannot see a territory the unit sweeps past within one tick; the probe found up to 16 of 56 marches leaving 20 intermediate territories unread.
