**The answer in one paragraph.** A menace is one thing: a hostile presence the DM places in the world that belongs to no settlement. The DM gives it a name in the DM's own words, says which country or road it is in, how mighty it is, and what it does there — stays and raids, lays siege to a town, goes somewhere, or roams — and that is all the engine is ever told. The engine never asks what it is. It has no mind: it does not choose, trade, believe or breed. It moves only on the course the DM set; it presses the towns it is among through the crisis machinery those towns already have; the roads near it grow dangerous; its news travels from the towns that saw it; and the courts that hear of it arm, send help or keep it home, and find common cause with neighbours they used to ignore. It can be worn down and fall back, and a menace beaten below its last strength wanes until the DM ends it. When it stands at a town's walls, every week of a week-by-week advance is a hearing: the DM may let the engine decide, tilt it, or give the verdict — and only the DM can let a town fall to it. Seen from the table it is unpredictable; asked "why", every step has its reason.

## §1 THE LAWS (MN1–MN15)

**MN1 — ONE KIND; THE STORY IS THE DM'S.** A menace is one generic kind. The engine never asks, stores or infers what it is. The DM's whole interface is a NAME and three choices — where, how mighty, and what it does there (§2). There is no type list, no motive, no behaviour catalogue, and no rule anywhere reads the name.

**MN2 — THE NAME IS QUOTED, NEVER PARSED.** The DM's words appear in narration exactly as written (the §934.36 flavour tier); no rule, classifier, regex or AI reads meaning from them. A blank name reads "an unnamed menace". The name rides only fields no rule reads: the record's `name`, the driven stressor's `originContext.attackerLabel` (the landed display path), the Herald's and the rumor's `menaceName`. It never rides a stressor's `label` or `name` and never an untyped threat label. 

**MN3 — ON THE MAP, FULL CONSEQUENCE; BEYOND IT, A PHANTOM.** A menace is ON-STAGE: the besieged town's reads continue as for any siege — the siege row on the war tab and in seal 6 (`liveSieges`' menace arm), the `siege` stressor the town already knows, the weekly hearing — and the world answers in full. A force beyond the map stays a PHANTOM under §934.43 (home procedures and a record). The two never merge. CN cn-5's "external authored host" IS a menace: MN-0 and MN-2 supersede cn-5's Produces 1–2, its flag `externalBesiegersEnabled` and its `disposition` bucket (dropped — the DM's story, MN1); §128b's conversion valve — the DM's COMMIT THE FORCE on a sealed siege — converts the whole besieging army into a menace (OD-MN-10) . 

**MN4 — IT DRIVES THE ONE CRISIS TRUTH, THROUGH ITS OWN RECORDS.** On every town it presses, the menace drives the stressor the world already knows — `monster_raider_pressure` while it raids, `siege` while it stands at the walls — as ITS OWN stressor record: an explicit id `world_stressor.<type>.<originSettlementId>.menace.<menaceId>` (idFor passes an explicit id through), stamped `originContext = { variant: 'menace', menaceId, attackerLabel: <the name>, attackerSettlementId: null, sponsorSettlementId: null }`, listed in the record's `drives`. Each record is born with its own EPISODIC policy (`durationPolicy: 'episodic'` on the record, the landed per-record idiom — a menace's siege is never left structural). While the menace presses it, the stressor is HELD: it is not rolled for resolution, it never falls below the menace's own severity (`MIGHT_SEVERITY[might]`; decay above that floor proceeds, so nothing ratchets), it does not spread, and it does not ESCALATE — its severity is the menace's might, which is the DM's, so the held record sits at that severity (or at the spent floor, §4.1) for as long as it is held.  When the menace leaves, is stilled, wanes or is ended — or the flags go dark — the hold drops, the record keeps its id, and it runs its episodic life to its end by its own type's counterforce, leaving its own residue (abandoned roads, militia burden, fearful hamlets); the release is receipted with the menace's event (§3.2). The birth is a DM-authored `consequence` birth, canon by construction, registered in the drama governor and never governed; while it presses, the governor COUNTS its one held record as one live arc, and its released tails not at all (§4.1) .  Migration, faith disorder, conditions and the stressor's counterforce already read the stressor. A menace never mints a parallel crisis. 

**MN5 — IT FEEDS ONE PRESENCE TERM IN THE ONE DANGER TRUTH.** Where it is, `embattlement` takes ONE bounded PRESENCE term, composed inside `advanceEmbattlement` before the step (it already receives the ledgers) by THE SHARED PRESENCE COMPOSER — an OWNED unit: its own manifest row, its owner volume THIS one, cut as its own unit ahead of whichever of C14 · C15 · C16 · C10 is cut first, every feeder registering its own row in its own wave and none re-implementing it ; it runs when ANY registered feeder's own flag is lit, each row gated by its own flag, so `menacesEnabled` gates only the menace's rows (§3.3, §8) : per town the MAX of its feeders, capped once — FOUR registered feeders: the menaces (MN), THE SITES' untied holders (`DESIGN_FP_ARCH_ST.md` §3.4, brief L-ST item 21), THE PASSAGE's enemy columns (`DESIGN_FP_ARCH_PS.md` PS12) and THE BODIES' row (`DESIGN_FP_ARCH_BD.md` §8.2, inside the suppressible road-country part)  — so three dangers on one road never triple; the ONE composition sentence is §3.3's `presenceTerms`, which ST §3.4, PS12 and PR §6.1 cite and never restate . Each term is sized to CROSS THE LATCH (`ENTER_THRESHOLD` 0.55), or no consumer would ever move: at the walls the landed siege weight net of the town's capped security relief (the kernel's own read and constants, so an army's siege and a menace's read the same); raiding a country, holding a road or travelling one, 0.60 at each town touched — and ONLY the towns the menace presses THIS tick: a town it has left reads no term from it after one tick (§4.2) . **MN6 — IT HAS NO MIND AND IS NOT PEOPLE.** No deciders, no economy, no faction, no belief of its own, no population, no census credit; the people ledger's conservation (`peopleLedger.js :: CONSERVATION_ENDPOINTS`) is untouched; the deaths it causes are ordinary sinks; it can never be "absorbed" into a town — and it holds no people of its own: a gang that goes over to it (the `enlists` course, §6) leaves the people count by the ledger's DM sink, never by the ledger's `enlist` event, which credits a unit (§6) . **MN7 — THE WORLD KNOWS IT BY REPORT.** Courts answer what they have heard (Law One). **MN8 — ITS SIEGES ARE HEARD; THE ENGINE NEVER SACKS AND NEVER RAZES.** A menace at a town's walls is a STANDING engagement (`menace_siege`) on the registry's one siege vocabulary `holds · falls · relieved · lifted`. The engine's worst default is a BREACH — a receipt inside `holds`: institutions ruined through `ruinInstitution`, deaths and flight as ordinary deltas, a bounded loss keyed `menace:<id>:<tick>`, capped per PRESENCE (the menace at that town, across lifts and regroups — the press envelope, §4.1)  — and NEVER the calamity strike, its stamp, its cooldown or its disaster-response crisis. That a town FALLS to a menace is the DM's verdict only, and with no conqueror a fall is a SACK: the breach at its full bound. Razing or abandoning a town is a separate DM act through the landed roads. 

**MN9 — THE DM HOLDS BOTH HATS (§129), THROUGH ONE DOOR.** Four realm verbs (§2), each ≤ four dials, each a realm-manifest row (J-EM-2), each a proposal whose `targetSaveId` is a REAL settlement — the menace's anchor — with the menace riding a subject dial. AUTHOR acts ("it is so") are provenance-marked as authorship and, like every realm verb, staged then approved (the landed shape, recorded); ORDER acts go through the world's grammar. Every verb is premium (the simulator is gated for premium). 

**MN10 — A MENACE CAN LOSE, AND THEN IT WANES.** It loses a band of might when a siege is lifted or relieved — including a siege that wears itself out (a season at the walls with no breach lifts by exhaustion) — one or two when an army beats it in the field, and one when the towns it raids hold it off for a season.  Below `thin` it WANES: the record stays on the ledger pressing nothing until the DM ends it. The DM may HOLD its might (worn by nothing). 

**MN11 — A STRIKE IS NOT A PRESENCE.** A calamity is a strike: once, then its tail. A menace is a presence: it stays, presses, can be fought, moves and can lose. A "beast wave" the DM wants as a single blow is a calamity; as a host that lingers on the road, a menace. They share the bounded arithmetic (`resolvePopulationLoss`, `selectStrikeTargets`), never the calamity record: a breach is never stamped "Great Calamity", never resets the natural cooldown and never puts the ruler under a disaster-response crisis. They never double-count.

Ambient raider stressors are born by their own gates as today (`stressorGates.js :: monsterRaiderGate`), unnamed. A body becomes a menace only by the DM's `PROMOTE_BODY` (§2) or by joining a menace whose authored course `enlists` (§6) — never by the engine [r4: L-BD's routes (2)–(3)]. 

**MN13 — THE MOUNT RULE.** Zero lines in `pulseKernel.js` (1581), `applyWorldPulse.js` (907), `warTermination.js` (818). The per-tick menace stage is a LEAF called at the head of an existing stage, `calamityKernel.js :: advanceCalamity` (461 effective lines), measured and recommended by MN-0's car-0 (§9) with its STOP conditions; hosts at the 800 ceiling (`generosityKernel.js` 800, `armyTransitKernel.js` 798) take nothing. Every behaviour enters an existing decider as ONE bounded signal (the E14 precedent); the pressed court's signal rides the chooser's ONE shared-signal car (SX-08). 

**MN14 — THE PHYSICAL WORLD IS A SEAM.** Until the world canon lands, WHERE is the country around a settlement or a road between two (node-keyed). The world canon is the concrete carrier of this seam (the chair's host ruling of 2026-10-18, from the map builder's document): when it lands, WHERE becomes what the canon carries — a WORLD POINT (H5: per-seat queries and points; terrain is not FP's law, its law 5) or a ROUTE named by its seat pair (H12) — never a cell key. ### 3.2 The driven stressor's stamp

A menace's stressor is its own record: `{ id: 'world_stressor.<type>.<originSettlementId>.menace.<menaceId>', type: 'monster_raider_pressure' | 'siege', label: MENACE_STRESSOR_LABELS[type], originSettlementId, affectedSettlementIds, severityBySettlement?, severity: MIGHT_SEVERITY[might], originContext: { variant: 'menace', menaceId, attackerLabel: <name>, attackerSettlementId: null, sponsorSettlementId: null }, durationPolicy: 'episodic', decayRate: STRESSOR_POLICIES.episodic.decay }` — the record's OWN policy (the precedent: `relationshipRulesCore.js`'s rebellion mint) — minted through `normalizeStressor` (the one shape home). `MENACE_STRESSOR_LABELS = { monster_raider_pressure: 'Pressure from outside', siege: 'A siege from outside' }` — neutral NOUNS (the catalog speaks a label as a noun: "`${stressor.label} is deepening`", "`${stressor.label} still grips the settlement.`") chosen so `archetypeForStressor` promotes each exactly as its type alone does (`war_pressure` both; executed against the landed rules, `cure/D-MN-r1.md` §1(l)) ; a walker pin asserts it, and asserts that no menace record ever carries a non-null `attackerSettlementId` or `sponsorSettlementId`. A road raid is ONE record over both towns (`affectedSettlementIds: [a, b]`, `severityBySettlement` at the landed spread attenuation 0.72 for the far end). Re-pressing a town re-adopts the same id if that record is still active (no duplicate). **Adoption is always IN PLACE** : when `pressMenaces` presses a town with type T it takes, in order, (1) an active record of type T at that town that carries this menace's `menaceId` (held, or released and still running), (2) a record the DM's act hands it — the ambient raider record at `RAISE_MENACE` (MN12) or the war's sealed siege record at COMMIT THE FORCE (§5.6) — and only then (3) mints `world_stressor.<T>.<town>.menace.<menaceId>`. Two menaces at one town are two records; the town's conditions sum as today.  The variant `menace` is an 18th origin voice for the prose lane's corpus (`stressorsStateProse.js :: stressorOriginPoolKey` renders an unknown variant as nothing, so it is silent, never wrong, until voiced).

**The release.**  When a menace leaves a town (it moves on, falls back from the walls, is stilled, wanes, is ended, or the sack sends it into the country), the one writer takes the record out of `drives` — the hold drops that tick — stamps `originContext.menaceRelease = { tick, causedBy: <the menace's event receipt> }` and clears `attackerLabel` (no live besieger is shown; the cause walk follows `causedBy` to the menace). The id, the type and the `menaceId` stamp stay (provenance; `menaceHolds` reads `drives`, never the stamp). The record is episodic, so its own aging ends it fast — EXECUTED over 200 seeds with no counterforce (`cure/D-MN-r1-probe/probe2.out`): a siege record released at eight weeks old or more resolves within one to four weeks (at twenty weeks old, in the very next week: the past-`maxAge` bump and the age term take its chance to one), a younger one within eight; the same record left structural (the catalog's default) stays above the blockade gate for twenty-two weeks and resolves only at week thirty (`probe.out`); the siege's own counterforce, where the town has one, only shortens it; its residue (damaged walls, veteran unrest, defensive debt) is the catalog's — exactly the end the landed wind-down gives a war (`windDownSponsoredStressors`: "the wars-never-end failure this exists to fix"). ### 4.1 The press and the hold (MN-1)

- **Writer.** `menaceLedger.js :: pressMenaces`, inside the menace stage (MN-0's host), the ONE writer of menace stressor records, through `normalizeStressor` into `worldState.stressors` — the post-apply direct write of `pestilenceKernel.js` (which mints the ordinary `disease_outbreak` the same way: "we drive that stressor, never a parallel one"). The governor COUNTS it while it presses: `narrativeTempo.js :: countLiveMajorArcs(worldState)` counts every stressor in an active stage by type, and a pressing menace HOLDS exactly ONE record at a time (raiding: one raider record; at the walls: one siege record, its raider record released; `abroad`, `stilled` and `waned` hold none) — but every record it has RELEASED runs on for its episodic tail (median three weeks, at most about nine, EXECUTED by the verifier) and would still count. - **What it presses.** `raiding` in a country → `monster_raider_pressure` at that town; `raiding` on a road → one raider record over both towns; a waypoint week → the raider record at that town for that week; `at_the_walls` → `siege` at the town (its raider record there is released); `abroad`, `stilled`, `waned` → nothing.
