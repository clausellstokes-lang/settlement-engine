**The answer in one paragraph.** A kingdom is not a new thing in the world; it is the shape the vassal edges already make, read upward to the settlement that answers to no one. That settlement is the capital. Every town keeps its own court, its own beliefs and its own choices; being sworn adds one voice to every one of those choices — the liege's — and that voice grows band by band with the owner's own measure of the bond ("scales by how close the vassal is in culture and trade and relationship with the overlord"). ## §1 THE LAWS (PL1–PL17)

**PL1 — THE POLITY IS A PATTERN, NEVER AN ENTITY.** A polity is the closure of SWORN links upward to a PARAMOUNT: the settlement whose own looming read names no overlord. Members, tiers, the paramount and every band are DERIVED per read; membership is never stored. THE CAPITAL IS THE PARAMOUNT — derived, never a field (ODQ §12). A sworn link is a `foreignSeatOf` view with `regime: 'vassalage'` whose `basis` is the vassal EDGE (`vassal_edge` · `vassal_edge_treaty_floor`) or the matured rung (`occupation_rung_vassalized`) ONLY; a `treaty_subordinating_ties` view (a tributary, a puppet seat) is the SPHERE, never membership.   A patron is influence, not sovereignty, and is never a member.

**PL2 — ONE ROOT READ ABOVE THE ONE SEAT READ.** `polityOf` walks `foreignSeatOf(…).patronSettlementId` upward with a cycle guard and a depth cap; it never re-derives who looms over a settlement (W-SEAT: one resolver per quantity).    

**PL3 — THE ENGINE BUILDS THE BONES; THE DM NAMES THE KINGDOM.** The default display is descriptive ("Merewick and its sworn towns"); the engine never mints a polity name and NEVER MOVES ONE. The only persisted naming datum is the DM's christening, written only by the DM's `NAME_POLITY` act. 

**PL4 — THE LIEGE'S VOICE IS IN EVERY DECISION, AT A SHARE THAT GROWS WITH THE OWNER'S SCALAR, AND NEVER ORDERS.**   In every decision of a sworn member its immediate liege is a counterpart (§735.2: "an equal or greater counterpart in all decision-makings … scales by how close the vassal is in culture and trade and relationship with the overlord, and is still influential"). Its share is a MONOTONE BAND MAP of the owner's scalar `foreignSeatOf(…).weight01`, read through its landed band: `POLITY_LIEGE_SHARE = { marginal: 0.10, present: 0.25, strong: 0.38, dominant: 0.45 }` (DRAFT, UNMEASURED, FP-46 roster). So the voice scales band by band, and vassalage stays below the occupier's "always just greater" (§735.2: "it can be lower than an occupier's"). Wherever a decider reads the seat books, `keep = 1 − POLITY_LIEGE_SHARE[band]` for a sworn member; where a class's deciders read no book, the liege's voice enters through that class's landed seam (§3.2's second column). What the crown's HOLD governs is never which classes are heard, but (a) how far the PARAMOUNT's voice reaches past its own vassals (PL5) and (b) the obligation cascades (§4). Vassalage NEVER orders: primacy is occupation's alone, and the old "capped below an occupier's weight" clause stays dropped. PL mints NO loyalty quantity: a member's DEFERENCE is its seat weight and its word is the seat band. ⚑ OWNER-VISIBLE (OD-PL-12): the share map is the chair's reading of "an equal or greater counterpart … it can be lower than an occupier's", set beside the owner's quotation for his veto.

**PL5 — THE VASSAL OF MY VASSAL IS NOT MY VASSAL — EXCEPT AS FAR AS THE CROWN'S HOLD REACHES.**   The paramount reaches a sub-vassal through the mid-lord's own decisions AND, in the sub-vassal's own decisions, through ONE blend inside the member's one foreign book (`foreignBookFor`): the stance term is `(1 − r)·liege + r·paramount` (`liegeStanceToward` read for each), where `r = POLITY_PARAMOUNT_REACH[band of polityHoldOf(worldState, snapshot, paramountId, subVassalId)]` — the paramount's hold over ITS direct vassals as the deciding sub-vassal believes it — with `POLITY_PARAMOUNT_REACH = { frayed: 0, shaky: 0.1, staunch: 0.2, unbroken: 0.3 }` (DRAFT, UNMEASURED) — liege homage. At `frayed` the paramount's share is zero and the partition is bit-identical to a liege-only book. The quantity is served once as `crownReachAt` (§2): CH's crown retainer, PT's crown backing and the crown's ban read it. Frozen `warTermination.js` takes zero lines.

**PL6 — THE CROWN SPEAKS ABROAD; THE TOWN RULES AT HOME; OBLIGATION BINDS THE SEAM; THE HOLD SETS HOW FAR THE CROWN REACHES DOWN THE CHAIN.** (The precedence law; §3 is its table.) 

**PL7 — NOTHING IS FORBIDDEN; TWO ACTS ARE DEFINED, PRICED ON THE SPOT, AND HEARD.** A member's deciders remain its own; precedence is a weighted voice and receipted news (requirement 10). Two acts break fealty by definition: (a) a SEPARATE PEACE with its liege's enemy while the liege still fights it; (b) JOINING a war or coalition AGAINST its own liege.    

**PL8 — OBLIGATIONS RUN BOTH WAYS, ARE ANSWERED, AND ARE REMEMBERED IN THE FEALTY RECORD.** Up: SERVICE, DUES, AID. Down: PROTECTION, ARBITRATION, PASSAGE. An up-obligation is answered by the member's own fork; a down-obligation is a DECIDED act of the liege.
