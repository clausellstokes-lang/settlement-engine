**The answer in one paragraph.** The party is the one actor in the world the engine never moves. Its choices are made at the table and enter the world only as DEEDS the DM records with the act already in hand — the night's event, with marks that fill themselves in (the party's hand; who backed it, pre-filled only when the party is doing the work of a request it accepted; how it looks on its face) and a fourth (whether the reason was kept quiet, told or shown) that defaults to told; the DM changes any of them with one touch and never has to mark anything at all. A told reason carries only what the DM marked: if the DM says the burning was just, everyone who hears why takes it as just, and those who never hear why judge the act alone. A toggle says where the party is, and the party's last deed places it on its own whenever that word is the later one. Word of each deed travels the roads like any other tale — in the river towns they may only hear that the party burned an orphanage, never why — and every town, court, faith and person who hears it judges it by its own lights (THE RENOWN). Those who have heard and care then come for the party: to ask its help with what they need, to offer work or a title, to summon it, to turn it away at the town's gate, to post a warrant or a bounty, to lie in wait, to denounce or praise it in public. Each is a hearing in its week only: the DM lets it reach the table, strikes it without a trace, or amends it; left alone, nothing has happened to the party yet — the searcher is still searching, the ambushers still waiting — and the world goes on: the plague spreads, the siege runs its course, patience wears thin, and a summons ignored becomes a warrant, then a bounty, then hunters on the north road. When the party says yes, the need's own course is the clock; a promise the party could have kept and did not costs it, one it could never have kept costs nothing, and a party that went where it was needed in time is never blamed for a box the DM forgot to tick. The DM's window puts the party first — what its deeds have done, who knows of it and what they think, who is looking for it and what it owes — in the world's words; the bands and the odds wait for an intentional look.

## §1 THE LAWS (PT1–PT18)

**PT1 — THE PARTY IS THE WORLD'S ONE OUTSIDE ACTOR.** The party has no decider in the engine. Its choices enter the world only as DEEDS the DM records through the landed party idiom (`applyPartyImpact`, the twelve kinds, `cause: 'party'`, canon by construction, no roll, no proposal queue) and through the deed doors §3 names. No engine path moves, captures, jails, injures, kills or binds the party, and no hold or proposal is ever keyed to the party (R3 refinement 11). The one ruled departure from R3's "never writes a hold, edge or proposal keyed to the party" is the ban: RULINGS-AFTER-RESEARCH §4 widens the one ban system's edge to actor refs, so a court may close its own gate to the party (§9.3) — a fact about the door, never a force upon the party. The simulation stays indifferent to the party; the DM's VIEW puts it first (`partyView`, §12). 

**PT2 — THE DEED MARK RIDES THE ACT, WITH ZERO-CLICK DEFAULTS (the chair notes item 3).** The marks ride the act the DM already records, pre-filled: CONTRIBUTED = yes for a party impact (the door is the party's hand); BACKED BY = pre-selected ONLY when the act falls where the party is doing the work of a request it accepted — the act's place (`actAt`) is the settlement of an open `pledged` engagement's need and that requester's reach covers it (§10.1) — else none; MORAL WEIGHT = absent (RN's derived inherent value governs); PROOF = `spoken` (told), which is harmless because a told reason carries only a weight the DM marked (PT13). One act changes any mark. After an advance the DM's view offers BATCHED, non-blocking SUGGESTIONS ("The party was in Kell when the granary burned — did they have a hand in it?"), each answered by one act or ignored forever. The burden degrades gracefully: no marks at all is a coherent world in which the party is unknown, and nothing breaks; a pledge the party went to keep is judged kept from the world's own evidence even when the DM records nothing (§8.6). The ONE required mark is the party's answer to an accepted request (one act on its card, §8). 

**PT3 — PRESENCE IS THE DM'S TOGGLE; WHERE THE PARTY IS, IS THE LATER WORD.** "The party is here" (`presentAt`, the DM's toggle, with its tick) persists until the DM moves it. Every deed authored with the party's hand places the party at the act's settlement(s) (`lastHeard`, DERIVED from the newest unstruck placing deed — §4), so the toggle rarely needs touching. ONE whereabouts read serves everything: `whereaboutsOf(party)` = whichever of `presentAt` and `lastHeard` carries the LATER STAMP, with its source. A stamp is (tick, `partySeq`): the tick the act was recorded at and the party's own monotone sequence, which every `SET_PRESENCE`, every answer and every party deed takes from the sub-ledger as it is written — so a deed recorded after the toggle in the same session is the later word, and so is a toggle set after that deed; where no sequence distinguishes two stamps of one tick, the deed wins (RULINGS-ROUND2 B: "the deed is the newer fact"). The journey, fault, witnesses, suggestions, the backing default, nearness in the view and the champions' visits all read it. Searchers read the party's last-heard place AS THEY HEARD IT (belief); witnesses are the actors where the party is SEEN (the toggle, when it is the later word). A pair kind places the party near both endpoints (one rule: both are stored on the deed's `actAt`, and a journey from a pair starts at the nearer one). The engine never writes `presentAt`; only the DM does.  

**PT4 — FAME, REGARD, CREDIBILITY AND INHERENT VALUE ARE RN'S.** PT mints no fame, no regard store, no credibility law and no value table: the party is a SUBJECT of RN's fame and regard and the subject of RN's party credibility (RN1, RN10), RN §7's persisted holder on PT's sub-ledger written by the party door (§2.1; RULINGS-ROUND2 A.1); its allies-and-enemies list is RN's authored regard (`DECLARE_STANCE`, `declaredStance: ally · enemy`) on any holder the owner named — an NPC once persons light, a settlement, a court, a faction, a creed's faithful, and a KINGDOM (RN's key `c:<rootId>`, read through PL's `polityOf`; no kingdom while `polityEnabled` is dark); the net effect at any meeting is RN's body derivation (`courtRegardOf`) read with the place's politics (§9.1). PT supplies the deed doors, the marks and the rows its deed kinds register into RN's value table (§3.3; RN derives, PT registers — the registry idiom the chair ruled for CH's act kinds, SEAMS-r1-2).  

**PT5 — EVERY ENGAGEMENT IS A HEARING, ONLY ON THE WEEK (requirement 24, as the owner restated it).** Every act of the world toward the party — including a court, or a renowned person or company, that would praise or denounce it in public (`party_voiced`, the chair's ruling of SEAMS-r1-6; its voices RULINGS-ROUND3 A.11) — is a registered engagement kind (§13). A hearing is minted only at the DM's one-week advance and lives that one week; the next advance resolves it first; month, season and year advances run every one on its default without stopping; nothing queues and nothing is held over an advance. Each STANDING kind is a NEW hearing every week the actor still presses it, in its CURRENT state. 

**PT6 — THE PARTY'S DEFAULT IS ALWAYS THAT NOTHING HAS HAPPENED TO IT YET.** The engine's default verdict for every party kind leaves the party untouched; the RNG decides only the ACTOR's own course (keep looking, keep waiting, try again, find help elsewhere, escalate, give up, turn against). An ambush the DM leaves is ambushers still waiting; an arrest left is a warrant still unserved. What happens to the party happens at the table and returns as a deed. The ONLY deeds the engine records ABOUT the party are outcomes of hearings in their weeks: the pledge verdicts (`made_good · fell_short`, by the owner's own word, PT10), a backing voided or repudiated (§10.3; a correction of the backed deed, RULINGS-ROUND2 A.4), and a public voice the hearing let be spoken (RN's `voiced_regard`, §7.3) — none of them is an act upon the party's body or freedom.  

**PT7 — TWO ANSWERS, TWO HANDS, NEVER MERGED.** In the hearing's week the DM answers OUTSIDE the story through the HEARING SEAL (requirement 24(b): "keyed by ENGAGEMENT id with the registry's verdict vocabulary, read by the resolver FIRST") — leave it (the default), STRIKE it (the seal's verdict `struck`: no trace in the world; it quiets that actor's urge for a while), or AMEND it (a seal carrying a course word, or `reaches_party` to bring the actor to the table now). `STRIKE_ENGAGEMENT` and `AMEND_ENGAGEMENT` are that seal's UI, never a second writer: the resolver alone writes `strikes` and the engagement's state when it reads the seal at the next advance. At the table the DM records the party's answer INSIDE the story — `yes · later · no` — as a deed. A "no" is a deed with consequences; a strike is not a deed and leaves nothing. 

**PT8 — THE ACTOR'S STATES ARE STATES IN THE WORLD, NEVER PENDING HEARINGS.** An engagement stands in one of three states (`engagementState: seeking · awaiting · pledged`; prose "not found yet · not yet engaged · yes, but later" — the brief's "holds"), each shown with its reason in the world's words; a hearing is minted only in the week the actor presses it. Nothing the DM leaves outlives its week. To save an encounter, the DM leaves it and directs the actor later. 

**PT9 — SILENCE IS NEVER A DECISION.** With no answer in the hearing's week, the default is computed from the world, per kind (§13): `seeking` while the actor is elsewhere or does not know where the party is, `awaiting` once actor and party share a place (by `whereaboutsOf`, as the actor heard it) — and an ambush `awaiting` for as long as the party's whereabouts stay within its reach. If still live next week, next week's hearing shows it as it stands then. An engagement that closes by the world's own course (the actor gives up, the need passes) closes in the world's words with NO cost to the party — no deed, no regard change — because in the story the actor never reached them. 

**PT10 — TWO CLOCKS, NEVER MERGED; FAULT IS JUDGED AGAINST THE WORLD AS IT UNFOLDED.** PURSUIT (how long an actor keeps looking or waiting — by personality, urgency and distance) runs while the actor seeks or awaits; when it is spent the actor gives up at no cost to the party. Once the party says yes the NEED'S CLOCK governs where the need has one (a stressor, a war-layer siege, a war, a bounty's quarry, or an actor's own term — §8.2 ); PATIENCE governs only where it has none, and a pledge under patience alone can never be broken. A reachable miss is a BROKEN PLEDGE (`fell_short`) and COUNTS BY DEFAULT (the requester's regard falls, every other hearer reads it by its tie to the requester, and the party's word weighs less — RN §6.1's pledge reading and RN §7's `MISS_FALL`, never the lie charge); a pledge the party could never have reached costs nothing (`came_to_nothing`); a party that reached the need in time made good whether or not the DM said so (§8.6); a "later" set beyond an actor's own deadline is no promise the requester can lean on. 

**PT11 — THE WORLD NEVER WAITS.** A deferred engagement never freezes the actor's situation. A request closes only when its NEED closes by its own state ("Tolle's sickness has passed") — a champion already at work there is a reason on the card ("a healer of renown is already at work in Tolle") that lowers the request's draw, never a closure, because the plague still stands; patience running out makes a NEW engagement (seek other help · escalate · give up · turn against), never a verdict on the party. Escalation emerges — summons → warrant → bounty → hunters — each a hearing in its own week. 

**PT12 — SANCTION IS ONE MARK WITH A REACH AND A WEIGHT; IT NEVER EXPIRES ON ITS OWN.** The DM's backing mark (`backing { by, backerRole }`; "sanction" is the prose word) covers the backer's REACH, weighted by the backer's standing there (§10). Inside its reach and where heard, it softens a villainous-looking act and amplifies a good one through ONE bounded term (§10.2; RN's value rows carry no backing shift). It is shared ownership (credit to the backer; the backer's enemies inherit a grudge; a false reason exposed shares the blame). Its counterforces are named (§10.3) — a rival's COUNTER-BACKING (a warrant, bounty or ban from the rival, each its own hearing) is not RN's public voice; the backing ends only if VOIDED by a higher authority over the same lands or REPUDIATED by a successor, each a hearing. 

**PT13 — MUTATION RIDES THE LANDED WEATHERING; DRIFT NEVER INVENTS A REASON; A TOLD REASON CARRIES ONLY WHAT THE DM MARKED.** A deed travels as one telling whose PARTS (act · size · reason · proof · backing · doer) are VISIBLE by the landed completeness thresholds in a fixed order (reason and backing fall away first; size drifts by the landed band roll — as often down as up, so the greatest tales only fade; rarely the doer swaps to a real actor). No new draw for any non-deed event, no second engine. The REASON's content is the DM's: where the DM marked a moral weight, that weight REPLACES the act's derived band for every hearer that can see it — its version shows the reason (§11.3; RULINGS-ROUND2 A.2); where the reason never reached it, or was kept quiet, or the DM marked no weight, the hearer judges the act alone. A false reason exists only where a liar plants it (the slander, PT-S2's producer), traceable to its source. The truth can chase the rumor at the same speed (§11.2).  

**PT14 — BOUNDED RESPONSIVENESS.** Only actors who have HEARD of the party (RN's `fameIn`) and CARE (a need it is known to answer, or a regard away from indifference) engage it; each actor holds at most one open engagement with the party per family and DRAFT 2 in all; the party draws at most DRAFT 6 open engagements and at most DRAFT 3 new ones a week; a struck or closed engagement cools that actor for a while; every draw is seeded on a labelled key (same seed, same world).

**PT15 — ONE PARTY PER CAMPAIGN, AN ADDITIVE SLOT FOR A SECOND.** The actor ref is `{kind:'party'}` with no id; the sub-ledger holds one record. A second party is recorded as the additive slot (an `id` on the ref and a keyed map), never a second shape (RULINGS §5, R2 Q5).

**PT16 — THE MOUNT RULE.** Zero lines in `pulseKernel.js` (1581), `applyWorldPulse.js` (907) and `warTermination.js` (818); nothing in `generosityKernel.js` (800), `armyTransitKernel.js` (798), `roadsKernel.js` (838), `informationStatecraft.js` (781) or `npcDmVerbs.js` (711). `store/settlementSlice.js` (816, AT its size baseline) is touched only by a leaf-first car that moves its two world-reconciliation blocks out byte-identically and so SHRINKS it (§3.4). Every PT module is a leaf under `src/domain/party/`; every host is named with its effective lines (§14). 

**PT17 — THE PHYSICAL WORLD IS A SEAM.** Until the physical world lands, a place is a settlement and a journey is the named-person physics over the frozen digest, or one week a hop over the relationship graph where there is no map. Every wave is whole without a map (§15's degraded arms). When the map lands: the party's piece is an H17 presentation mover (presence, never intention — HR:667); the party may stand at a SITE once the owner's world-site record lands (a `site` arm on `presentAt`; the map reads the record from M-SITE-A on, HR:644), and PT is then the producer of ENC-HL-04's "the party reached a place" event (its reveal path in M-SITE-A, HR:647); the party's KNOWN PLACES (what the players have been told of) are the host's map record, written by the host's reveal from M-SITE-A, never by PT (ENC-HS-01, HR:601; day-one Q10); a road closed by a menace, a war or the DM's `physicalChange` reaches the journey through H12's route windows and H15's tick-head edits (PT-S1's producer, §8.4).  

**PT18 — BEHIND THE SCENES; THE EVIDENCE LAW.** The surface speaks in the world's words ("the Abbess grows impatient"; "in the river towns they say you burned an orphanage"); scrutiny shows the story's path, who twisted it, who is looking and why; bands, clocks and odds only on an intentional look. Every card names who, upon whom, where and the default — never what the DM might do; never a forecast; never a numeral; never a mechanism name; never the bare word "gate" (always "the town's gate" or "Kell's gate"). Unpredictable in prospect, legible and coherent on scrutiny; the DM's undo, strike and amend are the safety net.

## §8 THE CLOCKS, REACHABILITY AND FAULT (PT-2)

### 11.3 What a told reason does — it carries only the DM's marked weight (PT13; PT owns the field, RN reads it)
