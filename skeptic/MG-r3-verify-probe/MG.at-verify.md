## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH (for the DM)

Every thing that moves on your map — an army, a part of an army sent ahead or left behind, a fleet, a town's riders, a gang or a flock or a pack, a named traveller — carries at most ONE movement word a week from a short closed list: keep its course, hold, hide, lie in wait, double back, shadow, feint, force the pace, split, regroup, reroute, or (only when beaten) retreat. An army, a part of an army and a fleet CHOOSE theirs: whoever commands chooses on what its own court BELIEVES about its pursuers, its quarry, its clock, its roads and its own strength, leaning the way its court makes war; every choice has a reason from a closed list and a price (weeks against the campaign's clock, a part staked, a trick read once it is seen). A traveller, a band of riders and a gang or a beast are NAMED by the choice they already make for themselves (an envoy's errand, a champion's aim, the riders' round, the gang's week) — the grammar gives the map and the Why panel one word for it, never a second decision. The other side never sees the reason — it sees what the unit appears to do, or nothing at all, and decides for itself what that means. Nothing here is a new road, a new speed or a new battle: units travel the one road network at the one pace the season allows; a forced pace, a rider's gain and an ambush's edge are each priced once, by the volume that owns that physics; an army moves at its slowest member; a great host cannot hide; a ship never splits; a fleet waits at anchor or holds its course, and turns at sea only once a sea re-plan exists [CURED 2026-10-20 (fp-final P6 r2) — MG-V1]; a part sent apart from an army waits whenever the army itself is held at a border, by a fort or by riders, and never strays into a stranger's land [CURED 2026-10-20 (fp-final P6 r3) — MG-V3]. Nothing is ever a hearing of its own — when two moving things meet, the meeting is the hearing its owner already registered, in its week. You see deeds ("Kell's host turned back at the ford"); you see the reasons only on that unit's own page; you can set any army's or fleet's choice yourself.
