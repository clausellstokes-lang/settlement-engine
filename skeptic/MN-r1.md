## The three I would defend hardest

1. **MN-r1-1 — the eternal siege.** The menace is always one besieger, and the landed gate never lets a solo attacker below 92% of the defence roll; that early return comes before the age ceiling. A weaker menace at the walls therefore holds forever, with no breach, lift, ceiling or wear. Relief or a tilt for the town can push a liftable siege into that state. MN10's "can lose" fails at the walls, read straight from `feasibilityGate.js` and `warSiegeVerdict.js`.
2. **MN-r1-2 — the siege that outlives its besieger.** A released menace siege is structural and unsponsored, so nothing winds it down. The landed wind-down's own comment calls this "the wars-never-end failure". Ending, stilling or sacking leaves a town blockaded and fleeing for months with no one at its walls.
3. **MN-r1-3 — the dead danger channel.** Raid 0.45 and road 0.35 sit under the latch's 0.55 ENTER threshold. A calm town under a raiding or road menace never becomes embattled, and every route, banditry and migration consumer reads the level, not the threat. M3 tests the threat, so the build would pass with the channel dead.
