# SETTLEMENTFORGE — THE BRAND KIT
> Answered from the built identity (option A: the brand exists). Sources: the craft
> law (docs/DESIGN_ORGANIC_CRAFT.md), the seal record, the entitlement rulings, the
> shipped copy registries, and the six research sweeps. Nothing here is invented
> fresh — it is the brand as constructed, stated once for external use.

## 1. Brand name
**SettlementForge.** One word, two honest halves: the thing made (a settlement) and
the making of it (forged, not rolled). Never abbreviated in public copy; "SF" lives
only in internal registers.

## 2. Brand slogan
Primary: **"A world that holds together."** (already shipped in the hero dek — the
coherency engine stated as a feeling). Ceremonial motto, used only as a caption under
the seal: **"State, never fate."** Footer oath: *"Simulated, not AI-generated."*

## 3. Brand positioning
The deterministic world-engine for tabletop play. Not a generator — a **simulator**:
where competitors draw maps (Inkarnate, Wonderdraft), store lore (World Anvil), or
roll tables (donjon), SettlementForge **runs the world** — economies, factions,
grudges, and history that explain each other, advance over time, and carry receipts
for every number. Category of one: the living settlement with causal provenance.
Premium-indie: priced like a tool, crafted like a book.

## 4. Target audience
Primary: **the weekly-table DM** (session prep in one click) and **the worldbuilder
between campaigns** (depth to explore for its own sake). Secondary: module writers
and publishers (exports), play-by-post communities (shared canonical worlds via
seed), solo-RPG players, and fiction writers who need a town that makes sense.

## 5. Brand personality
The surveyor's desk: **learned, calm, exact, warm.** Handmade, never techy; premium
like a bound volume, not like chrome. Honest to a fault — the brand states its
mechanics plainly (flat task prices, the export covenant, "the engine writes canon,
AI only proposes") and never markets with reassurance it can't receipt. Wonder comes
from depth, not decoration.

## 6. Color palette (the shipped ink-and-parchment system)
- Parchment ground (light): `#EFE6D4` · Umber ground (dim/lamplight): `#221C17`
- Ink, warm black: `#2B2320` · Ink, pale (on dim): `#E8DCC8`
- **Rubric oxblood (the one accent): `#9A2B1E`** · dim-field rubric: `#C05A3F`
- Burnished gold (the single action voice): the `gold` token family (~`#B08D3F`)
- Slate (the AI's honest channel): the `slate` token family (~`#5A6672`)
Law: one tonal ink ramp, one rubric accent, no gradients, no pure white/black.
Canonical values live in `src/design/tokens.js` — the file is the palette of record.

## 7. Typography direction
**Lora** (Google Fonts) at display and body — the period voice with real italics;
**Nunito** (Google Fonts) for instrument labels and UI small caps. Old-style figures
where offered; letter-spaced true small caps for labels; faux small caps banned.
Recorded upgrade path: an IM Fell-class display face for ceremonial heads (asset-gated).

## 8. Logo direction
**The Station Seal** (approved, built, live): a ring broken by a settlement roofline,
the surveyor's station triangle centered, one oxblood seal-point at its centroid.
Four strokes and a dot; hand-inked; **no text inside the mark, ever** — the name is
always adjacent type. Registers: the device alone (favicon/stamps) and the wordmark
lockup (device + "SettlementForge" in Lora). On exports it pairs with the settlement's
own seeded medallion — seal and counterseal.

## 9. Product description
Forge a settlement in one click — hamlet to metropolis — and receive a **dossier**:
who holds power, what's about to break, who matters tonight, with every number able
to explain its own origin on hover. Advance time and the world *runs*: wars resolve,
prices move, faiths spread, NPCs climb and fall, chronicles write themselves. Town
maps with lenses, panorama, and building interiors; exports (PDF, VTT) that are yours
forever, account or not. Deterministic to the seed: same seed, same world, every
time, on any machine. The optional Surveyor AI reads and proposes in its own slate
ink — **only the deterministic engine writes canon**.

## 10. Key benefits
1. Session prep in one click — the Summary tab *is* tonight's notes.
2. Every number explains itself — provenance on hover, receipts throughout.
3. The world keeps living — advance a month or a decade; it holds together.
4. Yours forever — exports survive cancellation; the covenant is written and tested.
5. Honest AI — flat task prices, proposes-never-writes, bring your own key.
6. Shareable canon — a seed is a world; hand it to anyone.

## 11. Visual mood
**The surveyor's desk by lamplight.** Parchment with real grain; warm ink; oxblood
wax; brass dividers and a folded map at frame's edge; engraved-illustration style
(never painterly fantasy art, never 3D render gloss). Light rakes low and warm across
paper. Motion is paper physics: documents lay down, pages turn, seals impress once —
nothing bounces, nothing glows. Atmosphere: a working scriptorium, not a museum.

## 12. Website goal
Not a pre-order page — **a working door.** The landing demonstrates rather than
describes: the visitor forges a real settlement inside their first minute, free, no
account. Goal ladder: first forge → free account (saves) → Cartographer (the living
Realm). Every claim on the page is backed by a live artifact the visitor can touch.

## 14. Cinematic video brief (for the Higgsfield MCP — NOT generated yet)
**"The Surveyor's Desk"** — a loopable cinematic background, 12–20s, 2.39:1 crop-safe.
SCENE: a dark oak desk by lamplight (single warm key, low and raking; deep umber
falloff to `#221C17`). On it: a parchment map of a small walled town, mid-inking —
rooflines and a harbour visible; brass dividers laid across the margin; a stick of
oxblood sealing wax; an unlit second candle. CAMERA: slow macro dolly along the inked
coastline — ink lines resolving into rooftops as focus travels; a gentle settle, then
a hand-free wax seal PRESS in the final third (the impression is the station device:
ring, roofline, triangle, dot — matching `public/` seal geometry; supply as reference
frame). PARTICLES: drifting dust motes in the lamp beam; a single curl of candle
smoke; NO magic glows, NO sound-wave effects. MATERIALS: paper tooth, wet-vs-dry ink
sheen, brass patina, wax gloss. GRADE: warm parchment highlights `#EFE6D4`, oxblood
accent only in the wax `#9A2B1E`, nothing cool except a whisper of slate in the
shadows. MOOD: patient, exact, alive — a world being written, not rendered. RULES:
no text on screen, no real-world brands, loop-seamless, motion subtle enough to sit
behind display type at 40% overlay.
