⛔ ZSH TRAP THAT FAKED A CLEAN SWEEP: `S="a b c"; grep -r "$w" $S` does NOT
word-split in zsh — it becomes ONE nonexistent path and grep returns ZERO for
EVERY term. My first POP+WY sweep read as all-zero for this reason, and the
negative control (grepping a known-BUILT wave) is what caught it. Use a bash
array, or `${=S}`. Any lane greping multiple paths from a variable must run a
known-positive control first.
