# ARCH-BRIEF — THE COMPOSED-PROSE MODEL (owner commission, 2026-09-07 ~22:10)

## The commission, in the owner's words
- "comprehensively architect it fully and coherently, and then implement it where appropriate in the sequence."
- The want: prose that grows more specific with every relevant fact — "we have language that states that a town's walls are fully funded by a garrison that is underpaid or not adequately funded; if we also take into account the associated power for the guard (evil, neutral, good), or that any of their members are compromised or not, or that the granaries are lacking, then we have more specific prose for a very specific situation, getting more specific with every relevant combination … this applies to every single thing in a settlement in every aspect of its state."
- The danger the owner names: "we are exponentially increasing prose based solely on this, which would need to be bounded."
- The benefit the owner wants: "nothing will ever be stale, and if something is repeated, it is because that very specific instance is repeated."
- The map: "see if there is already a prebuilt map within the files, in which case you use that … a comprehensive mapping to what text is available to a fact, or combination of facts, and then what is missing or what could be added [for the authoring wave]."

## The chair's method (given to the owner; adopted; every number vetoable)
Author the PIECES, buy the COMBINATIONS with grammar:
- SPINE — one sentence keyed on the block's primary fact.
- MODIFIERS — each keyed on ONE secondary fact, joined to the spine by a connective that carries the RELATION (tension, consequence, contrast), authored once, attachable to any spine of the block.
- TURNS — hand-written only where a combination has a NAME in the world (compromised guards on a funded wall = "the gate is sold"); keyed on the engine's typed EXPLANATIONS (its causal chains), never on raw fact conjunctions.
- BOUNDS: OCCURRENCE (a cell earns hand-written text only above a measured rate over the 200-town sample; rarer cells read through composition); DEPTH (pairs by default; triples only where a frequent pair changes meaning with a third fact; never four); RELATION (only along the engine's causal edges); LICENSING (every piece entitled to its claim by the fields it reads — the checkers enforce; composition cannot manufacture a false sentence); the exemplar BUDGET / DEPTH / PERFECTION CEILING per grain (Part B §16–§16.2) still apply to composed prose.
- THE FLOOR: every block's minimum shape is a spine plus up to two modifiers — specificity rises everywhere with no bespoke cell.
- SALIENCE: a block attaches the two MOST NOTABLE secondary facts for THIS town (departure from the norm; tension with the spine; recent change), scored deterministically so a seed's town reads the same on every visit, and two towns with the same facts foreground differently.
- REFUSED: any language model at render time (THE PROMISE; the finite-semantics law; "AI = clerk, never writer").
- Estimate (PLAUSIBLE, not measured): ~1,800 new sentences (about today's corpus, 2,266 variants) lift a block's distinct readings from hundreds to tens of thousands.


## THE OWNER'S RULE OF ~22:30, CLARIFIED ~22:40 (binding; written before the design run launched)
"You must make sure that everything follows ALL of the work we have done for our voice, because otherwise all that work was for naught." Then: the variations DETERMINED BY STATE and the specific situation of the settlement or its elements are one thing (the semantic variants — today's pool variants with their angles, and every planned spine/modifier/turn); "for every current and planned and designed authored variation I want you to QUADRUPLE that with more vocabulary or rhythm that fits within our voice, to combat staleness" — and "NEVER TRIM" (no authored variant or wording is ever discarded). The roll: "every time something comes up it's an unweighted one-in-four dice roll of which of the four of that specific combination comes to the surface."
- Reading (the chair's, vetoable): two layers. LAYER ONE, MEANING: the state and situation select the semantic variant exactly as today (eligibility by state; the angles). LAYER TWO, WORDING: every semantic variant — every one of today's 2,266 and every planned piece — becomes a FAMILY OF FOUR WORDINGS that say the same licensed thing in different vocabulary and rhythm inside the register; the world's seed rolls an UNWEIGHTED die over the family (one in four; one in N where a family holds more — never trim). Same seed, same face, forever.
- What a wording family must be: the same CLAIM SET (the parent's licence is inherited; the contradiction walker and check-pair must find the four claim-equal), the same slots, four genuinely different surfaces (vocabulary from the register's lexicon; rhythm spanning the length classes so every face sits inside the exemplar bands; a minimum fingerprint DISTANCE between siblings set by measurement so a family is never four synonym-swaps, which read as a tell), and every voice law binding each face.
- Arithmetic: today 2,266 variants → 9,064 wordings (4×); every planned spine/modifier/turn authored as a family of four from birth; the corpus stays LINEAR in pieces and is 4× steeper in wordings; the prose data (640 KB today, lazy chunk) grows to roughly 2.5 MB raw (ESTIMATE; gzip in the lazy chunk; measure the size ratchets).
- The draw: the kernel's existing seeded uniform draw (`drawVariant`, murmur3 fmix32 over seed × pool identity, `% poolLength`) over the flattened list of all wordings of all eligible variants is uniform at the wording level and is the design's default; the design must state whether a two-level roll (variant, then wording) is preferred and why, and must guarantee every face of a family is ELIGIBLE whenever its parent is.
- Rhythm and variation are guaranteed by AUTHORING (the family spans the length classes) and by the GATE (fingerprint walker; spread and presence measures; the sibling-distance arm), never by weighting the draw — the chair's earlier rhythm-aware draw is WITHDRAWN.
- Sequence: the re-voicing happens INSIDE the rewrite pass (each variant rewritten to the voice AND given its four faces in one pass, never a second pass), and every new piece is born as a family.
- THE PROMISE: a pool whose wording count changes RE-ROLLS every existing world's wording for that pool (`% poolLength`) — the declared same-seed TEXT shift (owner-signed), never a fact shift; the migration car proves wording-only on the golden sample.

## The laws the architecture must satisfy (name each where it binds)
