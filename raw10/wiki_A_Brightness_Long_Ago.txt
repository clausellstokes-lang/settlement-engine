'''''A Brightness Long Ago''''' is a [[historical fantasy]] novel<ref name="Caposerre and Jones" /> by Canadian writer [[Guy Gavriel Kay]] published in 2019 by [[Viking Press]].<ref name="Domise" /> It is inspired by the events of 15th-century Italy leading to the [[Italian Wars]],<ref name="Domise" /> specifically the feud between [[Federico da Montefeltro]] and [[Sigismondo Pandolfo Malatesta]].<ref name="Caposerre and Jones" />

It is a pseudo-prequel<ref name="Caposerre and Jones" /> set in the [[fantasy world]] of Batiara decades before the events of the 2016 novel ''[[Children of Earth and Sky]]'',<ref name="Domise" /> but readable as a stand-alone novel.<ref name="Caposerre and Jones" /> In this novel, Batiara serves as Kay's analogue to early [[Italian Renaissance|Renaissance Italy]].<ref name="Caposerre and Jones" />

The [[dust jacket]] cover art of the Canadian and US editions depicted birds and was created by Adrian Hillman.<ref name="BWCA" /> Embedded in the dust jacket were specks of [[gold foil]] whose sparkling reflection of light made it "stand out" on bookstore shelves.<ref name="Echo" />{{rp|32}}

==Plot==
The story is set about 1,000 years after the events of ''[[The Sarantine Mosaic]]'' duology, shortly before the fall of Sarantium.<ref name="Wolfe" /> It begins as a narrative recollection by Guidanio Cerra, an aging councillor nicknamed Danio who in his youth was admitted to a prestigious school despite being a tailor's son.<ref name="Caposerre and Jones" /> This leads him to meet the chief steward of nobleman Count Uberto, and later a woman named Adria Ripoli. The latter is daughter of a duke, and plans to assassinate Uberto, known as The Beast.<ref name="Wolfe" />

From Danio's recollection the plot shifts attention to a healer named Jelena, and subsequently to a political conflict between Folco Cino d’Acorsi, who is the uncle to Adria, and Teobaldo Monticola di Remigio.<ref name="Wolfe" /><ref name="Caposerre and Jones" /> Danio's narrative describes the military and personal clashes between Cino and Monticola.<ref name="Caposerre and Jones" />

Adria and Cino conceive a plan for her to reside in a farmhouse near the city, which eventually brings her to the attention of Uberto, known as "The Beast" for his sexual and murderous proclivities.<ref name="Caposerre and Jones" /> Danio, now employed in the palace of Uberto, may endanger himself or Adria when she is summoned by Uberto to the palace.

==Reception==
In a [[Book review|review]] for ''[[The Globe and Mail]]'', Andray Domise states that ''A Brightness Long Ago'' is a "return to the narrative form" Kay demonstrated in ''[[The Lions of Al-Rassan]]'', with characters that are better developed as a result of greater focus on the story revolving around the principal characters.<ref name="Domise" /> Kay also devotes more of the story to describing the lives of the female characters, instead of focusing on an exceptional female lead as he had in ''Children of Earth and Sky'', in which the secondary women characters were "accessories and playthings".<ref name="Domise" /> Gary K. Wolfe, in a review for ''[[Locus (magazine)|Locus]]'', states that the two principal female characters in the novel "are among [the] most impressive" Kay has written.<ref name="Wolfe" />

Bill Capossere describes Kay's writing as having "supreme elegance and grace", and Tadiana Jones states that Kay has "lyrical and profoundly thoughtful storytelling".<ref name="Caposerre and Jones" /> Niall Alexander, in his review for ''[[Tor.com]]'', states that the fringe characters were created with a "depth and tenderness and intelligence" by which the insignificant are given significance.<ref name="Alexander" />

A review by ''[[Kirkus Reviews]]'' stated that the book exhibits Kay's "usual elements", including numerous characters, extensive [[worldbuilding]] based on historical research, significant events altering the world, and writing "that sometimes gets carried away with itself".<ref name="Kirkus" />

It was one of six finalists for the 2020 [[Aurora Award for Best Novel]].<ref name="Locus" />
