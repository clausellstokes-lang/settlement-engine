# The Edit-Mode train — packet charter (2026-09-19)

**Authority:** `docs/DESIGN_EDIT_MODE_AND_DECREES.md` (§12 governs) · `docs/ARCH_EDIT_MODE_AND_DECREES.md` as amended by design §12 · ODQ §934.36–§934.40. **Standard:** `docs/implementation/PACKET_STANDARD.md` — one behaviour family per packet, the default hard scope budget, a verified base, a change manifest, required symbols resolved against live source, sealed dispatch, pause-and-resume at every gate (§934.33). **Base:** the `master` tip after the 2026-09-18 consist merges (packets are pre-proved there; until then the consist tip stands in). **Gate:** the whole editor is `TIER_GATE.premium` (Cartographer) via a new `editMode` field. **Order of programs:** this train runs after the push and before the simulator (§934.38).

Each packet below is written to READY by an Opus pre-proof lane that verifies every "verified fact" row against the tree (file:line), fills the change manifest and required symbols, and runs the sealed dispatch; the chair validates and cuts. Collision groups serialize. Every packet lands with its instruments green and the chair's compose.
