## §1 · THE BEHAVIOUR THIS WAVE ADDS

Today a settlement's patron seat changes hands and **nothing records that it happened, or
why**. Executed at this base: `advanceReligionStates`
(`src/domain/worldPulse/religiousContest.js`) compares `state.patronRef !== prevPatron` and
mints a `religious_conversion_fracture` outcome whose `cause` is a two-token
`occupation | contest` literal — the only surviving trace of a fall, and it is discarded at
the end of the tick. `worldState.religionStates[cid]` carries no fall history; a grep for
`patronFalls` over `src/` and `tests/` returns **zero hits** outside the pre-authored
dossier prose corpus.

WF-1a makes the fall **typed and persisted**: when `faithUnseatingEnabled === true`, every
patron displacement appends one `{ ref, cause, atTick }` record to a capped
`religionStates[cid].patronFalls` ring, `cause` drawn from a closed vocabulary that this
packet proves reachable arm by arm.

## §2 · THE CURE

### 2.1 The closed cause vocabulary is FOUR tokens, not five

The volume's `PATRON_FALL_CAUSES` is `{displaced, discredited, abandoned, imposed,
suppressed}`. ⛔ **`abandoned` is CUT, and the cut is a measurement.**

`abandoned` is chartered as "the sink crossing (share lost to `none`, nobody took the
seat)". Executed against `applyUnaffiliatedSink` (`religionState.js`): the function calls
`renormShares(state.deities, 100 - noneInt)` and recomputes standings. **It never touches
`state.patronRef`, never suppresses an entry, and `'none'` is a scalar accumulator that the
module's own header declares "NEVER enters state.deities — it can never rank, contest, or
hold the seat."** It is also invoked AFTER the patron seat is decided in the fold. A
settlement can secularize to the `SINK_MAX` ceiling of 45 with the patron still seated.
`abandoned` therefore has **zero producers at this base**, and the estate's standing law —
every producer lands in the same commit as its vocabulary member (GR-5A; INT-3B) — forbids
minting it. It is refused with this measurement, not deferred silently.

The landed vocabulary is:

| token | fire site (all four MEASURED reachable) |
|---|---|
| `imposed` | the garrison flip (`occupations[cid].occupierId` truthy at the patron-change site) **or** the DM `SET_PRIMARY_DEITY` re-assign inside `ensureReligionState` |
| `suppressed` | the seat evicted by force — `attemptEntry` returns `{ evicted }` naming the outgoing patron |
| `discredited` | `resolvePatronContest` returned `true` and owned the seat this tick (the schism / legitimacy-floor road) |
| `displaced` | everything else — the organic `selectPatron` share flip past `PATRON_FLIP_MARGIN` × `PATRON_FLIP_TICKS` |

**Precedence is total and is settled here, not by the implementer:** `imposed` >
`suppressed` > `discredited` > `displaced`. First match wins; the classifier returns exactly
one token and never null for a real transition.

### 2.2 Every input the classifier needs is ALREADY in the fold's scope

This is the finding that makes the wave cheap, and each half is executed:

1. **`resolvePatronContest` already returns the boolean.** The fold reads it today —
   `if (!contestRng || !resolvePatronContest(state, contestRng)) selectPatron(state);` — so
   `discredited` versus `displaced` is derivable with **no signature change** to either
   function. Capture the boolean into a local instead of discarding it inside the condition.
2. **`attemptEntry` already returns `{ entered, path, evicted }` and the fold DISCARDS it.**
   Executed: the call site is a bare expression statement,
   `attemptEntry(state, deity, strength, { force: Boolean(occupied) });`. Assigning the
   result and testing `evicted === priorPatron` yields `suppressed` for free.
3. ⛔ **`prevPatron` IS BLIND TO THE DM FLIP, AND THIS IS A REFUTATION OF THE CHARTER'S
   `imposed` ARM.** Executed order in the fold:
   `const state = ensureReligionState(prior[cid], settlement, tier);` and only THEN
   `const prevPatron = state.patronRef;`. `ensureReligionState`'s DM RE-ASSIGN branch has
   **already** written `s.patronRef = ref` by that point, so a `SET_PRIMARY_DEITY` flip
   leaves `state.patronRef === prevPatron` and fires nothing. The cure is one line and it is
   mandatory: capture `const priorPatron = prior[cid]?.patronRef ?? null;` **before**
   `ensureReligionState` and classify against that, not against `prevPatron`.
   ⚠ `prevPatron` is left alone — it is the existing conversion-outcome block's own input
   and moving it would shift that receipt.

## §4 · DEITY-DOCTRINE AND SPINE COMPLIANCE (binding, per member)

- **Faith is cultural, never theological.** Every one of the four causes names a
  BELIEVER-SIDE or POLITICAL act — a garrison, an eviction, a contest, a share flip. No
  token, receipt or field asserts anything about a god. `imposed` records what the garrison
  did, never what the god failed to do.
- **No premade deity pool.** WF-1a mints no deity, reads no deity catalogue, and adds no
  entry to any allow-list. Fixtures acquire deities **only** through the doctrine path —
  the `SET_PRIMARY_DEITY` / `IMPOSE_CULT` mutation verbs — never by poking config.
- **Typed buckets (FINITE-SEMANTICS).** `cause` is a closed four-token frozen vocabulary.
  `atTick` is an integer. `ref` is an existing deity ref. There is no free text anywhere in
  the record.
- **AI is clerk, never writer.** No AI surface reads or writes `patronFalls`; the two
  edge-shared bundles this wave regenerates are downstream artefacts of `simulationRules.js`
  and carry no faith payload.
- **Spine req 13 (alignment engagement):** **alignment-EMPTY, with reason.** A fall is
  believer bookkeeping over shares and legitimacy; no cause reads a deity's authored axes.
  Recorded, not omitted.
- **Spine req 14 (the DM edit story):** `patronFalls` is DM-visible history —
  **RECORDED: engine-only.** History is not editable, and the DM's lever is the EXISTING
  `SET_PRIMARY_DEITY` verb, which now CREATES an `imposed` fall through the same classifier.
  The verb story is the existing verb. Vetoable at the chair under the spine's req-14 escape.
