   it true, the question rides `worldState.proposals`; silence and expiry mean

5. The current owner ledger's universal authorization removes permission as a
   blocker but explicitly does not waive READY authority, scope, STOPs, gates,


### 5.3 Stable identity and collision refusal

`successionQuestionKey(question, openedTick)` is `JSON.stringify` over this
fixed-order tuple and no other shape:

```text

 cause, kind, answer, severity01, pressure01]
```

the proposal row's `tick` at mint. It remains the authoritative original tick
