### Five findings that change the design before a line is written

1. **§16's load-bearing sentence is not true of the tree.** §16 says "the decision-fork registry; **forty-two forks today, each with a typed outcome vocabulary**." Measured: **one** of the 42 rows carries an `actionVocabulary` (HBF-17 → `STRATEGY_MOVES`). Forty-one carry `actionVocabulary: null`, and the file's own header says so: *"`arity` IS DECLARED ONLY WHERE IT WAS MEASURED"* and *"`circumstanceClasses` IS EMPTY ON EVERY ROW TODAY."* `pin-fork` over "the fork's own vocabulary" has exactly one fork to work on today; every other pin is a measurement owed first.
2. **Not one of the owner's named acts is a registered fork.** The registry holds no siege fork, no coup fork, no peace-accept fork and no envoy-reception fork. `resolveSiegeVerdict` (`warSiegeVerdict.js:340`) and `resolveCoupVerdict` (`rulingPowerCoup.js:210,222`) are **unregistered draws**; "sue for peace" is already a **built realm verb**, not a fork at all.
3. **Three DM-act surfaces already exist and §16/§17 do not name them.** `REALM_MANIFEST` (16 verbs with dials, world-state `predicate`s and teaching veto prose — i.e. §18's precondition machinery, already built), `affordanceManifest.js` (41 typed settlement events — i.e. §16's "event catalogue"), and `PARTY_IMPACT_KINDS` (12 kinds with magnitudes — i.e. §16's `cause: 'party'`). A new `pin-fork` op that does not route through `authorityFor` (`changeAuthorityPolicy.js:381`) and `ACTOR_INITIATED_MAJOR_TYPES` (`actorMajorApproval.js:57`) is a **second write path** into the approval queue.
4. **The draw surface the DM can reach is 20% registered.** 34 of 122 idiom hits sit in a registered symbol. The other 82, across 51 symbols, are exactly where "let this specific thing happen" lives — road hazards, ransoms, the patron contest, the festival, the season, the exposure, the wandering stressor.
5. **`generateCrossSettlementConflictsDeterministic`** (`src/generators/crossSettlementConflicts.js:289`) is a **pinned twin of a drawing generator that already exists**. §14's `rederive(record, config′, layer)` shape is not novel here; it has a working precedent to copy.
