**One consequence by design, stated first.** `normalizeSettlement` takes every record without a `schemaVersion` through the migration chain — and a freshly generated record has none. So from step 3 on, every RETURNED generated record is v2: the generator still builds the grouping and the shim's two fields inside the pipeline (`power/factionGrouping.js`, `people/legacyShim.js`: the landing's), and the v1 → v2 step removes them at the boundary. That is the runner's declared design (`settlement.schema.js`: "`normalizeSettlement()` runs at boundaries … to produce the canonical shape"), it is what makes the register's `GENERATED_KEYS = 40` true at this tip, and it is why the grouping's readers outside the edit layer see nothing at this tip (§4, §5).

**The ops round-trip proofs** (`tests/domain/people5bEditOps.test.js`, 9/9): P1 create (a DM card: `memberships: []`, `institution: null`; the id seed reads the powers); P2 reassign through the op (pure), the intent (admits `memberships` and a house, refuses seven malformed or retired targets) and the writer (refuses a power the record lacks, applies one it has); P3 a power rename moves every `memberships[].power` and a minted face's `institution.key`, a house rename moves a house face's `institution.key` (its title source) and nothing of a power-kind face, and on a GENERATED v2 town every holder of the renamed power follows it; P4 an appointment appends its power once, a death impairs every power the card stands in, the successors overlap on the powers.
