# The auxiliary-contribution directive (owner, 2026-08-06, in-chat)

**Owner's intent (verbatim spine):** an allied, vassalized, or occupied
settlement may elect to send reinforcements and supplies (in the same
vein as to their own army) to an ally's, overlord's, or occupier's army
(the occupied case almost entirely coerced), at the cost of reinforcing
their own — treaty-linked, creating "cost memory / charity / debt"
(owner: "however you want to spin it, I'm just naming things").
Alternatively they contribute INSTEAD of fielding their own army: they
have joined the war, but at minimum involvement — and impact, good and
bad, plus contributions are minimum to them as well. All of it enters
STRATEGY (how much in vs out; relationships among all warring/hostile
parties; own-troops-for-own-army vs supporting others; DISTANCE applies
throughout). Allies form a mutually beneficial support network whose
efficiency scales toward the LAWFUL side, depending on the efficiency of
the nearest allied settlement.

**Chair refinements (accepted 2026-08-06, each vetoable):**
1. CONTRIBUTION CREDIT — the favor ledger: conserved, bilateral, typed
   (troops-lent / supplies-delivered, per war, per pair), written AT
   ARRIVAL never at pledge (the arrival law); feeds treaty compliance
   grading, CALL-IN RIGHTS (an indebted refusal is breach-grade), and
   the Herald.
2. THE WAR-STANCE LADDER (closed): neutral → materiel → auxiliary →
   belligerent → principal. Everything scales by rung both directions:
   exhaustion, spoils share, AND casus exposure (materiel IS joining —
   the enemy gains a proportionally WEAKER grievance).
3. INTERDICTION — auxiliary supply travels real wayfare roads ⇒
   legitimate targets (the encounter table; army×caravan rows or one new
   row); distance = exposure = physics.
4. THE LAWFUL RELAY — allied logistics stage through the NEAREST allied
   settlement (the entrepôt machinery); per-hop efficiency reads the
   participants' law bands: lawful = standing compacts (low loss, fast);
   chaotic = ad-hoc + SKIM (diverted fraction, visible as ledger
   shortfall, feeding grievance/reputation).
5. STRATEGY ENTRY — the settlement strategist's move vocabulary (being
   minted by HB-1 — ⚠ COORDINATE: the auxiliary moves
   send-reinforcements-to / send-supplies-to / recall-support must join
   that mint or amend it at this directive's fold) weighted by
   relationship, treaty obligation, distance feasibility, own threat,
   favor-ledger balance, law band, and CONTRIBUTION HABIT (the arsenal-
   state personality via the habit system).
6. THE COERCION GRADIENT + ITS INVERSE — occupied requisition exists
   (built); ELECTIVE OVERSHOOT by vassals/occupied = loyalty signaling →
   standing toward better terms/release/conveyance value (sovereignty
   market); chronic under-delivery feeds resistance narrative.
7. CALL-INS RIDE THE ERRAND SPINE — typed envoy errand; the answer is a
   decision fork, habit-ready, graded at war close (did the supported
   side win; was credit honored).
8. PRICED SHIELDING — terms weight at the peace table scales with
   contribution credit: minimal involvement buys safety at the cost of
   irrelevance. Self-balancing.
9. THE DOCTRINE RELIABILITY PAGE — contribution closes feed
   believed-doctrine (who is an arsenal, who skims, who answers);
   spies steal ally-reliability intelligence (ES tap product).

**EXTENSION (owner, 2026-08-06, same exchange): COMPOSITE ARMIES + THE
PEOPLE LEDGER.** (a) Armies become ORIGIN-TAGGED UNIT-BLOCK ROSTERS

dissolution at war's end returns blocks home to RECONTRIBUTE to
population and defense (conscription-debit return machinery exists).
(c) DEFECTION vs OCCUPATION of a contributing home are DISTINCT: the
occupied did not choose — their blocks stay formally allied but WATCHED
(ES taint); the fork per block GATES ON NEWS ARRIVAL (belief at road
speed — columns may fight for coalitions their homes already left):
stay | return | defect-for-family-fear (retaliation stays INSTITUTIONAL
leverage — never engine-resolved fates) | return-to-resist (feeds the
existing occupation-resistance quantity). Chair refinements: BLOCK
COHESION IS LAW-BAND MODULATED (lawful blocks decide as one; chaotic
blocks FRAGMENT into mixed outcomes) · a block fighting on while home
lies occupied still EARNS ITS HOME contribution credit toward the peace
table. (d) CULTURAL INTERMINGLING: composite service accumulates a
BLEND record (faiths/traditions/ideas, deepening with campaign length);
dissolution sends veterans home with typed INTRODUCTION ATTEMPTS rolled
against home receptivity via the existing tradition-claim machinery —
exposure is an event, adoption is a contest, most attempts fail.

(f) REALIZED STANCE OUTRANKS DECLARED (owner, 2026-08-06, the drift
edge case): the war-stance rung is DERIVED from the contribution ledger
— realized share of the composite army, time-integrated — never from a

casus exposure, terms weight, spoils, and exhaustion all re-derive as
the share drifts (priced shielding becomes un-gameable); the CROSSING

casus reads its BELIEF of the share, lagging at news speed (the update

this force) becomes an ES tap product — spies count banners.
(g) CULTURAL SCALING (owner, same exchange): blend absorbed scales with
SERVICE DURATION x (1 − own share); blend imparted scales with own
share — the asymmetry yields real assimilation (the small block in a
big foreign army comes home most changed; the dominant block spreads
most, changes least; long wars melt, short wars introduce), and drift
(f) composes: a mid-war share shift moves the army's blend with it.

(h) COMRADESHIP (owner, 2026-08-06: "those that fight together are
also more ingratiated to each other... between the settlements"):
shared composite service accrues RELATIONAL WARMTH between contributing
settlements, riding the SAME roster integrals as (g) — scaled by shared
service duration x shared hardship (intensity, not victory; brothers of
a lost siege still bond) — written into the EXISTING relationship
machinery, so it immediately feeds caravan density (the WY law),
alliance odds, call-in answer weights, and relay efficiency. Chair
riders: COMRADES KNOW EACH OTHER — former co-belligerents hold
unusually ACCURATE believed-doctrine of each other (free mutual
intelligence from shared service; former comrades turned rivals
anticipate like no strangers — old brotherhoods make the sharpest
wars) · THE CHANNEL IS SIGNED AND BETRAYAL PRICED BY INTIMACY — skim,
token contribution while comrades bled (measurable via (f)'s realized
blood-share), and mid-war defection chill MORE between comrades, in
proportion to the bond broken: the deepest feuds are born between old
friends, never old enemies.
