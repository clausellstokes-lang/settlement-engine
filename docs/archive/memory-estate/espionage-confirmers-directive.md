## The owner's design (verbatim intent, binding)

NPCs/envoys/traveling merchants take COVERT CONFIRMATION MISSIONS: travel
covertly to a target settlement to gather/confirm information for accuracy.
MAGIC-ENABLED maps: instant magical transmission home once confirmed (travel
in is still physical); NON-MAGIC: must physically return. Covert = less
likely captured BUT less credible on any simultaneous diplomatic/trade
purpose. Best against high-resentment targets or war enemies/coalitions —
but NOT war-only: serves trade, treaty, and all statecraft. Mission = an NPC
goal with benefits on success; on capture: reputational damage, increased
resentment, LONGER hostage terms, HIGHER ransom. Risk/strategy/alignment all
gate it. HOME settlement's criminal power + criminal activity scale covert
competence; targets with high criminal power/activity + LOW internal
security are WORSE at catching spies. Capture risk ONLY at route segments
carrying enemy military and at hostile settlements/chains. While away, the
NPC has NO influence on home politics. Influence scales INVERSELY with spy
skill (power ranking × faction influence — the notable are noteworthy).
COMPOSITE MISSIONS: a diplomatic leg may extend covertly from its
destination (chained); cap 2-3 legs per journey (any diplomatic/spy mix)
before returning home. THE MISSING-NPC TELL: another settlement confirming
an NPC is absent is DAMNING — hostage or spy, either way ears rise and
in-flight missions are impacted. ALIGNMENT DOCTRINE: good/evil AND
lawful/chaotic shape who is targeted (friend vs foe), methods, frequency,
and leniency/strictness of employment.

## Owner additions (2026-08-04, later the same night — same authority as above)

A. SPY-BEFORE-DECISION ("i leave that to you"): settlements MAY dispatch a
   confirmation mission before a hasty decision and DEFER (receipted) — but
   MUST NOT always wait ("should the situation demand it"). Chair design:
   deliberation gated on stale/low-confidence deciding legs × doctrine
   patience; URGENCY bands (siege/overflow/war strain) force deciding on
   stale pictures; patience has a TIMEOUT band (spy lost ⇒ decide anyway,
   worse off than when the wait began).
B. THE CONFIDENCE GRADIENT: intel accrues along the route — per-waypoint
   second-hand reads (the HOST's beliefs about the target, capped by host
   freshness + hop distance) + incidental intel about each host; FIRST-HAND
   maximum confidence only at the target on arrival. Consequences: the
   information-rich-vs-safe ROUTING tension (target's neighbors know most,
   watch most); magic worlds bank partials as gathered (capture keeps the
   gradient), mundane capture loses everything collected.
C. INFILTRATION MODIFIERS: most STRESSORS on the target discount detection
   (a stressed town is porous); SMUGGLER TUNNELS (verify substrate; else
   derive from criminal activity) ease entry; a COMPROMISED NPC in the
   target ("compromised" = CORRUPTED per owner vocabulary — the
   corruptionWeb) reachable from the home settlement = an INSIDE ASSET
   (easier entry, richer access). Symmetric by construction: a compromised
   NPC in YOUR settlement is how enemies get into you — the corruption web
   IS espionage infrastructure, coupling INT and IN.

D. THE STANDOFF OPTION: when the target's gate is too risky (hostile target
   OR a similarly-hostile surrounding cluster), the spy MAY hold at a
   satellite/surrounding settlement and improve confidence WITHOUT terminal
   confirmation (the gradient's plateau — capped, safe). The reach-vs-
   standoff choice is a RISK ASSESSMENT reading: approach topology (the
   cluster, not just the gate), the HOME settlement's state (desperation
   lowers risk-aversion — couples with the deliberation urgency), and the
   NPC's TEMPERAMENT AND FLAWS — temperament shapes the choice; flaws
   DISTORT the assessment itself (pride over-reaches, cravenness
   under-reaches), deterministic keyed on the NPC, receipted in character.
   Census check: what temperament/flaw fields NPC records carry today.
E. PER-STOP TRANSMISSION (magic worlds): a covert spy shares home at EVERY
   settlement stop — streaming intelligence; deferred decisions may resolve
   EARLY when streamed confidence crosses threshold; urgency decisions use
   partials. Mundane worlds: nothing arrives until the return — the
   patience-timeout band carries the drama.

F. THE DOUBLE AGENT: a dispatched spy who is themselves COMPROMISED
   (corrupted) toward an ENEMY settlement LEAKS to that enemy — the
   mission's existence, itinerary, and gathered intel — whether or not that
   enemy is the target. Consequences (chair-developed, accepted): the leak
   is META-INTELLIGENCE (the enemy learns what the court seeks = its
   intentions); the SILENT SUCCESS shape (mission works, leak invisible;
   discovery only via consequence patterns or the web's exposure machinery;
   Herald retroactive on exposure); SELECTION/VETTING becomes a real
   decision — the ideal-operative profile (low notoriety, criminal-capable)
   IS the most corruptible profile, and vetting quality derives from home
   internal security + own-web health (internal rot ⇒ external blindness);
   leak timing mirrors the report channel's world rules (magic streams to
   two addresses; mundane travels by enemy reach). SCOPE RULING
   (chair, vetoable): LEAK-ONLY this program — false reporting home is
   deliberately excluded (belongs to info-statecraft disinfo if ever);
   the confirmation product stays honest.

G. THE ROOTED SPY ("gather or govern"): spies best not overstay; they MAY
   dwell rooted to keep gathering/confirming (re-sample per interval; live
   feed in magic worlds) at TWO accumulating costs — capture odds climb by
   dwell band (place-dependent; vigilance + local suspicion accrete) and
   prolonged absence feeds the PROMOTION RISK REGISTER (ladder standing
   decays; rivals advance). MISSION SUCCESS IS GRADED BY INFORMATION
   QUALITY (achieved confidence vs the goal's demand) and successful
   missions ADVANCE careers — the career is espionage's currency on BOTH
   sides (cost = absence; reward = promotion credit). Self-limits
   embedding; temperament decides the trade in character.
H. THE TAP-ORDER RULING (owner asked the chair to figure the order; ruled,
   vetoable): a spy taps BY ACCESS DEPTH and can NEVER report purer than
   their access. Standoff/shallow cover ⇒ the OUTBOUND PERFORMANCE (told
   version, deliberate mutations included). Embedded covert ⇒ the host's
   INTERNAL BELIEFS (post-organic-drift, pre-performance — nobody sees
   past the host's own errors; the epistemic constitution holds). With an
   INSIDE ASSET ⇒ both layers + THE DELTA (the lie itself detected — the
   highest-value product; counter-disinfo by access depth). Reports carry
   the TAP LEVEL as fidelity metadata, receipted in house voice ("as told
   in X's markets" / "as X itself believes" / "X speaks one thing and
   believes another"); home discounts by tap level × host trust.
   CONSEQUENCE: the standoff option becomes a THREE-way trade — risk vs
   confidence vs INTEGRITY (standing off in a hostile-to-home settlement
   is physically safe and informationally poisoned).

I. COVERT MANIPULATION AT LOCATION (owner, 2026-08-05, post-cohesion —
   folds as an AMENDMENT at the integration pass with a cohesion re-check):
   covert NPCs may ATTEMPT information manipulation at any location they
   occupy, for any reason, while gathering. Attempts may FAIL; EVERY
   attempt raises accumulated exposure/capture odds, feeding the
   reach/standoff/dwell risk strategy. SCOPE COMPATIBILITY (chair): this
   does NOT reopen the leak-only ruling — the spy still reports honestly
   HOME; manipulation attacks the HOST's information environment (the
   info-statecraft LIE/plant machinery given an embodied local agent).
   COMPOSITION (accepted): success scales covert competence vs host
   vigilance; the severity-durability law prices big plants (harder to
   seed, contradict more corroborating streams — big lies cost more, now
   with a face); TAP-ORDER depth governs WHERE a plant can land (shallow =
   market talk; embedded = internal belief; inside asset = deepest); the
   double agent composes (planting for the true master). PIN: attempts are
   GRADED AND RECEIPTED (attempted/seeded/took-root/exposed) so the
   mole-hunt and the Herald have truth to tell. "Unless deliberately
   manipulated" (rumor-durability §1) now has its AGENT.

4. NOTORIETY (power rank × faction influence): worse at hiding AND worth

   low-notoriety operatives; criminal capacity sets their quality.
5. Merchants = the BEST cover (wandering is expected); trade legs couple
   espionage to the route graph.
6. Exposure cost scales INVERSELY with target hostility; caught spying on a
   FRIEND = betrayal event (grievance mint + trust collapse + minor casus).
7. Absence = faction council weight drops while away; rivals can exploit
   the window (the two-books texture).
8. Leg risk mildly SUPERLINEAR so 2-3 is a choice; capture only at the
   owner's named exposure points (reuses interception route checks).
9. Herald: departures/confirmations dm-only covert (fail-closed upstream);

10. THE MISSING-NPC TELL implemented as belief-derived VIGILANCE: a court
    believing "one of theirs is missing" gains a capture-odds modifier on
