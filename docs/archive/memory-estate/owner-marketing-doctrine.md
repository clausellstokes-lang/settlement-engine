# Experience-first marketing doctrine (owner, 2026-07-21, in-chat)

The owner ratified this over the manager's positioning-first pushback, with the
argument that won: **distinction doesn't make people try a product — curiosity,
resonance, and immersion do; distinction is what they discover after.** The
audience is DMs "simply looking for the thing," not tool-evaluators comparing
categories. Marketing surfaces must "set the expectation of their EXPERIENCE,
not their understanding at first look."

**Rules:**
- Hero/landing copy leads with the felt experience: "the world breathes,"
  "discover your own world," immersion language. Mechanism claims ("it
  simulates," determinism, receipts) are the SECOND beat — subheaders, support
  lines, the About page. "Nothing wrong with advertising that it simulates,
  but that is second to the experience itself."
- Corollary ruling, same session: the buy-dossier caption ("One-time, no
  account needed.") moved to hover because visible transactional microcopy
  "breaks immersion a bit" — **immersion outranks conversion-copy visibility**
  on product surfaces. Manager's mobile-conversion objection was heard and
  overruled; do not re-litigate.
- The 2026-07-21 home-page audit found the doctrine ALREADY holds nearly
  everywhere ("You have just watched this town grow," "The world holds
  together. Yours can too.") — the Create hero ("Most generators roll on a
  table. This one simulates.") was the one mechanism-first outlier, and the
  owner's instinct targeted exactly it.

**Sharpened (owner, 2026-08-05, before the r/rpg post):** "the things that
YOU find great are not what the community finds great. Determinism is great
architecturally; they are looking for experience." The correction targets the
assistant's own instinct — architecture-aesthetics (determinism, corpus
discipline, conservation, receipts) are the builder's loves, not the reader's.
Every outward claim translates to the reader's side of the fact before it
ships: determinism → "the war you watched start three sessions ago is still
the same war"; regen-surviving edits → "what you write is canon; the sim
builds around you, never over you"; hand-authored corpus → "the news reads
like someone who lives there wrote it"; rumor physics → "news arrives late,
garbled, and more garbled the farther it traveled." If a sentence's subject
is the SYSTEM rather than the table experience, rewrite it.

**THE OWNER'S VOICE (calibrated 2026-08-05 from four sample rewrites for
the r/rpg post — this is the register for outward copy in his name):**
thesis-first declaratives; lists of abstract FORCES ("reason, belief,
history, grievances") over scene-painting; triadic builds with an
ellipsis pause before the capstone ("responsibly, dramatically, and most
importantly... coherently"); metaphor stacks ("a machine, a menu, a
buffet"); first-person builder pride ("I built"); short punch closers
("never mandated"). Assistant-instinct cinematic moments are EVIDENCE
under his credo lines, never the spine. Canonical samples live in the
2026-08-05 session; blend = his claim, one concrete table moment as
proof, zero redundancy.

**⛔ THE SAME-SEED CLAIM (resolved 2026-08-05, two rounds):** the owner
pushed back on the first catch: PRNG forks everywhere were INTENTIONAL
for variance-within-a-seed, and byte-for-byte replay ORIGINATED as a
bug-reproduction affordance, not as marketing. Both owner points true
AND the engine remains a pure function of (seed + full interaction
history) — the forks buy subsystem stream INDEPENDENCE (rich divergence,
no lockstep), reroll draws give in-seed variance ON DEMAND, and goldens/
replays (executed byte-identical this very session) prove identical
inputs replay identically. The marketing-true renderings: "nothing is
scripted — the likely thing is only likely, never promised" · "even the
same world, played twice, will not tell the same story" (true: no two
click streams are ever identical) · "don't like a stretch of history?
reroll it — the world offers another version of itself." NEVER ship the
literal "same seed + same clicks → different outcomes" (the goldens
falsify it); never lead copy with determinism either — it is plumbing,
not the experience. THE PROMISE itself stands un-revised.

**LAUNCH STRATEGY (owner, 2026-08-05, final for now):** NO pre-launch
teaser post — "i'm determined to wait until we have the whole thing
complete. then i'll drop it into the world to advertise and let it speak
for itself." The finished r/rpg post drafts (v2 final, em-dash-free,
working-demo line, origin story: the improv/Mercer confession + the
wizard's-tower-in-a-thorpe line + the burned-mill consequence + "a
campaign SETTING engine, not a campaign engine") live in the 2026-08-05
session transcript — they are the LAUNCH post's skeleton. The r/rpg
research (run wf_ed421b12-fed) stands: Rule 7 active-account requirement
(90% non-promo, 6 months — the waiting period is the compliance window),
Rule 9 (no AI mention in-body from a low-karma account), winners ship a
clickable thing + GIF + specific asks + visible comment shipping.
"Complete" = the written endgame tail (soak → tuning → ONE REGEN → walk),
not a feeling. Harvest launch GIFs during testing as they occur.

**Why:** the owner's funnel model — top-of-funnel serves the discoverer
persona, not the evaluator; the evaluator finds the About page on their own.

**How to apply:** any new marketing/landing/onboarding copy leads
experiential, in the house voice (no em-dashes, no bangs unless owner banks an
exception); simulation/differentiation claims live in second beats. When
copy-judgment conflicts arise between distinction and immersion, immersion
wins. See [[the-promise-ratified]] and [[finite-semantics-law]] for the
product laws the experience copy must still never contradict.
