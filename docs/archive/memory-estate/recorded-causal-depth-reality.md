The emergentArcSoak instrument (tests/simulation/emergentArcSoak.test.js, built
cycle-2, on claude/fix-instruments 0a49deb2) drove full_simulation 15 years with
provenanceLedgerEnabled and measured the RECORDED cause-DAG via buildRecordedEdges:
3144 edges · 118 cross-system · 64 cross-tick delayed consequences · 93
multi-system cause-cones · 4 triple-system · 8 subsystem families — BUT recorded
link-depth ≥2 = **ZERO**.

**The reality (corrects a repeated overstatement):** the engine records ONE-HOP-
TO-ROOT attribution — each recorded edge names its ULTIMATE root, intermediate
waves flattened onto that root, hop-generation marked in the receipt id. The ONLY
minter of a true outcome→outcome recorded edge is roads V-24d (release→capture),
which is outside full_simulation AND doesn't survive the ≤18-slot durable
impactDigest. So there is NO walkable multi-hop recorded chain in a shipped world.

- COUPLING depth is real (~9 subsystems interact deep — famine→generosity→
  gratitude→legitimacy→ladder→coup genuinely propagates through the physics).
- RECORDED provenance is one-hop-to-root, so the cause-walk retrieves root +
  wave markers, NOT an A→B→C→D hop chain.
- Distinction: broad multi-system CONES (many effects → shared root) exist and
  are rich; deep recorded CHAINS do not.

**Do NOT say** "causal chains measured/traced to 8-9 recorded links" — say
"coupling is ~9 deep; recorded attribution is one-hop-to-root; cross-tick
delayed consequences are recorded (64 over 15 years)."

**Enhancement path** (if literal hop-by-hop cause-walk is wanted): have more
kernels mint outcome→outcome `causedBy` edges (the deepChains metric is wired to
light up); ties to [[round3-f3-hazards-and-flags]] idx19 contests-openedTick and
the broader provenance-threading. Related: [[whole-sim-empirical-assessment]].
