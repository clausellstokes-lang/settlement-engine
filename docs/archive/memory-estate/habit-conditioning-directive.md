# The habit-conditioning directive (owner, 2026-08-05, in-chat)

**Owner's words (intent, binding when architected):** "weighted rolls
[should] operate a bit in memory. The things that were most successful
for a circumstance or situation should likely be the thing that is most
likely to be chosen more often. That is called habit, conditioning, and
learning. The things that often failed in the situation should lose
relative weight. They should go for anything that regard[s]
decision-making for the actors. But countered to this these are things
that also should be anticipated by enemies and allies in their own
strategy as a counterforce. If settlement [A] knows that settlement B
genuinely does action Z in a circumstance, this adds into the war of
information and espionage on how settlement would react and whether
settlement B knows settlement A's plans (which changes the situation and
circumstance) and vice versa."

**THE RESOLUTION OF THE NO-LEARNING BOUNDARY (chair concession,
recorded):** the prior law said learning breaks replay + explainability.
That is TRUE ONLY of weight-FUNCTION mutation. The owner's shape is
LEARNING-AS-STATE: frozen, owner-signed formulas READ a new persisted
input — habitStrength(actor, circumstanceClass, action) — derived
deterministically from the world's own graded outcomes. Replay-exact
(same history ⇒ same habits, THE PROMISE intact), MORE explainable (habit
becomes a NAMED reason family: "as it has thrice before when granaries
ran thin"), and dossier-legible (the doctrine sheet IS an intelligence
product).

**Chair refinements (accepted in the same exchange, each vetoable):**
1. FROZEN CURVES, LEARNED INPUTS — learning rate / decay half-life /
   caps are owner-signed tuning constants; habits ride the EXISTING
   decay law (decayTowardNeutral family — never a second decay law).
2. TILT, NEVER LOCK — habit strength capped and floored: the likely
   thing stays only likely; no option driven to zero (dead-arm law).
3. TYPED CIRCUMSTANCE CLASSES — a closed vocabulary of situation
   classes (finite semantics), so every habit is nameable in prose.
4. CREDIT AT CLOSE — outcomes grade the opening action when the
   circumstance CLOSES (the MISSION_GRADES precedent); no sophisticated
   attribution.
5. ONE LEVEL OF ANTICIPATION, BELIEF-ROUTED — A's counter-strategy reads
   A's BELIEF about B's habits (never B's truth); bounded at level one,
   one typed factor, no regress. Believed-doctrine = a new belief-axis
   family riding SP-B's machinery; spies steal doctrine sheets through
   the ES tap ladder; PLANTED doctrine (disinformation about one's own
   habits) rides the existing plant machinery.
6. THE SELF-BALANCING LOOP (the owner's counterforce, named): habits
   that become KNOWN become EXPLOITABLE — anticipation punishes
   over-conditioning, applying selection pressure against variance
   collapse; a watched actor's deviate-from-pattern candidate gains
   weight with counterintelligence awareness. The information war IS the
   variance regulator.
7. DARK-BUILDABLE — its own flag family, CQ5 trios, four-fence dormancy;
   dark worlds byte-identical.

**SECOND-ROUND REFINEMENTS (chair, 2026-08-06, under the owner's
"refine it where you think necessary" — enter at the amend round, each
vetoable):**
8. INSTITUTIONAL BY DEFAULT — habits attach to the DECIDING IDENTITY:
   the settlement-institution for settlement decisions, with a
   SEAT-CHANGE DECAY SHOCK (a new ruler partially resets doctrine — "the
   new seat has yet to learn the old lessons"); per-NPC habits ONLY
   where per-NPC decision machinery already exists (roads courage, spy
   choices) — prevents ledger explosion at NPC grain.
9. SMALL CLOSED CIRCUMSTANCE VOCABULARY — initial set ≤ 12 classes;
   growth by owner-signed addition only.
10. COLD-START = DARK-STATE = NEUTRAL — an empty habit ledger yields
   exactly the pre-habit weights; a fresh world's early years are
   habit-free by construction; pinned as the neutral-identity fence.
11. NO CROSS-CLASS BLEED in v1 — classes are independent; bleed is a
   recorded future-widening (owner question), never a tuning knob.
12. SEVERITY-BANDED REINFORCEMENT — grades come ONLY from existing
   outcome vocabularies; mixed/ruinous outcomes teach less (the
   bandFamilies severity ladder scales the update); no new judgment
   machinery.
13. OBSERVED-DOCTRINE ACCRUAL — believed-doctrine is never free: it
   accrues slowly from PUBLICLY OBSERVED actions (the existing
   news/belief roads, hop-worn), and the ES tap product JUMPS it toward
   truth-accuracy — so the counterforce is emergent even before
   espionage lights, and spying stays the sharp edge.
14. OWN-SHEET TRUTH — an actor's own doctrine sheet is its own truth
   (the dossier shows the DM; the settlement "knows itself").

**SCOPE EMPHASIS (owner, 2026-08-06, verbatim intent):** "Learning
shouldn't be limited just to war it should be for any decision fork in
the program per actor per circumstance per action and on and on." —
TOTAL SCOPE IS LAW: every decision fork, both grains (institutional +
per-NPC where machinery exists), enforced by the chooser-totality STOP
law + a coverage walker so no future fork escapes.
15. THE GRADED-CLOSE PRECONDITION — a fork joins the learning system
   exactly when its circumstance has a graded close (credit-at-close is
   the law; no grade ⇒ nothing honest to learn from); forks without
   closes are ENUMERATED as deferred-with-reason, visible on the books,
   with close-minting as nameable future waves — total in principle,
   gated by gradeability in practice, the gap never silent.

16. LAW-BAND VOLATILITY MODULATION (owner, 2026-08-06: "chaotic to
   lawful should influence that learning and memory. Chaotic makes both
   more volatile.") — the learning-rate + decay-half-life constants are
   MODULATED by the actor's law word (the CR-ES-3 unified vocabulary
   lawless|balanced|lawful via lawWordFor): lawless = hot learning, fast
   forgetting (recency-dominant); lawful = slow accretion, long
   retention; balanced = base curve. Three bands x two constants, all in
   the frozen owner-signed family; caps/floors stay UNIVERSAL
   (volatility is speed, never lock-or-die permission). CONSEQUENCE
   (designed, not accidental): believed-doctrine decays on the same
   modulated clock ⇒ INTELLIGENCE SHELF LIFE — a lawful target's stolen
   doctrine sheet is durable treasure, a chaotic target's is fresh fish;
   predictability becomes a cultural trait and both legibility and
   illegibility become weapons. Composes with refinement 8's seat shock
   without new machinery.

17. THE NAMED-DOMAIN TOTALITY CHECKLIST (owner, 2026-08-06: "trade …
   faith, strategy, route creation, route choice, and dynamics between
   settlements, goals, and goal orientation, etc. … make it cohesive
   fully") — beyond the idiom sweep, the volume dispositions BY NAME:
   TRADE (partnership formation/breaking, embargoes, caravan routing) ·
   FAITH (tradition imposition/claims, patron adoption, clergy forks) ·
   STRATEGY (the settlement strategist, already in) · ROUTE CREATION
   (the road-investment decision near the genesis machinery) · ROUTE
   CHOICE (⚠ likely COST-ARGMAX today, invisible to the weighted-fork
   sweep — each such fork gets an explicit call: CONVERT to a weighted
   fork with a habit term, STAY-DETERMINISTIC with a stated reason, or
   DEFER; never silently excluded) · INTER-SETTLEMENT DYNAMICS
   (relationship forks) · GOALS (selection, persistence-vs-abandonment,

   personality-forming loop). Each row LEARN / STAY / DEFER-with-
