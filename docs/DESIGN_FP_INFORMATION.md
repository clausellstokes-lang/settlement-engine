## §1 THE LAWS THAT BIND EVERY WAVE

### 1a Constitutional (inherited verbatim — the war volume's §1a plus the spine's twelve)
Same-seed byte identity · dormancy behind virtual flags with goldens captured
FIRST · seeded purity (no transcendentals in engine paths) · monotone ratchets ·
receipts carry enforcement (id + address chain + typed action + settlements by

### 1b Medium-specific laws (each grounded in an owner ruling or a built idiom)
- **LAW ONE, MEDIUM FORM:** the engine models what courts BELIEVE and what the
  record SUGGESTS — never a nested mind, never a truth the DM has not authored.
  Second-order belief is a DERIVED banded heuristic over the outbound record
  (spine SP-2, settled); a stored "what B believes" map is a design defect.
- **THE ANONYMITY / NO-FATES BOUNDARY (owner ruling 2026-07-19, recorded at
  informationStatecraft.js:43-50):** every exposure is a REPUTATION cost — no
  one is removed, killed, or dissolved by an information event. The spy is
  burned, never hanged by the engine; the accused house is shamed, never
  shuttered. Downstream falls happen through the existing legitimacy/coup
  machinery on their own law.
- **THE ONE-SUBSTRATE DISTANCE LAW:** five independent consumers price the SAME
  physical distance through one shared reader (latency hopWeeks, recency
  surcharge ×0.5, route impedance, fidelity attenuation 1/(1+0.75×delay), query
  counter surcharge — survey-verified). No wave may mint a sixth distance price;
  everything that moves rides the transit kernel and law M's one-week floor
  (SP-7 asserts the time base).
- **THE ASYMMETRY OF TRUST IS PRESERVED:** credibility rises at 0.6 and falls at
  5 on a 52-tick half-life (CREDIBILITY_TUNING, survey-verified) — trust builds
  in years and dies in an afternoon. No mechanism in this volume may make lying
  cheap: every new lie surface prices exposure through the SAME credibility
  stock and the SAME blowback triple (credibility charge + E1-machinery
  grievance + legitimacy prose). One stock, one afternoon.
- **THE ANTI-HUM LAW (the anti-whisper-war ruling, recorded as a deliberate
  absence at informationStatecraft.js:1444-1472):** no autonomous act spammer.
  Every new act is trigger-only, cooldown-gated, per-tick capped — the
  INTEL_TRADE_TUNING idiom (26-week pair cooldown, 12/tick cap) is the house
  shape. A quiet decade must stay quiet.
- **K3'S KINSHIP — STRUCTURAL TRUTH-READ ENFORCEMENT:** every new evaluator that
  claims to be belief-side gets the war program's structural treatment: pinned
  import list, source token scan for true-state reads, and the guard-the-guard
  positive control proving the scan bites on a module that legitimately reads
  truth. Convention is not enforcement.
- **THE CONTAMINATION-HONESTY LAW (knowledgeLaneEvidence.js's discipline,
  survey-verified):** no certification row claims the knowledge mover family
  while the residual lists are non-empty; every NEW beat this volume mints
  classifies knowledge on its OWN vocabulary, never through the bare `news`
  token — and the residual lists only shrink (IN-6's ratchet).
- **THE PACING GOVERNOR IS THE EDITOR (spine §1.12):** every news class below
  registers with pacing/significance machinery. The medium graduating from 1.5%
  to war-class density must never become wallpaper — flow control by law.
- **THE TWO-TIMESCALE ECHO, MEDIUM FORM (spine §1.9):** the fast layer is the
  arrival — the week the word lands, the beat fires; the slow verdict is the
  stock — credibility (52-tick half-life), the grievance ledger, the war a lie
  started, concluding over years. Every wave below names both.
