## THE ANSWER, IN ONE PARAGRAPH (for the DM)

Every town already has a culture on its strip: Latin, Steppe, Mesoamerican, or a blend of two. Today that word is paint. THE CULTURE makes it a living thing. A town's court speaks an OFFICIAL culture, and under it the town's people are a MIX, told in bands: most of the town is Latin, a large minority is Steppe, a handful came from elsewhere. That mix moves only as people move. When a refugee column out of a Steppe town reaches a Latin port, the port leans Steppe by as much as the column was of the port; the children of a generation lean toward the town's majority, but a quarter large enough keeps its own ways; a long trade road leaves a trace. Whatever leaves a town carries the town's make-up with it: the army Kell musters is as Steppe as Kell is, and so is the column that flees it. The court's official culture has a LEGITIMACY in the words faith uses: secure where the people are its own and the court has long spoken it, tenuous where the town has filled with others, contested where a rival way holds the margin; a court may adopt its people's culture as its own, at a cost. The town's IDENTITY, the phrase on the strip, moves only when the mix has held a lead for years, and never under your feet: "Varn is a Latin town" becomes "Varn is a Latin town with a Steppe quarter" and, a generation later, "Varn, once Latin, is a Steppe town", with a Herald line the week it turns. A crown presses its culture down the chain as it presses its faith: where crown and vassal share a culture the chain holds better; where they differ, the vassal's court is pressed and its people grieve. Allies and trade partners soften a town's identity, enemies harden it, so a frontier town stays itself for centuries. Towns of kindred culture come to terms sooner and ask for less; a victor may ask the loser's court to adopt its culture, to guarantee tolerance of a minority or to resettle a column, and each term costs legitimacy or grievance and can be refused. No named person is given a culture by this, no culture is better than another, and the map takes nothing. Everything is dark until a thousand-year run shows the realm neither freezes nor melts into one people.

## §1 THE LAWS (CU1 to CU14)

**CU1: NO CULTURE IS BETTER (minted here, CU-R11; its ground is `src/data/cultureProfiles.js`'s header: "texture, not destiny"; "Culture changes likelihood, never eligibility").** No family is kin to another by authored fiat, none stronger, holier, richer or more warlike, and no mechanism of this volume keys a weight, a cap or a chance on WHICH family a share names: every number reads the SHAPE of the composition (a share, a margin, a shared share, a tenure), never its family. An 11x11 table of any kind is a design defect. Prose names families as the dossier does and never ranks them.

**CU2: BANDS, NOT COUNTS (cited: requirement 1, "no census realism"; `DESIGN_FP_GRAMMAR.md` §4 "bands and rights, never headcounts"; `DESIGN_FP_ARCH_SP.md` L5 "no raw floats in prose; each clause names ONE band").** The composition is a 100-point ledger read only through bands (`shareBandLabel`'s six phrases); the legitimacy is read only through its band word; an arrival moves shares by a BANDED fraction; a muster's make-up is printed in bands. No surface shows a point value.

**CU3: COMPOSITION MOVES ONLY WITH PEOPLE.** The only writers of a town's shares are the kernels that move people: a column's arrival (its frozen `composition`, by the headcount band), the demographic step (births toward the majority, deaths pro rata), the trade trace (a partner's people, at trace weight) and the terms that compel a column (`resettlement`, which is itself a column). No term, verdict, edict or crown act moves a share directly (the GRAMMAR's conversion-mandate exclusion, generalised); the court's `official_culture_adoption` moves the court's token and nothing under it.

**CU4: THE COURT SPEAKS THE OFFICIAL TOKEN; THE PEOPLE ARE THE COMPOSITION.** `official` is the court's declaration (seeded from the dossier's token; changed only by the court's own adoption or a term the court accepts, §9). The shares are the people. Legitimacy is the standing of the court's claim over its own people (§4). `official` is never a standing word (CU-R4).

**CU5: LEGITIMACY IS A CLAIM'S STANDING, IN FAITH'S WORDS.** The official token's legitimacy is a 0..1 that lags a target (the `stepDeityLegitimacy` form) and is read only as `secure · established · tenuous · contested` (`faithPanelModel.js :: legitimacyBand`'s thresholds, by name). One meaning on every surface: a claim's standing (fold §5 row, §21).

**CU6: IDENTITY NEVER CHANGES UNDER THE READER'S FEET (minted here, CU-R11; its ground is requirement 24 (b)'s held docket, "the tick COMPLETES normally", and `recordRegister.js`'s `culturalIdentity: 'READING'`).** The identity phrase is WRITTEN once per yearly step, at the step's end, and every surface READS the written phrase; nothing recomputes it on read. The leading family flips only after the margin has held its ticks (§5), and a flip driven by a foreign act waits on its hearing (§12). A DM who opens a town mid-advance sees the phrase the last step wrote, and the Herald names the week it turns.

**CU7: THE REPRESENTATIVENESS LAW (CU-R5).** Nothing departs a settlement with a make-up the settlement could not have produced. A column carries `composition`, the origin's banded shares frozen at departure; a muster's make-up is DERIVED from the town's shares at the muster tick and printed as one receipt line in bands; a caravan's and a delegation's likewise where those objects exist (today streams and errands, so the line is a slot, §17). Named NPCs are never assigned a culture by this law (requirement 4).

**CU8: NAMED PEOPLE ARE NEVER FATED BY CULTURE (cited: requirement 4; `DESIGN_FP_SPINE.md` "never resolves a fate"; `DESIGN_FP_POPULATIONS.md` "The engine never resolves a named character's fate"; `DESIGN_FP_ARCHITECTURE.md` COURT BONDS "names no person's fate").** No named NPC carries a culture field written by this volume; a name is drawn from a family by shares at the NPC's mint and that is the whole of it; no verb, hearing or term acts on a person for their culture.

**CU9: THE MAP TAKES NOTHING (cited: `docs/HANDOFF_CURRENT.md` "culture tokens select no geometry"; `MF-W3S1.md`).** Composition, legitimacy and identity select no geometry, no morphology, no palette. The map may READ the identity phrase on a card at its own wave (ROUTE, §17); the settlement map reads nothing.

**CU10: THE CROWN REACHES CULTURE DOWN THE SAME CHANNEL AS FAITH (CU-R7).** The liege's official token rides `faithCarriersOut`'s edges and channels at their landed strengths, enters the member's legitimacy by the courts-first seam, wears as `culture_imposed` where it presses, and is dampened by distance and by tenure through `TENURE_HALF` only. No second channel, weight family or damper is minted.

**CU11: ENEMIES DEFINE EACH OTHER (CU-R8).** The neighbourhood term is believed and weighted by relationship kind: allied, trade_partner and patron neighbours soften a town's identity hold; rival, hostile and cold_war neighbours HARDEN it; a vassal carries the crown term instead. The hardening is the counterforce to convergence (CU13).

**CU12: AFFINITY SITS AT THE CONSUMER (CU-R6).** The pairwise band enters the pact appraisal, the responder's valuation, the peace reasons and the relationship drift. It never enters `courtPostureOf` (SP-C's law) and never a counter-offer (none exists).

**CU13: NO CONVERGENCE WITHOUT ITS COUNTERFORCE (requirement 2).** Every force toward one culture names its opposite over the same evidence: births toward the majority against the enclave's retention; the crown's press against the fault line and the wear; an ally's softening against an enemy's hardening; the trade trace against its cap at `present`. THE THOUSAND-YEAR MEASURE (§10) is the law's proof and nothing lights before it holds.

**CU14: DARK MEANS ABSENT.** Every flag absent: no `settlement.culture` key, no `composition` on any column, no incident, no reason, no term row drafted, no phrase on the strip beyond today's, byte-identical (§16 row 1). Lit-then-dark: the record stays as lived history and nothing moves it; today's surfaces read today's token.

## §3 COMPOSITION: THE PEOPLE A TOWN IS MADE OF, MOVED ONLY BY THE PEOPLE WHO MOVE (wave CU-1; `cultureEnabled`)

### 3.1 What it reads (TRUTH)
The record's shares; the migration column ledger as the adoption pass already reads it (`traditionsKernel.js`, the §9 influx capture of each in-transit column's origin and count); the destination's population band; the demographics bands (`demographicsRates.js :: BIRTH_BANDS`, `NATURAL_DEATH_BANDS` by tier) where `demographicsEnabled` is lit, else a flat yearly step; the `trade_partner` edges of the regional graph.

### 3.2 Arrivals blend by the headcount band (the column carries its make-up)
- **The column's field.** `MigrationColumn` gains `composition` (POP's shape, POP's to confirm, §17): the ORIGIN's shares at the departure tick, frozen, whole points, drop-when-absent, on every column class (`refugee · voluntary`; WC's three the day WC stamps one; `resettlement`'s, §9). A column minted dark carries none and blends nothing (folk from elsewhere, `other`).
- **The blend.** At the landing (the stage POP-1's clearing lands at, `demographicsMigration.js :: advanceDemographicMigration`, the M4 arrivals' fold; one leaf call), the destination's shares move toward the column's by the fraction `f = arrivals / destinationPopulation`, BANDED (`ARRIVAL_SHARE_BANDS`, DRAFT UNMEASURED): `arrival_slight` under one percent moves nothing; `arrival_marked` from one to three percent moves by `f`; `arrival_sweeping` above three percent moves by `f` capped at `CULTURE_ARRIVAL_CAP` (DRAFT 0.08) per column. `shares' = renorm((1 − f) · shares + f · composition)`. A refused or dispersed column (`column_at_the_wall`'s `refused · dispersed`, POP-1) blends nothing; an `interned` column blends at the next yearly step if it is admitted then.
- **The arrival's receipt.** The planting line (the fold §4): "A column out of Kell, most of them Steppe, came to Varn this spring", in the record's voice; on scrutiny, the band it fell in.

### 3.3 Births toward the majority, against the enclave's retention (the yearly step)
- Each yearly step, the shares drift toward the LEADING family by `d = CULTURE_BIRTH_DRIFT × birthBand01` (DRAFT 0.6 points a year at the `steady` band, UNMEASURED; `birthBand01` is the tier's `BIRTH_BANDS` rate normalised against `steady` where `demographicsEnabled` is lit, else 1): the children of a generation lean toward the town they are born in.
- **THE ENCLAVE RETENTION (the counterforce).** A family at `present` or above gives up only `(1 − CULTURE_RETENTION) × d_f` of its drift (DRAFT retention 0.7 at `present`, 0.9 at `prevailing`; UNMEASURED): a quarter large enough to marry within itself keeps its ways. A family in `enclave` standing but above `CULTURE_ENCLAVE_FLOOR` (DRAFT 5 points) keeps `CULTURE_ENCLAVE_RETENTION` (DRAFT 0.5); below the floor it fades into the leading family at the full drift and, below one point, into `other`. The floor is what lets a Steppe quarter stand three hundred years in a Latin town (§16 row 7) and what lets a handful vanish in a generation.
- Deaths move no share (pro rata, the people-ledger's `mortality` rule by analogy); the step is one renorm.

### 3.4 Trade familiarity at trace weight
- Each yearly step, for every `trade_partner` edge (and every `allied` edge, at half), the town's shares move toward the partner's LEADING family by `CULTURE_TRADE_TRACE` (DRAFT 0.2 points a year, UNMEASURED), from `other` first and then pro rata from the rest; the trace never lifts a family past `present` (its cap is the standing's lower bound, 15): factors, carters and marriages along a road leave a quarter, never a majority. A road alone never flips an identity (§16 row 8).
- The trace is dropped entirely where the pair is `hostile` or `cold_war` that year (no trade, no trace).

### 3.5 THE REPRESENTATIVENESS LAW in the kernels (CU7)
- **A column** carries `composition` (§3.2): what flees Kell is as Steppe as Kell.
- **A muster.** Where WC's `muster` verb raises a force from the people ledger (`DESIGN_FP_ARCH_WC.md` §1.13; dark), the force's make-up is `musterCompositionOf(record)` at the muster tick, printed as ONE receipt line in bands on the muster's own receipt ("Kell's levy marched: most of them Steppe, a large minority Latin"); no field is stored on the army (the army is not a culture carrier; its return home is the same people). The line is a SLOT on WC's receipt until WC-1 lands (§17).
- **A caravan** and **a delegation** likewise where the objects exist: today a caravan is a stream (`supplyShipments.js`) and a delegation an errand (`errandMint.js :: mintErrandSpine`, dark), so each is a slot: WY-3's caravan record and the errand card's one line, each at its owner's wave.
- **Named NPCs** are never assigned a culture by this law: an envoy is a person with a name drawn as §11 draws names, nothing more.

### 3.6 Clock · tier · counterforce · receipt · felt
- **Clock (requirement 9).** Arrivals blend at the landing tick (weekly, as columns land); the drift, the trace and the renorm run ONCE a year at the kernel's yearly step, denominated in `INTERVAL_WEEKS` (`worldPulse/intervalWeeks.js`), keyed by `stepTick` so a month, season or year advance steps exactly once a year. FIRST-VISIBLE (requirement 16): the arrival's planting line the week a marked column lands (inside a month where columns move); the slow verdict is the identity's turn (§5), years.
