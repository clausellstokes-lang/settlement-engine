## THE ANSWER, IN ONE PARAGRAPH (for the DM)

Every town already has a culture on its strip: Latin, Steppe, Mesoamerican, or a blend of two. Today that word is paint. THE CULTURE makes it a living thing. A town's court speaks an OFFICIAL culture, and under it the town's people are a MIX, told in bands: most of the town is Latin, a large minority is Steppe, a handful came from elsewhere. That mix moves as people move. When a refugee column out of a Steppe town reaches a Latin port, the port leans Steppe by as much as the column was of the port; the children of a generation lean toward the town's majority, but a quarter large enough keeps its own ways; a long road to an ally or a trade partner, a liege's authority over a vassal, an occupier's garrison, each carries a little of its people's ways the way it already carries a creed. Whatever leaves a town carries the town's make-up with it: the army Kell musters is as Steppe as Kell is, and so is the column that flees it. The court's official culture has a CLAIM on its people, told in the words faith uses: secure where the people are its own and the court has long spoken it, tenuous where the town has filled with others, contested where a rival way holds the margin; a court may adopt its people's culture as its own, at a cost. The town's IDENTITY, the phrase on the strip, moves only when the mix has held a lead for years, and never under your feet: "Varn is a Latin town" becomes "Varn is a Latin town with a Steppe quarter" and, a generation later, "Varn, once Latin, is a Steppe town", with a Herald line the week it turns. A crown presses its culture down the chain as it presses its faith: where crown and vassal share a culture the chain holds better; where they differ, the vassal's court is pressed, a little of the crown's people settle, and the vassal's people grieve when the crown acts. Allies and trade partners soften a town's identity, enemies harden it, so a frontier town stays itself for centuries. Towns of kindred culture come to terms sooner and ask for less; a victor may ask the loser's court to adopt its culture, to guarantee tolerance of a minority or to resettle a column, and each term costs legitimacy or grievance and can be refused. No named person is given a culture by this, no culture is better than another, and the map takes nothing. Everything is dark until a thousand-year run shows the realm neither freezes nor melts into one people.

## §1 THE LAWS (CU1 to CU14)

**CU1: NO CULTURE IS BETTER (minted here, CU-R11; its ground is `src/data/cultureProfiles.js`'s header: "texture, not destiny"; "Culture changes likelihood, never eligibility").** No family is kin to another by authored fiat, none stronger, holier, richer or more warlike, and no mechanism of this volume keys a weight, a cap or a chance on WHICH family a share names: every number reads the SHAPE of the composition (a share, a margin, a shared share, a tenure) or the RELATIONSHIP a carrier rides, never a family. An 11x11 table of any kind is a design defect. Births and deaths are family-blind (§20 row 5). Prose names families as the dossier does and never ranks them.

**CU2: BANDS, NOT COUNTS (cited: requirement 1, "no census realism"; `DESIGN_FP_GRAMMAR.md` §4 "bands and rights, never headcounts"; `DESIGN_FP_ARCH_SP.md` L5 "no raw floats in prose; each clause names ONE band").** The composition is a 100-point ledger of whole points read only through bands (`shareBandLabel`'s six phrases), its sub-point forces held in a fractional accumulator that releases whole points (CU-R18, §2.1); the claim is read only through its band word; a muster's make-up is printed in bands. No surface shows a point or a residue.

**CU3: COMPOSITION MOVES WITH PEOPLE, AND WITH THE CARRIERS THAT ALREADY MOVE FAITH (CU-R16).** The only writers of a town's shares are: a column's arrival (its frozen `composition`, by its fraction of the town); the demographic step (births toward the majority, deaths pro rata); the CARRIED SHARE along `faithCarriersOut`'s edges and channels (an ally's or partner's road, a liege's authority, an occupier's garrison, each at its landed strength, distance-weighted, exactly as a creed travels); and the `resettlement` column a term compels. No term, verdict, verb or declaration moves a share directly (the GRAMMAR's conversion-mandate exclusion, generalised): the court's `official_culture_adoption` moves the court's token and nothing under it. Law One holds because a court's word changes no belief by fiat; only the slow carried share does, and it is braked by the floors (CU-R19) and the hold (CU13).

**CU4: THE COURT SPEAKS THE OFFICIAL TOKEN; THE PEOPLE ARE THE COMPOSITION.** `official` is the court's declaration (seeded from the dossier's token; changed only by the court's own adoption, a term the court accepts, PL's appointment at PL's writer, or the DM's verb, §4.2, §9, §11). The shares are the people. The claim is the standing of the court's declaration over its own people (§4). `official` is never a standing word (CU-R4).

**CU5: THE CLAIM'S STANDING IS IN FAITH'S WORDS.** The official token's claim is a 0..1 that lags a target (the `stepDeityLegitimacy` form) and is read only as `secure · established · tenuous · contested` (`faithPanelModel.js :: legitimacyBand`'s thresholds). One meaning on every surface: a claim's standing (fold §5 row, §21). Its field is `officialClaim01`, never `legitimacy01` (a landed ORDINAL in `cultureDistance.js` and a score in four other modules, §2.4).

**CU6: IDENTITY NEVER CHANGES UNDER THE READER'S FEET (minted here, CU-R11; its ground is requirement 24 (b)'s held docket, "the tick COMPLETES normally", and `recordRegister.js`'s `culturalIdentity: 'READING'`).** The identity phrase is WRITTEN once per yearly step, at the step's end, and every surface READS the written phrase; nothing recomputes it on read. The leading family flips only after the margin has held its ticks (§5), and a flip driven by a foreign act waits on its hearing (§12). A DM who opens a town mid-advance sees the phrase the last step wrote, and the Herald names the week it turns.

**CU7: THE REPRESENTATIVENESS LAW (CU-R5).** Nothing departs a settlement with a make-up the settlement could not have produced. A column carries `composition`, the origin's shares frozen at departure (whole points, read in bands); a muster's make-up is DERIVED from the town's shares at the muster tick and printed as one receipt line in bands; a caravan's and a delegation's likewise where those objects exist (today streams and errands, so the line is a slot, §17). Named NPCs are never assigned a culture by this law (requirement 4): a name drawn by shares at an NPC's mint is the only cultural mark a person carries, and it is never re-read as a culture (§11).

**CU8: NAMED PEOPLE ARE NEVER FATED BY CULTURE (cited: requirement 4; `DESIGN_FP_SPINE.md` "never resolves a fate"; `DESIGN_FP_POPULATIONS.md` "The engine never resolves a named character's fate"; `DESIGN_FP_ARCHITECTURE.md` COURT BONDS "names no person's fate").** No named NPC carries a culture field written by this volume; no verb, hearing or term acts on a person for their culture.

**CU9: THE MAP TAKES NOTHING (cited: `docs/HANDOFF_CURRENT.md` "culture tokens select no geometry"; `MF-W3S1.md`).** Composition, claim and identity select no geometry, no morphology, no palette. The map may READ the identity phrase on a card at its own wave (ROUTE, §17); the settlement map reads nothing.

**CU10: THE CROWN REACHES CULTURE DOWN THE SAME CHANNEL AS FAITH (CU-R7, CU-R16).** The liege's official token rides `faithCarriersOut`'s edges and channels at their landed strengths: it enters the member's CLAIM by the courts-first seam, it enters the member's SHARES as the carried share, it wears as `culture_imposed` where the crown ACTS, and it is dampened by distance and by the member's own tenure (through `tenureYears`, one damper FORM; Faith's `TENURE_HALF` is in ticks and is never re-declared). No second channel or weight family is minted.

**CU11: ENEMIES DEFINE EACH OTHER (CU-R8).** The neighbourhood term is believed and weighted by relationship kind: allied, trade_partner and patron neighbours SOFTEN a town's identity hold; rival, hostile and cold_war neighbours HARDEN it, and hardening also halves what an enemy's people can plant (its columns' blend and its channels' carried share); a vassal carries the crown term instead. The hardening is the counterforce to convergence (CU13).

**CU12: AFFINITY SITS AT THE CONSUMER (CU-R6).** The pairwise band enters the pact appraisal, the responder's valuation, the peace reasons and the relationship drift. It never enters `courtPostureOf` (SP-C's law) and never a counter-offer (none exists). The other direction of requirement 10 is honoured where CU decides: the court's adoption CONSUMES `courtRiskAppetiteOf` as its patience (§4.2).

**CU13: NO CONVERGENCE AND NO FREEZE WITHOUT ITS COUNTERFORCE (requirement 2).** Every force toward one culture names its brake over the same evidence, and every brake names the force it slows: births toward the majority against the floors (CU-R19); the carried share against the floors, the hold, the hardening and the fault line; allies' softening against enemies' hardening; the arrival blend against the clearing and the cap; a kindred destination's pull against the refuge weight. THE THOUSAND-YEAR MEASURE (§10) rejects BOTH hazards, a monoculture and a frozen map, and nothing lights before it holds.

**CU14: DARK MEANS ABSENT.** Every flag absent: no `cultureStates` key, no `composition` on any column, no incident, no reason, no term row drafted, no phrase on the strip beyond today's, byte-identical (§16 row 1). Lit-then-dark: an existing record stays as lived history and nothing moves it; the strip, Daily Life and Foundations read an EXISTING phrase lit or dark (PA's inked-read idiom), so a lit-then-dark world keeps its sentence; no step, blend, incident, reason or term runs.

## §10 THE CONVERGENCE COUNTERFORCES AND THE THOUSAND-YEAR MEASURE (no flag; the acceptance harness; CU-R9, CU-R17, CU-R18, CU-R19)

**The two hazards.** A region of towns under these forces can fail two ways. It can MELT: the carried share down the chain, the kindred pull on columns and births toward the majority make one family of a region in a few centuries. It can FREEZE: births entrench every leader, the edges are capped, the hold hardens, sub-point forces round to nothing, and no town ever turns. Either is a ratchet (requirement 2's "proven killer"). The measure rejects both (CU-R17), and the two chair rulings of the cure make the middle hold by construction where it can: the accumulator (CU-R18) means every force moves the ledger, so nothing freezes for want of a whole point; the floors (CU-R19) mean no family that ever stood as a quarter is emptied, so nothing melts wholly.

**The arithmetic, at the DRAFT numbers (every figure UNMEASURED; the run tunes them).** The drift is 0.6 points a year, drawn from the non-leading families above their floors. A seedling planted in a 96/4 town is pulled back by `0.6 × s / (s + 4)` a year (`elsewhere`'s four points share the pull), so a carrier of `c` points a year grows a seedling iff `c > 0.6 s / (s + 4)`: it reaches the ENCLAVE floor (5) iff `c > 0.33`, the QUARTER floor (15) iff `c > 0.47`, and can take the LEAD iff `c` exceeds the pull near parity, about 0.54. The authority channel carries `0.328 m` and an edge `0.18 m`, `m` clamped [0.12, 4]. So: an edge from an equal source (`m = 1`) sustains a seedling under two points and plants nothing (§3.4); an edge from a source twice the mass plants an enclave, and its cap holds it under the quarter; the authority channel plants an enclave at `m ≥ 1.0`, a quarter at about `m ≥ 1.45` and can take the lead at about `m ≥ 1.65` (`CULTURE_PLANT_RATIO`'s three DRAFT rungs; `TIER_MASS` makes a town over a village `m = 2`, a city over a town `m = 2`, equals `m = 1`).
