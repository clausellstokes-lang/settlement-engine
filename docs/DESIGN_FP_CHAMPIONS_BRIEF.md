## §2 THE SHAPE AS DISCUSSED (the chair's proposals the owner responded to; none is ruled yet)

**Entry.** Champions and villains enter ONLY through custom content, authored by the DM like deities. The engine never creates one.

**One person, marked.** A champion is not a new kind of NPC. It is a MARK on a person the estate already models (a general, an envoy, a spy, a legate): the mark adds an archetype, a polarity (champion or villain), one bolstered signature trait, goals, a bound, a failure setting, renown, and a presence gift. No parallel NPC system.

**Eight domains, each with a champion and a villain** (the owner liked the two added roles, the warden and the merchant prince):

| Domain | Champion | Villain | Where they go | The gift when they choose to use it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Faith | saint | heretic | where their creed is weak but ripe | conversion and unseating raised, or undermined |
| War | captain | warlord | where war or pay is | battle odds for the side served; the warlord stirs hostility and may turn |
| Diplomacy | envoy | provocateur | war-weary courts, courts needing terms | acceptance and terms improved, or treaties soured |
| Information | spymaster | deceiver | intrigue and paymasters | missions and counter-intelligence, or false reports planted |
| Health | doctor | plaguebearer | worst plague, no doctor yet | plague eased, or worsened — always the one plague truth |
| The wilds | warden | beast-caller | where menaces press | defences raised and menaces worn, or menaces strengthened; steering a menace only if the DM bound it |
| Trade | merchant prince | speculator | dear or glutted markets | supply eased, or markets cornered |
| Order | magistrate | agitator | legitimacy crises | unrest calmed, or stirred (the owner's disrupters and anarchists) |

**Presence is a chance, action is a choice (owner, message 3).** Each week the character decides whether to use its gift there. The choice weighs its traits (charitable acts unpaid; mercenary only under contract; capricious varies; cowardly avoids danger; patient waits for the best moment), its contract (a duty weighed against its nature — shirking costs renown and the employer's trust), what it believes is at stake, its mood and ties, and its HABITS. When the gift is used, the engine's default verdicts and rates in that domain lean toward its side by a bounded amount; the DM still holds every hearing.

**Failure.** One per-character setting authored in custom content: `fated · steady · fallible · brittle` (fated is the earlier "always succeed"). It sets how often efforts fail and how far a failure falls: a setback, a stumble that costs renown, a disgrace that also costs the employer's trust, or capture. RENOWN (not "reputation", a landed token) rises and falls with deeds, scales the gift and the price, and is known to courts by report. Capture and ransom ride the landed hold and ransom roads (a spy caught at court is held and ransomed; a captain taken in battle is a prisoner; a wanderer taken by bandits pays no ransom, per the owner's §125.5). The chair's recommendation: death stays the DM's verdict, never an engine roll (the named-fate scope rule) — to confirm with the owner.

**The mind.** A calling (archetype); the full standard NPC traits and goals with one bolstered trait; moods that build and fade (fatigue, restlessness, weariness of a region — "tired of the north", homesickness, fear after capture, grief, pride and shame); memory of places and people (who paid, who betrayed, a nemesis); beliefs (it acts on what it has heard, Law One); HABITS (owner, message 3) learned with the habit program's own curve at person grain inside this program (consistent with OD-FC-42: court habits stay court-level), shaping whether it acts, where it goes and whom it serves — and readable ("has made a habit of wintering in Tolle").

**The weekly choice.** Stay and work · go to a known place · take or leave work · meet a handler · rest · go home · chase a nemesis. A seeded draw (exact replay); a commitment held until something clearly better appears ("until a better deal or reason comes along"); every choice keeps its top reasons for the dossier. Drift (the owner's "mutations"): traits shift slowly with experience.

**Bound and free (owner: distinct but related).** BOUND = an office, so always employed: moves on its own initiative inside its employer's lands (a town, or a whole kingdom); outside only on the court's errand or with an army or column the court sends; leaving on its own is resigning (it becomes free; priced; heard). FREE = goes where its goals lead; may take temporary contracts that last while goals and pay align; breaking one costs trust with future employers.

**Employment both ways.** Free characters seek work where they are needed and values or pay fit; courts seek characters they have HEARD of, sending a recruiter to where they believe the character is (who may have gone). Offers are hearings; two courts may bid.

**The line home (owner's handlers).** A bound operative abroad (spymaster, deceiver, provocateur, agitator) meets a handler from its home court at a rendezvous on the edge of home territory: orders and the true state of home in, its findings out, both pictures refreshed; the meeting can be caught by the target's counter-intelligence. Envoys keep the line by dispatches, saints by their mother house.

**Targeting.** The saint's ripeness is the faith contest's own conversion odds as the saint believes them (hardship, low prosperity, a weak seated patron, a foothold of believers help; a far creed, a large population, persecution risk slow or endanger it; the patron's recorded axes set priorities — the followers act, the god does not). The doctor goes where plague is worst and no doctor is. The saint in a migrant column changes the column's faith (needs wayfare's faith cargo).

**Contests.** A champion and villain of one domain in one town contest it (a hearing); repeated contests make a nemesis arc.

**Refusal (owner, message 5).** A champion can be refused: an employment offer refused by either side, and ENTRY refused at the gate. A court bars a character for CIRCUMSTANCE (a quarantine in plague, a war, a rival creed's saint or a heretic in a pious town, a rival's captain, a known enemy's envoy) or for RENOWN (infamy known by report, a disgraced champion, a villain whose deeds have reached it). The gate decides on what the court BELIEVES, so a villain whose deeds have not reached the town walks in, and a hero slandered by a deceiver can be turned away. The answer is `admitted · refused · admitted under watch`, a hearing (`champion_at_the_gate`), reusing the populations program's gate idiom (the column at the wall, `MIGRATION_REFUSALS`). A refused character reroutes, waits outside, or, by its own nature, tries another way in.

**Hearings to register** (requirement 24): employment offer · champion–villain contest · ransom · a failure severe enough to matter · a bound character resigning; and the character's participation shown on every engagement card it touches ("Captain Vey is in Kell and has not taken up arms").

**Where it sits.** After the polity and the menace (crowns hire champions; menaces draw wardens). Each domain lights when its own system does — faith and order first (faith unseating and the stressors are already live); the captain waits on the generals design, the spymaster on the statecraft chain, the merchant on EC.

## §3 GUARDRAILS (from the estate's laws)

- No engine-spawned champions; no parsing of the DM's names; no always-on gifts; no parallel NPC system; no second learning law; no second plague, danger or crisis truth.
- Every gift is a bounded lean; villains are the counterforce the constitution requires (requirement 2) made into characters.
- Zero lines in the frozen kernel; leaf modules; hosts at the ceiling only through leaf-first cars (`npcAgency.js` is at 796/800).
- Premium (the simulator is gated for premium); dark behind flags until the owner's gate; same-seed shifts signed at their units.
