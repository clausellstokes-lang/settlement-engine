## §0 THE THESIS

Two findings organize everything here. FIRST: the pairwise law (I — no war
object, no coalition object, membership derived from edges + ties) means
allies of convenience are not a feature to add — they are the engine's ONLY
kind of ally, and multi-coalition wars are a graph phenomenon that resolves
as a CASCADE of pairwise terminations. What is missing is not machinery but
RECORDS: the exit has no closed vocabulary, the side-switch has no receipted
shape, betrayal history prices nothing, and the alignment axes color none of
it. SECOND: the unequal peace is incomplete — streams and stores exist, but
the victor cannot demand a SHARE of what the loser is (its yield, its labor,
its people, its growth), and the demand has no doctrine (why THIS ask) and no
honest resentment arithmetic (what the ask costs the asker). The cure is one
term family and one drafting layer, riding the one-instrument law end to end.
Nothing below adds an actor class, an N-party object, a timer, a clamp, or a
coin. Everything below is records, weights, bands, and receipts on machinery
the volumes already specify.

### CV-1 — THE EXIT VOCABULARY (the back-out gets its records)

A closed exit set, minted at the moment a member stops discharging:
- **`exit_settled`** — the quitter squares its coalition debt (adequacy band
  met) and, where its own edge carries live causes, sues its separate peace
  through the normal envoy lane. Records: debt_forgiven; the treaty.
- **`exit_defaulted`** — it just stops. alliance_obligation defaults at a
  severity banded by the GAP between recorded contributions (apportionment
  weights) and the obligation — the realm that bled two years and leaves is
  read differently from the token-levy quitter. Records: ingratitude_debt +
  a grievance ENTRY on each ally's pair ledger. THE RESENTMENT CHAIN IS
  HEREBY NAMED: unsettled ingratitude_debt feeds grievance mass that the war
  reasons layer READS — the betrayal casus is derivable, never implied
  (one clause, one pin; VERIFY-AT-BUILD the feed spelling).
- **`exit_cold`** — withdrawal with NO separate peace: the quitter's own
  edge keeps its live causes at zero deployments — THE COLD EDGE, a named
  dormant state with its receipt ("The war was not ended; it was only set
  down."). Re-ignition is the counterparty's choice through the NORMAL
  opener gates (no new opener, no new casus mint — the old causes stand).
Per-observer treatment then COMPUTES (CT-LAW-1): the enemy reads isolation +
believed weakness (opportunism), gated by whatever treaty exists; each ally
reads its own debt + grievance; the reckoning arrives via GR-5
renegotiation-from-strength (the winner reopens standing arrangements
against the quitter's recorded damage) or debt settlement. NOTHING NEW —
the wave is the vocabulary, the receipts, and the debt→grievance clause.
- Pins (negative hardest): the three exits distinguishable on one seeded
  coalition fixture; a cold edge re-ignites WITHOUT new casus; a
  settled-exit quitter with a NAP is NOT attackable through the treaty
  block (WR-0c item 1's machinery); an exit_defaulted quitter's ally holds
  the sized grievance (gap-band asserted); no global "neutral" state
  anywhere (source scan: no neutrality field).
- Bands: adequacy-gap severity ladder; cold-edge significance class
  (SP-6a assignment). Clock: fast = the exit act; slow = the reckoning.
- Endings: none new (fragmentation stands; exits are ACTS on the way).
- Dossier: the exit, standing debts, and cold edges render on the war/treaty
  panels with the cause walk intact. Herald: pool per receipt kind under the
  content-depth floor (four variants each, the annex discipline).
