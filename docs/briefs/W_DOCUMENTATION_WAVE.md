## 1. THE ABOUT PAGE (#19) — skeleton (from the manifesto-arc + digestibility syntheses)

1. **HERO:** one outcome sentence + ONE static-legible annotated schematic of the whole
   loop (engine → dossier → maps → AI → play). The skimmer leaves here with the thesis.
2. **THE PHILOSOPHY LADDER** (concrete before abstract): open with the felt question
   ("what if the town remembered what your players did?"), one before/after beat
   (burned granary → next season's grain prices), THEN name constraint-driven
   worldbuilding. The existing DM Philosophy tab's best prose survives here.
3. **THE COVENANT** (Obsidian form): 5-6 one-sentence structural promises phrased as
   what CANNOT happen, each with claim → mechanism → inspectable receipt: same seed,
   same world, forever · every event carries a receipt · the AI has no write path to
   canon (structural, not policy) · your world exports and leaves with you · state,
   never fate (we never resolve your named characters) · nothing painted the world
   doesn't hold. The maker's name and face sit directly beneath.
4. **THE MECHANISM, dependency-order** (Ciechanowski): each section solves the problem
   the previous raised; the tick diagram appears BEFORE its text; permission to skip
   jargon granted; the one optional interactive = the same-seed reroll demo (the
   landing's forge-this-exact-town machinery reused — three seconds, never load-bearing).
5. **THE AI SECTION:** two-sentence concession first (the community's distrust is
   earned), then the three-part boundary taxonomy: what the AI may READ / may PROPOSE /
   what only the deterministic engine may WRITE. Link to the op registry as proof.
6. **CLOSE with the human + the audit invitation** (Tarsnap): deep links into the
   Compendium, the constitution, a receipt. Quiet pricing link. No ladder.
   Manual weight sheds to Quick Start + Compendium delegation (existing pattern).

## 5. ART DIRECTION (the medieval-art synthesis — binding for all four pages)

**Period art supplies the drawing vocabulary; modern chrome supplies the frame.** The
UI never cosplays parchment-rot: quote PERIOD CRAFT (engraved line, 2-3 ink weights,
hatching as tone, wash over linework, flat scarce gold), never AGE DAMAGE (stains,
burnt edges, sepia vignettes). Ground map-adjacent art in the Braun & Hogenberg
vocabulary (public domain; already the v2 engine's register); Nuremberg-woodcut weight
for icons/small sizes; ONE house compass rose + cartouche family as the brand
signature; the medieval schematic tradition (rota/T-O diagrams, da Vinci annotation)
for the About tick-diagram and system schematics; ink carries everything, gold flat and
scarce, at most two precious accents. Type: one period display voice at 20px+ against
the readable serif for text. **The product's own output is the art** (v2 craft samples,
panorama, the canonical demo world's plates) — supplemented by house-drawn schematics,
never stock fantasy art, never AI-generated illustration (the no-generative-art pledge
belongs in the About AI section).
