## one MEDIUM (information), negotiated in one GRAMMAR (diplomacy), driven by the

## §1 THE TWELVE REQUIREMENTS (the density contract — every mechanism in every
## volume carries ALL twelve or it is a defect; the audit stage hunts shallow
## spots wearing deep clothing)

1. **THE HEURISTIC LAW (Law One, owner-dictated):** the engine models believers,
   buyers, migrants, and courts — never the underlying truth of a DM's world.
   Band-shaped, reflavorable, integratable into ANY construction. No price
   engines, no theology, no census realism. The engine never confirms the divine,
   never resolves a fate, never contradicts a DM's canon.
2. **FORCES AND COUNTERFORCES:** every force names its opposition, scoring off
   the SAME evidence. A force without a counterforce is a ratchet; ratchets are
   the estate's proven killer (the runaway's lesson).
3. **DEPTH-DENSITY:** wide is welcome; every inch of width carries full depth.
   Each mechanism ships its law, force, counterforce, belief posture, receipts,
   pins (negative case hardest), bands, and couplings.
4. **THE NAMED-ACTOR RULE:** durable NPCs carry stories where persons beat
   systems — read through their EXISTING planes (powers, factions, temperament
   via W0/H3, flaws, rankings, goals, rivalries). Casting is read-wiring, never
   new NPC state. Never-kill and never-resolve-fates hold absolutely.
5. **THE ERRAND SPINE:** all purposeful named-person travel rides ONE substrate
   (the WR-7a design generalized): typed purposes {diplomatic, commercial,
   religious, factional, personal, covert}, DECLARED vs TRUE purpose riding the
   covert/revealed seam (every traveler may be traveling for someone else — the
   Q amendment generalized), the K.2 decaying snapshot, interceptability, and
   the M speed law. One ledger, one writer, six consumers.
6. **THE DOSSIER COHERENCE LAW (the crown):** every mechanism sources from
   settlement state (amendment B generalized: in character for the place, never
   contradicting a live read) AND lands back in settlement state with a dossier
   projection. Every mechanism ends with its DOSSIER ROUND-TRIP pin: open the
   town, find the trace where a DM would look. A system with no dossier landing
   does not exist.
7. **HISTORICAL MESSINESS, DERIVABLE:** organic mess is welcome — long causal
   chains across layers — but every mess is receipt-traceable end to end. The
   test: any "why is this village suddenly X?" walks backward link by named link.
8. **DEEP COUPLING:** every designed coupling names its reads, receipts, and
   counterforce in DESIGN_FP_COUPLINGS.md. Couplings ride existing reads —
   leakage is not coupling.
9. **THE TEMPORAL CONTRACT:** every mechanism declares its clock (cadence,
   dwell, decay half-life, duration) denominated against INTERVAL_WEEKS (the
   one time truth; a time-base walker asserts it — the 12-tick-year defect
   class dies estate-wide). Everything that moves obeys the one travel physics
   (transit kernel, km/week mode table, the week floor). THE TWO-TIMESCALE
   ECHO is universal law: the world responds quickly and concludes slowly,
   everywhere — every program names its fast layer and its slow verdict.
10. **THE RISK POSTURE:** every decision surface consumes a state-derived,
    outcome-learned, banded risk tolerance (the merchantAppetite pattern
    generalized; reversal pinned — no courage ratchets) composed into a
    SETTLEMENT STRATEGIC POSTURE that colours every verb across all layers:
    one character, not seven dice. Out-of-posture acts are priced and
    receipted news, never forbidden.
11. **NARRATION PARITY:** war narrates everything it does; so will everyone.
    Each program carries a story-kind expansion to war's density class, an
    ENDINGS VOCABULARY with share envelopes (the WR-9 discipline), receipts in
    the house voice at the Bound-Book register floor, and its TELLABLE list —
    the survey's IMPOSSIBLE/PARTIAL stories become pins.
12. **THE PACING GOVERNOR IS THE EDITOR:** history at full coupling can flood
    the Herald; every program's news classes register with the pacing/
    significance machinery so depth never becomes wallpaper. Story density has
    flow control by law.
13. **THE ALIGNMENT COUPLING (both axes, both directions) [CHAIR AMENDMENT
    2026-08-02 under full owner delegation — vetoable; the corpus's "twelve
    requirements" phrases refer to the ratified core above, and waves pick
    13–14 up at build]:** the settlement's alignment is a DERIVED two-axis
    read — `alignmentOf(id) → { lawfulness01, malice01 }` (beliefMap.js:915,
    informationStatecraft.js:584) — composed from its people; it is never a
    stored settlement stock and gains NO writer (never-store-a-derivable).
    Every mechanism names its READ engagement — which axes colour it
    (quadrant reads, contraband/conscience gates, the extremity and
    just-razing gates, the world's judgment on the observer's axis) — or
    declares alignment-empty WITH REASON (a decision, not an omission).
    WRITE-side: a mechanism that moves a town's soul moves the CONSTITUENTS
    — NPC alignment, roster, institutions — through their existing writers
    only; a parallel settlement-alignment stock or writer is a design
    defect. Each wave carries an Alignment line; the audit stage enforces
    totality. POPULATIONS' current zero engagement resolves at POP build
    under this requirement.
14. **THE EDIT VERB (the DM's pen and the clerk's proposal) [CHAIR AMENDMENT
    2026-08-02 under full owner delegation — vetoable]:** every new
    player/DM-visible state ships its edit story IN THE SAME WAVE: (a) the
    DM VERB through the established store-action lane (operationRegistry-
    registered, compendium data regenerated, approval-routed where the edit
    overrides an engine outcome — the REPUDIATE_TREATY twin discipline);
    (b) LIFECYCLE SURVIVAL — the edit survives regen/undo/import via the
    edits-delta idiom, round-trip pinned (the estate's most-bitten class);
    (c) the AI SURFACE — the AI edits nothing directly, ever: it proposes
    TYPED operations from closed vocabularies that drive the SAME DM verb
    (finite-semantics law: the AI is a bucketing clerk, never a writer). A
    state with no verb is a RECORDED decision ("engine-only, because X"),
    never silence. GRAMMAR's approval-lane rows and INTERIOR's burial verb
    are the house pattern; TRADE/POP/INFO currently name no verbs and pick
    theirs up at build under this requirement.
