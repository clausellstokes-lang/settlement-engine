# THE ONTOLOGY OF SETTLEMENTFORGE
## The complete map of what exists, what it carries, what binds it, what moves it, and what governs it
### Fable 5, 2026-07-16 — written at the owner's request from equidistance; the canonical WHAT, current to the D7 freeze. Successor doc to the vision corpus's world-model sections.

---

## 0. THE METAPHYSICS — the ground rules of existence

Reality in this world is split into two realms of being, joined by law:

**THE FROZEN** — canon. What generation minted: the dossier, the settlement config (intent),
the spatial digest (geometry, distances, territory, gates), the mint-time receipts (including
the TRUE intent weights of every generous act). Frozen things are immutable, byte-stable, and
replayable: the world is a pure function of (seed, config, operation-log). Same seed, same
world, to the byte, forever.

**THE LIVING** — the overlay. Everything that happens lives in sparse conditional ledgers
(spatialLedgers, politicsLedgers, and their kin) written OVER the frozen base, never into it.
Effective reality = frozen base + clamped overlay deltas, read through chokepoints. An unlit
layer is not empty — it is ABSENT, and absence means the prior world exactly (dormancy).

**TIME** is the week-tick. The world advances only through the tick engine or the DM's
operations; wall-clock is structurally excluded from the world's interior. Catch-up is
collapsed time, not skipped time.

**CAUSALITY IS TOTAL AND LEGIBLE.** Nothing happens without a typed reason; every event
carries receipts naming its lineage. The dice are loaded, never blind: every stochastic draw
samples a situation-weighted distribution on a stable seeded key, and the weights are
receipted. Chance chooses among fates the situation already priced.

**CONSERVATION.** People sum (war dead are the only sink; migration conserves; satellites are
transfers). Goods sum (produced + before = after + consumed + lost; seizures re-credit). Every
upswing debits a named source — no wealth from nothing.

**THE TWO AUDIENCES.** Every fact carries a visibility posture: DM-truth or projection
(player-safe, public, anon). The seam is fail-closed and enforced twice (client + SQL twins).
Tier never touches generation; the premium boundary is a reveal boundary.

**STATE, NEVER FATE.** The engine resolves institutions, powers, populations, and places.
It never resolves a named person. People modulate the world; the DM owns their souls.

## 1. THE BEINGS — the entity taxonomy

**THE REALM / CAMPAIGN** — the container: membership roster, the world clock, the regional
graph (nodes, edges, channels, impacts), the proposal docket, the ruleset (with its receipted
change log).

**THE SETTLEMENT** — the atom of the world. Tiered thorp → hamlet → village → town → city →
capital. Triune being: config (frozen intent), dossier (frozen expression: economy, NPCs,
institutions, history, hooks), live state (conditions, prosperity band, legitimacy, mobilization,
beliefs, everything the ticks wrote). Sub-beings: **satellites/steadings** (orbit-bound
proto-settlements with birth/growth/starvation/convergence lifecycles, population-conserved
from the parent), **remnants** (the terminally dead — cell preserved, movers skip them),
**relic ruins** (the rare earned dead: only a peak-tier ≥ city arc leaves one).

**THE INSTITUTION** — a settlement's organs. Catalog + custom (first-class by the facet law);
sources rolled/required/forced/custom (the DM-contract classes); upgrade ladders and
subsumption; service menus; geography affinities (impossible-not-rare where terrain forbids);
facets as machine-readable character (`clandestine`, `subterranean`, `mercenary`, …). Newest
member: the **Underground Network** — public existence, covert operations.

**THE NPC** — a named person: office/role (structural placeholders keep unled offices legible),
temperament and flaws (which derive momentum thresholds), goals, secrets, corruption flags
(covert vs revealed), rank. NPCs tilt formation, direction, and fragility of everything they
touch — and are never resolved.

**THE FACTION** — in-settlement collectives with categories, goals, strength, and competition
candidacies; stress-born factions (insurgents, converts, revolters) join organically.

**THE BLOC / COALITION** — political collectives above factions: capped ledgers, glue typology
(seat-held concessions, people-held loyalty, doctrine, threat, covert compromise), conspiracies
as covert blocs, fracture on succession, exposure detonations.

**THE COURT / RULING POWER** — the seat: legitimacy, temperament-derived reconsideration
cliffs, succession (which rerolls the seat's psychology — the new-ruler peace emerges free).

**THE DEITY & CULT** — the faith stratum: the activated pantheon (public by design) and the
LATENT pantheon (secret until revealed — the strictest privacy object in the world); piety,
stances, contest machinery, conduct profiles (mercy-gods and contract-gods interpret the world
differently — see D7).

**THE ARMY / COLUMN / CONVOY / NAVY** — military embodiments, DERIVED from settlement
capability, existing as records with strict lifecycles (mint → transit → engagement →
homecoming/retirement). The one-army law binds; navies obey land-parity (a blockade IS a
siege); mercenary institutions reinforce but never act.

**THE COVERT ORGANIZATION** — thieves' guilds, foreign patrons, cutouts, corruption assets:
the leash architecture (influence purchased through obligation), scarce by law.

**THE RESOURCE / DEPOSIT** — base endowment (frozen) + the discovery/depletion overlay;
renewable vs nonrenewable as a first-class axis; terrain-legal latent pools; the full
lifecycle latent → discovered → productive → declining → depleted (dwell) → removed.

**THE TRADE GOOD / CHAIN / MARKET** — catalogs with id-first joins; supply chains computed
from institutions + resources; markets and prices as display-derived reads (endogeneity-safe).

**THE ROUTE / EDGE** — the spatial weave: frozen digest distances, terrain costs, gates,
sea lanes, seasonal overlays, teleport circles; embattlement as a danger field over it.

**THE TREATY / TERM** — typed agreements (tribute, defense, sovereignty, non-intervention,
restitution…), durations, compliance strain, defection windows, fraying legible term-by-term.

**THE CLAIM / REASON** — the normative stratum: typed war reasons and peace reasons (the
Blainey taxonomy), casus belli (organic and DM-decreed, with decay), grievances (memory-scaled),
obligations/credit (with maturity, default, and the hardened-lender heart), and — once D7
builds — REFRAMED claims (the same frozen act re-read as debt, racket, or gift-forgiven).

**THE EVENT / OUTCOME / PROPOSAL** — canon events (38 authorable types), party impacts, realm
verbs (14+), queued intentions (the mutable docket), proposals awaiting the DM's word, with
refusals that are always visible.

**THE CONDITION / CRISIS / STRESSOR** — afflictions and pressures: condition archetypes with
severity and drift, dual-written for regeneration survival; crises with full lifecycles;
fifteen stressor types with counterforces, synergies, and spawn gates.

**THE KNOWLEDGE OBJECT** — the epistemic stratum's citizens: rumors (provenance × recency ×
independence), beliefs (banded, per observer-subject pair), credibility stocks, statecraft
marks (lies, sightings, concealments), fog, misjudgments — and interpretations (D7): beliefs
about MOTIVES.

**THE PSYCHOLOGY OBJECT** — commitment stocks keyed to courses (war:X, contest:X|side,
doctrine:X…), reconsideration cliffs, moral drift, dispositions.

**THE NARRATIVE OBJECT** — chronicle entries, news in the house voice, history beats, the
seven drama classes under the tempo governor, receipts, whispers (the guidance registry),
the generated glossary.

**THE PARTY** — the observed observer: its impacts enter as events; it never feeds fit math.

**THE DM** — sovereign, outside the ontology, with exactly one door in: the operation
vocabulary. Every op validated, previewed, receipted, refusable — and byte-equivalent to the
organic act it mirrors (force ≡ organic).

## 2. THE STATE STRATA — what beings carry

Three strata, strictly ordered by mutability:

1. **FROZEN** (never changes): config, dossier, digest, mint receipts, golden history.
2. **PERSISTENT LIVE** (changes only through ticks/ops): worldState — the tick, proposals,
   the conditional ledger families (spatial, politics, commitments, obligations, interventions,
   satellites, naval, resource overlays, calamity history…), settlement live fields, the
   regional graph. All drop-when-empty; all dormancy-lawful.
3. **DERIVED** (never stored, always recomputed): every read-model, effective endowment,
   prices, hegemony spheres, interpretations, projections, the entire display layer. Derived
   truth cannot drift from engine truth because it has no independent existence.

## 3. THE RELATIONAL FABRIC — what binds beings

Typed relationship edges (allied, trade, client, cold-war, hostile) with warmth and MEMORY
(decay scaled by the population's lifespan — elves do not forget); war and faith channels;
§G named ties (kinship, mentorship — bounded modulators); dependence webs (supply chains,
umbilicals, arteries — which booms ride and busts sever); hierarchies (vassal/tribute ties
that aggregate into SPHERES the hegemony read makes visible; parent-satellite orbits); and
the corruption web's leashes — the shadow fabric binding courts to foreign patrons through
cutouts, covert until exposed.

## 4. THE PROCESS ENGINE — the movers (what changes what, in composed order)

One tick composes ~20 movers, each dormancy-gated, keyed-fork deterministic,
read-last/write-next, receipted: stressor dynamics (pressures spawn, counter, synergize) →
cause lifecycles → the CALAMITY BUCKET (consequence, never cause — flavor is the DM's) →
GENEROSITY (aid, credit, purchase, overture — intent × capacity, floors held) →
INTERVENTION/CONVERGENCE (foreign armies in internal contests; sides are aim-groups; the
vulture waits) → the UPSWING ARCS (reconstruction debiting ally credit; boom→bust riding real
throughput; flourishing under long peace) → the LIFECYCLE (steadings born of surplus, dying of
starvation, converging into hamlets; terminal death; resettlement on ruins) → RESOURCE
DYNAMICS (terrain-legal discovery; nonrenewable exhaustion; the mine founds itself) →
MIGRATION (conserved flows) → TRADE (shipments, commodity flow, entrepôts, tolls) → SMUGGLING
(the refusal's shadow — soon housed in the underways) → the NAVAL LAYER (convoys, sea battles
by land rules, blockades-as-sieges) → the WAR LAYER (mobilization → deployment → siege/field →
occupation → homecoming; exhaustion scars; hard ceilings; conservation audited) → WAR/PEACE
REASONS (Blainey: self reads truth, the foe reads belief) → TREATIES (terms, strain,
revanchism) → POLITICS (blocs form on interests × people; conspiracies in autarchies) →
RELIGION (contest, piety, conversion, crisis) → INFORMATION STATECRAFT (lies priced against
credibility) → BELIEF RECONCILE (rumor → banded belief, lagged, fogged, distance-priced) →
MOMENTUM (public acts deposit; cliffs by temperament; cracks priced, off-ramps honored) →
the TEMPO GOVERNOR (drama budgeted, starvation-weighted) → PROPOSAL ROUTING (autonomy modes;
the DM's word) → the DISPLAY FOLDS (news, chronicle, receipts).

## 5. THE LAWS — the constitution and its derived statutes

**Constitutional:** same-seed byte-identity · dormancy/additivity · the premium seam ·
endogeneity (the party observes) · the first-paint ratchet · bold-over-safe within the rest.

**Statutes of the simulation:** the loaded-dice law (§H) · the counterpart criterion (every
mechanism forceable, every verb an organic twin, force ≡ organic) · the facet law (custom
content first-class in every coherence) · the unification law (both signs, one system —
booms/busts, commitment/reconsideration, grudge/gratitude, dark/bright misreadings) ·
conservation · scarcity-as-law (corruption assets, reframe transitions — story-grade things

relic-ruin scarcity · the one-cycle law (calamity and reconstruction are one system) ·

scaled collective memory; contracts never scale, memories do) · the locality scaling law
(per-settlement density is realm-size-invariant; budgets scale √N; eviction is receipted) ·
facts-frozen-meaning-derived (D7) · and the display-side laws: the immersion law, the
dissociation test, the diegetic register (engine vocabulary structurally cannot reach the
player's fiction).

## 6. THE EPISTEMIC ARCHITECTURE — the three-layer reality

The ontology's deepest structural choice: **reality exists three times.**

1. **TRUTH** — the engine's ground state. The DM's birthright.
2. **BELIEF** — per-observer: what each court thinks is true, fed by rumors through lag, fog,
   distance (D1), and credibility; wrong in both directions; and — with D7 — beliefs about
   MOTIVES, not just facts. Decisions consume beliefs, never truth. Wars begin in misjudgment;
   alliances can too (the bright misreading lane, rare by negativity bias).
3. **PROJECTION** — per-audience renderings: player-safe, public, anon — fail-closed,
   twin-enforced, structurally unable to leak the other layers.

The irony reads live in the gaps: fact-divergence (what they believe vs what is) and, with D7,
motive-divergence (why they think it happened vs why it did) — surfaced to the DM as story.

## 7. THE COVENANT STRATUM — the DM interface

Operations (the only write path) → validation (typed vetoes, no op type = no effect) →
proposals → the DM's word (approve / edit / dismiss, with residue-strip byte-equivalence for
dismissals) → application → receipts. The docket is mutable until consumed; the forecast is
preview ≡ apply, pinned to the byte; refusals are always visible; whispers teach as world
furniture; the glossary derives from code and cannot drift. The AI layer (designed, S1–S7)
enters as a SECOND CLIENT of this same stratum — a compiler to operations, never an author of
state — inheriting every rail above by construction.

## 8. THE MORTALITY WEB — every lifecycle in the ontology

Settlements: birth (satellite) → growth → charter-pending → promotion, or starvation →
dispersal → death; terminal death after dwell; remnants forever unless resettled; relic ruins
earned. Institutions: founded (rolled, forced, gap-detected — the mine and the warren found
themselves) → upgraded → subsumed → destroyed. Seats: succession rerolls psychology. Resources:
latent → struck → productive → declining → depleted → removed (nonrenewables) or recovering
(renewables). Treaties: minted → strained → frayed → expired/defected → revanchism. Wars:
reasons → mobilization → campaign → exhaustion → peace → the peace's own decay. Grudges:
minted → memory-scaled decay → reframed (D7) → reconciled or hardened. Beliefs: seeded →
reinforced/contradicted → cracked. Booms: sourced → built → flourishing → cooled or bust.
Everything that lives in this world can die, and everything that dies leaves a receipt.

## 9. THE META-OBSERVATION

The ontology describes the product, and it also describes the process that built it: the
codebase itself runs on frozen facts (commits), conditional ledgers (the playbook), typed
receipts (gate numbers), conservation (budgets that only ratchet), visibility seams (owner-
gated decisions), and legible causality (every change carries its reason). One philosophy,
two instances. The world explains itself because the system that made it insists on
explaining itself.
