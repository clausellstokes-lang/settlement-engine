   never a command spec through C3's `execute` (the fold §3.1.20; OD-FC-38) — becoming a

   settlement; at peace, edit mode on the war tab carries no command center — rendered
   as in-fiction absence, the C13 idiom.

   render in-fiction absence until lit; ONE view model feeds tab AND PDF; the da-cured
   chokepoints (C15) are consumed from birth.
5. **The seal suppresses the resolution arm ONLY (§128.2):** everything else keeps
   simulating — the famine keeps starving, the siege keeps choking trade, beliefs keep
   spreading. Never a settlement freeze.
6. **Clamped floors (§128.3):** a sealed stressor's TERMINAL arms clamp at floors derived
   by delegation (§42/§43) — the seal preserves the suffering, never completes it.
7. **The dyadic clause (§128.4):** where a stressor has participants, the seal holds the
   WHOLE relationship in stasis — the attacker's commitment persists at its own clamped
   floor. Parties outside the stressor stay fully organic.
8. **The besieger union (§128a):** settlement-faction army OR external authored host —
   the host a MENACE (MN-0's record: name, might band; setting-agnostic buckets, never a
   simulated character; no `disposition` — a menace has no mind, MN6), no faction economy
   behind it. The besieged side simulates identically: the machinery reads THE SIEGE,
   not the besieger's pedigree. Unsealed menace sieges resolve organically on MN-2's
   resolver against the might band; a sealed one IS authored state — the dyadic clause
   holds it trivially.
9. **Exclusivity absolute + the conversion valve (§128b, §129.1):** a committed army is
   committed; the seal never exempts it. On a sealed two-party siege, edit mode offers
   ONE sanctioned action — COMMIT THE FORCE: the army converts to the §128a external
   host, a MENACE minted from its current strength band (MN §5.6); the siege continues
   identically; the settlement books a recorded, provenance-carried loss; capacity
   frees. v1 converts the WHOLE force; valve available ONLY with the seal; unsealing a
   converted siege leaves the menace authored. The valve GENERALIZES (§129.1) to any
   seal that locks a committed resource forever; seals that lock no resource (a famine)
   need no valve.
10. **The two-hat law (§129.2–3):** every console verb declares its hat. ORDER (default)
