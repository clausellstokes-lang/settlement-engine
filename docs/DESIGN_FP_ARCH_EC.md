### 3a Goods

contradicted).

its existing presence substrate and its one conserved stock layer: a closed
five-rung condition per (settlement, good) with typed pressures, a posture-

declared chain graph, a derived trade stance per pair, a derived grade profile
with a minimum specification on procurement classes, and structural verdicts

law (§776) and the product thesis (a DM cohesion engine, not a quantitative
economy) forbid absolute truth and make it worthless at the table; closed
bands because finite semantics is constitutional and a float that reaches a
surface or a prompt is a defect class this estate has paid for; finite,

propagation along data because the chain graph already exists three times
and a fourth encoding would be the bug; a profile for grade because the
only sentence a DM reads is "the court wants what the smithy cannot make";
stances because an embargo that moves nothing is a lie the Herald tells;
receipts first because a condition that cannot say why does not exist
(SPINE §1.6).

owner law and useless to a DM who sets coin. (2) Event-to-price modifiers —
a war scalar explains nothing and ratchets everything; the tree already
refuses it. (3) A 0–100 index substrate — a second `causalState` with
twenty-two writers, each owing the twelve requirements; the brief's own
anchors already exist. (4) A capacity/utilization matrix — hard-sim shape,
no DM sentence, Law One. (5) A quality inventory split — doubles the
conservation surface for a phrase. (6) A market object with clearing —
T1/FP-TRADE §4; the house, the stance and the smuggle channel are the
topology. (7) Leaving TR beside EC — two compiled volumes over one writer
family.
