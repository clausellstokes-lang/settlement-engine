## THE READER'S GUIDE

1. WHAT THIS IS. A menace is ONE generic hostile presence from outside the settlement system that the DM alone names, places, sizes and sends (MN1, §2). The engine never asks what it is and gives it no mind; the world answers it with the machinery the towns already have — the crisis it drives, the danger on the roads, readiness, report, relief and common cause (§4) — and its sieges are heard on the week (§5, §7).
2. ITS ONE OWNER. **What each word means for THE MENACE** — every clause already true of the design (OD-MN-37). THE WORLD HOSTS THE CAMPAIGN — a menace has no plot, no agenda toward the party and no quest to give; it is setting and consequence on the DM's table, a backdrop until the DM moves it (MN1, MN6). PURPOSE ONLY BY THE DM — a menace goes where the DM's COURSE sends it and nowhere else; left alone it `stays` (or `wanders` within one hop of where the DM set it roaming); the engine never gives it a target, a goal or a grudge. DM-AUTHORED ONLY, NEVER EMERGENT — MN12: the engine never spawns a menace; the only two births are the DM's `RAISE_MENACE` and the DM's COMMIT THE FORCE (§10 A); promoting ambient brigandage into a named menace is the DM's act (the adoption at raise), and dens made by engine-born brigand bands are THE SITES' and WC-12's, never a menace (ST15). MACRO OVER MICRO — a menace is ONE record whose effects ride the numbers the world already keeps (the town's crisis, the road's danger, readiness, belief); no skirmish-by-skirmish ledger, no casualty roll per raid. MEDIEVAL FOR MILLENNIA — over any span a menace stays the kind of thing the DM named; its might moves only by wear and the DM's hand. NO ENGINE MORTALITY CLOCK — a menace has no lifespan: it wanes when beaten and ends only by the DM's `MENACE_STATE end`. THE SEA AND THE SEASONS — in the books and in the code (§6).

**The answer in one paragraph.** A menace is one thing: a hostile presence the DM places in the world that belongs to no settlement. The DM gives it a name in the DM's own words, says which country or road it is in, how mighty it is, and what it does there — stays and raids, lays siege to a town, goes somewhere, or roams — and that is all the engine is ever told. The engine never asks what it is. It has no mind: it does not choose, trade, believe or breed. It moves only on the course the DM set; it presses the towns it is among through the crisis machinery those towns already have; the roads near it grow dangerous; its news travels from the towns that saw it; and the courts that hear of it arm, send help or keep it home, and find common cause with neighbours they used to ignore. It can be worn down and fall back, and a menace beaten below its last strength wanes until the DM ends it. When it stands at a town's walls, every week of a week-by-week advance is a hearing: the DM may let the engine decide, tilt it, or give the verdict — and only the DM can let a town fall to it. Seen from the table it is unpredictable; asked "why", every step has its reason.

## §1 THE LAWS (MN1–MN15)

**MN1 — ONE KIND; THE STORY IS THE DM'S.** A menace is one generic kind. The engine never asks, stores or infers what it is. The DM's whole interface is a NAME and three choices — where, how mighty, and what it does there (§2). There is no type list, no motive, no behaviour catalogue, and no rule anywhere reads the name.

**MN2 — THE NAME IS QUOTED, NEVER PARSED.** The DM's words appear in narration exactly as written (the §934.36 flavour tier); no rule, classifier, regex or AI reads meaning from them. A blank name reads "an unnamed menace". The name rides only fields no rule reads: the record's `name`, the driven stressor's `originContext.attackerLabel` (the landed display path), the Herald's and the rumor's `menaceName`. It never rides a stressor's `label` or `name` and never an untyped threat label. `conditionPromotion.js :: archetypeForStressor` tests `${type} ${name} ${label}` first-match with the plague rule first, so a driven stressor labelled "the Plague Riders" would promote as a PLAGUE; the driven stressors therefore carry a fixed neutral label from MN's own table, pinned to promote exactly as their type does (§3.2).

**MN3 — ON THE MAP, FULL CONSEQUENCE; BEYOND IT, A PHANTOM.** A menace is ON-STAGE: the besieged town's reads continue as for any siege — the siege row on the war tab and in seal 6 (`liveSieges`' menace arm), the `siege` stressor the town already knows, the weekly hearing — and the world answers in full. A force beyond the map stays a PHANTOM under §934.43 (home procedures and a record). The two never merge. CN cn-5's "external authored host" IS a menace: MN-0 and MN-2 supersede cn-5's Produces 1–2, its flag `externalBesiegersEnabled` and its `disposition` bucket (dropped — the DM's story, MN1); §128b's conversion valve — the DM's COMMIT THE FORCE on a sealed siege — converts the whole besieging army into a menace (OD-MN-10). "Simulates identically" is honoured where the besieged side is read — the siege surfaces, the stressor and the hearing; the war verdict itself is not run for a besieger with no army (MN-2 runs the war's capacity CORE on the menace's might and the town's home defence — never the war's feasibility gate, which never rolls for a lone besieger), and the readers that never consult `liveSieges` (embattlement, the chooser, relief) take the menace through MN's own seams (§4).

**MN4 — IT DRIVES THE ONE CRISIS TRUTH, THROUGH ITS OWN RECORDS.** On every town it presses, the menace drives the stressor the world already knows — `monster_raider_pressure` while it raids, `siege` while it stands at the walls — as ITS OWN stressor record: an explicit id `world_stressor.<type>.<originSettlementId>.menace.<menaceId>` (idFor passes an explicit id through), stamped `originContext = { variant: 'menace', menaceId, attackerLabel: <the name>, attackerSettlementId: null, sponsorSettlementId: null }`, listed in the record's `drives`. Each record is born with its own EPISODIC policy (`durationPolicy: 'episodic'` on the record, the landed per-record idiom — a menace's siege is never left structural). While the menace presses it, the stressor is HELD: it is not rolled for resolution, it never falls below the menace's own severity (`MIGHT_SEVERITY[might]`; decay above that floor proceeds, so nothing ratchets), it does not spread, and it does not ESCALATE — its severity is the menace's might, which is the DM's, so the held record sits at that severity (or at the spent floor, §4.1) for as long as it is held. The fence covers EVERY held record: at strong and formidable might the escalation arm would mint a DM PROPOSAL ("… is deepening") on a fact the weekly siege hearing already holds — one fact, one hearing (EXECUTED, `cure/D-MN-r2.md` §1(a)). When the menace leaves, is stilled, wanes or is ended — or the flags go dark — the hold drops, the record keeps its id, and it runs its episodic life to its end by its own type's counterforce, leaving its own residue (abandoned roads, militia burden, fearful hamlets); the release is receipted with the menace's event (§3.2). The birth is a DM-authored `consequence` birth, canon by construction, registered in the drama governor and never governed; while it presses, the governor COUNTS its one held record as one live arc, and its released tails not at all (§4.1). Migration, faith disorder, conditions and the stressor's counterforce already read the stressor. A menace never mints a parallel crisis.

**MN6 — IT HAS NO MIND AND IS NOT PEOPLE.** No deciders, no economy, no faction, no belief of its own, no population, no census credit; the people ledger's conservation (`peopleLedger.js :: CONSERVATION_ENDPOINTS`) is untouched; the deaths it causes are ordinary sinks; it can never be "absorbed" into a town — and it holds no people of its own: a gang that goes over to it (the `enlists` course, §6) leaves the people count by the ledger's DM sink, never by the ledger's `enlist` event, which credits a unit (§6). **MN8 — ITS SIEGES ARE HEARD; THE ENGINE NEVER SACKS AND NEVER RAZES.** A menace at a town's walls is a STANDING engagement (`menace_siege`) on the registry's one siege vocabulary `holds · falls · relieved · lifted`. **MN9 — THE DM HOLDS BOTH HATS (§129), THROUGH ONE DOOR.** Four realm verbs (§2), each ≤ four dials, each a realm-manifest row (J-EM-2), each a proposal whose `targetSaveId` is a REAL settlement — the menace's anchor — with the menace riding a subject dial. Every verb is premium (the simulator is gated for premium).

**MN10 — A MENACE CAN LOSE, AND THEN IT WANES.** It loses a band of might when a siege is lifted or relieved — including a siege that wears itself out (a season at the walls with no breach lifts by exhaustion) — one or two when an army beats it in the field, and one when the towns it raids hold it off for a season. Below `thin` it WANES: the record stays on the ledger pressing nothing until the DM ends it. The DM may HOLD its might (worn by nothing).

**MN11 — A STRIKE IS NOT A PRESENCE.** A calamity is a strike: once, then its tail. A menace is a presence: it stays, presses, can be fought, moves and can lose. A "beast wave" the DM wants as a single blow is a calamity; as a host that lingers on the road, a menace. They share the bounded arithmetic (`resolvePopulationLoss`, `selectStrikeTargets`), never the calamity record: a breach is never stamped "Great Calamity", never resets the natural cooldown and never puts the ruler under a disaster-response crisis. ## §5 THE SIEGE (MN-2)

### 5.1 How a siege begins and what it is

A menace whose course is `besieges X` and which reaches X's country (or is raised with posture `besieges`) stands `at_the_walls` the next tick: its record gains `siege = { targetId: X, sinceTick, quietWeeks: 0 }` and, if this is the menace's first press of X in this presence, opens `pressTally[X]` with `headAtStart` = X's head count (§4.1); it drives `siege` at X; `liveSieges` shows the siege (§5.7); `worldConditions.js` gains the predicate `menaceAtWalls` (L10(b)(i): true in a real world the tick a record stands at that town's walls); each week of a week-by-week advance is a `menace_siege` hearing (§7).

### 5.2 The weekly verdict — the one siege arithmetic, one vocabulary

A menace is always one besieger, and the war's gate never lets a lone attacker below about 92 % of the defence roll (`PLAUSIBLE_FLOOR` 0.78 × `HOME_GROUND_MULTIPLIER` 1.18) and returns before `SIEGE_MAX_AGE`, so the war's function would hold a modest menace for ever, and relief could push a liftable siege into that band; MN-2 therefore reads `SIEGE_VERDICT_BANDS` on might against `homeDefense` directly, with the exhaustion clock as its age ceiling. MN-2's resolver `menaceSiege.js :: resolveMenaceSiegeWeek` runs the war's capacity CORE, not the war's function: `logOdds = SIEGE_CAPACITY_K · (MIGHT_LATENT[might] − (homeDefense(target) + reliefSent)) − SIEGE_CAPACITY_HOLD_BIAS` — MIGHT_LATENT (DRAFT, UNMEASURED) is each band's midpoint on the 0..100 latent scale, 13 · 34 · 51 · 69 · 89; `homeDefense` comes from `warCapacityReads.js :: buildCapacityLookup(snapshot, worldState.deployments)`; `reliefSent` = Σ (`homeDefense` − `siteDefense`)(reliever) × `ALLY_RELIEF_FRACTION` over the card's `sent` lines — the reliever's FIELD FORCE, `computeAllyRelief`'s per-ally term (`warCapacityReads.js`: "`relief += (Number(capacityFor(ally)?.homeDefense) || 0) * ALLY_RELIEF_FRACTION;`") as THE SITES' ST10 amends it ("relief SUBTRACTS it"), because a reliever's ready keep stays at its own walls and neither marches nor is drawn (ST10), while the BESIEGED town's `homeDefense` keeps its own keep's `siteDefense` (the keep defends the walls it stands on); `siteDefense` is 0 wherever THE SITES is dark, so the term is the landed one there; PL's liege share (PL §4.3), PS's border defender (OD-PS-38) and MN's relief read one field force — and `pFall = logistic(logOdds)`. ONE keyed draw `u` per menace and week, `rng.fork(\`siege:${stablePart(menaceId)}:${stablePart(targetId)}:${tick}\`)` under the stage's fork (keyed by the MENACE, so two menaces at one town draw independently), moved by the declared biases (below) to `u′ = clamp01(u + clampShift(u × m + tilt − u))` — `m` the paragon lean's multiplier (1 when none), `tilt` the DM's draw bias, the total shift clamped to ±0.20; `falls = u′ < pFall`; the band by the war's own margin — a fall with `(pFall − u′) > SIEGE_BAND_MARGIN` is `decisive_success`, else `costly_success`; a hold with `(u′ − pFall) < SIEGE_BAND_MARGIN` is `narrow_fail`, else `decisive_fail` — so the band words are exactly `SIEGE_VERDICT_BANDS`'. The war host EXPORTS what MN reuses — `SIEGE_CAPACITY_K` (0.16), `SIEGE_CAPACITY_HOLD_BIAS` (3) and the margin promoted from the literal `0.18` to `SIEGE_BAND_MARGIN` (the same value; the war's behaviour byte-identical) — and nothing else changes there.
