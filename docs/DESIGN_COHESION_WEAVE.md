## A. TRADE AS ROBUST AS GENEROSITY (the owner's parity directive)
Trade has deep physics (M2/M6) but thin RECEIPTED MOTIVE. The parity fix mirrors the causal
layer: typed, receipted reasons on route FORMATION and SEVERANCE, consumed by the same
causal-contributor idiom.
- WHY ROUTES OPEN: complementarity (my surplus band × your deficit band — the natural pair,
  named in the receipt); SECURITY OF SUPPLY (the big new reason: a fragility scare — the M2
  <2-independent-sources flag — drives REDUNDANCY-SEEKING route formation; near-starvation
  teaches diversification, receipted "after the winter of the cut pass, Thornwall courted a
  second grain road"); diplomacy (the E1 overture); profit (entrepôt/appetite); the acquisition
  ladder rung 1 (§D). WHY THEY CLOSE beyond war: value divergence (the conscience/tolerance
  embargo — exists), better alternatives (failover — exists), risk re-pricing (M6c — exists),
  treaty exclusivity (§11). All get WHY receipts.
- TRADE POSTURE by character (one named modulator, read by M6c appetite + formation): mercantile-
  open / autarkic-suspicious / predatory (dumping, denial — the evil-lean instrument set).
- TRADE WRITES BACK (already mostly wired — the parity is naming it): normalization drifts
  values, dependence creates leverage, flows warm labels (G1d), wealth signals travel (rumor).
The deliverable is small: reason-receipts on formation/severance + the redundancy-seeking
trigger + the posture modulator. Trade then explains itself exactly as war, peace, and
generosity do.

## B. THE FAITH × ALIGNMENT QUADRANTS (distinct axes, cross-cutting by design)
Same-patron vs rival-patron × kindred vs opposite alignment = four named postures, ALREADY
COMPUTABLE (cultureDistance's faith axis + alignment proximity + relationshipCompatibility) —
the weave makes the quadrant ONE NAMED MODULATOR read consistently everywhere:
- **SAME DEITY + KINDRED ALIGNMENT — "brothers":** max bond baseline; generosity flows easiest;
  natural coalitions; wars between them are CIVIL-WAR-flavored — rare, tragic, with the
  strongest temple-mediation peace pressure (the shared priesthood demands it).
- **SAME DEITY + OPPOSITE ALIGNMENT — "the schism axis":** co-religionists who despise each
  other's ways. Generosity is guilt-driven but grudging; wars are DOCTRINAL (who speaks for the
  god?) and feed the intra-faith contest/heresy machinery; temple mediation is CONFLICTED — the
  shared clergy splits, and the split is legible (council/temple friction receipts).
- **RIVAL DEITY + KINDRED ALIGNMENT — "the respectable rival":** kin in temperament, rivals in
  heaven. Trade flows fine (conduct values align); generosity runs on strategy and history;
  wars are PROFESSIONAL — limited, low-atrocity, ended by term-driven peaces; missionary access
  is the sensitive prize (§15 ranks it high exactly here).
- **RIVAL DEITY + OPPOSITE ALIGNMENT — "the natural enemy":** trade thin (conscience embargoes),
  generosity's gate almost never opens, war casus STACK (faith + antipathy), atrocity thresholds
  sit lower, peaces come harsh and brittle (§5 revanchism runs hot).
- **CROSS-PRESSURE — the peacemaker mechanic:** a settlement whose faith-brother fights its
  alignment-kin is TORN — coalition reluctance, mediation impulse. Cross-cutting cleavages
  produce neutral brokers (straight from political science, free from reads we have): the
  natural mediator of any war is the neighbor pulled both ways, and the engine can NAME it
  ("Greyharbor, kin to both courts, carried the terms").
Consumers of the quadrant: generosity bond/conscience weights, trade normalization rate, war
casus weights (holy war vs mere rivalry), peace term selection + mediation strength, coalition
formation, atrocity thresholds.

## C. STRUCTURAL LENSES (economic base × ruling power — who decides, what they fear, how they act)
- ECONOMIC BASE (from economicState: extraction / agrarian / trade-hub / craft / mixed) sets
  NEEDS (imports), FEARS (mine depletion vs harvest failure vs route cuts — each base's
  nightmare weights its withhold/war/peace terms differently), COVETS (the §15 prize ranking
  reads the appraiser's base), and WAR STYLE (agrarian powers field seasonal armies — the
  harvest imperative bites them hardest; trade-hubs fight route wars and fold to embargoes;
  extraction towns turtle their chokepoints).
- RULING POWER (powerStructure) sets WHO DECIDES and HOW: the coalition weights (designed in
  E1/§4) plus DECISION TEMPO AND TEXTURE — autocrat: fast, temper-driven (one W0 read, high
  variance); council: slow, averaged, moderate (weights blended, hysteresis widened);
  theocracy: doctrine-driven (deity stance amplified, contest reasons weighted up); merchant
  league: profit-weighted (economic reasons up, grievance down — they'll trade with anyone);
  criminal seat: leverage-weighted, treaty-credibility DISCOUNTED. Each structure also has its
  LEGITIMACY NERVE (councils fear unrest, lords fear coups, theocracies fear heresy) — the
  domestic terms in every chooser read the right nerve. TREATY CREDIBILITY becomes structure-
  flavored: the compliance-reliability priors read the ruling power (a league honors contracts;
  a warlord honors strength) — priced into §12 monitoring and §1 reliability discounts.
All lens values derive from EXISTING generation state — zero new authored data; one lens table.

## D. THE ACQUISITION LADDER (the depleted metropolis — resource pressure seeks outlets)
A standing RESOURCE PRESSURE (consumption vs production+imports, banded, persisting) climbs an
escalation ladder, each rung a different subsystem, entry point and climb speed set by character:
1. **TRADE**: buy more — need-premium rises (M6c), new routes form (§A), premium prices paid
   (the numeric-prices read shows it: "grain sells dear to the hungry city").
2. **TREATY/ALLY**: secure preferential access — and here the peace engine GENERALIZES:
  **the §11 terms vocabulary works in PEACETIME** — commercial pacts (resource-share,
  exclusivity, toll relief) negotiated from mutual benefit or asymmetric leverage, no war
  required. The treaty machinery is the contract machinery; war-endings were just its
  harshest use case.
3. **COERCION**: leverage short of war — embargo, toll pressure, purchase denial, supply-web
   squeeze (the W-DOCTRINE's bloodless instruments).
4. **CONQUEST**: the economic-hunger casus fires (§14.1) — take the mines, hold them (and pay
   occupation's burden).
CHARACTER SETS THE CLIMB: good-aligned starts at 1 and stalls at 2-3; evil skips rungs; merchant
leagues live at 2 forever; warlords start at 4; DESPERATION (the existential band) overrides
everything; and BELIEFS gate it all — a metropolis that (rightly or wrongly) believes trade
will fail escalates early, receipted.
THE COUNTERWEIGHTS (all free): the hungry metropolis is LEGIBLE — its buying pressure moves
prices and travels the rumor network, so everyone sees it coming; its SUPPLIERS GAIN LEVERAGE
(the village that feeds a metropolis becomes a diplomatic actor with terms of its own);
prospective targets coalition-up (the threat/moral-outrage casus); and conquest's prize decays
under occupation burden (garrisons eat what the mines yield). Imperialism with all brakes
attached — the engine's biggest-canvas story arc, from a band going red in one city's ledger.

## F. THE GAP-HUNT INTEGRATIONS (owner-ruled 2026-07-14)
1. **THE PACING GOVERNOR** — promoted to its own design (DESIGN_PACING_GOVERNOR.md); builds
   FIRST (E0). Every engine below registers its spontaneous-birth classes with it.
2. **DYNASTIC COMPACTS = FLAVOR/PROSE (owner ruling).** Mechanically a marriage alliance IS an
   alliance/treaty — already bucketed under the relationship types. The dynastic layer is a
   DISPLAY/VOICE variant: when an alliance or treaty forms between lordly/dynastic ruling
   structures (the lens table knows), the receipt/crier/treaty prose MAY render it as a compact
   sealed "by a marriage between the houses" — HOUSE-LEVEL PHRASING ONLY, never naming
   individuals (the named-fate law: prose must not mint canon the sim could contradict). The
   conjunction-ladder/newsVoice authoring pattern; zero new state; quadrant- and lens-aware
   (theocracies seal with rites, leagues with charters, lords with weddings — the same treaty,
   three voices). A DM who wants REAL dynastic play authors it as canon events — the engine
   renders texture, the DM owns persons.
3. **MULTI-PARTY COMPACTS**: the treaties ledger admits parties > 2 — standing leagues
   (defensive pacts, trade leagues) as PEACETIME multi-party treaties: shared obligations,
   a lead seat (founder or strongest), join/leave as receipted events with the full reaction
   ledger (leaving a defensive league before a storm = the separate-exit betrayal math). The
   §11 vocabulary + §12 compliance apply per-member. No new machinery beyond the party-count.
4. **DECLARED NEUTRALITY**: a stance term in the vocabulary — a settlement declares (or a
   treaty imposes) neutrality toward a conflict: it trades with both sides (the profiteer's
   posture, legible), belligerents may PRESSURE it (trading-with-the-enemy grievance; blockade
   tension against neutral caravans = an escalation seam), and VIOLATING declared neutrality
   (arming a side, passage grants) is a casus with the oathbreach machinery behind it. Composes
   with the cross-pressure peacemaker: the torn neighbor often declares, then mediates.
5. **COVERT STATECRAFT** (the acquisition ladder gains rung 2.5; the instruments family):
   fund-dissidents (feeds the EXISTING faction-competition/coup-pressure lanes in the target),
   sedition (legitimacy erosion, slow, deniable), supply-web sabotage (severance without
   armies — the doctrine's night variant), counter-intelligence (the warding-vs-scrying backlog
   item finds its home). ALL belief/compromise-mediated: deniability = the sponsor's identity
   travels as a RUMOR with lineage (discovery odds scale with the target's counter-intel +
   the sponsor's tradecraft = alignment/archetype read); DISCOVERY = scandal + casus + moral
   drift + the reputation broadcast. AGGREGATE ONLY: destabilization, never assassination —
   named fates remain the DM's. Sequencing: designs with W-DOCTRINE's build wave.
6. **GENERATIONAL MEMORY (one rule)**: grievance/obligation records carry a holder tag —
   SEAT-HELD (minted by rulers' choices: treaties, betrayals at court) vs PEOPLE-HELD (minted
   by suffering: sacks, famines, occupations). Succession softens seat-held records sharply
   (the new ruler inherits the paper, not the hate) and people-held records not at all (the
   town remembers its burned quarter regardless of who wears the chain). One tag, one decay
   modifier, deep texture — and it makes succession-peace (§14.2) mechanically honest.

### F.3b THE UNNAMED EMPIRE (owner refinement: the hegemonic counterpart — emergent, never named)
An empire is A PATTERN, NEVER AN ENTITY. The engine mints no empire object, no imperial ledger,
no name — hegemony is a DERIVED READ over the existing topology: a center C holding ≥K
subordinate ties (vassalized, tributary, compelled-ally, puppet-seat, garrison-protectorate —
all §11 products). The read recomputes each tick from edges + treaties; when the edges dissolve,
the "empire" simply stops matching — no lifecycle, no death event, no persistence, no undo
burden. WHY NEVER NAMED (four reasons, each load-bearing): (1) an entity class would demand
state/lifecycle/undo machinery for something the topology already expresses; (2) an unnamed
pattern FRAYS GRACEFULLY edge-by-edge — historically how hegemonies actually end; (3) NAMING
POLITIES IS DM TERRITORY (the named-fate law's sibling): the engine builds the bones, THE DM
BAPTIZES — a DM-authored canon label ("The Thornwall Hegemony") is then adopted by every display
surface, making christening a meaningful DM act; (4) the sub-century horizon wants empires as
tides within decades, not thousand-year named institutions.
RISE (all existing): the acquisition ladder + W-DOCTRINE + accumulated treaty terms concentrate
subordinate ties on one center. STRAIN: every §12 compliance strain, people-held grievances,
distance-scaled monitoring fog (a large hegemony is HARD TO SEE ACROSS — cheating scales with
size; the compelled-intel terms exist precisely because centers know this). BREAKS: defection
windows (compelled allies fall away when the center stumbles — the crumbling-hegemon cascade),
tribute defaults, occupation revolts, and — the quiet killer — TERM LIMITS: an empire of
treaties EXPIRES on schedule; hegemony is maintenance-hungry by law, each renewal renegotiated
at the CURRENT ratio (a weakened center renews on worse terms or not at all). SUCCESSIONS: the
generational-memory rule makes the center's death the classic fracture — seat-held ties soften
sharply on succession (the new ruler inherits paper, not loyalty), so a weak heir opens the
defection window REALM-WIDE: the empire-dies-with-the-emperor arc, fully emergent from rules
already written. SELF-LIMITING: hegemony IS the common threat — the §14.2 realignment mechanic
makes every overgrown center manufacture its own encircling coalition ("the rest" unite against
whoever grows past the margin). Empires sow their encirclement; the engine needs no anti-empire
rule because the realignment read is one. LEGIBILITY: the dashboard/map renders the cluster as
spheres; the brief counts it ("seven towns pay tribute to Thornwall; the eighth arms"); the
chronicle narrates rise and fray in plain fiction — descriptive always ("Thornwall and its
tributaries"), christened only by the DM. Pins: the pattern-read derives with zero persisted
state; a center succession opens measurable defection; the encirclement coalition forms against
a ≥K center under the same seed; term expiry without renewal shrinks the cluster.

## G. NAMED TIES AS PRIVILEGED EDGES (owner-commissioned 2026-07-14 — cross-settlement NPC relationships in the five-domain weave)

### G.0 The one law
The sim already carries cross-settlement NPC relationships (the inter-settlement relationship
links maintained by the library's rename/persist cascade, plus the Lane-2 canon-relationship
verbs the DM declares). Their treatment in the weave is a single sentence: **named ties are
privileged edges and bounded modulators — never actors.** The constitutional line (no named
character's fate is ever resolved by the machine) does the design work: ties cannot DRIVE the
five domains, but they are the perfect instrument for TEXTURING them — they carry information,
dampen wars, select mediators, deepen gratitude, and route trade, all as small clamped weights
with the tie cited as the receipt.

### G.1 Per-domain hooks (each lands with its host wave)
- **INFORMATION (→ W-DOCTRINE, with DESIGN_INFORMATION_STATECRAFT):** a tie is a private
  channel — a low-latency, high-fidelity edge in the rumor network for that settlement pair
  ("word came through the miller's cousin at Thornwall"), bypassing road-hop staleness for what
  that household would plausibly know. Symmetrically it is the LEAK SURFACE: a settlement's
  secrecy posture (§2.2 of the statecraft doc) is degraded in proportion to its outbound tie
  density — kin talk across borders, and HIDE must price that honestly. Tie channels carry the
  NAMED party's credibility, not the court's.
- **WAR (→ W-PEACE/W-DOCTRINE):** cross-border ties generate war reluctance — a bounded,
  people-held term in the casus/appetite math (kin across the lines = desertion pressure,
  cold feet at the muster, merchants who won't provision the column). War STRAINS ties (a
  relationship-state transition on the tie's own ledger — strain, not death; the engine never
  kills the brother). Atrocities against a tied settlement multiply the grievance through the
  tie (it happened to THEIR kin — personal, people-held, slow to decay).
- **PEACE (→ W-PEACE):** the peace engine's cross-pressured mediator is GROUNDED here — the
  natural broker is the settlement whose named people bridge both courts, and mediator scoring
  reads bridging-tie density + the tied parties' credibility. Peace pressure accumulates through
  ties ("the courts' kin pressed for it" — a typed peace reason with named receipts). Marriage/
  hostage terms remain house-level prose per the standing ruling: the tie RECORDS the
  relationship the treaty created; the persons stay the storyteller's.
- **TRADE (→ NUMERIC PRICES + the §A trade parity lane):** routes preferentially FORM and
  PERSIST along kinship/partnership lines (diaspora economics — the cousin's warehouse is the
  beachhead), a small prior in route-formation EV and a stickiness term against route decay.
  Tie-linked pairs get a haggling-friction discount the price voice can cite ("old partners;
  the rate is kind").
- **GENEROSITY (→ E1, THE FIRST CONSUMER):** the engine's first law gates on relationship AND
  history — ties add MICRO-HISTORY: a people-held gratitude/grievance ledger at tie grain
  (your caravan master owes their harbormaster) that feeds the settlement-level bond as a
  bounded contribution. Generosity acts THROUGH a tie bind faster (the widow's-mite math knows
  the gift traveled a named road); refusals THROUGH a tie wound deeper (it was kin who was
  turned away).

### G.2 The guardrails (what keeps this constitutional and sane)
1. **Clamped aggregate:** total named-tie influence on any single decision is capped (house
   clamp idiom) — a dense web should visibly matter; a single friendship must never overturn
   strategy. The cap is a tuning rail candidate (display-band class), never auto-shifting sim
   math.
2. **State, never fate:** the engine mutates tie STATE only (warm/strained/dormant, each with
   decay clocks) — never existence, residence, life, or love. Creation and destruction of ties
   belong to generation, the DM's canon verbs, and prose-that-never-mints-canon.
3. **People-held by default:** tie effects tag people-held (succession does not launder a
   kin-grievance); the seat-held component exists only where the tie itself is institutional
   (guild charter partnerships).
4. **Receipts:** every tie contribution is a typed reason with the tie named — legibility is
   the point ("why did the war stall? — three houses straddle that border, and the levies
   dragged their feet").
5. **Endogeneity + party law:** DM-declared ties are inputs like any canon op; party-member
   relationships NEVER feed the math (the party observes; endogeneity law 4 applies to ties
   exactly as to everything else).
6. **Dormancy:** tie-modulation state nests under the existing ledger namespaces, absent ⇒
   byte-identical; lights per-domain with each host wave's gate.
