A surveyor’s dossier

Thornwall

Tier Town · Souls 2,400 · On the coast road; a tidal harbour

“A wall town that learned to trade with the sea it once feared.”

Arrival

You come to Thornwall by the coast road, and the wall shows first — grey stone the height of four men, salt-stained on its seaward face. The gate stands open in daylight; a toll-clerk keeps a ledger you are welcome to read and unwelcome to argue with. Beyond, the town falls toward a tidal harbour where the fishing fleet lies careened at low water and the warehouses smell of pitch and drying cod.

Active crisis — The harbour silts

A winter of storms has pushed the bar across the harbour mouth; deep-keeled ships now wait for the tide or unload offshore.

The hook. The council will pay well for a survey of the bar — and better for anyone who can move it.

Key figures

Alderman Coss Rell — Head of the merchant council. Reads every ledger; trusts none of them.

Mother Vane — Keeper of the sea-shrine. Buried three husbands to the tide; blesses the fleet anyway.

Dovey Ash — Harbour-guild boss. Would rather dredge than be dredged out of business.

Plot hooks
  1. The toll-clerk’s ledger shows a barge that pays the road toll but is never seen on the road.
  2. Mother Vane wants the old sea-shrine on the bar re-consecrated before the dredging disturbs it.
  3. A Redwater factor is quietly buying up silted-harbour warehouses at a loss — or an investment.
