> A face is LAWFUL unless it CONTRADICTS the record. "The card does not license it" is no longer a finding.
> Silence in the record is PERMISSION.

**Verdicts.** CONTRADICTION (a face breaching it states what the record or another surface on the same page
DENIES — survives as a refuter's finding, field named) · MODEL (floor 4, the engine's own positive model) ·
SCOPE (floor 3, product scope + deity doctrine) · STRUCK (it policed permission, tone or construction; now
licensed flavour) · SPLIT (kept half + struck half).

**How the re-cut bites these two sections.** The label traps divide cleanly in two. Where the engine prints
the label's engine meaning on a surface beside the prose (the roster, the safety panel, the stress banner, the
food label, the seat chip), reading the label at its dictionary sense makes the page contradict itself — that
is floor 1 and it keeps every tooth. Where the label is merely coarse, a proxy, a default, an else-arm or an
unread instrument column, the old bar said only "you have not been licensed to feel anything about this", and
that is exactly the bar the owner struck. The alias traps divide the same way at a finer grain: nearly every
row bundles ONE real contradiction (asserting a BODY, a BUILDING or a RECORD the roster or the holder card
denies) with a PRESCRIBED VOCABULARY ("the works", "the stores", "those tending the sick"). The body half
survives; the vocabulary half is struck outright — those prescriptions are the measured source of the flavour
collapse (168 distinct words against the corpus's 519), because they forced twelve renderings onto one
approved noun.

## WHAT THE STRIKES BUY (the owner's complaint, answered in this slice)

1. **The always-safe spelling column is gone.** Thirty-one alias rows prescribed one approved noun each
   ("the works", "the stores", "the approach", "those tending the sick", "the town's own reckoning"). Those
   prescriptions, not the contradictions, produced `country` once every 21 words. A writer now chooses the
   word and only owes the record a body, a building or a citation it does not deny.
2. **Materials come back.** A palisade is sharpened timber; a town wall is stone. Only the material SOURCE
   and a decaying wall stay refused (the chain and floor 4).
3. **People come back.** W22's blanket is struck: the unnamed reeve, clerk, collector, gate-keeper and
   quarter-master may act, hold keys, be resented, be avoided. Only a NAMED character's fate is refused.
4. **The absences stop being silences.** "No polity exists", "no camp model", "no ward model", "no meeting
   record" were absences, not denials. The crown, the camp outside the walls and the quarter are now free.
5. **What keeps its teeth is exactly what makes the page lie to itself**: a watch, a garrison, a wall, a
   granary, a church, a court, a market or a mill the roster denies; a label read at its dictionary sense
   where the engine prints its engine sense beside the prose; a citation to a holder the card calls
   unresolved; a number or a date.
