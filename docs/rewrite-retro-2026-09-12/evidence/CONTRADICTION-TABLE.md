## THE TEST, IN THE REFUTER'S OWN WORDS

You are not checking whether the record permits the sentence. You are checking whether the record **denies** it.

Read the face. Ask one question: **what would have to be false for this sentence to be true?** If the answer is a field, a flag, a band, a label or a roster row on this town's dossier — you have a finding, and you must name it: the field, its file and line, the face quoted, and which of the two is the record. If the answer is "nothing; the record simply does not say" — the face is lawful and you move on. **Silence is permission. "The card does not license it" is not a finding and never will be again.**

Four floors, and only four:

1. **NO SELF-CONTRADICTION ON THE DOSSIER.** Not the pool's own read — the whole town's record, and every other surface printed from it.
2. **NO INVENTED HISTORY, DATE, NUMBER OR ELAPSED COURSE** (THE PROMISE, constitutional). A seed is a starting world forever.
3. **PRODUCT SCOPE AND THE DEITY DOCTRINE** as ADDENDUM 15 amends it. The followers act; the god does not.
4. **THE ENGINE'S OWN POSITIVE MODEL.** Where the engine models a thing, prose may not deny it.

And one craft verdict, at the POOL grain and never at the face: **DULL** — a pool whose renderings collapse onto one construction or one vocabulary. Name the collapse.

The dossier is a set of PLOT HOOKS a game master reads to be immersed and changes at will. It is not a receipt anybody audits. A sentence that is unimpeachable and gives the reader nothing has failed at the only job it had — but that failure is DULL at the pool, never a finding at the face.

**Verdict tokens on a finding.** `[F1]` contradiction · `[F2]` promise · `[F3]` scope · `[F4]` model. (The chair mints **F2/PROMISE** here: two slices reported that with tokens for floors 1, 3 and 4 only, a refuter would read floor 2 as struck. Roughly forty rows' only survivor is floor 2.)
