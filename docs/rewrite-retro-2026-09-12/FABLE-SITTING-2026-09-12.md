## 1. THE VERDICT

**The re-cut is better, and it holds.** The direction — NON-CONTRADICTION, not LICENCE — is the correct diagnosis of a test that could not be satisfied and be interesting at once. Of 1,075 judgments the Opus chair made on 2026-09-12 (plus 62 appendix rows, 1,137 in all), **not one CONTRADICTION or MODEL verdict was wrong**; every kept half stands on a real field at a line that still reads as cited. **63 rows are REVERSED and 149 CHALLENGED; 925 stand.** The reversals sit almost entirely where the chair predicted an error would be invisible — struck halves and permission cells that hand back what the fold's own §F2 and §F4 rows refuse (a rate, a count, a duration, a comparative, a split purse) or a body noun the same archive keeps as a label trap on another desk. **None re-opens a block.** Every reversal returns as a row of `CONTRADICTION-TABLE.md` §V with its field and floor, and the two blocks landed since are re-refuted against those rows alone, as the record promised.

**The prose is better — CONFIRMED on both counts the owner asked about.** At an equal ~1,900-token budget the re-cut's first draft round uses **377 distinct words** (content types 349) against the licence test's **168** (143) and the shipped corpus's 519 (489); the per-pool content ratio doubled (median 0.34 → 0.61); 18 of 24 pools pass the pool-grain DULL test. Refuted face by face, the re-cut has **2 unconditional failures in 288 new faces** where the shipped rows had **27 in 78** (and the floor-2 disease that infected one shipped line in three is gone); the licence text had 0 in 312, and bought it with prose the owner rejected. A game master reading five pools side by side prefers the re-cut in five of five.

**Where the re-cut actually fails is not where the chair predicted.** The predicted failure mode — floor 2, aspect and rate — did not materialise (one modal course in 288 faces; no rate, durative or comparative). The real failure mode is **floor 1 by inference**: a body inferred into a key's silence — a muster ROLL, a barracks, a responder, a written note — that a `required: true` roster row or a same-tab machine sentence denies. Thirty-eight of the 288 faces are conditionally exposed that way (about 8 % of faces on a town-tier settlement; under 3 % at village). The cure is one line at the head of every card: the tier's required rows.

**And the re-cut was applied to the law but not to the machinery** (§4). That is the finding with the largest consequence, and it is why the owner's later word — *"without the machinery becoming the very obstacle for its completion"* — became ADDENDUM 18.
