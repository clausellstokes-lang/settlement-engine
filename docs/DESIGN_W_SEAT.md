## §0 · The mandate (verbatim intent)

1. **THE FOREIGN SEAT (§735.2):** an occupied or vassal settlement's ruling power has "an equal or greater counterpart in all decision-makings derived from the overlord or occupier." Occupation: the occupier's influence is "always just greater" than the legitimate ruler and can compete in coalitions against the ruler to the overlord's benefit; the occupier holds a mixed responsibility to cultivate and exploit. Vassalage: influence may be lower than an occupier's, scales with cultural, trade, and relationship closeness to the overlord, and remains influential.
2. **LEGITIMACY-SCALED UPHEAVAL (§736):** any attempted coup or rebellion has a scaling factor according to the legitimacy of the ruling powers.
3. **ANTICIPATED REACTION (§741):** each settlement's (or competing power's) risk assessment for any major decision considers how each relevant foreign power — or its counterpart seat in that settlement — will react.
4. Cohesion bindings from the same sitting: the occupied-economics spec is SHARED with W-COIN (A1.14's occupied-yields-zero rule; W-COIN-3's extraction flows are the seat's material legs); settlement-layer mapmaking is module-side and nothing here touches it.

## §3 · The architecture

**D1 · `foreignSeatOf(worldState, snapshot, settlementId)` — the one seat read.** Returns `null` or a derived `ForeignSeatView { regime: 'occupation'|'vassalage', patronSettlementId, weight01, band, postureBand, basis }`. Resolution order: (1) occupations ledger (regime `occupation`; grip from rung × (1−resistance)); (2) vassal relationship edge via the unified role resolver (regime `vassalage`); (3) treaty subordinating ties add a compliance-scaled floor (the hegemony orientation read). Scalers per the owner's three signals, all from existing homes: `cultureAffinity` (the §II.5-2 derived-similarity composite — NO new 11×11 table; heritage kinship stays un-modeled pending Q-S5), pairwise trade (confirmed channel strength ∨ flow-band index), and the verbatim `stableVassalage` blend. Band floors `dominant ≥0.7 · strong ≥0.45 · present ≥0.2` (PROVISIONAL, tuning-adjacent). One resolver answers the treaty-tie-without-edge question CONSISTENTLY: a subordinating tie alone yields at most `present` (influence without a compact is pressure, not a seat) — vetoable ruling.

**D2 · Primacy and weight at the decision spine.** Three integration edits serve everything:
- **`readWarSeatBooks` generalizes its book split**: the covert patron book becomes the precedent for an overt `foreignBook` — occupation: the foreign book takes the senior share with the legitimate seat's book still computed (competition is visible and narratable); vassalage: the foreign book's share = the D1 weight. One read; nine existing consumers (peace, coalition join/refuse, ratification ballots, envoy negotiation, sales) inherit the seat for free.
- **`authorityFor` gains the PRIMACY AXIS**: for occupied settlements, decisions in the typed OVERRIDE CLASS list (initial: war initiation & mobilization posture · taxation posture [the W-COIN seam] · trade policy/embargo · levy/garrison · treaty signature) resolve to the occupier's side or are vetoed — one edit at the choke serving all ~30 families; decisions OUTSIDE the class (faith, internal justice, culture) can only be pressured, never overridden (the imposed-cult idiom is the ceiling of force there). **Every exercised override burns**: a typed legitimacy price on the occupier's standing + a resistance bump — winning every argument loses the town.
- **The strategy chooser and other deterministic deciders** take bounded seat terms via the existing §H decision-weight kernel — the design's overt-foreign instance beside the ruling-bloc (overt-domestic) and corruption-web (covert-foreign) instances already sharing that kernel.

**D3 · The posture — cultivate/exploit becomes a CHOICE.** A new typed axis `posture ∈ {exploit, administer, cultivate}` CHOSEN by the occupier's court through the candidate machinery (authority-classed, seat-books-read, so a DM occupier-side lock works), persisted on the occupations record (Q-S2), absent ⇒ derived from the occupier's disposition. It modulates the EXISTING constants only: exploit → benefit up now, RESISTANCE_GROW up, legitimacy price up, suitability down (the wasting asset); cultivate → benefit down, occupier pays (W-COIN sink when lit), RESISTANCE_DECAY up, suitability up → the honest road to `vassalized` (occupation maturing into the cheaper regime — the arc the owner described). The ladder itself is untouched: posture tilts the classifier's inputs, never forks a second state machine. W-COIN-3's extraction fraction reads the posture band — seat, coin, and the committed prose become one system.

**D4 · Legitimacy-scaled upheaval — completing the three moments.** ATTEMPT: gate `mult+reason` rows extend `coupSpawnGate`/`rebellionGate`/`insurgencyGate` and the vassal `independencePressure` — scaled by the ruler's legitimacy band AND, per-archetype, by a small closed sensitivity table (autocrats brittle to crisis, councils to slow decline — tuning-docket constants); under occupation, rebellion risk reads `resistance` + posture (exploit raises, cultivate dampens — §736's exact words). SUCCESS: `foreignSeatAdj` as the fifth additive term in the coup clamp (the seat backs or resists a challenger; 0-when-dark), riding beside the already-live `govMultiplier` amplification. AFTERMATH: the reseed pulled by seat weight within the existing cause vocabulary (a coup under a dominant foreign seat seats a client, warmer to the patron, colder to the town); mint severities scale by the same read. The coupSpawnGate's occupation-null moves from the stressor spelling to THE occupied predicate (law §2.1).

**D5 · `anticipatedReactions` — the forecast read (§741).** One pure derivation, invoked ONLY for structurally-major decisions (`decisionTier`), over the BOUNDED relevant set (relationship edges + treaty parties + neighbors-by-graph + the foreign seat's patron). For each relevant power: a typed reaction from the CLOSED vocabulary `{approves, indifferent, protests, sanctions, casus_grade}`, computed from the DECIDER'S believed picture of that power (beliefMaps entry; dark/omniscient ⇒ ground truth verbatim — zero forks, the beliefMap fallback law), discounted by confidence. Consumed as bounded score terms at the chokepoints (chooser terms; a reaction row in the seat books; an escalation nudge at `authorityFor` for casus-grade forecasts). When a forecast CHANGES an outcome, the decision receipt names it ("held back, fearing X's answer"); when a court acts on a stale picture and the true reaction differs, the EXISTING misjudgment receipt machinery narrates it. K4 holds: each court's forecast is its own; nothing merges.

**D6 · Coalitions and the seat.** The occupier's roster faction gains honest bloc standing: its faction-states archetype routes to the `outsider/patron` END vocabulary (fixing the occupation→military fold — a one-line reroute with a declared-shift check), its bloc goals draw from `serve_patron/bind_external_patron`, and its conduct ties to the OVERLORD court's interest via the seat read. Outbidding, exposure, and dissolution machinery are inherited untouched.

**D7 · Liberation un-installs (SEAT-5 — a repair, not a feature).** The liberation outcomes gain the reversal half: remove the occupier faction row, strip `occupied/disarmed` modifiers, restore cut power by the recorded factors, seat the strongest legitimate faction via `transferRulingPower(cause:'liberation'…existing vocabulary permitting — else 'appointment' with a typed reason)`, and mint the scar as a TYPED condition/echo (not a stale roster). **D8 · Narration.** New kinds (seat override, posture change, seat-backed challenge, reaction-held decision, liberation restoration) pay the full V6-B3 bill; all scalar-free reader text; covert seat actions (a vassal overlord's quiet pressure) ride the covert/audience discipline; every kind routes explicitly in heraldRouting.

**D9 · Repression deployments (owner, in-sitting: "an allied or occupying or overlord army can send in a military unit to repress a rebellion or coup").** The census answer: coups YES today (the typed interventions channel, either side, strength-weighted into the verdict); vassal rebellions ABSTRACT ONLY (quash is relationship math — the military_protection channel is a promise with no army behind it); occupier-vs-insurgency STRUCTURAL ONLY (garrison suitability). D9 generalizes the interventions channel to all three: an allied/overlord/occupier court may deploy a REPRESSION FORCE against an active coup, rebellion, or insurgency — a physical army act (armyTransit, upkeep, a W-COIN sink when lit) recorded as a sided intervention row; its strength feeds the EXISTING resolution (coup: the adj already wired; rebellion: a physical suppression term joins successPressure; insurgency: garrison reinforcement into stabilizationSuitability). INVITED repression (the seat requests it) is legitimacy-neutral for the ruler and deepens dependency; UNINVITED is a sovereignty act feeding resentment and the §736 risk it suppresses. Failure receipts and the marching court's belief picture ride D5/D7's laws. Lands in SEAT-2 (the intervention generalization) + SEAT-3 (posture interplay: an exploit-posture occupier represses harder and pays more).
