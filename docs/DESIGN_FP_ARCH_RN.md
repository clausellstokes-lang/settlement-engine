**The answer in one paragraph.** Everyone the world remembers — an ordinary townsman, a high priest, a ruler, a champion, a villain, the party — is known and judged by ONE model. Four facts about a person are kept apart and never merged: how widely they are KNOWN (fame), how each other actor FEELS about them (regard), whether their WORD is trusted (credibility), and, for a ruler only, their RIGHT TO RULE (legitimacy, which renown never writes). Fame comes from office ("the High Priest of Kell") and from deeds; every deed is written down once, as lived history, with how far its tale could travel, and fame fades over years as the tale is forgotten — a town knows a person only as far as word of them has reached it. Regard is sparse: an actor feels something about someone only when the DM says so (the allies-and-enemies list, a champion's authored ties) or when it has heard of something they did; it judges every deed by two readings — what the act is, judged by its own alignment, and whom it helped or hurt, judged by its friends and rivals — with its personality setting the mix, so a good rival grumbles that its enemy was helped and an evil one keeps the grudge. A court feels what its people of power feel, weighted by their power, plus what was done to the court itself; each of its acts comes from the officer whose office does that work, so on scrutiny the why is that officer's grudge. On the surface the DM reads words ("known across the river towns as a fair judge"; "Varn's lords curse the party for saving their rivals; their people do not"); bands, weights and reasons wait for an intentional look; and the DM can set any actor's regard at will. A person or a court enters this one model through a registered deed door (the party's, a paragon's, a verdict's); the engine's own doors for courts and ordinary persons are recorded slots (§3.1), so until each lands such a person is known by office alone.

**The owner's doctrines of the hosting world, read against this volume.** THE WORLD HOSTS THE CAMPAIGN, NEVER COMPETES: renown is the world's memory of what was done; it gives the party no importance it did not earn by a recorded deed, generates no quest, and a voice naming the party is a hearing whose default decides nothing for the party (§5.5). MACRO OVER MICRO: a person's renown is a band and one reason per holder, never a trial; the landed W-H2 verdicts are the only justice RN reads (§3.1). NO ENGINE MORTALITY CLOCK: RN reads death only as the DM's KILL (the holder's entries skipped, the deed standing, §14); nothing in RN ends a life. MEDIEVAL FOR MILLENNIA: fame fades on the one half-life and the deed ledger keeps its facts for any span (the `reach` compaction, §14), so a long skip leaves old deeds as faded tales, never a new era. MENACES NEVER EMERGENT and PURPOSE ONLY BY THE DM: a menace holds no regard and is never a key (§2.2); RN gives no person a goal — a voice is a holder's regard crossing a band, and what any actor does with regard is its owning program's decision under the DM's direction.

## §1 THE LAWS (RN1–RN16)

**RN1 — FOUR FACTS, NEVER MERGED.** FAME (how widely a person is known), REGARD (how each actor feels about them), CREDIBILITY (whether their word is trusted) and LEGITIMACY (a ruler's right to rule) are four quantities with four homes, and no read, band or sentence composes two of them into one. Fame is RN's derived read (§4). Regard is RN's sparse ledger plus its derivation (§5). Credibility is the landed `npcCredibility` law for persons, a holder on the party's sub-ledger for the party (§7), and the landed court stock for courts (never touched by RN). Legitimacy is `governanceLedger.js :: governanceLedger`, which RN never writes. "Infamy" is not a fifth fact: it is fame read beside a negative regard, on read, for one surface (RN4). FAME is held by an actor the world speaks of by name — a person (`p:`), a free company carrying the paragon mark (THE CORPS, `u:`, §2.2; CH §16b) or the party. A SETTLEMENT doer's deed (a court's act: a passage, a site taken, a patrol's clash) adds no fame to anyone: it travels as a TALE (its telling), the persons and paragons who hear it regard the doing court (§2.1 `doer`), and a court is known through its ruler's office (§4.2). A NAMED BODY of THE BODIES — a gang or a conclave past BD's naming threshold — is an actor the world speaks of by name ("the Hollow Road gang"), so it holds fame by its deeds alone (no office, §4.1) and its notoriety reads through §4.4's one table; a nameless body and every beast hold no fame and are no key (§2.2).

**RN2 — DEEDS ARE THE STORE; LIVED HISTORY IS IMMUTABLE.** Every renown fact that can change traces to a DEED: one append-only record in `spatialLedgers.deeds` (the `concludedWars` idiom: "APPEND-ONLY … never rewritten, folded or pruned — that is lived history"), minted with a zero-rng id that is never borrowed from `outcome.id` (R2 hazard 6: two removals in one settlement share an outcome id and would merge as one rumor). A deed's projected scope is computed ONCE at mint and frozen (§3.2). The DM's STRIKE writes a `struck` mark and the deed stops counting everywhere; the record stays (THE PROMISE; the landed party door has `undoState:'none'`, so the mark is its undo). A CORRECTION is its own deed carrying `correctsRef`, and it REPLACES the version it corrects wherever it is heard. Nothing is stored per NPC beyond what an office already gives.

**RN3 — FAME IS BELIEF, DERIVED ON READ, AND FADES OVER YEARS.** A person's fame derives from OFFICE (a ruler, a high priest, a guildmaster is known by position) and from the DEEDS they did, each deed contributing its frozen projected scope decayed on the spine's banded-stock half-life (`bandedStock.js :: decayTowardNeutral`, band `a_decade`, DRAFT; `a_year` for an `npc_verdict` deed, §3.1) — no second decay law. A town knows a person only as far as word of them has reached it: the observer read asks whether the town lies inside a deed's projected reach and whether the tale has ARRIVED there — in the road presets by the rumor road's own relay, while the omniscient arm applies no delay (`hopDelayTicks` reads 0 there, the landed identity). Fame is never stored per town and never stored per NPC. The OBSERVER read decays with the same ordinal: a deed is known at a town only inside the ring its DECAYED scope still covers — its whole reach while undecayed, within the rumor road's notable hop budget (2) of its witnesses once decayed to `regional`, at its witnesses only once `settlement`, nowhere once `unknown` (§4.3). In the road presets a town knows a deed only if it HEARD it — a live telling in its rumor ledger, or a heard term the person stage wrote there; the projected scope stays the deed's fame BAND and the omniscient arm's knowing set. FAME AND REGARD ARE TWO FACTS AND MAY PART ONCE A DEED HAS DECAYED: whether a town HEARD a deed (the one heard test, `heardAt`, §4.3 — the deed's WHOLE projected reach in the omniscient arm, the road's arrival in the belief presets) never shrinks, and a regard term born of the hearing fades only on its own half-life; whether the town still SPEAKS of the deed (fame) shrinks with the decayed ring. A town at the edge of a realm deed's reach may therefore no longer count the doer as known by it while its people still bear the grudge — the heard term is the town's memory, fame the world's — and the dossier says both in words ("little spoken of in Varn now; Varn has not forgotten the siege").

**RN4 — ONE FAME, ONE FADE.** The landed roamer facet `notorietyBand` ("How widely and how badly a person is spoken of") stays STAMPED exactly as today — the W-H2 verdict's truth, written by `npcVerdictApply.js :: applyNpcVerdict`, never dropped — and under `renownEnabled` (ONE flag) every OBSERVER read of it goes through RN's PROJECTION: its "how widely" from RN's fame at that observer, its "how badly" from the observer's believed moral read (RN9), through one table (§4.4). The verdict ALSO has an additive `npc_verdict` deed, minted by THE ONE PERSON STAGE from the verdict's own ledger record (a PUBLIC verdict only — a `dm_only` turncoat is never told), so the person's fame has a deed behind it — a deed CAPPED at the stamp's breadth, never read louder than the stamp, and fading on the ladder's `a_year` (§3.1, §4.4). ONE FADE per person: a person with an RN deed is faded by RN's decay (§4.3) and the landed `beliefFadeSteps` is never stacked on top; a person stamped with no deed (sentenced while RN was dark) keeps the landed fade, byte-identical. `reputationRaceConsumer.js` reads the stamp directly and stays unchanged (truth, by its own design). ES's `operativeNotoriety01` IS fame by office (`embassyEnvoyWeight01` of importance and faction power) and is re-pointed to RN's fame at the target court through a signature car (§4.5): the office floor is identical, deeds can only raise it. There is no third fame. Both hosts are dark in every preset, so neither change is a shipped shift.

**RN5 — REGARD IS SPARSE, EVENT-BORN, TWO-SIGNED, BANDED, WITH ONE PLAIN REASON.** An actor feels something about a subject only when an AUTHORED seed (the DM's allies-and-enemies list; a champion's authored relations) or a DEED it has heard of creates it; nothing is pre-populated. A regard holds two signs apart (warmth and grudge, the `factionPairStates` idiom, so "loved and hated" is expressible on scrutiny) and reads as one band on the seven-word ladder (§2.4); every change keeps ONE dominant reason in the world's words (the typed incident, concern 9b). Each holder tracks at most a capped number of EMERGENT subjects (DRAFT 12, UNMEASURED); the DM's seeds and sets (`declared`, `dmSet`) are exempt from the cap and from eviction; the weakest emergent subject — the one whose strongest decayed term is smallest — drops first. A town's or a court's feeling about ANOTHER town or court is never an RN entry: it is the landed pair state (`relationshipState`; law E14), and RN's writer refuses such a pair; where RN or its caller consults that pair state for an actor's CHOICE, it reads it THROUGH BELIEF (`beliefMap.js :: readBeliefRelationship` — the truth type only that read's fallback input), and RN's own regard ledger is belief by construction (it is what each holder has heard).

**RN6 — ONE DEED MOVES ONE HOLDER ONCE PER VERSION HEARD; NO RATCHETS.** A holder's regard of a subject carries at most one term per deed; a later, better or corrected version REPLACES that term, never adds (concern 9c). Every term decays toward its baseline on the half-life — authored links toward the authored band, emergent ones toward indifference (`decayTowardNeutral` is anti-ratchet by property: "never further from neutral … never on the far side"); repeated contact reinforces only by new deeds. Grudges fade unless renewed (requirement 2).

**RN7 — A BODY FEELS WHAT ITS PEOPLE OF POWER FEEL.** A court's (or a faction's, or a creed's faithful's) regard of anyone is DERIVED: its members' regards weighted by each member's power in it (the governing faction's share, ladder standing, the seat), plus its INSTITUTIONAL RECORD (the deeds whose target was the body itself), plus the DM's authored baseline for that body. There is never a second stored court opinion. A new ruler brings new attitudes and the court's stance moves with the people. In the belief presets a body knows only the VERSIONS ITS TOWN HEARD (the heard terms the person stage writes at arrival): it re-reads each through its ruler's character and never reads the deed ledger directly, so a court never knows a part its commons did not hear (§5.3). A KINGDOM is the body at its root: its regard is the root court's, felt in a member's court at PL's one crown-reach share (`crownReachAt(...).share01` — the liege share for a direct vassal, that share × the paramount's reach by the hold for a sub-vassal), RN keeping no reach map of its own (§5.3).

**RN8 — EACH ACT COMES FROM ITS OFFICE.** A court's attempt toward the party or a person is made by the member whose office does that work (the closed `ACT_OFFICE` table over the landed `INSTITUTION_ROLES` and `rulerId`, §5.2), driven by that member's own regard as well as the court's derived stance — so a welcoming court can still have the party watched by a hostile officer, and on scrutiny the why is that officer's grudge. RN supplies the read; the act itself is a hearing card owned by the program that owns the act (PT, CH). How courts treat each other stays with the landed diplomacy and war deciders (law E14).

**RN9 — EVERY ACTOR READS A DEED THROUGH TWO READINGS.** A deed's INHERENT VALUE is derived from its kind and its target on the landed alignment axes (kindness and lawfulness, plus aggressiveness as the third coordinate) — never a second judgment the DM must make (§6). Reading one: what the act is, read through the holder's own alignment. Reading two: whom it helped or hurt, read through the holder's friends and rivals — for a town or a court, FIRST its LANDED relationship with the town the deed helped or hurt (`relationshipState`: `rival · cold_war · hostile · allied …`, with trust and resentment), AS THE HOLDER BELIEVES IT (`readBeliefRelationship`); for a person or the party, RN's regard link. PERSONALITY sets the mix (the landed trait adjectives); a holder with no tie to anyone the deed touched judges the act alone. A good or neutral rival grumbles and holds nothing against the doer; an unambiguously evil rival keeps the grudge; an evil holder with no tie shrugs at a kindness and holds no grudge; condemning a deed good on its face without a reason costs the condemner its own people's regard (one bounded term, never legitimacy); an ambiguous act reads NEUTRAL until the DM marks it. A TOWN's character enters reading one only as the alignment axes' LANDED BANDS (`lawWordFor`, `natureWordFor`) and never through the aggression drive, so the host's catalogued alignment defects can move a town's reading only across a band edge (§6.2).

**RN10 — THE PARTY IS JUDGED, NEVER JUDGES.** The party is a SUBJECT of fame and regard and a holder of credibility; it holds no regard of anyone, because the party's feelings are the table's. The engine never moves, captures or harms the party (PT's law); RN only reads what the world believes of it. A party-held regard would be the engine deciding the players' feelings.

**RN11 — THE DM'S HAND IS TOTAL.** The DM may set any holder's regard of any subject at will (a `dmSet` that wins until the DM lifts it), author seeds (the allies-and-enemies list: `declaredStance: ally · enemy`, or any regard band as an authored baseline), strike any deed (a mask, so unstriking is exact), and override a deed's moral weight (PT's mark). Every act is one typed DM verb through the established store-action lane (requirement 14; L10's one door) under the AUTHOR hat — stage-then-approve, the landed shape of every AUTHOR act (ODQ §129) — with its inverse for undo. The AI proposes typed operations only (finite semantics).

**RN12 — RENOWN LEANS, NEVER WRITES OTHER TRUTHS.** Renown never writes legitimacy, a court's credibility stock, a share, a strength or a stressor. Where fame or regard should matter to another program, it enters as ONE bounded lean `{ mult, reason }` at ONE landed seam that already receives `worldState` (§5.4), each lean named and capped; it never takes the coup's open slot (the budget is 0.815 of 0.82).

**RN13 — BEHIND THE SCENES.** The surface speaks in the world's words ("known across the river towns as a fair judge"; "in Varn they curse the party's name"); the reasons and the path of a tale appear on scrutiny (the Why panel, the cause walk); bands, weights and terms only on an intentional look. Unpredictable in prospect, legible and coherent on scrutiny: every regard has its reason, every fame its deeds.

**RN14 — THE MOUNT RULE.** Zero lines in `pulseKernel.js` (1581), `applyWorldPulse.js` (907) and `warTermination.js` (818); nothing in `generosityKernel.js` (800) or `armyTransitKernel.js` (798). Every RN module is a leaf under `src/domain/renown/`; every host it enters is named with its effective lines (§5, §10), and the one writer of persisted regard rides THE ONE PERSON STAGE in `envoyPulse.js` (§5.1; OD-RN-18).

**RN15 — THE PHYSICAL WORLD IS A SEAM.** Until the physical world lands, a place is a settlement and "how far word goes" is the rumor road's own scope words over settlement hops; afterwards the road arms take map distance through the rumor road's own relay timing (`hopWeeks`, the curve `hopDelayTicks` also prices) unchanged, and the omniscient arm stays undelayed (the landed identity). Every RN wave is whole without a canonized map: aspatially, a deed is known at once inside its projected scope (the omniscient arm, §8). On a map-module world (HOST-REQUIREMENTS-2026-10-18 H5/H22) `hopDelayTicks` reads the digest the host rebuilds, unchanged; the road arm keys on the preset's infoMode, never on the canon's entitlement (§8).

**RN16 — ONE IDENTITY PER PERSON.** A person-keyed link (as holder or subject) keys on the durable `wnpc_` id and nothing else (`characterDrift.js`'s law: positional ids rebind on reroll). Graduation is the ledger's own (`graduateNpc`); until `npcConsequencesEnabled` lights (C1's lighting unit, OD-FC-39's slot), RN's regard is town-and-court level only — bodies derive their regard from their ruler's character over the versions their town heard (the deeds inside scope in the omniscient arm) — and person fame is office fame plus the deeds of persons that already have a durable key (the party has its own ref, `{kind:'party'}`). A CORPS (CH §16b) is not a person and takes no `wnpc_`; it keys on WC's free-unit key (`spatialLedgers.freeUnits[unitKey]`, WC §1.7.2: `unit.<rootOriginId>.<fissionTick>` for a fission product; a DM-authored company's key minted by WC's DM-authority birth event, CH §16b) and on nothing else. ### 3.2 The projected scope (frozen at mint; replaces "peak reach observed")

The projected scope is computed ONCE at mint by a pure BFS and stored, immutable — the deed's own telling, part of the event (R2 R-2). `deedLedger.js :: projectScope({ regionalGraph, digest, witnessIds, significance, score }) → { projectedScope, reach }`: a breadth-first walk from the witnesses over the landed carriers' adjacency — `rumorNetwork.js :: tradeNeighbours(graph, id)` (confirmed trade channels, both ways, codepoint-sorted), plus `distanceRead.js :: seaLaneNeighbourMap(digest)` and `teleportNeighbourMap(digest)` where a digest exists — to the rumor road's own hop budget `maxHopsFor({ significance, score })` (major 4, 5 at score ≥ 95; notable 2). ### 3.3 Strike and correction

`strikeDeed({ worldState, deedId, tick })` sets `struck: { tick }`; every read MASKS it — fame, the observer read, every regard term carrying that `deedId`, a body's re-reading — and NOTHING is deleted: the persisted terms stay where they are, skipped while the mark stands. `unstrikeDeed` clears the mark and every reading returns exactly (the terms were never dropped, so the telling's 26/13-week expiry does not matter). Dropping a struck term on the next normalize (R2 R-13) would make an unstrike inexact once the telling had expired. A CORRECTION is minted as its own deed (`correctsRef` = the original; PT's door: "the truth can chase the rumor") and travels the same road at the same speed; wherever it is heard, the holder's term for the original is REPLACED by its RE-READING of the original under the corrected version (§5.1) — ONE re-read mechanism for every correction: PT's `correction` ADDS the parts its own telling carries (the reason, the backing; a correction whose telling lost both brings nothing and replaces nothing, PT §11.2); a `backing_voided` or `backing_repudiated` deed MASKS the original's backing part, and only for hearers inside the voider's reach (PT §10.3); a correction marked `contradicts` is also the party's LIE charge (§7). A void is a correction (`correctsRef`), never a masked re-read of PT's own: `voidsRef` is folded into `correctsRef`. Corrections never re-use the original's `eventRef` (`mergeArrival` keeps `relayedTick`: "a settlement never re-relays a corrected telling", R2 hazard 4).

## §4 FAME (RN-0)

### 4.1 The read

The band `a_decade` (520 weeks; R2 offered `a_few_years` or `a_decade`) with rounding gives "obscurity returns with time" at a felt pace: a realm-wide deed is known realm-wide about two and a half years, across its region about ten, in its own town about twenty-six (DRAFT, UNMEASURED, FP-46 roster). **Reads:** the deed ledger (truth of what was done) and office (truth of position); the RESULT is the world's widest knowledge, never a town's belief — the observer read (4.3) is the belief. **Writes:** nothing (derived; memoized per snapshot). **Counterforce:** time, by the one half-life; a struck deed; no deed, no fame beyond office.
