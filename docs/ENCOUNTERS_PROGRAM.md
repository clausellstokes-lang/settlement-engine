## 0 · THE ORDER (owner, in chat, 2026-09-02 02:5x–03:2x ET, consolidated verbatim in substance; ledger §881.10–§881.13)
"We have an envoy system and a spy system. If a named NPC travels to another settlement, whether the goal is declared or hidden and no matter the situation, there is a random chance encounter with a named NPC of that settlement — or two named NPCs from different settlements travelling to a third location accidentally meet. From there, any combination of: (1) nothing; (2) the two create a new bond, rivalry or respect across settlements — EXTENDING the existing NPC relationship vocabulary to the inter-settlement realm; (3) they influence each other's personality moderately, scaling with how different their personalities are; (4) one may be COMPROMISED to the other settlement (the owner's word: "turning means compromised to another settlement", never a defection) — near-certainly if ambitious with negative traits that intuitively justify it, otherwise by a weighted roll over: how different their personality and experiences are from their own settlement at large, how similar they are to the other NPC, and how the other settlement's character relates to their own values (the good magistrate of an evil town won over by an honest envoy from a good one, who might then lean toward supporting the good ones or even toward being influenced or vassalage); (4.1) an attempt to compromise can be REJECTED, and a rejection can be EXPOSED, harming the two settlements' relationship — more likely when the other party is a spy." Then: "so now architect it fully and implement it."
