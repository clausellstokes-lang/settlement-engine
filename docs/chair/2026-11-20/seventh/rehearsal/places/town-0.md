## Safety and trade

- **Safety**: Moderate — Most trouble here keeps to the edges and the late hours. The watch holds the gates and walks the main streets; quieter spots after dark carry real risk. Residents keep to the places they know once night falls. Whoever comes in passes the gates; the trouble that remains is the place's own.
- **Guard**: Some of the town watch take bribes from whoever has coin.
