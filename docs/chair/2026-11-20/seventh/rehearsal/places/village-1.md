# Suryaagam — village (visitor-village-1)

## Overview

- **Size**: village, population 145
- **Arrival**: The first building you pass on the way into Suryaagam is somebody's house, and so is the second, and so, it turns out, is most of the rest. Households cluster around shared wells and water channels. Flour dust lies pale on the doorsteps, and the whole place smells of the ovens. Food is being handed out to anyone who asks, and the line is patient.
- **Pressure**: The most devout households want a say over who may trade and when, and not everyone here agrees.
- **Why here**: Sited on a route already worn deep before anyone built here; a road that old is not soon abandoned.

## The powers

- **Elected Reeve** (governing) — Dominant
  One of the peasantry, elected by the rest, manages labour and mediates disputes, and answers to the people who chose them.
- **Religious Authorities** — Strong
  Church holds substantial temporal power; tithes fund civic works and the clergy's opinion on appointments carries decisive weight. They operate in the shadow of the Elected Reeve, close enough to matter and too far behind to lead.

## Safety and trade

- **Safety**: Moderate — Safety here depends on where you are, and when. Locals watch over the main paths; quieter spots after dark carry genuine risk. Most people here know where trouble is likely, and keep clear of it.
- **Guard**: There is no watch. When someone does wrong, the neighbours deal with it themselves, and the oldest families have the last word.
- **Economic drag**: Some goods change hands here without a licence or a duty paid. It is noticed, and so far it does little harm.
- **Prosperity**: Moderate — Trade proceeds at an ordinary pace for a settlement of this size.
- **Trade access**: road
- **Exports**: Grain surplus, Milled flour, Sacred wood, Ritual components
- **Imports**: Salt, Incense and votive candles, Sandalwood and camphor

## History (the recent line)

- **Character**: People here are careful not to show off their money. The Trade Collapse is long past, but flaunting wealth still looks like having learned nothing from it
- **Most recent (16 years ago): The Pilgrimage**: Word of a miracle brought pilgrims in numbers the settlement had never been meant to hold, and the coin and strangers that came with them left it permanently altered
