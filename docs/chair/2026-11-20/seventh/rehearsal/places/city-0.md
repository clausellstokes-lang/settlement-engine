## The powers

- **Merchant oligarchy** (governing) — Strong; military-dominated
  Wealthy merchants hold exclusive power; political office is effectively purchased through commercial success. The craft guilds are present but outpaced; merchants set prices, craft masters object, and merchants win more often than not.
- **Merchant Guilds** — Significant
  The ruling class and the merchant class are the same people; commercial decisions are political decisions and civic access is purchased. They operate in the shadow of the Merchant oligarchy, close enough to matter and too far behind to lead. The craft guilds are present but outpaced; merchants set prices, craft masters object, and merchants win more often than not.
- **Military/Guard** — Minor
  City watch; it answers for keeping order and is growing more sure of its own authority. Close enough to the two above that their support is worth buying; they play the Merchant Guilds and the Merchant oligarchy against each other when they can.
- **Craft Guilds** — Minor
  The trades regulate their own production and apprenticeships; a reliable secondary presence in civic life. The merchants consistently outvote them on pricing and labour standards, so craft masters now win what they can by adding their own conditions to each deal.
- **The Merchant Bloc** — Significant
  The ruling class and the merchant class are the same people; commercial decisions are political decisions and civic access is purchased. The craft guilds currently carry more weight than they do, and the merchants are working to change that.
