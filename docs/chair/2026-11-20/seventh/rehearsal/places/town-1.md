## The powers

- **Merchant Guilds** — Strong
  The ruling class and the merchant class are the same people; commercial decisions are political decisions and civic access is purchased.
- **Religious Authorities** — Significant
  Church holds substantial temporal power; tithes fund civic works and the clergy's opinion on appointments carries decisive weight. One of two rival sides, each claiming to be the true faith: the legal standing of their records is contested. The gap between them and the Merchant Guilds is narrow. A single shift in patronage, scandal, or armed muscle could swap their positions.
- **Merchant Council** (governing) — Significant; commerce-driven
  Merchant interests dominate the council; trade policy is the primary concern. Under pressure from both sides of the faith to make a formal declaration of support. Has so far avoided doing so. Close enough to the two above that their support is worth buying; they play the Religious Authorities and the Merchant Guilds against each other when they can.

## Safety and trade

- **Safety**: Suspicious — Religious Upheaval — The change of faith has split the people here. Each side suspects the other of informing, and the new rules are enforced one day and ignored the next.
- **Guard**: The town watch walks the usual rounds. That stops a pickpocket or a brawl; anyone who has learned the rounds can work between them. Everyone comes and goes by the gates, so smugglers have to get past the town watch.
- **Economic drag**: Some goods change hands here without a licence or a duty paid. It is noticed, and so far it does little harm.
- **Prosperity**: Poor — Trade proceeds at an ordinary pace for a settlement of this size.
