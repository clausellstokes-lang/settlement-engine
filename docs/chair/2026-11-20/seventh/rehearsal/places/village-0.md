# Rasmahal — village (visitor-village-0)

## Overview

- **Size**: village, population 229
- **Arrival**: Smoke on the skyline is all you see of Rasmahal for the last hour of the road, and then the roofs are suddenly there. Courtyard gates reveal planted interiors and carefully managed water. Lengths of dyed cloth hang drying in bright strips between the houses. Bread is set out at a doorway for whoever needs it, and the household that set it there does not wait to be thanked. A tree twisted into an impossible shape stands where the magic broke loose, fenced and left alone.
- **Pressure**: A dispute over grazing rights and water access has run for two seasons, and the families on each side no longer speak to one another.
- **Why here**: Isolated from major trade routes. What the settlement needs, it makes; what it cannot make, it learns to do without.
