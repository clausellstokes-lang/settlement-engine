## The powers

- **Grand Merchant Oligarchy** (governing) — Strong; corruption-riddled
  The richest merchant houses of the metropolis sit as its senate, so the houses that control the trade also make the law. The craft guilds are present but outpaced; merchants set prices, craft masters object, and merchants win more often than not. They tolerate the guild's cut because fighting it costs more than paying it, and nobody says so aloud.
- **Merchant Guilds** — Significant
  Dominant commercial class; their capital and networks give them leverage even formal institutions must respect. Behind the Grand Merchant Oligarchy by too much to challenge openly, so they get their way through petitions and procedure instead. The craft guilds are present but outpaced; merchants set prices, craft masters object, and merchants win more often than not. They tolerate the guild's cut because fighting it costs more than paying it, and nobody says so aloud.
- **Thieves' Guild** — Minor
  Criminal network operating in shadows; controls illicit trade and profits from the gap between law and enforcement. They are always at the table, but cannot set their own terms. They have more real pull than the military right now: officers on their payroll, and commanders who know better than to ask questions.
- **Craft Guilds** — Minor
  The trades regulate their own production and apprenticeships; a reliable secondary presence in civic life. The merchants consistently outvote them on pricing and labour standards, so craft masters now win what they can by adding their own conditions to each deal.
- **Arcane Orders** — Minor
  Magical practitioners are consulted but not formally empowered. Their influence is advisory, transactional, and quietly resented.
- **Military/Guard** — Suppressed
  Undermanned and underfunded; unable to enforce law consistently and aware of it. The guild is stronger than they are. They can only make arrests where it is safe to, and everyone knows it.
- **The Shield Compact** — Suppressed
  Undermanned and underfunded; unable to enforce law consistently and aware of it.
