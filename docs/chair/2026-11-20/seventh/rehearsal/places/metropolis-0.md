## The powers

- **Religious Authorities** — Strong
  Church holds substantial temporal power; tithes fund civic works and the clergy's opinion on appointments carries decisive weight. The pastoral burden of wartime (soldiers praying before departure, families grieving) has made the institution indispensable in a way it wasn't before. The arcane orders are present but operate in the clergy's shadow. Questions of what is sanctioned magic and what is heresy remain deliberately unresolved. They denounce the guild from the pulpit; the guild gives openly to the poor and makes the denunciations look selective.
- **City-State Council** (governing) — Significant; corruption-riddled
  A city-state council governs with considerable autonomy; camps within it compete for control of policy.
- **War Council** — Minor
  Emergency body with powers over requisition, conscription, and price controls. Unpopular. Accused of favouritism in contract awards. Probably correct on the military decisions. They are always at the table, but cannot set their own terms.
- **Thieves' Guild** — Minor
  Organised criminal network controls the black market and several informal revenue streams; present in council discussions through intermediaries. Shortage and requisition have pushed trade into the black market, where the guild takes its cut.
- **Merchant Guilds** — Suppressed
  Merchants with local reach; they trade here, but have little say in how the place is run. Trade disruption and requisition are hurting the bottom line. The power is lobbying for compensation and receiving promises. The craft guilds are present but outpaced; merchants set prices, craft masters object, and merchants win more often than not. The criminal network reaches further than the merchant guilds right now. Some merchants are paying for protection; others have become silent partners.
- **Arcane Orders** — Suppressed
  Magical practitioners are consulted but not formally empowered. Their influence is advisory, transactional, and quietly resented. The clergy have more say in public affairs, so the arcane orders work around them and never challenge them openly.
- **Craft Guilds** — Suppressed
  The trades regulate their own production and apprenticeships, but carry little weight beyond the workshops. Trade disruption and requisition are hurting the bottom line. The power is lobbying for compensation and receiving promises. The merchants consistently outvote them on pricing and labour standards, so craft masters now win what they can by adding their own conditions to each deal.
- **The Trade Compact** — Suppressed
  The trades regulate their own production and apprenticeships, but carry little weight beyond the workshops. Trade disruption and requisition are hurting the bottom line. The power is lobbying for compensation and receiving promises.
- **The Underground** — Minor
  A rival criminal network that wants a share of the black market and its takings, and contests them with the established one.
