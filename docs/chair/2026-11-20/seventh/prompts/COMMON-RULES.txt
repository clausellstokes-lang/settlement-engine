1. THE ONE PATTERN. A default sentence that says nothing is replaced by one that says something the record holds. Each wording declares the facts it NEEDS in the vocabulary's own atoms. Pools are selected THROUGH THE ONE DOOR, `src/domain/facts/drawDeclared.js` (the presumed-things walker credits only pools passed through `drawDeclared`, `filterDeclared`, `resolveDeclared`, `resolveSibling`, `resolveSiblingSentences`, `noteDeclared`). A keyed pool takes the kernel's mixed pick (`src/kernel/proseHash.js :: pickVariantMixed`, or `mix32(fnv1a32(…))` for a rank); a pool drawn by a roll stays on its roll and changes no draw count. No new draw. Every new pool in its own data file. THE TWO-TREE PROOF: 0 non-string differences (a unit that must move a non-string field says which, and nothing else moves).
2. A LITERAL HAS ONE HOME. A line already in the tree is never copied into a pool file as its index 0: the pool handed to the door is composed at the call site as [the existing line, ...the new siblings]. A table is never keyed by a sentence's full text where an index or a generated hash will do.
3. THE BAR. No sentence on more than about 15% of ALL places of a size (a top to 20% passes as sampling), on 150 places a size; for a field a place prints several of (hooks, ties, secrets, arrival beats), the share a second place repeats under 30%. A pool carries at least reach/0.15 eligible lines. A sentence is not printed twice inside one place.
4. WHAT A LINE MAY SAY. No new line says "the town" or "a town" of the place. No season, climate, age or household a record may contradict. No house or feature the place may lack unless it is declared as a need. No feeling or scheme its branch does not compute. A RATING THAT IS LOW BECAUSE A PLACE IS SMALL IS NOT A DANGER AND NOT A CHANGE. WHAT A HOOK IS: a lead a DM can use tonight; it may propose a twist; never a question, a mood, or a house the place lacks.
5. THE HIDEABLE RULE. No wording of a field a shared read serves is chosen by a house of a hideable kind (`src/domain/people/cardVeil.js :: isHideableHouse`) or by any fact about crime, unless that wording is owner-only by construction or withheld from every shared read that hides the house, and LISTED; every other house a wording needs is LISTED in the hand-back for the security reader.
