FRAME-7 · F5R — the seat line on six framed owner sheets, for the chair's eye before the merge.
BEFORE = the base 90874e392 (FRAME-7's lane tip, C2b merged). AFTER = the F5R tip, the commit that carries this file (branch frame7-f5r). Each town born fresh at each tree by that tree's own generator: generateSettlementPipeline({ settType: tier }, null, { seed, customContent: {} }).
OWNER = composeTonightSheet(s, { audience: 'owner' }); STRANGER = composeTonightSheet(s). Digests: sha256 of the sheet's JSON, 16 hex.
THE ONE LINE (tonightSheet.js :: seatLineOf): "Holds the seat: {name}, {office}." — the calm sheet's own seat row's name and office (its role), printed under the three sides on the Table View, Session Mode, the PDF and the journal; never among the people to voice.
Framed owner sheets in the 330-town calm pool, by size: thorp 0, hamlet 8, village 15, town 7, city 14, metropolis 19. THE THORP HAS NONE: no calm thorp of the pool holds a trouble of weight, so no thorp sheet is framed and none carries the line.
Five sheets below are one a size (hamlet to metropolis), each the first of its size by seed index whose holder stands on no side; the sixth is the first framed sheet whose holder IS a face, which prints no seat line.
Format: each side is 'words: who — why. Wants…'; the people to voice are 'name · role [why]'; each hook ends with its source.
