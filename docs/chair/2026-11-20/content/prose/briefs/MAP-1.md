## THE QUESTION

SAME-1 found that for many states every place of a size prints the SAME sentence (`OFF-LIST.md`: `git show origin/lane/same-1-2026-11-20:docs/chair/2026-11-20/content/same/census/OFF-LIST.md`). For each such pool we must know HOW MANY VARIANTS A PLACE OF EACH SIZE IS ELIGIBLE FOR, and WHY the commonest wins: (a) the pool holds one variant; (b) a mark or a slot excludes the others for that place; (c) several are eligible and the same one still wins (a pick that is not keyed — the kernel's fault, not the corpus's).

## HOW A VARIANT IS PICKED (read, so you instrument the right place)

`src/domain/display/stateProse/stateProseKernel.js`: `eligibleVariants(pool, options)` filters by audience (`dm-only` mark), anchoring (every `{slot}` the variant names must have a filled string), the demoted state dimensions (marks in `STATE_MARK_DIMENSIONS`), and fact declarations (other marks, read against `facts`). `drawVariant(eligible, blockId, poolKey, seed)` is the ARGMAX of `hashKey(`${seed}::${blockId}::${poolKey}::v${vid}`)` over the eligible set (seedless → `eligible[0]`). `readStateProse` (same module) calls both internally; `composeStateProse.js` imports and calls `drawVariant` directly. The seed is `String(s._seed ?? s.id)`. The pools are `src/data/dossierStateProse/{defense,economy,general,power,stressors,warFaith}.generated.js` (each variant: `text`, `angle`, `marks`, `slots`, `vid`).
