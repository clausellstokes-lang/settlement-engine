WHAT A SHARED PAGE'S TRADITION DESCRIPTIONS NO LONGER SAY (for the owner; TRAD-4, written 2026-11-20 13:33 UTC)

Your own page is unchanged: every description there still says everything the town's record holds. A page you share
(the public view or the party link) shows the same traditions, but each one's description is written so that
nothing you might hide later can slip out through it, so it leaves out three things. It never says who founded the
town: where your page reads, for example, "kept since the town was founded by the river guild, 276 years ago", the shared page reads
"kept since the town was founded, 276 years ago". It never gives a remembered disaster its own name: where your page
says, for example, "in memory of the Burning of Lowgate", the shared page says "in memory of the great fire" (likewise "the siege",
"the flood", "the years of sickness"). And it leaves off a tradition that stands only on a market or other house whose
use you set yourself, or on a house of a kind you can hide whole (a criminal or clandestine one): the shared page
lists only the traditions it can stand behind without them. In the 432 sample towns the engine makes on its own, that
last rule removed nothing: 0 of 432 towns show a shorter list of traditions on the shared page than on yours. It only
bites on a town you have edited by hand.
