## The fault
Goals are dealt card by card (`goals.js :: dealGoals`). The exact-text `held` sets stop two cards printing the SAME FILLED sentence, but nothing stops one TEMPLATE being dealt twice with different names ("Be in the room the next time the Guard must decide..." / "...the Merchant Guilds must decide..."). Measured on 300 towns at untouched settings: a metropolis deals about 52 template repeats (short + long).

## The rule (the chair's GR1, verbatim in spirit)
Within one town no two people hold the same goal WORDING once the town's names are masked. Where the deal would repeat, the LATER person takes the NEXT wording of its kind that the town has not used and that its own record admits, BY ROTATION FROM THE INDEX IT DREW, with NO NEW DRAW. A person who did not repeat keeps their goal BYTE FOR BYTE, and no other step's draw moves. Where every wording is used, the repeat stands and is COUNTED.

## The design (build exactly this; stop and tell GOALS-1 on a mismatch)
1. **The wording key.** Each candidate carries a `wording` string: its TEMPLATE (`templateOf(line)`) filled with every NAME slot replaced by `§` and every other slot by its value. The name slots: the person slots (`PERSON_SLOTS` plus `priest`), the power slots (`power`, `rival`, `guild`) and the house slots (`house`, `otherHouse`, `front`, `targetHouse`, `shrine`, `arcaneHouse`). The others (`resource`, `import`, `issue`, `chain`) are filled with their values. Fill the agreement forms the same way for every card: leave `{power|it|them}` as written in the key (the key is never printed). A CRISIS candidate (no template) takes its text as its key.
2. **The rotation group.** Every `cellCandidates` call stamps its candidates with one call id (a counter on `ctx`). A drawn candidate's group is the candidates of ITS call, in list order: one family, one kind, one target, one set of slots. The intent therefore never changes on rotation (family, kind, key and target stay); only the text and the receipt's slots may change.
3. **Reserve lines (no content in this unit).** Add an export `GOAL_RESERVE_LINES` to `goalFamilies.js`, the same shape as `GOAL_FAMILY_LINES` (`[family][kind][vantage]` → `cell([...])`), EMPTY (`Object.freeze({})`) in this unit. `cellCandidates` builds the reserve lines' candidates exactly as it builds the cell's (the declared gate, the fill, `secondMentions`, the exact-text `held` refusal, the members' refusal) but keeps them OUT of its returned list: it files them on `ctx` under the call id. So `by`, every `fork.pick`, every `fork.weightedPick`, `admittedAfter` and the families/kinds lists are exactly today's. Appending to a cell would change `fork.pick`'s modulus and move every draw of that kind: that is why the new lines live apart.
4. **Two passes.** Pass one is today's loop, unchanged in every draw and every `held` addition, except that (a) it records, per card and vantage, the chosen candidate and its group (the call's main candidates followed by its reserve candidates), and (b) it defers `noteDrawn` (see 6). Pass two, in roster order, short then long per card: the town's `seen` keys start with the keepers' (a kept card's texts, as their own keys); a holding whose key is in `seen` is a REPEAT. A repeat rotates: for k = 1 … n−1, the candidate at (position + k) mod n of its group is TAKEN if it is not `refused` and its key is in neither `taken` nor `seen`; the first such wins. `taken` = every key pass one dealt in the town (all cards, both vantages) plus every key pass two has rotated to. So a rotation never lands on a wording any other person holds, and nobody who did not repeat moves. None free: the repeat stands, and the receipt says so (`receipt.repeat = { short: true }` / `{ long: true }`, beside today's fields). The holding's key (rotated or not) joins `seen`.
5. **The floor** (`personalityFloor`) is not rotated (it is the floor); a floor that repeats is counted as above.
6. **The presumption ledger.** `noteDrawn` is called for the FINAL candidate of each holding, after pass two, in the same card order and short-then-long order as today, so the ledger still records the line the card prints.
7. **The receipt** of a rotated holding gains `rotated: { from: <the drawn candidate's index in its group>, to: <the taken index> }` and its `slots` are the taken candidate's. Nothing new reaches the card record: `next.goal` keeps its shape `{ short, long, intent }`.

## AMENDMENT (10:30 UTC, the chair's rulings of 10:28 UTC; where it differs from the text above, THIS GOVERNS)
1. **Repeat status is read from pass one alone.** A holding is a REPEAT iff an EARLIER holding of pass one (or a keeper) has its key. A holding that is not a repeat is never moved, whatever pass two does. Pass two keeps a count per key: every key pass one dealt to a non-repeater, plus every key pass two assigns (rotated to, or a repeat that stands).
2. **A repeat chooses, in rotation order from its drawn position (k = 0 … n−1, k = 0 being the drawn candidate itself, skipping `refused`), in this order of preference:**
   (i) the first candidate whose key has count 0 (k = 0 cannot qualify: a repeat's own key has count ≥ 1);
   (ii) else the first whose key has count 1 and whose one holder is NOT of the same house as this card (`institution.kind === 'house'` and the same `institution.key`), NOT tied to this card (any relationship in `relationships` between the two, either direction, covert or not), and shares NO power with it (`domain/facts/sharedPower.js :: sharesPower`);
   (iii) else the first whose key has count 1 and whose holder is neither of the same house nor tied to this card;
   (iv) else the drawn candidate stands (counted: `receipt.repeat`, as before).
   So no template is held more than twice where the cell allows, and a template held twice is never held by two of one house or two people tied to each other.
3. **The receipt** of a repeat says which rung chose it: `receipt.repeat = { short: 'free' | 'twice' | 'twice-same-power' | 'stands' }` (`free` for (i)). `rotated` stays as before.
4. **Tests:** (a) holds as written.
