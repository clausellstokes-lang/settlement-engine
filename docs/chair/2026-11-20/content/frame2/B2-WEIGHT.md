## The ruling (the chair's CF1 as amended and CF11, FINAL; vetoable by the owner)

A town IN CRISIS keeps its crisis sheet exactly as it is. A CALM town (no active crisis) takes the frame ONLY where the record holds a TROUBLE OF WEIGHT; every other calm town keeps TODAY'S SHEET BYTE FOR BYTE. A trouble of weight is a tension of `history.currentTensions` that is ABOUT a graded strain the town holds, from this closed list:

- **W1, the seat under real pressure**: the town holds W1 when `coupContenders(s).incumbent.gated === false`, or its legitimacy is below 45 by the gates' own read (`governanceLedger(s).legitimacyScore`, the read `politicalFractureGate` makes). A tension is about W1 only by its MATTER: its type is one of `legitimacy_crisis`, `succession_crisis`, `leadership_vacuum`, `tyranny`, `popular_uprising`; and `corruption_scandal` ONLY where its own text is about the ruling body (read the template's texts for that type in `src/data/historyData.js` and the stored tensions on the pool; decide which reading the record supports, implement it, and say which way you read it and why). Legitimacy below 45 ALONE is not weight.
- **W2, two powers in a recorded conflict**: a row of `powerStructure.conflicts` whose `intensity` is `high`, ONLY. (Not `moderate`; not `factionRelationships`.) A tension is about W2 when one of its sides IS one of that row's two powers (the side's words equal the power's name, compared as B1's results file describes).
- **W3, a material strain short of a crisis**: (W3a) `economicState.foodSecurity` below Secure; (W3b) a creditor power standing among `powerStructure.factions` (as B1 found the record marks it); (W3c) an `impaired` chain in `economicState.activeChains`. A tension is about W3 when its type is one of `trade_collapse`, `trade_dispute`, `resource_scarcity`, `outside_debt`, `market_crash` (NOT `guild_conflict`, NOT `economic_disparity`). W3a and W3b stay in the code though dead on calm towns today.

THE TROUBLE THE SHEET OPENS ON: among the town's tensions about a strain it holds, the one whose strain has the highest grade, in this order: legitimacy below 30; then legitimacy below 45 (and the ungated incumbent: place it with below 30 or below 45 by what `resolveCoup` makes of it, and say which); then a high conflict; then an impaired chain (and W3a, W3b beside it); the record's own order breaks a tie. The tension's `severity` is NOT read (it is the template's list: FRAME-1 measured it). Nothing is tuned toward a share of towns.
