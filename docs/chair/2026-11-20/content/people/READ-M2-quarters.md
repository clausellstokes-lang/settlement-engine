The proposal: `PROPOSAL-quarters-desc.md` §1–§6 (the first draft), and §7 (the rewrite toward the existing vocabulary, which supersedes §4's atom list). Wordings: `measures/m2-quarters-pools-atoms.mjs`.

1. **What the field does today.** Every quarter type prints one sentence in 100% of the places that hold it. On top of that, the lines presume what the place lacks. "Houses of worship, cloisters, … priests and pilgrims" prints in all 179 thorps and hamlets that only have ACCESS to a neighbour's church. The cause is the gate `has('church')`, which matches "Access to parish church". "Cloisters" is false in 100 villages and 84 towns. The dyers, butchers, towers, hidden markets, the coast line inland and the alehouse are false where those houses do not stand. Two registers bank these lines; none refuses them.
2. **I propose §7: no new atom.** The wordings are rewritten toward atoms the vocabulary already has: trade at work, exported goods, ruler kind, the existing institution keys, organised crime, enchanting. With no grant, no false presumption survives, there are no fallbacks, the size top is 10–14%, and every type at n ≥ 30 is at or under 15%. The cost: today's line leaves five types entirely (Noxious, Waterfront, Mages', Shadows, Alehouse), because what makes it true has no atom. The chair may grant, in measured order: burial, customs house, mages' guild, black market, weekly market, gambling, butcher, theatre. I ask for none now; the bar holds without them.
