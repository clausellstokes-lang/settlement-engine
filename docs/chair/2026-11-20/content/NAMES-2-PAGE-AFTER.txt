The place
The road into Farosaltus becomes part of the settlement at a point you can't precisely identify. The houses gather around you gradually. A wall circuit, unremarkable but intact. Bolts of cloth and bales of wool fill the carts waiting on the road out. A notary's clerk hurries past with a satchel of contracts. Patched roofs and patched clothes say money is short here.

Defensive posture
Vulnerable
Guard assessment
The town watch patrols on a regular round, so petty theft is often caught and planned crime seldom is. A wrong done here goes before the court, and the guilty can be locked up for it. Everyone comes and goes by the gates, so smugglers have to get past the town watch.
Farosaltus has people under arms, but too few and too badly placed to hold off a serious attack for long.

There is a line around Farosaltus and it is kept: the gates shut, the works are repaired, and a stranger who comes by the road enters where the town intends.
