## Rossleigh (a thorp; seed hxd-thorp-0)

- Outsider impressions: A visitor soon learns that little happens here without the Elder Council. People here keep an eye on the wild country and mention it often. Keeping the peace here rests on goodwill more than on any force.
- Dawn work: Water is fetched at first light in company, never alone, and the day's work starts after.
- Food culture: The everyday fare is a simple stew and whatever else the day provides.
- Gathering places: Where the water is drawn is where people meet and pass on their news.
- Child warnings: Children are warned about straying out of sight of the houses once the light starts to go.
- Unspoken topics: People here do not talk about the wild magic, and they do not like being asked.
- Recent changes: The mark the Great Fire left on the place has not faded.

## Steinhausen (a hamlet; seed hxd-hamlet-0)

- Outsider impressions: Outsiders notice the Headman's Authority's mark is on every door that matters. Visitors are asked which way they came, and whether they met anything on the way.
- Dawn work: The merchants are up before anyone else, and their business is under way by first light.
- Food culture: Food is steady here, if not generous, and every household has enough with little left over.
- Gathering places: People gather at the periodic market as much to talk as to buy.
- Child warnings: Children are warned about going anywhere alone after dark.
- Commoner resentments: People do as the Headman's Authority says, but few of them think well of it.
- Unspoken topics: How the place should answer the danger it has been warned of is argued in private, not before strangers.
- Recent changes: The mark the Flood left on the place has not faded. Beasts have overrun the country around, and nobody travels alone any more.

## Dobroki (a village; seed hxd-village-0)

- Outsider impressions: Ask who decides things here, and a visitor hears more than one name. The means of keeping order here are slight, and a visitor sees it soon enough.
- Dawn work: The first light goes to walking the fields to see how they stand, and then the day's work begins.
- Food culture: Meals are plain and filling, and little goes to waste.
- Gathering places: People meet on the common and by the house of worship, where most of the talk of the place goes on.
- Child warnings: Children are warned about giving anything away without asking first.
- Commoner resentments: People do as the Headman's Authority says, but few of them think well of it.
- Unspoken topics: The old argument over fire, and who should bear the cost of preventing it, is not aired in company.
- Recent changes: Ask how things came to be as they are here, and the Succession Crisis soon comes up.

## Petenmul (a town; seed hxd-town-0)

- Outsider impressions: Ask who decides things here, and the answer is the Town Mayor. A visitor soon learns that the country beyond is not to be walked carelessly. Sickness is something people here deal with at home, as best they can.
- Dawn work: The night's guard comes down off the walls at first light, and the day's guard goes up.
- Food culture: A pot of stew over the fire is the usual supper, and there is usually enough to go round.
- Gathering places: People cross the market square on their way to the houses of worship, and many stop to talk on the way.
- Child warnings: Children are warned about being outside the walls once the gates are shut, and about crowds that gather quickly.
- Recent changes: The Religious Conflict still shapes how people here go about things.

## Shangjin (a metropolis; seed hxd-metropolis-0)

- Outsider impressions: No single name comes up first when a visitor asks who holds sway here. Frontier habits show here: people go out in company and come back before dark. Getting anything decided here takes time, for those who decide are few and busy.
- Dawn work: People are at the fish market before light, waiting to see what food comes in and what it will cost.
- Food culture: Rations are tight, and the poor eat watered stew.
- Gathering places: People gather on the steps of the great cathedral, where there is always someone to talk to.
- Child warnings: Children are warned about the road past sundown.
- Commoner resentments: Few here would say that the Ducal Governorship speaks for them. Nobody likes to see the pot so thin, and everyone has someone to blame for it.
- Unspoken topics: How the place should answer the danger it has been warned of is argued in private, not before strangers.
- Recent changes: People here still talk of the Migration, and of what it changed. War is close enough now to be felt in everyday business and in the mood of the place.
