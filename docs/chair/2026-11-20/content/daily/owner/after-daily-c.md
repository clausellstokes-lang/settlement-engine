## Rossleigh (a thorp; seed hxd-thorp-0)

- Outsider impressions: A visitor who wants anything settled here is told to speak to the Elder Council first. The wild is not far off, and visitors hear stories of what comes out of it. Nobody stands guard here, and a visitor walks in unchallenged.
- Dawn work: People go out to their work together at dawn, and no one strays far from the houses alone.
- Food culture: The everyday fare is a simple stew and whatever else the day provides.
- Gathering places: When the boats are drawn up, people gather at the landing to sort the catch and talk.
- Child warnings: Children are warned about going anywhere alone after dark.
- Unspoken topics: People here do not talk about the wild magic, and they do not like being asked.
- Recent changes: The mark the Great Fire left on the place has not faded.

## Steinhausen (a hamlet; seed hxd-hamlet-0)

- Outsider impressions: Visitors learn early that it pays to stay on good terms with the Headman's Authority.
- Dawn work: The merchants are up before anyone else, and their business is under way by first light.
- Food culture: Food is steady here, if not generous, and every household has enough with little left over.
- Gathering places: Most days people stop to talk by the wayside shrine on their way to and from their work.
- Child warnings: Children are warned about going down for water alone after dark.
- Commoner resentments: Many here feel that the Headman's Authority no longer listens to people like them.
- Unspoken topics: How the place should answer the danger it has been warned of is argued in private, not before strangers.
- Recent changes: Much of how the place works now goes back to the Flood. Beasts have overrun the country around, and nobody travels alone any more.
