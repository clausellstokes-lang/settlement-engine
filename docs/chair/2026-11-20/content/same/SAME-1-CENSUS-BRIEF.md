## THE MEASURE
- Plan: 50 places a size, seeds `hxd-<size>-0..49`, the six sizes; the same plan under `hxu-` is the CONFIRMATION corpus.
- MASK: the place's own proper names (its name, each person's name and each word of it longer than three letters, each power's `name` and `faction`), longest first, to `#`; every run of digits to `0`. Goods and houses are NOT masked. This is census.mjs's mask; keep it identical so the two agree.
- SENTENCE: each printed line split at sentence ends (`.`, `!`, `?`, `…` followed by space and a capital or quote); a sentence is a masked piece of at least 25 characters with a space (census.mjs's threshold).
- SLOT: `surface | source`. SOURCE is the stored path (indices folded to `[]`) where the masked sentence is a sentence of a masked stored string word for word (several paths: the shortest, all listed); else `COMPOSED@<heading>` (the heading it prints under), with the MODULE that composed it where you can find it (best effort: a static index of the string and template fragments of `src/**/*.{js,jsx}`, the longest fragment the sentence contains; else `unfound`).
- Per slot and size: places holding it; distinct sentences; sentences per place; TOP (the commonest sentence, its places / the holders); SEEN (census.mjs's: the mean share of a place's sentences at the slot that a second place also prints).
- THREE TABLES A SIZE. (a) ONE A PLACE (sentences per place at most 1.5): by top share. (b) MANY A PLACE: by SEEN. (c) PRINTED TWICE INSIDE ONE PLACE: within one surface FAMILY (the web dossier, all tabs; one PDF variant; the session sheet), a masked sentence printed at two or more slots: the slots, and the places of each size where it happens (the web and the PDF printing the same sentence is by design and not counted).
- CLASS, every row, with a one-line reason: CATALOGUE (a house's or a service's own definition: the same by design), LABEL (a band's word, or the page's own furniture), STATE (chosen deterministically by the place's state: a band → a sentence), NARRATIVE (drawn from a pool or composed as the place's story). Decide by the source: the path and the module. Where you cannot tell, write `?` beside your best class.
