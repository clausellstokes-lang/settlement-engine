# Six places, before and after

One place of each size, as a visitor who changes no setting would make it. For each place: what the page said about its troubles before the work, and what it says now.
For each trouble: the sentence that explains it, who stands on each side, and the openings for the players (the hooks).

## 1. Portacolonia, a thorp

### Before the work

**Trouble 1, corruption scandal.** Evidence of official corruption has surfaced but powerful figures resist investigation

The sides: Elder Council; Reformers; Complicit beneficiaries.

Openings for the players:

- The investigator who uncovered the corruption has received a death threat
- A key witness can be found, but won't speak without protection
- The corruption goes higher than anyone yet suspects; those pressing to expose it are being used

### After the work

**Trouble 1, corruption scandal.** Evidence of official corruption has surfaced but powerful figures resist investigation

The sides: Elder Council; Reformers; Complicit beneficiaries.

Openings for the players:

- The investigator who uncovered the corruption has received a death threat
- The corruption goes higher than anyone yet suspects; those pressing to expose it are being used
- An official named in the evidence is selling property in a hurry

## 2. Niederwalde, a hamlet

### Before the work

**Trouble 1, crime wave.** Crime is rising faster than those in charge can answer it, and some talk of taking the law into their own hands

The sides: Those who would take the law into their own hands; the criminal network.

Openings for the players:

- The crime wave is coordinated. Someone is directing it to create a pretext
- Those who would take the law into their own hands have picked out a culprit on bad information

### After the work

**Trouble 1, crime wave.** Crime is rising, and the law here is too weak to hold it back.

The sides: Those who would take the law into their own hands; the criminal network.

Openings for the players:

- An old woman's savings have gone from under her floor, and only her family knew where they were kept
- A household woke to find its door forced and nothing taken, and wants to know what the thieves were looking for
- A cart left loaded overnight was emptied before dawn, and the carter wants to know who was awake

## 3. Rasmahal, a village

### Before the work

**Trouble 1, disputed land.** A land claim from an old charter contradicts current occupation, with both sides legally plausible

The sides: Original claimants; Current occupants; Legal class profiting from ambiguity.

Openings for the players:

- The original charter is held in a private archive whose owner has gone missing
- Something of value was found on the disputed land, which is why the claim is suddenly being pressed
- A forged document is about to be introduced as evidence; someone knows it's fake

**Trouble 2, wild magic.** An uncontrolled surge of magic left its mark on the low ground and on the people who were there, and the effects have never fully faded

The sides: The changed and their descendants; Those who fear them; Scholars studying the residue.

Openings for the players:

- The surge was no accident. An experiment went wrong, and the notes were hidden, not destroyed
- A bloodline touched by the surge is now manifesting something the family cannot conceal

## 6. Pianomare, a metropolis

### Before the work

**Trouble 1, external threat.** Word of a gathering threat split the settlement over whether to fight, treat, or flee

The sides: Prepare for war; Seek diplomacy; Flee/hide.

Openings for the players:

- The threat is being exaggerated by someone who profits from wartime contracts
- A diplomatic channel exists that the war party is suppressing
- The threat has an agent already inside the settlement

**Trouble 2, guild conflict.** Rival guilds fought over who may sell what, and over a say in how the place is run

The sides: Established guilds; Upstart guilds; Non-guild workers.

Openings for the players:

- A guild master's records have gone missing ahead of an audit
- A journeyman denied mastership has proof the exam was rigged
- Someone is sabotaging both guilds to destabilize the market for a third party

**Trouble 3, population friction.** A recently arrived group and the established population fell into sustained, low-level conflict

The sides: Old families; Newcomers; Merchants who benefit from cheap newcomer labour.

Openings for the players:

- The newcomers fled something, and that something has sent agents after them
- A series of 'accidents' targeting newcomers is being attributed to chance
- An old family is deliberately inflaming tensions to drive out a newcomer who knows something

### After the work

**Trouble 1, external threat.** The war has come close, and the people are divided over whether to fight, bargain or flee.

The sides: Prepare for war; Seek diplomacy; Flee/hide.

Openings for the players:

- A way to come to terms exists, and those who want a fight are keeping it quiet
- A family is packing to leave before dawn, and the neighbours are trying to stop them spreading panic
- A stretch of the wall has been found crumbling, and mending it needs more hands than there are

**Trouble 2, guild conflict.** Trade has been fought over here before, in an earlier guild war, and now the guild and those outside it are at odds.

The sides: Established guilds; Upstart guilds; Non-guild workers.

Openings for the players:

- A family that sells to both sides is being pressed to choose, and has asked for a few more days
- The guild is about to fine a member for buying from an outsider, and the member has threatened to name who else does it
- A trader outside the guild has been offered protection by people outside the law, and is tempted

**Trouble 3, population friction.** Newcomers and long-settled residents settled into a slow, grinding antagonism that never quite boiled over

The sides: Old families; Newcomers; Merchants who benefit from cheap newcomer labour.

Openings for the players:

- The newcomers fled something, and that something has sent agents after them
- A series of 'accidents' targeting newcomers is being attributed to chance
- An old family is deliberately inflaming tensions to drive out a newcomer who knows something
