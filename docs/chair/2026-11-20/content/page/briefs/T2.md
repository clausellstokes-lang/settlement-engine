**THE OWNER'S LAW FOR EVERY LINE (2026-11-20: "Don't invent drama… We are not creating the campaign").** A wording is the
SAME tension in other words: never a new event, a new fact, a named victim, a death, a secret, a crime or a party the
unanchored sentence does not hold; no number; no proper name; no place word (a quarter, a district, a street) unless the
unanchored sentence has one. Present tense, one sentence, no full stop at the end (match the existing lines), the dry literary
register of the lines beside it (a fact and its complication, ~15–30 words). It may name a force, a market, a guild, a
temple or a court ONLY where it DECLARES the atom that makes it true (`needs`, existing vocabulary atoms only:
`src/domain/facts/factVocabulary.js`, read only; e.g. `'market'`, `'guild'`, `'legal house'`, `'tier: ≤ village'`,
`'tier: ≥ town'`); a wording true at every size declares nothing. BY SIZE: of each kind's new wordings at least one is
written for a small place (`'tier: ≤ village'`: a thorp or a village has neighbours, not "citizens" or "authorities") and at
least one for a large one, and every size keeps at least two admitted forms. A wording that needs an atom that does not exist is
rewritten to need none. Tokens: only those the unanchored form of the kind already uses (`{location}`, `{quarter}` …), each at
most once.
