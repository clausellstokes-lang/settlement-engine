The place
Portacolonia has no one thing to look at as you come in, only a great many small ones: a mended fence, a painted door, a bench beside a house. Deep eaves shade the open ground between the houses. Someone has left fresh flowers at a small shrine set into a wall. A benefactor's name is carved fresh above a mended well, and people make sure you notice it. A few old signs still carry the occupiers' words, painted over but showing through.

Food endurance: No granary: under siege the outlying fields would be lost; household stores would carry the thorp for about a month and a half at full rations, about three months on half, and what is still harvested would stretch them further.
Criminal Architecture & Public Order▲
Little here goes unnoticed for long; the neighbours see to that.
With no gaol at Portacolonia, nobody who does wrong here can be locked away; most go on living beside the people they wronged.
Portacolonia's books record no gang, guild or racket: what wrongdoing the thorp has, people do on their own account.
Armed Forces & Fortifications▲
## Armed Forces & Fortifications
Unfortified. No perimeter walls. Defenders cannot control entry points or create chokepoints.
Portacolonia has no wall, and the beasts of the country around it come right up to the last houses.
No organized force. Defense relies on individual armed citizens. No command structure, no training, no coordinated response.
The thorp has an edge rather than a boundary at Portacolonia, and the edge is wherever the last building happens to be. Nobody at Portacolonia is responsible for defending the thorp; ask who is, and nobody can name anyone. It retains no specialists. Whatever comes out of the country is met by whoever the thorp has, whether or not they are the right answer to it. The thorp has no arcane defense: what arrives unseen here goes undetected and therefore unanswered.
