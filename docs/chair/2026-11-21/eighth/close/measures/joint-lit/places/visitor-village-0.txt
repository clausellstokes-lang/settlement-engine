The place
Smoke on the skyline is all you see of Rasmahal for the last hour of the road, and then the roofs are suddenly there. Courtyard gates reveal planted interiors and carefully managed water. Lengths of dyed cloth hang drying in bright strips between the houses. Bread is set out at a doorway for whoever needs it, and the household that set it there does not wait to be thanked. A tree twisted into an impossible shape stands where the magic broke loose, fenced and left alone.

== web:Systems/Defense ==
Defensive posture
Vulnerable
Guard assessment
Nobody here is paid to keep order. A quarrel goes to the heads of the oldest families, and most are settled before they come to blows.
Counted up, the people Rasmahal could put under arms make a thin defence.
Threat Assessment
Each bar shows how ready the settlement is for that threat, as judged at the first survey; Disasters & Famine is re-judged as your campaign goes on. A longer bar is safer.
Beasts & Monsters
Critical▼
Whoever walks out of Rasmahal, in any direction and at any hour, is unlikely to meet a beast.
Invasion & War
Weak▼
Rasmahal has no line and no force. Organized aggression cannot be resisted here; what preserves the village is distance, diplomacy, or being beneath notice.
Internal Security
If you are wronged at Rasmahal, there is no court to take it to, and no local is surprised.
Economic Survival
Rasmahal pays nobody to defend it, and could not find the wages if it had to hire someone in a hurry.
Disasters & Famine
Adequate▼
Food endurance: No granary: under siege the outlying fields would be lost; household stores would carry the village for about two months at full rations, about four months on half, and what is still harvested would stretch them further.
Criminal Architecture & Public Order▲
Order holds in most of the place, but not everywhere. Locals watch over the main ways themselves; after dark, the quieter spots carry genuine risk. Locals can tell a newcomer which places to steer clear of.
Rasmahal pays nobody to keep the peace, and trouble here is settled among its own people.
Rasmahal's books record no gang, guild or racket: what wrongdoing the village has, people do on their own account.
Armed Forces & Fortifications▲
## Armed Forces & Fortifications
Unfortified. No perimeter walls. Defenders cannot control entry points or create chokepoints.
Rasmahal is too small to wall and knows it; the village's safety is its neighbours, its distance, and its unimportance.
No organized force. Defense relies on individual armed citizens. No command structure, no training, no coordinated response.
You can walk into Rasmahal from any direction and pass nobody on the way in. The village has no organized force. Defense here is individual armed citizens, with no command, no training and no way to coordinate a response. Whatever defence Rasmahal keeps, a visitor would find nothing arcane in it.
Supporting Capabilities▲
Legal InfrastructureNone
No deterrence beyond force.
Medical Readiness
Healer (divine, 1st level). Wounds and fevers treated one at a time; no beds to contain an outbreak.

Rasmahal's treasury takes in a little more than it pays out each year. It is enough, and no more.
The forge is lit early, and its hammering is the first sound of most mornings.
Of an evening people sit out at their doors, and by day they meet by the house of worship.
Children are warned about getting mixed up in the grown-ups' quarrels.

## Relationships
Relationships
Internal relationships
•
Saad al-Masri and Hana al-Misri · Outstanding Debt
Hana al-Misri paid Saad al-Masri's bail eleven years ago and has never mentioned it. Saad al-Masri mentions it to no one.
Hana al-Misri has asked Saad al-Masri for a vote against his own interests. It is the first payment Hana al-Misri has asked for.
•
Halima al-Nuri and Tamim al-Isfahani · Professional Rivalry
Halima al-Nuri and Tamim al-Isfahani trained together and have competed ever since. The settlement keeps score, and so do they.
A post opens at midsummer, and only Halima al-Nuri or Tamim al-Isfahani can fill it.
•
Uzma al-Basri and Umar al-Aziz · Outstanding Debt
Uzma al-Basri owes Umar al-Aziz a large sum in coin, borrowed in a bad year and repaid in small amounts ever since.
Uzma al-Basri has offered to clear what is left of the debt in one payment. Umar al-Aziz would rather be repaid in favours.
•
Umar al-Aziz and Ola al-Masri · Professional Rivalry
Umar al-Aziz wants Ola al-Masri's position, and she wants his reputation. They are civil in public and unforgiving in private.
Ola al-Masri has been telling clients that Umar al-Aziz's work is slipping. Two of the clients have repeated it to Umar al-Aziz.
•
Jassim al-Mawsili and Halima al-Nuri · Pragmatic Alliance
Jassim al-Mawsili and Halima al-Nuri are allies with separate interests. They meet to coordinate and leave by different doors.
Halima al-Nuri has asked Jassim al-Mawsili to back a candidate him distrusts. Refusing would cost the alliance its majority.
•
Husayn al-Farabi and Amina al-Isfahani · Professional Rivalry
Every friend Husayn al-Farabi wins is one Amina al-Isfahani lost, and the reverse. The two keep a polite distance and an exact tally.
Husayn al-Farabi and Amina al-Isfahani will both present proposals at the next council session. Only one will be accepted.
•
Tamim al-Isfahani and Jassim al-Mawsili · Genuine Respect
Tamim al-Isfahani trusts Jassim al-Mawsili's word over any written contract. Jassim al-Mawsili has never given a reason to regret it.
Jassim al-Mawsili has publicly backed a plan that Tamim al-Isfahani thinks will fail. Tamim al-Isfahani has said so once, in private.
•
Ola al-Masri and Halima al-Nuri · Outstanding Debt
Halima al-Nuri kept Ola al-Masri's name out of a scandal that ruined two others. Ola al-Masri has taken Halima al-Nuri's side ever since.
Halima al-Nuri has mentioned the debt aloud, at a dinner, in front of Ola al-Masri's partners.
•
Amina al-Isfahani and Halima al-Nuri · Mentor and Successor
Amina al-Isfahani vouched for Halima al-Nuri when nobody else would. Halima al-Nuri's first post came from that word.
Halima al-Nuri corrected Amina al-Isfahani in public last month, and was right.
•
Hana al-Misri and Halima al-Nuri · Outstanding Debt
Halima al-Nuri nursed Hana al-Misri through a winter fever when no one else would come. Hana al-Misri still owes the debt and still knows it.
Hana al-Misri has refused Halima al-Nuri a small favour for the first time in years.
