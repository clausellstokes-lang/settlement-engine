The place
The first person you meet on the road into Prabhapuram wants to know your business, and the second already knows it. Shaded doorways open onto the ground between the houses. A barrel thuds down from a cart, and somebody inside cheers its arrival. People draw water in an order nobody needs to explain. A merchant shows off a new cloak, and several neighbours make a point of noticing.

Guard assessment
Nobody here is paid to keep order. A quarrel goes to the heads of the oldest families, and most are settled before they come to blows.
Prabhapuram would struggle to hold against anything more than a handful of raiders, and the people who live there know it.

Internal Security
No court sits at Prabhapuram to decide a quarrel, and the place gets along without one.
Economic Survival
Weak▼
Prabhapuram is well off, but little of its wealth is set aside for its defence, and a long crisis would soon outrun what is.
Disasters & Famine
Food endurance: No granary: under siege the outlying fields would be lost; household stores would carry the hamlet for about a month and a half at full rations, about three months on half, and what is still harvested would stretch them further.
Criminal Architecture & Public Order▲
Order here rests with the neighbours: little happens that someone does not see.
With no gaol at Prabhapuram, nobody who does wrong here can be locked away; most go on living beside the people they wronged.
What goes wrong at Prabhapuram goes wrong one thing at a time and belongs to whoever did it.
Armed Forces & Fortifications▲
## Armed Forces & Fortifications
Unfortified. No perimeter walls. Defenders cannot control entry points or create chokepoints.
Prabhapuram is too small to wall and knows it; the hamlet's safety is its neighbours, its distance, and its unimportance.
No organized force. Defense relies on individual armed citizens. No command structure, no training, no coordinated response.
You can walk into Prabhapuram from any direction and pass nobody on the way in. Nobody at Prabhapuram is responsible for defending the hamlet; ask who is, and nobody can name anyone. Anything unseen that came at Prabhapuram would find nothing to meet it: the hamlet keeps no wards or seers to notice it.

Prabhapuram is well past getting by and short of the deepest wealth: it spends freely on itself, and keeps no great hoard.
The day begins with the walk out to the fields, and the work there runs on until midday.
After the day in the fields, people gather at the alehouse to talk it over.
Children are warned about the usual things: trampling the young crops, and straying past the last of the fields.

Kept in Seedtime, the third and fourth weeks.
