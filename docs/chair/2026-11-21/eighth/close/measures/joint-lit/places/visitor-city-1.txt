Celtic-inspired; people here live in a place built around kin districts and an assembly ground. Governed by Merchant City Council · Stable
"Money is discussed openly here in a way that scandalises visitors from more genteel places. The Economic Divide taught everyone that pretending not to care about it is a luxury for people who already have it"

The place
A child runs past you on the road into Caersike, chasing something or being chased by something (unclear which). Nobody in the street pays attention. The citadel on the high ground makes clear that this settlement has been defended before and expects to be again. Magic is a trade in Caersike like any other: signs offer enchanting and identification by the hour. A boy is introduced to a neighbour as the household's foster-son, and is treated in every way as one of its own. Window boxes of flowers hang below more than one sill.

Defensive posture
Fortress
Guard assessment
The garrison and city watch walk the usual rounds. That stops a pickpocket or a brawl; anyone who has learned the rounds can work between them. Offenders the garrison and city watch catches are tried at the court and held in the prison. Everyone comes and goes by the gates, so smugglers have to get past the garrison and city watch.
The city does not spend much time thinking about being attacked: its defences are in good order, and its people know it.

Strong▼
The country around Caersike holds very little in the way of creatures; whatever the city's works are for, it is not them.
Invasion & War
Approaching Caersike, you see the walls and the soldiers who belong on them, and any attacker would think again about the cost. While the trades behind Caersike's defence keep working, its defenders will not go short.
Internal Security
Bring a complaint at Caersike and there is a court to hear it and a cell for whoever it finds against.
Economic Survival
The city could go through a bad season with its arrangements intact, and the people who would have to be paid through one know it.
Disasters & Famine
Adequate▼
Food endurance: Under siege the outlying fields would be lost; the granary would carry the city for about nine months at full rations, more than a year on half, and what is still harvested would stretch it further.
Criminal Architecture & Public Order▲
Moderate day to day; the law is strong.
Most trouble here keeps to the edges and the late hours. The garrison holds the gates and patrols the main streets; quieter spots after dark carry real risk. Locals know which lanes to keep out of. The walls see to what comes from outside; what trouble there is starts at home.
Caersike keeps a working balance: the law is present, so is crime, and neither has the city to itself.
Criminal structure: Semi-organized networks
Criminal activity is coordinated enough to maintain routes and territories but lacks a single controlling authority. Several gangs may be competing. Less predictable than a guild, more structured than street crime.

Territorial gangs control specific districts, extorting local businesses and residents. Violence is episodic rather than constant: mostly enforcing territorial boundaries.
More than one crew works Caersike, but the city's law is heavier than any of them, and none of them can fight for the streets in the open. Anyone at Caersike who knows whom to ask can buy contraband or stolen goods at the black market.

## The Green Fair
Founding
Kept in Seedtime, the fifth and sixth weeks.
A fair day that marks the founding. It falls to the Merchant City Council. It goes back 357 years, to the founding of Caersike by royal decree and a century of forced investment.

Stone
Kept in Deepwinter, the eleventh and twelfth weeks.
A fair with traders from outside that stays true to the standing stones. It is held at the market squares, in the keeping of the Merchant City Council. Market taxes bring Caersike more than anything else.

GOVERNANCE
The Merchant City Council governs Caersike. Its seat is held by Nechtan Fitzgerald, the innkeeper and Lord Burgomaster. Prosperity has earned the Merchant City Council the approval of Caersike. Two rival guilds are contesting control of the main trade route. Neither side will back down and the Merchant City Council is avoiding the question.
