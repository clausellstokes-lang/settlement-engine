The place
Trees lean over the river on the way into Hartgrund, and the first houses stand just above the line where the bank turns to mud. The gatehouse arch frames the main road. Magic is a trade in Hartgrund like any other: signs offer enchanting and identification by the hour. A craftsman shows off a finished piece in his doorway, and passers-by judge it out loud. Doors and shutters here have been mended more often than they have been painted.

Criminal Architecture & Public Order▲
Most trouble here keeps to the edges and the late hours. The watch holds the gates and walks the main streets; quieter spots after dark carry real risk. Residents keep to the places they know once night falls. Whoever comes in passes the gates; the trouble that remains is the place's own.
Visitors to Hartgrund are told which few streets to avoid, and the advice turns out to be right.
Criminal structure: Diffuse criminal presence
Thieves, fences and bandits each work for themselves; there is no boss to threaten or to bribe, which makes them harder to stamp out.
Crime at Hartgrund is opportunistic and unorganized: fences, bandits and small operators working for themselves, with nothing to threaten and nobody to buy.

GOVERNANCE
Adelheid Rosenfeld, the alchemist and Archmage of the Council, holds the seat of the Arcane Council. The Arcane Council governs Hartgrund. Poverty has left the Arcane Council's right to rule contested in Hartgrund. The Arcane Council has been debating market levies for three months. The merchants have argued against every one of them, and nothing has been settled yet.
