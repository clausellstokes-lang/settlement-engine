The place
The first building you pass on the way into Suryaagam is somebody's house, and so is the second, and so, it turns out, is most of the rest. Households cluster around shared wells and water channels. Flour dust lies pale on the doorsteps, and the whole place smells of the ovens. Food is being handed out to anyone who asks, and the line is patient.

Suryaagam grows more than it eats, with some to put by against a bad year.

What it puts on the table itself is bread from the home fields. Irrigation, tanks, street kitchens, preservation, and ritual food rules shape supply and consumption.

Disasters & Famine
Adequate▼
Food endurance: No granary: under siege the outlying fields would be lost; household stores would carry the village for about a month and a half at full rations, about three months on half, and what is still harvested would stretch them further.
Criminal Architecture & Public Order▲
Safety here depends on where you are, and when. Locals watch over the main paths; quieter spots after dark carry genuine risk. Most people here know where trouble is likely, and keep clear of it.
Visitors to Suryaagam are told which few streets to avoid, and the advice turns out to be right.
Suryaagam's books record no gang, guild or racket: what wrongdoing the village has, people do on their own account.
Armed Forces & Fortifications▲
## Armed Forces & Fortifications
Unfortified. No perimeter walls. Defenders cannot control entry points or create chokepoints.
Suryaagam has no wall, and the beasts of the country around it come right up to the last houses.
No organized force. Defense relies on individual armed citizens. No command structure, no training, no coordinated response.
You can walk into Suryaagam from any direction and pass nobody on the way in. The village has no organized force. Defense here is individual armed citizens, with no command, no training and no way to coordinate a response. The village sends whoever it can against things they are not trained for, and pays for it each time. Whoever defends Suryaagam, none of them works with magic.
