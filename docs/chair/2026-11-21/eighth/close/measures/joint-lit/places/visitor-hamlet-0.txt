The place
Washing hangs in the yards along the road into Niederwalde, and someone calls a greeting from behind it without looking to see who you are. Half-timbered upper floors project above the busier lanes. Drovers push their herds along the road, and the whole place smells of the pens. Two neighbours argue over a boundary stone, each quoting what their grandfathers agreed.

## Armed Forces & Fortifications
Unfortified. No perimeter walls. Defenders cannot control entry points or create chokepoints.
No wall marks where Niederwalde ends; the last houses simply give way to open country.
No organized force. Defense relies on individual armed citizens. No command structure, no training, no coordinated response.
Niederwalde is unfortified. There is no perimeter, which means the hamlet cannot control entry and cannot make a chokepoint of anything. Nobody at Niederwalde is responsible for defending the hamlet; ask who is, and nobody can name anyone. The hamlet sends whoever it can against things they are not trained for, and pays for it each time. Whoever defends Niederwalde, none of them works with magic.
