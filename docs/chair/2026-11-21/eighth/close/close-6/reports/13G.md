## Step 2: THE STOP
**The case.** File `src/generators/power/factionStanding.js :: annotateFactionStanding`. The rule asks that its two
`seatBought ? ''` gates "take THE RECORD'S READING at every re-annotation AFTER the coherence seam". The function cannot be given
that reading by its arguments at those re-annotations.

**1. What the function receives at the re-annotation** (read, CONFIRMED).
- Both re-annotations go through `power/rosterText.js :: standingsOf(roster, force, seat)`:
  - `settleStandingToRoster`, lines 85 and 86, called from `steps/assembleSettlement.js:360`;
  - `settleStandingToLegitimacy`, lines 213 and 214.
- `standingsOf` hands `annotateFactionStanding` two things:
  - `seats`, copies of `standingRosterOf(...)` rows that carry only `{ faction, power, isGoverning, desc: '' }`;
  - `seat = { publicLegitimacy }`.
- No relation row, no `captureState`, and no record reaches it.
- `settleStandingToRoster`'s own `powerStructure` does hold the rewritten rows (it runs after `generateCoherence` at line 343), but
  it does not pass them on.

**2. Even with the rows, a gate inside the function could not move a card** (read, CONFIRMED).
- Both settles work by difference. Each computes a `before` and an `after` standing through the same function, and rewrites a card
  only where the two differ.
- Any reading the function took from what it is handed would be the same on both sides. The standings would come out equal, so the
  printed war lines would stay.
- The cure needs `before` to read "not bought" (what the power step wrote) and `after` to read the record's rows. Only the callers
  in `rosterText.js` can tell those two calls apart.
- `rosterText.js` is outside this unit's manifest. The brief allows `factionStanding.js` and, only for a helper, the module that
  owns the rows' capture.

**3. The order on the named births** (probe, CONFIRMED). I re-used LIT-13G's instrumented scratch copy of the flip tree's `src`,
with my driver `scratchpad/13g-probe/probe.mjs`. That driver is `probeOrder13g.mjs` with a `both1` force. Run through the mutex;
log `scratchpad/flip/13G/probe.log`, one file per birth in `scratchpad/13g-probe/`. It covered all seven named cases (four lit,
three dark) and r2r-city-5 lit. On each one:
- `withHallCapture` turns the rows `corrupted` first.
- Then `settleStandingToRoster` re-annotates (`rosterText.js:85/86`) with `seatBought:false` and the war lines kept.
- On r2r-city-5 lit, `settleStandingToLegitimacy` (`:213/214`) does the same again.
