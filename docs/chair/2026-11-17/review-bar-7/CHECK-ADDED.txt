 (4) WHAT THE PAGE NOW PRINTS BY DESIGN (the readers' brief, its paragraphs "NEW SINCE THE LAST READ, AND BY DESIGN", "THE HOUSE'S MARK ON A PERSON'S CARD", "VERY RARE SECRETS" and, where it has it, "THE RESOURCES TAB'S Nearby Resources BLOCK": READ THEM FIRST). A flag against something those paragraphs call by design is EXPLAINED (say which sentence of the brief covers it); a flag that is one of their "WHAT IS A FAULT" cases STANDS if the town's record bears it out: check the supply story in the record itself (`config.nearbyResources`, `config.nearbyResourcesDepleted`, `economicState.primaryImports`, `resourceAnalysis.gaps`, the house's card and mark in the dump). THE RESOURCES BLOCK IS PRINTED LABEL-FIRST: under "Nearby Resources" stand first the grounds a house proves (no label), then the line "Depleted" FOLLOWED BY the depleted grounds, then the line "Abundant" FOLLOWED BY the grounds still worked. Most of the first readers did not know this: a flag that read a ground as "Abundant" (or "Depleted") only because that word stood on the line AFTER it is EXPLAINED where the label-first reading agrees with the record's two lists: quote both lists. (5) KNOWN AND ALREADY BEING CURED: judge these as any other flag (a real contradiction STANDS), and OPEN YOUR NOTE WITH "IN FLIGHT:" when the flag is one of: a chain card that lists a mine under "Via" for a chain "From … (depleted)" while that mine is unmarked and at work; an income share printed plain ("Metal is smelted or worked here.") where the only smith is short of iron and a mine works; a maltster or brewer that "requires a grain surplus" at work where the food line is short; a forge scene line beside a smith marked short of iron; a history line naming "a rival lord" or "the reformers" where the town has none; a brewer who "hears everything the taverns say" in a place with no tavern; a soft "eats well" line where the food word is not Secure; an export or "Produced locally" pill naming a good whose every maker is marked short.
