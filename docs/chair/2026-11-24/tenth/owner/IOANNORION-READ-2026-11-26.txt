######## SUMMARY (153 lines)
Ioannorion
Village · 215 pop. · crossroads · est. ~113 yrs ago
Copy
Ioannorion is a comfortable village. A crossroads market. Founded approximately 113 years ago. It sits on an active frontier. Power rests with religious authorities; stability: anxious — people fear the sickness more than they heed orders. The streets are not safe. The sick have been shut away under quarantine. Many ignore it, and two people who tried to enforce it were beaten..
Disease Outbreak
Active crisis
Something is spreading in Ioannorion. It is not yet a plague, but it will be if nothing changes. The sick outnumber those who can tend them. The origin is disputed. Quarantine measures are being resisted.
Hook:
The first person to name the outbreak has gone quiet. Their last message suggested the disease is not natural. Reaching them means crossing a quarantine that is not being enforced consistently.
Arrival
Some of Ioannorion's market stalls are closed. Not all, not most, but several, in a pattern that isn't about the day of the week. The ones that are open are busy; the ones that are closed have been for a while. Carts of grain rattle toward the mills while the ovens send the smell of bread down every lane. Members of some association walk together, each wearing the same small badge, and greet one another by it.

Ioannorion's purse closes every year ahead. The margin is not large but it is dependable, and there is money laid by behind it.
Ask who decides things here, and the answer is the Religious Authorities. This is frontier country, and people speak of the wild beyond as close at hand. Any business with the Elder Council means a long wait, and visitors are warned of it.
The first hour is spent seeing that nothing was disturbed in the night; only then does the ordinary work begin.
The hunters bring in more than enough, and game is often in the pot.
Where the water is drawn and out on the common, people stop to talk as they pass.

Commoners resent the council's indifference to ordinary complaints.
