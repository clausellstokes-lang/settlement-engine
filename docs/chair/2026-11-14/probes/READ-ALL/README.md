# READ-ALL: every distinct sentence the tabs print, read once against the hardest town that prints it

- `build_packets.py <census out-dir (720)> <bar out-dir (10)> <out-dir> [words a packet]`: the packets (`main-NN`, `crisis-NN`, `cal-NN`) and `index.json` (id: the folded sentence, the towns that print it, its sections, the town it was shown under). The census out-dirs are the CENSUS-3 command's (`census3.sh <tree> <out> 4`, and again with `--seeds seeds.bar0.txt`), run at the joined line's e5b421802.
- `packets/*.txt.gz`: the packets as the readers were given them. `index.compact.json.gz`: the index without the town lists (a count instead).
- `merge_flags.py <packets dir> <repo with the readers' branches fetched> <bar list json> <bar dumps dir> <out dir>`: the flags merged, one row a sentence ranked by the towns that print it, and the read's recall on the calibration pages against the bar's first read.
- `sentences.py`: the first measure (how many distinct sentences there are).
- The readers' brief: `docs/chair/2026-11-14/briefs/BRIEF-READ-ALL.md`. Their flags: `docs/chair/2026-11-14/read-all/<packet>.flags.jsonl` on `lane/read-all-2026-11-14-<packet>` (crisis-07's file is on the crisis-06 branch).
- `inventory.py <census out-dir> <out.json>` and `inventory_diff.py <before.json> <after.json> [<worklist.json>]`: THE INVENTORY DIFF: what a change did to the page text across the census towns: every sentence that left, arrived, or prints in fewer or more towns, the worklist's rows before and after, and THE INHERITORS (what now prints in the towns that lost a sentence). Two census runs of the same seeds (before and after a batch of cures) are its whole input.
- `trace_flags.py <merge out dir> <census out-dir> <tree> <CENSUS-3 probes dir> <out dir>`: each flagged sentence traced to its writer by the census's own tracer and given to the lane that holds the writer's file: one `WORKLIST-<lane>.md` a lane.
- THE SECOND READ: `build_packets2.py` (run with `SKIP_FOLDS=<the first read's flagged.sentences.json>`): a fuller header (FOOD, LAND, QUARRELS, PAST, PEOPLE) and the first read's HIGH and MED sentences left out; its packets under `packets2/`; its brief `briefs/BRIEF-READ-ALL-2.md`; its flags `docs/chair/2026-11-14/read-all-2/<packet>.flags.jsonl` on `lane/read-all-2-2026-11-14-<packet>`.
- `group_worklists.py <trace dir> <merge dir> <out dir>`: the traced rows partitioned into groups of files (no file in two groups): `docs/chair/2026-11-14/read-all/WORKLIST-<group>.md` and `.json`, `RECORD-FLAGS.md` (every flag on a header: the record's own incoherence), `LOW-FLAGS.json`.
