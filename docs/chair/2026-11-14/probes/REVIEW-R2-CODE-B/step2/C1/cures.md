## Method
- food-tip.tsv: every surface's food word per tip town (Overview Systems Health, Daily Life tile, Crisis "Food:", tonight.txt, PDF canon Tonight block and Identity anchor, Foundry Tonight and Economics, Economics "Short:", Foundations), with the record's label, band, gapPct, normalImportPct and storageMonths (rec.mjs, foodsurf.mjs, tally.cjs).
- Q Tonight: composeTonightSheet at q over births-q (tnfood.mjs); spans checked against storageMonths (span.cjs).
- Scope line: cultureScopeLine at q and tip over each tree's births (scope-all.mjs); residual counts by tier and populationBandOf (scope-cmp2.mjs); diffs grouped (scope-changed.json).
- Keepers: seatKeeperOf/keeperFormOf at each tree (keeper.mjs), checked against the Power tab and Relationships in the tip dumps.
- Wants: composeTonightSheet people vs npcs[].goal.short at q and tip (wants.mjs); the replaced lines judged against seat vacancy, grammar and membership (wantsjudge.mjs).
- Needs: "^Needs …[a-z][A-Z]" in the Q and tip dumps; the impact sentence's towns checked against stress and route.
- Quick guide: composeSettlementQuickGuide's material-truth food clause at tip (qg2.mjs).
