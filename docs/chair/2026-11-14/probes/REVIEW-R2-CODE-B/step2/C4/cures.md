## Method
- I birthed the row towns at tip and Q with tools/births.mjs (the rw3 and rr2s seeds). For the rww- and rpf- seeds I used a copy (births2.mjs) that sends them to rw3's cfgFor. I checked that the facts match the rows (power figures, Li Song, the forced isolation rows) before using them.
- Writers were called on the records: faithPanelModel (faith line), relationshipsDeskLists + the tab's conflictsElsewhere predicate, seatKeeperOf/keeperFormOf (the relationship card's roleShown), allegianceOf + the card's handlerOfHouse predicate. Each was checked against the 1536 tip page dumps: keeper lines 207/207, handler lines 1256/1256, faith lines on the page and the PDF.
- Removals were read by script on the records (faith Q vs tip; political weight against powerStructure factions, powerLabel and the page's "decisions that matter are made by" line; handler house and memberships), plus page text samples.
- Scripts and outputs are in step2/C4/ (faith.mjs, keeper.mjs, alleg.mjs, rel.mjs, blabel.mjs, *.jsonl).
