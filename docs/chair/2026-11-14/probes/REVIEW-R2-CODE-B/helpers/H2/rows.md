## Method
Scripts in /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-settlement-engine/a2e70bbe-8ee3-5a4b-b408-20d6830901c8/scratchpad/helpers/H2/:
- load.mjs: streams the records. REC=<file> selects another birth file.
- temps.mjs, then temps.txt: every minted person.
- brunt2.mjs: house member counts and the lowest rank for the brunt reasons.
- bruntstake.mjs: the brunt house's stake on the same tab.
- cast2.mjs: lever Access rows, the defender house and any face of it, treater roles, instigatorGains stakes, gainsHolds faces, gainsClaim.
- plague.mjs: crisisHookFor over every plague entry, with the holder's roster role and healer cards and houses.
- hooks.mjs: crisisHooksOf over every entry, flagging brackets, institution holders and suppressed plural holders.
- famine.mjs and narr.mjs: famine variants against foodSources.
- war.mjs: wartime variants against the atoms, through factsOf and atomHolds.
- pdfcrisis.mjs: the PDF cast and lever lines through composeCrisisPage and institutionHouseLabel.
- tonight.mjs and tonight2.mjs: the minted persons through composeTonightSheet, against the Tonight facts.
- tempcards.mjs, then tempcards-r2.txt: each census minted card and every line naming the person, from the dumps.
- crisis.sh: prints a dump's Crisis tab.

Every count was checked with grep -l -F over render-tip/dumps and surf-tip (pdf, foundry, tonight).
