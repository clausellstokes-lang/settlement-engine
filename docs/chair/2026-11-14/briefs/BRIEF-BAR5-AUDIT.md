## The laws

Each detector is a pure function over one town: `{ record, tabs }` where `tabs` maps a tab's name to its printed lines. It returns instances `{ law, town, tab, line, against: { tab, line or record field }, why }`. A detector is CONSERVATIVE FIRST: an instance must be a contradiction a careful reader would accept by the second check's rules (the page marks a row the crisis touches, and a description of ordinary times under a marked row or card is NOT false; a plot hook or a rumour states its own fact; fantasy is no fault). Read `note` and `evidence` of every control row before you write a pattern: they say exactly what disagrees with what.
