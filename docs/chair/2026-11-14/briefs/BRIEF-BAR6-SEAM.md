S1. YOUR BASE is the joined line at the tip your prompt names. You cut your one branch from it. You merge nothing, ever.
S2. THE CHAIR CANNOT REACH YOU INSIDE A LONG TURN (a line sent to a seat waited two and a half hours last night).
    EVERYTHING YOU NEED IS IN THIS FILE. Decide inside your section. What you cannot do cleanly, LEAVE BY NAME in your
    hand-back with the reason and what you measured. Ask the chair only where your section says "ASK", and then end
    your turn so the answer can reach you.

    yours, whatever the cure seems to need: name it. The shared facts' files (`src/domain/facts/factVocabulary.js`,

    `sovereign`, `monster frontier`, `food surplus`, `crime wave`, `force: kept|paid|volunteers`, `exported: <good>`,

    RE-KEYED BY IDENTITY (the same claim on another town that still bears it, the assertion's form kept, a dated note

    <render-dir> <out.json>`, or `docs/chair/2026-11-16/bar5/AUDIT/run-a.mjs` / `runB.mjs`), over the 150 wide-read towns

S7. YOUR NET BEFORE YOUR LAST PUSH, each alone, to a file through `sh scripts/gate-tail.sh`: `npm run typecheck:domain:strict`;

    ADD NO FRESH LINE to either; stale rows are the close's: you run no `--shrink` and no `--bank`).
