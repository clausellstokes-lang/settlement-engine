## The rules

**C1 · EVERY RED IS CLASSED BEFORE IT IS TOUCHED.** For each failing test of your list, read its own message and its
file's header, and class it, in your hand-back, as exactly one of:
 (a) A STORED FIGURE MOVED BY LANDED WORK: the figure is right for the tree and the stored value is old. Re-taken (C2).
 (b) A STALE ANCHOR: the test's claim still holds, its town or row moved. Re-keyed BY IDENTITY (the same claim, another
     anchor, a dated note naming the old anchor and why it left; shown red once in scratch, then restored).
 (c) A FAULT: the page, the record or the code is wrong, or the move is one no landed work explains. NOT re-taken, NOT
     re-keyed: named to the chair in one line with the evidence, and left red. A close that hides a fault is worse
     than a late close.
 (d) THE MACHINE'S OWN: a watchdog by the clock, a missing git ref, a tool not installed. Named, with the proof that the
     assertion itself holds.

**C2 · A FIGURE IS RE-TAKEN ONLY THROUGH ITS OWN DOOR.** The tool its test's message or its file's header names (a
script's `--write`, `--shrink`, `--bank-new`, `--tighten`, an `UPDATE_…` variable, a generator, a build), run as that
header says (a clean committed tree where it asks for one). A figure kept by hand is edited in the form its own header
prescribes (a dated note, a declared row, a SHIFT RECORD entry: copy the form of the last lawful entry). NEVER: an
assertion loosened, a ceiling raised without its declared row, a test skipped, quarantined, deleted or wrapped in a
condition; never a blanket snapshot update whose diff you did not read.

**C3 · EVERY RE-TAKE IS ACCOUNTED.** One row a figure in your hand-back: the file · the reading test · the old value ·
the new value · the door used (the command, verbatim) · THE CAUSE: the landed point or points that moved it (the rows
of `docs/chair/2026-11-17/sixth/LANDINGS-SIXTH.md`, points 33 to 53, and `docs/chair/2026-11-14/JOIN-17-HANDBACK.md`,
points 1 to 32, name each landing's declared moves), or "accumulated over points N to M" where the figure is a count.
A move you cannot lay to landed work is class (c).

**C4 · A REGISTER OF DEBT ONLY SHRINKS.** A shrink-only register is shrunk through its door. Where the tree holds MORE
than the register allows (a fresh row; a count over its frozen ceiling; a new unbanked line), you do NOT bank it and do
NOT raise the ceiling on your own: you list the rows for the chair (file, row, the landed point that added each, and
the lawful cure the test's message names: declare the line, convert the test, add the roster row with its reason). The
chair rules each, or asks the owner.

**C5 · THE CLOSE MOVES NO TOWN AND NO PAGE.** No change under `src/` that changes what a settlement is or what any
surface prints. A GENERATED artifact (a bundle, a generated table, a census file) is regenerated by its own generator,
and its diff is explained in your hand-back (what moved and why). If a door's run changes a file under `src/` that is
not a generated artifact, STOP and tell the chair.

**C6 · YOUR FILES ARE YOUR MANIFEST.** Your prompt lists the tests you clear and the stored files you may write. A red
of your list whose cure is in another seat's file is named to the chair, not taken. Two seats never write one file.

**C7 · THE NET.** Each test you cleared, alone, green at your tip (and red at the base: say so). All of `tests/lint`
BY MESSAGE at the base and at your tip (a register, roster or walker that counts files or tests moves when you add or
change one: say each such move; they are re-taken last, C8). Both type gates; `npx eslint` on what you changed. Every
gate alone, through `sh scripts/gate-tail.sh <command…>`, never read through a pipe.

**C8 · THE ORDER.** The chair merges the seats' branches on the line one after another. THE FIGURES THAT COUNT THE TREE
ITSELF (the estate's file census, the test ratchet, the mutation manifest's totality, byte and size ceilings, the
committed bundles and their freshness stamps) ARE RE-TAKEN LAST, by the last seat, on the tree that holds every other
close commit: do not re-take one of these unless your prompt gives it to you.

**C9 · PUSH DISCIPLINE.** Every push to this repository is counted as one deployment by the site's host, whose daily
allowance is spent. COMMIT AS OFTEN AS YOU LIKE; PUSH ONLY: your first commit (the hand-back's title line, to prove
you can), then at most once an hour, and once at your hand-back. If you are about to stop for any reason, push first.
Every push runs its hooks: never `--no-verify`, never a force push. Never a pull request, never `master`, never a
deploy, a hosted database or a production credential. A refusal by your own permission check stands: tell the chair.

**C10 · THE HAND-BACK** (`docs/chair/2026-11-18/close/<SEAT>-HANDBACK.md`): the table of C3; the class of every red of
your list (C1); the rows you listed for the chair (C4); the net (C7); what you left, by name, with the file and the
line. Then ONE message to the chair of at most 150 words (the send_message tool of the Claude Code Remote MCP server,
session_id session_01FbPLbcLDSJzZFzb5B7EjxR): your tip; how many of your list are green, how many left and why.
