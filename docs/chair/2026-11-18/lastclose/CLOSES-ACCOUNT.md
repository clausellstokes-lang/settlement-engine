# THE CLOSES' ACCOUNT

Compiled 2026-11-19 from the hand-backs of the first close and of both seats of the last close (the signing seat's section and the reconciliations by the chair). Read-only compilation: no repository file was changed. Every figure is quoted as the hand-back writes it, with its source in the last column.

## In plain words

A "close" is the moment the program's stored reference figures are brought up to date. These are the pinned counts, the registers of known debt, the golden hashes and the size ceilings that tests compare freshly generated towns against. Landed work has moved them. Each figure is re-taken through its own procedure (its "door"), and every change is laid to the landed work ("point") that caused it. The first close (2026-11-18 morning) covers points 33 to 53 and ran under the owner's word "yes: refresh the stored reference files and open the pull request." The last close (2026-11-18/19 night) covers points 54 to 70 and ran under the owner's "Yes to both". That word covered the refresh and the golden door's signing, and for this last close only. Neither word covers a merge to master, a deploy or a migration. The chair ruled each doubtful case (the rulings CL.., FR.. and LC.. named below), and a register of debt only shrinks unless a ruling lets it grow. The last close's second seat (the golden door and the figures that count the tree) is not finished.

Abbreviations for sources (all under `docs/chair/2026-11-18/`): **1A** = `close/CLOSE-DOORS-1A-HANDBACK.md`; **1B** = `close/CLOSE-DOORS-1B-HANDBACK.md`; **D2** = `close/CLOSE-DOORS-2-HANDBACK.md`; **SW** = `close/CLOSE-SWEEP-HANDBACK.md`; **WK** = `close/CLOSE-WALKERS-HANDBACK.md`; **LD** = `lastclose/LASTCLOSE-DOORS-HANDBACK.md`; **BRIEF-C** = `(the chair lane) docs/chair/2026-11-18/close/BRIEF-CLOSE.md`; **BRIEF-L** = `(the chair lane) docs/chair/2026-11-18/lastclose/BRIEF-LASTCLOSE.md`; **LAND** = `docs/chair/2026-11-17/sixth/LANDINGS-SIXTH.md`. A note on causes: the first-close hand-backs mostly name the landing by its round and branch (BAR5-.. to BAR8-.., DEBT-1/2, SECRETS-1, BIRTH-1/3) and by a JOIN-17 point number (1 to 32), as well as the sixth chair's points 33 to 53. They are quoted that way.
