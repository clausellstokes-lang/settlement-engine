# The Economics page in the 97 towns where the label and the output disagree (COHERENCE-UPKEEP, ruling 7)

The corpus is the lane's 360 births, drawn on the upkeep lane. Prosperity and output move in none of them between the AGREE tip 65ce74ce6 and the upkeep tip, so the list is the same at both. Seeds are `agree-<tier>-<i>`, every dial random. A town is listed where the economy's output says one thing and the prosperity label says another: the output is 50 or more (the old full-funding point) in a Poor or Struggling town, or under 50 in a Comfortable, Prosperous or Wealthy one. There are 97: Comfortable 48, Prosperous 34, Poor 12, Struggling 3.

Each row gives three things: the header chip ("Output N/100", coloured green at 60 and above, amber at 35 and above, red below 35); DS-ECO-1, the header sentence; and DS-ECO-8, the prosperity rung.

**What it shows.** Every sentence follows the label. DS-ECO-1's pool is keyed on the prosperity rank (`economyStateProse.js :: prosperityHeaderPoolKey`), and so is the rung. **No sentence contradicts the label**, so no sentence is a class member, and none was cured.

**The one thing that disagrees is the chip**, which is a number and not a sentence:
- 43 Comfortable or Prosperous towns print a red Output (as low as 4/100) beside their word.
- 7 Poor or Struggling towns print a green Output (up to 70/100).

The chip is the owner's UI ruling (RULINGS-T2 T2-1: the Output score is printed once on the header chip). It is reported here and was not changed.
