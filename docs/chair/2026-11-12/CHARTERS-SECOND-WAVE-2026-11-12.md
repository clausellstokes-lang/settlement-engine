## 0. What binds every lane of this wave

The first wave's shared law still binds (`docs/chair/2026-11-11/CHARTERS-COHERENCE-2026-11-11.md`, section 0), with what the first wave taught written over it here.

1. **Read first**, at your checkout: `docs/OPERATING_STANDARD.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, this file whole, and the census rows of your own patterns. This charter is your whole assignment: no adjacent repair, however tempting.

3. **The five laws.** (L1) At every commit that touches `src/`: the strict-domain gate, the type ratchet, and the linter on the touched files, each read through `scripts/gate-tail.sh`, never through a pipe. (L2) Every count on all six sizes, calm and in crisis. (L3) A claim that nothing moves is a field diff, base against tip, over the golden's 525 configurations and the corpus. (L4) The lane's ONE draft record declares all six golden surfaces moved or unmoved by measure: the generator golden master, the espionage fence, the dossier prose manifest, the PDF golden, the Herald desk's golden and the preset-lighting witness. (L5) The last statement of failing tests is over the whole suite, once, at the final tip, every red title classed.
4. **Guard first, red first, one cause per commit.** A guard is a test with its register behind its own door; a register is shrink-only from its first commit; no row is banked in the test ratchet; no test is skipped; no pin is loosened (re-key by identity, with the cause); no ceiling of the repetition guard is raised and its tightening door is the chair's alone.
5. **A fact has one home.** Every cure names the home, makes every writer read it, and leaves a member in a guard that would have caught the fault. No new key in the settlement record and no change of its shape.
6. **The rendered-dossier arm** (new; the first lane builds it as its first act, and every later page check uses it). The page battery (`src/domain/coherence/coherenceBattery.js`) has checks and nothing that runs them at scale. So: (i) a census script beside the repetition guard's, which births a corpus, renders each dossier through the real container as its owner sees it (every group, every sub-tab, every disclosure opened; style elements skipped), and runs every page check over it, with a register behind its own door and a door for a fresh corpus by seed prefix; (ii) a walker in the suite on a fixed grid of seeds in six sizes, calm and in crisis, bounded to stay inside the suite's time; (iii) every page check added in this wave has a row in that arm and a planted fault that reds it, executed and named in the hand-back.
7. **The goldens are the merge seat's to sign.** A lane runs no golden's door. It drafts one shift record, named for the lane, beside this file, and names the rows red at its tip as the merge seat's act.
8. **New words** obey the owner's style law, are declared from their birth by the pool census's law, and are listed in the hand-back for the owner's veto.
9. **Stops** — write to the chair and wait on that item: a label, a band or a score moves; in a display-only lane, ANY byte of a generated record moves; a person, a roster or a history event is re-dealt beyond what your section declares; a cure needs a file another lane holds; a rate more than twice the census's; anything that would need a pin loosened, a test skipped or a ceiling raised; context near 450k (bring the hand-back file current first).
10. **Your files only.** A member of your class in another lane's file is reported, never edited.
11. **The edge bundles** are re-emitted through their own scripts as your last commit, alone.
12. **Where you work and how you push.** Never change the main checkout: make a scratch worktree of your lane branch (`git worktree add <scratchpad>/lane lane/<branch>`), run `npm ci` there, and merge, commit and push from it. Push with `git push -u origin <your lane>` as one plain command: never `--no-verify`, never with nothing to send (an empty push runs the whole check). The pre-push hook runs three fast gates on a lane push; if its wrappers are not installed in your worktree, run those three gates yourself first. If your permission check refuses a merge or a commit inside the worktree, stop and say so: it is then the owner's word in your session. No pull request.
13. **Messages.** The chair's messages wait in a queue: read the notifications after every commit. WRITE TO THE CHAIR ONLY for a stop that blocks everything you have left, or for your hand-back. Any other stop goes in your hand-back file with your recommendation: where it only declares or names (a red classed, a move declared inside a bound already ruled, a row named for the merge seat or a later wave), take it and go on, listing it under "calls taken without the chair"; where it would change a ruled design, a bound, a pin or another lane's file, park that item, go on with the rest, and send the parked items together, once. A message is 150 words at most; the detail lives in the file.
14. **The hand-back** is a file beside this one, named for the lane, and ONE message: the tip read back from the remote; each pattern's rate before and after on the same seeds, on the census's corpus and on fresh seeds; each guard member with its planted fault; the field diffs by family; the six surfaces; the three gates and the repetition guard; the whole suite's failing titles classed; the new words; what you found that is not yours. An independent reviewer reads the lane before it is named to the merge seat.
