# Zoltanhiid at the table · a played session, and what the dossier was good for

**What this is.** One fresh town made the way a visitor makes one (build cc11c9504, "Forge a town", no choices), its whole
dossier saved (about 12,000 words, every tab open), then one evening of play: I was the game master, working only from
that dossier plus a small campaign of my own; the four players were played by a separate helper that never saw the
dossier, so their questions were not steered toward what the dossier happens to cover. Dice were rolled for real. Every
time the game master needed something, it went in the ledger below with where the answer came from.

**The town.** Zoltanhiid: a port town of 652, 71 years old, steppe-style (corrals and an assembly ground among the halls),
wealthy, ruled by an Arcane Council under the Archmage Sarnai Arulad, lightly defended (a part-time night watch, no walls,
no ships, no wards), feeding itself mostly on fish and buying grain by sea. 17 named people, 24 houses, 17 debts and
grudges between the people, 3 traditions, a short history, and a grain-corner hook.

## The answer

| use | verdict | how often (71 moments in the evening) |
|---|---|---|
| **Directly** (read out or played as written) | Yes, in pieces: the opening scene, the people's "how they deal" lines, their wants, the hooks, the law lines | something used as written in 25 |
| **As a reference** (look a fact up mid-game) | Yes for who, what, who owes whom, what's legal, what's here; no for prices, names of places, looks, distances | a fact looked up in 23 |
| **As a base to improvise from** | Its strongest use: an invented answer stays true to the rest of the town, and the whole night's plot came out of six facts in it | an answer built on its facts in 46 |
| Gave nothing | Only my campaign's own facts (sums, my villain's men, the time of day) | 6 |

Of the 30 moments where I still had to invent part of the answer, the missing pieces were always the same few kinds:
prices (it has exactly one, a healing draught), names of inns, taverns, ships and ordinary people, what anyone looks
like, how far things are, which two guilds are quarrelling, and what happens at a festival.

Faults met: two places where the dossier disagrees with itself (the watch "holds the gates" of a town with no walls; the
guild master "wants to pay back" a debt that, on the relationship card, is owed to him), one puzzle (the "Arcane Council"
is nine tradespeople), and the opening scene is written for arriving by road, so a party arriving by sea has to adapt it.
None of them derailed play.

## The game master's prep (private) · campaign "The Grain Road" · session 1 in Zoltanhiid

## My campaign (invented by me, not by the generator)
Two thin harvests along the coast. A trading house of Calder's Reach, Varro & Daughters, sends grain north. Its buyer in
Zoltanhiid, Ansel Pike (MY NPC), stopped writing six weeks ago; the house's last three grain cargoes sent there were sold
on arrival to one buyer at a loss, under Ansel's seal. The house hires the party: find Ansel, find who is cornering the
grain, get the grain flowing. Behind it (my campaign's long arc): an outside syndicate, the Saltmarch Compact (MINE),
cornering grain port by port with front traders.

## What I took from the dossier to build session 1 (seeds, used by me)
- THE CORNER: Plot Hooks › Outlook "Trade Monopoly: one importer now holds all the grain that comes in from outside and has
  quadrupled its price". The importer is unnamed: I make it the front trader Temür Jalair (MINE), who is the "young trader
  undercutting [Dokuz Salji'ud] in ways that shouldn't be possible on their claimed capital" (Dokuz's hook): the capital is
  the Compact's.
- THE CHEAP GRAIN: Chilaun Mangghud (granary reeve) "takes a bribe from merchants to sell from the stores at low prices
  when grain is dear" (secret). Temür buys the town's own stores cheap, then sells at four times.
- THE HIDING PLACE: Toghan Tanghud (factor, warehouses) "rents out a locked bay at night to people who never sign the
  books" (secret) and "a shipment they weren't expecting arrived ... they don't know who sent it" (hook): it is the Varro
  grain, re-sealed.
- ANSEL: Jochi Önggirad's hook "a family they look after has stopped answering the door, and the shutters have stayed
  closed for a week": Ansel lodged with that family; he is hiding there, sick with fear (alive).
- THE INN: Oljeitu Bukhara (inns) "reads the letters guests leave in their rooms, and sells what is in them".
- THE HARBOUR: Sorqan Borjigin "asks the name of your ship, its cargo and its last port; checks the answers"; takes coin
  to let certain ships dock without inspection. Oghul Önggirad (wharfinger) sells the best berths for a little extra.

## The session · "The Grain Road", session 1, in Zoltanhiid

## Round 1
**DM (opening):** Dawn, two weeks out of Calder's Reach. Your ship, the cog Brave Margery, rounds the headland and Zoltanhiid comes up out of the haze: masts above the roofs, gulls wheeling over the quays, salt bright in the air. Behind the waterfront the town looks odd for a port: there is as much open ground as building, corrals and an open assembly ground among the halls. Kes, even from the deck you feel the air prickle faintly, the way it does where a great deal of spellwork is done. Fresh paint and well-kept roofs say the place is doing well. A clerk with a wax tablet comes down the quay; behind, longshoremen swing bales off a ship with a crane; a broad-shouldered official in a good coat watches your ship.

**Players:** Ilse (Dana) watches the tablet: what's on it, is there a seal, what are the dues called, is there a tariff board, who is the man in the coat; she tries to read whose marks are on the bales. Odo (Marcus) prays to the Drowned Mother: is there a shrine or chapel, are the dockers fed, what's the mood, who leads them. Kes (Priya) feels the prickle: what kind of magic, from where, is Detect Magic legal, is the crane magic, what does the official wear, is there an arcane guild, does anyone else notice. Torvald (Jake): where are the guards, how many, how tough; is there a tavern; anything odd about the ships; he pokes open a sack. Table: "Nobody sign anything."

**DM:** The clerk Altani, deputy to the Master of the Harbour: "Name of your ship, its cargo, and its last port," checking each answer against a list. Dues: anchorage by the day, port duty on cargo, customs inspection of all cargo; 4 silver a day and 1 gold duty. The official: Oghul Önggirad, wharfinger, asks what you carry and for whom and writes it down; the inner berths are kept for friends of the harbour, "and a friend pays a little extra". Ilse (24): the hulk under the crane flies no pennant; its grain sacks bear a fresh hook-and-ring mark over the ghost of the Varro mark; no clerk goes near it. Odo: no temple on the quay but a sea-post with salt; a handful of congregations, each in its own quarter; "the harbour has always been lucky with fish: nobody knows why, and nobody questions it"; the Fair of the Tide in high summer; dockers well fed but quiet, a half-scrubbed A FAIR WAGE FOR A FAIR TIDE; the ganger Bortei won't talk (Insight 7). Kes (19): small magic everywhere, strongest uphill; Detect Magic is legal (magic celebrated and unregulated, no licensing, though an Arcane Council rules); the crane has an old lightening charm; the harbour water itself glows faintly, deep down. Torvald: no watch by day (part-time, they walk the night); the Salt Horse tavern, loud not rough; the hulk's crew stay aboard; Stealth 5: good wheat, and a docker growls "Hands off. That's Jalair's."

## Round 2
**Players:** Ilse names the ship and cargo, pays anchorage for three days, wants stamped receipts, asks whether duty is a flat rate, what the wharfinger's "little extra" costs and whether it is written down, whether Ansel's seal passed through this office; she counts the hulk's sacks. Odo salts the post and asks whose it is, who can explain the quarters, where the poor eat and sleep; he tries Bortei again. Kes asks where to buy potions and components, what a healing potion costs, where the council sits and who is on it, who cast the crane charm, and what the water aura is. Torvald talks the docker down ("Who's Jalair?") and asks about ale prices, dice, and the hulk's sailors.

**DM:** Receipts stamped; duty a tenth of declared value, port law kept at the harbour office. Oghul's "little extra" is 5 gold, no receipt: "friends of the harbour don't need paper." Altani: an outside trader's sale goes on the guild's books ("accept the guild's terms or sell to a member at the member's price"), and, too pleased, "people used to go to the Master for that; they come to me now." About four hundred sacks; a private tally-man aboard the Grey Heron; no inspection. The landfall post: salt for the fish-luck; the Quiet Landfall Procession ends there each spring; a child's bracelet tied on this week. The dean, Sübetei Qatagin, leads the congregations; the houses of worship give poor relief; the pawnbroker is the labourer's only credit. Bortei's eyes go to the clerk's tablet. One alchemist's shop, the Archmage Sarnai Arulad's own; a healing draught costs a labourer's week; the council's nine members named. The water aura is "more like a prayer that never stopped" (Arcana 11). The docker (Persuasion 20): "Temür Jalair, the young trader buying every grain sack in this port. Nobody knew his name a year ago." A brawl costs a fine: "there's nowhere to put you." Ale is dear because bread is dear; nobody from the Grey Heron has come ashore in four days.

## Round 3
**Players:** They split. Ilse and Odo: who checks the duty, where is the guild, is Jalair a member, can we read the port law, is the harbour master back. Odo also wants the dean, the meaning of the bracelet, a room and the poor-relief kitchen. Kes goes to the town hall: reagent prices, a public gallery or library, the Archmage's reputation. Torvald goes to the Salt Horse: who's there, the menu, dice, Jalair's men, talk of Ansel; what does Jalair look like.

**DM:** The port law is chained to a lectern: a tenth by the manifest, any cargo may be inspected. Sorqan Borjigin checks their answers against the tablet, talks freely (Persuasion 22) until "Grey Heron", then goes grey: "Its papers were in order. Take it to the guild." (Insight 17: frightened, lying; he mutters about "the winter stores".) The guild's master Toragana Besud keeps away and sends them to the second, Gantulga Qatagin, the pawnbroker, who would love to speak for the guild on this: seven members, Jalair not one; the three Varro cargoes sold under Ansel's seal through the factor Toghan Tanghud to "T. Jalair". Odo rolls a 1 with the dean Sübetei Qatagin: the dean learns everything, Odo learns that the founders blessed the harbour ("an old story"), that the poor eat at dusk at the parish hall, and that Oljeitu Bukhara's inn is clean. Kes: no library (the ruins were picked clean); the council is "again" avoiding the two rival guilds' quarrel over the main trade route; the Archmage is well regarded, polite but brief until you mention something she wants; reagent prices. Torvald: salted fish and pottage, dear bread; loses 4 silver; the host Jochi Önggirad says "never heard of him" too fast; the bracelet is for a shut-in family behind the tavern whom Jochi feeds.

## Round 4
**Players:** Ilse wants the dates, sums, commission and seal of the three sales, the name behind "T. Jalair", who lends to him, where the warehouses are, and what "winter stores" means. Odo replays the dean in hindsight, asks about the inn, its host, and the poor-relief kitchen. Kes asks what the Archmage wants and what to offer her, which two guilds, buys quartz, and checks the hall's wards. Torvald asks who lives in the shut house, whether Jochi lies (with a drink), and who is watching.

**DM:** The three sales copied; the seal is Ansel's own ring (Investigation 21). Gantulga: "the young man borrows from nobody here"; "ask the granary reeve how his winter stores are counted" (Ilse's Insight 4: he seems sincere). Winter stores bought on credit; the reeve Chilaun Mangghud sits on the council. The dean went still at "Jalair" and glanced at the bread at "grain" (Insight 19). The parish hall's loaves "go further than they should". The Archmage wants her shop through the season without borrowing; herbs from the south would interest her. Her nervous student minds the shop and keeps looking at the harbour. The town hall has no wards at all. Jochi (Insight 21) is lying, frightened for someone else, and ill: "If you're here to help that family, come back after dark, by the yard." Two of Jalair's dockers watch Torvald; one leaves.

## Round 5
**Players (noon, the Salt Horse's back room):** they pool what they know. Ilse wants the factor's ledger and the Grey Heron's manifest, and Ansel's lodgings. Odo will take bread to the shut-in family. Kes wants the student alone, a quiet Detect Magic on the shop's back room, and a look at the harbour from his window. Torvald wants to know where the docker went. Plan: warehouses and the shop in the afternoon; the parish hall at dusk; the yard after dark.

**DM:** A docker trails Ilse (Perception 23). The factor Toghan Tanghud "talks in rates and weights" and wants a patron; the house of Varro's goodwill opens his ledger (Persuasion 19): the Varro grain stored two days, then "out by the buyer's carts"; the Heron has no manifest at all. His trouble: "a shipment I wasn't expecting arrived, no sender, fees paid in advance; I don't know what to do with it": three crates branded with Ansel's pike. Ansel's letters came "care of the Salt Horse". The student, Möngke, asked the Archmage three weeks ago what lies under the harbour; she couldn't answer, and he went looking: "the Mother's stone, laid in the harbour at the first landing". A jar of harbour water on his bench glows. The docker vanishes toward the Heron, whose plank is now guarded. At dusk the dean heals a crushed hand with one hand in a sleeve, shaken (Odo's Religion 24: the power is old and salt, kin to the Drowned Mother; something in the sleeve hums). "Take this loaf to the family behind the Salt Horse. They won't open for me any more."

## Round 6 (full dark, the tavern yard)
**Players:** Odo will knock with the dean's loaf: what does he hear, see, smell; is the family's name known; will the dean's name help. Ilse: other ways in, watchers, can she pick the lock and what does the law say. Kes casts Detect Magic from the yard. Torvald watches the lane: lights, footsteps, a watchman, the Heron's crew, how many if it comes to a fight; does Jochi come.

**DM:** Jochi slips out of the tavern's back door, grey-faced, and knocks the family's knock. Through the door: a man's cough, a child shushed, a sliver of light, the smell of a fish-oil lamp and a banked fire. Kes's Detect Magic: no ward anywhere (nothing in this town is warded); the only aura in the yard is the loaf in Odo's arms, faint and old and salt, the same "prayer" as the harbour. Ilse (a 1) watches the windows and misses the face at the upper window across the lane. If she had to pick the lock, the law here can fine her or put her out of town, but cannot hold her. The door opens a crack: a widow who mends nets, two children behind her, and by the fire a thin, unshaven man with ink-stained fingers and a pale band of skin where a ring used to be. "You're Varro's," says Ansel Pike. "Thank the gods. Jalair took my ring at knifepoint. The reeve sold him the winter stores: the granary is counted full and it's half empty, and when the town finds out, Jalair names his price for the rest. The crates at Toghan's are the house's books. I sent them where he'd never look..." At the mouth of the lane Torvald (Perception 19) watches a watchman's lantern go by on its rounds. As its light fades, four big shapes with hooks and cudgels turn into the lane: they have learned the rounds, and they are working between them. Carts creak on the quay toward the warehouses' back bay. **End of session one. Roll initiative next time.**
