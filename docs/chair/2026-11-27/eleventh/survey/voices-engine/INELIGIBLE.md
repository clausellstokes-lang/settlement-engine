## The nouns the texts name (the presumed-things walker's) and the class words (the presumption-classes walker's)

The nouns go through `NOUN_DECLARATION`: watch → `watch`; armed force → `guardPresence` (soldiers → `soldiers`);
streets → `tier: ≥ village`; open trade → excludes `crisis: under_siege`; market → `market`; smuggling → `house: smuggling`.
The class words go through `CLASS_ANSWERS` and `CHANGED_WORDS`, each version as read in `CLASS-HITS.md`.
