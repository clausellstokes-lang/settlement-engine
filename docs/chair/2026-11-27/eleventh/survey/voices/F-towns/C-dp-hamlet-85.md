> Copanchihua is visibly well off. The halls, the houses and the roads are all better than a hamlet this size strictly requires.
>
> Newcomers soon notice how often talk here comes round to the Feudal Stewardship. The wild is not far off, and visitors hear stories of what comes out of it. Those who fall ill here cannot count on much care.
>
> Before anyone goes out to work, each household looks to its own door and sees that all is as it was left.
>
> A pot of stew over the fire is the usual supper, and there is usually enough to go round.
>
> In the evenings people gather at the wayside inn, where the day's news is passed around.
>
> Children are warned about repeating what they hear said at home about those in charge, and about going anywhere alone after dark.
>
> People here still talk of the Occupation, and of what it changed.
