## THE COLD READ (five lines; my read as a DM: PLAUSIBLE)

1. GAINS: the magistrate saying "People resent how little is done about wrongdoing that everyone knows of" reads as an admission and a hook; the watch captain repeating "people say the guard is doing nothing" is drama for free.
2. LOSES: the criminal-structure facts on Defense ("A street gang controls part of the place, extorting…") become a nameless local's say-so; the DM loses the plain statement of what is there.
3. READS WRONG: "Suspicion of hidden enemies runs deep, and nobody voices it in front of strangers", voiced by the fishmonger to the reader; the watch captain voicing the charge against her own watch works once, not as a default.
4. TOO MANY PEOPLE?: 12 speakers, none above 2; but 7 different named people in Daily Life's 9 lines is a roll-call a DM cannot hold.
5. VERDICT: about even: better as hooks, worse as reference.
