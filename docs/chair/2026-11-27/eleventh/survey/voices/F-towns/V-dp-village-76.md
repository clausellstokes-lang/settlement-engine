> Families in Megachorio mend what they have and put off buying anything new.
>
> Food is what holds Megachorio back. However its trades do, the village cannot grow richer while it cannot feed itself.
>
> A visitor finds more than one door to knock on before anything is decided here. Travellers here talk of beasts seen at the edge of the settled land. There is not much here to stop trouble once it starts, and people know it.
>
> The beasts are seen to before light, and nothing they give is wasted.
>
> Rations are tight, and the poor eat watered stew.
>
> People meet out on the common by day, and at one another's doors of an evening.
>
> Children are warned about joining in when the grown-ups' talk turns angry, and about begging favours from neighbours who have little enough themselves.
>
> People bring their complaints to the Feudal Stewardship and expect little to come of them. There is grumbling in every kitchen about how little there is to put on the table.
>
> For the DM only. Nothing here appears on a player view or a shared page.
>
> Ordinary folk resent that the law here bends for anyone who can pay.
>
> Nobody asks a neighbour how they are managing for supplies; it is not a question to ask.
>
> The Trade Collapse is still remembered here, and not lightly. Food has become a public worry here, not just a private one.
