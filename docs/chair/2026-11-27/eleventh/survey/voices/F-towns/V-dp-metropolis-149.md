## Systems/Defense: AS IT PRINTS TODAY

> Defensive posture
>
> Well-defended
>
> Guard assessment
>
> The garrison and city watch are disciplined but resource-constrained: motivated with inadequate equipment and irregular pay. Effective in a fight; vulnerable to sustained corruption. A wrong done here goes before the court, and the guilty can be locked up for it. The garrison and city watch keep the gates, but smugglers have ways in beneath the walls. Hired swords back the garrison when the purse allows.
>
> Chernoko reads as a defended place from the road in: the approaches are watched, the works are kept up, and none of it looks like it was thrown together.
>
> Military status
>
> Migration surge
>
> On the road into Chernoko, you pass families camped wherever there is room, watching every cart that goes by.
>
> More people are arriving at Chernoko than its guards can keep in order, so they spend their days on the crowds, not on the roads. The metropolis is shaken by this trouble, but it is still standing and means to stay that way.
>
