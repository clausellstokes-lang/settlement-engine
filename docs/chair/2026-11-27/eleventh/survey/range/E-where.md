## WHAT THE DECLARATION RULES COST A WRITER (CONFIRMED where counted; the reading of the rules PLAUSIBLE)
1. **THE TWO WALKERS.** A new line in `src/data`, `src/generators`, `src/domain` or `src/components` reds the presumed-things
   walker if it names a thing (a mill, a watch, a gate) without a declared atom a draw door reads, and the presumption-classes
   walker if it holds a class word (size, goods, plenty, ease, threat, office, outside…) whose dimension it does not declare.
   Both registers are shrink-only: a new line cannot be banked. Of the thirty keys' 96 existing lines, 40 hold such a word;
   of my 44 demonstration lines, 8 do (the walkers' matchers run on them: `tools/cost.mjs`). **THE HOUSE DESCRIPTIONS ARE
   NEARLY FREE**: their file is one of the walker's BOUND files (`presumptionClasses.shared.mjs :: BOUND_FILES`), where a
   goods, resource, office, iron or animals word is answered by the house itself; only a size word ("streets") or a thing
   the house does not imply needs an atom. The seat sentence, the DM power lines and the crisis mark take a line freely if it
   names nothing new. The state annex's keys mostly declare already (marks), and a new variant must carry the key's marks and
   is written in the receipt (`docs/content/RECEIPT_POOLS_DOSSIER_STATE.md`) and regenerated.
2. **WHERE THE LINE IS DRAWN.** The house descriptions, the tie lines, goals, secrets and the patrol frames are drawn AT BIRTH
   and stored (checked: on 40 pages of C, 37 of 37 house-description pages and 39 of 39 tie-line pages hold the printed text in
   the record; the seat sentence and the capture line are in no record). `pickVariant` picks by a hash modulo the pool's
   length, so ONE LINE ADDED to a birth-drawn key re-deals that key in about (n)/(n+1) of the towns that hold it: **BIRTHS
   MOVE**, which is the owner's signing of goldens; a town already saved keeps its old line. A view-time key (the seat
   sentence, the DM lines, the state annex, Daily Life, the crisis mark) moves no birth. A writer who wants the houses varied
   without moving births needs a view-time re-pick (the ranked pick of point 121, "stable under pool edits", would re-deal
   only the towns a new line outranks).
3. **BYTES.** The state annex's generated leaves have room under their declared ceilings (general 489 KB, defence 201 KB of
   headroom: `scripts/.prose-byte-baseline.json`); every growth is a declared row of that ratchet. The causal leaf is nearly
   full (10 KB). The house-description file's header says "its byte ceiling is signed" (PLAUSIBLE: a budget, the owner's).
4. **THE STYLE LAW AND THE LANE RULES** ("rather than" struck, "a stranger" struck, no joining connectives in the seat
   paragraph) bind every new line; none of them requires the lines of a key to be alike.

So: **a new line is cheapest where it is needed most.** In the house descriptions it needs no atom for its trade words, but it
moves births. In the view-time keys (the seat sentence, the DM lines, the annex frames) it moves no birth, and needs an atom
only for a thing it names.
