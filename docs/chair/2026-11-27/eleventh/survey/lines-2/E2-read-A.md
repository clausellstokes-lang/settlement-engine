## THE TEN OF MY LINES I TRUST LEAST, AND WHY
1. THE CARD-KIND DECLARATIONS (fixes 2 to 4: "a body with members and meetings", "not an occupier", "an old order"): no
   reader exists for them yet. `domain/voices/cardWords.js` answers card words by a HOUSE's catalogue id, not a POWER's
   kind. Until the engine adds a power-kind card word (from `factionArchetype`), the generator files these words as
   answered nowhere, so those versions are ineligible. The same holds for `tier: ≤ town`, a real atom that
   `NEEDS_ATOM` has no needs word for. These are named for the build, not invented here.
2. `SAID.power.lesser-contender.8` C "Those of us who ask questions get told to be quiet.": its W, T and D are now keyed,
   but C is not. In an occupier's mouth, "told to be quiet" reads oddly.
3. `SAID.crisis.seat.4` C "Thieves are taking what they can while nobody's in charge.": with the hedge dropped (fix 7),
   it is now a firmer claim of theft.
4. `SAID.power.seat-captured.4` T "Some people's fines just seem to disappear.": it presumes fines are levied, which
   suggests a court. No class word hits, but it leans.
5. `SAID.tension.arcane.4` T "Nobody will buy a house where something strange happened.": it presumes houses change hands.
   That is true of most places, but it is a thing.
6. `SAID.power.seat-contested.8` W "The children have picked favourites too, and cheer for them.": in a thorp the contest
   may be two households. Children cheering may read too bright.
7. `SAID.tension.money.5` W "Here and there, well-off families have forgiven small debts.": "Here and there" is still a
   hedge, though not the formula. (siege.5 T, which had people slipping out through the siege, became "Every day of
   waiting eats more of what's left.")
8. `SAID.power.seat-secure.6` T "A ruling costs a day's wait, and it sticks.": "a day's" sits close to a number, and
   "sticks" is close to a turned word.
9. `SAID.tension.neighbours.9` D "Folk only visit when asked now.": "Folk" is a register step from the rest. I changed it
   from "No one visits…" to keep off the formula.
10. `SAID.power.lesser-standing.7` D "Very old-fashioned.": thin. On a military or arcane power, "old-fashioned" may read
    as a joke.
