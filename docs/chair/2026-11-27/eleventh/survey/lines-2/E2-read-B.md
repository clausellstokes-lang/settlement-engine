## THE TEN OF MY LINES I TRUST LEAST, AND WHY
1. `SAID.inst.any-short.4` W and T, and `.8` T (carters, empty trips, buying the good): wrong for a house that WORKS the good (a mine or quarry short of ore, a farm short of livestock). They want a card condition (the house buys {good} in) that I could not declare.
2. `SAID.inst.any-crisisunmarked.5` (all four "Customers …") and `.6` W ("Spare stock …"): the unmarked crisis pool prints on every speaking family, a temple or a watch among them. The old line "Customers buy less …" has the same reach.
3. `SAID.inst.any-crisisunmarked.7` G "Better to be home before dark these days.": it implies danger after dark, which a conversion, a debt crisis or a succession does not give.
4. `SAID.trad.feast.6` C "Crowded tables, all night.": under a famine or in a thorp it states a crowd and a full table. No class hits it; the chair may want it to exclude `crisis: hunger`.
5. `SAID.inst.inn-ordinary.8` (beds, bedding): the inn family holds bathhouses, stables, toll bridges and carriers.
6. `SAID.inst.watch-ordinary.6` G "Certain doors never get a knock, however late it is." (the chair's words): right for a watch, odd on a garrison, the walls or a mercenary company.
7. `LIE.retainer.6` T "A lost key would cost a month's wages, so none get lost.": the holder may be the head of the house, who draws no wage.
8. `LIE.office.hall.5` (the lists): right for a court, a council or a customs house, thinner for a watch or a garrison.
9. `SAID.trad.procession.5` T "Some houses charge a coin to watch from their windows.": in a hamlet's procession it presumes a street of windows.
10. `SAID.trad.fair.7` (hiring at the fair): not every fair is a hiring fair. It is a remark, but it is a claim about what the fair does.
