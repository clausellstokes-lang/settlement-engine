## THE TEN LINES I TRUST LEAST, AND WHY
1. **SAID.power.seat-contested.2**: a member of the ruling power says "the people in charge" in the third person, which reads like an outsider. Reword it to "We aren't going anywhere", or give it to one who depends on the seat.
2. **The six LIE.office lines**: written for a magistrate, a customs officer or a watch officer. An office-shelf holder may be a priest, an almoner or a mage, and inspections, permits and rulings do not come from those mouths. (D) needs a second key, the holder's house family (hall or watch, against temple, care or learning).
3. **LIE.front.5** ("Customers only"): front-capable houses include colleges and temples, and "customers" is not guaranteed. Key it to market, inn and workshop fronts only.
4. **SAID.tension.crime.1 to 3 on a corruption scandal**: the L-1 merge leaves no line about officials. One graft line in the pool would mend it.
5. **SAID.tension.neighbours.1 to 3 on infiltration_fear and occupation_legacy**: "neighbours not speaking" fits mistrust only loosely. The merge is cheap here too.
6. **SAID.inst.inn-ordinary.2** ("The same regulars every night"): the inn family holds toll bridges, stables, post relays and carriers, and "every night" is wrong for them. Narrow it to inns and taverns, or reword it.
7. **SAID.inst.water-ordinary.1** ("The water takes someone most years"): a claim of deaths, heavy in a two-person thorp or a city fish market. Skew allows it, but it may read as a fact.
8. **SAID.inst.learning-ordinary.3** ("People are a little afraid of this work"): it fits a mage's house, not a library, a scribe or a cartographer.
9. **SAID.inst.watch-ordinary.1**: the proud "quiet nights" is right by L-1 ruling 7. Beside a crime card it rests wholly on the promise's "people may see it differently" being printed.
10. **The `{crisisNoun}` lines with "split between the powers" and "debt crisis"**: "The split between the powers or not, it goes ahead" is stiff. It is readable, but those two values want their own word.
