## THE TEN LINES I TRUST LEAST, AND WHY
1. **LIE.office.faith.1, the guarded version** ("Nobody has ever asked where the gifts go, and nobody needs to."): it comes close to a wink. Plainer: "The gifts all go to the poor, as they always have."
2. **SAID.inst.watch-ordinary.1, the warm version** ("Most nights are quiet, thank goodness."): it is right by L-1 ruling 7, but beside a crime card it rests wholly on the promise's "people may see it differently" being printed.
3. **SAID.tension.crime.1 to 5 on a corruption scandal**: the L-1 merge leaves the officials out. Two of the five lines fit, three are about theft. Unmerging graft would cost three lines.
4. **SAID.tension.neighbours.1 to 5 on fear of infiltrators and the legacy of an occupation**: neighbours.5 fits, the others only loosely. The same trade.
5. **SAID.inst.inn-ordinary.2** ("The same regulars every night ..."): the inn family includes toll bridges, stables and post relays, where "every night" is wrong.
6. **SAID.inst.temple-ordinary.3 and .4** (burials, weddings): the temple family includes wayside shrines and groves, which hold neither. Narrow them to churches, cathedrals and monasteries, or accept the skew.
7. **SAID.crisis.raids.3, the curt version** ("Another animal missing."): a city under raids may have no animals of its own to lose.
8. **SAID.inst.show-ordinary.2, the curt version** ("Plenty of hypocrites."): plain, but the hardest word in the file. The owner may find it too sharp.
9. **LIE.extortion.3, the guarded version**: it moves from the corner lads to closing businesses, which is a second thing the tie touches. It is true to the tie, but it is the one liar line whose four versions do not share one subject.
10. **The `{crisisNoun}` lines with "split between the powers" and "debt crisis"** ("Customers buy less with the split between the powers on everyone's mind."): readable but stiff. Those two values want a shorter word from the engine.
