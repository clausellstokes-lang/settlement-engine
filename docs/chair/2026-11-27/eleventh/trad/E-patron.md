# (E) R14, THE "PATRON DEITY" BLOCK

**THE COMPONENT AND ITS STATE.** `src/components/OutputContainer.jsx :: renderTab`, case `power`: `<PowerTab …/>` then `{!readOnly && <Suspense fallback={null}><DeityAssignmentPanel /></Suspense>}`. `DeityAssignmentPanel` is its own lazy chunk (`src/components/dossier/dossierLazyTabs.js:37`, separate from `PowerTab`'s). It reads the STORE (`useStore(s => s.settlement)`, `canUseCustomContent`, the config's `primaryDeitySnapshot`/`cultDeitySnapshots`), not the town's facts. With no custom-content right (the census store has none), every town takes the upsell or the lapsed branch, and both print the heading `td('assign.patronHeading')` = "Patron deity" (`src/copy/deityAuthoring.js:72`). The upsell adds the line "Assign a patron god … to author and assign deities." That makes 2 lines a page, and 2 towns give the handoff's 4 lines.

**REPRODUCED (CONFIRMED, `scripts/harness-E.jsx`, `boot-E.mjs`; rows in `E/`).** I rendered dp-thorp-0 (C) through the tree's real OutputContainer with the census store, in plain node under jsdom.
- On the esbuild bundle every lazy chunk resolves in a microtask. The block prints on every visit in every order: walk-fresh, power-first and walk-after-another-town, on dp-thorp-0, -1 and -2. The earlier seat's census walk (`tools/harness.jsx`) prints it on dp-thorp-0 to -3 alike.
- I then gave the panel's chunk a load time, as a real chunk or a vitest transform takes (300 ms, `harnessE-slow.mjs`). In walk order in a fresh process, dp-thorp-0 shows NO block at the walk's moment and the block 300 ms later; dp-thorp-1 and -2 show it at once. In power-first order, dp-thorp-0's FIRST Power visit lacks it and its SECOND, in the same town, has it. The census walk with every chunk slowed (`rbundle-slow.mjs`) gives the same: dp-thorp-0 0 lines, dp-thorp-1 to -3 2 lines each.

**THE CAUSE, IN THREE LINES.**
1. The walk snapshots a tab as soon as its panel holds more than 15 characters (`pageCensusWalk.shared.mjs :: walkDossier`'s waitFor), and PowerTab's text alone passes that test.
2. The patron panel is a second lazy chunk under its own `Suspense fallback={null}`, so on the FIRST Power mount of a process it is still loading at that moment, and it prints nothing.
3. From the second mount on, the chunk is cached and the block prints. Its presence therefore follows which town first opens Power in each process or worker, not the town. This is a fault of the measure, not of the product: in the app the block appears a moment later.

**THE SMALLEST CURE (for the measure, where the fault is).** `walkDossier` waits for the tab's lazy boundaries to settle before it snapshots. Two ways, one line each: on an editable Power view, also `waitFor` `[data-testid="deity-assignment-panel"]`; or have the walk warm every chunk of `dossierLazyTabs.js` once before its first town. A product cure is optional. Folding the panel into PowerTab's chunk (one lazy import for both) would make them arrive together, and it would also remove the block's pop-in.

**TOWNS IT CAN TOUCH.** Every owner page of C and V (1,800 of 1,800, PLAUSIBLE, read): the panel gates on no fact of the place, only on `!readOnly`, so every editable Power view carries it. 7 of 7 rendered towns carry it (CONFIRMED). The shared page never does (readOnly). The towns it DOES touch in a run are the first Power mount of each process: one per process or worker, which is 1 in a single-process run, 4 with four workers, and 12 per corpus in chunks of 75. It touches nothing the product prints.
