## 1. My own attack, cold

### 1.1 The instrument

One harness (`attack.probe.jsx`), two sides.

- **Master's shape.** Towns born by master's own generator, shared by master's own `writeShareVeil` and
  `galleryPayloadFor`, stored in clones of three chains: `203` (production today), `203 + 211` (211 alone) and
  `001..211` (the release).
- **The new shape.** The same with the lane's code, the row stamped with `data.publicProse` as the new client's
  save stamps it; one chain, `001..211`.

For every share and each of the four reads the harness builds THE REFERENCE: that side's own client veil
(`resolveVeil` and `applyVeil`, with the deltas as stored) over the chain's own unveiled projection of the same row
(its sanitiser or DM-full strip, its member splice, the frozen copy where the share is frozen). The response is
compared with the reference leaf by leaf. A leaf the response carries and the reference removed is a hidden thing
sent (`EXTRA`). A leaf the reference keeps and the response lacks is something taken (`MISSING`). Beside the diff,
every key an eye governs is salted with a unique token on every card; a token the reference removed that turns up
anywhere in the whole response row, any column, is counted with no classifier in between.

Shares per town, each with the DM reveal off and on, live and frozen: unmarked; the default veil; every eye shut;
every default opened; random subsets; each card kind's eyes alone (people, houses, powers, events, tensions,
conflicts, ties); a third of the people revealed by hand with their goals, ties, secret, personality, corruption,
whereabouts and hooks shut by hand, and a third hidden whole; a narrated share. The groupings (notes, decrees, the
criminal blocks, the housekeeping rows) are in every "every eye shut" and random share. The frozen shares' live
rows were edited after the freeze.
