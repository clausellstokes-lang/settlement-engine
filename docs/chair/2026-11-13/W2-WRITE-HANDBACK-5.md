## The method this seat works to (record 386, copied)
(1) a line is read where it prints before it is pushed: three towns of the pool's own cells, a siege, a famine or an occupation among them wherever the pool can print under one, the smallest and the largest tier it reaches; towns and what printed in the commit body. (2) a twin carries every condition its siblings carry, unless the commit says why not. (3) a line that names a house, a role, a force, a good or a place reads the record through a registered condition; a slot never prints a catalogue label, never breaks agreement. (4) a line that says how the model works is not written. (5) a line that cannot be made true by its conditions is not written: silence is not a false sentence; a line true somewhere is declared off the towns that make it false; a case left empty gets a line only if read true in three towns. (6) every disqualifier cure has its guard: a fixed town in which the cured line must not print.
