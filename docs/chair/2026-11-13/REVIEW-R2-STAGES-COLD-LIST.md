The five renders are in `dumps20-5trees.tar.gz`.
- **Five trees:** A tip 2f114a4c9 · A and B base e969a75b7 · B tip 80b960171 · C tip d79ea4c70 · C base 4534ea5e3.
- **Ten cold readers** (`READER-BRIEF.md`, `readers/reader-N.json`), each told nothing of the stages, their files or their claims, and forbidden `docs/chair/**` and every commit message. Each town was read whole as V1 (A's tip), and then every line that any other tree prints and no earlier version printed (V2 B tip, V3 C tip, V4 the A/B base, V5 C's base), each against its own record. So every line that prints on any of the five pages of the twenty towns was read once: 25,847 distinct lines. Each listed sentence was checked by the readers against the record (query) before it was listed.
- **Placed by script** (`merge-cold.mjs`): each sentence searched on each of the five pages of its town (substring of a page line; a sentence of 12 characters or fewer matches a whole line). All 1,433 rows placed.
