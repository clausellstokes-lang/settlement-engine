### 1.1 The instrument

`cold.probe.jsx`, on the last reader's planted-marker method so the counts compare, with these differences:

1. **My own markers:** ` vq6n<n>z` (lower-case letters and digits only, so a slug, a case fold or a join keeps it).
2. **Planted during the birth** through the generator's own `options.onStep` hook, as the last reader did: in every
   key an eye governs on every card kind of the side's own `CARD_KINDS` (people, ties, houses, powers, events,
   tensions, conflicts) and in the data of every grouping. A value that is a record or a list gets a marker of its
   own; at the end of the birth every sentence INSIDE it (twelve characters and three words or more, never an id
   or the name of a card) gets one too.
3. **Propagation at the end of the birth.** The last reader's first limit was a field derived inside the same step
   that wrote its source. Here every planted string's text as it stood before the marker is kept; at the end of the
   birth every other string of the record that still holds that text, or a sentence of it, is given the same
   marker. A verbatim copy or an embedding made in ANY step carries the marker of its source.
4. **The hidden text is searched too** (each hidden string and each of its sentences, in every response): a
   marker lost to a truncation would otherwise hide a leak. Only strings whose source is still in the STORED record
   count.
5. **More homes planted by hand after the birth:** `dossierNotes`, `dmNotes`, `notes`, `narrativeNotes`,

6. **The shares, per town, each stored in every chain:** EVERY EYE SHUT BY HAND (every grouping, every switch,

### 1.4 The four classes, as I read them cold

- **A, the notes.** A user cannot hide a note by hand: no eye, grouping or switch names one. They travel only when
  the sharer turned Reveal DM on, only in the gallery read and the import. Whether the page of such a share prints
  them I have not yet looked at; if it does not, this is the last review's V5-3 class (the response carries what
  its page hides) for three more keys.
- **B, a hidden grouping's sentence in the viability block.** The grouping "Structural issues" is hidden by default
  and was hidden by hand here; one of its rows restates a dependency of the town's economy, and that dependency's
  own entry is sent with the same sentence (and with its title, impact and suggested fixes, which say the same thing
  in other words). By the law's letter it is a hidden string in a second home, and the last review's list does not
  have it. 1 town of 24 in each shape.
- **C, the receipt's copies.** The reverse case: the hidden thing (the generation receipt) holds a COPY of a string
  whose own home is open (the stress's label, the founder, the layout). Hiding the receipt hides the receipt; the
  open home is not a second home of it. I list it because the instrument found it, and for a ruling if the chair
  reads it otherwise.
- **D, the impression.** Examined after the run: in both towns the impression that carries the marker belongs to a
  DIFFERENT person from the one whose appearance was hidden (Vatannuur: the printer's feature "eyes that never stop
  moving"; the black marketeer's impression "Unremarkable clothes, a quiet voice and eyes that never stop moving.").
  Two people drew one phrase from one pool. Of 629 people with an impression in the 24 new-shape towns and 221 in
  master's shape, **none** has a stored impression that embeds a string of its OWN stored appearance (six
  characters or more; `out/` has the query's counts). So the last review's row 10 does not reproduce in my towns as
  a person's own appearance in their own impression.

### 1.6 What the cold pass did not do

- It did not run the client's own veil (the lane's page); only the server's responses were searched. That is
  section 2's work.
- It did not read the migration's text, so it could not aim at a branch of it; the attack is black-box.
- A hidden fact turned into different words (the last review's R3 class) escapes a marker and a text search alike.
- The rendered page, the PDF and the exports were not drawn. No edge function was called (`og-image` reads through
  `get_gallery_dossier`; read in source only).
- Towns advanced by the world pulse or edited by hand were not stored; the towns are births, marked.
