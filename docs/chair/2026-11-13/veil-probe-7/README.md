# veil-probe-7 — 211 frozen, its later cures re-homed into 212, proved equal (COHERENCE-VEIL-7)

Scratch tools and their outputs, not pins. Every database was a local scratch PostgreSQL 16.15 cluster on a unix
socket (`/var/tmp/cv7`, trust auth, no TCP). Nothing here touches a hosted database.

- `tools/gen212.py` built `supabase/migrations/212_share_veil_later_cures.sql` and its down file mechanically:
  `tools/spans.py` cuts every `create or replace function` statement out of a file; every statement the lane's 211
  at 28f52b1e4 adds or changes against the frozen 211 (333b33973) is copied verbatim into 212 (lead comments with
  it, the file's own order), the two restated reads' revoke/grant after them and 211's section 7 last; the down file
  copies the frozen 211's statements of the five changed functions verbatim and drops the ten new by signature.
  The headers are `tools/header212*.sql`. `tools/fdiff.py` and `tools/rest.py` measured, first, that the two 211s
  differ in those fifteen statements and in comments only (`out/fdiff-*.txt`, `out/rest-*.txt`, `out/verbatim-212.txt`).
- `native/rehearse7.sh` is the lane's rehearsal comparison (`veil-probe-6/native/rehearse.sh`: the review's
  `chain.sh` and `catalog.sql`, from `lane/review-veil-3-2026-11-12` at 4700d33eb,
  `docs/chair/2026-11-13/review-veil-5-probes/native/`) plus every comment (`native/comments.sql`):
  `sh native/rehearse7.sh <review native dir with comments.sql beside it> <the lane's migrations at 28f52b1e4> <supabase/migrations> <supabase/rollback> <out dir>`.
  Its output: `out/rehearse7.txt`.
- `native/planted-run.sh` runs the review's planted-marker probe (`review-veil-5-probes/planted.probe.jsx` and
  `planted-summary.mjs`, unchanged, at 4700d33eb) in two shards: two seeds × six sizes × calm and infiltrated, every
  eye shut by hand, the DM reveal off and on, live and frozen (96 shares a side), the whole payload of each of the
  four reads searched as text. Template chains built by the review's `chain.sh` from this lane's migrations:
  `t203p211` (203 + the frozen 211), `t211f` (001..211), `t203p212` (203 + 211 + 212), `t212` (001..212).
  `out/planted/`:
  - `A-master-frozen211.txt`: master's shape (db832c892's generator and writer) on `203 + 211` and `001..211`:
    **byte-identical** to the review's `out/planted-master.txt` (its seventeen rows a chain).
  - `B-tip333-frozen211.txt`: the new shape (333b33973's code, the review's own tip) on `001..211`: identical to
    the review's `out/planted-tip.txt` but the chain's label (`qfull` here, `qtip` there).
  - `C-master-211and212.txt`: master's shape on `203 + 211 + 212` and `001..212`: **0 markers, 0 house names**.
  - `D-tip-211and212.txt`: the new shape (this tip's code) on `001..212`: **0, 0**; identical to the sixth seat's
    `veil-probe-6/out/planted-tip-final.txt` but the label.
- THE OWNER'S PACK FOR 212 (`docs/chair/2026-11-13/veil-212/`) is generated by `tools/genpack.py` (the README from
  `tools/README212.tmpl.md`): the fingerprints it checks are `native/fp.sql` over a chain at 203 + 211 (45 functions;
  equal on 001..211) and at 203 + 211 + 212 (55; equal on 001..212 and on the lane's 211 at 28f52b1e4).
  `native/pack-rehearse.sh` runs its four files in order on clones of both chains (psql's own exit status, never a
  pipe's): `out/pack-rehearsal.txt`: read-first 45 | 45 and 40 | 0; apply exit 0; check-after 50 | 0, 55 | 55 and
  the pinned search_path; the rollback returns each clone to 211 with 0 catalogue lines differing; on a chain at 203
  without 211 the read-first block reads 45 | 0 (its STOP); a write planted inside it is refused (read-only).
- `native/timing.sql`: the slowest of each read over the 96 stored shares of the planted run (master's shape) at
  203 + 211 and 203 + 211 + 212, under load on this machine (`out/timing.txt`).
- `native/respcmp.sh` with `native/reads.sql`: the same stored rows (both shapes) loaded into a chain with the lane's
  211 and one with 211 + 212, every response of the four reads hashed whole and compared.
