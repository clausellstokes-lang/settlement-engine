- A holder is read from a lane's own commits; six older lanes have no merge base in the chair's shallow clone: use the cut's holders for them, or deepen.

- A seat's call is not accepted on the seat's own measure while its reader is still reading: the chair accepted `clergy` and `armedForce` at about 22:36 UTC and withdrew it at 22:39 when the reader's draw (5 of 10) arrived (record 407). And put NO time label in a message to a seat: the notification's own header carries the time.
