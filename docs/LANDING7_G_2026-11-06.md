## What the chair must rule

1. **The thorp-11 vacancy pair, left red.** The Power tab's missing-seat reading
   (`src/domain/dossier/powerStrata.js :: rulingChainOf`) keys on faction names matching `/claimant/i`.
   Since RUNG1-TRUTH unit D (`src/generators/power/stressFactions.js :: firstUnusedPoolName`), a
   non-royal `succession_void` names its claimants from the noble pool. A vacant governing faction
   therefore reads as an ordinary seat: 20 of 1,000 thorps carry the `vacant` modifier and none
   reads "Stands empty". This is an engine/view question, not a fixture question. One option is for
   the view to key on the `vacant` modifier or a typed stressor, and the pin to be re-stated on the
   pool names. `seatHolder`'s first test uses the same `/claimant/i` predicate and now counts 0
   vacancies, so it passes vacuously on that limb.
2. **Proxy hosts.** After `175320e56` no urban Housing row is proxy-keyed, so Housing rows now
   credit "Housing (500-4000 households)" as their institution. Only the fixture was moved here, per
   the brief; the sibling lane that retires the proxy mechanism should know.
3. **The showcase seed (row 6), left red.** Under ruling E1's rule (the first seed forging the WHOLE
   story) no crown- seed in 0..20000 qualifies at this tip. Per-claim misses over the 21,001 seeds:
   the coup (a guild master formalising) is missing in about 93%, and so is the Mining & Quarrying
   chain. Their conjunction, with the receipts, never occurs. The chair carries this to the owner's
   veto list, since it is his veto point. The options: (a) loosen the story (drop the coup or the
   chain from the synopsis, and re-choose by the same rule among the seeds that then qualify); (b)
   scan the besi- family or a wider range; (c) treat the coup's disappearance at `1264a8152` as a
   generator question, not a seed question.
