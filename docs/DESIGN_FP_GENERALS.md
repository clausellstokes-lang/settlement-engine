## §0 WHAT THE OWNER ASKED FOR, IN ONE PARAGRAPH (the verbatim words are in the kit; §12 is the ledger)

Every army gets a general — one of the settlement's NAMED NPCs, chosen by the court that fields the army, the military/guard faction preferred. A general who loses can be CAPTURED and is then out of the war until returned (exchange, ransom, release at the peace, escape); the court may send another. Every captive shifts the LEVERAGE at the peace. A general's victories — sieges above battles — grow their INFLUENCE at home; capture costs it. The general joins the war through the military faction but stays in their HOME faction, so their political weight is SPLIT between the two. A general who comes home and coups puts their HOME faction in power with the military second. Personality and BELIEF steer the army's movements. Standing (rank in faction, rank of the faction, reputation) scales how much of the settlement and how many units take the field — and the court's RISK CALCULUS chooses whom to send: a cheap loss or a costly chance. A general can be COMPROMISED by another court; a general who wins too much DRAWS FIRE; a vassal's general moves under the OVERLORD's general; the general's ORIGIN faction tilts the targeting; RECENT experience amplifies, and fades; HABITS form from outcomes; AFFECTED enemies remember how the general fights and what the general did to them, and that memory outlives the war into trade, embassy and mediation; MERCY and RUTHLESSNESS are each the other's counterforce, and mercy is repaid at the table. "All of this is counterforced."

## §1 THESIS

**A general is a READ WIRED ONTO THE ARMY THE WAR LAYER ALREADY MARCHES, plus three small memories.** The army record (`warArmyRecord.js :: seedDeploymentState`) carries no leader today — measured: its fields are strength, facets, logistics, objective, return condition. This program adds ONE conditional field to that record (`deployment.general`, §4 M1), casts its holder from the roster the envoy casting already reads (`envoyCasting.js :: castableRoster`), lets the general's personality and belief colour decisions the war layer already makes (the engage/withdraw/storm/starve choices at `armyTransitKernel`, `warDeployment` step 3 and `warSiegeVerdict`), takes a captured general off stage through the ONE participation chokepoint the roads already own (`roads/state.js :: isOffStage`, the `hostage` whereabouts, the ransom record and its roads home), and remembers three things on the per-person ledger the growth layer already keeps (`spatialLedgers.npcGrowth`, §4 M3): what the general has done lately (experience, fading), what each AFFECTED court holds against or for them (the scourge, signed, fading slower), and how each affected court has seen them fight (conduct, fading slowest). Everything else — the leverage at the peace, the recall and the coup, the fame that draws fire, the overlord's hand on the vassal, the origin tilts, the habits, the mercy — is a BANDED COUPLING on a plane the estate already carries, named by symbol in §7 and §8, each with its force and its counterforce scored off the same evidence (§5.3).

**But the chain is red at its first hop, and that is the volume's first finding (§3.1).** The owner's whole design presumes armies that march, fight, win, lose and are captured. The opening measurement (FP-40) finds that in the three read seeds across the three presets the DM plays, over twenty yearly advances each, armies marched TWICE in 180 realm-years, both in year one, both resolved by a storm inside ten ticks, both ending in a vassalized loser and nineteen silent years; no field battle was fought in any run; no named person was captured by an army; no coup succeeded. At tick grain the courts that WANT war (every tick, on the one `rival` edge) reach the war-ready rung on about two percent of ticks, because the posture ramp is cooled on about ninety-seven percent of ticks by two floors the live worlds sit permanently under — stored food below a month and a half, legitimacy below thirty (it reads ZERO in every court of both measured realms). A general who never fights is invisible, so **UNIT 0 is the cure of the march** (`mobilization.js :: stepPosture`'s cool triggers, banded against the measured spread; the deploy gate's second refusal measured at build), and the chain is accepted only by an experience read that sees a general cast, a battle or storm, a capture, a leverage line at a peace and a homecoming — in that order, on these seeds (§10).

**SIMPLICITY OVER FIDELITY governs every choice below (boundary 3).** Where the owner's words could be read as a new subsystem — a battle AI, a prisoner ledger, a court-keyed memory of persons, a second habit model, a second covert model, a second experience counter — this volume chose the smaller shape on an existing plane and says so at each place (§4's "deliberately NOT modelled", §8's "the smaller shape").
