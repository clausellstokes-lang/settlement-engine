# DESIGN — INFORMATION STATECRAFT (the fifth domain gets its verbs)
## Fable 5 architecture, 2026-07-14 — commissioned by the owner ("as we have made trade, war, peace, and generosity robust, have we done the same for information?" → "do it!")
### Companions: DESIGN_SUPPLY_WEB_WARFARE (shares the W-DOCTRINE wave), DESIGN_PEACE_ENGINE (disclosure terms land in its catalog), DESIGN_GENEROSITY_ENGINE (intel-sharing lands in its act catalog), DESIGN_COHESION_WEAVE §G (named ties as the human-scale channels)

## 0. THE PARITY FINDING (why this doc exists)

Information is the sim's most robust SUBSTRATE — it travels roads at hop-speed, degrades,
corroborates by independent lineage, settles into per-settlement/per-faction beliefs, and every
other engine already consumes it (peace is an information problem; supply-web strikes read the
target through fog; treaty compliance is monitored through beliefs; prizes are belief-appraised).
But trade, war, peace, and generosity are domains of DELIBERATE ACTION — typed reasons in,
receipted verdicts out — while information remains ambient. Nobody yet ACTS on the medium.
This doc gives information its four verbs: SEE, HIDE, LIE, SHARE. No new substrate is built;
every verb writes into machinery that already exists.

## 1. WHY THIS IS CHEAP (the substrate inventory)

Rumor lineages + corroboration weights (the rumor network), belief ledgers with decay and
truth-divergence, fog as distance-mediated staleness, merchants/routes as the physical carrier
layer, the EV-kernel idiom (dispatchEV/generosityEV precedents) for choosing among options, the
hysteresis idiom for posture changes, spatialLedgers dormancy for any new state. The verbs are
thin decision layers over these. The one genuinely new mechanic is CREDIBILITY AS A STOCK (§4),
and even that is an extension of corroboration weighting, not a parallel system.

## 2. THE FOUR VERBS

### 2.1 SEE — intelligence as investment (the information rung of the acquisition ladder)
A settlement with a standing interest (a prize ranking, a war footing, a treaty to monitor) may
BUY SIGHT: debrief passing merchants (cheap, low fidelity, no cost to relations), retain paid
eyes in a target settlement (dearer, better fidelity, DENIABLE-UNTIL-LINEAGE like the covert
instruments), or commission a survey of routes/stocks (the supply-web read, §2 of the war
doctrine, priced instead of free). Mechanically: an intelligence posture per (watcher, target)
pair — a small ledger entry that REDUCES BELIEF STALENESS and RAISES FIDELITY for that pair's
reads, at an upkeep cost in coin and (if exposed) grievance. The informed-vs-blind asymmetry the
war doctrine already models becomes a CHOSEN, PAID position. EV shape: expected value of better
sight scales with the stakes held against that target (war footing, treaty exposure, trade
dependence) — a settlement watches those it fears, owes, or feeds on. Character colors it:
merchant leagues debrief (it's free with the caravans), theocracies hear confessions
(temple-network sight, §5), paranoid archetypes over-invest.

### 2.2 HIDE — counter-intelligence (information denial, priced honestly)
The opposite posture: close the market to foreign merchants (buys opacity, COSTS trade — the
carrier layer and the commerce layer are the same wagons, so secrecy is never free), guard the
passes (slows all lineages through you, friend and foe), keep court business behind walls
(reduces leak fidelity from inside). Mechanically a secrecy posture (hysteresis enter/exit —
paranoia is sticky) that INCREASES rivals' staleness/fog about you while applying an honest
trade/relations tax. Counter-play to SEE: a high secrecy posture degrades paid eyes and raises
their exposure odds. The tension is self-balancing: the settlement that hides best trades worst
— seal the gates and the world stops telling you things too (your own inbound sight dims;
isolation is symmetric).

### 2.3 LIE — disinformation (alignment-gated, blowback-priced)
Deliberately seed a false rumor into the network with a forged or laundered lineage: inflate
your garrison, conceal the bad harvest, plant the story that the pass is cut. Mechanically this
is the EXISTING organic-rumor machinery invoked with a falsehood payload and a synthetic origin;
it propagates, degrades, and corroborates exactly like truth — that is what makes it dangerous
and what makes it discoverable (a lie meets independent contradiction; contradiction is already
a first-class event in belief reconciliation). GATING: alignment and structure decide
willingness (a lawful-good council will not; a deceitful or desperate seat will; theocracies lie
rarely but catastrophically about doctrine), and the E0 governor drama-classes campaign-scale
deception so it stays an event, not a hum. BLOWBACK (the equal-and-opposite law): exposure —
which happens through the SAME lineage-surfacing mechanic that outs covert funding — converts
the lie into grievance (people-held, against the liar), a legitimacy hit at home (the court that
lies to neighbors lies to you), and a CREDIBILITY charge (§4). Success is real too: a believed
garrison-bluff deters a war (the Blainey mechanic consumed the false belief), a concealed famine
holds a trade price. Lies WORK — that is why the price must be structural.

### 2.4 SHARE — information as a good (the trade, generosity, and peace hooks)
- **SELL (trade lane):** intelligence as a priced good between non-intimate parties — route
  conditions, stock levels, a rival's posture — sold at fidelity-discounted prices. Sold
  information carries the SELLER'S lineage: bad product damages the seller's credibility stock,
  so an information market self-polices (§4).
- **GIVE (generosity lane, lands in E1's act catalog):** WARNING an ally — of the raid you
  learned of, the plague on the road, the coalition forming — is a generosity act with real
  sacrifice math: you spend strategic advantage (and sometimes expose your paid eyes — the
  widow's-mite math prices what the telling cost you, not what it was worth to hear). Gratitude
  scales accordingly; a warning that saved the granary binds like grain given in famine.
- **DEMAND (peace lane, lands in W-PEACE's term catalog):** disclosure clauses — the victor
  demands sight inside the loser's court (an observer's seat, an open ledger, a resident envoy)
  — duration-capped like every term, monitored through beliefs like every term, straining like
  every term (hosting your conqueror's eyes breeds quiet fury; expiry or expulsion is a
  compliance event). The weaker side pays in opacity, the coin the informed value most.

## 3. THE ONE LAW THAT BINDS THE VERBS
Every verb manipulates BELIEFS, never TRUTH. SEE lowers your divergence from truth; HIDE raises
theirs; LIE raises theirs deliberately; SHARE transfers your belief (with your lineage attached,
at your fidelity). The world's ground truth is only ever changed by the other four domains.
This keeps endogeneity clean and makes every information act auditable as a belief delta —
which is exactly what the dramatic-irony brief already renders.

## 4. CREDIBILITY AS A STOCK (the new mechanic — small, load-bearing)
Each settlement (and faction voice) carries a credibility weight consumed by the EXISTING
corroboration math: information arriving on your lineage is weighted by it. It RISES slowly when
your information proves out (your warnings were true, your sold reads were good) and FALLS
sharply on exposed deception (asymmetric by design — trust builds in years, dies in an
afternoon). Effects compound coherently with zero extra rules: a caught liar's future warnings
are discounted (the boy who cried wolf, mechanically), their sold intelligence loses market
value (the trade hook prices credibility in), their peace assurances count for less at the
table (the Blainey convergence needs MORE evidence from a low-credibility court — wars against
proven liars last longer, which is historically exact). Decay: credibility regresses toward
neutral on a generational clock; people-held, with a seat-held component for the court itself
(a new dynasty inherits SOME of the old court's reputation — the paper, not the hatred).

## 5. COHERENCE MATRIX (the completeness rule, applied to information)
- **× trade:** carriers are wagons; secrecy taxes commerce; intelligence is a market good.
- **× war:** SEE is the supply-web read priced; LIE is the garrison bluff; the war doctrine's
  informed-strangler strategy now has a purchasable prerequisite and a counter (HIDE).
- **× peace:** disclosure terms; credibility gates convergence speed; a mediator's value
  includes their credibility stock (the trusted broker is trusted BECAUSE their lineage is).
- **× generosity:** warnings as gifts with sacrifice math; sharing sight IS taking a side
  (aid-into-a-siege logic applies — warning a besieged town of the relief column's delay is an
  act the besieger will remember).
- **× faith/alignment:** temple networks as a parallel carrier lane (pilgrims and clergy carry
  news between same-faith settlements — brothers-quadrant pairs enjoy standing fidelity bonuses;
  natural-enemies pairs get nothing and suspect everything); alignment gates LIE willingness.
- **× factions/structure:** merchant leagues see through commerce, theocracies through
  congregations, criminal-leaning seats through smugglers (their carrier lane is the smuggle
  routes already modeled); councils leak more than autarchs (more mouths at the table).
- **× named ties (COHESION_WEAVE §G):** cross-settlement NPC relationships are privileged
  edges — the human-scale channels these verbs run on, and the leak surface HIDE must price.

## 6. CONSTITUTIONAL POSTURE
World-only (no party-facing spy missions — that pen is the DM's; the world's information war is
scenery and consequence, never a quest generator). No named-character fates: paid eyes and
exposed agents are ANONYMOUS AGGREGATES ("her eyes in Thornwall went quiet"); a named NPC may be
CITED as a channel in prose (via §G ties) but is never burned, turned, or executed by the
engine. (PER-NPC CREDIBILITY is the deep-couplings layer's sanctioned province — DESIGN_DEEP_COUPLINGS
D-2, owner ruling 2026-07-19: a personal credibility stock and a lie-stigma standing hit are
REPUTATION costs, not fate resolutions; no exposure removes, kills, or disappears the NPC — fates
remain unresolvable everywhere.) Dormant/additive: all posture state nests under the spatialLedgers namespace, absent ⇒
byte-identical; the verbs light with W-DOCTRINE's gate. Party never feeds the math; tier never
touches it. E0 governs initiation tempo (deception and posture-change are drama-classed
spontaneity; consequence chains — exposure, grievance, credibility collapse — are never
throttled). DM sovereignty: every verb is also a manual operation with identical receipts.

## 7. LEGIBILITY (receipts all the way down)
The dramatic-irony brief gains the information war's best surfaces almost free, because beliefs
and lineages are already rendered: "Thornwall believes the garrison doubled (it did not — the
rumor traces to Marsh Gate, a source twice wrong this decade)". Postures are instrument-panel
states (watching/guarded/open per pair); credibility renders as a reputation band with its last
three proofs/failures cited; an exposed lie is a chronicle event with the full lineage as its
receipt. The DM sees truth and belief side by side; the player-safe brief sees only what its
projection has earned — the seam already sealed.

## 8. PINS + SOAK (what proves it)
Same-seed byte-identity with the gate off (dormancy). With the gate on: a paid-eyes posture
measurably lowers belief divergence for its pair (and only its pair); secrecy raises rivals'
staleness AND self-inflicts inbound dimming (the symmetry pin); a seeded lie propagates,
corroborates, then dies on independent contradiction with the credibility charge applied; an
exposed lie's author takes the grievance + legitimacy + credibility triple; a warning-gift
creates a gratitude ledger entry priced by sacrifice, not value; a disclosure term strains and
expires like every term; wars against low-credibility courts take measurably longer to converge
(the Blainey pin). Soak: the everything-on run must show information acts at story tempo (E0
classed), not a constant whisper-war hum.
