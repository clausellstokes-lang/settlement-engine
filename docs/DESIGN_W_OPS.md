## 1. The unified operation model

Three operation classes, ONE grammar — differing only in who wills them:

| Class | Willed by | Exists today as |
|---|---|---|
| **GOAL** | the NPC (endogenous) | `shortGoal`/`longGoal` + rebranch + culmination (npcAgency) |
| **MISSION** | a principal — court, faction, patron, cult | ES-1's door (dispatcher deferred); corruption web tasking |
| **ERRAND** | a seat, travel-borne | the errand spine: envoy embassies, covert journeys |

The one grammar every operation walks: **charter** (typed kind from a closed catalog) →
**casting/acceptance** (the two casting laws for who; the ⟨F8⟩ vetting refusal for whether —
reading effective character, KNOWN character of counterparties, §802 R1 willingness) →
**method** (the R3b menu: actor fit × counterparty fit × context) → **resolution** (seeded roll;
choose-on-known, resolve-on-true; per-method failure modes) → **receipts** (evidence-bound;
Herald-legible) → **praxis** (R3/R3c: the practice mark + the signed outcome lesson, feeding
drift, feeding the next choice). A player/user act interrupts anywhere (the DM's mandate).

**Acceptance reads the personal risk register (W-LIVES §13, ODQ §803.1):** every accept/refuse/
continue decision compares the operation's priced risk against `riskRegister(npc, context)` —
center from effective character × desperation, breadth from the CHAOS projection (lawful narrow,
chaotic broad). The two-scale seam: the court's SP-C appetite governs what is OFFERED; the
person's register governs what is ACCEPTED. Seek-more and seek-less (§803.1 R5) are both
expressions of this one seam — the riskier, better-paying mission is ambition's honest door, and
declining it is a character statement the principal receipts.

## 3. The infiltration ladder (extends ES §3.4/§3.4b upward; L0–L2 EXIST, L3–L4 NEW)

| Level | Name | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | **Passing ear** | waypoint hearing on a journey — the host's own beliefs | ES §3.4 (built shape) |
| L1 | **Observer** | target dwell — counting granaries, seeing the place | ES §3.4 (built shape) |
| L2 | **Rooted** | the gather-or-govern dwell: re-sampling, ramping catch odds, bench/career bleeding at home | ES §3.4b (owner addition G — ruled) |
| L3 | **Placed** | cover residence + a POSITION inside a host institution/faction (guild bench, clergy roster, garrison) under a false face | NEW |
| L4 | **Seated** | a court/faction SEAT held under foreign will — the mole | NEW, converging with the corruption web |

**The two new rungs, ruled:**
- **L3 PLACED** — placement is an operation (its own mission kind, vetted, resolved by R3b: the
  method is the cover story — the deceitful place well). A placed spy's **access** widens from
  observing the place to reading the INSTITUTION's interior (its rosters, its backing, its
  doctrine posture — products the ES gradient can carry home); their catch model shifts from the
  market-face ramp to institutional vetting events (seeded, sparser, sharper). **The cover is an
  information-layer object**: a claimed character and purpose riding the existing
  `declaredPurpose`/`truePurpose` seam — the host settlement's R2 KNOWN character of the spy IS
  the cover while it holds, and exposure retroacts through the ES §3.5 taint cascade unchanged.
- **L4 SEATED** — the mole is NOT a parallel system: a seat held under foreign will is exactly
  the corruption web's **foreign-leashed seat** (W-DOCTRINE-3b), reached by PLACEMENT instead of
  by corruption — a second door into that one state, precisely as §802 R1 was for corruption
  itself. Everything the web already rules (leash resolution, foreign grip, exposure machinery)
  applies verbatim; what this volume adds is the ROAD there and the tasking read.
- **Depth prices drift (the W-LIVES coupling, and the drama):** the milieu term's exposure
  multiplier scales with level — L0 barely brushes; L4 LIVES the host's life. A long-embedded
  spy drifts toward the host's character, and their FIDELITY axis strains against their
  principal: **going native is an emergent arc, not a scripted event** — the R4 bond grammar
  covers the endgame (a mole with a changed heart is duress-bound to WHICHEVER side holds their
  receipts). Bounded exactly as all drift is: anti-ratchet home, milieu rates smallest, the §9
  identity-erosion property extended to covers.
- **Depth prices custody:** ES §3.10's scaling stands — deeper is worth more and held longer;
  ransom/pardon/confession exits per R4. **Exits per level:** extraction (an errand), burn
  (voluntary exposure at home's choosing), root permanently (defection — the FIDELITY reversal
  receipted), or ride the R4 arc.
