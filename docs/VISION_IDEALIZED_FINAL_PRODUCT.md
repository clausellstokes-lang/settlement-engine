# THE IDEALIZED FINAL PRODUCT — the canonical vision (v2)
## Fable 5, 2026-07-14 — updated after the fix program + the complete E-family design corpus
### Successors: this is WHAT settlementforge.com is being built toward; the program docs are HOW. v1 is superseded in place; the five-layer frame stands, deepened.

## IDENTITY
SettlementForge, complete, is **the world engine behind the table**: a deterministic,
causally-receipted, belief-aware, motive-bearing living world that a game master operates by
hand or by voice; that runs while they are away and greets them with what it did; that remembers
everything, forgives selectively, explains itself completely, and never takes the pen out of the
human's hand. Its identity in one contrast: **other tools write what happens next;
SettlementForge runs what happens next — and can tell you why.**

## LAYER 1 — THE FORGE (generation)
Every settlement is an argument, not a roll. Twenty causally-ordered steps turn a config into an
internally-consistent place: economy from resources, institutions from economy, factions from
institutions, people from factions, secrets from pressures, history from all of it — each step
receipted three causes deep, so "why is the guard corrupt?" always has a real answer. Player
choices all matter (the seams are contract-tested); the dramatic town types arrive dramatic
(a slave-revolt town opens on shut gates, not market day); histories run deep; eleven naming
cultures speak at researched register. Same seed, same town, forever — proven live with a
button on the landing page. And at the end-state you can build by SPEAKING: describe the
fortified river town with three claimant factions and no internal security, and the intent
compiler configures this same causal generator, compares the result against your stated
constraints, confesses its deviations, and lets you revise. The AI configures the forge; it
never fakes the forging.

## LAYER 2 — THE CLOCK (the living world)
Canonize a realm and the map becomes physics; weeks tick; and the world lives on five strata:

**2a. THE MOVING WORLD.** Information travels roads at hop-speed, degrades organically,
corroborates by independent lineage, and settles into per-settlement, per-faction BELIEFS that
decay, contradict, and diverge from truth. Goods are finite stocks on real routes through tolls,
banditry, and smuggle runs; supply starves; entrepôts earn centrality. People migrate with exact
conservation. Armies posture, march, collide, retreat, recall, besiege, and starve sieges out.
Plague walks the trade roads into the temples' care; the flood-year strikes the riverside town
its terrain always promised, and the tail runs itself.

**2b. THE SOCIETY — motive, character, memory.** Every settlement has INTERESTS (prize rankings
over what it values in others; a standing resource pressure that climbs the acquisition ladder —
trade, then treaty, then coercion, then conquest — at the pace its character allows), CHARACTER
(the faith×alignment quadrants: brothers, schismatics, respectable rivals, natural enemies —
crossed with structural lenses: what an agrarian council fears, how a merchant league decides,
which nerve a theocracy's legitimacy sits on), and MEMORY (grievances, obligations, refusals,
betrayals — each with its own decay clock, and each tagged seat-held or people-held: a new ruler
inherits the paper, never the people's hate). Decisions consume typed, receipted REASONS — wars
have a casus list, peaces have their grounds, trade routes know why they opened ("after the
winter of the cut pass, Thornwall courted a second grain road") — under a symmetry law that
keeps causes for war and causes for peace equally robust forever.

**2c. THE GENEROSITY ENGINE.** Kindness is a real decision under a real ledger: bond and history
gate it, conscience and strategy argue for it, the granary's forward margin and the deployed
army's appetite argue against, and the verdict comes tiered — full, partial, as-credit, or
refusal — with the reasoning receipted in the house voice ("the granaries stayed shut: the army
at the front eats first"). Every act reacts: gifts bind (gratitude scales with the giver's
SACRIFICE — the widow's mite), refusals wound (judged through the refused party's BELIEFS — a
known hardship is forgiven, an imagined comfort reads as betrayal), giving broadcasts wealth to
raiders and character to allies, aid into a siege takes a side, and mercy to a plagued town may
carry the plague home. Evil generosity exists and is priced: the lender who gives to indebt.

**2d. STATECRAFT.** War is not only assault: the informed strangle supply webs before the final
confrontation (reading the target's dependency graph through their own fog — and sometimes
burning the wrong village); the bloodless wage embargo, toll war, and purchase denial; the
covert fund dissidents and sedition, deniable until the rumor lineage surfaces them. Peace is an
information problem — wars end when the fighting has taught both courts the same truth — and a
pricing problem: the victor's believed advantage buys a TERM BUDGET spent on what it values most
from the loser (tribute streams, resource shares, compelled alliances, demilitarization, puppet
seats, missionary access, non-aggression), every term duration-capped (perpetual extraction is
forbidden — a treaty is a season of history, never a law of physics), every term monitored
through beliefs (a distant victor can be cheated), every term straining the compliant seat until
grievance, default, or genuine conversion. Harsh peace breeds revanchism and the next war;
magnanimous peace IS generosity and can turn enemies into allies. Coalitions negotiate together
or fracture into separate exits with betrayal priced; cross-pressured neighbors — kin to both
courts — become the world's natural mediators; declared neutrals profit until the belligerents'
patience ends.

**2e. EMERGENT POLITIES — AND THE TEMPO.** Hegemonies rise UNNAMED: an empire is a pattern the
topology exhibits — one center holding vassals, tributaries, compelled allies, puppets — never
an entity the engine mints. It strains under compliance and distance-fog, frays through
defection windows and treaty expiries, fractures on succession, and manufactures its own
encirclement (an overgrown center IS the common threat that realigns everyone else). The engine
builds the bones; THE DM BAPTIZES — christening the empire is a human act. Leagues stand as
multi-party pacts; booms and busts, reconstructions and golden ages give history its upswing.
And over it all, the NARRATIVE TEMPO GOVERNOR keeps the composed world breathing — throttling
spontaneous births by drama class, never censoring a consequence chain — so a decade holds one
great war and one golden age instead of four of each, and a held-back storm reads as the quiet
before it. Drama is dialed (the presets finally mean their names), delayed but never denied.

## LAYER 3 — THE COVENANT (authority)
One trust model governs everything: something proposes — the simulator validates and resolves —
the DM authorizes. The DM is sovereign: approving canon, protecting entities, dialing autonomy
per domain, overriding anything; the party observes the world without ever feeding its math; no
named character's fate is ever resolved by the machine — and no PROSE ever mints canon the
simulator would have to honor (dynastic weddings are rendered at house level; persons belong to
the storyteller). Manual controls and natural language are equal citizens of ONE operation layer
with ONE history: change by hand, ask the AI to continue, edit its proposal, approve, advance —
identical receipts throughout, everything auditable, everything reversible that can be:
operation undo, checkpoints, branches, reproducible counterfactuals.

## LAYER 4 — THE VOICE (legibility)
Depth without legibility is noise, so explanation is a first-class organ. The dossier reads as
fiction with receipts under every claim; the chronicle scrolls decades with causes attached; the
crier speaks every mover's news in period voice; the market quotes real prices ("four silver the
bushel — dear, for the roads are cut"). Continuous conditions live on instruments — fronts and
plague and belief-divergence drawn on the realm map. TREATIES ARE DOCUMENTS: parties, terms,
years remaining, compliance strain term by term ("tribute paid grudgingly; the garrison
resented") — a fraying peace is legible at the seam that will tear. Wars and peaces carry WHY
lines; the dramatic-irony brief shows reasons ACCUMULATING ("three of five peace reasons now
present; this war is dying") and beliefs diverging ("both courts think they are winning").
Briefs compress scale: the weekly digest, the session-prep sheet, faction dossiers, the
player-safe brief composed only from the public projection so it cannot leak. The PDFs are
shelf-worthy artifacts — the dossier, the campaign war room with its treaty table. THE
SETTLEMENT MAP (owner-commissioned 2026-07-14, design frozen: DESIGN_SETTLEMENT_MAP.md) joins
the library: a [Dossier|Map] toggle above every saved settlement, the town drawn
deterministically from its own dossier — hover any building and the institution explains
itself, districts carry their wealth and their dangers, and because the map is a pure
projection of the dossier, whatever impacts one impacts the other, always. And at the road's
end, the parked last thing: the map's DEEP LIVING form — persistent scarring history, the
burned quarter still scarred years later, the boom district visibly risen, the temple grown two
sizes since the plague made it beloved.

## LAYER 5 — THE INTERPRETER (Surveyor)
Natural language as the second control surface over the same operation layer. ASK anything and
the analyst answers from receipts, citing the causal chain. COMMAND anything — "make the king's
grip slip without civil war" — and the intent compiler returns a labeled, conservative,
reviewable proposal: required, inferred, optional, uncertain, protected. After each session,
tell the world what your players did in plain sentences; approve what it distills; advance;
harvest next week's material — play, tell, approve, advance, harvest: the campaign's heartbeat.
The AI is interface, interpreter, planner, analyst, and editor; NEVER the engine: it cannot
silently alter canon, must confess mechanics the simulator lacks, cannot see DM truth when
writing for players, and leaves a permanent record of everything proposed and decided. Campaign
instructions persist as law over interpretation, never over truth. Providers are rented; the
world model is owned; bring your own key.

## THE COVENANTS (the constitution as promises to the user)
1. Same seed, same world, forever — canon is stable, shareable, reproducible.
2. New features never disturb old worlds — everything ships dormant; absent means unchanged.
3. Truth and belief are separate and the seam is sealed on every surface, including the AI's.
4. No named fate is ever resolved by the machine — and no prose mints one either.
5. The party observes; it never feeds the math.
6. Every fact, every war, every peace, every kindness can explain itself — receipts all the way down.
7. Bounded emergence — rescuable, never annihilated; recovery as real as ruin; every force a brake.
8. The governor throttles spontaneity, never causality — history breathes, chains never censor.
9. Patterns over entities — empires and eras are read, not minted; the DM baptizes.
10. Fast on day one and fast in year ten, both by enforced budget.

## THE BUSINESS
A ladder that mirrors the depth: free forging with the anonymous funnel; $2.99 buys any single
dossier as a permanent artifact; Premium buys the living world; Surveyor ($19.99) buys the
interpreter — priced against prep hours, not tool subscriptions — with managed outcome-priced
credits and BYOK for the heavy and the sovereign; thirty lifetime Founders. The gallery spreads
sanitized worlds; VTTs are complemented, never fought — finish the session in Foundry, tell
SettlementForge what happened, walk into next week prepared. Beneath it, the un-copyable asset:
human worldbuilding intent paired with expert canonical operations inside a causal world model —
and the benchmark that defines the category's quality bar, because the category is this product.

## WHAT IT IS NOT
Not a VTT. Not an AI storyteller. Not a wiki that remembers without understanding. Not a map
painter whose maps mean nothing. Not a grand-strategy game you play to win. Not a replacement
for the DM — the deepest commitment in the design is that the human at the head of the table is
the author, and the world is the co-author that never breaks character and never forgets.

## IN ONE SENTENCE
A world that is generated coherently, lives believably, wants things understandably, misinforms
itself realistically, gives and withholds humanly, wars strategically, makes peace at a price,
rises into unnamed empires and frays out of them, recovers movingly, paces itself like a story,
remembers generationally, explains itself completely, obeys its owner faithfully, and can be
spoken to — **the world engine behind the table.**
