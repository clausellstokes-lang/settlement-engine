## THE AUTHORING CONTRACT (read before adding a single line)

**THE FLOOR (SP-6, spine §2).** Every phrased kind ships a seeded variant pool of AT
LEAST FOUR templates, seeded per-entity so same-seed worlds keep their sentences
(THE PROMISE). The kind's WHAT_PHRASES registration walker asserts the floor — a
two-variant kind reds the gate exactly as an unregistered one does.

**THE FREQUENCY-SCALED FLOOR (spine amendment, 2026-08-03).** Four is the floor for a
kind a reader meets once. A kind a reader meets every season needs more, or the world
starts rhyming with itself. The amended floor scales by how often the kind FIRES:
chronic/routine kinds carry EIGHT to TWELVE angle-distinct variants, notable kinds SIX
or more, major/rare kinds FOUR or more. The FAMILY RULE below governs the counting —
slot-swaps are one family, not two variants — so a pool meets the amended floor only
on ANGLES, never on lines.

**[VERIFIER 2026-08-03 — POP-3's four ROUTINE pools were repaired to floor.]** The
deepening pass carried all of POP-3 to seven, which is the NOTABLE depth; four of its
kinds are classed `routine` and were therefore a variant short. `remembered_as_sent_forth`,
`memory_elder_face`, `ties_kept` and `ties_lapsed` each gained two angle-distinct
lines — the traveller's report and, per pool, the season's frame, the institution's
consequence, the small human detail, or the road's indifference — bringing them to
NINE, the depth every other routine pool in this annex already stands at. The repair is
APPEND ONLY: no existing variant was reworded, renumbered or dropped, and no `SLOTS:`
line changed. Every other pool in this annex clears its class floor as authored.

**THE FAMILY RULE.** Slot variety is not depth. Two templates differing only in slot
fills are ONE family for the floor's count. Every variant below takes a different
STRUCTURE and a different ANGLE off the angle palette: *the event plain · the street's
view · the ledger's or institution's view · the consequence forward · the understatement
or irony*, widened by the frequency-scaled floor with *the traveller's report · the
season's frame · the small human detail*. A pool that reads as one sentence five times
has not met the floor, whatever the count says.

**THE SLOT CONVENTION.** No proper noun and no cause is ever baked into a template. The
closed slot set:

| Slot | Fills with |
|---|---|
| `{settlement}` | the subject settlement, by name (the address law) |
| `{counterpart}` | the other settlement in the pair — destination, origin, host, neighbour |
| `{npc}` | a cast NPC, read-wired per episode; never minted, never a fate resolved |
| `{faction}` | the faction or seat named on the receipt |
| `{house}` | the merchant/landed house named on the receipt |
| `{temple}` | the temple or observance named on the receipt |
| `{band}` | a QUANTITY_BANDS phrase (`a few souls`, `a dozen or so`, `dozens`, `a hundred or so`, `several hundred`, `many hundreds`, `thousands`, `nobody`) or a duration band word — never a figure |
| `{reason}` | the recorded reason, in band words (the address law's fourth field) |
| `{good}` | the good or promise at issue — grain, silver, land, wages-for-hands |
| `{route}` | the road or lane, by its in-world name |

**NO DIGITS.** No variant line below contains a numeral, a ratio, a percentage or a
band token. Counts speak only through `{band}` or through band words already in the
prose (*a handful, one by one, a generation*). Structural labels in the headings (wave
ids such as POP-1) are metadata, not prose, and are the only digits in the file.

**THE EXEMPLAR-VERBATIM RULE, AS APPLIED.** Where the volume authors an exemplar
sentence for a kind, that sentence is variant 1, word for word — except that baked
proper nouns and bare counts are replaced by their slots. The slot law and the no-digit
law are estate law and outrank verbatim reproduction; the wording is otherwise
untouched. Every such variant is marked *(§8)* or *(volume exemplar)*.

**HONESTY (R-28).** Every verb below is entailable by the receipt its kind is minted
from. No line asserts a departure that did not happen, a death the terms cannot
support, or an arrival that was refused. Where the receipt genuinely cannot attribute,
the line says so in the house voice (J-POP-9).

**BELIEF ATTRIBUTION.** Belief-side and inference-side lines carry their attribution —
*they say · the word is · men said · no word has ever come · it is said*. The engine
never states a belief as its own truth.

**LAW ONE.** No line confirms that a god acted (believers read; courts judge). No line
resolves or states a named person's fate. The NAMED-CAST MERCY (volume §1b) means the
healer survives the sickness year BY CONSTRUCTION, and the receipts here may say so.

**AUDIENCE.** Every kind is authored PUBLIC. One kind in this volume is covert and
carries `AUDIENCE: dm-only`; it rides includeCovert exactly as the premium-isolation
law requires.

**SIGNIFICANCE.** Classes are the spine's SP-6a family (`routine / notable / major`).
This annex ASSIGNS; it never authors a scale (volume §7).
