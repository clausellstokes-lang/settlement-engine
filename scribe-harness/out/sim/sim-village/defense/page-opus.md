[FAIL] The town keeps no house for the sick and no food put by. A traveller says a man who takes ill here is nursed by whoever has room for him, because there is no house for the sick. At the long table a woman says the town keeps no store, so a bad season is carried by whichever households had a good one. The older heads here hold that the town has done without a store as long as they can remember, and they are the ones asked about it in a bad spring. Asked at the church, the chapel's warden says the burying ground is the only thing the town keeps ready against a bad year. The women who gut the catch say the town keeps nothing past a market day, the grain no better than the fish.
