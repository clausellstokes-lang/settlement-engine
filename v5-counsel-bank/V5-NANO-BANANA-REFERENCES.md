## CHAIR VERDICTS (binding on the V5 spec; vetoable by the owner)

- **Depth/light canon** = image 7: one warm directional light, contact shadows
  where every object meets wood or parchment. Most of its realism is shadow,
  not detail — D3's system in evidence.
- **Cartouche material** = image 2's burned frame + gold-fill lettering at
  HEADER scale (the branded-cartouche ruling realized); image 4's torn
  gold-leaf organic silhouette becomes the LARGE-REGISTER variant in the size
  ladder. The bright mounted plate (images 1/6) is the retired-plate risk —
  reject as the header treatment.
- **Seal-in-O** = images 2 and 6: oversized wax O, edging over neighbors,
  device impression legible (image 6's triangle is nearest the estate device).
  This is D2's register target. The separate-seal-beside-the-plate variant
  (images 3/4/7) is NOT the directive but is noted as the large-register
  cartouche's optional companion.
- **Burned tabs** = image 6's "burns and words" + image 4's curved-barrel
  words: char with faint singe halo, letters riding the curve. D4 canon.
- **Bindings vocabulary** gains the SPIRAL LENGTHWISE BINDING (images 1/7) in
  addition to the end wraps + diagonal tie-offs; quill-through-wrap (image 3)
  is a keeper detail. All reuse the pinned V4C thread system tones.
- ⚠ **THE PENDANT PROBLEM** (image 5): vanes that merely dangle from bindings
  read as a talisman, not fletching. V5 law: each vane's LEADING EDGE runs
  visibly along the shaft (bound there) before the body droops — the hang must
  read as an aerodynamic vane at rest, never a hanging charm.
- **Lettering two-register ladder confirmed by eye**: blackletter is gorgeous
  at macro scale (images 3/4/6) and remains REJECTED at header scale
  (legibility law); cartographer's roman with swash caps for the header
  cartouche, the heavier hand unlocked at hero/About/PDF registers.
- **Feather barring**: the bold barred pattern in these generations reads
  "war arrow" strongly; V5 keeps the estate's grey-goose palette family with
  this barring RHYTHM as the pattern target.
