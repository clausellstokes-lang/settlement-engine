# FOUR DURATION-FREE VARIANTS FOR THE SILENT EVENT-TYPE ARMS — chair words act (Fable 5.1, 2026-09-05), for §900+
DESK-TIMEBAND measured (finding 5, re-derived exactly): `history.age` is sixth-band on 12/12 towns and 89/108 generated events are older than the adverbial ladder's reach, so every variant carrying `{timeband_since}` or `{timeband_age}` is undrawable on an old subject. Four arms of DS-GEN-9's `event type:` pools have NO duration-free variant and fall fully silent: `economic|anchored`, `religious|anchored`, `political|not anchored`, `demographic|not anchored`.

RULED (Fable chair): the ADVERBIAL SIXTH BAND IS REFUSED — it moves same-seed Herald output (2 of 18 disclosure lines), reds three correct pins, and overturns a law both annexes state in prose ("older than its bearers" is predicate-only). The cheaper route is taken: ONE duration-free variant per silent arm, appended as variant 5 of its pool, in the pool's own voice (persona tag · anchored dimension), carrying only `{event}` and `{settlement}`. STATE, never FATE; no digit; no em dash.

| pool (DS-GEN-9, `event type:` block in RECEIPT_POOLS_DOSSIER_STATE.md) | variant 5 |
|---|---|
| **economic** | 5. `[elder · anchored]` What {settlement} lives on is what {event} left it to live on, and the town stopped calling it new a long while back. |
| **religious** | 5. `[visitor · anchored]` {settlement}'s altars still stand where {event} moved them, and a stranger who asks is told the moving was long ago and the reasons were not. |
| **political** | 5. `[elder · not anchored]` {event} was argued out at {settlement} by people who are all gone now, and what they settled has been settled again since, differently, without anyone naming them. |
| **demographic** | 5. `[ledger · not anchored]` {settlement}'s rolls show {event} as a change in the count and nothing more; the families it added or took are not names anyone here still sorts by. |

Per-variant required slots: `{event} {settlement}` for all four (the walker pins `requiredSlots.length === pool.length`; each pool grows 4 → 5). The §0d ladder tables in BOTH annexes stay byte-identical (this act touches no band). The `.generated.js` pools regenerate by the estate's generator from the annex; the anchored dimension of each new variant is declared in its tag and must be honoured by the drawer (a `not anchored` variant never draws for an anchored record, and the reverse).
