9. **UNHELD OR HELD BY A FORCE TIED TO NO SETTLEMENT = A GENERAL HAZARD.** When `holder` is `none`, a brigand body, a menace, or any actor with no settlement behind it, NO attached town gets a bonus and EVERY attached town takes the NEGATIVE lean, scaled by its distance band and by the site's quality (a `ruined` site held by nobody → the mildest hazard; a `sound` site held by brigands → the strongest): security down, danger up on the roads under it, contraband, the raider source. The effect table carries this as its third holder class (`none · untied`) beside `settlement` and `faith/person`.
10. **THE LAST OWNER TO ACT HAS THE FIRST CLAIM.** The record keeps `lastHolder` (the last settlement-tied holder) and the week it last ACTED on the site (funded, garrisoned, restored). When a site falls empty or is taken by an untied force, `lastHolder`'s court is OFFERED THE RECLAIM FIRST — `site_reclaim` (CLIMAX; `moves · declines`) in its week — before any other court's decider may move on it; a decline (or the default when the court cannot) opens the site to whoever acts next (neighbours by distance band; the party by deed). Preference, never a right: nothing is forbidden.
11. **HOW A SITE CHANGES HANDS — by the world's ordinary roads, each named:** (a) ABANDONED by falling prosperity: the holder's funding band is derived from its treasury/prosperity rung (the landed prosperity ladder; the treasury when lit); when it reaches `none` the status goes `abandoned` after a dwell (DRAFT one season, UNMEASURED) and quality wears; (b) TRADED between settlements WITH THEIR SURROUNDING TERRITORY: the landed sovereignty conveyance road (WR-10, `sovereigntyIntent.js`; Amendment S: originals and overlords may trade their satellites) generalised to a site + its attached cells — the SITE and its ATTACHMENT convey at once; the LAND's holder changes through the host's own territory mechanism (on a canonized world a receipted, DM-confirmed re-canonize — the map builder reserves geometry-shifting changes to that act, J388; the per-gate political-holder policy H16 when it lands; aspatially the attachment alone); the hearing is the landed `sovereignty_conveyance` row; (c) CONQUERED BY SIEGE, SAME AS A SETTLEMENT: a held site is a siege target: `site_siege` (STANDING; the registry's `holds · falls · relieved · lifted`; the garrison's strength band in place of the walls; the attacker's might vs the garrison; a `falls` hands the site to the attacker's holder class — a hostile settlement, or an untied force → item 9); (d) OTHER REASONABLE ROADS, each through an existing writer: ceded as a PEACE TERM (GRAMMAR's catalog, a `site_cession` row beside the trade-rights rows); GRANTED by a liege as a fief (PL's charter occasion: a site given to a member with its attachment); SEIZED in a polity war (the verdict writer's patch); CLAIMED by a faith for a shrine (WF's hierarchy; the clergy as holder); SOLD (the treasury when lit; the conveyance road with coin).
