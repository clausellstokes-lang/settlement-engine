# THE BLOCK RE-CUT TO THE OWNER'S CLARITY STANDARD (rulings 42, 42a, 42b, 42c)

The owner has seen the landed prose and ruled. **Re-cut all of DS-DEF-2 — 24 written pools, ~550 faces — to the standard below.** This is a LINE EDIT, not a re-draft, and the distinction is what makes it affordable: the claims are already refuted and must not move.

## THE STANDARD, in the owner's own words and his own worked lines
> The town has walls. It has no soldiers.
> A clerk at the town hall says the town pays to repair the walls and pays nobody to guard them.
> A priest says the sick house takes anyone who comes. The grain store turns away anyone without an order.
> At the tavern they say the gatekeeper locks the gate at night and opens it for anyone he knows.
> A traveller found the steps to the wall blocked with stored grain.
> Local families repair the walls and are paid nothing.

**(42) CLEAR BEFORE CRAFTED.** One fact, one speaker. Cut any second clause that explains the first — that is the writer telling the reader what to make of it. Put the qualifier inside the sentence, never in a clause after it ("is their problem only till dawn").
**(42a) TWO SENTENCES ARE FINE; A SENTENCE THAT EXPLAINS THE ONE BEFORE IT IS NOT.** THE TEST: delete the second sentence. If the first is now missing a FACT, keep it. If the first already said everything and the second told the reader what to think, cut it.
**(42b) ONE CLAIM A READER CAN ACT ON.** "A rich man is tried first" has two readings and commits to neither, so it says nothing. "The court is slow" measures nothing. "The town argues about this" names no disagreement. **If two readings fit the sentence, it makes no claim** — pick the one the pool's own state supports and say it.
**(42c) PLAIN MODERN WORDS.** grain store not granary · sick house not infirmary · a travelling trader not a pedlar · a guild member not a factor · a guard not a watchman. The archaism licence of ruling 20 is WITHDRAWN.
**(42d) THE SPEAKER'S STAKE — ADDED 2026-09-14 BY THE CHAIR; THE RULING ARRIVED MID-RUN AFTER BATCH 1 HAD LANDED AND THIS BRIEF WAS NEVER AMENDED. An auditor found the omission by reading this file.** From the line ALONE a reader must be able to state the FACT and name what the SPEAKER stands to gain or lose. The stake is drawn from what the source ALREADY IS on its card — never an invented motive. Putting the speaker into his own sentence (a pronoun; his own party-word: the guilds' trades, the hall's purse, the stallholder's stall, the guard's round and wage, the priest's ground) is how it is done. Two lines in batch 1 were named as UNFIXABLE rather than forced, and that is the right answer when the only stake available would be a new claim.
⛔⛔ **AND THE FAILURE MODE 42d HAS, LEARNED THE HARD WAY IN BATCH 4: pulling the speaker into his own sentence tempts a HABITUAL PRESENT into a SINGLE PAST EPISODE** — "the town answers a question about the store with a question" becomes "his question was answered with a question". That invents history (floor 2), it moves the claim, and the wave gate catches it as a new `C3 · is this clause historical?`. **WHERE 42d AND THE FREEZE COLLIDE, THE FREEZE WINS:** a stake that can only be given by changing the tense, the actor or the scope is not given at all.
⛔ THE SAME FAMILY: do not narrow the SCOPE to buy a stake. "everybody here" is the town and "everybody at the table" is the tavern; "nobody here" is the town and "no drinker there" is the room. Both are moved claims.

**AND VARY THE SHAPE.** The owner's own note on the clear versions: four lines in seven were "a role says a fact". Clear writing pulls hard toward one frame. Use the others the roster already allows — someone FOUND something · someone DOES something · two people DISAGREE · nobody HAS DONE something · the bare fact with no speaker where the engine holds it (ruling 40).

## ⛔ THE CLAIM FREEZE — the rule that makes this cheap and safe
**A re-cut line must make the SAME CLAIM as the line it replaces.** Every face in this block is already refuted against its card; if the claim does not move, that refutation still stands and no floor-1 work is needed. Change the words, the shape, the length, the vocabulary, the voice — never the fact.
**THE ONE EXCEPTION IS 42b**, which by its nature sharpens a vague line into a definite claim. Those are NOT frozen and are NOT covered by the old refutation. **Collect every 42b line in a separate list as you go**, with the old text, the new text, and which of the two readings you chose and why the pool's state supports it. They get refuted afterwards; the rest do not.
**DO NOT delete a speaker. DO NOT drop an attribution.** A previous cure did and the instrument that rewarded it has been corrected.
