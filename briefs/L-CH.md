## The chair's rulings for this volume (by delegation; vetoable; execute unless R1/R3 refutes one — then STOP and say so)
1. **ENTRY ONLY THROUGH CUSTOM CONTENT, like deities.** The engine never creates one; the DM authors as many as the story wants (the number is the DM's); no successor (the fate area); death is never the engine's (a champion may fail, lose renown, be captured, be ransomed; dying is the DM's alone). The custom-content pipeline is the template (R1 §6): name the registries, generated files and walkers a new authored type costs, as CH-0's car.
2. **ONE PERSON, MARKED — no parallel NPC system.** The mark is a sub-key on the NPC record (R1 §2 names the admitting normalizer): `archetype` (the eight domains) · `polarity` · one BOLSTERED trait · goals · `bound` (an employer court or none) · `failure` (`fated · steady · fallible · brittle`) · authored relations · an authored ban list · habits (the curve at person grain, R1 §3, no second learning law) · a capped belief store · memory of places and people · moods. The TOKEN: `champion` is LANDED (`'champion-npc'`); take R1's recommendation for the module/ledger/flag token (provisional `paragon`), and register `champion`/`villain` as prose and polarity words with the homonym note.
3. **EIGHT DOMAINS × TWO POLARITIES** (the brief's table; the owner liked the warden and the merchant prince): the gift is ONE BOUNDED LEAN at ONE landed seam per domain (R3 §5 names each seam and its headroom); where the seam is dark (EC's market; the generals' battle lean; the statecraft chain) the domain's gift is recorded with its SLOT and the character still travels, chooses and relates. Villains are the counterforce the constitution requires (requirement 2) made into persons.
4. **PRESENCE IS A CHANCE, ACTION IS A CHOICE (the owner's message 3).** Each week the character decides whether to use its gift: a seeded draw over traits (charitable · mercenary · capricious · cowardly · patient…), its contract, what it believes is at stake, mood, ties, HABITS. Every choice keeps its top reasons for the dossier (legible on scrutiny; the surface says "she turned south").
5. **THE WEEKLY CHOICE as a closed set**: stay and work · go to a known place · take or leave work · meet a handler · rest · go home · chase a nemesis · visit the party (party brief §6: with a reason, or from curiosity alone — rare, cooling, never repeated once met). A commitment holds until something clearly better appears. Drift: traits shift slowly with experience (a bounded, seeded step; no second learning law).
6. **BOUND AND FREE (the owner: distinct but related).** BOUND = an office, always employed: moves on its own initiative inside the employer's lands (a town or a whole polity — PL's walk), outside only on the court's errand or with the court's army/column; leaving is resigning (becomes free; priced; a hearing). FREE = goes where goals lead; temporary contracts while goals and pay align ("until a better deal or reason comes along"); breaking one costs trust. EMPLOYMENT BOTH WAYS: free characters seek work where needed and values/pay fit; courts seek characters they have HEARD of (RN's fame) by sending a recruiter where they believe the character is (may have gone); offers are hearings; two courts may bid.
7. **THE LINE HOME (the owner's handlers).** A bound operative abroad (spymaster · deceiver · provocateur · agitator) meets a handler from home at a rendezvous on the edge of home territory: orders and the true state of home in, findings out; the meeting can be caught by the target's counter-intelligence (R3 §2). Envoys keep the line by dispatches, saints by their mother house.
8. **COURTS MAY HIRE VILLAINS, CLANDESTINELY (the owner's message 6).** The hire is a court SECRET; it can leak by rumor, an enemy spy's `confirm` product, a caught handler's meeting, or the captured villain's own nature; exposure is a SCANDAL: the employer's standing falls at home and abroad through ONE landed quantity (R3 §2 names it) and the court worked against gains a grievance; belief-decided, so a court can be accused before it is confirmed and a deceiver can fake the trail.
9. **RELATIONS (message 7)**: authored at formation and emergent from deeds; the ONE regard idiom (RN); two-sided; PROXIMITY enters the weekly choice with personality setting direction and strength; deities under the deity doctrine (the followers act — authored pantheon relations shape believers, the faith contest's odds, the champions of those gods; the emergent layer is the CREEDS' relation, never a god's mind); sparse, capped, decaying; legible with reasons.
10. **BANS (message 8)**: authored (custom content or Edit Mode; canon until lifted) and emergent (a court's own decider from what it believes; a hearing `…_banned`); kingdom-wide through the crown's hold (PL): a member's gate carries it as a priced input, a member that harbours pays in deference; a region = a named set of settlements until the map; emergent bans lapse (the half-life) or are lifted by pardon; the character's side: a barrier in its choices, personality decides (most go around; a defiant one enters in secret and may be taken); belief rules both sides.
11. **REFUSAL (message 5)**: entry refused at the gate for CIRCUMSTANCE or RENOWN, on what the court BELIEVES; `admitted · refused · admitted under watch`, a hearing reusing POP's gate idiom (R3 §4 says whether one function serves columns, champions and the party — rule accordingly).
12. **FAILURE**: `fated · steady · fallible · brittle` sets how often efforts fail and how far a failure falls (a setback · a stumble costing renown · a disgrace costing the employer's trust · capture); capture and ransom ride the landed hold and ransom roads (R3 §1; the owner's §125.5: a wanderer taken by bandits pays no ransom).
13. **THE EIGHTEEN PROPOSALS (§5)**: the owner asked for them ("What else should we add to deepen…"); rule each IN as a wave or recorded WITH A SLOT, by whether R1/R3 found a landed carrier: values + one line never crossed; needs as bands; the calling vs the personal goal; turning points incl. the fall and redemption as HEARINGS (polarity never flips silently; requirement 24); secrets the spy system uncovers; debts and favours (WC's cost-memory idiom); fellowships and rivalries (the regard idiom); faction entanglement through the lit faction competition only (the coup budget is full); their own picture of the world (they can be deceived); legends precede them (RN); ONE CALL BOARD — a derived, belief-read registry of CALLS keyed by domain from every system's needs (plague · menace · war · heresy · unrest · dear markets), the one seam every character weighs (and the party volume reads it for requests to the party); seasons (the clock law); a material life without mortality (purse band, upkeep, wounds, exhaustion); legacy marks; schemes as staged errands the spy system can detect; networks through the corruption web; named arcs in the Herald and dossier; big personal decisions as hearings.
14. **CONTESTS**: a champion and villain of one domain in one town contest it (a hearing); repeated contests make a nemesis arc.
15. **HEARINGS to register** (requirement 24; the default for each named): employment offer · the contest · ransom (the landed row) · a grave failure · a bound character resigning · at the gate · banned · a banished one found · a polarity turn · a great contract or a line crossed · the visit to the party (the party volume owns the party-side card; cite it).
