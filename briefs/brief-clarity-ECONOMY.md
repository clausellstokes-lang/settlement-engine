## THE CLAIM FREEZE, absolute
A re-cut spine makes the SAME CLAIM as the spine it replaces: same tense (habitual stays habitual), same
actor, same scope (the town stays the town), same quantity, same certainty. Words, shape, length and
vocabulary may change; the fact may not. The engine's move grammar is read from the words: run the sweep
after every pool and put back any CONSEQUENCE/ABSENCE the cut dropped (inside the sentence, not as the
explaining clause); never add PROVENANCE or HISTORY; never lose a LEVEL1 order id. 42b sharpenings (a vague
spine made definite) are the ONE exception — collect them in the commit body with old → new → the reading
and why the pool's own key supports it. NO SPEAKER DELETED where a stance carries one.
