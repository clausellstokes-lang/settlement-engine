## 3. THE FIDELITY LAW (what makes the pass meaning-preserving; the verifier's checklist)
(a) **The latest wins, nothing new.** For every amended passage, the content as of `1beabdc85` after the LATEST bracket is the text; the lane never resolves a conflict two brackets left open — it writes `⛔ STOP <V>-C-n` in its ledger and leaves the passage marked, the chair rules. (b) **THE FIDELITY LEDGER** `consolidate/<V>-ledger.md`: one row per bracket, `↳` row, round header and STOP marker in the OLD text (its id or its first 60 characters · the old section · what happened: FOLDED into <new section> / DROPPED as superseded by <id> / MOVED to history / KEPT as a design mark) — none missing (the lane counts the old text's brackets by script and the ledger's rows must equal that count). (c) **THE RATCHETS (scripts, run by the lane and re-run by the verifier):** the set of backticked tokens in the old text equals the set in the new text (a token that disappears must be a superseded name named in the ledger); the counts of laws, hearing kinds, flags, waves, persisted shapes, scenario rows and OD rulings are equal or every difference is explained in the ledger (a superseded ruling folded into its successor is one allowed difference); every `path :: symbol` cite survives; every ⭐ owner row survives; every DRAFT/UNMEASURED survives. (d) **Cross-references** resolve: every "see §x" in the new text points at a section that exists in the new text (the ledger carries the old→new section map); every reference to ANOTHER volume cites that volume's section by title, never by line. (e) **The voice:** complete sentences; the house vocabulary (fold §5); no mechanism names in a felt sentence; no numerals in a DM sentence; no dates in the running text (header and rulings table only); no "the chair", "the lane", "the skeptic", "round", "finding" in the running text — the design speaks in its own voice.
