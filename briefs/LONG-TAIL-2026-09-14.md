Of the 43, twenty-three are yours and nobody else's, and that is the honest headline: the largest blocker in this estate is not unbuilt code, it is unanswered questions. The second is that a great deal of the last two months of judgment was made on a model seat you asked to be re-checked later, and that re-check only starts when you call it — roughly a hundred and thirty sections whose authorship cannot now be recovered, plus a hundred and forty-seven unruled rows in the older queue.
