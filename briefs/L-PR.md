## THE OWNER'S SECOND WORD ON PATROLS (2026-10-18 ~06:1x EDT), verbatim: "and what about the interaction of patrols from two different allied or settlemetns of the same kingdom?"
12. **ALLIED AND KINGDOM PATROLS (answered in chat; vetoable).** (a) RECOGNITION IS BELIEF: patrols meeting at a shared border gate know each other as far as their towns' beliefs go (K3); allies and members pass without contest; stale belief (an alliance broken, a war begun this week, not yet heard) makes incidents between friends, and a provocateur (CH) can stage one with a false report. (b) SHARED EYES: allied patrols EXCHANGE REPORTS at the gate (the witness idiom, a hop sooner — a belief channel between allied towns); a dense kingdom net sees its whole land. (c) JURISDICTION = PS's ladder with a higher tolerance band for a patrol's size: an ally in hot pursuit is tolerated (urgency, receipted); a member under a firm+ hold crosses by the polity-wide passage charter; under a frayed hold members still ask; a cool ally's uninvited patrol is `tolerated` with a grumble or `refused_with_warning`. (d) GROUPING ACROSS TOWNS: against a believed threat bigger than either, an allied patrol may JOIN the other's detachment — a decided act (`patrol_joins`: `rides · will_see · refused`) by belief and regard (RN: warm rides, cold will see); each keeps its own command (two units on one leg, the mirror-the-leg idiom); credited separately (WC-1's contribution idiom → regard; a refusal remembered on the relationship record). (e) THE CROWN'S WARDENS have PRECEDENCE where the hold is firm+: they ride every member's roads, members' patrols defer on shared stretches, and the crown may ORDER members' patrols to the war's roads (a small summons: `patrol_order` answered `answered · refused` by the member's fork, feeding WC-1's credit); under a frayed hold the wardens keep to the crown's own land and members ignore them. (f) FRICTION: the coverage term CAPS PER STRETCH (overlap is waste); members in a FEUD (PL §6) treat each other's patrols as hostile on shared roads and a clash is the feud escalating (the liege's arbitration); a den on a shared border is cleared by whoever believes and acts first (ST item 10 for a site's claim), credit shared if grouped. (g) ESCORT HANDOVER at the gate: the next territory's patrol takes the caravan if present, else it proceeds unescorted; a kingdom's wardens escort end to end. Hearings: `patrol_joins` (CLIMAX), `patrol_order` (CLIMAX); incidents between friends ride PS's border kinds.

(b) SCREEN AND DELAY — a patrol or grouped detachment screens: harasses the march, holds a gate, a ford or a crossing (ST), delaying the invader's next leg by ONE bounded week (DRAFT, UNMEASURED) — the week the town needs to raise the levy, man the keep (ST item 17: patrols are the fort's road arm in week one) and call allies (WC-5); a real army brushes a patrol aside (`patrol_engages`: `screened · withdrew · destroyed`): the first casualties, a receipt and a deed ("Kell's riders fell at the ford"). (d) HOLD THE BORDER FORTS in week one. (e) ABSORBED — once the levy is up, patrols fold into the field force or the town's defence (the one manpower pool); roads behind the front go UNPATROLLED → brigandage rises in wartime, dens grow behind the lines (ST): a chooser that keeps patrols on its roads keeps its rear safe and its field army smaller — that trade-off is the decision (one bounded chooser signal via the shared-signal car).
