# Brief — SCRIBE W3c: THE STATIC TABLE'S READS RESOLVE ON A SETTLEMENT (chartered 2026-09-14 ~15:2x; not yet dispatched)

THE FINDING (W3b car 2, measured on the pinned town over thirteen tabs): 88 of the static table's 125 field paths can never resolve on a settlement, because the wiring census's `reads` column records the DESK'S LOCAL NAMES as the desk-read recipes spell them (`readings.scores`, `axis`, `conflict.intensity`, `link.npcConnections`, `!hasPatron`, `magicWorksAt({ settlement })`), and a settlement has no `readings` key. So the card prints UNREADABLE beside ~70 % of the corpus's fields, the model is told nothing where the engine decided plenty, and the Q arm's field-path licence (W3a car 3) has no field to license on those pools. It is the single largest lever left on the Scribe's coverage and on the second reader's FIELD test.

THE CURE, at the chokepoint: a RESOLUTION column beside `reads` — for every desk-local name, the settlement path (or the expression) it denotes on that desk, taken from the desk-read recipe that binds it (`readings` = the desk's `read(settlement)` result; `axis`/`link`/`conflict` = the local of the relation recipe …). Produce it from the recipes, not by hand: `scripts/scribe-static-card.mjs` gains a resolver that runs each desk's read recipe over a settlement and records, per local name, the settlement path it was read from (the recipes are pure and the estate's desk reads are already instrumented for the census's `producerIndexFiles` walk); where a local is a derived value with no single path, record the expression and mark it `derived` with its inputs. `townCard.js` then resolves `fields[]` through the column; `fieldState` gains `derived` (a value computed here from named inputs, printed as the value with its inputs).
GATE: the census `--check` byte-identity (a new column in the static card is the static card's; the census's own stamp must not move, or the move is explained by name); the card golden re-recorded with the moved `fields[]` named per tab; a pin: the count of `unreadable` rows on the pinned town falls from 42 to a number the car states and explains; the corpus five with the pin at 2756; then RUN 4 on the grid to measure the coverage gain.
OWNER-GATED INSIDE IT: nothing (a static-card and card change; no shipped surface moves).
