## The chair's rulings for this volume (by delegation; vetoable; execute unless R2 refutes one — then STOP and say so)
1. **FOUR FACTS, NEVER MERGED.** FAME (how widely a person is known), REGARD (how each actor feels about them), CREDIBILITY (whether their word is trusted — the landed `npcCredibility` for NPCs; a new instance for the party), LEGITIMACY (a ruler's right to rule — the landed seat reads; renown NEVER writes it; where renown should matter to it, it enters only through an existing input and never as a coup term — the coup budget is full).
2. **FAME = one band + a REACH per person, derived.** Fame derives from (a) OFFICE (a ruler, a general, a high priest is known by position — a closed table by role, reach by the seat's own reach) and (b) DEEDS: each deed record keeps its PEAK REACH, written while the tale lives on the rumor road (a monotone max over the arrival ledger, ≤ the rumor TTL) and then frozen — lived history stays immutable after the tale dies (THE PROMISE; declare this as the deed's own telling, part of the event). Fame fades over YEARS on the spine's banded-stock half-life (SP-A's idiom; NO second decay law — cite `bandedStock.js`'s API). Fame is BELIEF: a town knows a person only as far as word reached it — derived on read from the deed's reach and the town's distance/witness status, never stored per town. Cheap enough for every NPC: nothing is stored per NPC beyond office; deeds are the store.
3. **REGARD is SPARSE, event-born, capped, two-sided, banded, with a memory of reasons.** A link exists only when an authored seed (the DM's allies-and-enemies list; a champion's authored relations) or an EVENT creates it; each actor tracks a capped number (DRAFT 12, UNMEASURED); authored links decay toward their authored baseline, emergent ones toward neutral, on the half-life; repeated contact reinforces; every change keeps ONE dominant reason in the world's words (concern 9b); one deed moves one actor's regard ONCE per version heard (a correction REPLACES, never adds — concern 9c); no ratchets. The band vocabulary: choose from R2's census so it does not collide with the landed relationship labels (`hostile`, `rival`, `allied`…) and RECONCILE with ransom's `RANSOM_REGARD_BANDS` (`forgotten · valued · beloved` = a court's regard of its OWN person, the ransom road's instance): rule either that the ransom bands are a projection of the one regard band onto three words (one idiom, a mapping table), or that they are registered as a homonym with the reason — pick the first if the mapping is faithful.
4. **A COURT'S regard is DERIVED**: from its members' regards weighted by each member's power in the court (R2 §6: the governing faction's share, ladder rungs, the seat) plus the court's INSTITUTIONAL RECORD (what was done TO the court as a body: a scandal, a betrayal, a gift) — never a second stored court opinion. Each ACT comes from the OFFICE that owns it (summons: the ruler; arrest: the marshal; a quiet watch: the spymaster), driven by that member's own regard plus the court's stance, so on scrutiny the why is that officer's grudge.
5. **OPINION LEADERS**: a renowned person's regard spreads further than an unknown's (a bounded multiplier on the rumor's reach, never a cascade; DRAFT, UNMEASURED).
6. **INHERENT VALUE and the TWO LENSES (party brief §8)** live here as the one read every actor uses on any deed (the party's, a champion's, later any named actor's): derived from the deed's kind + target on the landed alignment axes (`settlementAlignment.js`: lawfulness · malice; the third coordinate aggressiveness, concern 9e); lens one = what the act is, read through the actor's alignment; lens two = whom it helped or hurt (allies/rivals); PERSONALITY sets the mix; a good/neutral rival grumbles, an evil one keeps the grudge; condemning a good act without reason costs the condemner's own people's regard (one bounded term, never legitimacy); ambiguous acts default NEUTRAL until the DM marks (concern 9f). The party-impact kinds' value table is drafted here (twelve rows) and the party volume cites it.
7. **CREDIBILITY** for the party: a new instance of `npcCredibility`'s idiom (the same band law), moved by kept and broken promises (party brief §6) and by exposed false reasons (§7); never merged with fame or regard.
8. **THE OWNER'S "applied by role"** is a table: general (feared by enemies, sought by courts), healer (called to plague towns), spy (easier to expose — `operativeNotoriety01` IS this model's operative instance: say so and reconcile its scale), priest (word carries in the faith contest), envoy (opens doors) — each a bounded lean at ONE landed seam (R3 §5's seams) or recorded with a slot if the seam is dark.
