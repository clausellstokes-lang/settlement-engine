## The chair's rulings for this volume (by delegation; vetoable; execute unless R2/R3 refutes one — then STOP and say so)
1. **THE PARTY IS THE WORLD'S ONE OUTSIDE ACTOR.** No deciders; its choices enter only as DEEDS the DM records through the LANDED party idiom (`partyImpact.js`, the twelve kinds, `cause: 'party'`, canon by construction, no roll, no proposal queue). The engine never moves, captures, jails or harms the party. The DM's VIEW puts the party first (the party lens); the simulation stays indifferent.
2. **THE DEED MARK, ONE QUICK PASS (on the spot).** On the night's event (a party impact or any event): the party's hand (contributed) · SANCTIONED BY (one pick from the authorities whose reach covers the place: a power in the town by its share, the seat by its legitimacy, a liege, the crown by its hold, a faith's clergy by its standing — the engine computes the reach; never the god) · MORAL WEIGHT (heroic ↔ villainous; the DM's override of the derived inherent value, RN) · PROOF (kept quiet · told · shown). The engine SUGGESTS the mark when the party is present at an event (presence toggle); witnesses are suggested from presence. No writs, missions or commissions anywhere. "Legitimized" is SANCTIONED (legitimacy keeps meaning a ruler's right to rule). ZERO-CLICK DEFAULTS (the chair's ruling of 19:4x on the marking burden, vetoable): the three marks ride the act the DM already records with DERIVED DEFAULTS — contributed = yes for a party impact; the sanctioner pre-selected when the party is present and holds a warm regard or an open accepted request with that authority, else none; moral weight = RN's derived inherent value; proof = told — one act changes any; after an advance the lens offers BATCHED, non-blocking suggestions ("the party was in Kell when the granary burned — did they contribute?"); the burden DEGRADES GRACEFULLY (no marks = a coherent world in which the party is unknown; nothing breaks); the one required mark is the answer to an accepted request (one act on its card). Make this a PT law.
3. **PRESENCE**: "the party is here" — a DM toggle that persists; PRESENCE FOLLOWS EVENTS (the position updates from events authored with the party's hand, concern 9d); "last heard of" is what actors search by (the rumor road). Witnesses are the actors present; everyone else hears by rumor.
4. **FAME, REGARD, CREDIBILITY** are RN's (cite); the allies-and-enemies list is AUTHORED regard (the DM's seeds); the NET EFFECT at any meeting combines the regards that bear on it by that place's politics (the governing faction, the crown's hold, the temple's sway) and may CONFLICT ("the town loves them; its lord does not"); followers hold regard on behalf of their deity (the followers act).
5. **ENGAGEMENTS — ALWAYS HEARINGS, ONLY ON THE WEEK (requirement 24).** Requests for aid (from actors who have heard and need what the party is known for — THE CALL BOARD, the derived registry CH defines: cite it), offers (employment, rewards, titles, bribes), invitations and summonses, hostile attempts (refusal at the gate · arrest · a bounty · an ambush · slander). ONE registry kind per family, each STANDING: every week the actor still presses it is a NEW hearing in its CURRENT state; the DEFAULT is always "nothing has happened to the party yet" and the RNG decides only the actor's own course; month+ advances run the default without stopping; nothing queues. The DM's answer OUTSIDE the story (let it happen · strike (no trace; quiets the actor) · amend) is never merged with the party's answer INSIDE it (yes · later · no, recorded as deeds; a "no" is a deed with consequences).
6. **HOLDS are the actor's STATES IN THE WORLD, never pending hearings**: NOT FOUND YET (searching where the party was last heard of; derived from distance and what the actor heard; the transit kernel is at its ceiling so no new traveller) · NOT YET ENGAGED (found; waiting for the moment) · YES, BUT LATER (a PROMISE OWED, in the lens; mints a hearing when due). No DM hold outlives its week. SILENCE IS NEVER A DECISION: with no answer the default is computed from the world; an unattended card closes at NO cost to the party.
7. **TWO CLOCKS, NEVER MERGED**: PERSISTENCE (how long an actor keeps looking/waiting, by personality, urgency, distance; when it runs out they give up at no cost to the party); the NEED'S CLOCK once the party said yes (deadline · threshold · both, derived from the need itself — the siege's course, the plague's spread — never authored separately); PATIENCE only where the need has none. REACHABILITY: the fastest journey by the one travel physics (R2 §7) from the presence toggle against the clock, said in the world's words on the card; FAULT judged against the fastest journey through the world AS IT UNFOLDED; a REACHABLE MISS is a broken promise and COUNTS BY DEFAULT (regard and credibility fall by each actor's values; the rumor spreads; the DM may strike or amend in its week); never reachable costs nothing; a "later" beyond the need's clock is no promise to lean on.
8. **THE WORLD NEVER WAITS**: needs resolve without the party ("Tolle found its cure elsewhere"); patience running out makes a NEW card (seek other help · escalate · give up · turn against), never a verdict on the party; ESCALATION EMERGES (summons → warrant → bounty → hunters not found yet on the north road), each a hearing in its week.
9. **SANCTION (§7)**: a REACH and a WEIGHT broadening with level (the owner's ladder); inside its reach and where known, it softens a villainous-looking deed and amplifies a good one; SHARED OWNERSHIP (credit flows to the sanctioner; the sanctioner's enemies inherit a grudge; a false reason exposed shares the blame); COUNTERFORCES: outside the reach · unheard · a rival's condemnation (counter-sanction: bounty, ban through the one ban system, arrest — hearings) · VOIDED by a higher authority over the same lands · REPUDIATED by a successor (hearings; travel as news); never expires on its own.
10. **MUTATION rides the LANDED weathering (`rumorNetwork.js`) and never a second engine**: the deed travels as a story with PARTS (act · size · reason · proof · sanction · doer) on the arrival record (R2 §2 says how); the per-hop roll weathers the parts in a FIXED ORDER (reason and sanction first; then size drifts, usually swelling; rarely the doer swaps to a real actor); DRIFT NEVER INVENTS A REASON (a lie needs a liar: IN's plant road with a traceable source); THE TRUTH CAN CHASE THE RUMOR at the same speed, losing by a later start, less retelling and the credibility test; inside the sanctioner's lands its word carries better (one bounded multiplier); proof shown makes the reason survive longer.
11. **INHERENT VALUE and the two lenses** are RN's (cite its table of the twelve kinds); the DM's heroic ↔ villainous mark overrides only when the night's context differs; ambiguous acts neutral.
12. **CHAMPIONS COME TO MEET THE PARTY** (owner): with a reason (employment · history · relationship · regard) or from curiosity (belief of what they heard + closeness + alignment + personality + curiosity; rare; cools; never repeated once met); a meeting is a light card with no stakes; once met, a regard link forms (the DM may set it). A renowned ordinary NPC may come too, more rarely.
13. **THE PARTY CAN BE BANNED like a champion** (one ban system — CH's; cite) and refused at the gate (the gate idiom; the hearing's default: nothing happened). ONE PARTY PER CAMPAIGN at this compile (a record keyed by party id holding one entry, so a second is an additive change later — recorded as the slot, never a second shape).
14. **THE PARTY LENS (the DM's window)**: the consequences of their deeds, who has heard and what they think (the net effect per place, in words), requests and threats aimed at them, promises owed, who is looking for them, places where they are known — ranked by nearness and weight; the immersion doctrine: words on the surface ("the Abbess grows impatient"; "in the river towns they say you burned an orphanage"), the story's path and who twisted it on scrutiny, bands and odds only on an intentional look. The DM's hand: set any actor's regard; direct any actor toward the party (R2 §8's doors).
15. **PERSISTED SHAPES** (declare each with lifecycle): `campaign.party` (or the home R2 recommends): presence (settlement id · since), the allies-and-enemies seeds, credibility, promises owed, open engagements with their holds and clocks, strikes (quieting), the party's name as the DM's label; the deed mark's fields on the party-impact record (sanctionedBy · moralWeight · proof · witnesses); the deed's rumor parts; persistence through save, reload and undo as world state (undoing an answer unwinds its promise and any broken-promise deed; a strike survives reload); the week's hearings derived and re-minted (requirement 22a).
16. **BOUNDED RESPONSIVENESS**: only actors who have heard AND care respond; cooldowns; caps on open engagements per actor and in total; seeded search, persistence, patience and moments (same seed, same world).
17. **HEARINGS to register** (each STANDING with the party default): `party_request` · `party_offer` · `party_summons` · `party_gate` · `party_arrest` · `party_bounty` · `party_ambush` · `party_slander` (the slander is a planted rumor — a CLIMAX `plant_taken` on IN's road, cite) · `party_promise_due` · `party_visit` (the champion's or NPC's meeting) — the lane runs `rg -w` on each and adjusts; the actor convention for the party as a non-settlement party to a hearing (R3 §8).
