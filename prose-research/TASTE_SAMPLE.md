# TASTE SAMPLE — forty real variants from the §900 corpus, reconstructed under R1–R12
Chair-authored (Fable 5.1, 2026-09-05). Every BEFORE is a real variant at 6e8692b36 (pool named). Every AFTER keeps the same slots, the same claims, the same band, the same angle; nothing is added to the world. The rule applied is named. Where the chair judged the original already right, it says KEEP and why. This is for the owner's eye; nothing is changed in the corpus.

1. DS-POP-1 :: nobody [ledger]
BEFORE: Nobody left {settlement} in {season}. That is a small fact and, in a year like this one, not a trivial one.
AFTER: Nobody left {settlement} in {season}. In a year like this one the rolls would have shown it.
RULE: R3 (the second sentence explained the first; now it carries a fact the clerk can point at).

## What the sample shows
Of forty, eleven are KEEP. The other twenty-nine change by the same three moves in almost every case: split the stapled sentence, cut the gloss that explains the fact, and keep the contrast only where the contrast is the band's own information. No slot moved, no claim moved, no sentence got longer, and the register is still the archivist's. The simplification the owner asked for falls out of the rules rather than being a separate pass.
