## PART A — THE LAWS EVERY RECONSTRUCTION OBEYS (from RECEIPT_POOLS_DOSSIER_STATE.md §0b–§0f and the estate's standing laws; mechanised where a script can hold them)
A1 THE REGISTER (§0b): present tense, a condition that STANDS; **the state first, the cause second where a cause exists — never the cause first with the state as its payoff** (this is the law v1's R1 contradicted); one flowing sentence or two short ones — never a paragraph, a cascade or a list (`check-pair.mjs`: THREE+ SENTENCES); woven — droppable mid-section without a seam (no typed action verb lead, no headline turn, no desk or dateline gesture); the town's own voice about the town.
A2 THE ANGLE IS THE STANDPOINT, NOT A FLAVOUR (§0b): [ledger] the books; [street] the town's talk; [visitor] what a stranger notices first; [elder] the memory frame — a length to it; [unfolding] the movement still running; [counterforce] what did NOT happen, plainly; [threshold] the edge and what would tip it, **in the conditional or subjunctive** — a bare future indicative is a FATE breach anywhere (`check-pair.mjs`: FUTURE INDICATIVE ADDED / SUBJUNCTIVE REMOVED). Two variants differing only in slot fills are ONE for the repetition envelope.
A3 SLOTS ARE A CONTRACT (§0c): the slot SET of a variant is fixed (`check-pair.mjs`: SLOTS differ); each slot's fill SHAPE (`proper` / `phrase` / `bare-common`) decides its casing and article — a rewrite may not move a slot from mid-sentence to sentence-initial if its shape is `phrase` (lowercase) or `bare-common` (the seam supplies the article), and may not put a `proper` fill after an article.
A4 BANDS ONLY (§0d): no digits; durations only through the six-band table (attributive / span / adverbial / predicate forms; "older than its bearers" PREDICATE-ONLY); counts only in QUANTITY_BANDS words or an authored magnitude word (a handful, a score, most, all but); proportions in words never percent. A rewrite may not add a duration or count word the BEFORE did not carry, nor change the count NOUN (souls → households moved the band) (`check-pair.mjs`: DURATION/TIME words ADDED; COUNT words ADDED/LOST). T5: a band-blind variant asserts no duration.
A5 AUDIENCE IS INHERITED (§0e): a `dm-only` mark carries to the AFTER unchanged, and a rewrite of a covert seam may not leak a hint into the player projection (`check-pair.mjs`: MARKS).
A6 R-DST-B, THE CENTRAL LAW: a standing configuration field licenses a STRUCTURAL clause (what the town is arranged like) and never a HISTORICAL one (what happened); only an event-provenance field licenses history; a prosperity rung, a food label, a posture word and a market band carry NO provenance and get plain description only. A rewrite may not introduce a cause, an actor, a capacity fact, or a spatial fact the field does not carry ("the town is arranged around it", "can house them", "reopens it" all failed this). NOT mechanisable — the refuter's job, per item, against the block's STATE-KEY.
A7 THE POOL IS A SEED INPUT: variants are rewritten IN PLACE — none added, removed or reordered (the draw is `hash % length` and index 0 is canonical-at-zero); the pool KEY is part of the draw and never changes; a sentence that must say something new goes in a NEW pool key by a chair act, never appended.
A8 THE CONTRAST TEST (replaces v1's R4, which was two tests under one name): a contrast ("rather than", "not X but Y", "X, not Y") is KEPT when the rejected alternative names a SIBLING POOL KEY or a sibling band of the same block (the corpus's own discriminating fact — "departure rather than decline" beside `CANDIDATE KIND: decline`); it may be CUT only when no sibling key or band names it (`check-pair.mjs`: R4 CUT a word that names a SIBLING pool key — the executed half; the band half is the refuter's).
A9 THE GLOSS TEST, GUARDED (replaces v1's R3): a second sentence or a ", which is / which means" tail is cut only AFTER A8 has cleared it — a "That is / This is" sentence that carries the pool key's discriminating claim is not a gloss. A gnomic closer (a general truth the family states: "a decree binds only the hall that made it") is a device the corpus uses on purpose and gets its own rule in Part B; it is never created where the BEFORE had none.
A10 THE RATION IS A NET TEST AT THE EDIT (replaces v1's R11): none of {rather than · which is/means · nobody/no one/nothing · its own · whatever · enough to · kind of/sort of · quiet(ly) · still/yet/already} may be ADDED by a rewrite; the rubric counts the antithesis SHAPE, not the phrase (`check-pair.mjs`: RATIONED WORD ADDED; ANTITHESIS SHAPE ADDED; the NOTE when "rather than" becomes a bare "not").
A11 POOL SPREAD IS MEASURED AT THE EDIT (replaces v1's R5): a rewrite may not make two variants of one pool share their first two words, nor make every variant of a pool the same sentence count where they differed (the pool's other variants are inputs to the check, not an afterthought).
A12 A RATIONED ADVERB IS CHECKED AGAINST ITS SIBLING POOL (from U8): before cutting "quiet(ly)", ask whether a sibling pool (a `dm-only` COVERT twin, a `mobilization` pool) exists that the adverb distinguishes the variant from; if so it is the band's word and stays.
A13 VOICE PILLAR 8 CUTS BOTH WAYS: "a vivid image, a fragment, a turn of phrase all stay" — the corpus's deliberate devices (the tautological figure "the hall's decisions are the hall's", the echo across a pair of variants, the civic act named — "suing for peace") are not tics; the refuter's §3 list is the catalogue of what v1 over-cut.
A14 THE SAMPLE IS VERIFIED BEFORE THE OWNER SEES IT: every before/after pair passes `check-pair.mjs` mechanically AND an adversarial refuter on A6/A8/A9 claim-preservation; the receipts state the counts they measured, never the author's memory.
A15 THE HERALD AND EVERY OTHER REGISTER GET THEIR OWN PART A (the news address law, the two-sentence read-aloud law, the kind-pool floors, the seeded-pick laws) after PROBE-ALL names them; the dossier-state laws above are NOT assumed to transfer.
