Interviews with Wolfe

  • Malcolm Edwards interviewed Wolfe in Vector #65, May-June 1973
  • Michael Banks interviewed Wolfe in the fanzine ERG Quarterly #59, July 1977
  • Gardner Dozois and others interviewed Wolfe in the fanzine Xenolith #1, January 1979
  • Michael Bishop interviewed Wolfe in the fanzine Thrust #16, Fall 1980
  • Joan Gordon interviewed Wolfe in the fanzine Science Fiction Review #39, Summer 1981
  • Melissa Mia Hall interviewed Wolfe in Amazing, September 1981
  • Nancy Kress and Calvin Rich interviewed Wolfe as part of The Writers Forum series at the College of Brockport, 29 June 1982
  • Darrell Schweitzer interviewed Wolfe in Fantasy Newsletter issue 49, July 1982
  • Richard Wolinsky, Lawrence Davidson, and Richard A. Lupoff interviewed Wolfe at BayCon, Thanksgiving weekend, 1982 (audio)
  • Robert Frazier interviewed Wolfe in Thrust #19, Winter/Spring 1983
  • Michael Dirda interviewed Wolfe in The Washington Post Book World January 30, 1983
  • Locus interviewed Wolfe in January, 1984
  • Judith Hanna and Joseph Nicholas interviewed Wolfe in Vector #118, February 1984
  • Colin Greenland interviewed Wolfe in Foundation #31, July 1984
  • Chris Merrick interviewed Wolfe in 1984 and published by the American Audio Prose Library
  • Larry McCaffery interviewed Wolfe, 5 June 1985
  • Luc Pomerleau interviewed Wolfe in Solaris #62, July 1985 (French)
  • Wolfe was interviewed on The Science Fiction Radio Show
  • Wolfe was interviewed for the cable TV show Illinois Reads in 1985
  • Elliott Swanson interviewed Wolfe for the magazine Interzone, #17, Autumn 1986
  • Robert T. Garcia interviewed Wolfe and others on the subject of violence in fantasy in American Fantasy, Fall 1986

  • David B. Silva interviewed Wolfe and others in Horrorstruck, November/December 1987

  • Robert Loerzel interviewed Wolfe in the Barrington Courier-Review, March 18, 1993
  • Brendan Baber interviewed Wolfe, 20 March 1994
  • Wolfe participated in an unmoderated online chat on scifi.com in 1996
  • Elizabeth Counihan interviewed Wolfe in Interzone, May 1997
  • Cloud Chamber Q&A with Wolfe, mediated by Michael Andre-Driussi and annotated by Dave Langford, September 1997
  • The Chicago Tribune Magazine published a profile and interview with Wolfe, Oct 5, 1997
  • Lawrence Person interviewed Wolfe in Nova Express, Fall/Winter 1998
