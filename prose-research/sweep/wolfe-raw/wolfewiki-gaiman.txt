 How to Read Gene Wolfe

by Neil Gaiman

First published in The World Horror Convention 2002 Program Book. Both Wolfe and Gaiman were Guests of Honor at this convention. 

Republished in the special Gene Wolfe issue of The Magazine of Fantasy and Science Fiction, April, 2007.

Available online at: http://www.sfsite.com/fsf/2007/gwng0704.htm

Collected in The View From the Cheap Seats: Selected Nonfiction by Neil Gaiman, 2017.
