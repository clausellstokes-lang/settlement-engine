 James B. Jordan 1992 Interview 

James B. Jordan interviewed Gene Wolfe on October 30, 1992 during the World Fantasy Convention. Topics include religion, influence, politics, science, being a novelist, The Book of the New Sun, women, Peace, awards, The Soldier books, and Castleview.

Published on Lupine Nuncio, available at https://web.archive.org/web/20140725102806/http://mysite.verizon.net/~vze2tmhh/wolfejbj.html

2007 revised version published in Shadows of the New Sun: Wolfe on Writing / Writers on Wolfe, edited by Peter Wright
