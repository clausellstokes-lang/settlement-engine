## Seggri frame harvest (verbatim, <=12 word quotes marked)
- "The first recorded contact with Seggri was in year 242" — past tense, institutional calendar, no agent.
- "There is no record of further contact with Seggri until" — present-tense negative existence; the archive names its own gap.
- "We have only her report, from which three excerpts follow." — first-person-plural editorial we; states the limit of the holding.
- "disclosed the fact, but not the circumstances" — a death recorded as withheld.
- "Her recommendation was to observe and avoid." — nominalised institutional act, two bare verbs.
- "A limit of two hundred visitors from off world was set." — agentless passive; number spelled out.
- "the gender balance now stands at about 12:1" — present tense, hedged ratio.
- "As of the date of this report the Seggri have not" — record dates itself against itself.
- "The following is a memoir given to Ambassador Eritho te Ves" — provenance line: what, to whom, when.
- "They made Resehavanar's Choice as "all the truth slowly."" — named institutional policy, unglossed.
- "Small value was placed on preserving a "true" text" — passive generalisation about a culture's literature.
- "my record is herewith enclosed" — bureaucratic idiom closing a personal memoir.
- Captain's log = per-speaker register: "They are ordinary drudges, living in towns which belong"
