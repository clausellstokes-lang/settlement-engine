'''''All the Seas of the World''''' is a [[historical fantasy]] novel by Canadian writer [[Guy Gavriel Kay]] published May 17, 2022 by [[Viking Press]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=All the Seas of the World |url=https://brightweavings.com/books/books/all-the-seas-of-the-world/ |access-date=2025-10-20 |website=brightweavings.com}}</ref> It is inspired by Renaissance Italy, the Mediterranean, and North Africa.  

It is a pseudo-sequel, occurring five years after the events in ''[[A Brightness Long Ago]]'', but readable as a stand-alone novel.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=2025-10-20 |last=Capossere |first=Bill |title=All the Seas of the World: A master working at the top of his craft |website=Fantasy Literature |url=https://fantasyliterature.com/reviews/all-the-seas-of-the-world/ |access-date=2025-10-20 |language=en-US}}</ref> ''All the Seas of the World'' uses events based on the [[Barbary Coast]] [[Barbary slave trade|slave trade]] and the [[expulsion of Jews from Spain]] to explore the themes of power, vengeance, trauma, recovery, and belonging through the two protagonists: Rafel ben Natan and Nadia bint Dhiyan (Lenia Serrano).<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Fram |first=Noah |date=2022-05-17 |title=Review: 'All the Seas of the World' by Guy Gavriel Kay |url=https://www.bookpage.com/reviews/all-the-seas-of-the-world-review/ |access-date=2025-10-20 |website=BookPage |language=en-US}}</ref>

The [[dust jacket]] cover art of the Canadian and US editions was created by Lisa Jager.<ref>{{Cite web |title=All the Seas of the World Art |url=https://brightweavings.com/index-4/all-the-seas-of-the-world/ |access-date=2025-10-20 |website=brightweavings.com}}</ref>

== Plot ==
This novel takes place in the same world as ''[[The Lions of Al-Rassan]],'' ''[[The Sarantine Mosaic]]'', ''[[The Last Light of the Sun]],'' ''[[Children of Earth and Sky]]'', ''[[A Brightness Long Ago]]'', and ''[[Written on the Dark]]''. As in the other novels, the three main religions of the Jaddites, Kindath and Asharites presented based on Christianity, Judaism, and Islam, respectively.<ref name=":1" /> This story take place five years after the fall of Sarantium.<ref name=":0" />

The story centers on Rafel ben Natan, a Kindath merchant, and occasional corsair, and his partner, Nadia bint Dhiyan, later Lenia Serrano, a former slave escaped from Osmanli captivity.<ref name=":0" /> Rafel and Nadia are contracted by Zariq and Ziyar ibn Tihon, khalifs of neighbouring Tarouz, based on historical brothers [[Hayreddin Barbarossa]] and [[Aruj Barbarossa]] to undertake the assassination the Khalif of Abeneven, Kay's parallel to Algiers.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |last=Wiersema |first=Robert J. |date=2025-05-01 |title='We must imagine them as having people who loved them' Guy Gavriel Kay's new book reminds us to take a moment for — even fictional — lives lived |url=https://www.thestar.com/entertainment/books/we-must-imagine-them-as-having-people-who-loved-them-guy-gavriel-kay-s-new/article_eb694401-755d-580f-bc87-004a5236b57c.html |access-date=2025-10-20 |website=Toronto Star |language=en}}</ref>

While the assassination does not go according to plan, Rafel and Nadia, find themselves wealthy as a result of the contract and no longer on the periphery of events, but rather at the centre of them.<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |date=2022-06-25 |last=Wolfe |first=Gary K. |title=Gary K. Wolfe Reviews All the Seas of the World by Guy Gavriel Kay |url=https://locusmag.com/review/gary-k-wolfe-reviews-all-the-seas-of-the-world-by-guy-gavriel-kay/ |access-date=2025-10-20 |website=Locus Online |language=en-US}}</ref> Rafel and Nadia eventually join forces with Folco Cino d'Acorsi, one of the central figures of ''A Brightness Long Ago'', and undertake to finance the construction a warship.<ref name=":3" />

Rafel and Nadia's journey eventually brings them into contact with Guidanio Cerra, another central character from ''A Brightness Long Ago''.<ref name=":3" />

== Reception ==
Robert J. Wiersema, writing for the ''[[Toronto Star]]'', describes the books as "a thrilling and exciting reading experience, and one which will more than satisfy fans of Kay's fiction."<ref name=":2" /> Wiersema notes that this a book informed by loss; the book is dedicated to Kay's mother, and ''All the Seas of the World'' "has an elegiac quality, an aching awareness of the temporary nature of lives, empires, worlds".<ref name=":2" /> 

Wiersema acknowledges that "it's not quite the third book in a trilogy, it's much closer to that than it is to a stand-alone" and reading this book on its own "would rob the reader of the complexity of Kay's vision and the interrelatedness of the world he has created."<ref name=":2" />

Writing for ''[[Locus (magazine)|Locus]]'', Gary K. Wolfe stated that "the catalog of these characters offered at the beginning of the novel at first might seem intimidating... the on-the-ground tale is a model of clarity and focus" and that ''All the Seas of the World'' "is as rich a tapestry as we've come to expect from Kay – perhaps one of the richest."<ref name=":3" />

Noah Fram, writing for ''Bookpage'', described Kay as "a master of telling small stories in a big world".<ref name=":1" />

Bill Capossere described Kay as "A master working at the top of his craft" and that ''All the Seas of the World'' was "meticulously crafted in structure, pace, language, dialogue."<ref name=":0" /> Capossere stated that this book embodied the hallmark "joy mingled with sorrow", for which Kay is so well known: "the joy of first anticipating and then experiencing it. The sorrow of turning that last page."<ref name=":0" />

Capossere praised Kay's ability to craft strong female characters which remained grounded, as there was no "hand-waving away of the true circumstances of a woman's life in this time in these places."<ref name=":0" />

== Awards ==
''All the Seas of the World'' was nominated for the ''2023'' [[List of winners and nominees for the Aurora Award for Best Novel|Aurora Award for Best Novel]], finishing second.<ref>{{Cite web |title=2023 Aurora Awards |website=The Canadian Science Fiction and Fantasy Association |url=https://www.csffa.ca/aurora-archives-home/2023-aurora-awards/ |access-date=2025-10-21 |language=en-CA}}</ref>
