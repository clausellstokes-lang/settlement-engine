'''''Written on the Dark''''' is a [[historical fantasy]] novel<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |date=2025-05-21 |title=Written on the Dark by Guy Gavriel Kay: Review by Gary K. Wolfe |url=https://locusmag.com/review/written-on-the-dark-by-guy-gavriel-kay-review-by-gary-k-wolfe/ |access-date=2025-10-17 |website=Locus Online |language=en-US}}</ref> by Canadian writer [[Guy Gavriel Kay]] published May 27, 2025 by [[Viking Press]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Written on the Dark – BrightWeavings |url=https://brightweavings.com/books/books/written-on-the-dark/ |access-date=2025-10-17 |website=brightweavings.com}}</ref> It is inspired by 15th century France and the [[Hundred Years' War]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=2025-10-13 |title=Written on the Dark: Feels like Kay's most elegiac work {{!}} Fantasy Literature: Fantasy and Science Fiction Book and Audiobook Reviews |url=https://fantasyliterature.com/reviews/written-on-the-dark/ |access-date=2025-10-17 |language=en-US}}</ref> Specifically, it depicts versions of the figures [[Charles VI of France|Charles VI]], [[Joan of Arc]], and [[Henry V of England|Henry V]]'s campaign in France culminating in the [[Battle of Agincourt]], while the story focuses on the life and deeds of a tavern poet, inspired by [[François Villon]].<ref name=":0" /> The book uses major historical events as a backdrop to convey the themes of power and justice, and of women finding a space for themselves and their desires in the world.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-05-28 |title=The Big Idea: Guy Gavriel Kay |url=https://whatever.scalzi.com/2025/05/28/the-big-idea-guy-gavriel-kay-4/ |access-date=2025-10-17 |website=Whatever |language=en-US}}</ref> It is set in the same [[fantasy world]] as his recent novels, but in Ferrieres, Kay's analogue of France.<ref name=":0" />

The [[dust jacket]] cover art of the Canadian and US editions was created by Lisa Jager.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Written on the Dark Art – BrightWeavings |url=https://brightweavings.com/written-on-the-dark-art/ |access-date=2025-10-17 |website=brightweavings.com}}</ref>

== Plot ==
This novel takes place in the same world as ''[[The Lions of Al-Rassan]],'' ''[[The Sarantine Mosaic]], [[The Last Light of the Sun]],'' ''[[Children of Earth and Sky]]'', ''[[A Brightness Long Ago]]'', and [[All the Seas of the World]]. As in the other novels, the three main religions of the Jaddites, Kindath and Asharites are presented based on Christianity, Judaism, and Islam, respectively. Events take place before the cataclysmic fall of Sarantium.<ref name=":0" />

The story is set in Orane, the capital of Ferrieres, and opens with the character Thierry Villar heading out into a cold winter night to burgle a sanctuary in order to pay off a gambling debt.<ref name=":0" /> Villar, after being intercepted by the Provost of Orane, is compelled to abandon his planned heist and assist with the investigation into the brutal murder of Rollin Duke de Montereau (inspired by [[Louis I, Duke of Orléans|Louis I]]), brother to the mad king.<ref name=":1" /> Thierry is chosen because of his ability to converse with the ordinary folk of the city and his reputation in the taverns of Orane.<ref name=":2">{{Cite news |date=2025-06-06 |title=Review: Guy Gavriel Kay's Written on the Dark weaves together murder mystery, war epic and more |url=https://www.theglobeandmail.com/arts/books/reviews/article-guy-gavriel-kay-novel-written-on-the-dark/ |access-date=2025-10-17 |work=The Globe and Mail |language=en-CA}}</ref>

When it becomes apparent the murder was orchestrated by the king's cousin Laurent, Duke de Barratin (inspired by [[John the Fearless]]), Ferrieres finds itself on the brink of civil war, while King Hardan of Angland launches a cross channel invasion to reignite the generations-old conflict between the two kingdoms.<ref name=":1" /> When Thierry's investigation brings him into contact with renowned court poet, Marina di Seressa, whom he befriends and introduces him to court,<ref name=":0" /> Thierry finds himself forced to navigate the royal court of a kingdom on the verge of both civil war and invasion, while still pursuing his craft and seeking to protect those he cares for.

In the Epilogue, Medor Colle fights beside the last Emperor of Sarantium as well as a soldier and a cleric from Batiara. These are Trussio Monticola and Nardo Sarzerola from [[A Brightness Long Ago]]. Later in the Epilogue, Thierry visits a sanctuary in Varena to view the mosaics of the courts of Valerius II and Valerius III, which were crafted by Crispin of [[The Sarantine Mosaic]].

== Reception ==
In a [[Book review|review]] for ''[[The Globe and Mail]]'', David Moscrop praised ''Written on the Dark'' for its "accessibility and its density".<ref name=":2" />

Bill Capossere described ''Written on the Dark'' as "classic Kay in its elegant warmth, masterful use of POV, careful twisting of history, and admixture of the sweet and sorrowful" and a must read.<ref name=":0" /> Rob Rhodes noted that despite being about half the length as many of Kay's other works, the result was no less memorable.<ref name=":0" /> Kay was also praised for handling of prominent LGBTQ+ characters, stating that it is "not that their identity and sexuality solely define them or their deeds. They're fully realized characters — people — like those who occupy our world."<ref name=":0" />

Gary K. Wolfe, writing for [[Locus (magazine)|Locus]], stated that in ''Written on the Dark'', Kay has created some of his "most memorable characters."<ref name=":1" />

Judith Starkston, writing for [[Historical Novel Society]], praised Kay's "graceful language" and described ''Written on the Dark'' as a "masterful contribution to Kay's celebrated fictional world".<ref>{{Cite web |title=Written on the Dark |url=https://historicalnovelsociety.org/reviews/written-on-the-dark/ |access-date=2025-10-17 |website=Historical Novel Society}}</ref>

The novel was nominated for the 2026 [[Aurora Award for Best Novel]].<ref name=aurora2026>{{cite web |url=https://locusmag.com/2026/08/2026-aurora-awards-winners/ |title=2026 Aurora Awards Winners |publisher=[[Locus (magazine)|Locus]] |date=10 Aug 2026 |accessdate=10 Aug 2026}}</ref>
