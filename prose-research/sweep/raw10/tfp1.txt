Title: "The Lighthouse of the Lizardmen"

Module Code: T9

Intro:

The peaceful village of Seaport has long relied on the beacon of the lighthouse to guide ships safely through the treacherous rocks and reefs that line the coast. But recently, the light has been flickering and going out, and strange noises have been heard emanating from the lighthouse at night. The villagers are afraid to investigate, but rumors have reached the ears of the local lord, who is offering a generous reward for anyone brave enough to solve the mystery and restore the beacon.

Overview:

This module is designed for a group of 4-6 player characters of level 3-5. It can be played as a standalone adventure or incorporated into a larger campaign. The adventure takes place in and around the lighthouse, as well as in the nearby village of Seaport and the surrounding wilderness.

The players will need to explore the lighthouse and the surrounding area, gather clues, and interact with non-player characters (NPCs) to unravel the mystery of the flickering beacon. They will encounter traps, puzzles, and challenges along the way, and will ultimately confront the source of the problem: a tribe of lizardmen who have taken up residence in the lighthouse and are using its beacon to signal to their kin across the sea.

Background:

The lighthouse of Seaport has stood for centuries, guiding ships safely through the rocky coastal waters. It is a vital part of the village's economy, as the port is a major hub for trade and fishing. But recently, the beacon has been flickering and going out, and strange noises have been heard coming from the lighthouse at night. The villagers are too afraid to investigate, and have turned to the lord of the region for help.

The lord has offered a reward for anyone who can solve the mystery of the lighthouse and restore the beacon. He has also provided the players with a map of the area and some basic information about the village and the lighthouse.

Adventure Hooks:

There are several ways that the players could become involved in the adventure:

 The lord of the region has issued a call for adventurers to solve the mystery of the lighthouse and restore the beacon. The players may hear of the reward and decide to take on the task.

 The players may be passing through the village of Seaport and be drawn in by the strange goings-on at the lighthouse. They may decide to investigate for their own curiosity or to help the villagers.

 The players may have a personal connection to the village or the lighthouse, such as having friends or family in Seaport or having sailed through the area before. They may decide to help out for sentimental reasons.

 Adventure Outline:

 The players arrive in the village of Seaport and meet with the lord, who briefs them on the situation and provides them with a map and some basic information.

 The players explore the village and gather information from the NPCs about the lighthouse and the strange occurrences there.

 The players set out to the lighthouse, which is located on a rocky island a short distance from the village. They may need to contend with obstacles such as rough seas or hostile wildlife on the way.

 Once at the lighthouse, the players explore the interior and gather clues about what is causing the beacon
