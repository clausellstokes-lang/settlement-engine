This blog?

This blog ran from 2007 to 2013. I maintained it as a place for me to keep note of what I'd been reading, watching or hearing. Now I have rolled it up and put it away: the content has not been deleted, but access to it is no longer public. There is, however, a newer blog over at Sibilant Fricative. And more important, the best pieces here have been collected in a book. Go and check that out.
