 Cloud Chamber is another bloody Langford
 fanzine – an occasional slim outbreak formerly contributed to
 a variety of APAs (amateur press associations). For many years,
 since issue 40 in 1993, it appeared in Maureen Kincaid Speller's
 Acnestis – known to some as Acne – but this died
 in 2005. Now CC tends to be an occasional round-up of
 miscellanea that didn't fit into Ansible ,
 plus reviews of recent reading. #163 should be regarded as a
 shameful aberration, and a long gap followed....
