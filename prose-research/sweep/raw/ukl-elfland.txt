 From Elfland to Poughkeepsie
 State Library of Oregon
 University of Oregon Library
 This sewn-bound, paperback volume presents the text of a speech Ursula gave at the Second Annual Science Fiction Writers' Workshop in 1972 on the language and style of fantasy writing, with an introduction by Vonda McIntyre. It was the first book published by Pendragon Press (Portland), who in 1973 published it in a limited run of 776 copies. 
 “From Elfland to Poughkeepsie” was subsequently reprinted in a December 1975 edition of 1006 copies, and an April 1978 edition of 2000 copies. 
 The speech was also included in The Language of the Night: Essays on Fantasy and Science Fiction .
