 Editorial and Technical Review of "Readers Prefer Outputs of AI Trained on Copyrighted Books over Expert Human Writers" 
 Authors/Creators 
 Roy, Zeus Dutta
 (Researcher) 
 Description 
 This document presents an independent editorial and technical assessment of a publicly available preprint examining AI-generated writing in comparison with human authors. The review evaluates the manuscript’s methodological design, statistical robustness, and conceptual positioning, with particular attention to claims related to market substitution and copyright implications. The report identifies key strengths alongside critical limitations affecting interpretability and generalizability. This work is intended to contribute to ongoing discussions in AI ethics, research integrity, and scholarly publishing.
