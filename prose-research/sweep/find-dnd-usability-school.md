## The split inside the school (for the synthesis)
- CUT-THE-PROSE wing: Bryce Lynch (read-aloud should never be long), Dyson Logos (get rid of it; point-form list), Courtney Campbell (only the bolded words; "Any word that is not important, is not used"), Noonan/Decker (you get two sentences).
- PROSE-CARRIES-VOICE wing: Melan (style is not replaceable by layout; ultra-minimalism strips flavour), Brendan/Necropraxis (Campbell's outline "lacks poetry" — he bolds inside running prose instead), Tad Davis (a via media; won't eliminate boxed text), Luke Gearing (actively DE-bullet-pointed his own adventure back into prose), Hartlage (longer descriptions spare the DM the obvious first idea).
- Both wings agree on: sequencing before format; the first sentence must be what is perceived on entry; history/background belongs in a marked, brief, separable tag; the failure mode is padding to fill a format.
