## Sharpest counter-case atoms found
- Auerbach (Waggish 2007): the withholding defeats inference because Severian's INTENT is unknown - "we can't derive from the whole what the meaning of any particular piece is"; "indicates a serious failure on the part of Wolfe"; Priest is the contrast who anchors unreliable narrators in a determinate context.
- Allbery (2004): "he doesn't signal to the reader when something important has just happened"; "The facts are mostly undistorted, but Severian's interpretation is often worthless."
- Russ (F&SF 1971, on Operation Ares): names the oblique technique - "big events happen offstage" - and still calls the novel "a failure, shadowy and inconclusive".
- Keller (Innsmouth 2009): the only fully hostile prose-level review found - "cryptic, verbose", "long-winded sentences", "dense descriptions and slowing adjectives".
- COUNTER-COUNTER (worth as much): Ewing, Tachy, Speculiction and Jesse all deny the density charge at SENTENCE level - "quite cut back and simplified, almost Hemmingwayesque", "The prose itself is elegant but simple", "he generally only uses exotic nouns", "things which are incidental rather than pivotal". The ornament sits in the NOUN slot; the sentences are plain. That is the single most program-relevant finding of this angle.
