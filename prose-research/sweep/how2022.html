George RR Martin in Conversation: HOW Interviews GRRM! (2022)

November 21, 2024 | By History of Westeros

On August 18, 2022, we interviewed George RR Martin in person, at his office in Santa Fe. You can watch this episode on YouTube here, or listen to it in podcast form. We have also included a transcription at our website, linked below, for greater accessibility.

Episode Summary

Ever wondered what goes on behind the scenes of Westeros? In this special episode, we sat down with the one and only George R.R. Martin himself in Santa Fe! Join Ash and Aziz as they delve into an unforgettable conversation with the master of fantasy, exploring untold stories, fascinating insights into his creative process, and even some tantalizing hints about the future of A Song of Ice and Fire. This isn’t your average interview—it’s a journey into the mind of a legend. Available to watch in high-definition video on YouTube or listen to as a podcast, and fully transcribed for your convenience, this episode is a must-listen (or must-watch!) for any serious fan. So grab your favorite drink, settle in, and prepare to be transported to the heart of Westeros… and beyond!
