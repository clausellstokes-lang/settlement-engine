 D&D's Chris Perkins Talks About His Stories, Legacy and Anxiety

 Tech

 This article is more than 3 years old. 
 Share to Facebook 
 Share to Twitter

 Chris Perkins sits down with the glassy eyed look of a man working on 5 projects at once. He is clearly a man with a lot on his mind and has agreed to not only skip lunch but also leave his own head for an hour to talk to me about Dungeons & Dragons. Chris started off writing D&D adventures for Dungeon Magazine in 1988 and now sits as the games' Lead Story Designer. His schedule these days is nothing less than intense.
 Being interrupted while immersing yourself in the creative process can make anyone a little grumpy, or disconnected. Chris is neither of these things. From Dungeon Magazine to now, Chris has been creating content for D&D and with that comes an enormous amount of knowledge, the kind that makes you want to cast 'Detect Thoughts' and get him to spill all of his secrets.
 He doesn't do that though, but he does offer up insight into being a good Dungeon Master, getting the most out of your game sessions and about anxiety. Chris is an introvert and despite this he is thrust into situations that are typically a nightmare for people who like to keep to themselves. Chris has to share his time and personality, not only with me in interviews for Dungeon Life but to the world on a weekly basis on Dice, Camera, Action and on stage in front of live audience at Penny Arcade's Acquisitions Incorporated live shows. The anxiety not only keeps him sharp, but remarkably genuine. Chris is an easy guy to talk to and very quick to laugh. In these videos we talk about his legacy, fear, tips on making a great D&D game, and how much he loves his Beholder.
