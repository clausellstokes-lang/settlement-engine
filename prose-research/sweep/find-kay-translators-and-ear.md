## Live notes
- Retranslation fact chain (for the synthesis): Kay's French texts exist in TWO states — the Québec Alire translations by Élisabeth Vonarburg, and the later L'Atalante translations by Mikaël Cabon, several of which are RE-translations of books Vonarburg had already done (A Brightness Long Ago = "Un éclat d'antan" Vonarburg/Alire 2020, re-done as "Comme un diamant dans ma mémoire" Cabon/Atalante; Under Heaven = "Sous le ciel" Vonarburg, re-done as "Les Chevaux célestes" Cabon). Readers on Elbakin discovered the doubling only by reading, and no comparison of the two sentences was produced in-thread.
- Fréchette 2013 records one concrete translator decision and its register cost: Vonarburg rendered "Guinevere" as the modern French "Geneviève" rather than the older "Guenièvre"; Fréchette says the choice diminishes the legendary-name effect and she reads "Guenièvre" anyway.
