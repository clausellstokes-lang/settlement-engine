The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien
Letter 179
RecipientHugh Brogan
Date14 December 1955
Subject(s)Criticism

Letter 179 is a letter written by J.R.R. Tolkien and published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien.

Summary

Brogan had previously sent Tolkien a letter containing criticism that, in hindsight, he thought was stupid and tactless. Tolkien replied to him that he welcomed the criticism, and invited Brogan to write more, seeing as he pays such close attention to the books.
