 The Left Hand of Darkness Interviews 

 In 1997, Ursula was interviewed by Dr. Karen Mann at Western Illinois University. We're sharing a few clips from that interview, which covered topics ranging from the rhythm of writing to the role of gender and climate in The Left Hand of Darkness . Our thanks and acknowledgment to Dr. Mann, WIU, Arwen Curry, and Todd Barton.

 Episode 1: Here, Ursula talks about how "the idea of the truly independent man is hogwash."

 Episode 2: Here, Ursula talks about the role of mythic stories in her novel and in the traditions of societies.

 Episode 3: Here, Ursula talks about finding rhythm in the writing process.

 Episode 4: Here, Ursula talks about her thinking about gender when she was writing the novel.
