 Have you discussed opening up use of they/their as singular pronouns in the D&D style guide?
 January 4, 2019 Zoltar 
 have you discussed opening up use of they/their as singular pronouns in the #dnd style guide? I read a great article by @JohnHMcWhorter that has me trying to use them more: The current version of the D&D style guide permits the use of they/them as a singular pronoun, as long as the writer is careful about avoiding the common pitfalls ("Are you referring to this one person or this other group of people you also mentioned?").
 — Jeremy Crawford (@JeremyECrawford) December 21, 2018
