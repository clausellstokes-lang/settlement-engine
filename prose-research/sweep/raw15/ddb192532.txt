 Here is a list of the currently updated documents for D&D Adventurers League, as well as a list of important links. This list will be updated periodically - you can check back here, look for posts on D&D Beyond, or visit the Official D&D Discord - Adventurers League channels to keep up with the latest news.  Adventurers League Player's Guide If you're a player, this is really all you need to get started! Contains character creation rules, as well as rules for playing in the Forgotten Realms, Eberron, Dragonlance, Ravenloft, Planescape, and Critical Role campaigns! Version 2026.4. Effective July 2, 2026. 
 Adventurers League Dungeon Master's Guide If you're going to Dungeon Master games, this one's for you. Tips & tricks on running Adventurers League games and rules for awarding magic items, etc. await! Version 2026.2. Effective November 25, 2025. Adventurers League Adaptation Guide For Dungeon Masters looking to run any official D&D release, from  Tyranny of Dragons  through the most recent adventures - this contains guidance on how to ensure that your games are compatible with Adventurers League! Version 2026.2. Effective November 25, 2025. 
 Adventurers League Wild Beyond the Witchlight Pack A special pack for DMs, containing adaptation materials for Wild Beyond the Witchlight as well as a prequel adventure, "Lost Things!"  Effective February 7 through May 31, 2024. 
 Adventurers League Service Rewards  ( Form Fillable Version ) Get rewards for your Adventurers League characters when you Dungeon Master or help out the Adventurers League community!  Effective August 10, 2026. Adventurers League Dungeoncraft Creator Pack Everything you need to create your own adventures for D&D Adventurers League for publication on the DMsGuild is included here! Effective December 4, 2023. 
 Upcoming Documents Adventurers League Organizer Guide - coming soon! D&D Session Log -  coming soon! Links Adventurers League Article Hub on D&D Beyond - all the articles published on D&D Beyond for Adventurers League! Adventurers League DMsGuild Hub - browse the library of hundreds of Adventurers League adventures! Adventurers League on the Official D&D Discord - news and discussion on the official D&D Discord server! Adventurers League on X (formerly Twitter) - see what's happening with Adventurers League on social media! 
 Notes: updated AL service rewards and update date in title
