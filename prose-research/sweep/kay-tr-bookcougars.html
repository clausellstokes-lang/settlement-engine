Episode One Hundred Fifty Seven Show Notes
KEY: CW = Chris Wolak and EF = Emily Fine

Emily starts with a reading of the poem “Aubade with a Chance of Rain” by Danusha Lameris from her collection Bonfire Opera: Poems.

If you would like to make a charitable gift in Jenny Colvin’s memory, these two organizations have been suggested by those who knew her well: Greenville Animal Care promoting the compassionate treatment of animals was something that Jenny cared deeply for. Friends of the Furman Libraries is planning to make Jenny’s long-held dream of establishing a “mindfulness space” in the Duke Library a reality. Under comments please write “in memory of Jenny”.

– Biblio Adventures – [24:20]
Chris and Emily went on a joint couch biblio adventure via the Mark Twain House with Shelley Puhak, the author of The Dark Queens: The Bloody Rivalry That Forged the Medieval World. Watch the video here.

Chris and Emily went to Rhode Island, visited the Cross Mills Library on the way to a bookseller event with Soho Press at Sly Fox Den Too with authors Ramona Emerson, author of Shutter, and Marcie Rendon, author of the Cash Blackbear Series including Murder on the Red River, Girl Gone Missing, and Sinister Graves (release date 10/11/22).
