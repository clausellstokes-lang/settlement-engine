Hello Sojourners!

This is the preview to Season 5 Adventure The Rime of the Frostmaiden. Follow along with this chilling adventure as the sojourners brave into the wild lands of Ten Towns. Explore rugged fronteir masked by a desolation of Auril, the goddess of winter. Brave the frozen north in this apocalyptic horrifying (and sometimes humorous) journey. And so our story continues!

Dungeon Master - Will

  • Silmarn, the wood elf ranger of the artic

  • Risym, the drow fighter from the underdark

  • Sugardumplin, the halfling chef and wizard

  • Nesxan, the dragonborne paladin of Bahamut

Thanks to TableTopAudio for wonderful background music and ambience!
