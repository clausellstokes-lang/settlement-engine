 I am reading again, maybe it's the third time, the first book of the series, AGOT.
 I noticed that the chapters are very short compared to ADWD, so I calculated the average chapter length for each volume, here the results:
 Volume | Pages | Chapters | Avg Chapter Length
 AGOT | 704 | 73 | 9.6
 ACOK | 768 | 70 | 11
 ASOS | 992 | 82 | 12.1
 AFFC | 753 | 46 | 16.4
 ADWD | 1056 | 73 | 14.5
 What I think that short chapters are better, in my opinion the length of AFFC and ADWD chapters are not due to a more mature way of writing, but simply they are filled with nothing.
 In the chapter I have just read (Sansa I) an entire arc of a minor character, Mycah, is developed, and this chapter is relatevely short compared to the never ending Brienne chapters of AFFC.
 I hope that the last two volumes will be similar to AGOT way of writing.
 What do you think?
