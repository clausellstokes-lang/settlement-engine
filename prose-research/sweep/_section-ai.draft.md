## Part B. Sentence-level failures

- [1444] Meister, Vieira and Cotterell, 2020: in machine translation "beam search enforces uniform information density in text", and [1445] a lower standard deviation of surprisal went with higher BLEU, the opposite polarity to the burstiness claim; the resolution is the domain: translation is a constrained task with a reference, not open-ended generation, and neither result may be cited for the other's domain.
- [1110] Meister, Wiher, Pimentel and Cotterell, 2022: human-like text keeps its information content close to the entropy of natural language (cited by claim and section; the row's quotation is trimmed at the symbol).
- [1467] Meister and Cotterell, 2021, trained on Wikipedia dumps: "beam sampling leads to strong divergence from natural language distributions" of length, stopwords and symbols [R]; (partial, quote only) [1466]: nucleus text "aligns with natural language more closely than text produced using other" strategies, and [1468], [1469] on the type-token and Zipf readings.
- [1485] Ji et al., 2024, Wikipedia and news: sampling produces texts "which are often disjunctive in discourse, while search-based methods maintain" topic coherence at the cost of repetition [R]; [1486]: nucleus "achieves better diversity at human-level at the cost of low coherence"; [1487]: typical decoding gives the highest diversity with "severe topic shift (lowest coherence)". DISAGREEMENT, marked, and thinner than the previous draft had it (chair's note applied): the two-mode alternation rests only on partial rows, [180] and [101], whose wording "Alternating loquacious ponderous similes and tiny sentences" is Makin's parody passage written in the AI voice, and on [100], from the same parody. Reconstruction rule: sentence length follows the load. A single fact takes a short sentence; a list of holdings takes a long one; a year with nothing to record takes one line. The mechanism adds a measurement rule: the program's rhythm instrument reports surprisal under at least two scorers, and it never prizes a spectrum for its own sake.
