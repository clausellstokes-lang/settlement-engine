## §0 IN PLAIN ENGLISH (for the owner)

**What this was.** You asked for the writing in the product to be made not just acceptable but excellent, because people distrust prose they can tell a machine wrote. Over the last two days a research programme read seven authors and one catalogue of machine-writing faults, measured every prose surface in the product, and then a panel of agents argued over what to do about it — one set proposing rules for each kind of writing, another set trying to knock those rules down, a third hunting for contradictions between them, a fourth checking what was missed. This document is the result of that argument, written for you.

**What was decided.** Six kinds of writing in the product each get their own rule-book — the town record, the NPC compromise lines, the news, the year's chronicle, the DM's private page, and the product's own help and menus. Ninety-one rules survive the argument; one was thrown out whole because it legislated a surface that belongs to a different rule-book. Every surviving rule says which author's verified habit it rests on (your instruction: pick the author whose evidence fits each kind of writing, never blend them into one voice), what number it moves and by how much, what machine-writing fault it would itself become if applied mechanically, and how it is checked by a program rather than by anyone's taste.

Three of your own decisions run through everything. First, the writing must vary its shape by a controlled choice from a short list of shapes, never a template — so that six months from now no one sees the same order of sentences repeating. Second, every element of a town gets the full treatment; where the world holds no fact for a slot, the record either says honestly that it holds nothing, or says nothing, and each rule-book states which. Third, the Brackwater lesson: the writer is a clerk, not an author — no sentence may say a thing the data does not hold, so a sentence about a bailiff and a priest can only exist once a table of institutions says who counts and who is exempt. That table and the checker that reads every sentence against it are specified and are the first things to be built.

**What did NOT survive.** The proposed author percentages (35/30/20/10/5) — no evidence supports them, so the unit is the kind of writing, never the author. Several proposed target numbers were shown to be impossible, already met, or resting on an instrument that measures the wrong thing; those become "move in this direction; the checker's first run sets the size". Two rules that need data fields the product does not have are kept as directions but marked not-runnable until the field exists — and adding a field is your decision, not this programme's.

**What this asks of you.** Three numbers are yours to set (each comes with a recommended value and its reason): how often any one sentence-shape may recur; how often and in what form the record may say "nothing is held here"; and how often a paragraph may open with the town's own name. After those, about two dozen smaller decisions where the evidence points both ways, each with both sides written out. And a list of every rule that would change words a reader actually meets — those need your signature, because that is public copy.

**What happens next, in order.** The chair sits and ratifies or vetoes what is here. The checkers are built (by the implementing model) and proved on fixtures that must fail — including your own Brackwater sentence. One town block is rewritten as a sample: every variant of one pool, each in a different shape, the same block under two seeds, checked by program and by an adversary — shown to you as findings, never as a pass. Then a blind panel of game masters reads generated towns beside real published gazetteer text, several draws per pool, not knowing which is which — the acceptance test for "preventative". Only then the rewrite wave, in place, gated at the freeze, changing no fact. Then your walk. Then launch. The rewrite comes first, as you ruled at 14:0x; the sensory "ambiance" layer you first asked for and then withdrew is not in the wave (the rewrite adds no new fact) — what the wave carries instead is a measured report of how concrete each tab already is, and the option, later, of authoring new material where a pool is flat, which is yours to want.
