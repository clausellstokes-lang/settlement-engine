Cognitive rehabilitation focuses on learning how to deal with cognitive impairment. Cognitive rehabilitation starts with a thorough neuropsychological assessment, which mostly consists of paper-and-pencil tests to assess cognitive function. One drawback is that paper-and-pencil tests do not relate to the dynamics and complexity of daily life.
