Welcome to the Dorothy Dunnett Society
History and literature together as one
Our worldwide community is brought together by a shared love of Dorothy Dunnett’s writing. Some of us are discovering her books for the first time; others have been reading and rereading them for decades. What we share is an enduring fascination with her characters and stories, a deep interest in the history behind them, and the pleasure of exploring both together.

A Short Note
The Dorothy Dunnett Society is staffed entirely by volunteers, motivated by our shared love of her work.
Our members, volunteers, donors and supporters all play a part in building our worldwide community of Dunnetteers and helping us to share and celebrate Dorothy’s remarkable legacy.
Thank you so much for your support.
Nicky Cannon, Chair
