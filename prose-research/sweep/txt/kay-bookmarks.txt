 Images of Fionavar: Bookmarks

 Posted on November 15, 2014 by Deborah Meghnagi 

 Martin has created bookmarks with details from each of his posters – one for each of the books of The Fionavar Tapestry , and one of his Ceinwen poster. 

 They’re available to buy at $2 USD each, plus postage. The bookmarks are laminated, which means they’ll last. 

 Email Martin to order your sets.
