 Audio Interview with CBC

 Posted on November 15, 2014 by Deborah Meghnagi 

 This interview was conducted on CBC Radio 990’s Weekend Morning Show. It is part of Mr. Robinson’s Bookclub. Copyright © 2000 CBC Manitoba. All Rights Reserved.

 This is an interview from GGK’s media tour for The Sarantine Mosaic in March 2000. It is about 12 minutes long. Topics discussed include the blurring of boundaries between mainstream and fantasy fiction, the way history is made relevant to us through fiction, whether GGK’s books may be turned into films, and the themes and ideas behind The Sarantine Mosaic .
