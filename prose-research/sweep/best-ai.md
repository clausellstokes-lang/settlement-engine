# The faults, as constraints

## 1. The gloss — the summary second sentence

**Fault + mechanism.** The model states the meaning of a fact in the sentence after the fact, because
the objective rewards legible answers and the model cannot tell which tension a text needs to keep
open, so it resolves every one it finds. Measured: narrators explain the theme 77% against 52%
([0], [830]); 18% of professional edits removed exposition restating what was implied ([152]).

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line · herald-pools · compendium-docent (chair's
extension: a docent may explain a mechanism, never what a fact signifies) · chrome (extension).

**Constraint.** No sentence names what a fact means. Where a reader would want the meaning, the
archivist supplies a second fact. **A transferred exemplar technique must not become a gloss:** any
imported move that "lands" a detail by interpreting it — a resonant close, an authorial aside, a
"what this tells us" — is the fault under a better vocabulary.

**Evidence.** [0], [830], [58], [80], [152], [476], [90], [120], [121], [108], [390], [400], [669],
[733]; archival: [878], [286], [1413], [1410] (Wikipedia's guide), [1236] (ASIC assessor, SINGLE).
14 documents. **STRONG.** Archival register: YES.
On the page: "AI stories over-explain themes and favor tidy, single-track plots" [0].

## 2. The glossary tail — the participial significance close

**Fault + mechanism.** A present-participle phrase welded to the end of a factual sentence assigning
significance ("further enhancing its significance as a dynamic hub"). It is syntax, not vocabulary:
GPT-4o uses present participial clauses at 5.3 times the human rate across six registers.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line · herald-pools · compendium-docent · chrome.

**Constraint.** No sentence ends on a participle of significance; no population figure, road, market
or etymology is followed by what it creates, solidifies, enhances or reflects. **A transferred
cadence must not become this tail:** an exemplar's habit of closing a sentence on a rhythmic
subordinate clause is admissible only when that clause carries a *fact*, never an assessment.

**Evidence.** [286], [1413], [878], [1410], [876], [289] (Wikipedia's guide, one page family);
[32], [1344] (Reinhart et al., the 5.3× rate); [1313] (Huang et al. relaying Reinhart — a relay, not
independent). 3 documents, 2 independent. **MODERATE.** Archival register: YES (the whole limb).
On the page: "attaching a present participle phrase at the end of sentences" [286].

## 3. Reflexive antithesis — not X but Y

**Fault + mechanism.** Negative parallelism deployed as a tic rather than a choice: the model reaches
for the contrast whenever it wants to sound considered, and the construction survives every
generation while other tells drift. Contested as a *detector* (it is a legitimate human device); not
contested as a *saturation* fault.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist (banned outright) · herald-pools · chronicle-line · chrome
(extension: banned; it is the single most identifiable marketing tic) · compendium-docent ·
dm-page (one per page at most, the section's only allowance).

**Constraint.** The record states what is, never what it is not; an archivist has no misconception to
correct because the reader has not been told anything yet. **A transferred rhetorical contrast must
not become the default sentence shape:** an exemplar's one earned reversal per scene is not a licence
for the construction to become a grammar slot.

**Evidence.** [112], [178] (encyclopedic), [113], [115], [184], [217], [907], [1099]. 7 documents.
**STRONG.** Archival register: YES ([112], [178]).
On the page (the guide's own example): "not a mirror but a portal" [112].

## 4. The rule of three — tricolon and triadic scaffolding

**Fault + mechanism.** Three is the statistical average of a list, so the model pads or trims to
three, and uses the triad to make a superficial analysis look comprehensive. The rate is measured
once (7.13 per document against 3.73 for human experts) and the *purpose* is named by the guide.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · herald-pools · chronicle-line · chrome · compendium-docent ·
dm-page.

**Constraint.** A list runs to the true count of the things listed — two guilds, five wards, one
ferry, four gates of which one is walled up. Never three abstractions; no abstraction in a list at
all. **A transferred triad must not become a rota:** an exemplar's tricolon is admissible only where
the record holds exactly three of something.

**Evidence.** [73] (Bakhshi, the rate — SINGLE limb), [181], [192], [178], [892], [893], [1425],
[907]. 5 documents. **STRONG** for the fault; the rate is SINGLE. Archival register: YES.
On the page: "LLMs often use this structure to make superficial analyses appear more comprehensive."
[893].

## 5. The metronome — uniform sentence length and constant pacing

**Fault + mechanism.** Sentence length clusters in a narrow band and the cadence never varies,
because likelihood-trained decoding regresses toward uniform information density. In the record
register the measured drift is *longer* sentences, longer words, fewer to-be verbs and a higher
grade — less readable, not more.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line · herald-pools (hardest: short pools have the least
room to vary) · dm-page · chrome · compendium-docent.

**Constraint.** Sentence length follows the load: one fact, one short sentence; a list of holdings, a
long one; a quiet year, one line. Never alternate by rota — strict alternation is itself a metronome.
Keep the plain copula. Any burstiness instrument reports surprisal under at least two scorers,
because one scorer can invert the conclusion. **A transferred prose rhythm must not become a
schedule:** an exemplar's cadence may be imitated only as a response to load.

**Evidence.** [98], [665], [666], [657], [170], [71], [73], [936]; archival: [313], [920], [1335]
(length and grade only); mechanism: [1444], [1110], [1467], [1485], [1506], [1495]. 15 documents.
**STRONG.** Archival register: PARTIAL — length and grade are measured in the record register;
**variance is not** (critic §5.K), so the archival limb is by substitution.
On the page: "Sentences are roughly the same length. The cadence never varies." [665].

## 6. The evaluative close — the moral, the "Despite its X… challenges", the closing summary

**Fault + mechanism.** The model ends by assessing what it has just written: a lesson learnt, a
Challenges paragraph, an Awards section, a summary of significance. Instruction tuning rewards a
tidy, complete-looking artefact, and a closing assessment is the cheapest signal of completeness.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line (no moral at year's end) · herald-pools ·
compendium-docent · chrome (extension: the "Despite its X" shape is the native voice of marketing
copy and is banned there too) · dm-page.

**Constraint.** No entry closes on a moral, a summary, an aphorism or an outline-like closer; no
Challenges, Future prospects, Awards or Legacy paragraph exists. A paragraph closes on the standing
condition. **A transferred closing cadence must not become an assessment:** an exemplar's final line
may land a fact, never a verdict.

**Evidence.** [289], [295], [288], [876], [284], [1410] (the guide, encyclopedic), [48], [477],
[478], [106], [109], [43]. 6 documents. **STRONG.** Archival register: YES.
On the page: "Despite its [positive words], [subject] faces challenges..." [289].

## 7. Forced closure — every thread tied, no ambiguity held

**Fault + mechanism.** A post-training product: pre-instruction models failed to finish, every
instruction-tuned model from 2023 over-finishes. 79% of AI stories carry no subplot against 57%;
resolutions are protagonist-driven 69% against 46%; a measured inability to defer closure.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line · herald-pools · dm-page.

**Constraint.** Every dossier entry leaves at least one civic matter standing open and says so
plainly. Nothing gets a second ending; a matter closed in one year is not re-closed in the next.
**A transferred resolution technique must not become tidiness:** the analogue in a record is a matter
reported settled while the world state still holds it open.

**Evidence.** [1], [4], [5], [371], [107], [368], [833], [43], [84] (SINGLE, an unfetched preprint
via a blog), [48], [477], [478], [106], [109]. 7 documents. **STRONG.**
Archival register: **NO — fiction only.** This is an analogy for the archivist.
On the page: "Every scene ends properly. Every arc gets closed." [106].

## 8. Flat pressure — no escalation, swappable sentences

**Fault + mechanism.** Turning points arrive early, tension is not retained, and consecutive sentences
can be exchanged without loss, because a likelihood objective favours "the lowest common
denominator, rather than informative language" and truncation-based decoding cannot cover the human
distribution at all.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line (each year depends on the one before) · dm-page.

**Constraint.** Order entries so later ones depend on earlier: the levy follows the bad harvest, the
riot follows the levy. No two sentences may be swapped without loss. Pressure is a count that moves
(the garrison shrinks, the granary empties); the reader is never told that tension rises. Program
rule: the pools are authored so a rare true fact is never the low-probability continuation a sampler
would prune. **A transferred escalation technique must not become mood:** rising pressure is a
number, not an adjective.

**Evidence.** [9], [359], [54], [55], [49], [43], [134], [158], [162], [657], [663]; mechanism
[1047], [1113], [1074], [1450], [1447], [1123]. 16 documents. **STRONG.**
Archival register: **NO as prose** — the only archival trace is decoding measured on Wikitext-103
continuations of encyclopedic text, which is a base model continuing, not a chatbot writing.
On the page: "no modulation, no room to breathe" [657].

## 9. The fixed move order — one template for every culture, one shape for every entry

**Fault + mechanism.** One plot template for 236 countries; the first suggested food always pizza or
sushi; the poetic default breaks through contrary prompts; six models prioritise style over content.
The model has one plan and re-skins it, because the plan is the training distribution's mode.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line · herald-pools · dm-page · compendium-docent ·
chrome.

**Constraint (the owner's latent-grammar directive, evidenced).** The move ORDER varies by a seeded
choice among a closed set of grammars, measured by a walker; the claim never varies. A fixed move
order is the fault with better vocabulary. Culture is a typed bucket the pools respect: a
settlement's festival, food and forms of address come from its own bucket, never from a default.
**A transferred structure must never be installed as a visible template:** an exemplar's ordering of
moves enters as one grammar among several, never as *the* order.

**Evidence.** [28], [485], [486], [487], [491], [492], [499], [497], [474], [475], [467], [383],
[51], [35], [165], [48]; archival: [1370], [1368] (designer profiles, Rascal News — SINGLE
document). 11 documents. **STRONG.** Archival register: YES, weakly (one investigation).
On the page: "the first suggested food item was always pizza or sushi" [485] (trimmed).

## 10. Sameness — echoed elements, default names, the same well

**Fault + mechanism.** Idiosyncratic elements recur across generations and across model families:
Elias in 26.5% of 20,000 stories, "900 times more frequent in our corpus" than in published fiction;
Astrid over 10% of the time; the same corridor line a year apart. Homogenisation is not a defect that
scale is fixing — the newer model clusters tighter.

**Registers.** herald-pools (hardest — the pools are short) · dossier-archivist · chronicle-line ·
dm-page.

**Constraint.** Names, numbers and openings come from the engine's pools, and pools are measured for
**dispersion**, not size (a pool's length is a seed input). No personal name recurs across two
settlements in one world; no two entries in one dossier open on the same noun; a fired phrase is
retired for that settlement. Measure where our dossiers sit in the space of possible dossiers, and
how many fall in the rare decile. **A transferred naming or imagery habit must not become a pool
default:** an exemplar's signature noun is exactly what a pool must not standardise on.

**Evidence.** [39], [56], [57], [755], [195], [470], [810], [217], [664], [397], [399]; archival:
[942] (obituary spam repeating the same wrong detail), [1388]. 12 documents. **STRONG.**
Archival register: YES.
On the page: "900 times more frequent in our corpus" [56].

## 11. No grain — the missing particular, the vague authority, no testimony

**Fault + mechanism.** The model omits specific, unusual, nuanced facts because they are statistically
rare, and replaces them with generic positive description and attribution to nobody. In the record
register this is measured directly: the guide's smoothing finding, the obituaries with no quotes and
no outside reporting, the travel text that runs out of ideas, the summariser calling a hotel
"spotless" against a hundred food-poisoning mentions.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line · herald-pools · dm-page · compendium-docent ·
chrome (extension: chrome may be plain and brief but may not attribute to "experts" or "users").

**Constraint.** Every sentence carries at least one particular the engine holds. Attribution is to a
named record — the reeve's roll, the guild book, the tithe return — never "it is said", never
"experts hold". No evaluative adjective attaches to a place. Testimony is a named witness and a date,
or it is absent. **A transferred descriptive richness must not become adjectives:** the corpus's own
reconciliation is that the sensory language is present but "attached to the immaterial"; density of
adjectives is what the model has *instead of* the place.

**Evidence.** [91], [92], [369], [458], [488], [155], [48], [164], [212], [373], [209], [34], [667],
[848]; archival: [173], [873], [872], [874], [175], [176], [989], [1414] (the guide), [946], [947]
(obituary), [1156], [1159], [1160], [1143], [1162], [1163], [1166] (travel and local history),
[1168], [1169] (place summaries), [1191], [1367], [1403]. 24 documents. **STRONG.**
Archival register: YES, heavily.
On the page: "tends to omit specific, unusual, nuanced facts" [173].

## 12. Feeling by formula — bodies, weather as mirror, the mood vocabulary

**Fault + mechanism.** Emotion is conveyed through physical sensation and bodily metaphor 81% against
38%, smell imagery 82% against 57%, while explicit emotion labels drop to 8% from 29%. The model
reaches for sensory language because it correlates with good writing, then attaches it to the
immaterial.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line · herald-pools · compendium-docent.
(dm-page may be candid; it still may not render feeling as weather.)

**Constraint.** The archivist renders no feeling. Weather is recorded only where it has a civic
consequence, never as a mirror. The mood and spectral word lists are **house bans, not findings** —
their evidence is two essays and one poetry study. A sense verb takes a physical object: nothing
smells of dreams. **A transferred interiority technique must not become somatisation:** an exemplar's
bodily detail transfers only as a trade fact (the tannery, the fish market).

**Evidence.** [2], [367], [834], [835], [3], [836] (StoryScope, the measured limb), [127], [122]
(Vollmer — the mood list, SINGLE), [897], [912], [898], [899], [900] (Kriss — the spectral set,

SINGLE. Archival register: **NO — fiction and verse only.**
On the page: "Right ballpark, wrong physics." [127].

## 13. Dialogue — on the nose, curt, hooked, one voice

**Fault + mechanism.** Lines state what the speaker wants; dialogue serves philosophical debate 59%
against 34%; blocks end on cliffhangers; NPCs forget their faction and their century. Designer
dialogue beat GPT-4 infills 64% to 23%. The one-voice claim is a practitioner observation, not a
measurement, and a verified counter-instance exists (a role-keyed personality survived generation).

**Registers.** herald-pools · dm-page. (Not the archivist: the record carries no dialogue.)

**Constraint.** Each speaking role owns a pool with one verbal habit no other role shares; a line
never states what the speaker wants; no line ends on a hook; no line explains the world to someone
who lives in it. Anachronism is impossible by construction because the pool is drawn from typed
buckets. Irony, if it lives anywhere, is a typed bucket ([1301] is the measured absence, GPT-3.5,
January 2023 — era-bound). **A transferred voice technique must not become an accent:** one habit per
role, keyed to office, not a dialect sprinkled over every line.

**Evidence.** [831], [80], [183], [77], [483], [203], [205], [200], [208], [206], [194], [414],
[423], [341], [1301]. 14 documents. **STRONG.** Archival register: **NO — fiction and game text.**
On the page: "whereas ChatGPT never uses antiphrasis" [1301].

## 14. No one behind the sentence — the answer-shaped thing

**Fault + mechanism.** Text produced without communicative intent: coherence is in the reader's eye.
Its record-register form is the citation shaped to fit the register — "That was the type of language
that was expected" — and the invented reference that backs an invented fact.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line · herald-pools · dm-page · compendium-docent.

**Constraint.** The archivist has a position and a stake: the dossier is written from a record room by
an office that exists to settle something, and its sentences can be ordered only one way because they
follow the order of the record. Where two offices would record an event differently, the dossier says
which record it follows. A sentence that is answer-shaped without an answer — a count that is not a
count, a citation to a roll the engine never made — is refused **at the pool**, not polished at the
rendering. The record is third person; no clerk remembers a smell. **A transferred authorial voice
must not become a persona:** the office is the author.

**Evidence.** [516], [521], [638], [855], [86], [161], [446], [350], [685], [679], [693], [668],
[734], [375], [385]; archival: [976], [1041], [1188], [1364], [1374], [1004], [968]. 21 documents.
**STRONG.** Archival register: YES.
On the page: "They don't know what they're writing." [1364].

## 15. Manufactured emphasis — fragments, one-line paragraphs, the bulleted list

**Fault + mechanism.** Short standalone fragments, stacked negated fragments and cliffhanger blocks
substitute for emphasis the content has not earned. Register-split, and the corpus holds both
directions: the chat/expository register keeps hypotactic smoothness while the story register breaks
into fragments. Its record-register form is the bulleted list bolted to the end of an article.

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line · compendium-docent · chrome (extension: UI copy
may be terse, but a fragment used for punch is the same fault) · dm-page (allowed a designer's aside).

**Constraint.** No fragment stands as a sentence. No paragraph is one line unless it is a single-fact
year. No run of sentences opens on "Not". No entry ends on a hook, a kicker or a bulleted list.
Because the dossier is a record, it inherits the expository register's smoothness as a virtue and
adds only the variation of load. **A transferred punch must not become a fragment:** emphasis in a
record comes from the fact, never from the line break.

**Evidence.** [103], [183], [907], [908]; archival: [1201] (one editor's suspicion — SINGLE, and
suspicion, not measurement). 4 documents, each sub-tell resting on one. **MODERATE.**
Archival register: weak YES (suspicion only).
On the page: "see the ChatGPT-ish bulleted list at the end of the article" [1201].

## 16. Metaphor — off-target, piled up, scenery given intent

**Fault + mechanism.** Figures that sound clever rather than clarify, stacked at the sentence end, and
the conjunction of one abstract or incorporeal thing with one concrete one; inanimate objects get
active verbs and, by extension, intent. It survives explicit stylistic instruction ("R1 even
consistently does this in spite of user-specified stylistic directions").

**Registers.** dossier-archivist · chronicle-line · herald-pools · compendium-docent · chrome.

**Constraint.** The archivist uses no figurative language. A comparison is a measurement in words: the
wall is the height of two men. Inanimate things do not act with intent — the river floods, the roof
falls, and where a thing happened the record names who did it. No sentence ends on a comparison; no
sense verb takes an abstract object; no route is famous. The pools carry **no simile slot**.
**A transferred image must not become a slot:** an exemplar's metaphor is a one-off authored choice,
which is exactly what a pool cannot hold.

**Evidence.** [127], [128], [130], [157], [182], [735], [736], [667], [654], [655], [659], [660],
[909], [912], [897], [185]; archival: [1183], [1184] (the genre glitch — SINGLE and **suspected**,
not confirmed generation), plus the participial tail at [286], [1413]. 11 documents. **STRONG.**
Archival register: YES via the participial tail; the legal-record instance is suspected only.
On the page: "overassign active verbs, and by extension intent" [185].

## 17. Repetition without relief — the same word, the same shape, the loop

**Fault + mechanism.** A model reuses a word or a sentence shape because nothing stops it, and the
loop is its limit case: "The probability of a repeated phrase increases with each repetition"; human
corpora show 0.02% consecutive sentence-level repetition against a maximum-likelihood model's 14.50%.

## Two closing notes for the reconciliation

1. **The strongest tell is structural, not lexical** — narrative features alone separate human from AI
   at 93.2% macro-F1, 97% of what adding stylistic cues buys. A de-slopping pass that strips
   "tapestry" and em dashes leaves the discriminating signal untouched. Weight items 1, 2, 5–11, 21,
   26, 28 above item 19.
2. **A model upgrade is not a cure.** Better models cluster tighter, not looser; the idiosyncratic
