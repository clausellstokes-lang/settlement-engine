The travellers' pub is for general discussion on Wikivoyage, and the place to ask questions when you're confused, lost, afraid, tired, annoyed, thoughtful, or helpful. To start a new topic, click the "Add topic" tab, so that it gets added '''at the bottom''' of the page, and sign your post by appending four tildes (<nowiki>~~~~</nowiki>)

Before asking a question or making a comment:
* Have a look at our [[Wikivoyage:Help|Help]], [[Wikivoyage:FAQ|FAQ]] and [[Wikivoyage:Policies|Policies]] pages.
* If you are a '''new user''' and you have any questions about using the website, try the [[Wikivoyage:Arrivals lounge|Arrivals lounge]].
* If you have a '''question or suggestion about a particular article''', use the article's [[Project:using talk pages|talk page]] to keep the discussion associated with that article.
* If you'd like to '''draw attention to a comment''' to '''get feedback''' from other Wikivoyagers, try [[Wikivoyage:Requests for comment|Requests for comment]].
* If you are  '''wanting travel advice on a specific matter''' see the [[Wikivoyage:Tourist office|tourist office]].
* If you have an '''issue you need to bring to the attention of an administrator''', try [[Wikivoyage:Ongoing vandalism|Ongoing vandalism]].
* If you are having a '''problem that you think has to do with the [[mw:|MediaWiki software]]''', please post that on [[phab:|Phabricator]] instead.
* If you want to '''celebrate a significant contribution''' to Wikivoyage by yourself or others, hold a party at [[Project:Celebrate a contribution|Celebrate a contribution]].
* Discuss '''issues related to more than one language version''' of Wikivoyage in the [[meta:Wikivoyage/Lounge|Wikivoyage Lounge]] on Meta.
* Anything that is '''Nigeria-related''' is now meant to go in the [[Wikivoyage:Nigeria café|Nigeria café]] instead. Anything that is '''Kosovo or Albania related''' is now meant to go in the [[Wikivoyage:Kosovo and Albania cafe|Kosovo and Albania café]] instead. This includes announcements, initiatives, celebrations, and issues with certain articles.
You can review old Pub discussions in the [[Wikivoyage:Travellers' pub/Archives]].

:I've been backpacking pretty full on for about a decade now, with a bit of travel before that, and it has been interesting to see how the nature of backpacking has changed. Back in the day, you'd have a pretty large class of backpackers who travelled just to travel. Travel-bums, they were, like ski-bums. They'd populate hostels around the world, and generally formed the robust backbone of a strong travel community. They thrived less on external validation from their peers; the motivation was more a quiet rebellion against The Man, man. Drugs, alcohol, and doing more or less nothing (for days, weeks and even months) were far more prevalent behaviours than they are today. The demand for comfort was lower; the demand for untrammeled freedom and independence was higher. When non-travel-bums, normies (like me, at least then) visited an exotic locale, you'd get swept up into this culture. Suddenly, you'd find yourself doing nothing for days; sitting, perhaps socialising, perhaps laying in a hammock. The world stood still when you travelled, in a sense. It doesn't feel like this anymore. Today, backpacking seems more goals-based: I will accomplish this, this and this, and return home with baseline+x likes on Instagram, etc. Sitting on your arse (and using drugs, etc.) is no longer particularly accepted nor embraced, and you'll likely lead a lonely travel life if this is your M.O. Hostels and backpacker-oriented hotels have adapted. Hand-holding "experiences" and tours now dominate the typical hostel stay. One is now usually expected to arrive at hostel, and then assign themselves to a tour leaving the next day. At the same time, comfort has greatly improved. Dorm beds are now curtained and dorm rooms air conditioned. Private rooms are no longer a rarity exploited only by couples (long term or sudden), but by backpackers who simply demand a higher degree of comfort. The hostel industry has become winner-take-all, with those offering nightly events and daily tours being booked out days or even weeks in advance, while others languish with travellers that simply couldn't book in time. I think there are/were two major factors that have impacted the change: 1) social media and 2) the pandemic. The former creating the impetus for a certain type of goals-based travel, raising prices and killing off the old-breed of travel-bums, while the latter invoking a near-literal fear-of-god in younger travellers and establishing a higher baseline for safety, comfort, etc. (also raising prices). == AddSitelink ==

Hi everyone!

I’ve been using [[q:User:Saroj/AddSitelink|AddSitelink]] on English Wikiquote, and I thought it might also be useful on Wikivoyage.

AddSitelink makes it easier to connect a page to an existing Wikidata item using a corresponding Wikipedia article. It adds an "Add sitelink" option to the Tools menu on pages without a Wikidata item.

To try it, add the following to your [[Special:MyPage/common.js|common.js]]:

<syntaxhighlight lang="javascript"> mw.loader.load('//en.wikiquote.org/w/index.php?title=User:Saroj/AddSitelink.js&action=raw&ctype=text/javascript'); </syntaxhighlight>

Feedback is welcome! [[User:Saroj|Saroj]] ([[User talk:Saroj|talk]]) 09:37, 29 August 2026 (UTC)
