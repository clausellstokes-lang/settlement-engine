Welcome to '''WikiProject AI Cleanup''', a collaboration to combat [[Wikipedia:Artificial intelligence|AI-generated content on Wikipedia]]. If you would like to help, [[#Participants|add yourself as a participant]] in the project, inquire on the [[Wikipedia talk:WikiProject AI Cleanup|talk page]], and see the [[#Open tasks|to-do list]]. For advice about AI cleanup and resources, see [[Wikipedia:WikiProject AI Cleanup/Guide and resources|the guide]].

== Goals ==
Since 2022, [[large language models]] (LLMs), including [[Generative pre-trained transformer|GPT]]s, have become a convenient tool for creating text at scale. Unfortunately, these models virtually always fail to properly source claims and often introduce errors. Since March 2026, the Wikipedia guideline [[WP:LLM]] has prohibited the use of LLMs to generate or rewrite article content, with limited exceptions for translation and [[Wikipedia:Basic copyediting|basic copyediting]]. However, a lot of LLM-generated text inserted from 2022 to 2026 remains on Wikipedia.
The purpose of this project is to identify and address the misuse of AI in articles. Specifically, these are the project's goals:
* To identify text written by AI, and scrutinize such text to make sure it follows Wikipedia's policies. Any unsourced or likely inaccurate claims should be removed in priority.
* To identify AI-generated images and ensure appropriate usage.
* To keep track of AI-using editors who may not realize the deficiencies of AI as a writing tool. 
* To educate editors about the issues with AI-generated text and English Wikipedia's expectations around AI use.
* To protect against disruption from [[AI agent]]s.
=== Editing advice ===
* Tag articles with appropriate templates, remove unsourced information and/or content added in violation of [[WP:NEWLLM]], and warn users who add AI-generated content to articles.
* Any pages (not just articles) that are clearly entirely LLM-generated can be nominated for [[WP:SPEEDY|speedy deletion]] under [[WP:G15]].
* Identifying AI-assisted edits is difficult in most cases since the generated text is often indistinguishable from human text. The [[WP:AISIGNS|signs of AI writing]] page provides a list of characteristics that are associated with text generated by AI chatbots.
** Text that was present in an article before November 30, 2022 (the release date of [[ChatGPT]]) is very unlikely to be AI-generated.
* Most AI content is not "unsourced"—sometimes it has real sources that are unrelated to the article's topic, sometimes it creates its own [[Wikipedia:Fictitious references|fake sources]], and sometimes it uses legitimate sources to create the AI content. When removing bad AI content, check the cited sources for legitimacy, and if they are legitimate, consider preserving them on the article talk page for eventual use in human-written content.
** Example: the article [[Special:Permalink/1188374338|Leninist historiography]] was entirely written by AI and included a list of completely fake sources in Russian and Hungarian at the bottom of the page. Google turned up no results for these sources.
** Other example: the article ''[[Estola albosignata]]'', about a beetle species, had [https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Estola_albosignata&diff=prev&oldid=1152503574 paragraphs written by AI] sourced to actual German and French sources. While the sourced articles were real, they were completely off-topic, with the French one discussing a completely unrelated lifeform.
** As of 2026, recent models will usually cite real sources, although they will likely not verify the content they are being cited for. The existence of these sources should not, by itself, be taken as evidence that the content is human-written.
* Sometimes entire articles are AI-generated, and in such a case, make sure to check that the topic is legitimate and [[Wikipedia:Notability|notable]]. Occasionally, [[WP:HOAX]]es have made it onto Wikipedia because AI tools can create fake citations that may appear legitimate. 
** Example: the article [[Wikipedia:List of hoaxes on Wikipedia/Amberlihisar|Amberlihisar]] was created in January 2023, passed [[Wikipedia:articles for creation|articles for creation]], and was not discovered to be entirely fictional until December 2023. It has since been deleted from mainspace.

== Open tasks ==
See [[:Category:Articles containing suspected AI-generated texts]] for all articles that have been tagged as possibly {{tl|AI-generated}}. The [[Wikipedia:WikiProject AI Cleanup/Tasks|tasks page]] recommends ways to handle articles, talk page discussions, and sources that use AI-generated content.

Editors may also consider motivating project members or others who go out of their way to help with AI cleanup issues by thanking them through a personal note or through one of Wikipedia's "[[Wikipedia:Barnstars|awards]]", like {{tl|The AI Cleanup Barnstar}}.
