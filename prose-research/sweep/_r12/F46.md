Rule: The dossier learns the trade's direction and not its fashions: each generation moved toward the intelligible and the front-loaded, and each overshot somewhere (a wall of text, a delve, a glossary that contradicts its body, a bestiary with the lore cut to a phrase). The archivist front-loads what a reader needs, defines a term once and uses it the same way everywhere, and never cuts a place with an entry below a sentence (Reconciliation 6); its register is Holmes's dispassion with Moldvay's economy (811, 813), and its places imply a cosmology by what stands in them (815).
