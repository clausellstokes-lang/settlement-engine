## VI. How official D&D writes a settlement (the PLACE round, completed in round 11)

The round-9 section opened this Part by saying its four angles reached the publisher's settlement text by lawful routes only. That sentence is withdrawn. Two of the four routes are questioned, on the round-9 critic's finding (section 1 of `critic-dnd.md`, which also records that two finders in one round ruled opposite ways on the same route), and under the owner's delegation of 09-07 the chair rules as follows. The nineteen 2014 Dungeon Master's Guide rows, 944 to 962, were read from an Internet Archive item holding the OCR of the whole copyrighted book; the twenty-one 2024 Dungeon Master's Guide rows, 932 to 939 and 972 to 984, were read from Roll20 compendium pages whose fetched body opens with a purchase banner. The forty rows are marked ROUTE QUESTIONED. Thirty of them (932, 933, 934, 935, 937, 939, 944, 945, 946, 947, 948, 950, 951, 954, 955, 956, 957, 958, 959, 961, 962, 973, 974, 975, 976, 977, 980, 981, 982, 983) had been VERIFIED_VERBATIM or VERIFIED_SUBSTANCE and were lowered to PARTIAL by the round-12 regrade, with the witness itself, not a limb of the claim, as the unsupported limb; the other ten (936, 938, 949, 952, 953, 960, 972, 978, 979, 984) were already PARTIAL on a limb of their claim and carry the mark by the chair's ruling. Below, each is cited only with the mark (P, RQ), only for its quotation, and never as support; where the round-11 angle place-lawful-primaries landed a lawful witness for the same fact, the lawful witness is cited as support and the questioned row is said to be replaced. The table after the register map lists the forty with their route, their standing before the regrade, the features that cite them, and the lawful replacement where one exists; twenty-one of the forty facts have none and stand on the mark alone. The retention on disk of the two full-text captures the finders made (the 2014 guide's OCR and the paywalled 2024 sections) is the owner's call, recorded by the critic and not decided here.

The lawful routes that stand, and the round-11 angle that widened them: the Settlement Tracker as the publisher's own free PDF (940, 941; read again at the primary, 1153 to 1160); the two SRDs and the 2018 Basic Rules as the publisher's own PDFs; the Ravenloft creator guidance as the publisher's own DMs Guild PDF (906, 907); the Death House introductory adventure as the publisher's own free PDF (P 1161, P 1162); the publisher's platform, D&D Beyond, for its launch and update articles on the 2024 guide (1163, 1164), three Encounter of the Week columns of 2020 (Snowed In 1165 to 1169; Battle of the Braggarts 1170 to 1172; Trek to Ten-Towns 900 to 905, 1173, 1174), the Phandalin article that reprints the published arrival passage and its location table (1180 to 1186), the Phandalin Post handout (1187 to 1192), Merwin's boxed-text column at the primary (1198 to 1204), and the 2024 Free Rules (1205 to 1207); Perkins's collected 2011 columns through a Wayback capture of the publisher's own PDF (1073 to 1079, 1208 to 1210); the Dungeoncraft series through a preservation copy (1080 to 1084); the licensed magazine's guidelines at paizo.com (1085 to 1109); a reporter's account of the publisher's own preview with Perkins (1175 to 1177); PC Gamer (1194, 1195); a podcast read-through that names the 2014 guide's sections (1196, 1197); and the fifth-edition settlement chapters (Ten-Towns, the Baldur's Gate Gazetteer, Vallaki and the Village of Barovia, Sigil, the Sword Coast entries) still only through reviewers who quote them.
