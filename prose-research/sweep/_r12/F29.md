**29. The date of boxed text: an instruction to read a background aloud in 1977, area descriptions set in quotation marks for the Origins tournament of 1979, first boxed in the 1980 reissue, first boxed in a Gygax adventure in 1982; the purpose was a consistent competitive experience.**
Support: 16 kept rows on 10 documents; 4 PARTIAL rows cited for their quotation only. Hartlage 153, 664, 665; Alexander 429, 631, 646; Dyson 684, 861, 862; McKenzie 608, 610 (the module's own instruction); Holmes via Howard 761; Delta 782 (the 1979 quoted passages), 768 (the 1982 box); DM's Workshop 1149; Hartlage on 4e's casual-DM goal 607; (P) 38, 20, 155, 762.
True wording: "In 1979, Hidden Shrine of Tamoachan introduced boxed text descriptions" (664); "In either 1979 or 1980" (646); "the text was not yet boxed, instead appearing between quotation marks" (429); "marks its first appearance" (608, dated 1980, citing Merric Blackman); "a consistent play experience in a competitive environment" (665); "I tend to blame this on the tournament usage" (684, the author's supposition); "Hints of what may be done are given in this text" (610); "should read the background material above to the assembled players" (761); "not exactly in boxed text, but in specially quoted and indented" (782); "And hey, there's boxed text for the first time!" (768, of the 1982 Lost Caverns); "to standardize the game experience for each play group" (862); "boxed text was introduced in The Hidden Shrine of Tamoachan (1979)" (1149).
Resolution of the chair's flag, now tested: the date no longer rests on one hedged blog post through two witnesses, and the independence the round-9 text asserted is shown. Holmes's 1977 Basic Set instructs the Dungeon Master to read the Portown background to the assembled players (761), a read-aloud instruction two years before Tamoachan, applied to a background paragraph and not to keyed areas; Howard's reading that this predates boxed text is PARTIAL (P 762: the page never mentions boxed text). Delta, examining the 1979 printing independently of Alexander, records its initial area descriptions as quoted and indented passages rather than boxes (782), the mechanism 429 and 631 give; Hartlage dates the practice 1979 (664); the 1980 reissue boxes them (608, 646); and the first box in an adventure Gygax himself wrote is the 1982 Lost Caverns (768). Dyson's tournament attribution is his own supposition in his own words (684, 862, "I tend to blame"), with his dating to the 1e days beside it (861); DM's Workshop dates the introduction to 1979 and gives the competition purpose (1149). McKenzie's 1980 is the boxing date and still rests on Merric Blackman, whose live URL is dead (608, P 38). Alexander also gives a 1978 honourable mention (646, note). Four witnesses now agree on the sequence and differ only on which step they call the first.
Rule: DM page only, as history: the voiced block is a tournament instrument for making every table's experience the same. The dossier, which is not a tournament, borrows the consistency and not the recital.
