What the publisher prescribes for boxed text today is the Adventurers League template of 2023: very sparingly, only for an important event, never for rooms or normalcy, one or two brief descriptive sentences; and the 2024 guide, by Wyatt's own account, now defines boxed text so that adventures need not.**

The 2023 template's own margin notes ration the sidebar and the boxed text; the template is the corrective to its predecessor. Wyatt's sentence (1148) is the primary behind the round-9 section's Screen Rant paraphrase (208): the 2024 guide defines boxed text, and published adventures stop doing so.
Rule: The voiced block, where the dossier has one at all (the Herald's short pools), targets one or two sentences of concrete nouns and, at most, one line of speech, under the ceiling of four that feature 27 records (Reconciliation 10); it marks an event, never a room in its ordinary state. The substance of a place lives under its headings, and the headings are not so many that one door needs three of them (1120). Background names its keywords once, in emphasis, and does not bloat. Each part of a document gets a one- or two-sentence overview. An area is described under Dimensions and Terrain, Lighting, Sounds and Smells; emphasis marks creatures and keyed objects only.
