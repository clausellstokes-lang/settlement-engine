Rule (binds the archivist, and answers Reconciliation 8): A person of record is named as Title Given Surname, the office (Baron Vargas Vallakovich, Burgomaster of Vallaki; Starosta Ernath Noack, leader of the village), and gets a sentence: who, where found, what wanted (feature 50). Where the world's own usage is title and surname alone (Townmaster Wester), the record follows it and spells it the same on every page (1191 against 1192). The parenthetical of creature, alignment, species or gender is a game object and goes to the DM page (feature 9). A quarter is named for its trade, its feature or the people who live there (Tannery Square, Riverside, the Foreign Quarter), a house rule illustrated by the questioned rows. Names are plain and pronounceable and made strange by their use (feature 43), sounding human where their people are human (1079); the diction that surrounds them is the archivist's (feature 45).
