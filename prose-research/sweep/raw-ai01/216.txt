 Well, not literally destroying, but causing a lot of harm. In several ways. Take this " creator " for instance. This is their current KS:

But all of their projects are the same. It's nothing but AI. There are a lot of people on KS with projects just like this. Kickstarter does have a policy, but they don't enforce it. Take this section for example. Project owners are required to get consent for AI work. Here's how they answer that question. Note they don't even answer it. And KS doesn't care in its review process. Then again, how can you get consent for AI? It's an impossible question. How is it even possible for this "creator" to say they didn't use AI from artist's work? They don't know that unless they happen to own midjourney or chatgpt or copilot.

Or this "creator" , who is churning out projects left and right, and they are all Ai. It's garbage. All of it. And what it does is hurt all of us who are actual creators, who do actual human writing or artwork. It hurts us because it's taking money we'd normally get from customers and going towards these projects. It's hurting us because we're getting lost in the ocean of AI garbage. 

I really wish KS would get their ducks in order, and actually enforce their own policy.

 I'm not really alarmed by the existence of these Kickstarters. When new tools and new means makes something easier or more accessible, it'll always draw in new actors that were not capable with the former level of investment required.

What's more alarming is people giving these money. I consider myself quite AI-literate. I have professional artists around me that use AI in their pipeline and it's very useful. But it's obvious looking at the results (the examples you shared) that their blurbs is non-sense. They were not produced with the assistance of, they were created entirely by AI-tools. There was probably little to no revisions, and it probably only being rerunning the same prompt over and over with different seeds until they got a result they liked. You can tell by the look of it, it's generic AI art.

And again, if Steve, 54 years old, writes an adventure in a word document and shares it as PWYW and generated a cover with AI. I don't care.

But whenever I see these kickstarters (or the recent rise of websites and app being shared here) I always take 5 minutes to look at the content, and the majority of the time, the content is also written by AI. Which for me is a no-no.
