I'm not really alarmed by the existence of these Kickstarters. When new tools and new means makes something easier or more accessible, it'll always draw in new actors that were not capable with the former level of investment required.

What's more alarming is people giving these money. I consider myself quite AI-literate. I have professional artists around me that use AI in their pipeline and it's very useful. But it's obvious looking at the results (the examples you shared) that their blurbs is non-sense. They were not produced with the assistance of, they were created entirely by AI-tools. There was probably little to no revisions, and it probably only being rerunning the same prompt over and over with different seeds until they got a result they liked. You can tell by the look of it, it's generic AI art.

And again, if Steve, 54 years old, writes an adventure in a word document and shares it as PWYW and generated a cover with AI. I don't care.

But whenever I see these kickstarters (or the recent rise of websites and app being shared here) I always take 5 minutes to look at the content, and the majority of the time, the content is also written by AI. Which for me is a no-no.
