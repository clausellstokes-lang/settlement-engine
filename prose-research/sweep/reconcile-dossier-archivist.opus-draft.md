## 4. (d) THE ABSENCE RULE FOR THIS REGISTER

**The ruling, in two tiers.**

1. **Where the engine holds a recorded null — a field that exists and whose value is "no entry",
   "not held", "lost", "names nobody" — the absence is WRITTEN, in the clerk's third person, as an
   honest absence.** It is a sentence like any other and it is licensed by that field exactly as
   every other sentence is licensed. It fires rarely and in a different shape each time, and no
   dossier block carries more than one (DA-6).

2. **Where the engine holds no field at all — the world has not merely an empty value but no
   question — the slot is OMITTED, silently.** No line is written about it, and its omission is not
   itself announced.

**Why the ruling is two tiers and not one.** Ruling (4) gives every dossier element its full FORM,
and the owner's own qualification is decisive: full form is not full content, and a slot the world
has no fact for is an honest absence rather than a manufactured detail. But ruling (5) is equally
binding — no sentence may buy an effect with a fact — and `best-ai.md` fault 24 (15 documents,
STRONG, archival YES) names the inverse fabrication directly: a gap written where the world holds a
fact, or where the world holds no question at all, is a hole invented to look like a record. A
uniform "no entry" line would also be exactly hobb:19's metronome of lacunae and dnd:23's pretext,
and it would defeat leguin:4's placement rule that the gap lives **in the record and never in the
world**.

So the FORM is full — every element the world holds gets its entry, its slots and its typography,
sized by the place's own rank and never by its importance in play — and the CONTENT of a slot is
written, declared absent, or absent-and-unmentioned, in that order, according to what the engine
actually holds.

**The rate and the shape** are dnd:23's and hobb:19's: rare, specific, different in shape each time,
and correlated with something the simulation knows — a fire, a bad year, a change of office. The
proposed instrument and target are DA-6's (`apparatus rate`, 0 < rate ≤ 0.020 per variant in R1;
at most one dossier in three carrying one at all). **The rate is proposed, not measured** — no
current probe metric counts it, which is why DA-6 asks for a new one.

**What this ruling does not settle**: the DM page's empty slot (silent omission or the absence line
there too) is §7.Q5 and is not this register's to decide.
