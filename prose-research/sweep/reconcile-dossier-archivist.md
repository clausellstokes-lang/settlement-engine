## 4. (d) THE ABSENCE RULE FOR THIS REGISTER

**Ruling (4) asks this reconciliation to decide and state whether an empty slot is OMITTED or WRITTEN. For the dossier-archivist: WRITTEN — in three typed classes, never one line.**

1. **The civic lack (`none-exists`).** Where the world holds the fact that the settlement HAS NO X — no walls, no temple, no king, no word for the thing — that is a POSITIVE civic fact of the best kind and it is written flat and alone, in the clerk's third person, as the first half only, with no completing "but" (Le Guin 18 M2 the civic lack; Le Guin 17 the thing the record refuses to name; Hobb 5's first half at a rate the owner sets — Q19). It is licensed by the world-state field that holds the lack. This is R-DA-02's LACK move.
2. **The record's declared gap (`not-held`, with provenance).** Where the engine holds a typed absence in the RECORD — the roll for those years is lost, the register names nobody, the count stands uncorrected, the account was never given — the absence is written as a fact of the record in the record's flat voice (Le Guin 4 S3; Kay 11 S3's four forms — the unaccounted, the disputed, the reported-not-confirmed, the access-controlled; Hobb 19 S5; D&D 23 S4; Martin 10 S3; Tolkien F2 S5; ai fault 24 — "a gap in the record is recorded as a gap"). The licence is NOT rationed — a recorded null is always reported, as any field is; the CEILING binds only the elaborated apparatus sentence and its shape variety (no two of one shape in a dossier; never adjacent; never the opening move), and the RATE is the owner's number (Q5), this seat recommending ≤0.020 per variant in R1 to test. The gap is in the record and never in the world; a later regeneration may not fill a gap the record declared (THE PROMISE). This is R-DA-08.
3. **The slot the place's rank-form does not carry.** Where the element's full FORM, sized by the place's OWN rank (a hamlet's form has no ward list; a city's has), holds no such slot, nothing is written and the omission is not announced. This is not an empty slot but the absence of a question; ruling (4)'s "tiers by the place's own rank govern HOW" is what makes it lawful. D&D 16/18's findability survives here at the RECORD level only.

**What is NEVER written, in any class:** an absence where the world holds a fact (fault 24 in reverse — fabrication); a pretext (the Volo dodge — D 23, Baur 835); the hedged non-statement that covers ignorance — "keeps a low profile", "the matter is undocumented" — when no typed absence field exists (fault 29); a uniform "no entry" line at a fixed rate (Hobb 19's metronome of lacunae; D 23's "rare, specific, different in shape each time"); an absence that does not correlate with something the simulation knows — a fire, a bad year, a change of office (Hobb 19).

**Why three classes and not the Opus draft's two.** The draft's tier 1 ("a recorded null is written") and tier 2 ("no field at all → omitted silently") are right in direction, but they fold the civic LACK — a world fact — into the record's gap, and they attach a one-in-three RATION to the licence, which switches ruling (4)'s own mechanism off by a rate (the refuter's Ground 4 on DA-6, confirmed by this seat). The lack is a fact about the world and takes the LACK move; the gap is a fact about the record and takes the ABSENCE move; the missing question is neither.

**Full form is not full content.** Every element the world holds gets its entry, its slots and its typography sized by its own rank, never by its importance in play (owner 09-07 01:20); the CONTENT of a slot is written, written as a lack, written as a declared gap, or absent-and-unmentioned, in that order, according to what the engine holds. The DM page's empty slot is Q5's and not this register's.
