 Interfaces: An Anthology 
of Speculative Fiction

 ThriftBooks

 Powell's

 Biblio

 Hand picked by Ursula K. Le Guin, multiple award winning master of science fiction and fantasy, and Virginia Kidd, one of the field’s most knowledgeable agents and discriminating anthologists, this is a wildly eclectic bouquet of the best that speculative fiction has to offer. Hugo and Nebula winners Ed Bryant, Vonda McIntyre, and James Tiptree, Jr. have a free hand within these pages, as do the young writers who will be turning up on next year’s award ballots. Every story is appearing here for the first time, and every one is as unique an achievement as you would expect to find in the first anthology ever edited by Ursula K. Le Guin. This is a collection to be savored, a meeting of minds between fine authors and rare editorial excellence and imagination. This is Interfaces . 
 Originally published in 1980 by Ace Books, this collection is currently out of print.
