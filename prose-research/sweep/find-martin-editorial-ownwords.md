## Notes
- SSM998: Martin names the pipeline: editors' notes on the finished novel -> copyedit (grammar, syntax, spelling, internal consistency) -> typeset -> galleys. He refuses serial submission because he revises earlier chapters from later ones.
- Pajiba (relaying Groell's Suvudu Q&A): Groell tried to cut the repeated phrase "words are wind" (14 occurrences in ADWD); Martin "stubborn". Groell's initial edit of ADWD took two weeks, most of it back-checking continuity from previous books.
- SSM Heraldry: Martin's own account of Westerosi heraldry as an UNREGULATED institution — no laws, no college of heralds, no cadency rules; variation is individual choice; grounded in the Dark Ages / early Middle Ages. Directly on-point for herald-pools register.
- 1979 speech: an editor's letters quoted verbatim about Martin's own early prose — "facile way with words"; a story rejected as a "travelog"; another "eloquent, but not a story". Martin's own theory of a good editor: figure out what the writer was trying to do and help him do it better; never change without permission; the writer must have the last word.
- Reactor/Raets: praises Martin's chapter-by-chapter POV shifting; criticises Druon's omniscient narrator and Druon's inconsistent naming (name / title / territory) as forcing the reader back to the character list.
