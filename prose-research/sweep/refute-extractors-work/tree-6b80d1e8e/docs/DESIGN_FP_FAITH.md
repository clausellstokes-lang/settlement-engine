### 1b The twelve requirements, instantiated for faith (spine §1 — every WF wave
### carries all twelve or it is a defect)
1. **LAW ONE, faith's hardest reading:** the engine models BELIEVERS — shares,
   legitimacy, piety, readings, congregations — never gods. No theology, no doctrine
   trees, no miracle physics. The engine never confirms a divine act, never
   adjudicates whose god is real, never contradicts a DM's canon. Every mechanism
   below is a believer-side transition wearing the believers' own words.
2. **FORCES AND COUNTERFORCES:** every faith force names its opposition off the SAME
   evidence: unseating pressure ↔ incumbency (hold + tenure + backing); pilgrimage
   pull ↔ road danger; suppression zeal ↔ the martyr's backfire; the tithe ↔ tithe
   resentment; the wrath-reading ↔ the failed prophecy; the pact's accumulation ↔
   over-extension fraying [CORRECTED 2026-08-02 (fp-audit): WF-3's opposition is a
   force off the pact ledger, not the caps — caps and cooldowns are CONTAINMENT and
   this law does not accept containment as counterforce]. A faith without
   counterforce is a ratchet, and the estate's killer is the ratchet.
3. **DEPTH-DENSITY:** each wave below ships law, force, counterforce, belief
   posture, casting, receipts, pins (negative hardest), bands, couplings, clock,
   posture consumption, endings entries, and the dossier round-trip. The audit
   stage hunts shallow spots wearing deep clothing.
4. **NAMED-ACTOR RULE:** clergy already exist as planes (npcAgency religious roles
   priest/cleric/bishop/abbot/oracle; clergyTraitPlane's per-minister leans;
   rankings). Casting is read-wiring through those planes — never new NPC state,
   never a killed named soul, never a resolved fate. The exposure paths in WF-5
   obey this absolutely (§1c-4). **THE OFFICE-VOICE FALLBACK [CORRECTED 2026-08-02
   (fp-audit)]:** a deity-bearing settlement is NOT guaranteed a clergy NPC —
   deities mint through the custom-content path independently of the roster, and
   a thorp may carry a patron and no named minister. Where casting finds no
   ranking cleric, the receipt casts the unnamed OFFICE ("the priests of
   Greyfen") and the mechanism still fires — a missing name never silences a
   transition. Every casting wave (WF-1, WF-4, WF-5) carries a seeded no-clergy
   fixture proving both halves: the mechanism fires, and the office-voice receipt
   reads well without a named minister.
5. **THE ERRAND SPINE:** legates and named pilgrims ride SP-1 with typed purpose
   `religious`, DECLARED vs TRUE purpose on the covert/revealed seam, the K.2
   decaying snapshot, interceptability, and law M's week floor. The pilgrim SEASON
   is a flow read, not an errand (J-WF-2).
6. **DOSSIER COHERENCE (the crown):** every WF mechanism sources from settlement
   state and lands back in FaithSection / PantheonPanel / the Chronicle / the
   treaty document with a dossier round-trip pin. A faith system with no town-page
   trace does not exist.
7. **HISTORICAL MESSINESS, DERIVABLE:** the famine that becomes a wrath-reading
   that becomes a purge that drives a faith underground that resurges a decade
   later under a new patron — every link named, every receipt walkable backward.
8. **DEEP COUPLING:** every coupling this volume names (tithe→TRADE, pilgrim
   road→WAR, wrath-reading→POP flight, underground exposure→INFO, faith
   terms→GRAMMAR, mandate→INTERIOR) is declared in DESIGN_FP_COUPLINGS.md with
   reads, receipts, and counterforce. Couplings ride existing reads.

   week-denominated (STAIN_DECAY 0.06/tick ≈ an 11-week half-life; FLIP_TICKS 3);
   every NEW clock below names its cadence against INTERVAL_WEEKS and registers

   12-tick trap is cured law — WR-0c item 4 is precedent). Anything that moves

### 1c Faith-specific laws (standing law; violations are design defects)
1. **THE AGNOSTIC LAW (Law One sharpened):** no receipt, no term executor, no
   reading, no transition may assert a god ACTED. Believers read, courts judge,
   congregations move; the god never testifies. Even "divine schism" narrates the
   faith COMMUNITY acting (deityStanceLane.js:179-205 already holds this line —
   keep it everywhere).
2. **THE TWO-LANE GATE stands (religiousContest.js:17-33):** the LOCAL lane
   (per-settlement shares, patron seat, legitimacy, piety, mandate) runs on deity
   presence alone; the SPREAD lane (carriers, footholds, stances, sacred_claim)
   runs behind `faithSpreadEnabled` (with `religionDynamicsEnabled` its lockstep
   legacy alias, simulationRules.js:707-771). Every WF flag declares which lane it
   extends and ANDs accordingly (§3).
3. **NO LOCAL GENESIS (RELIGION_REWORK.md §2.7, standing):** spread-only; nothing
   in this program mints a deity. WF-5's schism splits a CONGREGATION and a
   TEMPLE, never a god. The deity doctrine (no premade pool) binds every fixture:
   test worlds author deities through the custom-content path
   (SET_PRIMARY_DEITY/IMPOSE_CULT, mutateEntities.js:832/:877), never by poking
   config.
4. **NEVER-RESOLVE-FATES in the dark places:** exposure of an underground
   congregation, the purge after a wrath-reading, the intercepted pilgrim — no
   path writes a named NPC's death. Consequences land on shares, legitimacy,
   facets, and circulation (flight into the wanderer register; recantation as a
   facet write). The nameless mass may suffer in banded arithmetic; the named
   never resolve.
5. **PREMIUM INHERITANCE (Feature D.0, LOCKED — GEOPOLITICAL_WAR_LAYER.md:335-341):**
   deities are premium custom content end-to-end; the pulse is premium by
   inheritance; a free user's god can never act. EVERYTHING in this program is
   therefore inert on free tier by construction. What each wave adds to the gate
   surface is stated in its dossier line and gathered in §3c. The two documented
   residuals stand unchanged and are NOT this program's to fix: the composer-lane
   UI-only gate (deferred Wave R-4 single-sourcing, settlementDeityHelpers.js:49-57)
   and the accepted D.0 client-only fail-open.
6. **NO ATHEIST ENDSTATE (religionState.js:73-77, standing):** the unaffiliated
   sink caps at SINK_MAX 45 — "a secular city, never a godless one." WF waves may
   hollow a faith; none may abolish faith itself.
7. **EXTEND, NEVER DUPLICATE (J-WR-11's idiom, imported):** worldState.religionStates
   and worldState.pantheon keep their ONE writer each; every WF state addition is
   an in-place conditional extension or a new conditional ledger with one new
   writer — never a parallel copy of an existing ledger.
