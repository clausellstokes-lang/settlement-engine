## Foundation additions (Phase 0 — serial, before any page)
1. MATERIAL LAYER: tiled paper-grain (light + dim), ink-bite edge weighting in
   plate/ornament SVGs. Static assets only; budget-measured.
2. MOTION GRAMMAR (~12 named behaviors, foundation-defined, token-pinned,
   prefers-reduced-motion-safe): lay-down page arrival · unfold panel reveal ·
   rule self-draw on first paint · hover = ink-darken (never lift) · instrument
   press · slip-in toasts/notes · almanac page-turn · seal impress-once ·
   medallion ink-pulse · warm-dim modal ground · draft-settle for AI proposals ·
   tally-strike. No bounce, no spring, no generic fade.
3. HERO MOMENTS (bespoke, beyond-recipe): THE ARRIVAL (forge→dossier delivery) ·
   THE FIRST ADVANCE (almanac page-turn + medallion pulses + report slip) ·
   THE EXPORT CEREMONY (seal meets medallion on the cover) · THE ROAD (Welcome,
   tuned). Each specified before build; each instant under reduced-motion.

## HERO SPECIFICATIONS (phase 0d — specs before build; lane copy of the annex)
> Written 2026-07-18 by the Deep Craft lane on claude/deep-craft. Every hero is
> composed FROM the motion grammar (design/organic/motion.js — the closed
> twelve; durations/easings are the tokens, never literals) and is INSTANT
> under prefers-reduced-motion (content mounts fully in the DOM at t=0 in every
> case; motion is presentation over complete content, never a gate in front of
> it — the retention law's a11y corollary). Naming note, recorded honestly: the
> dispatch brief calls the Welcome hero "THE SURVEY OF ONE SETTLEMENT"; this
> annex's Welcome entry specifies THE JOURNEY MECHANIC. Read as one design: the
> journey IS a survey, and the evolution series (one place, six ages — the
> MANIFEST ruling) supplies its stations. The annex entry stays normative.

### H1 · THE ARRIVAL (forge → dossier; ships with cluster 1)
- TRIGGER: generation completes and the dossier output mounts (the existing
  forge-completion render path; no new state, no store writes — a
  presentation-only orchestration class on the output container).
- SEQUENCE (the document laid on the desk, composing top-to-bottom): the config
  leaf yields to the output (existing view logic untouched); the dossier
  cartouche header LAYS DOWN (oc-m-laydown, t=0); its double rule RULE-DRAWS
  (oc-m-ruledraw, t≈180ms); then each successive top-level block of the dossier
  UNFOLDS (oc-m-unfold) on a fixed stagger (≈110ms per block, capped so the
  LAST block begins ≤1,300ms and settles ≤1,940ms — under the 2s law at any
  block count; blocks beyond the cap arrive together in the final rank).
- MECHANIC: one orchestration class (.oc-arrival) + a per-block index var
  (--oc-arrival-i) driving animation-delay; content is fully in the DOM and
  measurable at t=0 (selection, find-in-page, screen readers, and analytics
  see the finished document immediately).
- REDUCED-MOTION: the grammar's global collapse applies — the dossier appears
  complete, instantly. FAILURE MODE: if the animation layer errors, the
  document is simply present (both states are the end state; nothing waits).

### H2 · THE FIRST ADVANCE (Realm; spec only — builds with the Realm cluster)
- TRIGGER: the first committed advance of a session (the existing advance
  commit path; detection is read-side, never a new store field).
- SEQUENCE: the almanac strip's new page PAGE-TURNS in (oc-m-pageturn); the
  medallion of every settlement the tick touched INK-PULSES once
  (oc-m-inkpulse, staggered ≈80ms in reading order); the advance report
  arrives as a desk slip (oc-m-slipin). Total ≤1.4s.
- LAW: the pulse marks CHANGE, not spectacle — a medallion untouched by the
  tick does not pulse. Reduced-motion: page and slip are simply present.

### H3 · THE EXPORT CEREMONY (export sheet; spec only — builds with its cluster)
- TRIGGER: the export artifact is sealed (the moment the bundle/file is ready —
  the existing ready state, not a new one).
- SEQUENCE: on the cover plate, the house seal IMPRESSES ONCE (oc-m-impress —
  weight arrives, sets, done); the settlement's medallion INK-PULSES in answer
  (oc-m-inkpulse, t≈400ms — seal meets medallion, the counterseal rule); the
  dispatch line (format · size · date) SLIPS IN under them (oc-m-slipin).
  Total ≤1.3s. The download affordance is live at t=0 — ceremony NEVER delays
  the artifact (diegetic friction only where friction is the experience; a
  download is bookkeeping).
- REDUCED-MOTION: seal, medallion, and dispatch line are simply present.

### H4 · THE ROAD (Welcome — THE JOURNEY MECHANIC, "the survey of one
### settlement"; spec only — builds with the Welcome cluster)
- SUBSTRATE: an engine-rendered region map (parchment lens; REAL product
  output, committed as an SVG asset) is the page's traveling ground; the road
  is one SVG path across it.
- MECHANIC: the traveling line draws in oxblood via stroke-dashoffset bound to
  scroll progress (scroll distance ⇄ road distance, so the journey stays
  synced to content at any section count); the camera is a viewport transform
  gliding along path points (transform-only — GPU-cheap; no scroll-jacking:
  native scroll owns the tempo, the map answers it).
- WAYPOINTS: each existing landing section (Forge · the Brief · the Voice ·
  the living world · maps · pricing strip · covenant) is a station; on
  arrival its REAL DOM PLATE unfurls from the map (oc-m-unfold / oc-m-laydown
  — paper, never glow), carrying the section's existing content and
  functionality UNCHANGED. The evolution stills (one settlement, six ages)
  serve as station backdrops where the tier story is told — the journey is a
  survey of one place growing.
- REDUCED-MOTION: the full road renders drawn, the camera does not glide, and
  every plate is present in document order (which IS the DOM order — the
  boring-skeleton law). MOBILE: the journey compresses to a vertical road
  strip, same waypoints, FIELD posture. Ambient video loops stay taste-gated
  and optional; the SVG mechanic never depends on them.
