Yeah, that theory was put forth some years ago. It's one of those theories
that is so neat it *ought* to be true. But it can't be.

Severian and his pals were swimming in the Gyoll, so the weather was still
warm enough for swimming. As b sharp recently noted, "It is icy winter when
Severian encounters Triskele and Valeria."

Sev was outside trying to unclog a frozen drain pipe the day he found
Triskele. The dog ran away a week later, and Sev met Valeria. There was snow
on the ground that day. It was winter when Sev had the dog. There is no way
that Sev went swimming that week, and there is no indication in IGJ that it
was winter when Silkhorn saw the dog.
