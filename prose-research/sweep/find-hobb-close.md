- **Writing Excuses 11.Bonus-01 (transcript)** — Hobb's own words: galley/head/deck vs kitchen/bathroom/floor; the no-attribution dialogue test; sentence length per speaker; first person as natural voice; what an assassin notices vs a child; the pennypinching Charlotte derived from a tight household budget; the serving-boy reaction test.

## Round 2 additions (the high-value half)
- **Matthew Oliver, "History in the Margins: Epigraphs and Negative Space in Robin Hobb's Assassin's Apprentice", Mythlore 41.1 (Fall/Winter 2022)** — THE source for the dossier register. The epigraphs are "a formal history written in a distant, scholarly voice"; the main text is "the marginalia"; the style is "negative narration" (says what is NOT happening first — double negatives, sentences opening on what a character is not doing, "scarcely a page goes by" in the early chapters); the elevated fantasy register is named as "extensive parataxis, elevated figurative language, and parallelism"; the two registers are kept SEPARATE until "a poetic lyricism creeps into his style in the epigraph". Counter-evidence inside the same article: the dry objective history "feels less authoritative and satisfying" than the personal narrative, and Diana Wynne Jones satirised the device as "Gnomic Utterances". Route: live PDF is behind an interstitial (the 151,139-byte HTML decoy); Wayback `id_` capture + pdfminer worked. NOTE: the extracted text carries the PDF's irregular double spacing inside justified lines — two quotes preserve it deliberately.
- **Geoffrey B. Elliott, "A Robin Hobb Rereading Series" (elliottrwi.com, 2019– , 539 entries as of Sept 2026; MA thesis on Hobb)** — names the device as "the Asimovian move of grounding chapters in in-milieu reference materials"; catalogues what each chapter's in-milieu opening IS (an office gloss on King-in-Waiting; a technical note on herbs in Skill training; a formal AND informal message between two towns in the Rain Wilds front matter, which he praises for supplying "not just the encyclopedic, but the personal").
- **M Harold Page, Black Gate 2016 ("a dissection, not a review")** — two hard rules: "She only delivers description when it's part of a conflict" and characterisation served "when it is immediately significant"; the "unremitting rhythm of buts", "fractal"; and the DEVICE as a braiding rule — "a death resulting from the Thriller has domestic and child-rearing implications".
- **Vacuous Wastrel (tag archive)** — the sharpest statement of the household device: what Hobb spends pages on that other epic fantasy will not, e.g. "worrying about the apprentice fees for dependants"; "motivations are personal, and that consequences will also be personal"; "every level of the narration is imperfect". Also the strongest dissent on the prose: "her prose is unremarkable".
- **Robin Hobb, "What Works For Me" (robinhobb.com, 2022-05-20)** — her own compression sentence for four days of travel; "You can have a one sentence chapter if you want"; the thesaurus argument against "the same synonyms that every other writer is using"; "candy apple red with metal flake".
- **Jeffrey O, Substack 2024** — a reader's verdict on the archival register standing alone: "A pretty informative, yet boring, introduction epigraph".
