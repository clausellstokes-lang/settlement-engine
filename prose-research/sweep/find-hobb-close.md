## Notes per source
- **Looping Wor(l)d (Abalieno) 2025-09-22** — the only source that quotes a Hobb SENTENCE and argues from it (p.21 "brackish iodine smell"): the first person is LESS restricted than the child's experience. Also: dramatic events without melodrama; prose over dialogue; DENSE; slow pacing is the value; and a COUNTER on the device — "some actions don't seem to have any other consequence" in the last 40 pages.
- **Natasha Pulley, Tor.com/Reactor 2015-07-10 (rerun 2022-03-22)** — the best pure close reading found: a quarrel over the word "straw" carries both speakers' class histories; Hobb refuses the "narratively efficient" naming of a minor character.
- **Steven Erikson, Tor.com/Reactor 2016-04-27 (rerun 2022-01-27)** — naive/child POV; he rereads "line by line, to catch every subtle tell"; teaches the opening chapters as a POV set text.
- **Adam Whitehead, The Wertzone 2016-08-28** — states the reliability RULE: reliability falls off with distance from Fitz's perspective. Also a counter: "a tendency to meander".
- **Writing Excuses 11.Bonus-01 (transcript)** — Hobb's own words: galley/head/deck vs kitchen/bathroom/floor; the no-attribution dialogue test; sentence length per speaker; first person as natural voice; what an assassin notices vs a child; the pennypinching Charlotte derived from a tight household budget; the serving-boy reaction test.
- **The Arched Doorway 2014-11-24 (Wayback)** — Hobb's own words: setting as the fantasy writer's third element (1970s Chevrolet vs the horse and cart); "every sentence is freighted with character development, and setting, and plot"; the 250-page paperback binding constraint; first person tells only what the hero knows; tight third, one head per scene.
- **New Statesman 2018-07-27 (Wayback)** — Hobb on violence's consequences; reporter on names embodying virtue; Kyle Haven's greed → the family business.
- **Fantasy-Faction 2014-08-19** — COUNTER on adverbs; household↔polity coupling ("as their domestic situation worsens, so does the political situation"); morality as profit-vs-right.
- **HearWriteNow** — world information delivered "without it sounding explanatory"; one viewpoint per scene.
- **The Huntress And The Wolf 2026-02-15** — the reader must infer past the narrator; Fitz never narrates his own power; the room-inventory; history made "in bedrooms, kitchens, stables, sickrooms"; intimacy is not simplicity.
- **Dragonsteel Books 2024-12-19** — Hobb on wearing the villain like a coat.
- **lexlingua LiveJournal 2012-06-13** — reader review; debt repaid by a daughter's marriage.
- **Fantasy-Hive 2017-12-08**, **Grimdark**, **Quaint Book Nook**, **Omnilegent** — general reception, thin at the line level.
