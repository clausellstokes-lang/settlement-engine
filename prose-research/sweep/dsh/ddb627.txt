 Content Management Update

 Content Management Update

 by Andrew Searles 

 Oct 9, 2019 

 Category Icon 
 Old Changelog 

 15 

 A month and a half ago we released content management that allows you to choose which content you'd like to share.

 We've heard a lot of your feedback about the feature and there's still more that we want to do. As a short addition to this feature, we've added a couple of buttons at the top of the content management page that allows you too share all or block all of those sources. This allows you to easily deselect everything and choose just a few sources that you want to share.
