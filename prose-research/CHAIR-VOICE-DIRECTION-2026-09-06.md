# THE DIRECTION OF THE NEW VOICE — the chair's reading after the eight sweeps (Fable 5.1, 2026-09-06 15:05, for the owner; written before S12 so the reconciliation can argue with it)
Labels: CONFIRMED = measured in our corpus or verified against raw source text in a sweep; PLAUSIBLE = the chair's reading of the convergence. Nothing here is a rule yet; S12 writes the rules.

## 1. The verdict
The direction is right, and the research has made it narrower and more specific than the essay that proposed it. The archivist is a COMPILER OF A RECORD, not a narrator. The voice is defined first by what it refuses (the house reflexes we measured, and the AI catalogue's list), second by its RECORD APPARATUS (gaps entered as gaps, the document kind named, an office entered by what its holder does, evidence placed before it is needed and never labelled), and only third by any author's cadence. The beauty the owner asked for comes from exactness and rhythm and the glimpse, never from ornament: every exemplar that bears on a record register says so in its own words, and our own corpus already has zero ornament tells. Our failures are structural, house-flavoured, and measurable, which is the good news: the size of every move is a number.

## 2. What each body of findings contributes (PLAUSIBLE as a mapping; every cited feature is VERIFIED in its section)
- OUR OWN PROSE (PROBE_ALL, RULES-V2 B0; CONFIRMED): not AI-flavoured (zero em dashes, digits, questions, exclamations; real burstiness; none of the lexical tells). The dossier registers R1/R2 are the two LEAST like the voice bible, the chrome the most: the bible describes chrome, not the record. The gaps are one reflex ("rather than", 259 of 323 antitheses), the hedged qualification (", which is"), sentences that land on "it", flat rhythm, same-opener runs (the crier 0.42), and 407 of 708 pools uniform in shape. The NPC ladder opens "It is public that" 143 times and has almost no short sentence.
- LE GUIN: the discipline everywhere. Plainness as the noblest register; place through economy, technology and civic lacks; land first, the compass kept; the archive that must not read like all the notes (her own compendia carry that risk, and Horton names it). Binds the dossier body most completely of all eight.
- TOLKIEN: the civic record's apparatus. Compiled and translated, not authored; abridgement declared, conjectural dates flagged, the hall where each source lies; offices by function, an institution defined then recorded as lapsed, upheaval as headcount; a dense name-stock that "talks like maps" because it assures the reader the things exist. The annalistic voice binds the CHRONICLE LINE, not the body.
- MARTIN: the lexicon and the plain line. Native-root words, archaism as salt with a real date (one a paragraph), the concrete sensory noun, proverbs as the marks of a culture, names bent from real ones. His engine (rotating close third, cliffhangers) does not transfer; his one archivist mode does (the Archmaester's single voice). The chronicle he wrote has ZERO which-tails against our 0.051.
- KAY: the causal join and the withholding grammar. An entry may name a consequence and withhold its cause; what every inhabitant knows is not written; a lost document is entered as lost with the one thing known about its carrier; no lecture, no allegory, no map. Barred from the dossier: prolepsis, the elegiac sigh, the aphorism (the chronicle line may carry one of each).
- WOLFE: omission as information, fenced. Gaps stand; every withheld fact leaves its evidence in the record, placed before it is needed and unlabelled; the opening sentence is a plain civic fact whose grammar is exact; the closing is short and carries implication past the entry; institutions by procedure and silence. The hazard is his own: a record that hides its central fact from every reader has failed. On the dossier body this is a grammar of the record's gaps; the "stones tell a different story" glimpse lives on the DM page and in the Herald's information family, because truth prose never draws from the belief ledgers.
- HOBB: consequence on a household, the dry record on purpose, negative narration. The record cites witnesses, never the writer's acquaintance; warmth is supplied by the chronicle line and the Herald, not the archivist; economy at the settlement's scale; the object named, never the class. Role-bound by THE PROMISE: a household, never a named character's fate.
- D&D OFFICIAL: form, not voice. The house style is mechanical and says almost nothing about voice (one tone paragraph in nine pages; a 2019 revision supersedes the 2013 guide the first sweep read). What transfers: describe what stands there, no arrival, no reaction, no "appears to be" (uncertainty is a gap in the record, never a seeming); four sentences under a hundred words; atmosphere from named concrete things, evaluative adjectives for a people banned; the PLACE register (how official text describes a settlement) is still the one hole its critic names.
- THE AI CATALOGUE: the ban list, with a caveat. No gloss, open matters left open, a particular in every sentence, no feeling by formula, no antithesis reflex, no triad reflex, the plain lexicon, one name per thing. Every source measured FICTION; none measured the archival register we write, so the list is a floor, not a fingerprint of our failure. Our corpus already clears the lexical half; the structural half (the metronome, manufactured emphasis, closure forced) is where we still resemble it.

## 4. Examples
Each pair below is from the estate's own pools. Verdict labels are the checker's plus the Opus refuter's.

(a) THE REFLEX CURE, CONFIRMED (ill-1, DS-DEF-1 readiness WEAK, unfolding; the refuter's cure passes check-pair and preserves the claim):
BEFORE: {settlement} is getting by on not being tested. The margin has been narrowing rather than widening, and nothing in hand is closing it.
AFTER: {settlement} is getting by on not being tested. The margin narrows, and nothing in hand is closing it.
Why: "widening" names no sibling band, so the contrast was empty; the present perfect leaned on a duration the readiness band has no provenance for. Eighteen words for twenty-three, claim intact.

(b) THE QUALIFICATION GETS ITS OWN SENTENCE, CONFIRMED (ill-3, readiness ADEQUATE, street; the refuter's cure):
BEFORE: The town believes it could hold against the usual trouble and does not claim more than that, which is a fair reading of what it has.
AFTER: The town believes it could hold against the usual trouble. It claims no more, and that is a fair reading of what it has.
Why: the ", which is" tail goes; "a fair reading" stays, because the band is ADEQUATE and the hedge is the band's word. The first draft wrote "bears the belief out" and over-claimed.

(c) WHERE THE STYLE CURE IS WRONG, CONFIRMED (ill-2, terrain EXPOSED, visitor; REFUTED):
BEFORE: {settlement} sits open. There is no ground here that helps it, and everything the town has must be built rather than found.
DRAFT AFTER (refused): {settlement} sits open. No ground here helps the town, and what defense it has, it has built.
Why refused: "found" names the sibling band, terrain FAVOURABLE to the defender ("The ground does more for {settlement} than the town does"), so the contrast is the block's own discriminating fact and stays; and "must be built" is a standing law of the site, while "it has built" is a history the terrain field has no provenance for. The rule this taught, B-CLAIM: a reconstruction may spend punctuation, word order and a rationed phrase; it may not spend a claim, a modality, a threat class, or a pool's spread.

(d) THE USABLE NEGATIVE, CONFIRMED (ill-4, walls AND professional garrison, ledger; REFUTED):
BEFORE: ... it is not a posture rated for a long siege without stores behind it.
DRAFT AFTER (refused): A long siege without stores behind the wall is another matter.
Why refused: the DM loses the flat negative capability claim and gets a shrug; the corpus's only "rated for", the clerk's own word, was spent to buy an idiom that asserts nothing. The record may withhold a cause; it does not withhold the town's capacity.

(e) A CLEAN PASS, CONFIRMED (smoke #11, sovereignty · strained, ledger; the only smoke pair that passes the v2 checker):
BEFORE: Foreign coin stirs beneath {settlement}'s internal contests again, and the pledge of non-intervention frays at the edges where the coin lands.
AFTER: Foreign coin is moving under {settlement}'s quarrels again. The pledge of non-intervention frays wherever the coin lands.

(f) RHYTHM AND OPENER SPREAD, CONFIRMED as the defect, the cure not yet written: the treaty pool R12 reads "The week passed quietly." / "The week passed without event." / "A quiet week, with little to note." on consecutive weeks; the cure is four shapes for four lines, not new vocabulary. The model for the short end is already ours, in the generators' arrival prose: "The market is open, the streets are swept, the guards are at their posts. Something is wrong anyway."

(g) THE RECORD APPARATUS, ILLUSTRATIVE (sentences the verified rules license; not yet rewrites of any pool):
- the gap entered as a gap (Kay 8, Wolfe 8, D&D 7): "The founder's name is not kept." · "The charter's second leaf is lost; its carrier was a mill-hand of the river ward."
- negative narration as fact (Hobb 8): "No bridge stands at the ford. The tithe was not paid."
- the office by its work (Tolkien 36): "The reeve's duties are the stray stock and the muster roll; the title of warden is ceremonial and is recorded as such."
- the opening civic fact and the short close (Wolfe 22/23, Le Guin 16): open on the land and the works before any name; close on the thing, in a sentence of two words if it carries.
- place through economy, technology and lacks (Le Guin 15): one thing traded, one thing burned or built with, one thing the town has none of, in that order, and no lecture between.
- the document kind named (Hobb 4): a roll, a ledger, a minute, a saying; the reader learns the settlement's literacy from what kinds it keeps.

## 5. What I would push back on, and what I would hold
- Author WEIGHTS are the wrong instrument (already refused in RULES-V2 Part B). The unit is the REGISTER: the same author binds one register and is barred from another (Kay's prolepsis binds the chronicle line and is forbidden on the dossier body). S12 should produce a register map, not a blend.
- The "glimpse of truths the inhabitants do not know" is real and fenced: DM page and the Herald's information family; on the body it is only the record's own reticence. Wolfe's own hazard is the proof: concealment that succeeds against every reader has failed.
- The reconstruction is claim-preserving or it is not done: three of the five first illustration rewrites spent a claim to buy a style gain, and the refuter found a cure at equal or shorter length every time. The checker (v2, with R4-BAND and A11) and a refuter sit in front of the owner's sample now.
- The structure is a latent grammar (owner, 09-06): a closed set of move grammars applied as seeded variation, measured by a walker; the taste sample must show one block in two grammars or it has not shown the variation.
- What we keep against every exemplar: zero em dashes, digits, questions, exclamations; bands not numbers; the present tense that stands.
