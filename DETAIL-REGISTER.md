## PROJECTION DOCTRINE (chair amendment, owner framing: "overhead bird's view")
The corpus's detail-instruction layer (zoom/spec/study) is overwhelmingly **strict plan / top-down orthographic** — hf342 says so on its face ("STRICT TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW") and hf122 ("close-up PLAN study"), hf265 ("HULLS FROM ABOVE"). The convention throughout: **light from the NW, shadow falling SE** — crowns, roof planes, and wall bands all shade on their SE side. Three deliberate departures exist and must be named when cited:
1. **Profile micro-glyphs on a plan ground** — tiny elevation pictograms for things a plan cannot say: watch beacon (hf113), park pale fence (hf321), bridge side-view in the master key (hf303), smoke plumes (hf147, hf267), windmills/kilns (hf267, hf140). This is the corpus's licensed exception: the GROUND stays plan; the glyph stands on it like a token.
2. **Mixed-projection dress plates** — hf34 (near-plan tinted fabric + walls in low oblique + monuments in 3/4 elevation), hf32 (plan fabric + oblique crenellated wall rings), hf123 (fully axonometric bird's-eye), hf5/hf20 previews (oblique blocks). These teach CONTENT (what exists); their projection is NOT the build target.
3. **Teaching elevations** — hf313 mixes plan and elevation vignettes per gate type; hf376's "splayed hill" is an explicitly pictorial relief system offered AGAINST the plan systems.
**Build rule: our geometry is top-down; translate any oblique exemplar into the plan grammar of its class anchor (the zoom/spec plates), and keep profile glyphs only for the licensed token set above.**

## 2. ROOFS
**Exemplars: hf208-spec-roof-ticks · hf34-metropolis-capital · hf378-spec-density-ladder · hf90-thorp-plains (preview) · hf120-burgage-frontage-zoom**
- hf208 (REQUIRED-DETAIL ANCHOR — the roof law): gable = one ridge line down the long axis; hip = ridge stopped short with angled hip lines to the corners; cat-slide = off-center ridge, stepped eaves; jetty = second lighter dashed outline; cross-gable = ridges meeting in T; lean-to = half-width attachment with a single eaves tick. Its bottom row is the corpus's own anti-exemplar: "without the hand" (flat silhouettes) vs "with the hand" (ridge/hip marks + tint).
- hf34: proves the law scales — every suburb house at metropolis scale carries individual ridge/hip marks; monuments get 3/4-elevation roofs.
- hf378: roofs persist down the density ladder — even the 3-building "scattered" panel draws ridges.
- **Projection — THE PLAN-VIEW ROOF GRAMMAR (the translation guide for our top-down geometry)**: hf208 is drawn in plan and is the law. From directly above a building is its ROOF, not its walls: (1) outline = eaves line; (2) **gable**: one ridge line down the long axis, splitting the rectangle into two planes; (3) **hip**: the ridge stops short of both ends, with four angled hip lines running ridge-end to corners; (4) **two-tone plane shading**: the plane facing SE (away from the NW light) takes the darker tint, the NW-facing plane the lighter — one value step, not a gradient — plus a cast shadow tick along the SE eaves; (5) **cat-slide/outshot**: off-center ridge with a stepped eaves line; (6) **jetty**: a second, lighter dashed outline outside the ground-floor line; (7) **cross-gable**: ridges meet in a T or cross, valleys implied by the plane joins; (8) **lean-to**: half-width attachment with a single eaves tick against the parent wall; (9) **chimneys**: small squares drawn astride or beside the ridge (the lived-in stage of hf379 and the hf90/hf3 tier plates carry them; a smoking chimney is a square only — smoke is not drawn on sound houses); (10) **dormers: no plan-view dormer convention was observed on any viewed anchor — treat as not-established rather than inventing one**. hf34/hf123 render roofs obliquely — content references only. hf304/hf207/hf206 are wall-plan (footprint) mode, the OTHER licensed building projection: interior-bearing plans for monuments and type-sheets; a build chooses roof-plan for fabric, wall-plan for a labeled interior, never both on one building.
- **Setting details (REQUIRED)**: the toft behind the roof — privy X-boxes, wells, sheds, garden bed grids (hf120, hf90), fold-yard hoofprint marks and troughs (hf206), middens and wagon ruts in yards (hf341), encroachment porches pushing into the street line (hf120).
- Anti-exemplar: hf51-lens-vtt (deliberate flat-plot VTT register, no roofs/shadow) — legitimate product surface, NEVER a dress reference.
