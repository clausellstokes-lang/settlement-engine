The chair re-cut the line, and the re-cutting is the most vetoable act in this row.
The old line sorted by CLASS — deletion, paid surface, ceiling, persisted shape,
constitutional. That sorting is wrong, and the owner's own instrument says why: if a

cannot un-send a push, and it cannot give the owner back an experience a machine
already had on their behalf.
