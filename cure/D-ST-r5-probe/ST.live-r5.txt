## THE ANSWER, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

A SITE is a place on the land outside a town — a keep, a watchtower, a mage's tower, a shrine, an inn on the road, a bridge, a mine, a hospice, a harbour mole, a barrow. The DM places it (or adopts one the map offers) and names it; the engine never invents one. Whoever HOLDS it decides what it does: a town's paid garrison in a sound keep makes the towns near it safer, harder to besiege and quicker to hear trouble; the same keep held by brigands or a DM's menace makes every town near it less safe; a small flock of some god that keeps a shrine or a hermitage helps the towns that welcome its god and touches no town that shuts it out — never a hazard; an EMPTY site does nothing at all. Every benefit is bought with upkeep and shrinks with distance and neglect; every hazard grows with the strength of whoever squats there. Sites change hands the way towns do — abandoned when the money runs out, traded with their land, ceded in a peace, granted as a fief, taken by siege, cleared by a sortie or by the party — and the last town that cared for a site gets the first chance to take it back. A site outlives the town built over it. Nothing a site does is a second number: each effect is one bounded lean inside a quantity the world already keeps (the town's defence in a siege, the danger on its roads, the crime and raider pressures, the plague's care, a creed's standing, a route's cost, what a town has heard), so every decider that already reads those quantities — the war chooser, the siege, the caravans, the migrants, the passage planner, the faith contest, the plague — considers sites with no wiring of its own.

## §1 THE LAWS (ST1–ST20)

**ST1 — A SITE IS ON THE LAND; AN INSTITUTION IS IN THE TOWN.**  A site is a place outside a settlement with a kind, a name and a holder. It is never an institution: `institutionFounding.js` and the institution roster are untouched, and a town's own walls stay its institutions (already folded into its generated military score — `defenseLedger.js`'s header names the double count it cured). No second institution system.

**ST2 — ONE SITE TRUTH, ONE WRITER.** `spatialLedgers.worldSites` is the only HOST record of a site; it is the host half of the map's asked `WorldSiteHost`/`WorldSiteRecord` (ENC-HL-01..03). The map keeps its OWN site document for its own values ("Every other site value is the map's own", ENC-MS-01) and JOINS this record by the map's site id (`mapSiteRef`, §2.1), so the HOST facts — existence, kind, name, holder, status, position — have one truth, never two; the channels are ST-4's (DR-V14-1 for the record, H13's `PolitySite` for held sites, H9's events window for each dated status) . One module writes it — `src/domain/sites/siteLedger.js :: applySiteEvent(worldState, event) → { worldState, siteId, applied }` — called only by a CLOSED, pinned list of callers (the realm verb arm, ST's one stage, the war layer's keep leaf, PT's party door for `party_cleared_site` (ST-r2-19), the existing writers of §6.3's roads, and THE BODIES' ledger and stage  — a walker fails on any other importer); every write is a dated EVENT (law 1 of the host).  Drop-when-empty at every optional key and at the ledger; absent while dark.

**ST3 — THE ENGINE NEVER SPAWNS A SITE.** The DM PLACES a site, or ADOPTS one the map offers (map-tier until adopted; the adoption is the DM's act), or accepts "leave a ruin" on a dying settlement's card. The engine changes a site's FATE (who holds it, its quality, its state) and never its existence. Ids are minted zero-rng (FNV over the minting verb's event id — RN's deed-id idiom), never borrowed.

**ST4 — NO SITE DECIDES ANYTHING; HOLDERS DECIDE.** A site has no mind, no economy, no faction, no belief, no population. Its holder's own deciders act (a court funds or abandons; a menace's course is the DM's; a body — gang, beast or conclave — takes, keeps and releases a site by its own weekly choice, THE BODIES §4 ); the site only changes the numbers those deciders already read.

**ST5 — EVERY EFFECT IS ONE BOUNDED LEAN INSIDE A QUANTITY THE WORLD ALREADY KEEPS.** Defence (`homeDefense`), danger (the one presence term), crime and raider pressure (the gates and counterforces), readiness (the threat index), health (`care01Of`), faith (the contest's strength), magic (the arcane plane), route cost (`scoreRoute`), and what a town notices (THE PATROLS' `witnessedCrossing`, through ST's registered input). Each lean is a named `{ mult, reason }` row or a named additive term at one seam, clamped, DRAFT, UNMEASURED, on the FP-46 roster. No site has a number of its own that any decider reads; no second defence score, danger, crime, plague, faith or magic law.

**ST6 — FUNDING IS A DERIVED BAND, NEVER A COIN STORE; THE DM'S BAND IS A CEILING.**  A holder's DERIVED band is read each tick from what it has: a settlement from its prosperity label (`settlementFundingBand`, §2.4 — THE ONE settlement band, which THE PATROLS' pay gate reads too), a creed from its standing in the holder's seat town, a person from its purse band (CH's derived purse), a BODY (a gang, or a conclave holding as `faith`) from its own supplies and never above `meagre` — THE REPAIR CEILING, lifted for a conclave only by a patron court that endows its creed (§2.4; L-BD 31) — and a menace or a beast from nothing (its sites only wear) . The DM may set a band by hand (`fundingSet`), and it is a CEILING: the EFFECTIVE band is `min(fundingSet, derived)` on the order `unfunded < meagre < funded < lavish`, so a holder whose prosperity falls lets its sites go even under a DM's band — the owner's "these areas can also simply be abandonded due to lower prosperity" fires on every world now, before any treasury lights — and the DM's band can only hold a site LOWER than the holder's means (a lord who stops paying). THE DM'S LEVERS ON A POOR HOLDER'S KEEP ARE TWO, both through the record and neither a site override: the HOLDER — `SITE_HOLDER` hands the keep to a holder whose means pay for it (a richer court, a crown, a creed's seat) — and the HOLDER'S OWN MEANS, which are the settlement's prosperity and move only where the settlement's own economy moves (the landed upswing mover steps it, `upswingKernel.js`: "`economicState: { ...ec, prosperity: nextProsperity }`"; a DM act on the settlement where one exists — PLAUSIBLE, no landed edit of `economicState.prosperity` was found at master, `cure/D-ST-r3.md` Q1). Re-garrisoning a keep with a holder that is still `unfunded` buys it ONE season, never more: every holder change drops the want's clock (`wantSince`, §6.1 step 3) and the clock re-arms on the next unfunded read, so the new garrison walks away a season later, in the open, with its receipt; and EVERY HOLDER CHANGE DROPS THE DM'S CEILING (`fundingSet`) WITH THE CLOCK — the holder compared as its FULL IDENTITY (class · ref · role · creed, §2.1) — so a new holder starts from its own derived band and the DM's first lever works the tick it is pulled (a keep a lord stopped paying for, handed to `Prosperous` Ashford, is `lavish` at once; S3b) . A BODY holder's want is its own supplies: it keeps or leaves a site by its own weekly choice (THE BODIES §4, through ST's writer), never by this clock (§6.1 step 3). . COIN is the W-COIN slot (RULINGS-ROUND2 D): ONE upkeep law (`treasury.js :: computeUpkeep`, "ONE LAW PARAMETERIZED BY LEDGER"), widened by `UPKEEP_LEDGERS` members each with its caller in the same act — W-SEAT SEAT-8's `civilContests` · `interventions` (their charters) and FP's `sites` · `retainers` (CH) · `patrols` (PR): one roster, one law. FP's three ride ONE leaf-first car, because the frozen kernel's only call passes no worldState ("`advanceTreasury(sieged, { interval: tickInterval, tick: worldState.tick, deployment, blockade, rules: simulationRules })`"): the holder's roll is carried on the settlement record `advanceTreasury` already receives, written by the owning stage through its `settlementUpdates` and read the next tick (CH OD-CH-10's car; PLAUSIBLE, measured at that unit) — never a leaf inside `treasury.js` reading worldState, never a kernel line. When it lands, the derived band reads the PAID share. 

**ST7 — EMPTY IS NOTHING.** A site with no holder leans on nothing — no bonus, no hazard, no draw (the owner's seventh word). Its quality still wears, so a later holder inherits what neglect left. For an EMPTY site, a call-board entry, a dungeon's draw for adventurers or a "something stirs in the old barrow" line is the DM's to author, never automatic; a HELD den is not empty — its call is the attached court's need on THE CHAMPIONS' board (§7.2) .

**ST8 — THE UNTIED HOLDER IS A GENERAL HAZARD.** A site held by a force tied to no settlement — a GANG or a BEAST (THE BODIES' engine-born bodies), a DM's MENACE, a villain paragon — gives NO town a bonus and EVERY attached town a hazard, scaled by the holder's MIGHT × the site's quality × the distance band (the owner's second word: "noone gets bonuses but everyone gets varying degrees of negative"). A CONCLAVE is never an untied holder and its SITE is never a hazard: a creed's flock holding a site holds it as `faith` (§2.1; §3.1 says how its sign follows each town's stance toward the creed); a HOSTILE flock's harm — a creed a town curbs or outlaws may raid that town for supplies and bodies and take captives (RULINGS-ROUND5 B.2, the owner's words) — is its own ACTS through THE BODIES' rows, never a term of its site  . Danger enters as ONE registered feeder of MN's `presenceTerms` — MN §3.3's ONE COMPOSITION SENTENCE, which this volume cites and never restates (§3.4), built as the SHARED CAR of A.3 — so three dens on one road never triple the danger (requirement 2). A HELD suppressor site never lowers the war's own drivers: it SUBTRACTS only from the suppressible part (MN's `roadCountry`), floored at zero, inside the one σ shared with THE PATROLS under one 0.25 cap, and NEVER from the walls term or an enemy column (OD-PS-19 STANDS: an army in the land always latches), so a besieged town never falls out of the latch (`W_SIEGE 0.85 − SECURITY_MAX_RELIEF 0.25 − ENTER_THRESHOLD 0.55` leaves 0.05 of margin). A KEPT ROAD keeps a calm town calm where the suppressed raid part stays at or below the latch — the counterforce working, by design (RULINGS-ROUND2b; MN M50) — but it never ends an embattlement the raid still feeds: the landed step lets an embattled town go only "`if (rawT < T.EXIT_THRESHOLD && (now - priorSince) >= T.MIN_DWELL_TICKS)`" (`EXIT_THRESHOLD` 0.30), so a menace's raid or a `capable` den kept down to 0.35 holds it embattled for as long as it presses, its level easing toward 0.35, while a `modest` den kept down to 0.15 lets a town with a low ramp of its own (T₀ below about 0.18) go after the dwell (EXECUTED, `cure/D-ST-r3-probe/latchExit.out`)  . The latch also tells what each hazard IS: only an untied FORTIFICATION at hand (a `capable` gang or stronger in a sound keep) crosses `ENTER_THRESHOLD` alone; every other untied kind's feeder is a TIPPER AND A SUSTAINER — it latches a town already pressed by crime, exhaustion or a neighbour's war (`T₀ + p·(1 − T₀) > 0.55`), keeps an embattled town embattled, and otherwise moves no danger consumer — while its OTHER arms fire regardless of the latch: the gate rows and the counterforce on every attached town by its distance band, the readiness membership on the AT-HAND towns only (ST9 governs) ; its felt lines say only what those arms did (§14) . 

**ST9 — A FORT IS NOT A THREAT; A DEN IS.** The readiness index (`buildThreatByCid`) counts an untied site at hand by MEMBERSHIP: its at-hand towns join the index's `raidHot` set ("all reads are membership tests"), and a menace holder's towns are already there by MN's own membership row, so nothing counts twice — the town mobilizes against the den on its road . A held fort adds no readiness term: it is standing defence, not alarm. 

**ST10 — THE READY KEEP IS THE TOWN'S FIRST LINE.** A `fortification` at hand that is `sound` and held by a funded settlement garrison enters `homeDefense` as a LARGE bounded term (the owner's fifth word: "drastically"), named on the envelope (`siteDefense` = `KEEP_DEFENSE_MAX` 18 × the keep's ONE readiness value `siteReadiness01`, §2.4). The term is the keep's DEFENSIBILITY — walls, height and stores, manned by the SITE'S OWN COMPLEMENT: a standing garrison paid through the holder by the site's funding band, drawn from NO town's manpower pool and never from its riders (macro over micro: no roll-call, no per-soldier upkeep). The keep neither marches nor is drawn — the levy draws riders, never a keep. The men who ride the keep's stretches are THE PATROLS' riders, from the town's one pool (PR3), and PR's round walks a held keep's stretches first; they are DIFFERENT MEN, so the keep's road rows (§3.3) and the riders' rows are two feeders of ONE capped presence suppression (`min(0.25, σ_site + σ_patrol)`) and ONE counterforce composition — never a double count, the cap the runaway guard . The siege verdict reads it on BOTH cores (§4.1): the aspatial capacity roll directly; on every canonized world the feasibility gate, the ceiling and capitulation through `defenderCurrent`, plus a LONGER starvation clock — and NEVER through `relief01`, so a bigger keep never raises pFall (a pin, S19). **ST11 — UNSCATHED IS A STOCK THE CRISIS SPENDS FIRST.** In its function's crisis a ready site is spent before the town: a siege's ROLLED fall lands on the keep first (`sound → worn`, then `worn → ruined`) and only then on the town — never a fall the DM gave (a hearing seal or an HBF-86 pin), a capitulation or the `SIEGE_MAX_AGE` storm (§4.2) ; a plague overwhelms the hospice before the town's own care; a desecration strikes the shrine before the seated creed. **ST12 — EVERY POSITIVE NAMES ITS COUNTERFORCE.** Upkeep (the funding band's wear), resentment (a crown's garrison in a member's land — PL's control instrument), the siege it invites (a fort is a target), a traitor at the gate (ES's product), a den if the garrison is withdrawn. A den invites a clearing. Requirement 2, per kind (§3).

**ST13 — THE LAST TO ACT HAS THE FIRST CLAIM; NOTHING IS FORBIDDEN.** The record keeps `lastHolder` (the last settlement-tied holder) and the week it last ACTED — a DATED act only: `garrisoned`, `restored`, `reclaimed`, or the DM's funding set on its site (a passively derived band writes no event and is no act) . A preference, never a right (the owner's second word).

**ST14 — A SITE OUTLIVES ITS TOWN.** No settlement's death deletes a site (the owner's third word). When a holder or anchor settlement fails (a remnant status, a razing, an abandonment, a world imported without it), the site PERSISTS: holder → none if it was that settlement, an `abandoned` event, quality unchanged then wearing, its attachments re-derived to the surviving neighbours, and the dead court's first claim LAPSES. A dying settlement's card OFFERS "leave a ruin" — a `ruin` site at its place, the DM's act — with the dead settlement as `lastHolder` for the chronicle only.

**ST15 — WHO MAKES A DEN.** A den arises only when an UNTIED holder takes a site: an engine-born GANG or BEAST (THE BODIES — emergent, the world's own, born from a fact the world holds, BD2; WC-12's located raiders ARE THE BODIES' gang records, BD §19; until C17 lands, engine-born dens do not occur), or a MENACE — and a menace is DM-AUTHORED ONLY and never organically emergent (MN12; the owner's ninth word): a menace takes a site only where the DM's course sent it. Every lifecycle and scenario row says which dens are emergent and which are the DM's. 

**ST16 — PURPOSE AND DESTINY ARE THE DM'S.** A site has no purpose, no quest and no destiny unless the DM directs one; left alone it is consequence only (the owner's ninth word and the hosting doctrine). A watchtower town is never written into a telling's `partyIds`: that field means the INVOLVED settlements (RULINGS-AFTER-RESEARCH §5), and a town that saw from its tower was not involved; a tower's part in the rumor road's arrival records is a slot on IN's witness plane (IN's inbox), never a fake party. Nothing here reveals truth outside the omniscient arm or places a site's id where a settlement's belongs (the hazard MN7 cured). 

**ST18 — SITES ARE CONSIDERED EVERYWHERE, BY CONSTRUCTION, AND BY ONE READ WHERE A SITE MUST BE KNOWN AS A SITE.** Every effect enters a single read the deciders already consume (§7's COVERAGE CENSUS, pinned by a walker: no decider reads a parallel number); where a decider must know a site AS a site — the war chooser's target, the passage planner's path, the call board, the menace's course, a paragon's choice, the party's doors, the dossier, a hearing's WHERE — it reads ONE derived read, `sitesNear(worldState, settlementId)` / `siteAt(worldState, siteId)`; a hearing card names the site as its WHERE (`ENGAGEMENT_WHERE_KINDS`' `site`) and carries it on the `subject` dial. ### 3.2 The scale (every factor DRAFT, UNMEASURED, FP-46 roster)

- TIED term — an ADDITIVE term is `BASE[kind][seam] × S`, and a GATE ROW (a multiplier) scales its DEVIATION from one, `mult = 1 + (BASE − 1) × S`, where `S = F(funding) × Q(quality) × D(distance)` ; `F`: unfunded 0.15 ("walls without defenders") · meagre 0.45 · funded 0.8 · lavish 1.0; `Q`: sound 1 · worn 0.5 · ruined 0; `D`: at_hand 1 · nearby 0.5 · distant 0.2. A `person` or `faith` holder of a `fortification` counts at half (a lord-errant's hall is not a garrison).
- UNTIED term — additive `BASE_H × S_H`, gate row `1 + (BASE_H − 1) × S_H`, with `S_H = M(might) × Qu(quality) × D(distance)`; `M`: thin 0.2 · modest 0.4 · capable 0.6 · strong 0.8 · formidable 1.0 (MN's might words, the J-CNC-5 keys); `Qu`: sound 1 · worn 0.65 · ruined 0.35 (a ruined site gives the mildest hazard, the owner's second word as the chair ruled). So "a sound keep of brigands at the gate is a crisis and a crumbling barrow three days off is a rumour" (ruling 16b).
- Per seam, per town: the terms of several sites COMBINE BY MAX within a sign (no stacking; requirement 2) and the result is clamped to the seam's cap below.
