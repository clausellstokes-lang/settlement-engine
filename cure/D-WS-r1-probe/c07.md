### 5.3 Long advances
A month, season or year advance runs the defaults: the derived way, the plan where one stands, the season's draw and latch, no hearing (requirement 24 — the war's own hearings run on their defaults). A year advance produces at most four deviation receipts per war (one latch per season) and their lapses, folded by the herald's landed digest. A pin is a week's act and never runs inside a long advance.
