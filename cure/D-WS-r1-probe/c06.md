## §4 THE BUNDLES — how a way leans the choices the world already makes (mechanism 2)

### 4.1 The rule
Every lean is a factor in [1 − `WS_LEAN_W`, 1 + `WS_LEAN_W`] (DRAFT 0.12, UNMEASURED) multiplied into ONE existing input of ONE existing fork, in the landed bounded-factor idiom (`coalitionLoad.factorFor(move)` — "a bounded, clamped multiplier … ×1 exactly … when politics is dormant"). A term unlisted for a way is ×1. The way reads TRUTH for its own court and BELIEF for any foe (§6). No lean may add a move, a target or a legality; the chooser's own text binds it: "no channel can add a target, turn a friend hostile, or bypass a legality gate" (`settlementStrategy.js`, WR-2's placement). Two kinds, by what the host already reads (A.8):
- THE HOST LEAN — on a host that ALREADY reads the court's character through `computeAggressiveness`'s drive (C1 the chooser, C2 the posture ramp, C6 the raid weight): `wayHostLeanOf(fit, way, term)` — the bundle's sign × W × the fit's STRENGTH share. The temper reaches these hosts ONCE, through the drive they already sum; the way adds only what the court HAS. A way chosen by the temper alone leans them ×1. [CURED 2026-10-20 (fp-final P6 r1) — WS-r1-1 (A.8); probe `cure/D-WS-r1-probe/deviation.out`: a temper-only fit leans `deploy` ×1, a half-and-half fit ×1.06, a strength-only fit ×0.88 on a `−` cell.]
- THE PRIOR LEAN — on a host that does NOT read the drive (C4, C5, C7–C12): `wayLeanOf(wayPriorOf(…, consumer), term)` — the whole bundle at W. Each such host CONFIRMS at its own unit that its inputs carry no second copy of the court's character (a host found reading the drive, the posture or a WR-2 channel for the SAME decision takes the host lean instead) — a ROUTE to each sibling.
Every lean is ×1 where the way is REPORTED, not applied (below the floor, or its GATE dark — §3.3), and a deviated war applies its lean at half weight (`WAY_DEVIATION_KEEP`, §5.2). The way never leans inside a landed chooser it reads — W-DOCTRINE-1's mint and instrument (HBF-16), W-DOCTRINE-3b's web, WR-8's march order (WS3).

THE CHOOSER'S ONE TERM rides the shared-signal car (fold §3.1.23; OD-FC-59; the ODQ's closing addition (a) of 10-19 names WC its owner): ONE registered term `way` in the car's context read, applied after `dispositionThresholds` and before the softmax, on the four base moves only, as a HOST LEAN. WS writes no line in `settlementStrategy.js` (762 effective); it REGISTERS its term with the car's owner (a ROUTE to WC), and the car admits no claimant that re-consumes an input the chooser already applied (WS-r1-1's half for WC and HB-7). ### 4.3 Surface · scrutiny · machinery
SURFACE: nothing per lean (a lean is never a sentence). SCRUTINY: the war card's Why line names the way and, for a chosen move, the lean that tipped it ("the long levy kept the host in the field"); a REPORTED way says so ("read, not yet in force: its costs are not kept in this world"). MACHINERY: the factor and its term.
