## §7 LEGIBILITY — what the DM sees, and where

- SURFACE (the dossier, Power or War tab, one line, only for a court that has warred by the war-memory read of §3.3): a deeds sentence of its way, built only from acts the world recorded — "Kell makes its wars long: when its host comes home beaten it raises another, and keeps it in the field until its enemies tire." A court never at war shows nothing (no forecast; the Evidence Law). A REPORTED way (below the floor, or its GATE dark) shows the same deeds line with no claim that it is in force.
- SURFACE (the deviation deed, in the herald's feed for that war, the week it is latched): §5.2's sentence.
- SURFACE (the shift, the age receipt): "In the year its old prince died and the temple council took the seat, Merewick turned from raiding to its walls."
- SCRUTINY (the Why panel, on purpose): the way's three strongest cells (§3.3) in words; the source (`derived · dm_set · dm_pinned · campaign_plan`); whether it is in force or only read ("its costs are not kept in this world"); each foe's BELIEVED way of this court and this court's believed way of each foe, side by side with the truth (the DM's view only).
- MACHINERY (never pushed): scores, factors, the bundle table, the draw.
- THE MAP: nothing new. The way is not drawn; its consequences (where hosts go, raids, sieges) are drawn by the layers that already draw them.
