## §1 THE LAWS (WS1–WS19)

**WS1 — ONE READ, DERIVED; NEVER A STORED TEMPERAMENT.** A court's way of war is `wayOfWarOf(court)`, derived on every read from records the world already keeps; no temperament, doctrine or strategy field is stored for a court. The stored things are the DM's own setting (P1, the owner's shape), the season's latch of one war's deviation (P4, the owner's shape — a fact about a war, never a court's character) and, only if the instrument proves the derivation flickers and only by the owner's word, a contingent memo (P3). "Temperament" is the world preset's word (`temperamentPresets.js`), PS's word for a court's alignment and `momentum.js`'s word for its NPCs' momentum; "doctrine" is a four-way homonym (fold §5); this volume uses neither. The way never adds a move, a target, a legality, a mechanism or a second chooser; it reorders what the world already weighs. A way's leans on hosts that do not read the drive (MG, PS, CH, GR, PR, ES, C4) are its whole bundle, each host confirming at its own unit that its inputs carry no second copy of the court's character. The week it moves, the crossing receipt says so and names the cause; the age receipt names every shift of the century (requirement 16). Inertia is the inputs' own; there is no second decay law (SP-B). **WS9 — THE WORLD HOSTS THE CAMPAIGN.** A way of war is the world's own. It never targets the party, never reads the party as a term, never schedules a war, never forecasts one. The party meets a court's way only as the court's deeds.

**WS10 — NO HEARING OF ITS OWN; THE WAR'S HEARINGS CARRY IT.** A way is not an engagement.
