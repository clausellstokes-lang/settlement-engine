## §8 THE HEARINGS (requirement 24)

THE WAY OF WAR OWNS NO ENGAGEMENT-REGISTRY KIND. A way is a court's own disposition, not an engagement between settlements, and a shift or a deviation is a court's own act; neither pauses the world. The war's own kinds carry it (registry rows of record in the fold §3.4; WHERE from `ENGAGEMENT_WHERE_KINDS` = `settlement · road · country · site · border_segment`):

| kind (owner) | WHERE | CLIMAX / STANDING (the fold §3.4) | what this volume adds to its card | the DM's three choices (requirement 24) | the DEFAULT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `war_declaration` (the war volume) | `settlement` (the declaring court) | CLIMAX | ONE line: the declaring court's way for this war and the foe's way AS THE DECLARING COURT BELIEVES IT; the season's turn and its cause, if latched | LEAVE (the derived/latched way) · TILT (pin THIS WEEK's deviation: one P2 pin on `war_way_deviation` — it binds every court's deviation this week, VISIBLE, OD-WS-2) · DECREE (`declared · stayed`, the kind's own words) | the derived way, turned by §5's draw; `declared` or `stayed` by the kind's own default |
| `siege` (the war volume) | `settlement` (the besieged town) | STANDING (every week) | the besieger's and the defender's ways for this war (the believed one for the other side) | LEAVE · TILT (this week's pin) · the kind's own decree (`holds · falls · relieved · lifted`) | the kind's own; the way unchanged |
