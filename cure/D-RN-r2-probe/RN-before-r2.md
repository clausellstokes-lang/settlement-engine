**The answer in one paragraph.** Everyone the world remembers — an ordinary townsman, a high priest, a ruler, a champion, a villain, the party — is known and judged by ONE model. Four facts about a person are kept apart and never merged: how widely they are KNOWN (fame), how each other actor FEELS about them (regard), whether their WORD is trusted (credibility), and, for a ruler only, their RIGHT TO RULE (legitimacy, which renown never writes). Fame comes from office ("the High Priest of Kell") and from deeds; every deed is written down once, as lived history, with how far its tale could travel, and fame fades over years as the tale is forgotten — a town knows a person only as far as word of them has reached it. Regard is sparse: an actor feels something about someone only when the DM says so (the allies-and-enemies list, a champion's authored ties) or when it has heard of something they did; it reads every deed through two lenses — what the act is, judged by its own alignment, and whom it helped or hurt, judged by its friends and rivals — with its personality setting the mix, so a good rival grumbles that its enemy was helped and an evil one keeps the grudge. A court feels what its people of power feel, weighted by their power, plus what was done to the court itself; each of its acts comes from the officer whose office does that work, so on scrutiny the why is that officer's grudge. On the surface the DM reads words ("known across the river towns as a fair judge"; "Varn's lords curse the party for saving their rivals; their people do not"); bands, weights and reasons wait for an intentional look; and the DM can set any actor's regard at will. ## §1 THE LAWS (RN1–RN16)

**RN1 — FOUR FACTS, NEVER MERGED.** FAME (how widely a person is known), REGARD (how each actor feels about them), CREDIBILITY (whether their word is trusted) and LEGITIMACY (a ruler's right to rule) are four quantities with four homes, and no read, band or sentence composes two of them into one. Fame is RN's derived read (§4). Regard is RN's sparse ledger plus its derivation (§5). Credibility is the landed `npcCredibility` law for persons, a holder on the party's sub-ledger for the party (§7), and the landed court stock for courts (never touched by RN). Legitimacy is `governanceLedger.js :: governanceLedger`, which RN never writes. "Infamy" is not a fifth fact: it is fame read beside a negative regard, on read, for one surface (RN4).

**RN2 — DEEDS ARE THE STORE; LIVED HISTORY IS IMMUTABLE.** Every renown fact that can change traces to a DEED: one append-only record in `spatialLedgers.deeds` (the `concludedWars` idiom: "APPEND-ONLY … never rewritten, folded or pruned — that is lived history"), minted with a zero-rng id that is never borrowed from `outcome.id` (R2 hazard 6: two removals in one settlement share an outcome id and would merge as one rumor). A deed's projected scope is computed ONCE at mint and frozen (§3.2). The DM's STRIKE writes a `struck` mark and the deed stops counting everywhere; the record stays (THE PROMISE; the landed party door has `undoState:'none'`, so the mark is its undo). A CORRECTION is its own deed carrying `correctsRef`, and it REPLACES the version it corrects wherever it is heard. Nothing is stored per NPC beyond what an office already gives.

**RN3 — FAME IS BELIEF, DERIVED ON READ, AND FADES OVER YEARS.** A person's fame derives from OFFICE (a ruler, a high priest, a guildmaster is known by position) and from the DEEDS they did, each deed contributing its frozen projected scope decayed on the spine's banded-stock half-life (`bandedStock.js :: decayTowardNeutral`, band `a_decade`, DRAFT) — no second decay law. A town knows a person only as far as word of them has reached it: the observer read asks whether the town lies inside a deed's projected reach and whether the tale has had time to arrive (`distancePricedNews.js :: hopDelayTicks` from the deed's witnesses). Fame is never stored per town and never stored per NPC. The OBSERVER read decays with the same ordinal: a deed is known at a town only inside the ring its DECAYED scope still covers — its whole reach while undecayed, within the rumor road's notable hop budget (2) of its witnesses once decayed to `regional`, at its witnesses only once `settlement`, nowhere once `unknown` (§4.3). In the road presets a town knows a deed only if it HEARD it — a live telling in its rumor ledger, or a heard term the person stage wrote there — so fame and regard agree at every town; the projected scope stays the deed's fame BAND and the omniscient arm's knowing set. **RN5 — REGARD IS SPARSE, EVENT-BORN, TWO-SIGNED, BANDED, WITH ONE PLAIN REASON.** An actor feels something about a subject only when an AUTHORED seed (the DM's allies-and-enemies list; a champion's authored relations) or a DEED it has heard of creates it; nothing is pre-populated. A regard holds two signs apart (warmth and grudge, the `factionPairStates` idiom, so "loved and hated" is expressible on scrutiny) and reads as one band on the seven-word ladder (§2.4); every change keeps ONE dominant reason in the world's words (the typed incident, concern 9b). A town's or a court's feeling about ANOTHER town or court is never an RN entry: it is the landed pair state (`relationshipState`; law E14), and RN's writer refuses such a pair. **RN6 — ONE DEED MOVES ONE HOLDER ONCE PER VERSION HEARD; NO RATCHETS.** A holder's regard of a subject carries at most one term per deed; a later, better or corrected version REPLACES that term, never adds (concern 9c).
