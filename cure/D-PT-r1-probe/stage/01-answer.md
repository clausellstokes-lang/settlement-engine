**The answer in one paragraph.** The party is the one actor in the world the engine never moves. Its choices are made at the table and enter the world only as DEEDS the DM records with the act already in hand — the night's event, with marks that fill themselves in (the party's hand; who backed it, pre-filled only when the party is doing the work of a request it accepted; how it looks on its face) and a fourth (whether the reason was kept quiet, told or shown) that defaults to told; the DM changes any of them with one touch and never has to mark anything at all. A told reason carries only what the DM marked: if the DM says the burning was just, those who hear why may believe it, and those who never hear why judge the act alone. A toggle says where the party is, and the party's last deed places it on its own whenever that word is the later one. Word of each deed travels the roads like any other tale — in the river towns they may only hear that the party burned an orphanage, never why — and every town, court, faith and person who hears it judges it by its own lights (THE RENOWN). Those who have heard and care then come for the party: to ask its help with what they need, to offer work or a title, to summon it, to turn it away at the town's gate, to post a warrant or a bounty, to lie in wait, to denounce or praise it in public. Each is a hearing in its week only: the DM lets it reach the table, strikes it without a trace, or amends it; left alone, nothing has happened to the party yet — the searcher is still searching, the ambushers still waiting — and the world goes on: the plague spreads, the siege runs its course, patience wears thin, and a summons ignored becomes a warrant, then a bounty, then hunters on the north road. When the party says yes, the need's own course is the clock; a promise the party could have kept and did not costs it, one it could never have kept costs nothing, and a party that went where it was needed in time is never blamed for a box the DM forgot to tick. The DM's window puts the party first — what its deeds have done, who knows of it and what they think, who is looking for it and what it owes — in the world's words; the bands and the odds wait for an intentional look.
