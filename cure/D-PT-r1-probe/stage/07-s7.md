### 7.2 What the party is known for (`partyKnownFor`) and THE CALL BOARD
The party's unstruck deeds map to CH's eight domains by their kind and target (a disease stressor resolved → `health`; a raider or monster stressor, or a menace ended → `wildland`; a war or siege stressor → `war`; a brokered relationship → `diplomacy`; a faction bolstered or a crime condition cleared → `order`; a market shock eased → `trade`; a faith condition cleared → `faith`; an attacker named → `information`) — DRAFT, one closed table. CH's `callBoard(worldState, snapshot, observerId) → { [domain]: Call[] }` (`DESIGN_FP_ARCH_CH.md` §14; each call `{ settlementId, domain, side, band, source }`, `band` a `SEVERITY_LADDER` word `glancing · telling · grave · ruinous`) is the source of every REQUEST. The REQUESTER is the court of the call's own `settlementId` (a court knows its own needs by presence); with PL lit, a liege may send instead for a sworn member's need where PL's `crownReachAt(…).share01` at that member is above zero and no engagement for that need is open (one engagement per need). It sends when it has heard of the party (`fameIn` ≥ `settlement`), the party is known for the call's domain, and its regard of the party is `indifference` or warmer: one seeded draw a week at DRAFT `0.2 × (rung + 1) / 4` (`rung` = the call's band index, glancing 0 … ruinous 3). A paragon of the call's domain already at work there is a REASON on the card ("a healer of renown is already at work in Tolle") and halves the draw (DRAFT) — it never closes the request: a request closes only when its NEED closes by its own state (CH §14: "never CLOSED by the answer"). **Escalation** (PT11): a `party_summons` that closes unanswered by the term's end mints, the same week, a `party_warrant` (its `causedBy` the summons); a warrant unserved while the party is outside the lands mints a `party_bounty`; a bounty mints `party_hunted`.
