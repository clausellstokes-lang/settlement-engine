## §9 THE NET EFFECT, THE TOWN'S GATE AND THE BAN (PT-1, PT-6)

### 9.1 The net effect at a meeting (`netEffectAt`)
At a town, the voices that bear: the town's commons (`s:`); its court (`c:`, RN's body derivation — which ALREADY carries the governing faction, through the ruler's weight `1 + factionPowerShare01(governing faction)` and its seats, and, with PL lit, the crown's regard by the paramount's reach into this member, RN §5.3); each NON-governing faction holding DRAFT ≥ 25% (`factionPowerShare01`, its `f:` body); and the creed whose standing there is `established`/`ascendant` (its faithful). There is no separate crown voice and no separate governing-faction voice — each is inside the court's; on scrutiny the court's reason names them ("its lord, and the crown behind him"). In the dark person arm (every preset today) a faction or creed body whose derivation reduces to the commons' — no authored baseline and no institutional record of its own (RN §5.3) — adds NO separate voice, because it would say exactly what the commons say. Each voice's band × its weight (commons 1 · court 1 · a faction its share · the creed its standing weight) → one verdict band; voices that differ by two bands or more are SHOWN as a conflict ("Kell loves the party; its lord does not"). Derived, never stored. **Surface:** the one sentence; **scrutiny:** each voice and its reason (RN's one plain reason); **machinery:** weights on intent. Pin: a one-faction town has one non-commons voice.
