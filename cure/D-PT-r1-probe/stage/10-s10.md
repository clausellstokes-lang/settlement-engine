## §10 SANCTION — THE BACKING (PT-3; `partyBackingEnabled`)

### 10.1 Reach and weight (`partyReach`) — the owner's ladder
| `backerRole` | Reach | Weight there |
|---|---|---|
| `town_power` (with `factionId`) | its own people in that town | `factionPowerShare01` ("relative influence in the settlement") |
| `town_court` | the town and its lands | `governanceLedger` legitimacy band |
| `liege_court` | its chain below it (`polityOf(...).path`) | PL's ONE reach read with the liege as the head of its own sub-tree (PL §3.1: "every lord is the head of its own sub-tree for this read"): `crownReachAt(worldState, snapshot, liegeId, memberId, observerId).share01` — 1 at the liege's own seat, `POLITY_LIEGE_SHARE[band]` at a direct vassal, the paramount-reach band map below it, 0 under a `frayed` hold |
| `paramount_court` | the polity | the same read with the root as the head: `crownReachAt(worldState, snapshot, rootId, memberId, observerId).share01` |
| `creed_clergy` (with `creed`) | its faithful wherever they stand | the creed's standing there (`cult · established · ascendant`) — never the god |

The observer is the HEARER's town (belief: the hold as it believes it). PL5 binds the chain: "the vassal of my vassal is not my vassal — except as far as the crown's hold reaches" — a liege's or a paramount's backing reaches a sub-vassal only by that read, never by walking the chain at full weight. With `polityEnabled` dark the two polity roles are not offered. ### 10.2 What it does (RN §6.2 reads it; PT supplies the reach and the ONE term)
Inside the reach AND where the backing part of the telling is visible: a villainous-looking act softens toward excusable, a good one is amplified — ONE bounded term per reading, DRAFT ±0.25 of lens one's magnitude × the weight. This term is the ONLY carrier of a backing in any reading: RN's value rows carry no backing shift (RN §6.1 struck `remove_npc`'s "0.6 inside a backing's reach" in round 1). SHARED OWNERSHIP: the backer earns a half-weight term of the credit (RN §6.2, the backer's key by RN §2.2's backer table) and the backer's enemies a grudge toward the party ("the duke's arsonists"); a reason EXPOSED as false — a `correction` the DM marks `contradicts` (`SEND_WORD(…, { contradicts: true })`), or IN's exposure (C1) — lands on the backer too and costs the party `LIE_FALL` (an exposed lie is RN §7's lie charge). Nothing else exposes a reason: the reason is the DM's prose, never parsed, so "contradicts" is never computed (Law One). ### 10.3 The counterforces (each named; each a hearing where it acts)
Outside the reach — the act judged alone · where the backing part fell away (§11) — judged alone · a rival's COUNTER-BACKING — a rival authority's own act against the backed deed: `party_warrant`, `party_bounty` or `party_banned` from that rival, its reason "they do the duke's work" (never RN's public voice, which is `party_voiced`) · `backing_voided` (CLIMAX, `voided · upheld · struck`; minted the week the higher authority's town first hears the backed deed, once per deed, §7.3; default `voided` when the higher authority regards the backer at `mistrust` or colder or the party at `enmity` or colder, else `upheld` — DRAFT) · `backing_repudiated` (CLIMAX, `repudiated · upheld · struck`; minted when `seatChangedSince(backerSeatId, deed.tick)`, §7.3; default by the new ruler's regard of the party and of its predecessor, the same thresholds). A void or a repudiation travels as its own deed (`party_event`, doer the voiding court, target the party, `voidsRef` the backed deed, significance `notable` so the news stays local — DRAFT); from the week a hearer's town hears it, that hearer re-reads the backed deed with the backing part masked (RN's re-read of a version), inside the voider's reach only. A backing never expires on its own.
