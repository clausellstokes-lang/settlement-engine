### 11.2 The truth chasing the rumor
`SEND_WORD(deedId, by, { contradicts? })` (the party, the backer or a witness town) mints a `party_event` `correction` deed with `correctsRef` (RN §3.3), its witnesses where the teller stands, its significance one step below the original's (less tellable, so retold less), its proof as marked. It rides the same road at the same speed and is read, wherever it arrives, as the ORIGINAL deed with the parts visible in EITHER telling — so it REPLACES the version heard (RN6) only where it brings a part the hearer lacked (the reason, the backing); a correction whose own telling lost the reason and the backing brings nothing and replaces nothing, so a grudge is never erased by a bare "it was not so". With `contradicts: true` it is an EXPOSURE of the original's reason (§10.2). It weathers like every telling — past about two hops it mostly arrives without its reason (15.5% at two, 2.4% at three, EXECUTED) — and so it loses by its later start, its narrower scope, the weathering and the credibility test, never by a slower road. ### 11.3 What a told reason does — it carries only the DM's marked weight (PT13; PT owns the field, RN reads it)
The reason's content is the DM's prose, quoted and never parsed, so the engine cannot know what it argues. What it CAN carry is the one thing the DM told the engine about the act: `moralWeight`. Therefore: where a deed carries a marked `moralWeight` AND a hearer's version shows the reason part (proof `spoken` or `shown`, completeness at or above the reason's threshold), lens one reads the act MOVED TOWARD the marked band by how far the hearer believes the teller — `kind01_read = kind01 + (marked01 − kind01) × belief`, the same for `law01` and `force01`, where `marked01` is the marked band's centre (DRAFT `heroic` 0.92 · `admirable` 0.73 · `neutral` 0.5 · `shameful` 0.27 · `villainous` 0.08) and `belief = clamp01(weight × proofFactor)`, `weight` the doer's credibility weight AT THE HEARER (`partyCredibilityAt(…).weight` for the party; neutral = 1.0) and `proofFactor` DRAFT `spoken` 0.6 · `shown` 0.9. Everywhere else — no weight marked, the reason weathered away, or `unspoken` — the hearer reads the derived inherent value: the act alone. The DM's own view always shows the marked weight. Consequences, each pinned (§17): the zero-click default `spoken` softens nothing unless the DM marked a weight, so no deed carries a phantom reason; the owner's orphanage burned for the infestation and TOLD is believed by most who hear why (a discredited party less); burned "without telling anybody" (`unspoken`) it reads villainous to every good hearer; three towns over, where the reason fell away, it reads villainous too.
