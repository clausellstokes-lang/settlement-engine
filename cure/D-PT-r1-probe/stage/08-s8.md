### 8.5 The answers
`yes` ⇒ `pledged`, the clock set, `earliestArrivalTick` seeded. `later` ⇒ `pledged` with `laterTick` (or open, under patience). Only against a DEADLINE can a `later` be "beyond the clock" (`laterTick > deadlineTick`): the card then states the actor's term as a present fact ("Tolle's council asked for the party before the new moon") and the requester's course may be `turns_elsewhere` at once (its own draw); against a THRESHOLD clock nothing is compared, because the clock's end is not yet a fact. `no` ⇒ a deed (`party_event`, kind `refused_request` — §3.3's row: a small slight at the requester, the rest by their ties to it), and the engagement closes. ### 8.6 The pledge verdict — `party_pledge_due` (CLIMAX)
Minted the week a pledge's clock ends or a `later` falls due. DEFAULT, from the world's own evidence first: **`made_good`** if the DM recorded `KEEP_PLEDGE`, or the party REACHED the need in time — `reachedTick ≤ T` (the first week the party's whereabouts named the need's settlement while the pledge stood: the DM's toggle there, or a placing deed there) — or a party deed resolved the need itself (its target is the need's stressor, siege town or war); else **`fell_short`** if `earliestArrivalTick ≤ T` (a reachable miss counts by default — the owner's word); else **`came_to_nothing`** (never reachable; no cost). The card and the receipt name the evidence that decided ("the party was in Tolle before the siege ended" · "Tolle lay within a fortnight's ride, and the party went to Merewick" · "Tolle lay beyond their reach") and the roads the journey was priced on (§8.4). `fell_short` mints a `party_pledge` deed `fell_short` (RN §6.1's pledge reading: the requester's `pledge_broken` term, every other hearer by its tie to the requester through lens two only; the party's credibility charged `MISS_FALL · mag`, RN §7 — a missed promise is not a lie); `made_good` mints `made_good` (`pledge_kept`; `TRUE_RISE · mag`); `came_to_nothing` mints nothing. The DM may strike (no trace) or amend (any verdict) in its week; a month advance runs the default and leaves its receipt ("resolved by the engine's default verdict"), naming the pledge with a one-click strike.
