**The answer in one paragraph.** The party is the one actor in the world the engine never moves. Its choices are made at the table and enter the world only as DEEDS the DM records with the act already in hand — the night's event, with three marks that fill themselves in (the party's hand; who backed it, if anyone; how it looks on its face) and a fourth (whether the reason was kept quiet, told or shown) that defaults to told; the DM changes any of them with one touch and never has to mark anything at all. A toggle says where the party is, and the party's last deed moves it on its own. Word of each deed travels the roads like any other tale — in the river towns they may only hear that the party burned an orphanage, never why — and every town, court, faith and person who hears it judges it by its own lights (THE RENOWN). Those who have heard and care then come for the party: to ask its help with what they need, to offer work or a title, to summon it, to turn it away at the gate, to post a warrant or a bounty, to lie in wait. Each is a hearing in its week only: the DM lets it reach the table, strikes it without a trace, or amends it; left alone, nothing has happened to the party yet — the searcher is still searching, the ambushers still waiting — and the world goes on: the plague spreads, the siege runs its course, patience wears thin, and a summons ignored becomes a warrant, then a bounty, then hunters on the north road. When the party says yes, the need's own course is the clock; a promise the party could have kept and did not costs it, one it could never have kept costs nothing. The DM's window puts the party first — what its deeds have done, who knows of it and what they think, who is looking for it and what it owes — in the world's words; the bands and the odds wait for an intentional look.

## §1 THE LAWS (PT1–PT18)

**PT1 — THE PARTY IS THE WORLD'S ONE OUTSIDE ACTOR.** The party has no decider in the engine. Its choices enter the world only as DEEDS the DM records through the landed party idiom (`applyPartyImpact`, the twelve kinds, `cause: 'party'`, canon by construction, no roll, no proposal queue) and through the deed doors §3 names. No engine path moves, captures, jails, injures, kills or binds the party; no hold, edge or proposal is ever keyed to the party as its object of force (R3 refinement 11). The simulation stays indifferent to the party; the DM's VIEW puts it first (`partyView`, §12).

**PT2 — THE DEED MARK RIDES THE ACT, WITH ZERO-CLICK DEFAULTS (the chair notes item 3).** The marks ride the act the DM already records, pre-filled: CONTRIBUTED = yes for a party impact (the door is the party's hand); BACKED BY = pre-selected when the party is present where the act happened and holds a warm regard (`fondness` or better, RN) or an open accepted request with exactly one authority whose reach covers the place, else none; MORAL WEIGHT = absent (RN's derived inherent value governs); PROOF = `spoken` (told). One act changes any mark. After an advance the DM's view offers BATCHED, non-blocking SUGGESTIONS ("The party was in Kell when the granary burned — did they have a hand in it?"), each answered by one act or ignored forever. The burden degrades gracefully: no marks at all is a coherent world in which the party is unknown, and nothing breaks. The ONE required mark is the party's answer to an accepted request (one act on its card, §8).

**PT3 — PRESENCE IS THE DM'S TOGGLE, AND IT FOLLOWS THE DEEDS.** "The party is here" persists until the DM moves it. A deed authored with the party's hand moves the party's LAST-HEARD place to the deed's settlement on its own (the two endpoints of a pair kind both become "last heard near"), so the toggle rarely needs touching. Presence is what the world KNOWS where the party is seen (witnesses); last-heard is what actors SEARCH by (the rumor road's word of it). The engine never writes `presentAt`; only the DM does.

**PT4 — FAME, REGARD, CREDIBILITY AND INHERENT VALUE ARE RN'S.** PT mints no fame, no regard store, no credibility law and no value table: the party is a SUBJECT of RN's fame and regard and the holder of RN's party credibility (RN1, RN10); its allies-and-enemies list is RN's authored regard (`DECLARE_STANCE`, `declaredStance: ally · enemy`); the net effect at any meeting is RN's body derivation (`courtRegardOf`) read with the place's politics (§9.1). PT supplies the deed doors and the marks.

**PT5 — EVERY ENGAGEMENT IS A HEARING, ONLY ON THE WEEK (requirement 24, as the owner restated it).** Every act of the world toward the party is a registered engagement kind (§13). A hearing is minted only at the DM's one-week advance and lives that one week; the next advance resolves it first; month, season and year advances run every one on its default without stopping; nothing queues and nothing is held over an advance. Each STANDING kind is a NEW hearing every week the actor still presses it, in its CURRENT state.

**PT6 — THE PARTY'S DEFAULT IS ALWAYS THAT NOTHING HAS HAPPENED TO IT YET.** The engine's default verdict for every party kind leaves the party untouched; the RNG decides only the ACTOR's own course (keep looking, keep waiting, try again, find help elsewhere, escalate, give up, turn against). An ambush the DM leaves is ambushers still waiting; an arrest left is a warrant still unserved. What happens to the party happens at the table and returns as a deed. The single engine-recorded deed ABOUT the party is the reachable broken pledge, by the owner's own word (PT10), and it too is a hearing in its week.

**PT7 — TWO ANSWERS, TWO HANDS, NEVER MERGED.** In the hearing's week the DM answers OUTSIDE the story — leave it (the default), STRIKE it (no trace in the world; it quiets that actor's urge for a while), or AMEND it (rewrite the actor's state or course, or direct the actor to the party now). At the table the DM records the party's answer INSIDE the story — `yes · later · no` — as a deed. A "no" is a deed with consequences; a strike is not a deed and leaves nothing.

**PT8 — HOLDS ARE THE ACTOR'S STATES IN THE WORLD, NEVER PENDING HEARINGS.** An engagement stands in one of three states (`engagementState: seeking · awaiting · pledged`; prose "not found yet · not yet engaged · yes, but later"), each shown with its reason in the world's words; a hearing is minted only in the week the actor presses it. No DM hold outlives its week. To save an encounter, the DM leaves it and directs the actor later.

**PT9 — SILENCE IS NEVER A DECISION.** With no answer in the hearing's week, the default is computed from the world: `seeking` while the actor is elsewhere or does not know where the party is, `awaiting` once actor and party share a place (presence, else last-heard). If still live next week, next week's hearing shows it as it stands then. An engagement that closes by the world's own course (the actor gives up, the need passes) closes in the world's words with NO cost to the party — no deed, no regard change — because in the story the actor never reached them.

**PT10 — TWO CLOCKS, NEVER MERGED; FAULT IS JUDGED AGAINST THE WORLD AS IT UNFOLDED.** PURSUIT (how long an actor keeps looking or waiting — by personality, urgency and distance) runs while the actor seeks or awaits; when it is spent the actor gives up at no cost to the party. Once the party says yes the NEED'S CLOCK governs (§8; PATIENCE only where the need has none). A reachable miss is a BROKEN PLEDGE and COUNTS BY DEFAULT (regard and credibility fall by each actor's values; word spreads); a pledge the party could never have reached costs nothing; a "later" set beyond the need's clock is no promise the requester can lean on.

**PT11 — THE WORLD NEVER WAITS.** A deferred engagement never freezes the actor's situation. A need resolved without the party closes the engagement ("Tolle found its cure elsewhere"); patience running out makes a NEW engagement (seek other help · escalate · give up · turn against), never a verdict on the party. Escalation emerges — summons → warrant → bounty → hunters — each a hearing in its own week.

**PT12 — SANCTION IS ONE MARK WITH A REACH AND A WEIGHT; IT NEVER EXPIRES ON ITS OWN.** The DM's backing mark (`backing { by: actorRef }`; "sanction" is the prose word) covers the backer's REACH, weighted by the backer's standing there (§10). Inside its reach and where heard, it softens a villainous-looking act and amplifies a good one (RN §6.2 reads it). It is shared ownership (credit to the backer; the backer's enemies inherit a grudge; a false reason exposed shares the blame). Its counterforces are named (§10.3); it ends only if VOIDED by a higher authority over the same lands or REPUDIATED by a successor, each a hearing.

**PT13 — MUTATION RIDES THE LANDED WEATHERING; DRIFT NEVER INVENTS A REASON.** A deed travels as one telling whose PARTS (act · size · reason · proof · backing · doer) are VISIBLE by the landed completeness thresholds in a fixed order (reason and backing fall away first; size drifts by the landed band roll; rarely the doer swaps to a real actor). No new draw for any non-deed event, no second engine. A false reason exists only where a liar plants it (the slander, ⛔ PT-S2's producer), traceable to its source. The truth can chase the rumor at the same speed (§11).

**PT14 — BOUNDED RESPONSIVENESS.** Only actors who have HEARD of the party (RN's `fameIn`) and CARE (a need it is known to answer, or a regard away from indifference) engage it; each actor holds at most one open engagement with the party per family and DRAFT 2 in all; the party draws at most DRAFT 6 open engagements and at most DRAFT 3 new ones a week; a struck or closed engagement cools that actor for a while; every draw is seeded on a labelled key (same seed, same world).

**PT15 — ONE PARTY PER CAMPAIGN, AN ADDITIVE SLOT FOR A SECOND.** The actor ref is `{kind:'party'}` with no id; the sub-ledger holds one record. A second party is recorded as the additive slot (an `id` on the ref and a keyed map), never a second shape (RULINGS §5, R2 Q5).

**PT16 — THE MOUNT RULE.** Zero lines in `pulseKernel.js` (1581), `applyWorldPulse.js` (907) and `warTermination.js` (818); nothing in `generosityKernel.js` (800), `armyTransitKernel.js` (798), `roadsKernel.js` (838) or `informationStatecraft.js` (781). Every PT module is a leaf under `src/domain/party/`; every host is named with its effective lines (§14).

**PT17 — THE PHYSICAL WORLD IS A SEAM.** Until the physical world lands, a place is a settlement and a journey is the named-person physics over the frozen digest, or one week a hop over the relationship graph where there is no map. Every wave is whole without a map (§15's degraded arms).

**PT18 — BEHIND THE SCENES; THE EVIDENCE LAW.** The surface speaks in the world's words ("the Abbess grows impatient"; "in the river towns they say you burned an orphanage"); scrutiny shows the story's path, who twisted it, who is looking and why; bands, clocks and odds only on an intentional look. Every card names who, upon whom, where and the default — never what the DM might do; never a forecast; never a numeral; never a mechanism name. Unpredictable in prospect, legible and coherent on scrutiny; the DM's undo, strike and amend are the safety net.
