## THE ANSWER, IN ONE PARAGRAPH (for the DM)

The world grows three kinds of body on its own, and each wants something different: a **conclave** (the small flock of a faith — the faith ladder's `cult` standing, never villainy) wants recognition and growth and acts as its god's character leans; a **gang** wants coin and safety and thrives where the town's law looks away; a **beast** pack wants food and territory, is hostile to everyone, and breathes with the seasons. None appears from nothing: each is born from a fact the world already holds — a town's quarter spilling out, a den's holder, the wild in its season, a routed levy's deserters, migrants turned away — and its card says which. They move on the same roads and seasons as everyone, but where a court hunts or outlaws them they take the hidden ways through forest, marsh and mountain, sortie onto the road at the junctions, and live off what they take; supplies are their readiness, their size and their very existence, and a held site is their home base and cohesion. They meet each other, the patrols, the caravans, the villages and the armies, and every such engagement is a hearing in its week. Their presence is what an embattled region is made of — the gauge stays the one gauge, the bodies feed it — so killing them makes a region QUIET, while only fixing what breeds them (the watch, the wages, the keep, the known trails, the bread) makes it SAFE. A body has no power of its own: its deeds move the standings of the towns, the courts, the faiths and the nobles who hold the sword. The engine never sends a body toward the party, never names a body's purpose, and never turns one into a menace; the DM may promote any body into a menace with one act, and may end, pin or re-stance any of them.

## §1 THE LAWS (BD1–BD22)

**BD1 — ONE IDIOM, THREE KINDS, THREE WANTS.** A body is one record shape on one ledger (§2.2) of three closed kinds — `conclave · gang · beast` — each with its own want (conclave: recognition and growth, weighted by its god; gang: coin and safety, weighted by the town's law; beast: food and territory, hostile to all), its own closed choice set (§4), its own source of supplies (§6) and its own births (§3). Everything else is shared: one weekly choice drawn by the seeded stream from a situation-scored set, one physics, one encounter read, one band contest, one stance grammar, one ledger writer. The three are related by law, never by coincidence (§7.2, §9): gangs and conclaves compete for the same desperate people; an outlawed conclave hides the way a gang does but stays a church; beasts lose ground as towns grow while gangs gain it as the watch thins; all three are what an embattled region is made of, are hunted by the same riders and take the same sites. [REFINED 2026-10-18 (fp-final) — the brief's "cults, bands, beasts": `cult` is the faith ladder's landed STANDING word (`religionState.js :: standingFor`) and `band` the landed SIZE word, so the code kinds are `conclave · gang · beast` (census `cure/L-BD.md` §1(a)); the Herald keeps the world's words — "a cult of the Grey Lady", "a band of brigands", "the wolves of the Tolle hills".]

**BD2 — NEVER FROM NOTHING; NEVER FOR NOTHING.** Every body is born from a fact the world already holds, through one of FIVE births (§3.2) — a settlement's spill, a den's holder, the wild in its season, a defeat's deserters, migrants cast out — and carries a BIRTHPLACE RECEIPT (`birth`, the Evidence Law: "formed from the deserters of Kell's levy in the Tolle hills"). A body also ends from a fact (§6.4): starved, scattered, dissolved into a town, merged into another, raised into a menace by the DM, or disbanded by the DM — receipted. No body has a purpose, a destiny or a target the DM did not give it (the owner, 01:3x: "they have no purpose or destiny unless directed by the user").

**BD3 — A CONCLAVE IS A ROW OR A BODY, NEVER BOTH.** A creed's small flock in a town IS the creed's landed row in that town at `cult` standing (`worldState.religionStates[cid].deities[ref]`), given a weekly choice that is DERIVED and never stored (§4.2): no field is added to the row. It returns to the row by the faith system's own door (`religionState.js :: attemptEntry`) and its body record ends `dissolved`. One creed's people at one place are counted once. - **Capacity:** a trail carries at most a `modest` body (DRAFT; "a body of thirty, never a column"); a `capable` or larger body is too many to hide and must take the road — so a gang that grows past the trails' capacity is forced into the open (a counterforce on recruitment).
