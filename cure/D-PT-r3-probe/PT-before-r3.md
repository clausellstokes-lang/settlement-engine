**The answer in one paragraph.** The party is the one actor in the world the engine never moves. Its choices are made at the table and enter the world only as DEEDS the DM records with the act already in hand — the night's event, with marks that fill themselves in (the party's hand; who backed it, pre-filled only when the party is doing the work of a request it accepted; how it looks on its face) and a fourth (whether the reason was kept quiet, told or shown) that defaults to told; the DM changes any of them with one touch and never has to mark anything at all. A told reason carries only what the DM marked: if the DM says the burning was just, everyone who hears why takes it as just, and those who never hear why judge the act alone. A toggle says where the party is, and the party's last deed places it on its own whenever that word is the later one. Word of each deed travels the roads like any other tale — in the river towns they may only hear that the party burned an orphanage, never why — and every town, court, faith and person who hears it judges it by its own lights (THE RENOWN). Those who have heard and care then come for the party: to ask its help with what they need, to offer work or a title, to summon it, to turn it away at the town's gate, to post a warrant or a bounty, to lie in wait, to denounce or praise it in public. Each is a hearing in its week only: the DM lets it reach the table, strikes it without a trace, or amends it; left alone, nothing has happened to the party yet — the searcher is still searching, the ambushers still waiting — and the world goes on: the plague spreads, the siege runs its course, patience wears thin, and a summons ignored becomes a warrant, then a bounty, then hunters on the north road. When the party says yes, the need's own course is the clock; a promise the party could have kept and did not costs it, one it could never have kept costs nothing, and a party that went where it was needed in time is never blamed for a box the DM forgot to tick. The DM's window puts the party first — what its deeds have done, who knows of it and what they think, who is looking for it and what it owes — in the world's words; the bands and the odds wait for an intentional look. ## §1 THE LAWS (PT1–PT18)

**PT1 — THE PARTY IS THE WORLD'S ONE OUTSIDE ACTOR.** The party has no decider in the engine. Its choices enter the world only as DEEDS the DM records through the landed party idiom (`applyPartyImpact`, the twelve kinds, `cause: 'party'`, canon by construction, no roll, no proposal queue) and through the deed doors §3 names. No engine path moves, captures, jails, injures, kills or binds the party, and no hold or proposal is ever keyed to the party (R3 refinement 11). The one ruled departure from R3's "never writes a hold, edge or proposal keyed to the party" is the ban: RULINGS-AFTER-RESEARCH §4 widens the one ban system's edge to actor refs, so a court may close its own gate to the party (§9.3) — a fact about the door, never a force upon the party. The simulation stays indifferent to the party; the DM's VIEW puts it first (`partyView`, §12). **PT2 — THE DEED MARK RIDES THE ACT, WITH ZERO-CLICK DEFAULTS (the chair notes item 3).** The marks ride the act the DM already records, pre-filled: CONTRIBUTED = yes for a party impact (the door is the party's hand); BACKED BY = pre-selected ONLY when the act falls where the party is doing the work of a request it accepted — the act's place (`actAt`) is the settlement of an open `pledged` engagement's need and that requester's reach covers it (§10.1) — else none; MORAL WEIGHT = absent (RN's derived inherent value governs); PROOF = `spoken` (told), which is harmless because a told reason carries only a weight the DM marked (PT13). One act changes any mark. After an advance the DM's view offers BATCHED, non-blocking SUGGESTIONS ("The party was in Kell when the granary burned — did they have a hand in it?"), each answered by one act or ignored forever. The burden degrades gracefully: no marks at all is a coherent world in which the party is unknown, and nothing breaks; a pledge the party went to keep is judged kept from the world's own evidence even when the DM records nothing (§8.6). **PT9 — SILENCE IS NEVER A DECISION.** With no answer in the hearing's week, the default is computed from the world, per kind (§13): `seeking` while the actor is elsewhere or does not know where the party is, `awaiting` once actor and party share a place (by `whereaboutsOf`, as the actor heard it) — and an ambush `awaiting` for as long as the party's whereabouts stay within its reach. If still live next week, next week's hearing shows it as it stands then. An engagement that closes by the world's own course (the actor gives up, the need passes) closes in the world's words with NO cost to the party — no deed, no regard change — because in the story the actor never reached them.
