**THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH.** A champion or a villain is a person the DM introduces — never the engine — and gives a calling (one of eight: faith, war, diplomacy, information, health, the wildland, trade, order), a side, one defining trait, a goal, a line they will not cross, and how sure their hand is. They are not a new kind of creature: they live in the world's one ledger of named people, so they travel the roads every named person travels, can be barred from a town, and are spoken of the way everyone is. Each week each one decides, for reasons of their own — their nature, their purse, their moods, their habits, what they have heard and whom they love or hate — whether to stay or go, where, and whether to use their gift where they stand. A saint who preaches tips the faith contest toward her creed; a doctor eases the plague; a magistrate calms the unrest; their villains do the opposite, always through the one truth each system already keeps and always by a bounded amount. Some are bound to a court as its officer and move inside its lands or on its errands; others are free, take work while it suits them and leave when something better comes. Courts hire the ones they have heard of; some hire villains in secret, and pay for it when the secret gets out. Every act that touches another court — an offer of service, a contest between a champion and a villain, a town's gate refused, a ban, a fall from grace — is a hearing the DM sees in its week and may leave, tilt or decide. On the surface the DM reads what happened ("Sister Aude has come to Tolle; the Lantern's faithful grow bolder"); the reasons are there for anyone who looks.

## §16b THE CORPS — A FREE COMPANY CARRIES THE MARK (CH-10)

[CURED 2026-10-18 (fp-final r2) — `RULINGS-ROUND2.md` B and `briefs/L-CH.md`'s addendum: the owner's word of 2026-10-18 ~07:3x, verbatim, "Mercenaries are fine as is. **What it is.** The paragon's mark may sit on a free COMPANY as well as on a person: `authored.holderKind: person · corps`. A corps is the WAR calling (`domain: war`) of either side, FREE (for hire) by default or BOUND to a court, and it lives exactly as a paragon does — the weekly choice ("until a better deal or reason comes along"), retainers both ways (§7), fame, regard and deeds (RN), bans and the town's gate (§11), the hearings (§18) — with three differences: it is a UNIT, not a person; it never holds custody; and its strength is a band its unit carries.

**Its record — WC's free unit, never a new ledger.** The mark is ONE conditional, drop-when-empty `paragon` field on WC's free-unit record (`spatialLedgers.freeUnits[unitKey]`, `DESIGN_FP_ARCH_WC.md` §1.7.2: `{ blocks, position, state, supplyBand, driftBand, beliefStaleness }`, `FREE_UNIT_STATES` `operating · foraging · raiding · dissolving`), normalized by the same leaf `normalizeParagon` in WC's free-unit normalizer, landed in the commit of its first writer (CH2's law, for a second host). A lost battle is WC's (casualties lower its band). NO CUSTODY ROAD: a company is defeated or disbanded, never held. DEATH = DISBANDMENT, THE DM'S: the DM's own act ends it (CH's door, a `disband` arm beside `introduce` for `holderKind: corps`; the free unit enters WC's `dissolving` state and its people re-enter WC's shed road); the engine never disbands a marked company — a corps whittled to nothing stands on its page with no men until the DM decides.
