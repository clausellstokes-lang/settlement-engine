C2 — THE DENOMINATOR. src/domain/display/stateProse/dossierMounts.js is a pure,
frozen, import-free table of {mount, tab, desk, blockId, rung}. It replaces
`sectionTarget` as the router, not because that field is dirty (it is, and that is
a separate parser repair) but because of what it MEANS: a section is a property of
the block and a position is a property of the page, and `sectionTarget:
['economics']` can never say where in EconomicsTab a block goes. The causal
register is the control that shows the distinction is real rather than invented
here — a join's section genuinely is a property of the join, which is why
causalFamiliesForSection is legitimate and a state equivalent would not be.

DOSSIER_MOUNTS IS EMPTY AT BIRTH, and that is the honest state rather than a
shortfall: no desk has landed, so no component carries a position, and a row
naming a position the tree does not build would be the registry drifting into
fiction on its first day. UNMOUNTED_BLOCKS carries all 68 blocks in a
machine-checked order, so the corpus's darkness stops being a silence and becomes
a number — shrink-only against tests/lint/.dossier-mounts-baseline.json, so a
block can leave that list and never join it.

C3 — THE ROOM. A block's SENTENCE rung renders at exactly one position per
page-set; every other position renders the glance and the detail and no sentence.
This is not a new law — economyStateProse.js already says a composed page draws at
most one of each and never both about the same fact (R-DST-A) — and it needs no
mechanism: no draw-key change, no rotation, nothing that races the one-way door.
It is held in two places that cannot disagree: `rung` is a field on every row, so
a component can only be given the depth its row names; and sentenceMountForBlock
is the only way to ask where a fact speaks, answering with at most one position or
with nothing at all, fail-closed on a contradiction because picking the first row
would choose a winner by array order. The one written exception is recorded in the
docblock so the next reader does not repair it: DS-GEN-6's tier overlay composes
after the route line and never instead of it, which is two pools at one position
and not one fact speaking twice.

THE WALKER, AND WHY ITS CONTROLS ARE NOT DECORATION. With an empty table, every
arm that reads a row would pass over nothing and prove nothing. So each rule is a
PURE FUNCTION over a registry passed in, and every one is driven over the design's
own worked example plus a planted violation, with the sibling rules asserted
SILENT so a plant convicts one arm rather than the file. Executed: flipping
power.tensions to the sentence rung reds the one-fact arm alone; withdrawing the
severity declaration reds the honesty arm alone; a mount with no JSX site and one
with two both red the reachability arm. The tab vocabulary is read off
OutputContainer's own renderTab switch through mustExtract rather than kept by
hand, and the corpus join is re-derived from the six leaves on every run.

MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED. 68 corpus blocks carry pools. Exactly 3 carry a demoted
dimension — DS-GEN-1 severity, DS-GEN-6 deficit, DS-GEN-9 anchor — and 65 carry
none. The new src leaf holds 0 em dashes and 0 exclamation marks in any string
literal, measured with the ratchet's own tokenizer, so tests/copy/voiceMechanics
needs no baseline entry for it; its four red arms are unchanged at 1108 and 40
against a clean checkout of the base sha, which is where those reds were proved
pre-existing rather than assumed to be.

THE PLANT IS REAL RATHER THAN A RATIONALE. Striking DS-CND-1 from the dark half
without mounting it reds the totality arm by name — 15 passed, 1 failed — and that
is deliberately the arm a shrink-only ratchet CANNOT see, because shrinking is the
direction the ratchet exists to permit. A list that gets shorter looks healthy to
a ratchet and looks wrong only to a join against the corpus.
