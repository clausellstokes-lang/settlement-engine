THE DEFECT, MEASURED. generate-dossier-state-prose.mjs split its SECTION-TARGET
declaration on `,` as well as the intended `·`, so a line reading `` `economy`
(economic resilience, prosperity, food) `` projected as the three fragments
"economy (economic resilience", "prosperity" and "food)". Measured against the
eight sections the causal annex declares in its own §0e table, 26 of the 45
declaring state blocks carried at least one string outside them; measured against
the ten the STATE register actually uses (the eight plus `overview` and
`viability`), 13 of 45 did. The causal register came out 100% canonical under the
same parser only because its authors happen to write nothing but backticked
tokens. The ARMS extractor two lines below has always read exactly that shape.

THE CURE (design E4a, chair rows J6/J10 at ledger §885.2). Three moves in one
file. (1) The extractor becomes the ARMS extractor's shape: backticked lowercase
tokens, prose ignored. Every backticked span on all 123 SECTION-TARGET lines in
both annexes already matches that token class, measured, so it drops nothing the
old rule caught. (2) A closed vocabulary of ten, CROSS-CHECKED AGAINST THE DOC on

section reds this projection instead of drifting past it. `overview` and

and their eight ship to the runtime as CAUSAL_SECTION_TARGETS. (3) An unknown
backticked token THROWS rather than being dropped: on this line a backticked
token is indistinguishable from a target, and a plausible-looking wrong section id
is worse than the fragments this repair removes. Un-backticked prose stays
ignored by construction, and the two blocks whose target surface is not a dossier
section at all — DS-GEN-10 (the printed chapter openers) and DS-HK-1 (the hooks
panel header) — are frozen in SECTION_TARGETLESS_BLOCKS, so a new silent one
cannot appear.
