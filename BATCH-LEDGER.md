**The owner's two sentences, verbatim (2026-09-20, in the live session):** "By the way, can you not combine some different areas into one gate if they are queued in line to preserve time?" — then: "And you will assign them into appropriate batches as they come correct or into new ones if they don't fit into an appropriate one".

**The practice.** A lane becomes GATE-READY when its ungated work is finished and its note carries what a composer needs (pathspec per commit, commit messages as files, which focused red-firsts are executed-and-quoted and which are owed, registers moved as deltas, every staged file's SHA-256). The moment a lane is gate-ready the chair ASSIGNS it: into the newest OPEN batch if it FITS, otherwise into a NEW batch. A batch is composed by ONE Opus composer lane onto a throwaway branch `batch-<n>-2026-09-20` cut at the green integration tip; the chair refreezes the lighting census once and absorbs the observed-shape baseline once; ONE full check proves the batch; green ⇒ `fixes-2026-09-18-consist` fast-forwards to the batch tip; red ⇒ attribute by the failing tests' subjects, drop or cure the member on the throwaway branch, re-run.

**THE FIT TEST (all must hold to join an OPEN batch):**
1. FILES — its change set is file-disjoint from every member's, or overlaps only in hunks the chair (or the forecast lane) has READ and found disjoint.
2. APPLY — its work applies at the batch tip without a same-region conflict (dry-run in a throwaway worktree; a conflict ⇒ RE-CUT SOLO, never forced).
3. REGISTERS — it moves no shrink-only register in a way that needs an order the ledger cannot state (lighting and the observed-shape baseline are the chair's, once per batch; mutation-coverage, anchors, wiring census, prose-numerics, size baseline: named per member below).
4. ONE CAUSE PER FIXTURE — at most ONE member per batch uses the owner's golden door or regenerates a byte-pinned fixture of the SAME artefact family, so every moved row has exactly one cause.
5. BUILD MEASURERS TOGETHER — lanes that mint a ceiling from a real build ride the BUILD BATCH and share one build.
6. SIZE — a batch closes at six members or about sixty files, whichever comes first, so a red run stays attributable in minutes.
7. NEVER BATCHED — a sealed Edit-Mode packet build (one packet, one seal, one slot).
A batch CLOSES to new members when its composer begins applying; the next gate-ready lane opens the next batch.
