## THE ANSWER, IN ONE PARAGRAPH (for the DM)

When a realm is born, every town already has its own past: when it was founded and by whom, and the sieges, plagues, debts, occupations and migrations it lived through, each so many years ago. But no town's past mentions another: the raiders who besieged Varn are "an external power", the creditor of Ashford's old debt is "a partner settlement", and nobody knows which older town Kell's founders came from. THE REALM'S PAST fills those blanks from the realm itself. It lines the towns' histories up on one timeline, oldest first, starting when the second-oldest town was founded, and wherever a recorded event implies another party it may name a town that existed then and was near enough: Ostmark's riders besieged Varn; Kell was founded by people out of Merewick; Ashford's debt was owed to Brindle. It never changes a word of any town's own record, never invents a conquest, a new ruler, a lost population or a faith the record does not show, and never runs the world forward to get there. Before the first advance the whole past is pencilled in: you see each fact with the event it explains, and you may cross it out, change it, confirm it or roll it again, for one town or the whole realm, and you see what else moves before anything changes. Where the realm's founding ties disagree with the towns' own founding ages, the past says so and leaves the choice to you. At the first advance the past is inked: it becomes the realm's history, the relationship desk remembers what each pair survived, and only what is still true today enters the living world, such as a pact still within its term. A town you add later joins the past at its own founding, and its ties stay pencilled until the next advance.

- **A forward run is ruled out by measurement as well as by law** (`findings/R-PA2-probe/tick.mjs`): the real kernel costs 58 to 252 ms a tick, four weeks changed every dossier's bytes, and a five-century run would take about 1.8 hours. An event-driven replay over the real histories costs milliseconds (1 to 12 ms for the largest realm, re-run by this compile) and leaves every dossier byte-identical.
- **The plane forgets in a year** (`cure/L-PA-probe/relax.out`, executed): a rival pair seeded far above its baseline is back at the baseline within a year on a `generational` pair and within five on a `long` one; only an `undying` pair keeps it. No generator declares or infers a memory band, so every generated pair is `generational`; every generated event is at least five years old.
- **A year is 52 ticks**, and a peacetime clause drafted by `draftPactSheet` runs 208 ticks, four years (`findings/R-PA3-probe/negtick.out`); renewal is lit in two presets only.

## §1 THE LAWS (PA1–PA18)

**PA1 — THE DOSSIER IS CANON (⭐ THE OWNER'S, OD-PA-1).** Every settlement's current dossier, generated or edited, is what the user intended, and the past is built around it, never into it. The past lives in its own layer (PA4) and points at a dossier's events by reference (PA6). It adds only what the dossier leaves open: it may name the other party of a raid, a debt, an occupation or a migration the dossier records, and it never invents a conquest, a change of rulers, a lost population or a faith the dossier does not show (PA9). The present-day ties it creates agree with the dossier's present: its government label, its named neighbour and that tie's type, its tensions and its stresses (`presentTiesAllowed`, §2.2). A user's later edit to a dossier wins, and the past re-reads it (PA6). THE BYTE LAW: for every member save `s`, `rawBitFormOf(s.settlement)` (`tests/helpers/dormancyOracle.js`, the standing claim) is equal before and after every PA act — a derivation, every choice of the user's, and the inking — compared over `settlement`, never over the save envelope (§19 rows 2–4).

**PA2 — THE PAST IS THE USER'S UNTIL THE FIRST ADVANCE (⭐ THE OWNER'S, OD-PA-2).** Every fact of the past is the user's to cross out or change before the world's first advance. Each fact is shown with its cause and the dossier event it explains. It can be crossed out, changed, confirmed or rolled again, for one town's ties or for the whole realm, with the knock-on shown before it applies (§9). A user's choice is intent; the rest is re-derived around it deterministically (the `chooseOrPin` shape: a chosen fact draws nothing, so the rest re-derives exactly). A contradiction with a dossier is flagged and never silently applied (§8). At the first advance the past is INKED and becomes history like any recorded event (§10). "The first advance" is the first advance that leaves `worldState.tick === 0`, whoever starts it (the DM, the folder, the Surveyor, a living world's catch-up); canonizing the world does not ink the past (the owner's word names the first advance), so the review stays open after canonize while the tick is still 0; undoing the first advance returns the past to pencilled, with every choice intact (the pulse snapshot carries the sidecar).

**PA3 — FROM THE PAST, NEVER FORWARD (⭐ THE OWNER'S, OD-PA-3).** The past is replayed over the dossiers' own histories, event by event; no forward run before the first week changes the generated present. PA runs no tick, no kernel stage, no `advanceInterval.js :: simulateCampaignWorldInterval`, no chained `one_year` advance (OD-FC-51's EP-N is the forward run this law declines) and no landed per-tick rule over past years. The law is measured as well as owned: four real weeks changed every dossier's bytes and five centuries would take about 1.8 hours, while the event-driven replay costs milliseconds and leaves every dossier byte-identical (§0.3). The one forward read PA offers is the landed forecast's clone-and-discard preview of the first weeks AFTER the inking (§9.5), which changes nothing.

**PA4 — ONE HOME, DERIVED UNTIL INKED.** The past has ONE home, the sidecar `worldState.spatialLedgers.realmPast` (§2.1): DM-only, persisted with the campaign, carried by the pulse snapshot, at zero first-paint cost. Before the first advance it holds only the address (the realm seed), the recorded whole-realm re-roll nonces and the user's choices; every fact is DERIVED on demand from the dossiers, the seed, the nonces and the choices, so the derivation is the past's one truth and a dossier edit is read at once. At the inking the derived facts are RECORDED ONCE in the sidecar's `inked` record, append-closed (the `concludedWars` idiom: lived history is never rewritten, folded or pruned), and from that moment the record is the one truth of the inked past and the derivation is never consulted for it; so no fact has two truths at once, and a later edit of one dossier can never move another town's history (⛔ PA-C-2, the recommended arm; §22). No negative tick and no "past" flag ever enters a landed ledger: four of six landed readers clamp, drop or throw on one, and the allowlist rebuilders drop an unnamed field (executed, `findings/R-PA3-probe/negtick.out`).

**PA5 — ONE INKING, AT ONE PLACE, AND ONLY TODAY'S CONSEQUENCES.** At the first advance the past becomes history at ONE place, `store/campaignAdvanceSession.js :: runAdvanceCampaignWorld`, inside Phase 1, after `capturePulseSnapshot` and the drain and before `simCampaign = cloneJson(c)`, through the leaf-first car `realmPast/realmPastInk.js :: inkRealmPastAt` (§10). Only the past's PRESENT-DAY consequences enter the landed ledgers, at tick 0, each carrying a back-pointer to the past fact it comes from (its `pastRef`, and its `causedBy` where the record keeps one): a pact still within its term (§7); a pair's old feeling only where the relationship plane's own memory law keeps it to birth (§6, ⛔ PA-C-1); a dated oath and a founding tie at their owners' waves (§13). Everything else stays in the sidecar, where the relationship desk and the cause walk read it.

**PA6 — A REFERENCE NAMES WHAT THE DOSSIER SAYS.** A dossier event is referenced by the triple (the member's save id, the event's `name`, its `yearsAgo`) — the key Edit Mode's merge (`recordRegister.js`: "`'history.historicalEvents': 'name'`") and the landed readers (`legacyGenerator.js`, `historyBeats.js`) already use; never by position (`historyPreservation.js`: "positional index is worthless across a reroll") and never by a minted id (an id on the dossier would be a dossier write). The founding is referenced as (save id, `founding`); a current tension as (save id, `tension`, its `type`). A reference is re-resolved on EVERY read. One that no longer resolves — the user removed the event, changed its name or its date, or regenerated the town — drops its LINK and only its link: before the first advance the fact is simply not derived again and a user's choice on it is flagged on the review ("the town's record no longer holds this event"), never silently dropped; after the first advance the inked fact stands and reads "a past the town's record no longer holds". A regeneration ("roll again" on a dossier, or a history section reroll) is the user's own edit and is read the same way: an event the new record still holds under the same name and date in the same town is, by this law, the same event and keeps its link; every other link drops. PA reads only generated events: a campaign-era entry (`campaignEra: true`) is lived history, never a past event to link.

**PA7 — ONE STREAM PER FACT; THE SAME SEED AND THE SAME CHOICES GIVE THE SAME PAST.** Every draw of the past is taken from its own labelled stream, a ROOT COMPOSITION in Edit Mode's idiom (`pools.js :: rollFrom`: "THE KEY IS A ROOT COMPOSITION, NEVER A FORK"): `` `realm-past:${seed}:${nonce}:${stableId}:${eventName}:${yearsAgo}:${rollIndex}` `` for an event's fact, and `` `realm-past:${seed}:${nonce}:pact:${stableIdA}|${stableIdB}:${yearsBefore}` `` for a growth step, where `stableId` is the dossier's own `_seed` (`crossSettlementConflicts.js :: stableIdOf`), never the service-minted save id, `nonce` is the newest whole-realm re-roll (empty until one is made) and `rollIndex` is the fact's own re-roll count (0 until one is made). So crossing out or re-rolling one fact moves no other (executed: with one shared stream a strike moves later links; with one stream per fact it does not, `cure/L-PA-probe/replay-fork.out`). Members iterate oldest first with codepoint order on `stableId` as the tie-break; no `Date.now()`, no `Math.random()`, no `localeCompare`. THE DETERMINISM PIN: the same campaign derived twice is byte-identical; two births of the same seed derive the same past once each save id is mapped to its dossier's `_seed`.

**PA8 — THE PAST'S TIME IS THE DOSSIER'S.** Every fact carries `yearsBefore`, a non-negative integer of years before the realm's week 0 on the dossiers' FROZEN clock — the dossier's own unit (`yearsAgo`; `eventsTimeline[].year = age − yearsAgo`). A realm year is DERIVED for display (§3) and never stored; a fact before week 0 has no tick; and `yearsBefore` is never compared with or added to `calendar.year` or a tick ("two clocks over one world … must never be compared or added", `historyGenerator.js`). A consequence that enters a landed ledger at the inking carries tick 0 and finds its age through its `pastRef`.

**PA9 — THE PAST SUPPLIES WHAT THE DOSSIER LEAVES OPEN, AND NOTHING ELSE.** PA produces: (i) a counterpart for a LINKABLE dossier event (§4.1), a town of the realm that existed then and was within reach — every implied party is anonymous, so filling one contradicts no word, and where none fits the event stays local; (ii) founding ties, who founded whom (§5); (iii) a pair's old grudge or old bond on an EXISTING edge, consistent with the pair's dossier type (§6); (iv) the date of an existing oath (PL's wave, §13); (v) past pacts, of which only those still within their term are minted at the inking, and only where pact formation is lit (§7); (vi) the realm's account at the inking, as truth, with each court's version derived on read (§11). PA NEVER produces: a write to any dossier — so neither `relationships/neighbourBackLink.js :: buildNeighbourBackLink` nor the traditions' relations, both of which write dossiers, is ever PA's writer; town-to-town regard (RN refuses the pair; the feeling is the landed pair state); a menace (MN's, by the DM only); a polity's NAME on its own authority (PA OFFERS a name as a pencilled fact at PL's wave, and the DM's keep is the christening, §13); a vassal chain without a dossier edge (PA dates an existing oath and never invents one); a person's deed (no durable person id exists at birth, RN16).

**PA10 — THE GENESIS TIES ARE CANON; WHERE THEY CONTRADICT THE FOUNDING AGES, THE PAST SAYS SO.** Plan law 2 writes 3, 8 or 12 founding ties into the dossiers at instant birth (`genesisDiplomacy.js :: materializeGenesisRelations`), by tier alone. They are dossier facts, canon under PA1. PA reads each one and EXPLAINS it where it agrees with the two towns' founding ages (an overlord older than its vassal; a patron older than its client) and FLAGS it on the review and in the account where it does not (a younger overlord of an older town) — for the user, never fixed and never linked around (§8). Whether PA's ordering may inform plan law 2 at birth in a later wave is the owner's (OPEN row OD-PA-13b).

**PA11 — TRUTH ONCE, BELIEF ON READ.** The past is held once, as truth: the derivation before the first advance, the inked record after it. Each court's version — whether it heard of a fact, how much of it it still remembers, how it tells it — is DERIVED ON READ (§11): the reach of the tale over the regional graph by the rumor road's own magnitude bands, its fading by the spine's one decay shape (`bandedStock.js :: decayTowardNeutral`), its distortion only in a belief infoMode through the landed `degradeTelling`. A stored per-court version would be "a design defect" (LAW ONE). A court knows its own past as truth; a pair's declared type is public.

**PA12 — ONLY LIT RULES GROW THE PAST, AND EACH PROGRAM REGISTERS ITS OWN.** PA's own derivations — the timeline, the links, the founding ties and the pairs' old feelings — are PA's rules and need no other program lit. GROWING through another program's rules uses only rules that are lit and that expose a pure step: in PA's first versions that is pact formation alone (GR-2's pure scorers, drafter and signer, `realistic_regional` and the other world-alive presets), and only on composer-born worlds until the birth door lands for plain births (OD-FC-62). Every other program's past rule — RN, PL, POP, PG, FAITH, TRADE — is registered by that program's own wave, never by PA (§13); a dark program's arm is simply absent.

**PA13 — NO HEARING; THE DM'S PEN IS A VERB.** PA mints NO engagement kind: the pencilled past is not an engagement between two settlements, it is never on the docket, and it is decided by the DM's own pen before any week runs (§15). PA owes requirement 14's verbs instead: six typed DM verbs through the established store-action lane (§9.2), each with its op-level inverse, its lifecycle survival (§18) and its AI surface (the clerk proposes typed choices from the closed lists; it writes nothing).

**PA14 — A NEWCOMER JOINS AT ITS OWN FOUNDING.** A settlement added after the first advance joins the realm's past at its own founding: its events may take counterparts among the towns that existed then, and its ties stay pencilled — the user's — until the first advance after it joins, when they are inked like the birth past (§12). It never alters an inked fact of an older member; it adds facts beside them. Its dossier is read on the realm's frozen clock as of the realm's week 0, because the two clocks are never compared (PA8); a newcomer event the DM knows the realm lived through is the DM's to cross out, and PA never links a newcomer to lived history (`pulseHistory`, campaign-era events). A town founded during play by the engine (`advanceInterval.js :: foldMemberBirthsOntoSaves`) has a lived founding and no past.

**PA15 — THE MOUNT RULE.** Zero lines in `pulseKernel.js` (1581), `applyWorldPulse.js` (907), `warTermination.js` (818), `historyGenerator.js` and every generator, `composeInstantWorld.js` (no composer step: a step would move every same-seed world's fingerprint) and `genesisDiplomacy.js`. Every PA module is a leaf under `src/domain/realmPast/` (the review's components under `src/components/realmPast/`). The hosts, with eslint's effective lines: `store/campaignAdvanceSession.js` 700 → ≈703 (an import and one call, the leaf-first car `realmPastInk.js` built and pinned first); `components/map/WorldPulsePanel.jsx` 558 → ≈564 (one lazy mount of `RealmPastReview.jsx`); `store/campaignWorldPulseSlice.js` 367 → ≈375 (the six verbs composed from the store leaf `store/realmPastActions.js`); `display/relationshipChronicle.js` 193 → ≈199 (the seventh archive read); at PA-2 `worldPulse/pactFormation.js` 565 → ≈567 (the signer's optional `pastRef`, GR's car); under ⛔ PA-C-1's recommended arm `worldPulse/relationshipState.js` 360 → 361 (`pastRef` named in the allowlist, the plane's car). No host at or near the 800 ceiling takes a line.

**PA16 — THE PHYSICAL-WORLD SEAM: THE PAST'S PLACE IS A TOWN, AND REACH IS THE REALM'S GRAPH.** A past fact happens at a settlement, never at a road, a site or a country. "Within reach" is measured in hops over the regional graph as it stands when the past is derived: its edges (the dossiers' neighbour links, the genesis ties among them) and its discovered channels (§4.3). On the aspatial arm — no graph — every member of the realm is within reach (a realm is five to fourteen towns at birth). When the map lands, reach follows whatever the regional graph carries, with no PA change. A fact dated before week 0 has no map channel today (the map's events window is keyed by an advance nonce or a pulse-history sequence, neither of which a past fact has); the map's past window reading the sidecar is a ROUTE to the map (§13).

**PA17 — DARK MEANS ABSENT.** Every PA flag absent ⇒ no `realmPast` key, no read, no review, no inking, no consequence: byte-identical (§19 row 1). Lit-then-dark: an INKED record stays readable as history (the relationship desk and the cause walk keep it), the consequences it minted stay as lived history, and a PENCILLED record is ignored — nothing derives and nothing inks. A world whose first advance came while PA was dark gets no past when PA lights (absent = never ran): the user's window has closed.

**PA18 — THE WORLD HOSTS THE CAMPAIGN; MACRO OVER MICRO.** The past names no party, sets no quest and gives no town a purpose; it supplies setting, and its felt outputs end in settlement states the dossier already shows (a pair's type, a pact within its term) and in the towns' own recorded events, which the DM reads into the ordinary life. It speaks of towns and courts, never of unnamed people, and of named persons only through the dossiers' own words. Medieval for millennia: a past of centuries is the same world, with no technological ages.

## §2 THE ONTOLOGY

### 2.1 The record — `worldState.spatialLedgers.realmPast` (persisted shape 1 of 1 owned by PA; NEW; OD-PA-4, VISIBLE)

```
worldState.spatialLedgers.realmPast = {
  v: 1,
  seed: string,                // the address: 'realm-past:' + (campaign.instantWorld.seed ?? worldState.rngSeed), written when the record is created
  nonces?: string[],           // the whole-realm re-rolls, newest last: generateSeed()-class, minted at the store layer, RECORDED (EP's generator side)
  choices?: {                  // the user's intent, keyed by factRef (§2.2); the only per-fact state stored before the inking
    [factRef: string]: {
      state?: 'user_held' | 'user_changed' | 'user_refused',   // absent = 'as_derived' (a re-rolled fact stays as derived)
      rollIndex?: number,      // the fact's own re-roll count (Edit Mode's segment); absent = 0
      value?: PastFactValue,   // a CHANGED fact's user value: { b?: saveId, kind?: PAST_FACT_KINDS member, yearsBefore?: number }
      order: number,           // the choice's place in the append-only history of choices (the undo order)
    }
  },
  inkedAtTick?: 0,             // THE MARKER: absent = the birth past is pencilled; present = inked at the first advance (always tick 0); `inkedMembers` records which members it covered
  inked?: {                    // ⛔ PA-C-2's arm: the facts RECORDED ONCE at the inking; append-closed
    [factRef: string]: InkedFact
  },
  inkedMembers?: {             // the tick each member's ties were inked: 0 for the birth members; a later tick for a member that joined after it (§12)
    [saveId: string]: number   // a member absent here, on an inked world, is a newcomer whose ties are pencilled
  },
}

InkedFact = {
  kind: PAST_FACT_KINDS member,      // §2.3
  a: saveId, b?: saveId,             // the town the fact is about, and its counterpart (absent for a fact that stayed local)
  yearsBefore: number,               // years before week 0 on the frozen clock (PA8)
  explains?: { saveId, name, yearsAgo } | { saveId, founding: true },   // the dossier fact it explains (PA6)
  cause: string,                     // the leading reason word of the draw (§4.4; closed per kind)
  magnitude: 0 | 1 | 2 | 3,          // the dossier event's severity, banded (minor 1 · major 2 · catastrophic 3); 0 for a tie
  state: PAST_FACT_STATES member,    // as it stood at the inking
  notices?: ['genesis_contradiction'],
  mintedAs?: { ledger: 'treaties' | 'relationshipStates', key: string },  // the landed consequence it seeded at the inking (§10.2)
}
```

- **Absent until needed; zero first-paint bytes.** A world the user has not touched carries no record: its past is derived from the address alone. The record is created by the one writer at the user's first choice or at the inking, whichever comes first, and nests under `spatialLedgers` (`setSpatialLedger`), the namespace whose new members "cost ZERO first-paint bytes". Drop-when-empty (`dropSpatialLedger`) holds for a record emptied by undo before the inking.
- **THE MARKER.** The birth past is PENCILLED while PA is active, `worldState.tick === 0` and `inkedAtTick` is absent; it is INKED once `inkedAtTick` is present. A world that is past tick 0 with no record never ran (PA17).
- **Why `spatialLedgers` and not the campaign record:** the pulse snapshot clones `worldState` (so undoing the first advance returns the past to pencilled, with every choice intact), `WORLD_SNAPSHOT_HARD_DENY` keeps it DM-only, and it rides the campaign's persistence and cloud sync with no new top-level key. A top-level campaign field would not be restored by the snapshot, which clones the campaign's `worldState`, `regionalGraph`, `wizardNews` and member saves only. It is never on a dossier (PA1) and never in the record register (L10 (e): worldState-side state is lived history outside `rederive`).
- **Size.** Before the inking: a few hundred bytes (the seed, the nonces, the choices). After it: one `InkedFact` per fact, tens of facts for the largest instant realm (fifty-eight dossier events of which most are linkable or local), a few kilobytes beside a worldState of 175–505 KB (`findings/R-PA2-probe/tick.mjs`).
- **ONE WRITER:** `src/domain/realmPast/realmPastWriter.js`, with two entry points — `applyRealmPastChoice(record, choice)` (the six verbs, §9.2; called from the store leaf `store/realmPastActions.js`) and `inkRealmPast(record, facts, tick)` (the inking, §10; called by the car `realmPastInk.js :: inkRealmPastAt`). Nothing else writes the record; the DM writes it only through the verbs.

## §3 THE TIMELINE — ONE REALM CLOCK OVER THE DOSSIERS' FROZEN YEARS (wave PA-1)

### 3.1 What it reads (TRUTH)
Each member's `history.age` (the founding, `founding.age` equal to it by the dossier's own consistency group), each generated event's `yearsAgo`, and each member's `_seed` for the tie-break. Never `traditions/genesis.js`'s second age (its `AGE_BY_TIER` is a different, synthesized number), never `calendar.year`, never a tick.

### 3.2 The clock (integer arithmetic, no new clock)
- Every fact's coordinate is `yearsBefore`: years before the realm's week 0 on the frozen clock. A town's founding is at `yearsBefore = age`; a generated event at `yearsBefore = yearsAgo` (PA8).
- THE REALM'S ORIGIN is the oldest founding, `A* = max(age)`; a REALM YEAR, shown on scrutiny only, is `A* − yearsBefore` (the realm's founding year 0, its present `A*`). Stored nowhere.
- THE CLOCK STARTS at the second-oldest founding (`yearsBefore = age` of the second-oldest town): the first moment two towns can know each other, in the owner's words "starting with the oldest and second oldest's history". Every event older than that has no other town to meet and stays local by construction (5.4% of events, §0.3).
- Equal ages break by codepoint order on `_seed`, never `localeCompare`.
- A town of age 0 (`settlementAgeMode: 'new'`) joins at the present with nothing to link. The opt-in ancient ruin (`yearsAgo` past its town's founding) is a local event before the clock, placed at its own year. A custom age up to 5,000 years costs nothing extra: the derivation steps by EVENT, never by year (PA3), so its cost is the realm's event count (milliseconds, §0.3).

### 3.3 Clock · tier · counterforce · receipt
- **Clock (requirement 9).** The past has no cadence in engine time: it is derived on demand before the first advance and recorded once at it. Its FIRST-VISIBLE latency is ZERO advances: the review stands at birth, before the first week (requirement 16). Its slow verdict is the inking.
- **Tier (requirement 17).** Aspatial-safe; the clock needs only the dossiers.
- **Counterforce (requirement 2).** The timeline is arithmetic over canon and moves nothing; the past as a whole has two counterforces — the dossier (PA1: every fact must agree with it, and a later edit wins) and the DM's pen (PA2: any fact can be crossed out before it is inked).
- **Surface / scrutiny.** SURFACE: the review lists the realm's towns oldest first, each with its founding told in the dossier's own words. SCRUTINY: the realm years and the clock's start. MACHINERY: `A*`, the tie-break.

Nothing before the inking: a link is derived. At the inking it is recorded (PA4) and its present form, if any, is decided by §6 (a pair's old feeling) or §7 (a pact).

### 4.6 Clock · tier · counterforce · receipt · felt

### 6.2 What reaches the relationship plane at the inking — ⛔ PA-C-1 (the recommended arm, pending its ruling, §22)

## §10 THE INKING — THE ONE MOMENT THE PAST BECOMES HISTORY (wave PA-1)

### 10.1 The seam (the leaf-first car `realmPast/realmPastInk.js :: inkRealmPastAt`, built and pinned before the host line)
In `store/campaignAdvanceSession.js :: runAdvanceCampaignWorld`, Phase 1, after `capturePulseSnapshot` and the drain, and before `simCampaign = cloneJson(c)`:

```

c.worldState = inkRealmPastAt(drained.worldState, c.regionalGraph, campaignSettlements(state, campaignId));   // was: c.worldState = drained.worldState;
```

One import and one changed line (700 → ≈702 effective lines). `inkRealmPastAt(worldState, regionalGraph, saves) → worldState` is pure over the values it reads (it only reads the producer's drafts and returns a new worldState), returns its input unchanged unless `realmPastActive(worldState.simulationRules)` and there is something pencilled (the birth past at tick 0, or a newcomer's ties, §12), and is idempotent (an inked world re-inks nothing). The car's own pins come first: dark ⇒ identity; dossiers byte-identical; idempotent; the snapshot taken before it.

### 10.2 What it writes (through the one writer, `realmPastWriter.js :: inkRealmPast`)
1. **The record.** The derived facts, with every choice applied, are RECORDED once in `realmPast.inked` (a crossed-out fact is not part of the realm's past and is not recorded; its choice stays in `choices` as provenance); `inkedAtTick: 0` is set and `inkedMembers` records every birth member at 0. From here the record is the one truth of the inked past (PA4, ⛔ PA-C-2).
2. **Today's consequences only, at tick 0, each with its back-pointer.** A pact still within its term (§7, PA-2); a pair's old feeling only where the plane's own memory law keeps it to birth (§6, ⛔ PA-C-1); at their owners' waves, a dated oath (PL) and a founding tie's departure memory (POP) (§13). Each landed row carries `pastRef` (the inked fact's `factRef`), and `causedBy` where the record keeps one (requirement 20); each inked fact names the row it seeded in `mintedAs`.
3. **Nothing else.** No feed entry — the Herald's feed clamps a past tick to 0 and keeps every entry of its first fifty-two weeks, so a long past would crowd the first year's live news; the realm's past has its own page. No relationship incident, no pulse-history record, no dossier byte.

### 10.3 The dossier landing (requirement 6)
The relationship desk (`display/relationshipChronicle.js`, "WHAT THIS RELATIONSHIP SURVIVED") reads the inked past through `pastFactsOf` as its SEVENTH durable archive, with no decay and no lookback cutoff, exactly as it reads the six it has (193 → ≈199 lines): open a pair and find what it survived before the realm's first week. The realm's past page — the review in its read-only form — holds the account (§11).

### 10.4 Who passes the seam, and what each does
The DM's advance (`useAdvanceSession.js`), the folder (`useCampaignAdvance.js`), the Surveyor's autonomy loop (`AutonomyPanel.jsx`, `{ autoResolve: true }`) and the living world's catch-up (`runCatchUpCampaignWorld`) all reach `runAdvanceCampaignWorld` through the slice wrapper, so each inks the past the same way the first time it moves the tick from 0. At instant birth every tone is `dm_advanced`, so the catch-up returns `not_living` and inks nothing. On a living or autonomous world a reopen IS a first advance with no press, which is why the review warns about it (§9.4); the first open with no cursor "SEEDS the cursor and advances nothing", so it inks nothing either. A first advance that pauses at a major has already inked in its Phase 1; its resume (`runResolveIntervalMajors`) continues the same interval and meets nothing pencilled. UNDOING the first advance restores the pre-ink worldState from the snapshot: the past is pencilled again, every choice intact; OD-FC-32's persisted undo (the newest two, unbuilt) makes that survive a reload.

### 10.5 Clock · tier · counterforce · receipt · felt
- **Clock.** Once, at the first advance (and once per newcomer, §12).
- **Tier.** Aspatial-safe; the pact arm needs pact formation lit (PA-2).
- **Counterforce.** The snapshot's undo (the inking is reversible exactly as the advance is); PA5 (only today's consequences reach the living world).

## §11 THE REALM'S ACCOUNT, AND EACH COURT'S VERSION (wave PA-1)

### 11.1 The account (TRUTH) — `realmPastAccountOf`
The short truth of the past as the inking finds it, for the DM, never the whole timeline: who founded whom; the old grudges still alive, which are the pairs the dossiers hold `rival`, `hostile` or `cold_war` today, each with the fact that explains it; the last war (the realm's most recent hostile counterpart); the pacts still standing; and every genesis contradiction. ### 11.2 Each court's version (BELIEF, derived on read — `courtPastOf`; never stored, LAW ONE)
- **Heard or never heard.** A court knows a fact when it is a party to it (a court knows itself) or when it lies within the tale's reach: hops over the regional graph at most the rumor road's own `rumorNetwork.js :: maxHopsFor(magnitudeBandOf(…))` for the fact's magnitude — the omniscient-arm idiom RN's `heardAt` uses ("inside the deed's WHOLE projected reach, at once"), with no relay timing, because the past has no ticks.
- **Remembered or faded.** What remains is `bandedStock.js :: decayTowardNeutral(magnitude01, 0, yearsBefore × 52, band)` on the spine's ONE decay shape (`a_generation` for a war-shaped fact or a founding, `a_few_years` for a pact's warmth; DRAFT bands, UNMEASURED, FP-46). Above a floor (DRAFT) the court tells it; below, it has forgotten it. This is how much a court REMEMBERS — a different quantity from the relationship plane's weekly scalar (§6.3), each under its one law.
- **Told true or worn.** Only in a belief infoMode (`unreliable`, `full`): the landed `degradeTelling(record, fork, digest)` with a fork keyed by the fact, the court and the hop (`realm-past-telling:<factRef>:<courtId>:<hop>`), deterministic, banded by one rung, swapping a name only for a real settlement's. Under `perfect_delayed` there is no wear; under `omniscient` (the default) every court's version is the truth it heard.
- **Who reads it.** The realm's past page ("as Varn tells it", on scrutiny) and the Why panel. It moves no decider: RN refuses a court's regard of a town, and a pair's standing is the landed pair state.
- **Felt.** (scrutiny) "In Varn they still tell how Ostmark's riders came over the ford; in Brindle, two valleys off, the tale has faded."

## §12 THE NEWCOMERS — A TOWN ADDED AFTER THE FIRST ADVANCE JOINS THE PAST AT ITS OWN FOUNDING (wave PA-3, under `realmPastEnabled`)

- **Its clock (PA8, PA14).** Its dossier's years are read on the realm's frozen clock as of the realm's week 0: its founding at `yearsBefore = age`, its events at `yearsBefore = yearsAgo`. The landed law forbids relating a dossier's frozen age to the moving calendar ("must never be compared or added"), and a save's real-time timestamp is not campaign time, so no re-anchoring is attempted (a re-anchoring by a save's timestamp and the calendar is refused). Where the DM knows that one of its recorded events happened while the realm was already living, the DM crosses that fact out; PA never links a newcomer to lived history (`pulseHistory`, a campaign-era event). Whether a DM-authored anchor is wanted is an open question (§22).
- **What it may link.** Its linkable events take counterparts among the members that existed at their dates (§4); its founding takes a mother town or a crown among the older members (§5); its existing ties may carry an old feeling (§6) and, where pact formation is lit, a past pact (§7). It never alters an inked fact of an older member; it adds facts beside them, and an older member's inked account gains the newcomer's lines.
- **Pencilled until the next advance.** From its joining until the next advance its facts are derived and pencilled — the user's, with the four pens, on the same review (§9). The car inks them at that advance (`inkedMembers[saveId] = tick`), after the snapshot, so undoing that advance makes them pencilled again. Its present-day consequences enter the landed ledgers at that tick, each with its back-pointer; a pact still within its term starts its remaining span there.
- **A member removed.** Before the inking its facts are simply not derived. After it, its inked facts stand as history (the town is gone, not its past), its references resolving to nothing and its links reading "a town the realm no longer holds".
- **Felt.** (review) "Thornwall joined the realm after its first week; its ties to the older towns are pencilled until the next advance."
