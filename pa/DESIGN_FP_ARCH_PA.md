## THE ANSWER, IN ONE PARAGRAPH (for the DM)

When a realm is born, every town already has its own past: when it was founded and by whom, and the alarms, plagues, debts, occupations and migrations it lived through, each so many years ago. But no town's past mentions another: the danger Varn armed against in its Siege comes from nowhere in particular, the creditor of Ashford's old debt is "an external power", and nobody knows which older town Kell's founders came from. THE REALM'S PAST fills those blanks from the realm itself. It lines the towns' histories up on one timeline, oldest first, starting when the second-oldest town was founded, and wherever a recorded event implies another party it may name a town that existed then and was near enough: the danger Varn armed against came out of Ostmark; Kell was founded by people out of Merewick; Ashford's debt was owed to Brindle. It names who, and never changes what happened: it changes no word of any town's own record, invents no conquest, new ruler, lost population or faith the record does not show, and never runs the world forward to get there. Before the first advance the whole past is pencilled in: you see each fact with the event it explains, on the review and in each town's own history, and you may cross it out, change it, confirm it or roll it again, for one town or the whole realm, and you see what else moves before anything changes. Where the realm's founding ties disagree with the towns' own founding ages, the past says so and leaves the choice to you; where the realm's two oldest towns were never tied, it offers you the tie to make. At the first advance the past is inked: it becomes the realm's history, the relationship desk remembers what each pair survived, and only what is still true today enters the living world, such as a pact still within its term. After that you may still cross out any fact, and if you later edit a town so that its own record and the realm's past disagree, the past says so instead of quietly rewriting itself. A town you add later joins the past at its own founding, and its facts stay pencilled until the next advance.

## §1 THE LAWS (PA1–PA18)

**PA1 — THE DOSSIER IS CANON (⭐ THE OWNER'S, OD-PA-1).** Every settlement's current dossier, generated or edited, is what the user intended, and the past is built around it, never into it. The past lives in its own layer (PA4) and points at a dossier's events by reference (PA6). It adds only what the dossier leaves open: it may name the other party of a raid, a debt, an occupation or a migration the dossier records, and it never invents a conquest, a change of rulers, a lost population or a faith the dossier does not show (PA9). The present-day ties it creates agree with the dossier's present: its government label, its named neighbour and that tie's type, its tensions and its stresses (`presentTiesAllowed`, §2.2). A user's later edit to a dossier wins, and the past re-reads it (PA6). THE BYTE LAW: for every member save `s`, `rawBitFormOf(s.settlement)` (`tests/helpers/dormancyOracle.js`, the standing claim) is equal before and after every PA act — a derivation, every choice of the user's, and the inking — compared over `settlement`, never over the save envelope (§19 rows 2–4).

**PA2 — THE PAST IS THE USER'S UNTIL THE FIRST ADVANCE (⭐ THE OWNER'S, OD-PA-2).** Every fact of the past is the user's to cross out or change before the world's first advance. Each fact is shown with its cause and the dossier event it explains. It can be crossed out, changed, confirmed or rolled again, for one town's ties or for the whole realm, with the knock-on shown before it applies (§9). A user's choice is intent; the rest is re-derived around it deterministically (the `chooseOrPin` shape: a chosen fact draws nothing, so the rest re-derives exactly). A contradiction with a dossier is flagged and never silently applied (§8). At the first advance the past is INKED and becomes history like any recorded event (§10). "The first advance" is the first advance that leaves `worldState.tick === 0`, whoever starts it (the DM, the folder, the Surveyor, a living world's catch-up); canonizing the world does not ink the past (the owner's word names the first advance), so the review stays open after canonize while the tick is still 0; a first advance that fails, throws or is abandoned before it commits inks nothing, so the past stays pencilled (§10.1); undoing the first advance returns the past to pencilled, with every choice intact (the pulse snapshot carries the sidecar).

**PA3 — FROM THE PAST, NEVER FORWARD (⭐ THE OWNER'S, OD-PA-3).** The past is replayed over the dossiers' own histories, event by event; no forward run before the first week changes the generated present. PA runs no tick, no kernel stage, no `advanceInterval.js :: simulateCampaignWorldInterval`, no chained `one_year` advance (OD-FC-51's EP-N is the forward run this law declines) and no landed per-tick rule over past years. The law is measured as well as owned: four real weeks changed every dossier's bytes and five centuries would take about 1.8 hours, while the event-driven replay costs milliseconds and leaves every dossier byte-identical (§0.3). The one forward read PA offers is the landed forecast's clone-and-discard preview of the first weeks AFTER the inking (§9.5), which changes nothing.

**PA4 — ONE HOME, DERIVED UNTIL INKED.** The past has ONE home, the sidecar `worldState.spatialLedgers.realmPast` (§2.1): DM-only, persisted with the campaign, carried by the pulse snapshot, at zero first-paint cost. Before the first advance it holds only the pen's log (the user's acts, the recorded whole-realm re-roll nonces among them); its address is the world's own `rngSeed`, read and never copied. Every fact is DERIVED on demand from the dossiers, the address and the log, so the derivation is the past's one truth, re-derived on demand, and a dossier edit is read at once: that is what lets the user cross out, change, confirm and roll again and see the knock-on. At the inking the derived facts are RECORDED ONCE in the sidecar's `inked` record, append-closed (the `concludedWars` idiom: lived history is never rewritten, folded or pruned), and from that moment the record is the one truth of the inked past and the derivation is never consulted for it; so no fact has two truths at once, and a later edit of one dossier can never silently rewrite another town's history. The dossiers stay canon for themselves: every read of the inked record re-checks each fact against the dossiers as they now stand and FLAGS one an edit contradicts, never redrawing it, and the DM may cross out any inked fact (PA6, §9.2), so a user's later edit still wins on every surface. No negative tick and no "past" flag ever enters a landed ledger: four of six landed readers clamp, drop or throw on one, and the allowlist rebuilders drop an unnamed field (executed, `findings/R-PA3-probe/negtick.out`).

**PA5 — ONE INKING, AT ONE PLACE, AND ONLY TODAY'S CONSEQUENCES.** At the first advance the past becomes history at ONE place, `store/campaignAdvanceSession.js :: runAdvanceCampaignWorld`, on the simulation clone that Phase 1 lifts out of the store and before the compute, through the leaf-first car `realmPast/realmPastInk.js :: inkRealmPastAt` (§10), so the inked record reaches the campaign only inside Phase 2's one atomic commit, with the advance it belongs to. Only the past's PRESENT-DAY consequences enter the landed ledgers, at tick 0, each carrying a back-pointer to the past fact it comes from (its `pastRef`, and its `causedBy` where the record keeps one): a past pact still within its term, only through the past-origin arm of GR's signer (§7), and a dated oath at PL's wave (§13). A pair's old feeling and a founding tie have no landed consequence (§5.4, §6). Everything else stays in the sidecar, where the review, each town's history section, the relationship desk and the cause walk read it.

**PA6 — A REFERENCE NAMES WHAT THE DOSSIER SAYS.** A dossier event is referenced by the triple (the member's save id, the event's `name`, its `yearsAgo`) — the key Edit Mode's merge (`recordRegister.js`: "`'history.historicalEvents': 'name'`") and the landed readers (`legacyGenerator.js`, `historyBeats.js`) already use; never by position (`historyPreservation.js`: "positional index is worthless across a reroll") and never by a minted id (an id on the dossier would be a dossier write). The member is named by its SAVE ID, the more durable of its two identities: the service's id is stable for the member's life in its world and is remapped by an account import's own id map (`accountImportBody.js`: "remaps settlementIds through the oldId→newId map"), while the dossier's `_seed` is stripped by every import path (`accountImport.js` and `galleryImportSettlement.js`: "`_seed: undefined`") and is absent on a gallery-imported member; so a carry of the record across an import remaps through that map (§18.1). The founding is referenced as (save id, its part: the founder, the founding's challenge or its rescue), each part its own fact; a current tension as (save id, `tension`, its `type`). A reference is re-resolved on EVERY read. One that no longer resolves — the user removed the event, changed its name or its date, or regenerated the town — drops its LINK and only its link: before the first advance the fact is simply not derived again and a user's choice on it is flagged on the review ("the town's record no longer holds this event"), never silently dropped; after the first advance the inked fact stands and reads "a past the town's record no longer holds". After the first advance every read also re-runs the agreement check (`pastAgreesWithDossier`) against the dossiers as they now stand, and an inked fact that an edit now contradicts — an edited founding age, a changed government label — is FLAGGED on the surface ("the realm's record and Kell's own history now disagree"), never redrawn; the DM may cross it out (§9.2). A regeneration ("roll again" on a dossier, or a history section reroll) is the user's own edit and is read the same way: an event the new record still holds under the same name and date in the same town is, by this law, the same event and keeps its link; every other link drops. PA reads only generated events: a campaign-era entry (`campaignEra: true`) is lived history, never a past event to link.

**PA7 — ONE STREAM PER FACT; THE SAME SEED AND THE SAME CHOICES GIVE THE SAME PAST.** Every draw of the past is taken from its own labelled stream, a ROOT COMPOSITION in Edit Mode's idiom (`pools.js :: rollFrom`: "THE KEY IS A ROOT COMPOSITION, NEVER A FORK"): `` `realm-past:${rngSeed}:${nonce}:${factKey}:${rollIndex}` `` for every fact and every growth step. `rngSeed` is the world's own address (§2.1). `factKey` is the fact's `factRef` (§2.2) with each save id replaced by its dossier's stable id — `ev:${stableId}:${eventName}:${yearsAgo}`, `found:${stableId}`, `found-challenge:${stableId}`, `found-rescue:${stableId}`, `pact:${stableIdA}|${stableIdB}:${yearsBefore}:${occasionName}` and so on — so every fact, a founding's tie, its challenge and its rescue included, draws on a stream of its own. `stableId` is `crossSettlementConflicts.js :: stableIdOf` ("`String(settlement?._seed ?? settlement?.id ?? settlement?.name ?? '')`"), never the service-minted save id; where two members share one (a dossier with no `_seed` in the realm twice), the later in the campaign's `settlementIds` order takes the suffix `#2`, `#3` …, so no two members ever share a stream. `nonce` is the newest standing whole-realm re-roll (empty until one is made) and `rollIndex` is the fact's own re-roll count (0 until one is made). So crossing out or re-rolling one fact moves no other (executed: with one shared stream a strike moves later links; with one stream per fact it does not, `cure/L-PA-probe/replay-fork.out`). Members iterate oldest first, with codepoint order on `stableId` and then the member's place in `settlementIds` as the total tie-break; no `Date.now()`, no `Math.random()`, no `localeCompare`. THE DETERMINISM PIN: the same campaign derived twice is byte-identical; two births of the same seed derive the same past once each save id is mapped to its dossier's `_seed`.

**PA8 — THE PAST'S TIME IS THE DOSSIER'S.** Every fact carries `yearsBefore`, a non-negative integer of years before the realm's week 0 on the dossiers' FROZEN clock — the dossier's own unit (`yearsAgo`; `eventsTimeline[].year = age − yearsAgo`). A realm year is DERIVED for display (§3) and never stored; a fact before week 0 has no tick; and `yearsBefore` is never compared with or added to `calendar.year` or a tick ("two clocks over one world … must never be compared or added", `historyGenerator.js`). A consequence that enters a landed ledger at the inking carries tick 0 and finds its age through its `pastRef`.

**PA9 — THE PAST SUPPLIES WHAT THE DOSSIER LEAVES OPEN, AND NOTHING ELSE.** PA produces: (i) a counterpart for a LINKABLE dossier event (§4.1), a town of the realm that existed then and was within reach — every implied party is anonymous, so filling one contradicts no word, and where none fits the event stays local; (ii) founding ties, who founded whom (§5); (iii) a pair's old grudge or old bond on an EXISTING edge, consistent with the pair's dossier type, kept as a record and never as a pair scalar (§6); (iv) the date of an existing oath (PL's wave, §13); (v) past pacts, of which only those still within their term enter the landed treaties at the inking, only through the past-origin arm of GR's signer and only where pact formation is lit (§7); (vi) the realm's account at the inking, as truth, with each court's version derived on read (§11); (vii) where the realm's two oldest towns share no dossier tie, a pencilled OFFER of one, which only the DM's own act through the dossier's link card can write (§6.1). PA NEVER produces: a write to any dossier — so neither `relationships/neighbourBackLink.js :: buildNeighbourBackLink`, nor the traditions' relations, nor the link card's handler, all of which write dossiers, is ever PA's writer; any row of `relationshipStates` (the plane's one writer is its own, §6.2); town-to-town regard (RN refuses the pair; the feeling is the landed pair state); a menace (MN's, by the DM only); a polity's NAME on its own authority (PA OFFERS a name as a pencilled fact at PL's wave, and the DM's keep is the christening, §13); a vassal chain without a dossier edge (PA dates an existing oath and never invents one); a person's deed (no durable person id exists at birth, RN16, so a past pact names no signatory).

**PA10 — THE GENESIS TIES ARE CANON; WHERE THEY CONTRADICT THE FOUNDING AGES, THE PAST SAYS SO.** Plan law 2 writes 3, 8 or 12 founding ties into the dossiers at instant birth (`genesisDiplomacy.js :: materializeGenesisRelations`), by tier alone. They are dossier facts, canon under PA1. PA reads each one and EXPLAINS it where it agrees with the two towns' founding ages (an overlord older than its vassal; a patron older than its client) and FLAGS it on the review and in the account where it does not (a younger overlord of an older town) — for the user, never fixed and never linked around (§8). Whether PA's ordering may inform plan law 2 at birth in a later wave is the owner's (OPEN row OD-PA-13b).

**PA11 — TRUTH ONCE, BELIEF ON READ.** The past is held once, as truth: the derivation before the first advance, the inked record after it. Each court's version — whether it heard of a fact, how much of it it still remembers, how it tells it — is DERIVED ON READ (§11): the reach of the tale over the regional graph by the rumor road's own magnitude bands, its fading by the spine's one decay shape (`bandedStock.js :: decayTowardNeutral`), its distortion only in a belief infoMode through the landed `degradeTelling`. **PA12 — ONLY LIT RULES GROW THE PAST, AND EACH PROGRAM REGISTERS ITS OWN.** PA's own derivations — the timeline, the links, the founding ties and the pairs' old feelings — are PA's rules and need no other program lit. - **THE ADDRESS.** The past's address is `worldState.rngSeed`, READ and never copied (one truth per quantity). It is written once, at birth (`worldPulse/worldState.js :: createDefaultWorldState`: "``rngSeed: `world-pulse:${seedPart}` ``"), and kept by every normalize ("`rngSeed: raw?.rngSeed || base.rngSeed`"). On an instant world it is seed-derived: the composer builds its world state over its own seed-derived campaign id (`composeInstantWorld.js`: "``iw::${seedRef.plan?.seed}::${_idN++}``"), and `runInstantWorld` carries that world state through its remint of the campaign id. On a hand-built world it is derived from the campaign id. So an untouched world and a world with choices derive on one root, and the user's first act moves no other fact.
- **Absent until needed; zero first-paint bytes.** A world the user has not touched carries no record: its past is derived from the address alone. The record is created by the one writer at the user's first act or at the inking, whichever comes first, and nests under `spatialLedgers` (`setSpatialLedger`), the namespace whose new members "cost ZERO first-paint bytes". Drop-when-empty (`dropSpatialLedger`) holds for a record emptied by undo before the inking.
- **THE PEN'S LOG, and a fact's standing.** A fact's standing is DERIVED by reading the log in order, in the `chooseOrPin` shape; its newest standing act decides:
  - a cross-out leaves it `user_crossed_out`: it contributes nothing;
  - a change leaves it `user_changed`, with the value the DM gave;
  - a confirm leaves it `user_confirmed`, with the value it showed when confirmed. That value stands: the fact draws nothing, and the value survives every re-roll, re-derivation, added channel or regeneration that keeps its reference, as Edit Mode's pin does ("It does NOT advance the stream when a pin is present … which is what makes a pinned re-derive reproduce the record");
  - a re-roll returns it to `as_derived` at its next `rollIndex`, the count of its standing re-rolls.

  The newest standing realm re-roll gives the nonce. Before the inking the pen is a pencil: an undo takes the newest act off the log. So change, change, undo leaves the first change; re-roll, confirm, undo leaves the re-roll; and a realm re-roll is undone in its own place among the choices. From the inking the log is APPEND-ONLY. Every act before it stands as the inked past's provenance, and every later act is added and never removed: the one post-inking verb (cross out), or its undo, which appends a lift of the newest standing post-inking cross-out (`lifts`, §9.2).
- **THE MARKER, and the closed record.** The birth past is PENCILLED while PA is active, `worldState.tick === 0` and `inkedAtTick` is absent; it is INKED once `inkedAtTick` is present. A world past tick 0 whose record has no `inkedAtTick` (its first advance came while PA was dark) is CLOSED (`pastIsClosed`): nothing derives, nothing inks, no surface reads it, and its log stays as it was (PA17). A world past tick 0 with no record never ran.
- **Why `spatialLedgers` and not the campaign record:** the pulse snapshot clones `worldState` (so undoing the first advance returns the past to pencilled, with every choice intact), `WORLD_SNAPSHOT_HARD_DENY` keeps it DM-only, and it rides the campaign's persistence and cloud sync with no new top-level key. A top-level campaign field would not be restored by the snapshot, which clones the campaign's `worldState`, `regionalGraph`, `wizardNews` and member saves only. It is never on a dossier (PA1) and never in the record register (L10 (e): worldState-side state is lived history outside `rederive`).
- **Size.** Before the inking: the log, tens of bytes an act. After it: one `InkedFact` per fact, tens of facts for the largest instant realm (fifty-eight dossier events of which most are linkable or local), a few kilobytes beside a worldState of 175–505 KB (`findings/R-PA2-probe/tick.mjs`).
- **ONE WRITER:** `src/domain/realmPast/realmPastWriter.js`, with two entry points — `applyRealmPastChoice(record, act)` (the verbs, before and after the inking, §9.2; called from the store leaf `store/realmPastActions.js`) and `inkRealmPast(record, facts, tick)` (the inking, §10; called by the car `realmPastInk.js :: inkRealmPastAt`). Nothing else writes the record; the DM writes it only through the verbs. The writer imports nothing from the world-pulse engine: a verb's payload carries what the review shows (a confirm carries each fact's shown value), so no derivation runs inside it.

## §3 THE TIMELINE — ONE REALM CLOCK OVER THE DOSSIERS' FROZEN YEARS (wave PA-1)

### 3.1 What it reads (TRUTH)
Each member's `history.age` (the founding, `founding.age` equal to it by the dossier's own consistency group), each generated event's `yearsAgo`, and each member's stable id and place in `settlementIds` for the tie-break. Never `traditions/genesis.js`'s second age (its `AGE_BY_TIER` is a different, synthesized number), never `calendar.year`, never a tick.

### 3.2 The clock (integer arithmetic, no new clock)
- Every fact's coordinate is `yearsBefore`: years before the realm's week 0 on the frozen clock. A town's founding is at `yearsBefore = age`; a generated event at `yearsBefore = yearsAgo` (PA8).
- THE REALM'S ORIGIN is the oldest founding, `A* = max(age)`; a REALM YEAR, shown on scrutiny only, is `A* − yearsBefore` (the realm's founding year 0, its present `A*`). Stored nowhere.
- THE CLOCK STARTS at the second-oldest founding (`yearsBefore = age` of the second-oldest town): the first moment two towns can know each other, in the owner's words "starting with the oldest and second oldest's history". Every event older than that has no other town to meet and stays local by construction (5.4% of events, §0.3).
- Equal ages break by codepoint order on the stable id (`stableIdOf`: the `_seed`, else the dossier's id or name), then by the member's place in the campaign's `settlementIds`, never `localeCompare` (PA7).
- A town of age 0 (`settlementAgeMode: 'new'`) joins at the present with nothing to link. The opt-in ancient ruin (`yearsAgo` past its town's founding) is a local event before the clock, placed at its own year. A custom age up to 5,000 years costs nothing extra: the derivation steps by EVENT, never by year (PA3), so its cost is the realm's event count (milliseconds, §0.3).

### 3.3 Clock · tier · counterforce · receipt
- **Clock (requirement 9).** The past has no cadence in engine time: it is derived on demand before the first advance and recorded once at it. Its FIRST-VISIBLE latency is ZERO advances: the review stands at birth, before the first week (requirement 16). Its slow verdict is the inking.
- **Tier (requirement 17).** Aspatial-safe; the clock needs only the dossiers.
- **Counterforce (requirement 2).** The timeline is arithmetic over canon and moves nothing; the past as a whole has two counterforces — the dossier (PA1: every fact must agree with it, and a later edit wins) and the DM's pen (PA2: any fact can be crossed out before it is inked).
- **Surface / scrutiny.** SURFACE: the review lists the realm's towns oldest first, each with its founding told in the dossier's own words. SCRUTINY: the realm years and the clock's start. MACHINERY: `A*`, the tie-break.

Current TENSIONS are present facts, never past events: a `trade_dispute` or `external_threat` tension is not linked; it is a fact the present ties must AGREE with (`presentTiesAllowed`, §2.2). A POWER, a MENACE and a FAITH are never a counterpart in PA's own rules: a power waits on PL's polity, a menace is the DM's alone (MN12), a faith waits on FAITH's wave — so where the text implies one of them and no realm town fits, the event stays local ("a link is never forced").

### 4.2 The candidates
A member `T` is a candidate for an event of town `A` when every one holds: `T` is not `A`; `T` existed at the event's date (`age_T ≥ yearsBefore`); `T` is within reach (§4.3); and the link's present form agrees with both dossiers (`pastAgreesWithDossier`): a past occupier is never made a present liege, and a debt's creditor is of no lesser tier. Where the debtor's own event says the payments go on (its `lastingEffects` hold "Tribute payments to the external creditor continue on a schedule"), the creditor is a present fact as well as a past one, so it must be a member the dossiers ALREADY tie to the debtor by a link whose type admits payment (`trade_partner`; `patron_of`/`client_of` or `overlord_of`/`vassal_of` with the creditor the senior); with no such tie the creditor stays beyond the realm. A debt whose record does not say the payments go on takes its creditor in the past tense alone. Where the dossier NAMES the other party — `neighborRelationship.name` resolving to a member, or a dossier link (`neighbourNetwork`) whose type fits the event (a `hostile`, `rival` or `cold_war` neighbour for a threat) — that member is the counterpart, drawn by nobody (cause `named_neighbour`: the pairing is reused, never contradicted).

### 4.3 Reach
Reach is hops over the regional graph as it stands at the derivation — its edges and its discovered channels (at instant birth every channel is `suggested`, and the composer discovers them twice) — at most `PAST_REACH_HOPS` (DRAFT, UNMEASURED, on the FP-46 roster: two for a threat, an occupation, a purge or a migration; three for a debt, a trade collapse or an iron dispute). On the aspatial arm (no graph) every member is within reach. When the map lands, reach follows whatever the regional graph carries, with no change here (PA16). Car-0 measures the hop distribution at birth on the three realm sizes (§17 PA-0).

### 4.4 The draw (one stream per fact, PA7)
Among the candidates and ONE OUTSIDE OUTCOME — the party came from beyond the realm, so the event stays local — ONE draw on the fact's own stream picks the counterpart. The weights (DRAFT, UNMEASURED, FP-46): base 1; nearness `1 / (1 + hops)`; today's agreement — for a hostile event, a pair the dossiers hold `rival`, `hostile` or `cold_war` ×2 (the old wound explains today's rivalry) and a pair they hold `allied` ×0.25 (history can heal, so never forbidden); standing — the greater town ×1.5 for a creditor or a threat's source; and the outside outcome `PAST_OUTSIDE_WEIGHT` (DRAFT 1.0 against the candidates' total; car-0 measures the share of linkable events that link, so the realm is never the whole world). THE CAUSE WORD is the leading term, closed (`PAST_LINK_CAUSES`, the seven of §2.3: `named_neighbour · sworn_today · today_rival · nearest_town · greater_town · from_beyond · oldest_pair`. An event's link names one of `named_neighbour`, `today_rival`, `nearest_town`, `greater_town` and `from_beyond`; `sworn_today` is the crown drawn by nobody (§5.2) and `oldest_pair` the offer's cause (§6.1)): it is the review's and the Why panel's one reason.

### 4.5 What it writes
Nothing before the inking: a link is derived. At the inking it is recorded (PA4) and its present form, if any, is decided by §6 (a pair's old feeling) or §7 (a pact).

### 4.6 Clock · tier · counterforce · receipt · felt
- **Clock.** As §3.3: derived on demand; first-visible at birth.
- **Tier.** Aspatial-safe; spatial reach where a graph exists; the degraded arm is "every member within reach".
- **Counterforce.** The outside outcome (a link is never forced); PA1's agreement (a candidate that disagrees with either dossier never shows); the DM's pen (§9).
- **Receipt.** Each link shows the dossier event it explains (name, town, years ago, in the dossier's words), its counterpart, its cause word, and the agreement it passed; `causedBy` on every consequence the inking mints names the link's `factRef` (requirement 20).
- **Felt (surface).** "The danger Varn armed against in the Siege came out of Ostmark." · "Ashford's old debt was owed to Brindle." · (outside) "The danger Varn armed against came from beyond the realm." Each sentence keeps the record's own deed: PA names WHO a recorded event came from, and never upgrades what happened.

## §5 THE FOUNDING TIES — WHO FOUNDED WHOM (wave PA-1; its present form at POP's wave)

### 5.1 The founder table (`FOUNDER_CLASSES`, frozen, keyed on the exact `FOUNDERS_BY_TIER` strings — never a regex — and pinned total against them by a SOURCE-TEXT read: the strings are a function-local literal inside a generator, "`const FOUNDERS_BY_TIER = {`" in `narrativeGenerator.js :: genArrivalDetail`, which PA15 forbids touching, so the pin reads that file as text, extracts every quoted founder of the block, and fails on one the table does not class)

- **A CROWN** (the town was granted or decreed): "a noble granted lands by the crown and obligated to populate them" · "a noble granted lands by the crown with the resources to develop them" · "royal decree and a century of forced investment" · "imperial decree and the systematic forced relocation of skilled populations" · "a dynastic decision that this crossroads would anchor the realm".

### 5.2 The rule
- **A mother town** `M` of a town `K`: older (`age_M > age_K`), of no lesser tier (`tier(M) ≥ tier(K)`, so a thorp never mothers a city), within reach (§4.3, DRAFT three hops), and agreeing with both dossiers; one draw on `K`'s founding stream among the candidates and the outside outcome (the mother may lie beyond the realm, or be gone). Cause words: `nearest_town` · `greater_town` · `from_beyond`.
- **A founder that implies an event in the mother.** Three founders say something happened in the town they left, and PA never writes that into a mother whose own record does not show it (PA1: no lost population, no war or persecution the dossier does not hold). So each takes as mother only a member whose OWN dossier records a matching event within the window before `K`'s founding (`age_K ≤ yearsAgo ≤ age_K + PAST_FOUNDING_WINDOW`, DRAFT ten years, FP-46); otherwise the mother stays beyond the realm (`from_beyond`). The matches form the frozen table `FOUNDER_MOTHER_EVENTS` (DRAFT; templates by `templateType`):
  - "refugees fleeing war or persecution who found the location defensible" — the mother records `external_threat`, `occupation_legacy`, `infiltration_fear`, `religious_tension` or `heresy_trial`;
  - "escaped serfs seeking a place beyond the reach of their former masters" — the mother records `tyranny` or `popular_uprising`;
  - "a community of tradespeople who left a larger settlement under contested circumstances" — the mother records `guild_conflict`, `economic_disparity`, `corruption_scandal` or `succession_crisis`.

  The matched event is then what the founding tie also explains, and the review shows it beside the tie. "a merchant coalition that grew too large for the town they started in" implies growth, not loss, and takes its mother by the plain rule above.
- **A crown** of `K`: an older member whose government is `apex` (Royal Authority) or a ducal seat (Ducal Governorship), within reach. Where `K`'s label `owes_fealty` and a dossier link already binds `K` to such a member as its overlord, that member is the crown, drawn by nobody (cause `sworn_today`). Otherwise the draw, with the outside outcome (a crown beyond the realm stays local until PL's polity can name it, §13).
- **The founding's challenge and its rescue** are founding-era events (§4.1), each its own fact (`found-challenge:<saveId>`, `found-rescue:<saveId>`), linked by §4's rules at `yearsBefore = age_K`.

### 5.3 An edited founding
Where the user has edited the founder (`settlement._userEdits['history.founding.foundedBy']` present), PA reads the user's words through the same frozen table: an edited sentence matches no row, so PA offers no founding tie and never parses the prose (MF's rule). The DM may CHANGE the fact to name the mother town (§9) — the DM's pen, never the engine's guess.

### 5.4 Its present form

Its lasting home is the inked record, read on the relationship desk, in each town's history strip and in the account ("Kell still calls Merewick its mother town"). ## §6 THE PAIRS' OLD FEELINGS — AN OLD GRUDGE OR AN OLD FRIENDSHIP ON AN EXISTING TIE, KEPT IN THE RECORD (wave PA-1)

### 6.1 What the past derives (TRUTH)
For every EXISTING tie — a pair the dossiers already link (`neighbourNetwork`, the genesis ties among them; an edge of the regional graph, never a channel) — the past derives at most one `pair_grudge` or one `pair_bond`, from the facts between the two: a grudge from a hostile counterpart (a threat, an occupation, a purge, a trade collapse, a debt); a bond from a founding tie, a founding rescue or a past pact. Its MAGNITUDE is the strongest such fact's band; its DATE is that fact's `yearsBefore`; it never changes the pair's type, which is dossier canon (PA1). Where the facts run against today's type (an old threat from a town the dossiers now hold `allied`), the past says so in the account — "an old wound the alliance has outlived" — and contradicts nothing. A pair with no tie gets no feeling: a relationship state exists only on a regional-graph edge (`relationshipKeyFromEdge`), and an edge exists only where a dossier link exists.

**The limit, plainly, and the oldest pair's offer.** A pair the dossiers never linked gets a shared past and no living tie: PA may record that Kell's founders came out of Merewick, but it cannot make the two towns neighbours, because the one door to a tie is a dossier link and PA writes no dossier (PA1). That limit reaches the owner's own named pair, "the oldest and second oldest". In two of the three executed instant realms the two oldest towns share no dossier tie (`cure/D-PA-r2-probe/ages.out` over `findings/R-PA2-probe/out-*.json`: the small realm's 246- and 195-year-old towns and the medium realm's 296- and 219-year-old towns are unlinked; the large realm's two oldest are allied), because genesis ties are drawn by tier alone. So where the realm's two oldest members share no tie, the past derives ONE pencilled `tie_offer` between them (`offer:<saveIdA>|<saveIdB>`). The offer carries its cause: the strongest fact the two share, or, where they share none, `oldest_pair` ("the realm's two oldest towns have never been tied"). It also carries a suggested type from the landed closed list `canonicalRelationship.js :: RELATIONSHIP_SELECTIONS`: `rival` where their strongest shared fact is a grudge, `allied` where it is a bond, otherwise `neutral` (DRAFT), and never a type `presentTiesAllowed` forbids for either town. Never a liege tie (OD-PA-28).

The offer is shown in the review beside the facts it rests on. Its "confirm" takes the DM to the dossier's own link card — "Link a Neighbouring Settlement" in the settlement record (`SettlementDetail.jsx` → `LinkNeighbourCard` → `SettlementsPanel.jsx :: handleLink`) — opened, with the partner selected and the suggested type set, either of which the DM may change before Apply. The DM's Apply there is the write: `handleLink` writes both dossiers' `neighbourNetwork` and their inter-settlement rows and persists them at once. That is the user's own intent, which PA1 makes canon; PA records nothing for it.

The landed card opens only by hand, so opening it this way amends the door in three cars. Each is sized by eslint's own `max-lines` count over a prototype of its edit on a copy of the code of record (executed, `cure/D-PA-r2-probe/proto.out`):
- **The route.** The review's own leaf, `RealmPastReview.jsx`, takes the DM to the town by the landed cross-view bridge that `map/useRealmEntityNav.js` already chains: `mapSlice.js :: setSelectedSettlementId(a)` and `hooks/useRoute.js :: navigate('settlements', { params: { id: a }, search })`, with the request carried in the address's query (`?linkTo=<b>&linkAs=<type>`). So the route needs no store field, and `WorldPulsePanel.jsx` takes no line beyond the review's mount (558 → ≈564).
- **The open toggle.** The card opens on `linking`, which is local state of `SettlementsPanel.jsx` (`useState(false)`) handed down to the detail. The panel gains the prefill's state and one effect. Once the requested save's detail is open, the effect reads the query, sets `linking` and the prefill, and drops the query from the address (`navigate(…, { replace: true })`). The panel's route→detail effect already opens the save, and its detail→route effect compares the path alone, so the query survives until it is read: 579 → ≈587.

### 6.2 What reaches the relationship plane: nothing
PA writes NOTHING to `relationshipStates`, ever: no scalar, no row, no field and no incident, on any pair of any memory horizon. The plane's own laws decide it, three times over:
- **The plane has one writer, and it is the plane's.** "ONE writer (applyRelationshipPatch — law 13's sanctioned applicator idiom …) … this is the single chokepoint … no hand-editing of relationshipStates anywhere" (`relationshipEvolution.js`). - **No horizon exists to key a lasting feeling on.** The one horizon that keeps an old feeling, `undying`, is a SETTLEMENT facet (`relationshipEvolution.js :: memoryHorizonBandOf` reads `facetOf(settlement, 'memoryHorizon')`, and an edge takes the larger of its two ends' horizons, `combineMemoryHorizon`). A second, slower decay law across the birth line is refused (one decay law per quantity), and so is a feeling seeded as if fresh, which the plane would cool within a season (a fiction about its date). A pair's old feeling therefore lives in the inked record and nowhere in the living plane; PA mints no pair scalar and no incident.

- **Tier.** Aspatial-safe.
- **Counterforce.** The dossier's type (never contradicted); the DM's pen.

## §11 THE REALM'S ACCOUNT, AND EACH COURT'S VERSION (wave PA-1)

### 11.1 The account (TRUTH) — `realmPastAccountOf`
The short truth of the past as the inking recorded it, for the DM, never the whole timeline: who founded whom; the old grudges still alive, which are the pairs the dossiers hold `rival`, `hostile` or `cold_war` today, each with the fact that explains it; the most recent old enmity (the realm's latest hostile counterpart, told as the record's own deed: a threat, an occupation, a purge, a debt — never a war the record does not hold); the pacts still standing; and every genesis contradiction. It is never redrawn.

### 11.2 Each court's version (BELIEF, derived on read — `courtPastOf`; never stored, LAW ONE)
- **Heard or never heard.** A court knows a fact when it is a party to it (a court knows itself) or when it lies within the tale's reach: hops over the regional graph (§4.3) at most the rumor road's own budget, `rumorNetwork.js :: maxHopsFor(record)`, which reads a RECORD ("`if (record && record.significance === 'major') { return Number(record.score) >= 95 ? 5 : 4; } return 2;`"), never a bare band. PA builds that record by RN's own significance rule for a deed's `projectScope` ("`major` when its magnitude band is ≥ 2 … else `notable`", `DESIGN_FP_ARCH_RN.md` §3.2): a fact of magnitude 2 or 3 is `{ significance: 'major' }` and is heard up to four hops away; a fact of magnitude 0 or 1 is `{ significance: 'notable' }` and is heard up to two. No score is set, so no past fact reaches the fifth hop. This is the omniscient-arm idiom RN's `heardAt` uses ("inside the deed's WHOLE projected reach, at once"), with no relay timing, because the past has no ticks.
- **Remembered or faded.** What remains is `bandedStock.js :: decayTowardNeutral(magnitude01, 0, yearsBefore × 52, band)` on the spine's ONE decay shape (`a_generation` for a war-shaped fact or a founding, `a_few_years` for a pact's warmth; DRAFT bands, UNMEASURED, FP-46). Above a floor (DRAFT) the court tells it; below, it has forgotten it. This is how much a court REMEMBERS — a different quantity from the relationship plane's weekly scalar (§6.3), each under its one law.
- **Told true or worn.** Only in a belief infoMode (`unreliable`, `full`): the landed `rumorNetwork.js :: degradeTelling(record, fork, digest)`, which weathers ONE hop of a `RumorArrivalRecord` (`{ completeness01, accuracy01, content: RumorContent }`). PA's adapter shapes the fact as a telling that starts whole — `completeness01: 1`, `accuracy01: 1`, `content: { what: <the fact's kind>, whereId: a, scope: 'regional', magnitude, partyIds: [a, b] }` — and weathers it once per hop along the walk's path to the court: the shortest path, codepoint-first among equals. Each hop's fork is keyed per (fact, edge, hop), `realm-past-telling:<factRef>:<edgeId>:<hop>`, by the rumor road's fork law ("per event+carrier+edge+hop, NEVER per settlement"; the past has one carrier, the realm's memory). So two courts on one path share its wear, the telling is deterministic, the band drifts only as the road's own weathering drifts it, and a name is swapped only for a real settlement's. Under `perfect_delayed` there is no wear; under `omniscient` (the default) every court's version is the truth it heard.
- **Who reads it.** The realm's past page ("as Varn tells it", on scrutiny) and the Why panel. It is derived over `inkedPastOf`'s lines, so no court tells a fact the DM crossed out. It moves no decider: RN refuses a court's regard of a town, and a pair's standing is the landed pair state.
- **Felt.** (scrutiny) "In Varn they still tell how the danger came out of Ostmark; in Brindle, two valleys off, the tale has faded."
