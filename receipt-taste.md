### M.11 N, AND ITS CONFIDENCE

**N = the whole population, because the walk is cheap and the harness measures the cost rather
than quoting one.** 528 microseconds per composed unit on the planted set; the exhaustive walk of
36 units costs 19 ms, and the rule is: under a second exhaustive, otherwise N = 384. **384 is the
widest 95 % normal half-width on an arm's fire rate (at p = 0.5) being 5.0 percentage points**,
which is the resolution the sitting reads a rate at. The sample sha is printed on every run and is
reproducible from N alone (a deterministic stride over the sorted addresses, never a seeded draw —
the `fnv1a32` ratchet forbids the alternative).
