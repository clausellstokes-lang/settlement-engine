--- NOTES

**The three variants, and what separates them.** The card licenses ONE claim (`may claim: that
`covert` holds, as a STANDING fact of the record`), so the three semantic variants are three
shapes of that one claim and are declared as such rather than smuggled as three facts: V1 puts
the purchase in the main clause and the covertness in the coordinate one; V2 leads on the
covertness and seats the purchase under it; V3 states the divided keeping and the silence as one
predicate. No variant adds a fact to another. Slot sets are equal inside every variant (arm A6):
V1 and V3 carry none, V2 carries `{settlement}` on all four faces.

**Per face, the clause of the card that licenses each claim it makes.**

V1 `[plain]` "The town's watch answers a buyer, and the arrangement is covert."
- *the watch is bought* — `may claim: that covert holds`; `reads: compromised.covert`, whose
  measured content is a security institution captured by an unexposed corrupt member.
- *covert* — the same clause; the word is the field's own leaf, so `armA0b`'s token test passes
  on this face without a vocabulary entry.
- *a buyer* — carried by `bought` in the pool key; no name, no office, no count (`may NOT: a
  count`; REFUSED COLUMNS: a named character).
- `answers` gives the clause the INSTITUTION detector's verb, so the text classifies as the
  `MOVE: INSTITUTION` the annex row declares.

V1 `[face]` "The town keeps a watch that has been got at, and got at quietly."
- *got at* — the same single claim in the plainest idiom the estate's register admits (§21.4,
  the density law: idiom that rewards the reader is part of the ceiling).
- *quietly* — the covert qualifier, written as a property of what is public and never of what
  anyone knows (see the refusal on knowledge below).
- Doubling `got at` is the office's own formula repeated on the WORD, never on the FACT
  (register card: "The WORD may recur; the FACT must not").

V1 `[face]` "In name the watch is the town's; in fact it is bought, and unremarked."
- *in name / in fact* — the compressed form of the same claim; the town's nominal holding of its
  own watch is the block's own premise (the pool attaches only to `WALLED-*` spines of a settled
  town), not a new fact.
- *unremarked* — the covert qualifier.
- The semicolon is the one in twelve faces (R-DA-06 rations it; the shipped spines of this block
  already carry the device, so the pool is not being handed a shape its siblings lack).

V1 `[face]` "With nothing public to show, the watch this town keeps has been sold."
- *sold* — `bought`, from the other side of the same transaction; one claim.
- *nothing public to show* — the covert qualifier as an absence of PUBLIC record, not an absence
  in the world (R-DA-08's rule for a gap; and it is a qualifier on the pool's own claim, not the
  ABSENCE move the annex refuses on a modifier's `MOVE:` line).

V2 `[plain]` "The watch {settlement} keeps is bought, and nothing public attaches to the buying."
- *bought* / *nothing public* — as above. `{settlement}` is the card's bag (`bag: {defwork:
  bare-common, settlement: proper}`, FILLED `{settlement}`); it never opens the sentence (arm A9,
  T-F8), and `{defwork}` is not used because the call sites do not fill it.

V2 `[face]` "Covertly, and undeclared, the watch {settlement} keeps has been suborned."
- *covertly* — the field's own leaf again (A0b token).
- *suborned* — the exact legal verb for a bought officer; the antique air sits in the word, not
  in the syntax (R-DA-18).

V2 `[face]` "Bought, and quietly, is the watch {settlement} keeps."
- The shortest face in the set; "the short line exists" (register card; R-DA-06's `< 8` floor is
  approached, not crossed, at eight words).

V2 `[face]` "Out of public view, the watch {settlement} keeps has changed hands."
- *changed hands* — the claim by implicature: the body the town keeps now answers a second
  keeper. This is the one face whose claim rides on an idiom rather than on the key's own word,
  and it is flagged here for the refuter rather than defended.

V3 `[plain]` "This town's watch is in someone's keeping besides its own, and nothing is said of it."
- *in someone's keeping besides its own* — the INSTITUTION move's "who holds an office", read
  off the captured institution; no name, no office noun from `OFFICE_NOUN_CANDIDATES`, no count.
- *nothing is said of it* — the covert qualifier, agentless.

V3 `[face]` "Unexposed, the watch this town keeps is bought."
- *unexposed* — the engine's own word for this field's discriminator against `revealed`.

V3 `[face]` "The watch here is bought, and the buying is nowhere public."
V3 `[face]` "What the town keeps for a watch is bought, and the buying goes unsaid."
- Both: the one claim, in two rhythms; `goes unsaid` and `nowhere public` are the covert
  qualifier in two vocabularies.

**REFUSALS — faces I could not make lawful, and why (a refusal is a result).**

1. **THE PROVENANCE FACE — REFUSED.** The natural clerk move for this fact is to name who holds
   the record ("the count is the watch's own, and the watch is bought"). The card is explicit:
   `source: (none) · standing SOURCE-UNRESOLVED` and `NO citation is licensed: a face naming a
   record holder here is refused by arm A13`. No face in this set names a holder, and none matches
   `moveGrammar.js`'s PROVENANCE detector; the provenance count for this pool is zero by refusal,
   not by omission. This is the whole face family the pool cannot have until SEAM car 5b resolves
   a holder for `compromised.covert`.

2. **THE KNOWLEDGE FACE — REFUSED.** "and the town does not know it" is the most natural rendering
   of `covert`, and it is a TOTALITY OVER PERSONS, which the card refuses in every column. Every
   face therefore states the covertness as a property of what is PUBLIC (nothing public attaches ·
   out of public view · nowhere public · undeclared · unexposed · goes unsaid) and never as a
   property of what anyone knows.

3. **THE THREAD'S OBVIOUS NOUNS — REFUSED.** The two nouns the spines hand forward are the
   `{defwork}` and the muster or its wages. Both are fields the attached spines TEST
   (`forces.walls.present`; `settlement.defenseProfile.economicGates.military`), so the card's
   `may NOT` bars them, and `WALLED-STRAINED`'s own second variant already bands the muster. The
   thread is therefore carried on **the town** in every one of the twelve faces (`the town`, `this
   town`, `{settlement}`), which is the one noun every attach spine names, and the watch is
   introduced as the sentence's object or subject immediately after it. Five faces open on an
   adverbial or participial phrase before naming the watch; of these **V2 face "Bought, and
   quietly, is the watch {settlement} keeps."** is the one whose opener could momentarily attach
   to the spine's `{defwork}` on a first reading, and it is named here for the refuter rather than
   quietly kept.

4. **THE COUNT, THE CAUSE AND THE BUYER'S NAME — REFUSED.** How many of the town's offices are
   bought, who bought them, when, and why are all barred (`may NOT: a count, a cause, a season, a
   future`; REFUSED COLUMNS: a named character and that character's fate). `a buyer` and
   `someone's` are indefinite and carry no name, no office and no number.

5. **A0b's TOKEN TEST — a finding the chair should see.** `claimTokensOf('compromised.covert')`
   returns `{covert, coverts}` only, so arm A0b passes on the three faces that carry `covert` or
   `covertly` and would report `under-claim` on the other nine unless the caller hands it a
   `vocabulary` entry for this field. Writing `covert` into all twelve faces would collapse the
   sibling distance the owner's four-faces rule exists to create, so the set is written for the
   voice and the vocabulary gap is reported instead. (`walkComposed` does not call A0b, so the
   taste harness will not show this; a later gate that does, will.)

6. **THE INSTITUTION'S NAME — a licensing hazard, not a refusal I could cure.**
   `SECURITY_INSTITUTION_RE` (`src/domain/corruption.js:630`) matches `watch|garrison|constab|
   guard|magistrate|court|barracks`, and `compromisedSecurityInstitutions` returns the matched
   institution's own recorded NAME. The card's bag offers no slot for that name, so every face in
   this pool must write the pool key's own word, `watch` — and on a town whose compromised body is
   the court or the barracks, all twelve faces name the wrong institution. The cure is a minted
   slot on this pool (the `{defwork}` precedent in this block's own SLOTS line: "fills with the
   settlement's own institution by its RECORDED name, never a baked or invented noun"), which is a
   chair act on the annex row and not a wording change. Recorded here because a wording set cannot
   fix it.

7. **THE `MOVE: INSTITUTION` TAG — seven of twelve classify, and the detector is why.**
   `CLAUSE_DETECTORS`'s INSTITUTION arm needs an institution noun followed within forty characters
   by a duty verb (`keeps|holds|takes|levies|collects|counts|registers|hears|issues|answers|sits|
   rules|decides`). "the watch is bought" carries none of them and falls to the PRESENT fallback.
   The seven that classify are V1 `[plain]` (`watch answers`), V1 face 3, all four of V2 (`watch
   {settlement} keeps`) and V3 face 1 (`watch this town keeps`); the other five read as PRESENT
   (measured by running the detector over the twelve, not asserted). Contorting the remaining five
   into a duty verb would have added a
   `whatItDoes` claim the card does not license (that the watch keeps its posts, walks its rounds,
   holds its wards), so the tag disagreement is reported instead of bought with an unlicensed
   fact. The detector has no arm for a captured institution; that is the finding.
