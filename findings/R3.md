## 2. THE ANSWERS

**Q1 Capture, hold, ransom.** There are TWO landed hold roads and one computed-only road, and they do not agree. (i) THE ROADS (`roads/state.js :: RansomRec`, `roadsKernel.js :: advanceRoads`) is the only one LIT and PERSISTED: a named NPC on a purposed mission (`PURPOSE_KINDS`) is captured by army (T1), siege (T2), embattled road (T3) or the host court (T4: `hostage · expelled · received`), held `termWeeks`, and ransomed over time through band-step legitimacy/prosperity writes; D-5 third-party ransom (`thirdPartyRansomEnabled`, dark) adds friend/ally/rival payers and the captive's trait refusal. (ii) WR-7b `foreignGuestHolds` holds ONLY an envoy interrupted on an errand by an army (`holdInterruptedEnvoy` is the one opener; the row demands `errandId`, `encounterId`, ≥1 `journeyLegs`); WR-7d's ransom over it is computed and returned, never persisted ("an OWNER GATE, NOT AN OVERSIGHT"), and `ransomChoices.js`'s `pay · refuse · abandon` has zero consumers. (iii) A caught SPY is not held at all (`custodyWritten: false`; three recorded blockers). So: who can be held today = a roads mission traveller (lit presets) and an intercepted envoy (dark); a spy no; a general no (the generals design proposes a roads mission of a new purpose `command` plus a new capture arm — inside the frozen roadsKernel). The hold persists (roads) `{ npcKey, homeId, captorId, threatClass, termWeeks, remainingWeeks, hostileAtCapture, willConvert, payerId? }`; ransom is answered by the HOME paying over the term (roads) — the `pay · refuse · abandon` choice and the `RANSOM_REGARD_BANDS` reason exist only as an unconsumed pure leaf. "A wanderer taken by bandits pays no ransom" rests on ODQ §125 item 5 alone ("NO ransom is issued (no settlement tie to ransom against)"); no captivity arm for the untied exists. A marked person CAN ride the roads road without a second hold IF its travel is a roads mission (a new additive purpose, e.g. `calling`), because `isOffStage`/`WHEREABOUTS_STATES` already remove a hostage from every participation read; a FREE champion with no home has no `homeId` and so no ransom payer — exactly §125.5's case, which needs the untied captivity arm (a time-bounded hold with no RansomRec). The party's would-be captor CANNOT ride any hold: the party has no roster identity and every hold keys a person id (`npcKey`/`npcId`); the party's "capture" is a hearing whose outcome returns from the table as a deed (brief §6), never a hold row.
