## 2. THE ANSWERS

**Q1 — Rites and culture today.** (CONFIRMED unless marked)
- **The one rite writer** is `traditions/relations.js :: applyImposition`, reached only through `advanceRelations`, whose only caller is `worldPulse/traditionsKernel.js` (PASS 1, per live settlement, after `advancePolitics`, before occurrences). Its trigger is `vassalOverlordOf(worldState, sid)` = the occupations ledger at rung `vassalized` (C2): a matured CONQUEST, one hop. The banner's "~0.3/yr" is right as a per-year draw (`createPRNG(\`${rngSeed}::tradition:impose:${sid}:${year}\`).chance(IMPOSE_CHANCE)` — keyed by YEAR, so every tick in a year re-reads the same draw), but it fires ONCE per overlord per vassalage (the `suppressedBy.overlordId` idempotency) — a single trade, not a flow. Restoration (`applyRestoration`) returns the suppressed rite when that overlord no longer holds the town vassalized. The imposed copy carries `adoptedFrom: overlordId`; `topTradition` does NOT exclude adopted rites, so a mid-lord whose top rite is an imposed copy would pass the crown's rite on one tier down a year or more later — tier-by-tier lag is ALREADY the shape, once the trigger reaches sworn links (PLAUSIBLE: needs the mid-lord's imposed copy to out-scale its own rites; `buildImposedCopy` caps scale at the local tier band).
- **Culture.** There are two cultures and neither is converted by any pulse writer.
