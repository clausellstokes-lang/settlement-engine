## A. THE CONSTITUTION — the laws that outrank everything

- **THE PROMISE.** Owner, 2026-07-20: *"a seed is a world, forever"* is permanent; auto-tuning against field data was rejected after deliberation (*"i agree with you then"*). **Amended by the owner 2026-08-05 — "a seed is a STARTING world, forever; a lived history, once lived, forever."** Every user-facing advance or reroll draws a fresh plausible outcome; the lived past never rewrites; internal byte-exact replay is kept only as a debugging door. Plain meaning: the town you generate from a seed is always born the same way, and once you have played a week of its history that week is permanent. `[END-STATE INTENT]` (source: memory `the-promise-ratified.md`)
- **Tuning is owner-signed, forever.** The field observatory may *watch* and *auto-draft* tuning releases; the owner signs every one; existing worlds pin their physics and only opt in to newer physics by consent. *"A SEED IS A WORLD, FOREVER — UNLESS YOU ASK IT TO GROW."* `[END-STATE INTENT]` (source: `the-promise-ratified.md`)
- **Product scope — four boundaries plus two.** (1) WORLD-ONLY: the product supplies the background world; what the party does with it is the DM's territory — no party renown, no quest hooks addressed to players. (2) SUB-CENTURY: campaigns rarely run past ~100 years, so multi-generation mechanics are low value. (3) SIMPLICITY OVER FIDELITY. (4) NEVER RESOLVE A NAMED CHARACTER'S FATE — world events act on aggregates and may *flag* a named person at risk as a hook; the DM resolves it. (5) The loaded-dice law: random draws are weighted by the situation and the weights are the receipt. (6) SETTING-AGNOSTIC MEDIEVAL — it must work in "a world made entirely of clouds", so no terrain-bound geophysics; the engine is cause-agnostic and consequence-focused. `[END-STATE INTENT]` (source: memory `product-scope-boundaries.md`)
- **The deity doctrine.** No premade gods ship; gods enter only through custom content. The engine is *total* over 0..MAX deities. Owner, 2026-08-06: faith *"is nothing more than an extension of culture and traditions… The users have to determine whether a divine act happens."* The engine simulates faith **sociologically, never theologically** — *"the engine simulates the congregation, never the miracle."* Amended 2026-09-12: **the followers act, the god does not** — a creed's people may be shown behaving in line with the record's axes and their standing in that town; nothing is ever predicated of the god itself. `[BUILT — both removal halves landed 2026-07-28]` (source: memory `deity-doctrine-no-premade-pool.md`)
- **The finite-semantics law.** Players have unlimited freedom; the world's memory of it is finite. Every effect that touches the engine is a typed record from a closed vocabulary with bounded magnitude. Free text has exactly three jobs: the DM's words on a receipt, fuel for flavor polish, and input the AI **buckets** into proposed typed records a human must confirm. **AI = bucketing clerk, never writer.** `[BUILT as law, enforced by source scans]` (source: memory `finite-semantics-law.md`)
- **Quality outranks time.** Owner, 2026-08-27: *"I want the absolute best product not compromises."* · *"I could've launched some version of all of this a year ago or a month ago. I will keep waiting on launch for the best product."* Operative rule: never present a cheaper option as the best available, and re-ask whether a self-imposed budget is load-bearing before letting it bound an ambition. `[END-STATE INTENT]` (source: memory `time-is-not-the-constraint-quality-is.md`)
- **Bold over safe.** Owner, re-ratified 2026-08-30: *"always choose to do the former, even if it introduces risk and then deal with the fallout… the absolute best product now before launch rather than try to solve it after we launch."* `[END-STATE INTENT]` (source: memory `owner-fix-philosophy.md`)
- **There is never any deferred work.** Owner, 2026-09-19: *"there should never be any deferred work. If anything comes up emergent to put it in the sequence."* Every emergent item gets, the same turn, one of three fates: a slot in the charter, an owner decision point with its moment named, or CLOSED with a reason. `[BUILT as process law]` (source: memory `owner-law-2026-09-19-there-is-never-any-deferred-work...`)
- **The presentation doctrine.** The dossier's reference half is a **tome / a set of scrolls** — rubrics, fore-edge tabs, marginalia, a colophon — never a dashboard; the header is deliberately striking (the door vs the book). The dossier is organized **by subject**, never by "the DM's question"; volume is handled by reference-book apparatus (ledes, an index of names, cross-references, typography), **never by progressive disclosure**. `[END-STATE INTENT]` (source: memory fold `archive-2026-09-20-index-fold-111.md`)

## C. THE DEPTH AMBITION — what is ultimately meant to be simulated

War is complete; this program brings the other six layers to war's grade — robust AND dramatic — after which foreign policy EMERGES and no seventh module is ever built.* `[END-STATE INTENT]` Its density contract requires every mechanism to carry a law, a force **and its counterforce**, a belief posture, receipts, pins, bands and couplings; a force without a counterforce is a ratchet, and *"ratchets are the estate's proven killer."*

## D. TUNING AS AN END-STATE ACTIVITY

- **What is tuned:** dials and load-bearing constants (corruption saturation curve, pressure weights, capacity thresholds, challenge rates), distributions, band thresholds, and pools. Tuning adjusts weights, **never fork keys or draw order** — that would be a golden-shift event. `[END-STATE INTENT]` (sources: `docs/SIMULATION_TO_10.md` P2.5; memory `product-scope-boundaries.md` loaded-dice law)
- **A band is a range on an observed metric, not a constant's value.** `docs/TUNING_BANDS.md`: *"The declared, RATIFIED target ranges the pre-launch soak proves the world against"* — all ratified by the owner; six families tabled. Governing rule: *"The tuning loop may propose a retune from a band divergence; it may never auto-apply one."* Changing a band needs explicit owner re-ratification plus a gate. `[BUILT]`
- **Governance.** `docs/SIGNED_CONSTANTS.md` is *"the tuning surface's walking sheet"* with three statuses — CHAIR-SIGNED, OWNER-SIGNED, UNMINTED-PENDING — and states that **the tuning signature itself is the owner's and is LAST**. `[BUILT as register]`
- **Golden shifts.** A "golden" is a stored sample output. The discipline is **no-golden-shift**: goldens stay byte-identical across a wave, and any legitimate change is recorded in a shift ledger for the owner's batched sign-off. Since **THE GENESIS FREEZE** (2026-09-16, cut on the owner's *"sign the golden, push them"*), a golden can only move through a door that demands a signed shift record and an `Owner-Signed:` trailer. `[BUILT]` (sources: `docs/GOLDEN_SHIFT_LEDGER_MAIN.md`; memory `state-2026-09-16-deploy-day-the-genesis-freeze-and-the-paused-runs.md`)
- **Soak runs.** Four profiles (`smoke` / `weekly` / `release` / `research`); only `release` can yield a product certificate, and *"A passing process is not automatically a certification."* The **diagnostic soak** is step 2 of the endgame, runs lit in a disposable tree, and is **findings-only**. Its stated purpose is *"the program's largest open risk: no layer has ever been lit alongside the others."* `[IN FLIGHT — two recorded RED runs on population bounds]` (sources: `docs/SOAK_PLAN_R2.md`; `docs/architected-volumes-pending-fold/DIAGNOSTIC_SOAK_DESIGN.md`)
- **The one known real defect tuning must cure:** the 300-year research soak found exponential population runaway (≈29 trillion by year 300, invisible at 30 years) while six settlements collapsed to a floor — *"the realm doesn't merely inflate — it bifurcates."* The cure is a banded carrying-capacity ceiling, soak-verified, not a hard clamp. Amended 2026-08-21: the tuning pass also gains a **map leg** (candidate dials judged on the maps their worlds produce). `[UNFIXED — the single most important tuning input]` (source: memory `population-runaway-300y.md`)
- **Target qualities:** plausibility inside ratified bands; variety without incoherence (loaded dice, stream independence); stability of same-seed output (goldens, byte-identity). **After launch, the Promise constrains tuning absolutely:** the observatory proposes, the owner signs, new worlds are born at the converged edge, existing worlds pin their physics, and a per-campaign opt-in is the only way a live world adopts newer physics. `[END-STATE INTENT]` (source: `the-promise-ratified.md`)

## THE AMBITION IN ONE PARAGRAPH

SettlementForge is meant to end up as **the world engine behind the table**: a game master types a few choices (or eventually describes a town in plain words), and in milliseconds gets a settlement that is not a roll on a table but an argument — an economy that implies its institutions, institutions that imply its factions, factions that imply its people and their secrets, with the reason for every fact stored underneath so "why is the guard corrupt?" always has a real answer. Then the DM marks it canon, connects it to neighbours, and lets weeks pass: news travels the roads and arrives late and garbled, courts act on what they *believe* rather than what is true, goods move on real routes through tolls and bandits, plague walks the trade roads, armies march and besiege and starve, peace is bought with duration-capped terms that strain until they fray, empires appear as patterns the DM gets to name, and every week's report reads like a chronicle with its causes attached. The DM stays sovereign throughout: they can edit any fact through one door where every value comes from a typed pool, queue changes as decrees the world applies in order, override any warning the engine offers, and never have the machine decide a named character's fate or a god's will. The whole thing must be trustworthy in a specific, unusual sense — the same seed always makes the same starting world, a lived history can never be rewritten, a new feature never disturbs an old save, tuning only ever changes with the owner's signature, and every sentence it prints can be traced to a fact it actually holds. And it is meant to be finished before it is launched: the owner has had a shippable version for a year and is deliberately waiting, because the product's whole moat is that it is orders of magnitude deeper than the alternatives *and* every fact in it agrees with every other.
