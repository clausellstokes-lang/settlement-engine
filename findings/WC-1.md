## 1. THESIS

WC compiles the owner's 2026-08-06 war auxiliary-contribution directive: a vassal/ally/occupied town may SEND troops and supplies to another party's army instead of (or at the cost of) fielding its own; realized contribution — never a declared label — sets a five-rung war stance that prices exhaustion, casus exposure and terms; armies become origin-tagged block rosters under a people ledger that conserves every person "census -> block -> composite army -> losses/returns/orphans -> census|defense|free-lance pool" (WC:197-202); the peace then pays out in homecomings, free companies, diaspora bonds and veterans.

- Call-ins (§1.9): *"Thornhold called, and Emberford came -- though the war was already lost by then"* (WC:2347-2349).
