**§4 item 7 answered:**
- *Can a deed's PARTS ride the arrival record and merge?* NOT AS-IS. `captureContent` copies only `what/whereId/scope/magnitude/partyIds/causeClass/deityName`; `normalizeEntry` drops any unnamed field. A deed needs (i) its own structured fields on the feed entry, named in `normalizeEntry` (the byte-neutral conditional-spread idiom, like `region`/`npcIds`), (ii) a `captureContent` arm copying them into `content`, and (iii) since `mergeArrival` supersedes WHOLE records, parts never merge field-by-field — the better telling wins entire. - *Can the per-hop roll carry a FIXED weathering order?* YES, WITHOUT A NEW DRAW: the landed completeness thresholds already ARE a fixed field-loss order. "Size drifts" is the existing 30% band drift. The sanctioner's-lands slower weathering = a reader-side threshold discount when the reading settlement is inside the sanction's reach (one bounded multiplier), not a different roll. CONFIRMED (substrate); the threshold values PLAUSIBLE.

CONFIRMED (failure mode, by T's execution); design PLAUSIBLE.

### Q4 — The regard idiom; the four facts
