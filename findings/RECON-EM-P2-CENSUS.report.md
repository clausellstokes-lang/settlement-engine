word.

Six of the 18 `mutates` rows are DRAWN, including **`corruptionPass` → `factions` and `npcs` (270/525

would flicker on every edit"*) is **confirmed and is the common case**, not a corner.

### C10 — drawn, on the record, and still not what a pin would hold

`generateEconomy`'s `economicState` is DRAWN 525/525 and lands at `record.economicState` by name and
value — but the perturbation reaches the settlement in only **332/525** rows, because
`economyReconcilePass` re-derives the key (`readsVersion` already records this two-production shape).
So "drawn + reaches the record" does not imply "pinning it changes anything", and a register with three
booleans cannot say so.

### C11 — two steps spend entropy on nothing, and §21 has no row for that

`isolationPass` (24/525) and `factionCorrelationPass` (1/525) draw, write no declared key, write no
UNDECLARED key (strict mode over all 25 such rows, above), and never move the settlement. Under EM-P0b
they would declare an EMPTY `choosers` list while still drawing — so the runner's "pin every chooser or
none" rule would be satisfied by pinning nothing, while the step keeps advancing a stream nobody reads.

### C12 — a mint census that cites `createPRNG(` call sites cannot see a stream re-minted from a copied seed

§21.1: *"Stream MINTS are not rows: they are the entropy census's (`createPRNG(`), cited, not
re-scanned."* There are **8 static `createPRNG(` hits in the 222-file closure**, and they do not describe
the runtime: per run there are **35 mints**, of which the seed `…::generatePower::power-structure` is

boundaries, position 0 each time".

   config corpus from the wizard's own option sets (EM-P3's home for them) rather than from the golden
   corpus, and re-run M3 over it. That is a cheap extension of `m3-drawn-or-pure.mjs`.

   sensitivity cannot separate "this field was chosen here" from "this field moved because a sibling
