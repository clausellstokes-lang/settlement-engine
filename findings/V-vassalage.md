## 6. Gaps — what a dominion design must newly build (facts, not proposals)

1. **No political unit above the settlement exists in any form.** No settlement record carries a state/realm/kingdom id (§1.15); `realmId` is the RNG seed; hegemony and the seat are derived per call with zero persisted state; ODQ §12 deleted `capital`. Every "kingdom" today is an emergent shape nobody reads as one.
2. **No transitive read.** Nothing walks overlord → overlord; there is no root/apex function, no depth, no cycle guard (§1.14). The only chain walk ever run was throwaway probe code (ODQ §934.87).
3. **No single-overlord invariant after genesis.** Subjugation, the DM link picker, and (by plane divergence) the sale can each produce a second overlord; the levy code already handles a shared vassal; the seat read hides multiplicity codepoint-first (§1.14). Patron/client multiplicity is born in ~10% of worlds (§1.14 table) and patrons are excluded from the seat by ruling (§735.2).
4. **No chain-aware duty or right.** Levy, wall relief, strategy recall, the trade override, tribute rules, must-go shipments, the cascade and the homecoming-coup check are all one hop: a vassal's vassal owes the apex nothing and is protected by it not at all (§1.11).
5. **Vassalage has three writers and no common record.** The `vassal` label (genesis, subjugation, vassalization, DM link), the occupations ledger (conquest; the sale), and subordinating treaty terms (peace). `foreignSeatOf` unifies only the READ; substrate-specific readers disagree about who is a vassal (§5.3).
6. **Hegemony does not see vassal labels or the ledger** — only treaties (§3 item 2's divergence) — so a genesis-born or war-matured vassalage never forms a sphere.
7. **No DM christening persists for any derived polity.** `canonLabelFor` has no caller (`RealmDashboard.jsx:420-423`: "when the persistence lane lands").
8. **No realm-borders lens.** Ruled (ODQ §934.87) but unbuilt; no cell polygons are captured (`sf-bridge.js:1291-1330`); `digest.territory` exists only after canonize; FMG makes zero states; `nativeStateBorders` is an inert key drawing 0 px.
9. **No civil-war mechanism.** Rebellion is vassal-vs-own-overlord only (`relationshipRulesCore.js:768-848`, success relabels to `rival`); co-vassals' hostility carries no overlord rule; there is no notion of "the same polity at war with itself" (no state, no flag, no kind; the only "civil war" token is a PDF comment).
10. **No apex title or polity name vocabulary.** Government labels are per settlement (`rulingPower.js:157-174`, e.g. 'Royal Authority' for a large noble seat); nothing labels a unit above one settlement.
11. **No apex succession.** Succession is titular and per settlement, and DARK (Register VII unsigned; `heirsEnabled` dark, `titularSuccession.js:59-67`); a dominion's apex passing (by coup, conquest, sale, death of a title) has no rule.
12. **Precedence between a settlement and its overlord exists only as:** the trade hard-override (`tradeWar.js:420-470`, war layer), the one-shot cascade at vassalization (`relationshipHierarchy.js`), and W-SEAT's weight/primacy (dark; the `authorityFor` primacy axis of W-SEAT §3 D2 is design). There is no general "the kingdom decides X for its members" rule for war, alliance or trade.
13. **Genesis knows no geography** (planned blind, `genesisDiplomacy.js:189-194`): a born chain need not be contiguous, and nothing ties a vassal edge to territory adjacency.
14. **The owner's word is homonym-loaded** (§5.2): `dominion` is a persisted roads purpose, `kingdom` routes the AI door to the whole campaign and meant "a settlement court" in the owner's earlier rulings, `realm`/`seat`/`sovereign`/`crown`/`capital`/`bloc`/`chain` are all landed with other meanings, and the vocabulary register (fold §5) has no row for any of them.
