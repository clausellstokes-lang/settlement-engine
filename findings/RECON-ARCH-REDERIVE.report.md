**The mechanism, in two parts:**
1. **Double enrichment (every failing row).** The record's `npcs` is the post-`mergeNPCLists`,
   post-dispersal, post-`buildPoliticalNarrative` roster. Pinning it makes `generatePopulation`
   emit it verbatim; `assembleSettlement` then runs the whole enrichment *again over its own
   output*. `mergeNPCLists` re-merges an already-merged roster (hence the ±entries and the

   `buildPoliticalNarrative` re-rolls `secret.what`/`secret.stakes`/`goal.short` from a different
   input even when the draw count is unchanged (`town|norse`: assembly draws identical, 5 secrets
   still differ).
2. **`factions[].members[]` follows `npcs` by construction** — `relinkFactionMembers` remaps
   members to the re-enriched objects by id, so every npc divergence is mirrored there. This is
   not a second bug; it is one.

`simulationTrace` diverges because `derivePopulation`'s two `recordTrace` calls report
`${npcs.length} npcs` and the direct/attraction/scatter linkage tally, and under a pin the roster
handed to the trace is the enriched one.

### 6.1 The three refusals, each traced to its invariant

1. **`assertPowerEconomyFreshness` (`power/economyReconciliation.js:353-368`)** fires at
   `assembleSettlement.js:204`, BEFORE any reconciliation can refresh it. The record's
   `powerStructure.economyInputFingerprint` is FNV-1a over
   `[version, tier, prosperity, safetyLabel, foodLabel]`. **Any edit that moves one of those three
   economic readings makes a HELD `powerStructure` stale by construction** — removing an
   institution and changing the terrain both do. There is no seam between the held value and the
   assertion.
2. **`assertStableGeneratedRoster` (`:191-200`)** fires inside `reconcilePowerStructure`.
   `factionIntentKey` (`:65-71`) returns `'governing-seat'` for the governing faction and
   `category::name` otherwise, so **moving the seat changes the key multiset and the estate
   refuses it**.
3. **The power-share edit is discarded, not refused**, because `reconcilePowerStructure` REPLAYS
   power generation from `ctx.powerIntent` and then assigns
   `merged = { ...current, ...projection }` plus `powerStructure.{governingName, government,
   publicLegitimacy, factionRelationships, criminalCaptureState}` — every one of them from the
   replay. ⛔ **And `powerIntent` is ABSENT from the record** (the census's C4), so the replay's
   authority can never be reconstructed from a saved settlement. **The power card is not editable
   through a held `powerStructure` at all**: the assembly overwrites the held value from a replay
   whose seed the record does not carry.

## 8. What I would tell an architect that the brief did not ask

1. ⭐ **THE PIN SEAM EXISTS IN ONE STEP OF TWENTY-TWO.** `git grep -n '__pins\|chooseOrPin' -- src`
   returns `pipeline.js:59` (the reserved key) and six lines of `generatePopulation.js`. Every
   other step ignores `options.pins` completely — the runner merges pins into the initial ctx and
   the producing step simply overwrites them. So today `rederive(record, …)` can hold exactly four
   keys, and every experiment above that holds `institutions`, `powerStructure`, `history` or
   `stress` does it with an `onStep` restore that does not exist in the product. **Nine of the ten
   fields EM-A1 declares as editable roots live behind keys that have no pin consult at all.**
2. ⭐ **THE RECORD'S HELD VALUES CORRESPOND TO A POINT INSIDE `assembleSettlement`, NOT TO A STEP
   BOUNDARY** — which is why every step-boundary seam scores 6/63 and the sub-step seam scores
   63/63 on the rosters (X3). Whatever the chair designs, the roster pin has to be consulted
   *inside* the enrichment (at `enrichNpcCoherence`), or the enrichment has to move out of
   assembly and into the population step, or the record has to start carrying the pre-enrichment
   roster as well as the enriched one. Those are the only three shapes the measurement leaves open.
   ⭐ **And the first one is safe for a reason the tree already engineered.** `enrichNpcCoherence`
   runs inside `inAssemblySubstream(rng, 'npc-enrichment', …)` — its own child stream. Skipping it
   costs 40–56 % of `assembleSettlement`'s draws and the record still reproduces, because its
   SIBLING substreams (`historical-character`, `prominent-relationship`, `coherence-notes`,
   `siege-capability`) are forked independently and do not move:


   I predicted the opposite (that S8 would hold the draw budget stable) and the measurement
   refuted me: S8 spends roughly HALF the assembly draws. The comment at
   `assembleSettlement.js:53-68` — *"A named child stream makes that dependency impossible"* —
   is what makes the halving harmless, and it is the strongest existing evidence that a pin
   consult can be placed there without a corpus-wide golden shift. **It must still be measured
   against the golden corpus before it is believed**, because `disperseNamedRoster` inside that
   substream also reads `getActiveRng()`.
3. ⛔ **THE POWER CARD CANNOT BE EDITED AT ALL THROUGH A HELD `powerStructure`, and it is not a
   pin-placement problem.** §21.5.3 amended §14 so that a computed key is pinnable — but
   `assembleSettlement` REPLAYS power generation from `ctx.powerIntent`, asserts the held roster's
   identity against the replay, and then OVERWRITES `governingName`, `government`,
   `publicLegitimacy`, `factionRelationships`, `criminalCaptureState` and every generated faction's
   projected fields from it. `powerIntent` is ABSENT from the record. Measured: the share edit
   moves 0 record keys; the seat edit throws. EM-A1's power card is dead on arrival until either
   `powerIntent` is persisted (a schema change) or the replay learns to consult a pin.
4. ⛔ **THE FRESHNESS INVARIANT IS THE HARD BLOCKER FOR WORLD-FACT EDITS.** §14's flagship example
   is the teleported port — terrain changed, held facts kept. Executed, terrain→desert THROWS on
   both a town and a city, at `assembleSettlement.js:204`, because the held power structure's
   economy fingerprint no longer matches the re-derived economy. Every world-fact edit that moves
   prosperity, the safety label or the food label hits this. This is the single measurement I
   would put in front of the owner, because it is the design's own headline example failing.
5. **The rename cascade and the re-derivation are two different repairs and neither subsumes the
   other.** 52 of 56 exact joins are by display name (X5). A re-derivation with `relationships`
   held leaves 4–5 dangling name joins after one NPC rename (X6); a re-derivation with
   `relationships` free re-rolls the entire settlement (X3 S10: 0/63). So a rename needs the
   cascade *and* the re-derivation, in that order — and the institution family has no cascade at
   all, while 30 of the 56 join paths point at an institution by name.
6. **`simulationTrace` is the universal residual and needs a ruling, not a fix.** It is the ONLY
   key that diverges under the best seam (49/63), it is written through by 12 different steps
   under the runner's `_LEDGER_KEYS` exemption, and `factionRename.js` already rules it
   "the GENERATION RECEIPT … rewriting it would falsify the trace". A re-derivation IS a new
   generation of the derived layer, so its trace arguably SHOULD move. I measured it; I am not
   ruling it.
7. **`mutates` is not a reliable isolation signal.** 6 of 18 declared rows never write through
   anything (they replace), and 3 undeclared write-throughs exist that are not the trace ledger,
   two of them on `provides` keys (`economyReconcilePass|economicState`,
   `coherenceRepairPass|generationRepairs`). A "deep-clone the keys a step mutates" rule built on
   the declaration would be both too wide and too narrow. The frozen-clone probe in
   `x4-mutators.mjs` is cheap (63 rows in one pass) and could become the ratchet that keeps the
   declaration honest.
8. **Cloning is free and should not be argued about.** 0.5–1.2 % of a re-derivation, 0/63
   caller-side mutations. `structuredClone` beats the JSON round trip at every tier.
9. **A "no dangling references" gate must be a DELTA.** A freshly generated record already carries
   14 (town) to 30 (city) dangling name joins, by design in three separate families (X5).
10. **The `factions[].members[]` mirror will read as flicker to any naive gate.** Editing one NPC

    same npc object in both places. That is the cascade working, not a flicker — but a
    "no unedited held fact moved" assertion will convict it unless the mirror is declared.
