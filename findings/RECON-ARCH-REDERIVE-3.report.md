## 6. Q5 — internal consistency of a merged record

**The instrument first.** `invariants.mjs`: 25 EXACT checks (arithmetic, count, prose-count,
referential), 5 BAND pairs judged by a sandwich test against 63 observations, and a LEARNED
flag-vector check. **Two candidate checks were REJECTED by their own control** and may not convict:
`criticalIssueCount = issues.length` (false on 6/63 plain records — it counts only the critical
ones) and `foodRatio = production/need` (false on plain thorps and hamlets — the ratio counts
imports and magic). The surviving instrument is clean on **62 of 63** plain records; the one
residual is a **pre-existing generator inconsistency**, reported here because it is real:

```
town|germanic|mountain|mountain_pass|…  V-SUMMARY-DEPS: summary says 6 operational dependencies
                                        but economicViability.dependencies.length=5
```

All convictions below are scored as a DELTA against the record's own violations, and split into
**the MERGE's** (introduced by mixing `R1` into the record) and **the EDIT's own** (already true of

## 8. Where §22.1 is WRONG or incomplete — smallest measured contradiction first

1. **The merge is not the identity with no edit as written.** "Compared leaf by leaf, keyed
   collections by their keys" reproduces the record in **9 of 63 rows** (§3). Two named causes: a
   kept collection is re-ordered into `R1`'s order (2,221 leaf paths over 20 rows in
   `availableServices` alone), and a collection `R0` and `R1` agree on but longer than the record's
   is PADDED with empty objects (`causes.length×338`). §22.1 needs the sentence it does not have:
   **compare and short-circuit at every NODE, and "the record's own value" includes ABSENT.** With
   it, 63/63 — so the fix is a clause, not a redesign.

2. **"Order taken from `R1`" is a reading that moves with no edit touching it, and it is undefined
   for a third of the cases.** Eight of 32 trials re-order a collection whose entries the merge kept
   from the record (`history.legacyAnnotations` on a town after removing one house;
   `availableServices.legal` on a city after adding one). And `R1` has no opinion about the order of
   entries `R1` does not carry (the settling-removal class), so the merge must invent one. **Either
   order is part of the reading (then it takes `R1`'s order only when the collection differs, which
   is what the short-circuit gives) or it is the record's (then say so).**

3. ⛔ **THE CENTRAL ONE: a merged reading can hold a label beside a derived sibling that contradicts
   it, and it does so on §14's flagship edit at the two largest tiers.** `city` and `metropolis`,
   terrain → desert: `foodSecurity.label="Import-Dependent"` with `isSecure=true` — a pair no
   generated record carries. Mechanism, exactly: the LABEL differs between `R0` and `R1` so it takes
   `R1`'s; the FLAG is identical in `R0` and `R1` so it keeps the record's. **§22.1's unit of merge
   is the LEAF; the generator's unit of consistency is the OBJECT.** Same mechanism, second
   instance: `economicViability.summary` says 3 plot hooks beside a list of 2 (village, terrain →
   desert). The measured risk surface is small and nameable — nine object paths outside the trace
   and the receipt (§6's MIXED census) — but §22.1 has no rule for them.

4. **The merge is not order-independent, and §22.1's own cache claim makes that a live question.**
   `merge∘merge ≠ merge(both)` in 34 leaf paths on the one interacting pair tried (remove an
   institution, then terrain → desert). Two DMs making the same two edits get different towns
   depending on whether they saved in between — and the one who saved in between gets the *more*
   coherent town (`isSecure=false`, consistent; the batch path is the one Finding 2 convicts).
   §22.1 says "a session pays one re-derivation per edit", which presumes the chain; it does not say
   the chain and the batch disagree.

5. **"Held facts are taken from the edited record untouched" re-breaks the member mirror that
   §22.1's own correction 2 exists to fix.** Measured on 4/4 tiers: `role="Harbourmaster"`,
   `mirror=["Priest"|"Guild Master"|"Elder"]`. Correction 2 fixes the SEAM; the merge then discards
   `R1`'s `factions` wholesale. **A mirror is a derived field inside a held key, and §22.1's
   held/reading split does not reach inside a key.** Taking `factions` from `R1` cures it (measured:
   `mirror=["Harbourmaster"]` 4/4) — and then ruling 9's "no unedited held fact moves" convicts that
   same cure (`factions:1` on 4/4), so the gate needs the mirror named as an exemption.

6. **Ruling 4's fingerprint refresh is still visible as a moved held fact, and the receipt recompute
   is the thing that makes it harmless.** `powerStructure:1` appears as an unedited held key moving
   in **5 of 32 trials** in the merge (and as seam flicker inside `R1` in **13 of 32**), and it is
   `economyInputFingerprint` every time (verified path-by-path).
   Recomputing it LAST over the merged record repairs it even in the no-edit case (arm C: moved
   9/63, merge still equal to the record 63/63). §22.1 correction 3 should say the recompute is what
   discharges it, not only that it is excluded from the gate.

7. **"Receipts (the power fingerprint) are recomputed over the merged record last" is a list of
   one, and the obvious second member cannot join it.** `generationCoherenceReceipt` is a digest of
   the record — but two of its three inputs (`ctx.generationRepairs`, `ctx.generationContext`) never
   reach the record, and under a held roster the repair passes find nothing to repair, so a
   recompute would empty a `repairs[]` the record carries. It is merged as a reading here, and it is
   in the MIXED census on 9 of 32 trials. §22.1 owes it a class.

8. **The power card's total is left broken by the edit and the merge cannot mend it.** Shares sum
   to 89 / 92 / 94 / 94 after a share edit, on 4/4 tiers, in the merged record, in `R1` and in the
   edited record alike. It is the price of ruling 5 (the replay does not run, which is why the share
   sticks), and it is an EDITOR decision — renormalise, or state that shares are independent
   weights — that §22.1 does not take.

9. **Ruling 3's newcomer is missing sixteen fields, not just relationships.** §22 and §22.1 discuss
   only the newcomer's ties. Measured: `title`, `category`, `personality`, `physical`, `goal`,
   `secret`, `plotHooks`, `influence`, `power`, `presentation`, `corrupt`, `factionAffiliation`,
   `structuralPosition`, `activeConstraint`, `settlementCondition`, `structuralRank`. A packet that

10. **Ruling 8's partition is not optional under the merge — it is what makes draw-SKIP legal.**

## 9. What I could not measure, and what would settle it

   tiers, reporting the leaf-path disagreement between chain and batch. About twenty minutes of
   wall clock on the scripts as they stand (`q6-sequences.mjs` takes a trial table).

   63-row ones. **Settles it:** `q3-merge-edits.mjs` over `sample63()` — it is I/O-free and would

   `generationRepairs` out of the `R1` run and recompute is a design question with a persisted-shape
   flavour I am not ruling. **Settles it:** call `buildGenerationCoherenceReceipt(merged, {seed,
   generationContext: R1ctx.generationContext, generationRepairs: R1ctx.generationRepairs})` — the
   ctx is already reachable through `seam3.mjs#rederiveFull` — and compare against the merged
