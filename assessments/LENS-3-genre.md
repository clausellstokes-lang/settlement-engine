# LENS 3 — THE GENRE: the FP simulation as designed, judged against the great world simulations

Assessor: Lens 3 (Opus 5.5), 2026-10-18. Read: the BOARD's OWNER lines; spine §1; fold §0, §2, §3.4, §6; CHAIR-NOTES; for PL · MN · RN · CH · PT · PS · ST · PR · BD the laws, answer paragraph and WHAT A DM READS; the headers of HB, EP, CW, IN, ES, WC, CN; generals §0–§1; the r3 counts. "I judge" marks my judgment; cited claims are the design's own.

## Verdict

As designed, FP is a real and novel member of the genre: a referee's simulation, whose user is the DM, whose output is setting and consequence, and which the law forbids a story of its own (spine req. 1, 2026-10-18 bracket; BOARD 01:31, 01:33). Its strongest ideas — the hearing week, belief-driven planning for every actor, one truth per quantity with bounded leans — have no close precedent among the greats, and its anti-storyteller doctrine is the most rigorous I have seen. But the corpus guards hardest against runaways, noise and the storyteller stealing the table, and weakly against the opposite failure: a world so damped by counterforces, caps, decay and DM-reserved verdicts that little happens unless the DM pushes. The one measurement that bears on it points the wrong way: armies marched twice in 180 realm-years (generals §1). Legibility on scrutiny, the owner's test, rests on an unbuilt cause walk and on `causedBy` adoption at zero volumes (fold §2, CW row). Proportion leans heavily to security and military logistics; persons and generations are thin. Excellent in its laws, unproven in its dynamics: the soak, not another review round, decides whether this is Dwarf Fortress or a well-documented still life.

## Emergence

The design's engine of emergence is sound in principle. Many actors make bounded, seeded choices over situation-scored sets (BD §4; CH4). They read belief rather than truth (spine req. 1; BD12; PR7; MN7). They learn from one another: patrols predict a body's habits, and the body learns the patrol's cadence (L-BD items 20–21 and 39; BD §5). Their effects compose into a few shared quantities rather than parallel laws (BD5; ST5; PR5; MN5's one presence term). That is closer to Dwarf Fortress's many agents over shared state than to Crusader Kings' scripted events, and better disciplined than either. BD7, "quiet is not safe", is the corpus's best single piece of sim design: presence (the embattled level) is split from habitat (what breeds bodies), so killing buys a season of quiet and only fixing the habitat buys safety — a two-timescale truth most sims flatten into one number.

I judge the risk is attenuation. Every force has its counterforce (req. 2); every effect is one capped lean (CH5, ST5, RN12); regard decays to neutral (RN6); the engine never sacks or razes (MN8, BD15), never kills a named person (req. 4, CH3), never spawns a menace, paragon or site (MN12, CH1, ST3). Each rule is right alone; stacked, they move irreversibility out of the engine into the DM's verdicts. It survives at the institutional grain — the patron seat (BD17), "the crown passes to Brenmoor" (PL §13), a gang's hamlet (BOARD 18:47), vassalage — but legends need such events at some rate, and the only rate measured is the war layer's near-silence: courts war-ready on about 2% of ticks, legitimacy zero in every measured court (generals §1). The push budget is a ceiling; requirement 16 is a per-mechanism sign; nothing sets a world-level floor.

## Legibility

The surface prose is the strongest legibility layer I have seen specified for any sim. Every program has a deeds-only sentence table under the Evidence Law (PL §13, MN §13, BD §18, PS §17, PR §18, ST §14, CH §24, PT §19, RN §17). Each line names who, which power and what effect. No line gives a numeral, a forecast or a mechanism name. "The hill roads are quiet; the quarter still breeds" (BD §18) is better than anything in RimWorld's log. Two choices are subtle and correct: "what is not seen did not happen to the holder" (PS8), and the nameless threshold, below which a gang makes no line (BOARD 18:58).

The scrutiny layer is another matter. It depends on CW-2x and the Why panel (unbuilt, re-sequenced to C0.5, fold §6) and on every child receipt populating `causedBy` (req. 20) — adoption at zero volumes, two engine writers in the tree (fold §2, CW row); the pacing governor has no registration surface for Herald news kinds (SP §2 R4). The legibility pin ("no orphan line") is the right gate, but today it is a promise across 28 volumes and, by my count of fold §3.4, about 78 hearing kinds. DF and CK earned legibility by building the log and tooltip first and the systems into them; FP specified the systems first. C0.5 is the correct correction; I would hold every later chain to it without exception.

## Proportion

The surfaces honour macro over micro (BOARD 06:32); the newest volumes' mechanism grain does not always: road halves, cadences by address and week, per-hop roll multipliers (PR4, PR6); a tolerance width spent by weeks × size, nine answers, five roads (PS5–PS7); hidden ways, supply, cohesion, ransom clocks, sponsorship deliveries, spillover (BD §3–§10; L-BD 34b, 46–48). Each reduces admirably to leans on landed quantities, but each volume runs 200–260 KB, and together they make security and war far deeper than persons, culture or generations. I judge a ten-year advance will expose a wargame under a fantasy skin: brigands, keeps and patrols churning richly while rulers neither age nor marry. The owner's reasons are sound (lifespan is the DM's, BOARD 05:57; no named fate), and the age receipt says it plainly (fold §6 C8); the cost should still be weighed knowingly.

## Agency as DM tooling

FP's most original move: the genre's agency is "I act in the world"; FP's is "I rule on the world" — leave, tilt or decree at each climax, standing engagements every week, defaults on longer advances (spine req. 24). The hand is total (RN11, PS16, PR17) and deterministic, and undo re-draws only the un-lived future (EP, "living futures, immutable pasts"). No great sim offers a referee this; CK's event choices are nearest, but there the chooser has a stake. The marking burden is handled with craft: zero-click marks, non-blocking batched suggestions, and a world coherent with no marks at all (PT2). One recorded limit: the "different winter" lives only within a session — the undo ring dies on reload (fold §2, EP row).

## What is novel

In my judgment, five things are new to the genre: (1) the hearing week, every climax adjudicable (spine req. 24); (2) belief-never-truth as every actor's planning substrate, patrols predicting and bodies evading on believed coverage (BD12, PR7, PR9) — DF's rumours and CK's secrets are local by comparison; (3) the quiet/safe split of disorder into presence and habitat (BD6, BD7); (4) a world deterministic, undoable and indifferent to the table (EP; PT1); (5) a DM-named outside force with no type and a name never parsed (MN1, MN2).

## Uncertainty

I judged the newer volumes from laws and sentences, the older from headers and the status of record. The attenuation claim is PLAUSIBLE reasoning; the generals measurement concerns the landed war layer, not the new designs. The kind count is my tally of fold §3.4 (BD's rows may not all be there yet). The r3 verifiers show textual convergence (0 new BLOCKING per volume, 1–4 new MAJOR each); convergence of text is not convergence of dynamics, which only a lit soak can show.
