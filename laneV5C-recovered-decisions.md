Every nontrivial call in v5-spec-draft.md, with the options weighed, the pick, and what
a veto costs. Rows marked ⚠ graze an owner-confirmed surface and are flagged in the
spec's open questions.

## Deferred, recorded (not bugs to re-find)
- THE 640 BREAKPOINT RECEIPT IS OWED AT BUILD (critic F9): the 640 composition —
  which objects survive, whether the burned shelf collapses into a menu — is a
  design decision this pass does not own, so no 640 geometry is claimed and no
  fake 640 render is produced. The breakpoint law binds the builder to shoot the
  real 640 before landing (640 clears the headless 500px floor).
- The nock build-and-show (ShaftNock) stays as-is pending the owner's glance; V5
  neither needs nor forbids it.
- The large-register torn-gold-leaf cartouche and its optional companion seal are
  specified as a register, not drawn in this pass's mockups (hero/About/PDF surfaces
  own them).
- Hover/press micro-motion for the vanes (a 1–2px settle) is deliberately out of
  scope: the two-state transition is the only motion V5 ships.
- The `d:path()` transition needs an engine-support check at build time; the
  cross-fade arm is the specified fallback either way.
