## §14 · HAZARDS FOR THE NEXT LANE

- ⛔⛔ **A CONTAINMENT REPAIR THAT MAKES AN EARLIER EPOCH DEPEND ON A LATER ONE REDS §240.4, AND IT
  SHOULD.** The inertia seam is not only about rng streams — a geometric dependency is re-derivation
  too. **A ring may depend on the epochs BEFORE it and never on the epochs after it.**
- ⛔⛔ **"A ⊂ B" IS NOT ONE PREDICATE.** A nesting repair that only moves vertices standing on the
  wrong side halts at the case where both boundaries are fine and they cross BETWEEN their vertices.
  Both arms or the census does not reach zero.
- ⛔⛔ **ABUTMENT IS NOT OVERLAP, AND TWO PREDICATES IN THIS TREE DISAGREE ABOUT IT.**
  `groundLaw.overlapping` shrinks both bodies first; `circuitDemotion`'s private `polysOverlap` did
  not. A body clipped exactly to a reserved kerb reads as standing on it under the second.
- ⛔⛔ **TWO EXPORTS NAMED `clipHalfPlane` KEEP OPPOSITE HALF-PLANES** (§9 RAISED-7). Check which one
  you imported before trusting a clip.
- ⚠⚠ **AN INDEX PAIRING BETWEEN TWO OFFSET POLYGONS IS A CLAIM ABOUT THEIR LENGTHS.**
  `offsetPolygonOutward` clamps its mitre and can emit a different vertex count; `Math.min(...)` over
  the lengths bounds the LOOP and asserts the correspondence. One bow-tie garden quad, caught by the
  ALL-RING census in the same session that created it.
- ⚠⚠ **A "BOTH SIDES OCCUR" ARM OVER A FAMILY THAT VARIES ONLY THE SEED IS A CLAIM ABOUT A
  THRESHOLD'S POSITION.** It survives exactly as long as the law's real input stays constant.
- ⚠⚠ **A NON-VACUITY COUNTER THAT WALKS ONE DRAWN FAMILY DIES WHEN THE CHANGE MOVES TO ANOTHER.**
  The inertia pin's `moved > 0` was passing on a single back-house while the real change was seven
  faubourg buildings.
- ⚠⚠ **A DEFAULT ON A FIELD NOBODY PUBLISHES IS NOT A FALLBACK, IT IS THE VALUE.** Two of them in
  one module pair, and one had a comment eleven lines away diagnosing the other.
- ⚠⚠ **SNAP-ROUNDING IS NOT MONOTONE IN ITS QUANTUM.** Coarsening merges nodes and a merge can
  create a crossing. Never pick one quantum and call it safer.
- ⚠ **A FILLED-DITCH GARDEN IS OPEN GROUND AND OUTSIDE `drawnBodies` BY DESIGN**, so §17.4 can never
  convict it. Anything that reserves ground for one must claim-test it explicitly.
- ⚠ **`reserveFossils` AND `siteFossils` BOTH THROW IF THEIR INJECTED PREDICATES ARE MISSING.** That
  is deliberate: an unexercised default reads as a working path.
- ⚠ **THE `MINT` RATCHET NOW READS 30 ACROSS 14 MODULES.** A new hand-minted fork key reds it, and
  curing any existing one is a same-seed shift.
- ⚠ **THE ARMS ARE BUILT BY `laneMFW3F-arms.py` AND EVERY PATCH IS ASSERTED.** If a switch moves, the
  build reds rather than producing an arm that withdrew nothing.
- ⛔⛔ **AND THE HAZARD I QUOTED AND THEN COMMITTED: A DIAGNOSTIC TREE'S ISOLATION CLAIM DECAYS EVERY
  TIME THE TIP MOVES.** `laneMFW3F-diagIR` drifted five files behind the tip and produced a stage
