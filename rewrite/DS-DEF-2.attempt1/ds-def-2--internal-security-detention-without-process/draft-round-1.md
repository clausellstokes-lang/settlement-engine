## THE ROWS — paste under the pool's heading, replacing rows 1 to 3

1. `[ledger]` `[plain]` The power to hold stands at {settlement} and the power to try does not, so enforcement here follows the person who applies the power.
   - `[face]` Detention at {settlement} runs with no process behind it. Whether a person stays held is answered by whoever is doing the holding, and by no other authority in the town.
   - `[face]` People are kept at {settlement} without being tried, and a keeping runs as long as one person's judgment runs.
   - `[face]` A person can be taken and held at {settlement} with no charge laid: what follows is the affair of whoever does the taking.

2. `[visitor]` `[plain]` A stranger at {settlement} goes with a care that a town with courts would not require, and cannot name the thing that is missing.
   - `[face]` Strangers go carefully at {settlement} and could not say why, in a town that keeps no court.
   - `[face]` The visitor at {settlement} is careful without cause given, and could not point to the court the town does not keep.
   - `[face]` A visitor at {settlement} is careful and is not told why. The telling would want a court, and the town keeps none.

3. `[street]` `[plain]` The town can put a person away at {settlement} and cannot say on what grounds. On whose, it does not ask.
   - `[face]` People go into confinement at {settlement} on the word of whoever confines them, and the town asks neither the ground nor the name.
   - `[face]` The holding at {settlement} stands on its own. On whose authority it stands, and on what grounds, is a question the town leaves alone.
   - `[face]` A held person at {settlement} stays held on no stated ground until somebody decides otherwise, and who that somebody is the town does not ask.

### 2. Refusals, removals and open rows (a refusal is a result)

**R1 — OPEN CARD ROW, not a refusal, but the chair's to rule.** The card's `may claim` line licenses only *that `court` holds*. The pool key is `detention without process`, whose branch is `prison` held and `court` absent, and variants 1 and 3 both claim the DETENTION. **R2 — A CLAIM REMOVED from variant 3, deliberately.** The shipped variant reads "and **has learned** not to ask on whose". That is a HISTORICAL clause on a standing configuration field, which R-DST-B (A6) refuses and which no event-provenance field in this pool's reads can license. All four wordings state the same fact as STANDING ("does not ask", "asks neither", "leaves alone"). Ruling 5 / R-DA-15 requires the removal of an unlicensed claim, and §22 permits shortening inside the band; the removal is recorded here because it is a claim change, not an edit.
