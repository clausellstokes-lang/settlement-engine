### Per face — what changed, and the law or target behind it

**Variant 1 `[counterforce]`** — claim set {C1a, C1b, C2, C2n}, the shipped variant's own four.

- **plain** — "against it all the same" became "the wall that faces it is a heavy one", and "whatever the wall answers to" became "whatever the wall is for"; "settled" entered. Two targets: R-DA-11 (a wall that *answers to* something is inanimate intent; a wall that *is for* something is the shipped line's own present-tense purpose refusal), and §21.1's sharper licensed fact — `settled` is the state key's own band word and the pool never once spent it, exactly as the landed sibling pools spend `plagued`. The weight is now stated, not implied, so C2 rides in the plain line rather than only in the faces.
- **face 2** — "a thin country" moved out of the first clause into its own predicate and "the works are there for" became "Nothing in it accounts for the works". Target: the pool's worst tic. The draft carried the same mold (*the creatures are not what X is for*) in three of four faces of this variant; this face now denies by refusing an account instead of restating a purpose, and the second sentence lands on the civic noun rather than on a pronoun (R-DA-04, pronoun closers held at zero).
- **face 3** — the draft's shortest counterforce face carried C1a, C1b and C2 but **not C2n**, so it was not claim-equal with its three siblings (arm A6; MOVE-GRAMMAR §3.4 arm C). "and the creatures least of all" restores the denial in five words rather than a fourth full clause, which is the density law's own trade (§21.4: compression that rewards the reader is the ceiling). "Nothing the country holds" became "Whatever the country holds is no match", to cut one of the draft's nine `nothing` faces.
- **face 4** — "the creatures in that country are not the reason for the wall" became "no beast in that country is why they stand", and the fabric noun moved from `wall` to `works`. Three targets: the fourth distinct mold for C2n (concessive · account · ranking · cause-refusal, one each); A11, because the draft's face 4 and the ledger's face 3 both opened "The wall"; and R-DA-10's noun dispersion, which now runs wall · works · perimeter · works across this variant.

**Variant 2 `[ledger]`** — claim set {C1a, C1b, C2}. The drafter's flagged claim-set refusal on this variant is INHERITED UNCHANGED and is still the chair's to veto (the shipped second sentence asserted `scores.internal` and a spending fact this pool reads nothing for).

- **plain** — the draft's two sentences became one. Targets: the colon-plus-doubled-clause was the draft's densest single face and its only three-part shape; the split gives the pool a fourteen-word two-sentence face, which is where the within-pool sd wants a member (R-DA-05), and "quiet" is a licensed rendering of `settled` that the pool had not yet spent.

"Asks" attributes intent to a country (R-DA-11); "needs" is the key's own measure. The repetition of the fabric noun inside eleven words is the compression, not an accident: it is the line that lands when read aloud, and it is the pool's short line under R-DA-05.

The draft's shortest face was already at the ceiling in shape, so the only refinement available was the sharper licensed fact, not a plainer line (§21.4's regression clause). Its close is the pool's one absence close (R-DA-04's varied kind).
