   - `[face]` Troubles here are answered with nothing, for want of anything to answer them with. Each one costs the next less to come about.

3. `[street]` `[plain]` A bad month is past what {settlement} could pay for, and the people who would be owed know where the money stands.
   - `[face]` Pay would stop at {settlement} inside a bad month, and the people it would stop for have the measure of the town's means.

   - `[face]` The money at {settlement} would not last a bad month, and the people waiting on that money know what is not there.
