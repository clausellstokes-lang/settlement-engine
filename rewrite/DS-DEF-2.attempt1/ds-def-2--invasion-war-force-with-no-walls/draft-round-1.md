- **c3 — no siege can be held here.** Variant 1's *"cannot hold a siege, because there is nothing here to hold"*. **DECLARED, not refused**: it is c2 restated in its own consequence (a siege is held at a work; the town holds no work), and the block's own PROVENANCE paragraph names capability clauses as this block's licensed causal kind and historical ones as its refusal. - **c4 — the ground of a fight is not the town's to fix.** Variant 2's *"people can be gone around"* and variant 3's *"decides where any fight would happen"*. **DECLARED, not refused**, on the same ground as c3: with no line there is no fixed front, so the fact is c2 in its consequence, subjunctive throughout (*would*, *can be*) under A2.

### REFUSALS AND CURES (a refusal is a result; the set is banked with its faces, never trimmed)

- **R-1 · variant 1 · claim c5 (the raid is answered WELL) — REFUSED, all four wordings.** No clause of the card licenses a grading. **C-reads** is the invasion situation alone; **C-pred** returns a situation, not a rating; **C-bag** shows `{band: RESERVED}`, so the block's own badge is not writable at this call site; **C-maynot** bars a second fact. MOVE-GRAMMAR §1.3 lists VERDICT/RATING among the moves that do not exist, and CL-7's rule is that a rating word is licensed only where a typed rating field holds it. The rewrite cannot cure it: dropping *well* would spend a claim the BEFORE carried (arm C-pair; §22 never-trim). All four wordings are written keeping c5 and the set is banked for the chair — either `defenseProfile.scores.military`'s band is added to this block's bag at its call sites (a wiring car, not a text act), or ruling 5's removal is authorised on the record. **The same refusal is visible on every sibling cell of this row** (*credible*, *real deterrence*, *counts for something*, *cannot be resisted*), so this is a pool-level finding with a row-level cause, and the chair may wish to rule it once for the row rather than six times.
- **R-2 · variant 3 · claim c6 (a stranger sees it) — CURED, not carried.** *A stranger sees soldiers at {settlement} … and can see how that decides* is a per-entry FRAME / VANTAGE, which MOVE-GRAMMAR §1.3 lists among the moves that do not exist anywhere in the estate (the office's origin is declared once on the front matter, never per entry — R-DA-01, fault 9 at document scale), and **C-maynot** bars a standpoint outright. It is not a typed claim about the world, so removing it spends nothing under arm C-pair: every typed claim the BEFORE carried (c1, c2, c4) is kept in all four wordings, and the `[visitor]` tag stands untouched, licensed by **C-angle** as the framing a face is set down in and not as an assertion that anybody is looking. The stricter reading available to a refuter is that the visibility of the absence was itself the BEFORE's content; on that reading the cure spends a claim and the wordings should be reverted to a stranger. The reading taken here is the sibling lane's (DS-DEF-1's packet refuses the observer on the same ground) and is flagged for the sitting.
- **R-3 · the pool as a whole · `may NOT: a second fact` — DECLARED, not refused.** Read literally, **C-maynot** convicts all twelve wordings and all three BEFOREs, because this pool's predicate is itself a two-field composite (`walls` × `garrison`) and the block's PROVENANCE paragraph directs it to carry a capability clause. The reading taken here is that the card's *second fact* bar is the modifier card's clause reprinted on a spine card, and that c3 and c4 are the predicate's negative half restated in its consequence rather than a second fact. If the chair reads it strictly, the pool is unsatisfiable as authored and takes a refusal row for all three variants; the wordings above stand either way, since they add nothing the BEFOREs did not hold.

### Laws checked, with what was found

- **Walls.** No em dash · no exclamation · no question · no digit and no numeral · no `which` in any form · no second person · no future indicative (`will` at zero; the edge is subjunctive throughout — *would fall*, *would happen*, *would meet*, *would be laid*) · no expletive opener (`There is` / `It is` at zero, and the absolute *there being* was written out of the plain to keep the existential arm clean) · no figure, no sense verb on an abstraction, no inanimate intent (*a siege is laid*, *an attack would fall where it was aimed* and *nothing here holds a siege* are passive or stative military measurement, never an abstraction with a want) · no named character and no fate · no theological claim.

MOVE-GRAMMAR §4.4.3 adds that a citation on a fact whose holder is the office itself is a finding, and §24 records the exemplar registers citing at zero per 786 sentences. Zero is therefore the lawful reading, not an omission.
- **Slots (C-bag).** The slot set is identical across each variant's four wordings, as ARCH §2.5's face rule requires: variant 1 `{settlement}` ×4, variant 2 the EMPTY set ×4 (the BEFORE names no slot), variant 3 `{settlement}` ×4.
