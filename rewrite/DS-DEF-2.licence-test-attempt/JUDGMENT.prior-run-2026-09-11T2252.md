## 1. The rules applied, and the chair's construction of them

The four dispositions are the brief's: (1) a FAIL that names a law and quotes the face REVERTS to its draft face where that draft face is CLEAN; (2) a WITHHELD is KEPT and listed; (3) a FAIL naming no law is treated as a WITHHELD — **no such verdict arose: every one of the 129 FAILs names at least one law**; (4) a FAIL whose draft face shares the fault is a CURE TARGET, never a pool refusal; (5) never trim — no face and no variant is removed, and none was.

Three constructions were needed to apply rule 1 consistently across 26 pools. They are recorded here so the chair can veto any of them.

**J-1 — a co-referring common noun satisfies R-i.** Two refuters WITHHELD a second sentence whose only link back was a definite common noun co-referring with the slot named in the first sentence, and each put the question to the chair (`plagued, perimeter AND organized force` v1 f0; `plagued, no perimeter and no force` v1 f1). The block's own refuters have already answered it in practice: three faces PASS on exactly that link and say so in terms ("the second sentence carries 'town' forward as 'that town'"; "the demonstrative has a real antecedent, unlike the sibling pool's failed 'That muster'"). I rule that a definite or demonstrative common noun co-referring with a noun or slot standing in the face's own first sentence CARRIES THE NOUN FORWARD for R-i; a fresh referent with no antecedent (the failed "That muster") does not. Both WITHHELDs therefore stand as KEPT, not as cure targets.

**J-2 — "CLEAN" is read against every ground the verdict's law line names, and against the pool as this judgment leaves it.** A revert stands only where the draft face (a) is not byte-identical to the failed face, (b) does not carry the quoted breaching clause or the same breaching construction in substance, and (c) is not itself reached by any ground named in that verdict's `law` line — including where the ground is only reached once the revert is in place (a draft face that becomes a sibling paraphrase of a face this judgment KEEPS is not clean of an A5 ground). Grounds raised only as secondary observations inside a finding — a page-level echo no wording can cure, a read-aloud note — do not defeat a revert; they are recorded in §4 for the re-refutation.

**J-3 — a draft face carrying a breach the same refuter FAILED elsewhere in the same pool is not clean.** Reverting must not install a wording the pool's own verdicts convict. This decided four faces: `no reserves, no medical provision` v1 f3, v2 f0 and v2 f3 (the draft faces carry "Nowhere at {settlement} takes the sick in" and "the community", failed at v2 f3 and v2 f1), and `granary, NO medical provision` v3 f2 (the draft carries the possessive-on-the-stock shape failed at v2 f3). The memory of this programme states the reason in one line: a revert to the draft cures nothing when the draft shares the fault.

**J-4 — one WITHHELD is converted by a chair's block ruling (rule 4, last clause).** `frontier, credible deterrence` v1 f1 ("an active frontier") was WITHHELD because the marker had left it as an OPEN ROW and "a refuter cannot settle a chair's open row."
