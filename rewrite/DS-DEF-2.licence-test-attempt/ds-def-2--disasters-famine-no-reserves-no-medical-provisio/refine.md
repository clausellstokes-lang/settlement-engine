## 2. THE STANDING CHECKS, RE-RUN OVER THE TWELVE REFINED FACES

- **Claims.** C1 ∧ C2 in every face; C3 in every face of v1 and v3 and in none of v2. Nothing added:
  no count, no cause, no season, no future, no standpoint, no second fact, no other civic object of
  the class `store`, no totality over persons, no exemption, no named person, no deity.
- **The stock trap.** No face uses a `store`-class word (*stores · reserve · reserves · stock · larder
  · harvest*) and no face asserts an emptiness. Every absence is of the BUILDING or of the care house;
  `grain` appears only as what an absent granary would be for (D-12's ENTAILS), never as a quantity.
  The engine's own 1.5–2.0 months of storage on a granary-less roster (`foodGenerator.js:161-165`;
  `foodStockpile.js:190-193`) is not contradicted anywhere.
- **The parish fence (skeleton §0.4).** The four draft clauses that swept past the care class
  (*nothing standing takes the sick in* · *No house stands at {settlement} to treat the sick* ·
  *Nowhere at {settlement} takes the sick in* · *no house takes the sick in*) are gone. Every care

  sick*, *anywhere for the sick* — so no face says, denies or implies anything about a church, a
  parish, a shrine or anyone tending the sick outside a hospital-class row. **This is the round's
  largest correction and it is a licence finding, not a taste one.**
