## 0. THE ONE FAULT THAT DROVE THIS ROUND

The draft is lawful. Its single failing against the CEILING (§21.1, §21.3) is a POOL-WIDE TIC:
nine of its twelve wordings hang the prison read on the same periphrasis — "the one a charge
names" / "the one it names" / "the one complained of" / "the one charged" (0.75 of the pool).
That is the shape a refuter names as a tic (R-DA-05's spread, R-DA-10's rationed-word ceiling

W22's load-bearing referent, the one law breach in the set. This round cuts the periphrasis to
three of twelve (0.25), each spelled differently, each in OBJECT position, none at a close;
spends the freed room on the prison read's three ratified spellings (a cell · a gaol · a place
of confinement, all three shipped on this tab's sibling pools, so rule 3's same-word-same-
referent holds across the tab); widens the frame nouns (a wrong · a charge · an offence · a
quarrel · a complaint) so the five matter-closes carry five different nouns; and re-cuts every
face's construction so the four faces of a variant differ in the SUBJECT, the ORDER of the two
reads and the LANDING NOUN, not in vocabulary alone (ADDENDUM 7 rule 4; W4 is untouched — no
variant is moved onto a sibling variant's construction; the [ledger] enters, the [street]
