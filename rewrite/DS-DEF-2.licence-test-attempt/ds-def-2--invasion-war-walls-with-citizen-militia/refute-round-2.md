## 2. FINDING A, stated once with its evidence — THE CO-EXTENSIVE `the townspeople` IS A TOTALITY OVER PERSONS

**The law.** The licence card's last line refuses, always: *a totality over persons* and *an exemption from a duty (whoIsExempt is null everywhere)*. ADDENDUM 13 A item 1 binds a recorded noun to what the word MEANS. W2 bars a totality over the town. The skeleton's §8 carries the same wall in its own words ("no totality over persons or over the town's means or safety").

**The record.** The `Citizen militia` row's recorded meaning at the tiers where this key actually fires is a RESTRICTED subset of the town's people, never the whole of them:

- hamlet, the catalogue row: `Able-bodied residents drill and muster against local threats. Part-time service.` (`src/data/institutionalCatalog.js:338`)
- hamlet, the seeded descs: `Fit residents train and turn out when trouble comes near.` · `The able-bodied drill together and assemble against local danger.` (`src/data/institutionDescVariants.js:442-443`)
- village: `Organised community defense.` · `A community drawn up for its own defence.` · `Organised local defence, mustered against raiders and monsters alike.` (`institutionalCatalog.js:870`; `institutionDescVariants.js:1158`)
- the ONE row that does carry a totality — `All able-bodied citizens obligated to defend town` (`institutionalCatalog.js:1344`) — is the TOWN row, and the town row **never generates natively**: the required `Town watch` shares `civilianDefense` and splices it (R-A; skeleton §0.2-b; entailment A-4). So on every native rendering of this key the totality is false of the record as well as refused by the card.

**What D-8 licenses, and what it does not.** D-8's ENTAILS reads `townspeople, part-time (HOLDS at hamlet/village)` — the table's gloss of "fit residents" / "the able-bodied" / "a community". It licenses `townspeople` as WHAT THE FORCE IS MADE OF. It does not license a claim that the force and the town's people are the same set. ADDENDUM 13 A.5's always-safe class words for the paid military are `the muster` and `the town's force`, below town `the community` — a definite `the townspeople` standing in an identification with the force is on none of those lists.

**The line this refutation draws, and why it is drawable.** A DEFINITE plural `the townspeople` in a CO-EXTENSIVE relation with the force — an equative (`the force is the townspeople`, `the townspeople are the town's force`, `what turns out is the townspeople`), or the class as the AGENT of the force's own act (`The townspeople turn out`) — asserts set identity over a bounded, specific set, and with `whoIsExempt` null the record may not say that nobody stands outside it. A BARE plural (`with townspeople behind them`), an indefinite-headed partitive (`a part-time muster of the townspeople`, `a force of the townspeople`, `a militia of the townspeople`), an explicit partitive (`drawn from the townspeople`) and a restricted plural (`the townspeople in it`) assert composition only and are clean. The corroboration is the corpus's own: the SHIPPED clause, which the owner's exemplar rule keeps as this variant's density floor, uses the bare plural.

**Reach.** Six faces trip it (1·3, 2·0, 2·1, 2·2, 3·0, 3·2); six do not (1·0, 1·2, 2·3, 3·1, 3·3). Nothing else in the pool is touched: no claim is added or dropped by any of the six cures, which are one-word or one-phrase changes.

**A note for the CURER.** The six cures must not all take the same frame, or the cure installs one formula where the fault was one construction (ADDENDUM 7 rule 4; W4). Three lawful frames already stand in the pool and should be spread across the six: the shipped BARE plural (`is townspeople, part-time`), the indefinite-headed PARTITIVE (`a muster / a force / a militia of the townspeople`), and the explicit partitive `drawn from the townspeople`. Face 3·2's cure should move the townspeople out of the subject so it does not read as face 3·0 reversed.
