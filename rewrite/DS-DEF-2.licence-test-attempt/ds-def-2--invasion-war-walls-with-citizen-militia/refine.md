### The round's one governing finding, stated before the per-face rows

The draft is lawful on every face and in band on every measure; the refiner's target is therefore §21.1's ceiling, not the law. Reading the twelve faces as a pool rather than one at a time, **one device carried every single line**: the backing idiom (`behind them` · `behind the walls` · `behind the town's walls`) appeared in **all three numbered lines and all nine faces — 12 of 12**. That is the pool tic the refinement round names by its own words ("zero tics — no fronted-place, inversion or ledger-closer habit repeated across the pool"), and it is a measurable spread fault under R-DA-05 and A11: a reader who meets two faces of this pool meets one construction twice. It is cured here to **7 of 12**, and the seven that keep it keep it because the phrase is load-bearing there: it is the shipped clause held verbatim (vid 1), the street's own way of naming the pair, the fronted locative the draft declared, and the one thing that disambiguates a free relative. The five faces that lose it take the card's other licensed conjunctions in its place — the corpus's own `with them` (the sibling annex rows carry `the town's muster stands with the works`), the plain `and` of the keyed condition, and the office's elliptical `and so are`. **No claim moves in any of the five**: the conjunction is the key itself (`walls === true` AND `militia === true`), which "and" states exactly, so what is dropped is a device and never a read. A second, smaller sweep cures the pool's two remaining repeats: the doubled inversion (`behind them is …` on two faces of vid 3, now one) and the one shared face frame (`The walls …` opening a face of vid 2 and a face of vid 3, now one).

Two nouns the card licenses and the draft never spent are put to work: **`the militia`** (the skeleton licenses it outright — the read IS `forces.militia.present`, true on every rendering of this key) now carries two faces, and D-6's **`the walls the town has built`** carries one. Neither is a new claim; both widen the pool's lexicon at the grain the entailment table already ratified (I6's TTR direction, R-DA-10).

**What did NOT move, deliberately.** Variant 2's numbered line, its short line and its two-sentence face, variant 1's numbered line, and variant 3's inverted face are kept **byte-identical**. Each was tested against three or more rewrites this round and every one was longer, collided with a sibling, or made a lawful compression plainer — which §21.4 names the regression. The density law cuts both ways: where a face already sits at the ceiling, changing it to show effort is the fault, not the cure.
