### 0. THE THREE TARGETS THIS ROUND WAS SPENT ON (named before the per-face lines)

**(a) THE ALIAS BAR, W15 / ALIAS TABLE A-15 — the round's one LAW cure.** The draft spelled the
absent `care`-class row *an infirmary* and *a sick-house* in eleven of its twelve faces. A-15's
always-safe column for `hasHospital` reads **"a house that takes in the sick" / "those tending the
sick"; never "the hospital" from the boolean** — the flag spans a hospital, a monastery that *may*
operate one, and one first-level divine healer who is a PERSON. *Infirmary* is a generic English word
naming a row (`care` = hospital · infirmary · healer · medical · physician · ward), and on this branch
**no care row resolves at all**, so it is exactly the case ADDENDUM 13 part A item 6 tells a refuter to
fail: *an ALIAS word used where the row it names does not resolve.* Every face now carries the
always-safe spelling **a house for the sick** — which is also the corpus's own word, shipped in the
sibling `NO reserves, NO medical provision` row. *A parish* is kept: it is A-14's own always-safe
spelling for the `hasChurch` class. The person-form ("those tending the sick") is deliberately NOT used
for an ABSENCE, because "nobody tending the sick" would be a **totality over persons**, a REFUSED
COLUMN on every card.

**(b) THE SEMICOLON, cured to zero.** The draft carried a semicolon on 1d and 3a. The marker convicted
the shipped v1's semicolon by name (claim 1.7): amendment S2 lets a clause ride on a sentence **only**
where it is a CONSEQUENCE the engine computed of that sentence's own fact, and nothing computes the
care LACK from the granary. Both joints are now the comma-and-connective form R-DA-02 licenses for a
LACK completing a PRESENT, or a two-sentence face (1b). The pool now carries **zero** semicolons.

**(c) THE CEILING, not the floor (§21.1).** Doubled verbs removed (`stands … stands` in the old 1a);
the landing vocabulary widened — *the town* landed five of twelve faces in the draft and lands **two**
now, the rest landing on the parish, the house for the sick, the granary's ground, the place, or the
flat absence; one two-sentence face restores part of the sentence-count spread the draft flattened
(the shipped v2 was two sentences; A11 / arm E: where variants differed in count they keep differing);
and no two of the twelve share their first two words, with the `{settlement}` token opening none of
them (order constraint 10; T-F8).

**What was NOT done, and why.** No claim was added or dropped: the granary's entailed purpose
("against hunger", "it buffers a harvest") is a MAY under W9 and adding it would ADD a claim the draft
did not make and break arm A6's claim-equality with the other faces. `{settlement}` was not added to
variant 2 (the shipped row carries no slot; the marker tags the naming as claim 1.1). No face names a
tier, a season, a count, a holder or a record word, and nothing of the shipped sentences' sufficiency
claim comes back.
