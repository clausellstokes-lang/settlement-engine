## 3. THE WORDING I RECOMMEND FOR FLOORS 1 AND 2

The adversaries improved on both. These are the chair's to sign.

### 3.1 FLOOR 1

> **FLOOR 1 — NO SELF-CONTRADICTION ON THE DOSSIER.**
>
> The unit is the **DOSSIER**, not the tab: the PDF prints the tabs as one document, and "the whole town's record" is the honest reading. A face may say anything the record does not deny; it may not state what the record denies.
>
> **A finding names three things: the face, the surface that denies it, and WHICH OF THE TWO IS THE RECORD.** The record is a FIELD, a FLAG, a BAND, a LABEL or a ROSTER ROW. Where the denying surface is engine PROSE or a projection reading a generation-time snapshot, **the face stands** and the collision is a WIRING ROW, recorded and routed to the desk's car. A first-survey band and a live band are two measurements, not a contradiction.
>
> **Five rosters are CLOSED WORLDS**, and a body, building, record-keeper, force or faith-house they do not carry may not be asserted: the institution roster (`priorityHelpers.js:45-77`, over the live roster), the force buckets (`defenseInstitutionBuckets.js`), the faction list, the faith entries, and the NPC office roster. **Every other absence is silence, and silence is permission.**
>
> A face is lawful iff it contradicts no state in **the pool key's preimage** — not merely no field of the one town on the skeleton.

The last sentence is the one that matters and the one that costs: it is the quantifier the programme has been running without, and it is what §2.3's seven artifacts exist to make answerable.

### 3.2 FLOOR 2

> **FLOOR 2 — THE MAGNITUDE AND TENSE FLOOR (THE PROMISE, constitutional).**
>
> **2a. THE BAND RULE.** A face may speak a magnitude — a count, a share, a size, a distance, a duration, a frequency, a proportion, an age — **only in the band word the read itself hands it.** The engine's band vocabularies are closed (`shareBandLabel`, `scoreBand`, `AGE_BANDS`, `treatyAgeBandWord`, `bandedStock`, `QUANTITY_BANDS`, `INSTITUTION_FOUNDING_KINDS`). **A magnitude the read does not carry is not spoken at all — not as a digit, and not as a word.** "A handful", "most nights", "a dozen", "for years" are magnitudes; refusing digits while licensing these refuses nothing. *The record's silence about a magnitude is not permission to supply one; it is the absence of the instrument that would make one sayable.*
>
> **2b. THE TENSE RULE.** The dossier describes a **starting world**. A face may not assert an ELAPSED COURSE: no past tense, no perfect or durative (*has stood, still, no longer, as ever, since, to this day*), no comparative against an earlier state (*thinner than it was*), no ordinal over events (*again, the second time, not for the first time*), no first or last occurrence, and **no prediction the pulse will adjudicate** (*will hold, would not last a winter*). The engine's own pin is the authority: `ageBands.js` sets `HISTORICIZE_BAND = 'years-past'` and refuses historicizing language below it, and a birth-time STATE carries no elapsed stamp at all, so it can bear no temporal register whatever. Where the record DOES hand a dated or elapsed reading (`inst.foundedAt`, a cause's origin tick, a treaty's age), the face speaks it in that reading's own kind or band word (`PRE_SEED`, `FOUNDED_UNDATED`, `years-past`) and never as a year or a count of seasons.
>
> **The SIMPLE HABITUAL PRESENT survives** — *the gate stands open; the collectors travel in pairs; the market sits on the seventh* — because it states a standing condition, which is what the engine holds. **What is refused is the RATE welded to it**: *more often than not, most nights, seldom, three times a week.* ⛔ *The chair's ADDENDUM 14 example is withdrawn in its rate form and kept in its plain one.*
>
> **2c. THE MUTABILITY RULE.** A face may not depend on a field the composed-prose layer is not wired to observe. `settlement.history` is replaced wholesale by a user reroll (`historyPreservation.js:1-30`; `operationRegistry.js:311`) and **no state-prose pool key reads a history field** — so a historical allusion desyncs on a button press even when it was true of the record it was written against. Atmosphere is free **in the present tense, over fields the key reads.**

**Why this is drawable where "no invented history, no invented numbers" is not.** 2a and 2b are decidable **from the face's grammar alone** — a refuter needs no world model to see a count word, a perfect tense or a frequency adverb — and they are anchored to named engine instruments rather than to taste. 2c is decidable from the pool key's own read list, which the census already prints. None of the three requires the refuter to hold the whole record, which is the capability §2.3 shows the programme does not have.

**⚠ THE JUDGMENT INSIDE 2b, stated so it can be vetoed.** The adversary's strict draft banned the habitual outright — *"inhabitation IS habituality; 'the gate stands open' is vivid precisely because it is iterative."* **I did not take the strict cut.** Habituality is where most of the exemplar pack's recovered ground lives, and the owner's complaint was flavourlessness. The class that actually collides with the pulse is the **rate** and the **elapsed course**, not the plain habitual: *"the gate stands open"* survives every tick and every reroll; *"the gate stands open more often than not"* is the same image with a frequency welded to it, and the frequency is the part the engine will contradict. The chair should sign or reverse this explicitly — it is the single wording decision with the largest effect on how the prose reads.

## 4. THE RESIDUAL RISK, AS SHORT AS I CAN HONESTLY PUT IT

**The owner is buying vivid prose with three things, and only the third is cheap to reduce.**

**One — a wrong hook cannot be edited out.** The re-cut's own warrant is *"plot hooks for the author to be immersed, and to change as they see fit."* Measured: the DM-editable prose register is a frozen closed list reaching **eight blocks in the whole estate** (`dmFieldProjection.js:85-94`), the editor for even those rides a panel its own header calls *(R-2, flag-off)*, and composed state-prose faces are **not persisted on the settlement at all** (`stateProseKernel.js:11-12`). Of the eleven defense blocks, **nine produce sentences the GM has no affordance anywhere in the product to edit, replace or suppress** — on a surface §885.3 rules PAID. The premise that makes a wrong hook harmless is not true in this build.

**Two — floor 1 is a property of the page, and the refuter reads one pool.** Thirteen of fourteen measured page-level collisions are invisible to the instrument as it stands, and more than half of the contradicting text on a defense tab is not corpus at all. Some will ship. The seven artifacts of §2.3 would cut this hard and none of them needs a new engine capability — but until they exist, "no self-contradiction on the dossier" is a floor nobody can stand on.

**Three — a face is written once, at tick 0, against one town, and renders over every town the key matches, at every tick, forever.** Several keys read fields the simulation never rewrites. A face that is true at birth can be flatly false at tick N, and nothing in the programme is looking.

**None of the three is an argument for the licence test.** The licence test bought immunity from all three — by making every face a restatement of its own read, which cannot collide with a badge, a sibling, a culture panel or the future — and it bought it at a price the owner has rightly refused to keep paying. These are the price of the re-cut, they are payable, and two of the three are cheap to reduce. They should be paid knowingly.
