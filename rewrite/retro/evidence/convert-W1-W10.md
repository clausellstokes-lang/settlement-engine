## NEW FINDINGS — real contradictions this slice's research shows, that no bar ever stated

1. **A face-initial possessive or pronoun binds to ANOTHER DESK'S subject at composition.** W1 charged this
   inside one face, where the record makes it harmless. The live case is the composed passage: the composer
   joins a spine and up to `k` added sentences from other pools (`composeStateProse.js:679`, the fact budget
   `k <= 3 - |spine.reads|`), so a face that opens "Its muster …" can land after a granary or market sentence
   and assert a possession the record gives to no such body. This is a COMPOSITION defect, not a writer's
   fault, and its cure is mechanical: refuse the SHAPE (as `passageShapes.js` already refuses shape 2 without
   a carried noun), not the sentence. Recorded for the register car.
2. **The town's AGE is printed, so history is now a live contradiction source rather than only a silence.**
   The identity strip prints `{hist.age} years old` and a `historicalCharacter` line
   (`OverviewTab.jsx:251`, `:259`). DS-DEF-11's card recorded "when the wall was raised has NO backing fact
   and is deliberately absent" — a licence argument that is void under ADDENDUM 14. What replaces it is
   sharper: age-flavour is now free ("older than the road", "raised by someone's grandfather") EXCEPT where it
   contradicts the printed age or the printed historical character. A wall "raised in living memory" on a
   four-hundred-year-old town is a floor-1 breach against a number on the same page.
3. **There are FOUR upkeep gates on the defense page, not two, and they are monotone in ONE input — so a face
   may never split their DIRECTION.** Military 0.6 (`defenseGenerator.js:189`), monster 0.7 (`:223`),
   internal/order 0.65 (`:251`), disaster 0.55 (`:614`) — every one of them `min(1, floor + econOutput/50 ×
   (1-floor))`. ADDENDUM 12's amendment stated the one-purse pattern WITHIN a class and named two purses;
   the cross-gate rule follows and was never written: at birth a face may not say one funded arm is flush
   while another starves (the watch paid and the muster not, patrols provisioned and the gaol unfunded).
   They differ in degree only, never in direction.
4. **The shortfall is BOUNDED, so a face may not write a total collapse of pay.** Every gate has a floor and
   the unpaid community baseline is exempt entirely (`defenseGenerator.js:186-192`). "Nothing has been paid
   in a year", "the wages stopped", "there is no watch left to pay" contradict the model even on the poorest
   town. The licensed extreme is "short", "late", "thin" — never "none".
5. **The engine's own contradiction module treats tension as a FEATURE, which is evidence for the re-cut and a
   bar on flattening.** `src/domain/contradictions.js:136-153` detects a cathedral in a hamlet and classifies
   it `interesting_tension`, emitting consequences and hooks rather than a defect. A face that explains a
   tension away ("nothing here is out of place") can therefore contradict a surface the same dossier prints.
6. **The same page calls the defwork a PALISADE regardless of what the wall row records.**
   `threatAssessment.js:59`, `:66`, `:80` hardcode "Palisade and citizen militia" and "The palisade creates a
   chokepoint" on branches that fire for any `institutions.walls` row, including `Town walls`, `citadel` and
   `massive walls` (`defenseInstitutionBuckets.js:84-86`). This is a product-code defect (OW-20's class:
   engine strings outside the REWRITE's corpus), but it is a floor-1 self-contradiction on the rendered page
   and it directly undercuts the MATERIAL bar's re-cut in the sibling slice (W11): the rewrite can be made
   correct about the wall's material while the row beside it says "palisade". Recorded for the owner via the
   register car.
