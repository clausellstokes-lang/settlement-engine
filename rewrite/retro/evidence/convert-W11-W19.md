# THE RE-CUT OF W11–W19 (ADDENDUM 13 part A, section B) UNDER ADDENDUM 14

**The test as re-cut:** a face is LAWFUL unless it CONTRADICTS the record. "The card does not license it" is no longer a finding. Silence is permission. Four floors survive: (1) no self-contradiction against the WHOLE town's record, (2) no invented history/date/number (THE PROMISE), (3) product scope + deity doctrine, (4) the engine's own positive model.

**Verdicts.** CONTRADICTION = a breach states what a named field, row or printed surface DENIES (survives as a refuter's finding, teeth intact) · MODEL = floor 4 · SCOPE = floor 3 (and, where noted, floor 2 THE PROMISE, which the verdict schema has no letter of its own for) · STRUCK = it policed permission, tone or construction and is now licensed flavour · SPLIT = keptAs survives, struckPart does not.

Every file:line below was read in the dock `laneRW-DEF2` on 2026-09-12. Nothing was written, run or built.

**Headline:** of 61 items, **9 CONTRADICTION · 2 MODEL · 1 SCOPE · 28 SPLIT · 21 STRUCK**. Counting a SPLIT's surviving half as a finding, 40 of 61 items keep some teeth and 21 die outright. The bars that survive are the ones that name a roster row, a printed panel sentence or a band the page prints beside the prose. The bars that die are the vocabulary lists — the always-safe word tables of W15 and W16 were a house style, not a truth test, and they are what made the corpus read at 168 distinct words against the shipped 519.
