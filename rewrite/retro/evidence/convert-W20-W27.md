# THE RE-CUT OF THE REFERENT BARS — W20 … W27 of ADDENDUM 13 PART B

**Slice:** the eight referent bars (layer · baked-power · person · fused-agent · record-word · external-body · visibility · angle-tag), decomposed into 20 items where a bar carried more than one rule.
**Test applied:** ADDENDUM 14. A face is LAWFUL unless it CONTRADICTS the record. "The card does not license it" is no longer a finding. Silence is permission. Four floors survive: (1) no self-contradiction against the WHOLE town's record or another surface on the same page, (2) no invented history/date/number, (3) product scope + deity doctrine, (4) the engine's own positive model.
**Sources read:** brief ADDENDA 12, 13A, 13B, 14 (whole); `rewrite/entailment/REFERENT-TABLE.draft.md` §§2.1, 4.1, 4.2, 5.1, 5.2; the engine read-only in the dock `laneRW-DEF2` (no git write, no test, no build).

**The shape of the result.** Of twenty items: 2 CONTRADICTION, 1 MODEL, 1 SCOPE, 9 STRUCK, 7 SPLIT. Almost every bar of this slice turns out to be a thin contradiction core wrapped in a thick licence rule. The core is always the SAME core — **the label trap**: a face names a body, a record, a hall or a force that the town's roster does not hold, and the safety panel on the same page prints the engine's own fallback sentence beside it. That is a defect. Everything else in W20–W27 policed permission, layer hygiene and stance, and it is struck.

**The one field that carries most of the teeth.** `getInstitutionNames` (`src/generators/priorityHelpers.js:45-52`) computes `hasGarrison · hasMilitia · hasWatch · hasMercenary · hasWalls · hasCharterHall · hasCourtSystem · hasMarket · hasGranary …` from the live roster names, and `src/generators/safetyProfile.js:281-300` prints the enforcement noun FROM THOSE FLAGS on the same page as the dossier prose — falling to `'Local enforcement'` (`:281`), `'Locals watch over'` (`:289`) and, at the bottom, the flat denial **"There is no meaningful guard presence."** (`:300`). A dossier face that seats a watch, a garrison or a militia the flags deny is contradicted verbatim beside itself.

## NEW FINDINGS — real contradictions the bars never stated

**NF-1 · A RENDERED SLOT'S OWN WORDS BIND THE CLAUSE AROUND IT.** A `proper` slot may render a STANDING-LADEN label: `{seat}` can be 'Corrupt Council', 'Corrupt City Council', 'Shadow Senate' (`src/generators/power/rulingStructure.js:245-256`, `:264-283`); `{counterpart}` can be 'Military/Guard', 'War Council', 'Conversion Enforcement Office' (`stressFactions.js:105-107`). So a clause that NEGATES or argues against what the fill's own words assert contradicts itself at render time and on every town that fills it that way — "Nothing criminal has reached the Corrupt Council of X." This generalises D-F27 from one pool to every slot-bearing face on every desk, and it is a refuter's finding with a field.

**NF-2 · A FACE THAT DENIES A CORRUPTION THE PAGE PRINTS.** The bar (W22d) policed the inference; the contradiction runs the other way and was never written down. Under `occupied` / `insurgency` the NPC roster MANDATES a `'Corrupt Official'` (`src/generators/npcGenerator.js:1511-1532`), and a REVEALED corruption impairment is a public scandal (`src/domain/corruption.js:654-658`, `:679`). A face asserting clean hands, an unbought office or an unreachable seat on such a town is denied by the roster beside it.

**NF-3 · THE SAFETY PANEL DENIES THE FORCE'S STATE, NOT ONLY ITS EXISTENCE.** W20 only ever policed the body NOUN. `safetyProfile.js:296-300` prints the force's CONDITION in the engine's own words — "The garrison is overwhelmed or corrupt." · "The watch is stretched far beyond its capacity." · "The militia cannot maintain consistent coverage." · "There is no meaningful guard presence." — and `:281-289` the opposite at the safe bands. A dossier face that has the watch comfortably covering the lanes beside `:297`, or a slack watch beside `:281`, is a same-page contradiction with a quotable sentence. This is the single richest untapped finding class in the slice, and it cuts BOTH ways, so it also tells a writer exactly which mood is licensed on which band.

**NF-4 · A POOLED FACE IS WRITTEN ONCE AND DRAWN BY MANY TOWNS.** Any proper noun a face carries that is not borne by a slot — a person's name, a tavern, a lane, a family — prints identically in every town that draws that pool. No per-town field denies it, so no licence test would ever have caught it; it is a cross-town identity collision and it belongs in the refuters' instrument as its own class (it is the real ground under W27b's "never be given a name").

**NF-5 · `plagued` CARRIES A CONSEQUENCE, NOT ONLY A CAUSE.** Floor 4 already says `plagued` is monsters and never disease. The engine goes further on the same page: at the safest band it prints "The constant monster threat keeps the guard exceptionally well-drilled and alert." (`safetyProfile.js:276`). So a `plagued` face writing a cowed, sickened or demoralised town contradicts the panel beside it wherever that branch fires.

**NF-6 · THE OCCUPIER IS SPELLED TWO WAYS ON TWO SURFACES.** `occupierName = nameFor(rec.occupierId)` is a foreign TOWN's name (`occupationStatus.js:83-96`), while the map overlay spells the occupier as the OCCUPIED town's installed `governingName` or the literal `'occupation authority'` (`warStatus.js:318-335`; `pulseKernel.js:1150`). A face naming one where the surface beside it prints the other is a same-page mismatch. It is a wiring row for the register car as much as a writer's finding, and it is recorded here so the refuters do not charge the writer for it.

## WHAT THE SLICE COSTS AND BUYS

Struck or half-struck: 16 of 20 items. The four that keep full teeth (W25a, W25b, W26a, W22c) are two alias faults, one audience model and one constitutional scope rule — none of them touches flavour. What comes back to the writer is the entire population of the town (W22a, W22b), every record word a reader knows (W24a, W24c), every stance's own idiom (W27a, W27b), relations between things (W23), and the freedom to narrate a band through the bodies the town actually has (W20). The one rule that survives everywhere, in six different costumes, is: **do not seat a body, a record, a hall or a force the town's roster does not hold** — and the roster is one grep away from any writer or refuter (`priorityHelpers.js:45-60`).
