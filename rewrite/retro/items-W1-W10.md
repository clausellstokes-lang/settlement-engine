**What this slice of the old law was FOR.** These sixteen items are the *sediment of one refute round*. On 2026-09-10 five Fable refuters read 48 wordings of the validation block DS-DEF-11 and returned 25 FAILs, each with a law and a quote; the chair then owed eight rulings on the disputed ones and, separately, folded the refuters' recurring *finding classes* into ten standing writer rules that would bind every block after. So this slice is not a theory of truth — it is a list of the ways one cohort of writers actually went wrong, promoted to law. That origin is exactly why it converts so hard: under the LICENCE test, "the refuters kept catching this" was sufficient grounds to make a rule, and most of what they caught was **permission bookkeeping** (does the card license this standpoint, this frame, this citation, this attribute?), **tone** (the maxim voice, the summarising beat, the house verbs), or **pipeline fidelity** (did the refiner keep the draft's declared grammar?). Under ADDENDUM 14 — a face is lawful unless it CONTRADICTS the record; silence is permission — none of those three classes is a finding any more. What survives survives because a named FIELD on a rendered surface would be made *false* by the breach: the one-purse upkeep multiplier, the `Invasion & War` row printed beside the block, and the tier word printed on the identity strip.

## THE STRIKES MOST LIKELY TO BE WRONG

Five, ordered by how badly a reviewer would be served if the strike stands and is wrong. Each argument is put as strongly as I can put it, against the decision recorded above.

**1. R-i — THE THREAD AT k = 0.** The strike says the ENGINE already holds the thread at `passageShapes.js:22-30`. Read the ground again: that guard governs the selection of the `sentence-then-spine` **shape**, which by definition exists only when there is an added sentence — that is, when **k ≥ 1**. R-i is named "THE THREAD AT **k = 0**". At k = 0 there is no added face, no shape to refuse, and therefore *no mechanical guard at all*: the spine renders alone and its own second sentence is the whole passage. The strike cites, as its cure, an instrument that is provably absent in exactly the case the rule was written for. Worse, the residual harm is not taste: a second sentence that "reads as if something precedes it" — a dangling "it", a bare "Its" — **asserts a referent the page never supplies**, which is a floor-1 defect against the rendered surface, not a preference about rhythm. The packet's own NEW FINDING 1 half-concedes the point and then displaces it to composition, where the k = 0 case cannot follow. If one strike in this slice should be reversed on the argument alone, it is this one.

**2. W1 — POSSESSOR BINDING.** The strike leans entirely on `holderTable.js:188-193` filing `walls`, `garrison`, `militia`, `mercenary` and `charter` under one holder kind. But that table says which **record holds which field** — it is a fact about record-keeping, not a claim that the wall possesses a body of men. "Its muster", after a `{defwork}`-subject clause, asserts a possession relation between a *structure* and a *force*, and the engine models no such relation anywhere; the shared roll is precisely the kind of coincidence the entailment law (ratified 09-11) forbids reading as an entailment — a recorded noun licenses what it MEANS, never what it typically HAS. Note also what the refuters actually caught: ADDENDUM 12 records that the possessive route "makes the wall's muster, **the shipped watch by another route**" — i.e. this exact construction was the vector by which a *different institution* (the watch, whose holder is not the muster kind) got asserted of the wall. And the REFERENT LAW's writer bars W20–W27, ratified **after** this rule and not part of this slice, fix a word's referent by the READ'S LAYER; a possessive that binds to the wrong layer is a referent breach on its own ground. The strike may have used a record-keeping row to overturn a rule that survives under a law the slice never consulted.

**3. R-vi and W7 — THE ROLL AS AGENT-SOURCE (two rows, so a wrong strike costs double).** The strike says citing the roll is "POSITIVELY supported" because the muster roll is a modelled record-keeper. That does not follow. Being the *holder of a field* in the prose layer's holder table is not being a *document that shows things to a reader*; the holder table is an authoring device for choosing a noun, not a model of an in-world artefact with contents. Decisively: **W24, THE RECORD-WORD BAR — ratified later, in ADDENDUM 13B, and living in the sibling slice W20–W27 — states that the `[ledger]` ANGLE "licenses NO record noun and no citation ('the books show' is a citation; 'entered as standing' is the office's formula, R-vi/W7)."** W24 cites R-vi/W7 *as its authority*. Striking R-vi here, in a slice that never opened W20–W27, either silently repeals a clause of a ratified law or leaves the two slices of one fold in open contradiction. A reviewer should treat this as the slice's clearest cross-slice hazard, independently of whether the citation bar is itself right.

**4. R-viii′'s struck half — the no-headcount bar.** The packet dismisses R-DST-A as "a cell-overlap rule, not a truth". But ADDENDUM 14's floor 1 is stated as *no self-contradiction against the WHOLE town's record **or another surface on the same page***. R-DST-A is nothing but a floor-1 instrument in operational dress: DS-DEF-5's armed-forces surface states the force's condition from the military score, and if DS-DEF-11's face independently narrates the men drifting off and posts unfilled, two surfaces on one page can state different things about the same quantity — precisely the breach floor 1 names. The packet's own defence ("the multiplier actually REDUCES the funded force, so slow thinning is model-TRUE") proves the *direction* is true and says nothing about whether *this desk* may state it; truth of the proposition was never the objection. Un-striking this half costs a writer almost nothing (they lose one image) and restores the only rule preventing two desks from narrating the same cell.

**5. W4 — THE CONSTRUCTION CONTRACT.** This is the one strike in the slice that removes an arm which **demonstrably caught something**: ADDENDUM 12 records that the refuters found "the CONSTRUCTION COLLAPSE in SMALL (the refiner moved variant 2 onto variant 1's grammar)". The replacement named in the strike — the pool-grain CRAFT verdict — is an instrument ADDENDUM 14 *creates*, i.e. one that does not yet exist, has never been run, and has no demonstrated sensitivity to construction collapse. Striking a working arm in favour of an unbuilt one is a net loss of coverage until the replacement is built and shown to fire. And the grains do not match: W4 charged a specific face and named the regression, whereas a pool-grain DULL verdict has no mechanism to say *which* variant moved or to send that variant back. If the reviewer accepts the strike in principle, the honest form is "SUSPENDED pending the CRAFT verdict's first firing", not STRUCK.
