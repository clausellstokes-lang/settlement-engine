# ITEMS — W20 … W27, THE EIGHT REFERENT BARS OF ADDENDUM 13 PART B

**Slice.** The writer bars **W20–W27** (layer · baked-power · person · fused-agent · record-word · external-body · visibility · angle-tag), as ratified in brief **ADDENDUM 13 PART B** on 2026-09-11, decomposed into **20 items** wherever one bar carried more than one rule. Item ids are the fold's (`W21a`, `W21b`, `W21c`, …); they are not in the original law, which numbered only the eight bars.

**Source packet.** `rewrite/retro/evidence/convert-W20-W27.md` (the re-cut of this slice under ADDENDUM 14). **Verbatim old-law text** in column 2 is quoted from the ratified brief itself — `rewrite/retro/verbatim/BRIEF-pre-recut-ADDENDA-1-to-13.md` §B lines 172–180 (rule-1 text for W22c from §A.1, line 166) — *not* from the packet's own "gist of the bar" column, which paraphrased. Grounds, fields and permissions in columns 4 and 5 are the packet's, condensed but not softened.

**Item count.** 20. **Verdict tally: CONTRADICTION 2 · MODEL 1 · SCOPE 1 · SPLIT 7 · STRUCK 9.** This matches the packet's own stated shape ("Of twenty items: 2 CONTRADICTION, 1 MODEL, 1 SCOPE, 9 STRUCK, 7 SPLIT") exactly. Struck or half-struck: 16 of 20.

**What this slice of the old law was FOR.** W20–W27 were the *referent* discipline: they governed **which thing in the world a face's nouns were allowed to be pointing at**. The premise was the LICENCE test — a face had to be provably backed by the record — and so each bar worked by matching the grain of a noun to the grain of the read that produced it. A number computed *over* institutions (a band, a score, a ladder rung) was not allowed to be spoken as any one institution; a power was not allowed to be a hall unless a hall row stood; a record could be cited only by the ratified noun of a keeper the card had actually resolved; a person could not carry weight at all, because the institution table reads no NPC edge; a stance tag (`[elder]`, `[street]`) licensed a voice and never a body. The bars were enforced not because any of these things were *false* but because the card could not *prove* them — the whole family is a permission regime, and that is precisely why ADDENDUM 14's re-cut to NON-CONTRADICTION cuts through it so hard. What survived the re-cut is one repeated core, in six costumes: **do not seat a body, a record, a hall or a force that the town's roster does not hold**, because the engine prints the roster's own denial on the same page.

## THE STRIKES MOST LIKELY TO BE WRONG

Five strikes in this slice a reviewer should challenge first. Each argument is put as strongly as it can honestly be put, *against* the fold's own decision.

**1 · W23, THE FUSED-AGENT BAR (STRUCK) — the slice treats two identical structures in opposite ways.** The fold struck W23 on the ground that the four typed causes at `institutionStatusModel.js:118-120` are "what the world-run may CITE, never an exhaustive list of what may be described". But three rows earlier, at **W21b**, the fold KEPT a bar precisely because `RULING_POWERS` is "a TOTAL classification failing soft to `mixed`" — i.e. a closed typed set was read as a positive model whose absent values are denials. `institutionStatusModel`'s cause set is the same kind of object: a typed, closed enumeration the engine uses to explain WHY an institution stands where it stands. If a closed value set denies unlisted values for ruling power, it denies unlisted causes for institutional decay, and "the resistance is bleeding the garrison" asserts a causal mechanism the model does not carry — a floor-4 contradiction, not mere silence. Worse, the fused relation is the single construction most likely to smuggle in floor-2 breaches: "the hall moves against the operators and the operators move back" asserts an *episode*, a sequence with a before and an after, which is invented history in everything but tense. The strike may have released the one sentence shape that reliably manufactures the two breaches the re-cut still calls fatal.

**2 · W24a, THE RECORD-WORD WHITELIST (STRUCK) — it is internally inconsistent with W24b, which was kept.** The fold kept W24b because a record noun whose keeper the roster denies is a label trap. But several of the barred words *are themselves keeper-entailing*: under the ENTAILMENT LAW (ADDENDUM 13A, ratified and NOT superseded by ADDENDUM 14 — a recorded noun licenses what it MEANS), "**minutes**" entail a deliberative body that sits and records its sitting; "**the writ**" and "**a licence**" entail an instrument-issuing legal authority; "**returns**" and "**manifests**" entail a reporting obligation running upward to a body that receives them. On a town where `inst.hasCourtSystem` and `inst.hasCharterHall` are false, "the minutes" and "the writ" seat a court and a chartering authority the roster denies — by the fold's own core rule, that is a contradiction printed beside the roster panel. The honest verdict for W24a is therefore SPLIT (strike the arbitrary per-kind noun allocations; keep the keeper-entailing words on a roster test), and STRUCK is over-broad by exactly the residue the fold itself preserved one row later.

**3 · W26b, A PLAYER FACE CITING AN INTERESTED HOLDER (STRUCK) — it is W26a's own ground, refused.** W26a was kept as MODEL on the reasoning that "a player face naming a covert fact denies the projection printed beside it". The capture the player face would be citing *through* is, at `corrupted` and `capture`, exactly such a DM-side fact, and `publicDossier` nulls the desk on the player projection (`PowerTab.jsx:194-215`). A player face that quotes the captured court's roll as a plain fact asserts that this record is publicly available and publicly credible — while the projection beside it is withholding the very condition that makes it neither. The fold's answer is that this is "unreliable narration, the most usable plot hook in the slice". But unreliability is an *authorial* stance, invisible at the face grain: a pooled sentence carries no frame telling the reader the citation is compromised, and the reader sees an assertion. The strike may be licensing the one move that turns a modelled audience split into a flat claim.

**4 · W22a, THE PERSON BAR (STRUCK) — the strike is wider than the relaxation that authorises it, and it moved 19 findings without re-examining one.** ADDENDUM 14 floor 3 licenses an UNNAMED person to "appear, act, keep a key, be avoided, be resented" — a singular, incidental person. The bar also refused **person-plural institutional practice**: "the clerks record", "the collectors travel", "the officers posted". Those phrases do not merely put a person on the page; they assert a *standing body* of clerks, a *collection circuit*, a *posting rota* — a body by implication, which is the label trap wearing a person's coat, and precisely the residue the fold was careful to preserve at W22b ("a garrison commander with no garrison row fails as a label trap"). W22a got no such carve-out. And the strike silently converts nineteen recorded defense-desk findings (D-F6…D-F15) from breaches to hooks in one stroke, with no per-finding check that each really was the incidental-person case rather than the institutional-plural case.

**5 · W24c, THE `[ledger]` CITATION BAN (STRUCK) — "a budget is not a truth condition" answers only half the bar.** The fold's ground quotes A13's self-description (a provenance move spending an unset budget) and concludes there is no truth condition. But a citation does two things: it spends a budget *and* it asserts that a record EXISTS and SAYS this. The second is a truth claim, and it is the exact claim W24b was kept to police. The `[ledger]` tag is the standing invitation to make that claim — it is the clerk's voice, and the clerk cites. Striking W24c wholesale while keeping W24b leaves the residue attached to the wrong bar: a `[ledger]` face on a SOURCE-UNRESOLVED read now reads as licensed by W24c's strike and refused by W24b's survival, and a refuter reading the bars in order will find them contradicting each other. At minimum this should have been SPLIT with the cross-reference made explicit.
