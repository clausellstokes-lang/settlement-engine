**Findings for the chair (reported, not refusals).**

1. **The pool key's discriminating claim is not licensed by its own card.** `WALLED-QUIET` is "walls, no live threat", but the card's may-claim names only `present`, its predicate line reads "(none recovered: the pool has no key-function branch)", and "a second fact" is refused outright. So the quiet — the half that distinguishes this pool from `WALLED-THREATENED` — is dropped from all three variants, and the three rewritten spines now carry the same typed claim a rewritten `WALLED-THREATENED` spine would carry. That collides with MOVE-GRAMMAR §3.4 / U9 ("a grammar is not licensed to drop a clause carrying the pool key's discriminating claim"). Two ways out, both above a writer's seat: recover the pool's predicate into the card so the quiet becomes claimable, or rule that a state key may select without being said. The rows above take the card as the authority, per the brief.
2. **`{defwork}` is NAMED BUT NEVER FILLED at this block's call sites** (the card's bag line). Every one of the twelve faces needs it — the pool cannot be written without naming its object, and the pool's SLOTS line forbids a baked noun. If the fill never arrives, every variant of this pool is either unrenderable or renders a raw token; this is a wiring row, not a wording row.
3. **Band positions this draft knowingly sits outside.** Within-pool words-per-sentence sd is ≈ 1.8 against R-DA-05's ≥ 4.0 floor (lengths 5 to 11 words), and R-DA-04's close-kind spread is compressed to two kinds (object and condition). Both follow from a one-claim licence: there is not enough licensed material to carry a long sentence or a varied close. One soft rule exceeded on the pool grain, well inside §16.1's budget of a third; reported as distance, not cured by inventing a fact.
4. **Sibling distance across FACES is thin by the same cause.** With a bag of two nouns and one claim, the faces separate by word order, aspect and register (visitor: plain declarative and inversion; elder: semicolon, fronted object, resumptive; ledger: entered / carried / standing work, colon, pseudo-cleft), not by lexical field. No face row opens on the proper-typed `{settlement}` slot (ARCH §2.5, T-F8).
