# DS-DEF-11 · `WALLED-STRAINED` · REWRITE draft round 1

*Seat: Opus 5 — Fable-unvalidated. Two existing variants, rewritten in place under their own
numbers and their own angle tags; no variant added, removed, merged or reordered. Four wordings
per variant: the numbered line is face one, the three `[face]` sub-rows are faces two to four.
Paste-ready under the pool's bold heading; the pool's typed lines are untouched and not repeated.*

**`WALLED-STRAINED`**
1. `[ledger]` {settlement}'s {defwork} stands, and the paid muster is short of its funding.
   - `[face]` {settlement} has its {defwork}. The pay does not cover the muster.
   - `[face]` {settlement} holds a {defwork}: the muster runs under its keep.
   - `[face]` {settlement} owns its {defwork}, and what the town lays out for the muster falls under its cost.
2. `[unfolding]` The {defwork} at {settlement} holds its place, and the muster's upkeep is a term unmet.
   - `[face]` The {defwork} is in place at {settlement}. The wage the muster comes to is an account the town has not answered.
   - `[face]` The {defwork} is {settlement}'s; what the muster costs is unsettled.
   - `[face]` The {defwork} belongs to {settlement}, and the muster's charge stands open.

--- NOTES

**THE CARD, AS THIS DRAFT READS IT (one interpretive call, stated so it can be vetoed).** The card
licenses two reads and one assertion. `forces.walls.present` is the candidacy read: it makes the
{defwork} a NAMEABLE civic object and its PRESENCE a fact of the record, and the may-NOT line
refuses "ANOTHER civic object of the class `wall`", which by its own wording leaves the first one
available (compare ARCH §8.3's exemplar card, which refuses "a granary's presence" precisely
because the granary is not among that card's reads). What is NOT licensed is the {defwork}'s
CONDITION — sound, whole, well kept, better than something else — because no read measures it.
Every face therefore says the enclosure IS, and nothing about how it fares. If the chair reads the
may-NOT's "a second fact" as reaching the presence read too, both variants collapse to the gate
alone and every face here loses its first clause; that ruling is the chair's, and this draft is
written under the reading above.

**WHAT EACH FACE CLAIMS, AND THE CLAUSE THAT LICENSES IT.** Both variants carry the same two-claim
set, which is the licensed residue of the old sentences:
- **C1 — the {defwork} is present.** Licensed by `reads: forces.walls.present (measured)`, and by
  `bag: {defwork: bare-common}` for the noun. Realised as: "stands" (1.1), "has its" (1.2), "holds
  a" (1.3), "owns its" (1.4), "holds its place" (2.1), "is in place" (2.2), "is {settlement}'s"
  (2.3), "belongs to" (2.4). No face predicates a state of repair of it.
- **C2 — the settlement's military upkeep gate is unmet.** Licensed by `reads:
  settlement.defenseProfile.economicGates.military (measured)`, `predicate: military < 1`, and
  `may claim: that military (< 1) holds, as a STANDING fact of the record`. Realised as: "short of
  its funding" (1.1), "the pay does not cover the muster" (1.2), "runs under its keep" (1.3),
  "what the town lays out falls under its cost" (1.4), "a term unmet" (2.1), "an account the town
  has not answered" (2.2), "unsettled" (2.3), "the charge stands open" (2.4).
- **{settlement}** in every face is licensed by `bag: {settlement: proper}`; the slot set is
  identical in all eight wordings and identical to the two BEFORE sentences, so the pair
  instrument's SLOTS arm holds.
- The gate is stated as a shortfall and never as a non-payment: `military < 1` is funding below
  what upkeep comes to, not funding at zero, so no face says the muster is unpaid.

**WHAT WAS DROPPED, AND UNDER WHICH REFUSAL.** Dropping these is the rewrite's purpose; the shipped
wordings are the corpus's known state.
- Variant 1 dropped **the comparison of the wall's condition to the watch's** ("stands better than
  the watch that should man it") on three counts: a condition claim no read measures; a DUTY
  ("should man it") absent from the institution table (CLERK-LAWS §1.3); and an office, "the
  watch", the card does not hold. The licensed noun for this field is the muster (ARCH §2.5's own
  exemplar row renders `economicGates.military` as "the paid muster is short of its funding").
- Variant 1 dropped **"stone keeps itself, and wages do not"**: a CAUSE (may NOT) and a life-general
  closer (R-DA-12's generalisation test). It is also the nearest thing in the block to a sibling's
  sentence — `WALLED-QUIET` v2 already holds "walls are easier to keep than to raise" — so keeping
  it would breach arm A1 as well.
- Variant 2 dropped **"is sound"** (condition, may NOT), **"is thinning"** (a movement over time: the
  card licenses a STANDING value and refuses a season and a future), and **"which is the kind of
  arithmetic a town notices late"** (a which-clause, a standpoint, and a verdict, all three refused).
- Nothing was added. No face carries a count, a cause, a season, a future, a standpoint, a totality
  over persons, a belief frame, or a second wall-class object.

**PROVENANCE, DELIBERATELY UNSPENT.** The card marks `source: muster · standing LICENSED`, so a
citation would be lawful, but §24's ceiling permits one only for two accounts that disagree, a
count from an interested party, or a record whose keeper is a power, and none of the three holds
here; the exemplar registers cite at zero per 786 sentences. No face names a record-holder, and
"on the books" was cut from an earlier draft of 1.1 for exactly that reason (the office does not
cite its own books, MOVE-GRAMMAR §4.4.3).

**GRAMMAR (level 1), for the annex's `grammar:` tag, which is not mine to write.** Variant 1 reads
as **V4 (OBJECT → PRESENT)**: the named enclosure, then the standing shortfall. Variant 2 reads as
**V6 (PRESENT → OPEN)**: the walled state, then the unmet term named as standing open, which is the
OPEN QUESTION move's own licence (a term unmet, declarative, never interrogative) and is what
keeps the two variants in distinct grammars as MOVE-GRAMMAR §2.1 requires of a pool of two. All
four faces of a variant keep that variant's move order, so no face disagrees with its tag.

**THE THREAD.** This pool is a SPINE, so each face is written to open a passage rather than to
follow one: every face ends on the civic thing a modifier can pick up (the muster, its cost, its
keep, its charge, the account, the term), and none turns outward. No face is a fragment, and none
opens on a comma or on a clause-list word.

**FORM AND BAND NOTES (reported, not gates).** No em dash, no exclamation, no digit, no percent, no
question, no `which`, no first or second person, no future indicative, no figure, no expletive
opener anywhere in the eight wordings. One colon (1.3) and one semicolon (2.3) across eight faces.
The settlement token opens variant 1 only, never variant 2, per R-DA-17's one-per-pool rule; no
face closes on a pronoun. Word counts run 10 to 21 with a pool standard deviation of 3.7, which
sits under R-DA-05's within-pool floor of 4.0: with one licensed assertion and one licensed noun,
a longer line can only be bought with an unlicensed claim, so the band is exceeded deliberately
and reported here rather than met by invention (§16's BUDGET: one soft rule exceeded, well inside
a third).

**A HAZARD FOR THE CHAIR, NOT A REFUSAL.** The card's bag says `{defwork}` is **NAMED BUT NEVER
FILLED** at this block's call sites, while `{settlement}` is filled. Every wording here uses
`{defwork}`, because the BEFORE sentences do and the pair instrument fails a rewrite that changes
the slot set — but if the block lights while `{defwork}` resolves to nothing, all eight wordings
render with an unfilled marker. That is a wiring row, not a text law, and the block landed dark;
it is raised so it is not discovered at the first render.

**REFUSALS: none.** Both variants are written and both are lawful under the reading declared above.
