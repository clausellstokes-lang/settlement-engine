# DS-DEF-11 · `WALLED-STRAINED` · REWRITE refinement round (arm A)

*Seat: Opus 5 — Fable-unvalidated; a different author than the drafter. One refinement pass over
the lawful draft at `draft-round-2.md`, one for one: two variants, their vids, their order and
their own angle tags unmoved; four wordings each, none added and none dropped. The pool's typed

the {defwork} is the town's, and the town's military upkeep gate is short — and adds nothing.*

**`WALLED-STRAINED`**
1. `[ledger]` The {defwork} at {settlement} is the town's. Its muster is paid short of upkeep.
   - `[face]` At {settlement} the {defwork} is the town's own work. The outlay on its muster does not cover what the upkeep comes to.
   - `[face]` The {defwork} is {settlement}'s own. Its muster costs more than the town pays.
   - `[face]` The town at {settlement} owns the {defwork}. Its muster's wage falls short.
2. `[unfolding]` The {defwork} of {settlement} is in the town's hands. Upkeep for its muster is a term unmet.
   - `[face]` At {settlement} the town is walled with the {defwork}. Its muster's pay is an account the town has not answered.
   - `[face]` The town at {settlement}, the {defwork} its own, has the muster's hire unsettled.
   - `[face]` The town at {settlement} holds the {defwork}. Provision for its muster is not made good.

--- NOTES

**1 `[ledger]` (numbered line).** *paid short of the upkeep* → *paid short of upkeep*: the definite
article let the shortfall read as the WALL's upkeep, and the receipt's own recorded law is the
opposite one (built walls keep standing; the wages are what go short), so the bare mass noun puts
the money back on the muster and hardens the close. Target: the sharpest licensed fact (§21.1);
guard: the card's `may NOT` on a second civic object of the class `wall`.

**1a.** *is a work the town owns* → *is the town's own work* (the possessive tightened to one beat),
and the muddy *the outlay against its muster falls under the charge* → *the outlay on its muster
does not cover what the upkeep comes to*: *falls under the charge* reads as jurisdiction, not
shortfall, and a line that misdirects is not compression but noise. *What it comes to* is the
ledger's way of naming a sum with no digit (A4). This is the pool's long face and its length is
bought with precision, never padding (§21.4).

**1b.** *What the town's muster costs runs past its funding* → *Its muster costs more than the town
pays*: the fronted *What* clause went (three of the draft's eight wordings opened a sentence on it —
a ledger-closer habit across the pool), and the predicate is now the card's own comparison, stated
as a measurement in words (R-DA-11). Shortest but one; the thread runs *{settlement}'s own → Its
muster*.

**1c.** *The {defwork} is held by the town at {settlement}* → *The town at {settlement} owns the
{defwork}*, and *What it sets aside for the muster does not reach the wage* → *Its muster's wage
falls short*: the passive presence half was the flattest of the four and its money half the most
prepositional. The face is now the pool's short-short rhythm, twelve words, and the enclosure sits
in object position so the plural member of the wall-name class renders correctly here.

**2 `[unfolding]` (numbered line).** Held at its draft wording. Under §21.2 a face is refined only
toward the ceiling, and every substitution tried against this one — a possessive for *in the town's
hands*, a fuller clause for *a term unmet* — traded the OPEN move's hardest four words for a
longer, plainer line. The refinement is recorded as a HOLD with its reason, not as a dry pass:
the other seven wordings moved.

**2a.** *The account for its muster's pay is one the town has not answered* → *Its muster's pay is
an account the town has not answered*: the copula-plus-*one* tail was flab, and the new subject
carries the {defwork} forward by *Its*, which the draft's sentence did not do — THE THREAD (§1.4.1)
wants the noun handed on, and *The account for its muster's pay* opened on a fact of its own.

**2b.** *the muster's wage unsettled* → *the muster's hire unsettled*: the appositive rhythm is the
best in the pool and is kept whole; the noun changes so that *wage* belongs to variant one alone and
this variant's four money-halves stay four vocabularies (term · account · hire · provision). The
antique air sits in the noun and nowhere in the syntax (R-DA-18).

**2c.** *The town at {settlement} owns the {defwork}* → *holds the {defwork}* (the verb *owns* is now
variant one's, so no two faces of this pool share a presence verb), and *What the muster's upkeep
comes to is a charge not made good* → *Provision for its muster is not made good*: the second *What*
opener went with the first, *charge* is left to variant one, and the OPEN move keeps its unmet form.
