**1 `[street]` (numbered)** — *was* `{settlement} is no larger than a village. The place stands
without a wall.` The tier sentence is kept deliberately: it is the flattest, most exact statement

the anchor the other seven wordings are spread from. The LACK sentence changes: `stands without a

sibling pool of DS-DEF-11 uses, where the drafted `stands` was the block's most-worn verb (it
carries `WALLED-THREATENED` and `WALLED-QUIET` between them nine times). Four words of predicate
instead of five, landing on the civic thing (R-DA-04) with the thread carried by **the place**
(MOVE-GRAMMAR §1.4.1). Target: sibling distance and the read-aloud ear.

**1 face a** — *was* `Village size is as far as {settlement} goes. Nothing walls the place.` The
second sentence was a direct collision with the sibling pool `UNWALLED-LARGE`, whose own face
reads *nothing walls {settlement}*: one block, two pools, one predicate, on opposite sides of the
same STATE-KEY. It is replaced by **The place goes unwalled**, which puts the adjective form of
the field's own word into the pool once and holds this face to ten
words. The tier half tightens from *as far as {settlement} goes* to **where {settlement}
stops** — the same ceiling claim, two words shorter, and it clears `goes` for use nowhere else in
the variant. Law: CLERK-LAWS C5 (sibling coherence) and the §21.1 sibling-distance target.

**1 face b** — *was* `The size of {settlement} stops short of a town. The place goes unwalled.`
Two changes, one of them a claim-neutral removal. First, the CONTRAST goes: *stops short of a
town* was the pool's single antithesis-shaped wording, licensed by R-DA-02 only because it named
the sibling key, and R-DA-02's direction is DOWN (per-variant shape ceiling 0.045); dropping it
takes the pool's antithesis rate from one in eight to zero and takes the word *town* out of a pool
whose C2 asserts the place is not one (R-DA-22, one term for one thing). The claim survives
unchanged in **A village is the whole of {settlement}**, a positive ceiling idiom. Second, the
LACK takes **No wall rings it** — four words, a verb used nowhere
else in the block, and the thread carried by the pronoun rather than by a repeated noun.

**1 face c** — *was* `A village is the size {settlement} keeps. The place holds no wall.` The
second sentence collided with `UNWALLED-LARGE`'s numbered row, *it holds no wall*, word for word
in its predicate; the first used **keeps**, which is `WALLED-QUIET`'s own verb (*{settlement} keeps

further than a village** and the LACK becomes **It is not a walled place** — a predicate nominal,

the same STATE-KEY. At sixteen words this is now the pool's longest wording against variant 2 face c's seven,
which widens the within-pool length spread the draft reported as unreached (R-DA-05) without
adding a claim to buy it.

**2 `[visitor]` (numbered)** — *was* `The village that {settlement} amounts to has no wall.` The

**2 face a** — *was* `No bigger than a village, {settlement} is a place without a wall.` The
