3. `[street]` `[plain]` Attack is not much thought about at {settlement}, and the calm is not complacency. The arrangements are in place, and the town leans on nothing else instead.

4. `[counterforce]` `[plain]` Nothing presses {settlement} at present, and the absence is not luck. A town set this well is not worth the price of an attempt.

   - `[face]` The present state at {settlement} is quiet, and accident has no share in it. A place this well arranged is not worth what an attempt on it would cost.
   - `[face]` The quiet around {settlement} owes nothing to luck: nobody is moving on the town at present, and a defended place of this order is a poor bargain to attempt.

Every OTHER claim in every face is licensed by NO clause of the card, because the card's `reads` and `predicate` lines are empty and its `may claim` line reads *nothing*. What the claims ARE licensed by, at the block grain, is the annex header's **STATE-KEY** (`defenseProfile.readiness.label` read against `scoreBand`, `STRONG` ≥ 65) — a typed field, so Part B §9 ruling 5 is satisfied at the block; the card is not. This is refusal R1 and it is pool-wide.

## B. Refusals

- **R1 · POOL-WIDE, the governing refusal.** The licence card for this pool licenses no claim: `reads` = "the census recovered no reading for this pool", `predicate` = "none recovered", `may claim` = "nothing: the census recovered no reading, so no claim is licensed". Under the rewrite rule "every claim in every face is licensed by the LICENCE CARD and by nothing else", **all four variants and all sixteen wordings are unlawful and are banked as a refusal set** (§21.2: banked with its faces, never trimmed). They are lawful against the BLOCK's typed STATE-KEY, which is why they are written out in full rather than left empty. The cure is not a writer's: the census must recover this pool's `reads` (`defenseProfile.readiness.label` against `scoreBand`) and its predicate (`= STRONG`), after which this draft should be re-gated unchanged. Nothing in this packet should be read as evidence that the pool is lawful.
- **R2 · The card contradicts itself on the standpoint.** `angle:` names `counterforce ledger street visitor`; `may NOT:` bars "a standpoint". Three of the four variants ARE standpoints by their tags, which are not mine to touch. Written under the `angle` clause; a chair ruling is owed on which line governs. If `may NOT` governs, variants 1, 3 and 4 cannot be made lawful in any wording and the pool collapses to the `[ledger]` variant alone — which would be a trim by another route, so the ruling matters.
- **R3 · A denied cause is still a cause.** Variant 3's "the reason is not complacency" and variant 4's "the absence is not luck" are claims about causation, which the card bars. Both are the parent's own claims; removing them is trimming (§22 (e): refinement is a one-for-one substitution, never a removal). Written, flagged, not cured.
- **R4 · More than one fact per unit.** The card's `may NOT` bars "a second fact"; variants 1, 2 and 3 each carry four claims and variant 4 carries three. A1 permits two sentences and the register card permits a second fact in its own sentence, so this is a card/register conflict rather than a writer's fault, but on the card's plain words no wording in this pool is lawful. **R4b:** variant 2's "the town knows as much" is a cognitive state of a collective and meets the FEELING non-move (MOVE-GRAMMAR §1.3); inherited from the parent.
- **R5 · The block's own PROVENANCE fence.** Variants 1 and 2 assert per-arm facts (the approaches watched, the works kept, a force fieldable) beneath a band word the block declares "a summary of the five arms" that "asserts nothing about any one of them". **R5b:** variant 4's "nothing presses at present" asserts a fact about the threat environment that no field in the STATE-KEY holds. Both are inherited; both are unfixable without dropping a claim.
- **R6 · The generalisation.** Variant 4's closing proposition generalises over towns of this band. R-DA-12's walker is the gate and its baseline has not run; the claim is the parent's and is kept.
