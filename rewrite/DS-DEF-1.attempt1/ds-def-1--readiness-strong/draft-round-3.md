- **R4 · More than one fact per unit.** The card's `may NOT` bars "a second fact"; variants 1, 2 and 3 each carry four claims and variant 4 carries three. - **R5 · The block's own PROVENANCE FENCE.** Variants 1 and 2 assert per-arm facts (approaches watched, works kept, a force fieldable) beneath a band word the block declares a summary of the five arms that asserts nothing about any one of them. **R5b:** variant 4's "nothing presses at present" asserts a fact about the threat environment that no field in the STATE-KEY holds. Both are inherited; neither is fixable without dropping a claim.
- **R6 · The generalisation.** Variant 4's closing proposition generalises over towns of this band. R-DA-12's walker is the gate and its baseline has not run; the claim is the parent's and is kept.
- **R7 · NOT a refusal, recorded so it is not re-found (round 2).** The colon was a wording fault, not an unfixable one; round 2 removed all of them at zero cost in claims and round 3 introduces none.
