--- NOTES

### The claim inventory the rewrite carries (C-pair: the AFTER carries the BEFORE's typed claim set, no more)

The three variants as they stand carry, between them, four claims:

- **c1 — the exposure fact.** The site affords the defender nothing; the terrain value is `Plains`. Licensed by **C-may** and **C-pred** (the predicate's own value), read from **C-reads**. Carried by all twelve wordings; it is the pool's only fully licensed claim.
- **c2 — construction, not endowment.** Whatever defence the town has was built rather than given by the site (variant 1's *"everything the town has must be built rather than found"*). Read here as the converse of c1 and therefore as the same typed triple (PRESENT · terrain · Plains) in a second wording, not as a second fact — **declared, not refused**; see the flag below.
- **c3 — cost, standing and continuing.** Every advantage was paid for and must keep being paid for (variant 2's *"one it has paid for and must keep paying for"*). **REFUSED** — see refusal R-1.
- **c4 — the town's own account.** The town does not pretend otherwise (variant 3's *"does not pretend otherwise"*). **REFUSED** — see refusal R-2.
- **c5 — holding by presence.** What the town holds it holds by standing on it (variant 3's close). Read as c1 restated on the town's side (nothing in the ground holds the place, so the town's own presence does) — licensed by **C-may**.

### REFUSALS (a refusal is a result; the sets are banked with their faces, never trimmed)

- **R-1 · variant 2 · claim c3 (the cost, standing and continuing) — REFUSED, all four wordings.** No clause of the card licenses a cost: **C-reads** is the terrain text alone, **C-may** admits the terrain fact alone, and **C-maynot** bars a cause and a second fact. MOVE-GRAMMAR §1.2 row 7 (CONSEQUENCE) is double-licensed by event provenance AND a household/office/institution row, neither of which this spine holds; §2.1's V2 (`PRESENT → CONSEQUENCE(structural)`) needs a structural-consequence field, and the card declares `move: (none declared)`. The rewrite cannot cure it: dropping c3 would spend a claim the BEFORE carried (arm C-pair; §22's never-trim). The variant is written keeping c3 and the set is banked for the chair — either a structural-cost field is minted for the terrain read, or ruling 5's removal is authorised on the record.
- **R-2 · variant 3 · claim c4 (the town does not pretend otherwise) — REFUSED, all four wordings.** **C-maynot** bars a standpoint outright, and no typed field carries the town's own account of its site. The four wordings render c4 as a speech ACT rather than an interior, which is the least unlawful form available (R-DA-14 / NL-5 seen-not-meant), but the claim itself is unlicensed and is banked with the set. Note the card's `angle: ledger street visitor` licenses the FRAMING a face is set down in, not a claim about anyone's view; no face here asserts an observer, a stranger's perception or a belief.
- **Declared, not refused · variant 1 · claim c2.** *Built rather than found* is read as the converse of the exposure fact (if the site affords nothing, anything present is not of the site) and so as one typed triple in two wordings, not a second fact. The stronger reading — that c2 asserts the town as the builder, which the terrain field does not hold — is available to a refuter; the reading taken here is the BEFORE's own (its clause is agentless: *must be built rather than found*), and every face keeps the universal quantification so the claim stays vacuous where the town has nothing. Flagged for the refuter and the sitting.
