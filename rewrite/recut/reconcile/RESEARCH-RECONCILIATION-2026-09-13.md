## 4. What the research shows the law still lacks, and the chair's decisions

Five gaps recur across the slices. Each gets a decision here; the mechanism goes to the car named.

**31. THE SENSES — one physical particular per page (slices C and A; the owner's own "vividness" ask).** The law's page is entirely institutional: accounts, purses, rosters, who says what. Nothing forbids a smell or a sound, and nothing asks for one. Decision: the writer's first lens (the telling particular) asks for the thing seen, heard or smelled by name; the public (ruling 28) and the archiver as witness (ruling 27) may carry it; the selector keeps ONE physical particular per rendered page, elected by the same seed that draws the roles, and never more, so the page does not become scenery; floor 1 binds it as any claimed fact. The presence measure (concrete sensory nouns per hundred words, the research's own instrument) is reported per pool beside the DULL verdict and refuses nothing. *Reversal: no sensory licence.*

**32. THE BILL — a state's cost lands on a bearer (slice B: ninety-nine sources, Hobb and Kay carrying it).** The law gives every face a speaker with a stake and never asks who pays. Decision: a CONSEQUENCE candidate class joins the writer's page: a face's second sentence may land the state's cost on a household, trade or office the roster holds, in its own clause ("The wall is kept up. The households that keep it are the ones not paid for it."). The selector keeps at most one per variant. Floor 1 binds: the bearer must be a row or a class the town has. *Reversal: no consequence class.*

**33. NO FORECAST (slices A and B).** Under non-contradiction a prediction about the town contradicts nothing, so nothing catches it, and it is exactly what "the game master decides what actually happens" forbids. Decision: the future indicative is barred on every player face and every note, mechanically, in the E2 ratchet (a "will" or "shall" before a verb refuses the face); the conditional and the subjunctive stay ("would stand the wall tomorrow if the standing were paid" is lawful). *Reversal: the ratchet loses the arm.*

**34. THE LANDING — the close has an instrument (slice D).** The veto list is all about how a face ends and nothing measures it; ruling 29 charters an opener class and no close class. Decision: car 18o adds a mechanical close-class tag beside the opener class (which-tail, summary, antithesis, reassurance, question, plain), reported at block grain; the only refusal is the reassurance class on a face not tagged compromised, which ruling 26 already made lawful there alone. *Reversal: report only.*

**35. A NUMBER THE READER SEES (slice A's verdict; slices B and D).** Ruling 1 retired the per-face band and put taste in a reader's DULL verdict with no figure attached, while the fingerprint that measured the fourteen exemplars is still computed and read by no verdict. ## 8. The chair's thoughts, in plain words

The research was right about the disease and could not build the cure, because the cure needed a speaker and the old law forbade one. The thirty rulings built the speaker. Five independent readers, given the research and the law, all say the same three things: the diagnosis is honoured, the instruments were retired faster than they were replaced, and the page is still a civic archive rather than a place. The decisions above put the instruments back as evidence rather than gates, put the senses and the bill on the writer's page as things they may do, and fence the one structural risk the rulings created. After this, the law is not longer; it is more measured. The next pool is where it earns it.
