## 3. VERDICT

Read whole, the game-master research would say the law has arrived at most of what it asked for, and in two places has gone past its sources. The two-to-three sentence unit cap is right and well-founded — the research's own two-sentence read-aloud law, the DMG's "needs no more than a brief description", and a designer describing his own heavy boxed text as a dated habit. But the research's instrument for that cap is a reaction point, "a PLACE, not a count", measured as a band; ruling 30 converts that band into arithmetic, and the pair's one-sentence halves are where a lawful, well-shaped line will be cut for a count rather than a reason. The observable-thing-first rule matches exactly. Plain-not-casual half-matches: WotC style encourages contractions and the research's own record calls a plainer rewrite with no law behind it "the regression" — ruling 20's archaic-word amendment is the reconciliation, and R-DA-18's rename and swap gates should stay in the refuter's hands so plain does not become flat. The drawn roles match best of anything in the law: the DMG's settlement chapter is itself a cast list, and Gygax's coherence doctrine is ruling 16 arriving a half-century late; the one casualty is the DMG's informal power, the wise elder who outweighs the reeve, which should be preserved in the source roster.

THE ONE THING THE BEST GAME-MASTER PROSE DOES THAT THE LAW STILL LACKS IS THE SENSES. The DMG's settlement advice is built on a question the law never asks — "What does it look, smell, and sound like?" — and a method the law has no rung for: "Settle on a single defining factor that sums up a settlement's personality." Read-aloud text earns its name because a game master says it out loud and a physical scene appears at the table. The law's page, after thirty rulings, is entirely institutional and social. Its "observable thing first" means the observable fact, not the observable world. Nothing in floor 1 forbids a smell or a sound — the gap is an absence of licence: no writer is told they may, no lens asks for it, no selector rewards it. The cheapest cure is one rule with a hard ration: ONE PHYSICAL PARTICULAR PER RENDERED PAGE, carried by the public or the archiver as witness, bound by floor 1 exactly as any claimed fact, elected by the same seed that picks the page's lead pool. Without it the dossier will read as a very good civic archive and not yet as something a game master reads aloud.
