## 7. THREE FINDINGS THE CHAIR DOES NOT YET HAVE

1. **`deities[ref].covert` has no writer.** `DS-FTH-3`'s STATE-KEY (`docs/…DOSSIER_STATE.md:4670,4676`) and its `COVERT CONGREGATION` pool (`:4809-4813`) key on a field that appears nowhere in `src/domain/worldPulse/religionState.js`. This is exactly the `templeWealth` shape, and unlike `templeWealth` it is not marked anywhere. The `covert` flag that DOES exist is on `warPosture` (`mobilization.js:128,147`), a different subsystem.
2. **`covert` on a posture is never below `war_preparation`.** `covert: prev.covert && nextIdx >= RAMP_INDEX.war_preparation` (`mobilization.js:366,391`), and it is written `false` on every peace/demobilizing arm (`:315,343,349,375`). So `DS-WAR-1`'s `mobilization: COVERT` pool can only fire at `war_preparation` or above — its variant 1, *"the muster is being taken as something else"* (`docs/…DOSSIER_STATE.md:3446`), is licensed; a variant implying an early, tentative stage would not be.
3. **The `DS-WAR-1` `occupierHoldings.*` pools have inverted slot roles against the reader that shares their name.** `warFaithStateProse.js:282-294` records it in full: *"It is a fluent, confident, FALSE sentence, and nothing in the tree would have caught it: both roles are `proper`, both fills are real names."* This is the desk's own worked example of the class the owner is asking about — a face that is grammatical, filled, and wrong — and it is worth the chair's attention as the pattern case, because the failure was in the CALL and not in the sentence.

## 8. WHAT THE DESK'S OWN EVIDENCE SUGGESTS ABOUT THE OWNER'S RULE (not a decision; the chair's)

- The card's `may claim` grammar licenses **the record's truth**, never the noun's nature (§1). Any definitional-entailment rule is an addition to that grammar and will need its own line on the card if it is to be auditable the way `may NOT` is.
- On this desk the definitional entailments that survive are almost all **relational, not material**: an *occupation* entails a holder; a *treaty* entails two parties; a *front* entails an army; a *creed* entails an observance. The material kind the owner's examples turn on (a palisade is timber) has **no instance on this desk** — the desk names no work, no wall class, no mill, no well, no ferry. Its one physical noun class, the religious institutions of `institutionalCatalog.js`, is the one slot the desk cannot fill.
- The desk's sharpest hazard is not the definitional entailment at all: it is the **typical association** — age, permanence, size, cause, competence, will — attaching to a band word. Five of the seven `ENGINE CONTRADICTS` rows in §4 are of that shape (the scar that decays, the blockade that only touches airships, the `rested` town whose strength is unread, the spoils that are not an economic gain, the `require_betrayal` verdict that is about this town rather than the attacker).
