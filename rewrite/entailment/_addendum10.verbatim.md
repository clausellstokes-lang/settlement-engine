## ADDENDUM 10 (chair, 2026-09-09 23:5x) — THE ENTAILMENT LAW (draft for ratification at the 8b fold; the survey `rewrite/entailment/` builds its table)

The watch is the guard/military power. The other things that you have mentioned as false in that statement, I agree with. THE LAW, as the chair drafts it (vetoable; binding on the remaining 8b blocks once the table is ratified; the drafters read it as an ADDENDUM until the licence card prints it):
1. **A recorded noun licenses what the word MEANS, never what the thing typically HAS.** A palisade is timber; a wall of the stone class is stone; an earthwork is earth; a citadel is a stronghold; a mill grinds; a well holds water; a ferry crosses water; a watch keeps watch. These DEFINITIONAL attributes ride with the recorded name and a face may say them without a second field. Age, height, condition, manning, prosperity, a cause, a history, a count, a season, what the thing is usually for elsewhere: NOT entailed; each still needs its own read.
2. **The entailment attaches to the RECORDED MEMBER, not to the class word.** The slot renders the town's recorded name, so an attribute that varies across the class (the wall's material: stone for wall · citadel · inner citadel · massive walls; timber for palisade; earth for earthwork) may only be spoken through a DERIVED FILL computed from the recorded name (a `{defmaterial}` slot, minted by the desk from the name class, is the wiring the register car owes), never baked into a face as a constant. An attribute constant across the class (a wall encloses; a watch keeps watch) may be baked.
3. **An entailment is licensed only where the ENGINE'S OWN MODEL is consistent with it** (the owner's principle: the prose is never more knowledgeable than the simulation). The generator's recorded rule is that built walls keep standing whatever they are made of and unpaid soldiers desert slowly (`src/generators/defenseGenerator.js:182-192`, the upkeep gate on "garrison wages, wall maintenance"). So "the {defwork} keeps itself, and the muster's wages do not" is the engine's own asymmetry and is LICENSED on WALLED-STRAINED (ADDENDUM 9's fence-2 ruling); "timber rots" or "stone endures" is real-world knowledge the model does not hold and is REFUSED even where common sense agrees, because it predicts a decay the world never runs.
4. **LABEL TRAPS: an engine value is not an English word.** `config.monsterThreat = plagued` means plagued by MONSTER activity (`src/components/new/SummaryTab.jsx:37`), never disease; `settled` means no live threat, never founded. A face renders the engine MEANING of a label and never its dictionary sense; the survey lists every label the desks read with its engine meaning quoted from code.
5. **ALIAS TRAPS: a generic English word may name a class the engine splits into rows.** The engine has a Watch (`inst.hasWatch`), a militia (the muster; the Citizen militia), mercenaries, a charter hall and a garrison as distinct institution flags all feeding the military score and all gated by the same upkeep multiplier (`defenseGenerator.js:178-192`). The owner's "the watch is the guard/military power" is right as English and the law adopts it AS A CLASS WORD: "the guard", "the muster", "the men on the wall" are always-safe spellings of the paid military the gate measures; "the watch" as the institution's NAME follows the holder-table row this town resolves (S3: the source of each construction), so it is CONDITIONAL on `hasWatch` and the derived holder fill is the wiring that makes it safe. The same rule reaches every desk: "the temple" vs the recorded faith-house class, "the market" vs the recorded market band, "the council" vs the recorded ruling structure.
6. **The gate does not walk semantics; the refuters do.** The mechanical gate cannot tell an entailment from an added claim (the taste's blind spot), so the table is the REFUTERS' instrument: a Fable refuter passes an attribute in the ENTAILS column, fails one in NOT ENTAILED, and fails a face that uses a CONDITIONAL entailment without its condition resolved. The licence card prints `entails:` lines per read once the table is ratified (a REWRITE instrument car at the consist, alongside the `{defmaterial}` derived fill).
WHAT THE SURVEY (`wf_8ca05b10-e44`: one Opus surveyor + one Fable refuter per desk, a Fable folder → `rewrite/entailment/ENTAILMENT-TABLE.draft.md`) OWES: the table per desk (noun · class members · ENTAILS with the refuter's verdict · NOT ENTAILED · ENGINE CONTRADICTS · source), the merged label-trap and alias-trap tables, and the CONDITIONAL rows that need a wiring decision. The chair ratifies the table into this brief and into the workflow's RULES before DEF-2 / DEF-1 / DEF-5 launch, so no block is drafted under one law and judged under another.
