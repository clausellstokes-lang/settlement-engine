THE LAW, as the chair drafts it (vetoable):
1. **A word's referent is fixed by the layer of the READ the sentence carries, never by the word.** A body read (presence · pay · upkeep · muster · service rows · walls) refers to the INSTITUTION AS A BODY and uses body words (the muster, the garrison, the militia, the watch as a bucket name, the men on the wall). A standing read (capture · corruption impairment · patronage · INTERESTED) refers to the ORGAN UNDER A POWER and names the power only through the typed slot the engine records (the faction's name; "the ruling structure" as the class word), with the institution as the OBJECT of the sentence ("the watch is bought", "the house has a patron"). A role read (an office that exists) refers to the ROLE and states a standing condition, never a fate.
2. **The marker's skeleton tags every claim's REFERENT LAYER beside its licence** (BODY / ORGAN-UNDER-POWER / ROLE), and the licence card prints the layer per read once ratified; a refuter fails a face whose noun's layer does not match its read's layer ("the bought watch" on a pay read; "the muster's wages" on a capture read; a captain doing anything on any read).
3. **SAME WORD, SAME REFERENT inside a composed unit.** The thread rule (the owner, 09-08: adjacent sentences carry a noun forward) carries the REFERENT forward, not the spelling: a unit may not say "the watch" as a body in one sentence and "the watch" as a captured organ in the next without the layer change being stated in words. Where a town holds BOTH (a corruption impairment on a security body AND a pay shortfall on the muster), they are TWO facts with two referents, seated as two units; a fused agent ("the bought watch is paid short") asserts a relation no field computes and is refused unless a relation row exists (S2's clause seat).
4. **Visibility follows the power layer.** A covert corruption impairment or a birth capture is the DM face (the pen line beside the passage, SITTING §T); the player face says what the state's organs would admit, which is the body read alone. A revealed impairment is a public scandal and both faces may name it.
5. **The alias overlap inside the engine is a wiring fact, not a writer's.** "professional city watch" in two buckets, and the WATCH holder kind that is not the watch bucket, go to the register car as a census/holder finding; until cured, a face never uses "the watch" for a pay-gate read (the muster kind does not contain the watch bucket).
