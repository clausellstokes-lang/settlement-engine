## 0. THE FOUR STRUCTURAL FINDINGS THE SURVEY DOES NOT CARRY

These are not entailment verdicts on a single noun; they are wiring facts that change the verdict on whole pool families, so they come first.

**0.1 DS-WAR-2's term pools carry no side arm, and every directional family is authored from the PAYER's chair.** `treatyTermPoolKey(family, complianceState)` (`src/domain/display/stateProse/warFaithStateProse.js:364-370`) takes the family and the compliance word and nothing else; the desk calls it `line('DS-WAR-2', treatyTermPoolKey(term.family, term.complianceState))` (`:851-853`) with no `documentSideOf` read. But the annex authors every `economic`, `relational`, `security`, `territorial`, `political` and `informational` cell from the obligor's side: *"The tribute out of {settlement} goes out on time"* (`docs/content/RECEIPT_POOLS_DOSSIER_STATE.md:3528`), *"{settlement} answers {counterpart}'s muster"* (`:3543`), *"{counterpart}'s garrison keeps the walls of {settlement}"* (`:3573`), *"The installed seat at {settlement}"* (`:3588`), *"The court at {settlement} stays open to {counterpart}'s watching eyes"* (`:3603`), and says so in its own fence: *"each cell below is authored from the page-owner's side"* (`:3524-3525`). On the VICTOR's page the same key fires and the victor is told it pays tribute, answers the muster, hosts the garrison, sits under the installed seat and opens its court. And the side cannot be recovered from the desk's inputs on a sale: the obligor of a sale is the BUYER, i.e. the document's `victorId` (`src/domain/worldPulse/treatyOrientation.js:132-138`), and the obligation axis lives on the raw ledger record, which the document read-model deliberately does not carry (`src/domain/display/treatyDocument.js:271-278`). Every directional term entailment below is therefore at best CONDITIONAL on `termObligationOf(record, term).obligorId === settlementId` (`treatyOrientation.js:256-286`), a reading the desk does not hold.

**0.2 `{counterpart}` on this desk is the MARTIAL counterpart, and DS-WAR-2 reads it as the treaty party.** The fill is `properFill(counterpartName(war))`, which is the occupier's name, else the lone besieger / lone target (`warFaithStateProse.js:795`, `:930-933`; `src/components/new/tabs/WarTab.jsx:478`: `counterpart: lone(status?.besiegedBy) || lone(status?.besiegingTargets)`). No treaty party is ever read into it; the document's own `victorName` / `loserName` are not consulted. So *"The tribute out of {settlement} … what the town owes {counterpart}"* (`DOSSIER_STATE.md:3529`) names whoever is besieging or occupying the town today, which need not be the party to the leading treaty, and on a peaceful treaty-bound town is empty (the `{counterpart}` variants drop, so the fault is a silent drop on peace and a wrong name under war). A treaty with C on a town occupied by B prints B as the treaty party.

**0.3 The `At war` residue fires on a healing scar, an alert posture, an army in transit and a holding of others.** `warStatusPoolKey(status, occupation, otherwiseAtWar)` returns `'statusLabel: At war …'` whenever `otherwiseAtWar` is true and nothing sharper fired (`warFaithStateProse.js:204-206`); the desk passes `martial = !!war?.warBeat` (`:837`, `:843`), and the tab builds `warBeat` as `!!(hasWar || war.unit || war.wars.length > 0)` where `hasWar` includes `war.exhaustionRaw > 0`, `!!war.mobilization` (any posture above `peace`, including `alert`) and `!!war.holdings` (`WarTab.jsx:485-492`, `:523`). A town whose only martial fact is a decaying scar from a war that ENDED (`src/domain/worldPulse/warHomeCosts.js:69`: `EXHAUSTION_DECAY_PER_TICK`), or that is merely `on alert` (`src/domain/display/mobilizationStatus.js:29`), draws *"The town is at war in the way a town can be at war without seeing any"* (`DOSSIER_STATE.md:3405`, the one variant that needs no `{counterpart}`). The surveyor's §5 row names `At war` a residue; it does not carry the consequence, which is that the key asserts a war the engine holds no record of.

**0.4 The `mobilization: COVERT` pool is dark at this tip.** The tab reads `settlementMobilization({ settlementId: sid, worldState })` with the default `includeCovert = false` (`WarTab.jsx:473`, its own comment: `// covert excluded`), which returns `null` for a covert record (`mobilizationStatus.js:116-117`). The desk then reads `!!war?.mobilization?.covert` (`warFaithStateProse.js:846`) as `false`, and `mobilizationPoolKey` never returns the covert key (`:254`). A covertly arming town also draws no ramp sentence, because `ticksToDeploy` is `undefined` off a null reading and `Number(undefined)` is not finite (`:257-258`). The surveyor's finding 2 (the covert flag is never below `war_preparation`) is true of the producer and moot at the desk.
