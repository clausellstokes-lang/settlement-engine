- conjecture / clarity: Everything the place does for itself passes through houses, and nothing passes through a purse: whatever goes toward a trouble goes in by hands nobody asks to account for it.

   on the 28 walled towns only, and the card measures every wall row on this preimage as timber,
   so the material is uniform on the subset the face can draw on [V-06, F1-32].
4. The duratives. Every perfect and durative in this packet runs over the key's own read or a

   it", "nothing hard has ever been asked of it", "nobody here has been asked what the keeping

5. The absences. Each is written as a positive arrangement or as an account's opinion, never as

   nearest house, the ask is made out loud. That is the floor-1-by-inference trap the card
   quantifies at 48 of 72, and it is the trap I wrote hardest against.
