# CANDIDATES — writer 1 (seat: Opus 5) · lens: THE TELLING PARTICULAR
block DS-DEF-2 · pool `Beasts & Monsters: settled, defenses beyond the need`

**THE LENS.** One object with a weight, an owner or a smell, always inside somebody's account
or inside a bare observation, never as description with nobody behind it; and the thing appears
only IN USE or IN DISPUTE. The card's section (9) is where this lens lives on this pool: the
excess is not ruin and not emptiness, it is a kept thing with no occasion, and what the works
are actually used for is domestic. Every candidate below carries one object, and a different
one from its siblings.

**WHAT THE KEY GIVES ME AND NOTHING MORE.** A settled country (`config.monsterThreat`
normalises to `heartland`, FROZEN, writers 0) and a live walls-bucket row standing. The key
consults no force bucket at all. So no candidate asserts who mans the works and none denies it
either: where a body speaks about itself it speaks under its own tag and draws only where its
row resolves. Nothing on the force appears in an untagged, `[stranger]`, `[public]` or
`[archiver · observed]` candidate anywhere in this packet.

**THE SEVEN TRAPS I WROTE AGAINST, named so the refuter can check I held to them.**
1. F1-34, the quiet read as safety. Nowhere does a candidate say nothing comes out of the
   country, that there is no danger, or that the country has never troubled the town. The
   register's line takes the shipped hedge ("not much among them") and stops there.
2. F1-28 / F1-25, the force denied. The exemplar pack's own flagship sentence — *nobody is paid
   to stand on it* — is FALSE on the forty-three city towns of this preimage, where `Garrison`
   and `Professional city watch` are both required. It is not in this packet in any coat, and
   neither is "nobody walks it after dark" or "no one would answer".
3. F1-07, the wall read as a perimeter. No ring, no circuit, no line round the place, no "all
   the way round", no "where the town ends", nothing on the top of the works, nothing "inside"
   them. The elders' candidate says "the households whose doors face the works" and not "every
   household", for exactly this reason.
4. F2-03 / F2-09, the raising narrated. The single most tempting sentence this pool offers is
   why the works went up. It is refused in every candidate: not one says when, against what, or
   at whose order.
5. F2-05, the elapsed course over a LIVE field. `institutions[bucket=walls]` carries thirty-eight
   pulse writers, so no candidate carries a perfect or a durative over the works. Duratives over
   the country would be lawful; I did not need one and took none, so the packet has no durative
   to argue about at all, on either page.
6. F1-08, the gate in both directions. Every bar, every way through and every asking of a
   stranger's business sits inside a `[gate]`-tagged candidate, which draws only where
   `GATE_NAMES` resolves. No untagged candidate assumes an arrival is stopped, and none assumes
   it is not.
7. F4-02, the split purse. One multiplier covers the keeping and any wages together, so no
   candidate has the town choosing the works over the men or paying one before the other. The
   guilds and the hall disagree about WHO IS ASKED, never about WHICH IS PAID FIRST.

**ALSO OBSERVED.** No `{settlement}` in any face (spine 1 carries the pool's one use, ruling 12).
No `{defwork}`, no `{band}`, no `{route}`: this call site mints `{settlement}` and
`{defmaterial}` only, and `{defmaterial}` is undefined across most of the metropolis end, so no
candidate names a material or needs one. No record cited by name anywhere — the shipped rows of
this pool carry no citation and I spend none (F1-24, the corpus-ceiling hazard). No named office
as a subject or an attribution: no mayor, no guard captain, no city watch chief, no priest. No
digit, no em dash, no semicolon, no exclamation mark, no contraction. No `will` or `shall`
before a verb (ruling 33). No compromised candidates: the covert table is closed at
hall · watch · court and this pool is on none of their marked lists.

**THE SPINES TAKE THREE SHAPES** (ruling 29). One: two plain sentences, the bare fact in the
archiver's own hand. Two: the entry form, then a fronted subject. Three: an account, carried by
the stranger. The angle tags are exactly as they stand in the current rows.

## Variant 1 — the works in use

1. `[counterforce]` The country around {settlement} is quiet. The town keeps built work facing that country.

   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A pedlar says the first thing he saw on the works was a line of washing put out to dry.
   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A drover says he was asked three questions about the road he came in on and none about the road he was leaving by. <!-- speaker: a drover on the road -->
   - `[face]` `[gate]` Whoever holds the way through says the bar is dropped at dusk, and that what it turns back is stock straying in off the road.
   - `[face]` `[hall · pair 1 · reinforce]` The hall says the keeping of the works is met out of the common purse like any other charge the town carries.
   - `[face]` `[court · pair 1 · reinforce]` The court says nothing about the keeping comes before it except the question of who owes the work.

   - `[face]` `[tavern]` At the tavern they say the works are where the wood gets stacked and where the dogs get tied.
   - `[face]` `[register]` The register says the ground is dug for the ordinary causes and the country outside is not much among them.

   - `[face]` `[garrison]` The garrison says the boots it walks in wear out on the road and not on the works.

## Variant 2 — what it costs and who carries it

### The public — what everyone saw, and what everyone makes of it (ruling 28)

One per variant. The seeing is a fact and binds under floor 1 in full; the second half is the
town's shared perception and may be mistaken, which on this pool is the best hook the key
offers: the town credits the works with a quiet the works had nothing to do with. Neither half
carries a durative, so nothing here runs over the LIVE walls row, and neither half says
anything about who mans the works — the card's (2b′) list bars the public from claiming it did
not see what a required row seats.

   - `[face]` `[public]` Everyone here sees the works kept and the country quiet, and the town takes the one for the reason the other stays so.  *(variant 1)*
   - `[face]` `[public]` Everyone here knows what the works cost the town, and the town counts the cost cheap against a country it does not have to think about.  *(variant 2)*
   - `[face]` `[public]` Everyone here sees strangers stop on the road to look at the works, and the town takes the stopping for a compliment.  *(variant 3)*

### The archiver's weighing (ruling 22) — one per pair, and every one of them opens

page may close a question here. Each is a conjecture, a plain statement that the matter stands,
or a confidence with its reason drawn from the other source's stake. No source is favoured twice
across the block.

   - `[face]` `[archiver · pair 1 · weigh]` On this the two agree, and there is no cause to doubt them.
   - `[face]` `[archiver · pair 2 · weigh]` It may be that the shelter is the only thing the works give the town that the town would miss.
