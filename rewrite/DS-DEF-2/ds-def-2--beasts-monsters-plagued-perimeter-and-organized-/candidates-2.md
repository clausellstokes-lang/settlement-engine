- **The pairs never cross a tier boundary.** hall+guild and market+guild co-resolve at town; hall+garrison and tavern+stranger at town and city; elders+muster below town; register+gate and muster+register everywhere.

- **`muster` faces carry no wage** (V-09): their cost is the day and the turn. The wage belongs to `garrison` and, through the pay gate, to `watch` (F4-19).
- **The physical particulars offered** are the bar going down when the light goes, the looking-over at the way through, and the ground kept clear. One per rendered page is the selector's to pick.
