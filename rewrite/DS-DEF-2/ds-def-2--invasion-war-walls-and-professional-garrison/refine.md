## 0. THE ONE FINDING THIS ROUND IS BUILT ON — THE POOL'S TIC

The draft is lawful and its two gate measures are cured; what it carries is a POOL-WIDE TIC the gate does not measure and §21.1 names as the refinement round's own target ("zero tics"). **Six of the draft's twelve faces carry the key's conjunction on the same preposition:** `and with them the works` · `with the works, the soldiers` · `The town's muster stands with the works` · `with the works it has soldiers` · `the works stand with them` · `With those works stands the town's force`. One device, half the pool, three variants — the definition of the template the spread arm looks for (§16 item 4: the same rule broken the same way everywhere is the template).

It is also the pool's one live **claim** risk. `stands with the works` / `stands with them` places the force AT the wall, and a position at the wall is a MANNING the fields do not hold — the entailment law's item 1 (a recorded noun licenses what it MEANS, never what it typically HAS), ADDENDUM 13 A.5's struck "the men on the wall", and the skeleton's own refusal of `to hold it` / `who belong to it` / `could be held` on all three shipped rows. The draft's other faces refuse the manning verb and then re-admit the manning by the preposition.

**So the round's single organising move: the conjunction is re-carried, face by face, on twelve devices and never on a spatial preposition. `with` appears in no face of the refined pool (draft: six).** Every replacement is a different construction of equal or greater density — none is the same fact said more plainly (§21.4), and each is named below with its law.
