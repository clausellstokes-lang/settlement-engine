# CANDIDATES · writer 2 (seat: Opus 5)

**Block** DS-DEF-2 · **Pool** `Invasion & War: walls with NO force`
**Lens** THE SOCIAL POSITION (who pays, who is owed, who is resented; the source's stake shows
in what it calls a fact) + THE WITHHELD REASON (the fact set down plainly, the reason not given).
**Voice** the master archiver's hand. Every sentence names its source; the archiver's own record
voice ("the survey finds", "it is not entered", "the survey does not record") carries the engine's
own facts. No first person anywhere. Symmetric attribution: every source gets `says`.

**What is held back on purpose, and why**

- No gate is asserted or denied outside a `[gate]`-tagged face (F1-08 cuts both ways; the tag is
  the seat guard, so a gate face renders only where the way through resolves).
- No material word for the works (V-06 keeps the material bar at the pool grain: palisade towns
  and walled towns draw the same face). No `{defmaterial}` fill used.
- The only absence written is the key's own: no force under arms, nobody under arms to it.
  Never "nobody is paid to stand on it", "nobody walks it", "no one would answer" · a required
  Town watch and the palisade row's own night service deny all three.
- No pay claim on a force in either direction, and no split of the one purse as a CHOICE
  (F4-02). The purse appears only as the engine models it: one heading covering the keeping and
  any wages, and a community baseline of household work the gate never sees.
- No count, date, season, duration, rate or age of fabric. Perfects and duratives appear only
  over the key's own reads ("nobody has been asked", "the keeping goes into no account").
- No named office, and nothing that reads as a predicate on the tier's one Guard Captain: the
  gate faces are about the toll and the discretion, never about who is in charge of the wall.
- No tier word inside a face; the place is "here", "inside the wall", "the households".
- Player faces carry no colon, ellipsis, em dash, digit or margin note.

Sources offered, in the closed vocabulary: `stranger` (universal, resolves everywhere) ·

## Variant 1 · the office's record

1. `[ledger]` The survey finds the wall at {settlement} kept and no force under arms to hold it. Both are entered as found.

### universal

   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A traveller's report of the approach is a wall kept up and nobody under arms to it. He does not say who he asked.
   - `[face]` `[stranger · pair 1 · view]` A traveller says the only work he saw going on outside the wall was the digging of the ground for the dead.

### below town · seated by the household elder, the village elder, the village headman

   - `[face]` `[elders]` The work on the wall is owed by the households, the elders say, and who owes what is remembered rather than written down.
   - `[face]` `[elders · pair 1 · view]` By the elders' account the ground for the dead lies outside the wall by custom, and the custom is older than the works.

### town · the hall, seated by the required Town hall

   - `[face]` `[hall]` The hall says the keeping of the wall and any wages sit under one heading in the accounts. It does not say what else the heading carries.
   - `[face]` `[hall · pair 2 · disagree]` The wall is kept, the hall says, and the hall says the keeping answers the question it was asked.

### town · the guilds, seated by the required craft guilds

   - `[face]` `[guild · pair 2 · disagree]` The guilds say the purse that keeps the wall is one they pay into, and they would have it spent nearer their own stock.
   - `[face]` `[guild]` What the wall keeps is their stock and their stalls, the guilds say, and they pay for the keeping whether or not anyone stands to it.

### town · the tavern, seated by the required taverns and inns

   - `[face]` `[tavern]` At the tavern they say the wall was never the question. The question is who comes when something comes, and the tavern says nobody has been asked.

### town · the watch, seated by the required Town watch

   - `[face]` `[watch]` They keep the walk and are not a force, the watch says, and each of them has a trade to be at in the morning.

### town · the register, seated by the parish churches and the parish burial grounds

   - `[face]` `[register]` The ground for the dead lies outside the wall, the register says, and who walks out with them is not entered anywhere.

### town · the market, seated by the required market square and weekly market

   - `[face]` `[market]` At the market they say the stallholders pay their dues to the hall, and that the wall is what the hall shows them for it.

### town · the court, seated by the required Town hall

   - `[face]` `[court]` The court has heard the keeping of the wall argued as a question of who owes the work, and the court does not record that the question was settled.

### wherever the way through resolves

   - `[face]` `[gate]` Whoever is on the bar takes the toll and keeps the toll book, and the gate says what the toll goes to keep is not his to ask.

## Variant 2 · a stranger's eye

2. `[visitor]` A traveller's account is entered beside the survey's, and both find the wall up and no one under arms to it.

### universal

   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A traveller says the wall was in sight before anybody was, and that he saw no one under arms on it at all.
   - `[face]` `[stranger · pair 2 · aside]` A traveller found a stair up to the walk with somebody's stores stacked on it.

### below town · seated by the household elder, the village elder, the village headman

   - `[face]` `[elders]` A traveller is told by the elders that the wall is the line the stock does not cross, and told it first.

### town · the hall, seated by the required Town hall

   - `[face]` `[hall]` Ask at the hall what the works cost and the hall reads from the accounts, and it reads the keeping only.
   - `[face]` `[hall]` At the hall the answer is that the wall is kept, and that the keeping is what the hall is answerable for.

### town · the tavern, seated by the required taverns and inns

   - `[face]` `[tavern · pair 1 · disagree]` At the tavern they say a traveller is safer inside the wall than outside it, and they say it to travellers.

### town · the watch, seated by the required Town watch

   - `[face]` `[watch · pair 1 · disagree]` To the watch the wall makes little difference to a traveller and a good deal to the walk they keep.

### town · the guilds, seated by the required craft guilds

   - `[face]` `[guild]` A traveller at the stalls is told by a guild's factor what the stock is worth, and not what keeps it.

### town · the register, seated by the parish churches and the parish burial grounds

   - `[face]` `[register]` A traveller who asks the register why the ground for the dead lies outside the wall is given the custom and not the reason.

### town · the market, seated by the required market square and weekly market

   - `[face]` `[market]` At the market they say the country people come in with the goods and go out before dark, and nobody at the market says what they are to do if they cannot.

### town · the court, seated by the required Town hall

   - `[face]` `[court]` The court says a stranger's complaint about the works would be heard the same as anyone's, and the court adds that it has not had one.

### wherever the way through resolves

   - `[face]` `[gate · pair 2 · aside]` Whoever holds the way through says the country people are to come inside when something comes, and that nobody has said who opens to them.
   - `[face]` `[gate]` Whoever is on the bar says he is asked for the toll and never for anything else.

## Variant 3 · the town's own talk

3. `[street]` The survey finds the wall kept and no force under arms. It is said here that the keeping falls to the households, and the survey does not record who asked them.

### universal

   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A traveller says he heard about the keeping of the wall before he heard about anything else here.
   - `[face]` `[stranger]` What a traveller hears here is the wall spoken of as work, and never as a defence.

### below town · seated by the household elder, the village elder, the village headman

   - `[face]` `[elders]` The elders say the work the households put into the wall is counted nowhere, and nobody here disputes that it is put in.
   - `[face]` `[elders]` The wall is a work owed the way the road is owed, the elders say, and who is behind on it is known here without being said.

### town · the hall, seated by the required Town hall

   - `[face]` `[hall · pair 1 · disagree]` The hall says the works are kept and the accounts show the keeping, and the hall says the rest is not a matter for the accounts.
   - `[face]` `[hall]` At the hall the works are entered as kept, and the hall says whoever wants more than that entered may come and ask.

### town · the watch, seated by the required Town watch

   - `[face]` `[watch · pair 1 · disagree]` The watch says the keeping the hall enters is not the same thing as the walk they keep, and that the difference falls on them.
   - `[face]` `[watch]` They are told the wall is kept and are not told by whom, the watch says.

### town · the tavern, seated by the required taverns and inns

   - `[face]` `[tavern]` At the tavern they name the households that do the keeping and the ones that do not, and they name them in front of both.

### town · the guilds, seated by the required craft guilds

   - `[face]` `[guild]` The guilds say the keeping comes off the same purse their dues go into, and they say it where the hall can hear.

### town · the register, seated by the parish churches and the parish burial grounds

   - `[face]` `[register · pair 2 · aside]` The register says the households keep a wall they will each be carried out past, and that it keeps the ground on the far side of it.

### town · the market, seated by the required market square and weekly market

   - `[face]` `[market · pair 2 · aside]` At the market they say the country people come in on market day and go out with the light, and the market says no one has ever told them to stay.

### town · the court, seated by the required Town hall

   - `[face]` `[court]` The court has had the keeping of the wall before it as a matter between households, and the court says it hears it as a boundary and not as a defence.

### wherever the way through resolves

   - `[face]` `[gate]` Whoever is on the bar says the toll is taken here and spent elsewhere, and that he has never been told where.

## APPENDIX · DM notebook candidates (NOT face rows)

Deliberately not written in the face syntax: the `dm-only` mark is the block's and was not on my
page, and no player face may carry a margin note. These are offered as the archiver's working
notes for whichever of the three variants the selector places them under, in five different
shades. Third person, one or two sentences, no digit, no sum, no named office; each names what it
circles and points toward the recorded fact rather than away from it.

- *(conjecture)* The keeping is entered and the work is not… somebody puts the hours in, the accounts carry the charge, and the two have never been set beside each other. Whose hours they are is worth asking before they stop.
- *(unsure)* The households do the keeping and nobody has asked them to; that is either custom or an arrangement that was made once and never written. If it was made, then with whom.
- *(accusatory, still a note)* Whoever keeps the toll book takes from people who pass and hands on what he says he took. The wall he takes it for has no one under arms to it, and he has never been asked to account for the difference between those two facts.
- *(hearsay)* It is said inside the wall that the country people are to come in when something comes. Nobody at the hall says it, and nobody says who would open to them.
- *(unsure, pointing at the roster)* The works are kept and no force stands to them. Every explanation offered here is about money, and none of them explains why nobody has been enrolled, which is not a money question at all.
- *(conjecture, the small object)* There is a bracket where a bell or a horn would hang and nothing hanging in it. The keeping covers the wall, so the bracket is somebody's too, and nobody has claimed it.
