### 2.2 — v1 f9 (market): is "everybody else" a magnitude, or a universal quantifier?

**A universal quantifier, and the question is settled by this pool's own record, not by my reading.**

sum, a price, a volume, a distance, a headcount…"* and every exemplar it prints is a cardinality or a
proportion. *Everybody else* is neither: it quantifies over the complement of a set the same sentence
defines and carries **no information about the size of that set**. The magnitude forms of this
sentence would be *most people come in with a basket* (a share) or *a handful come in with a basket*
(a count); neither is what is written.

**And the desk has already passed eight of these — this is not a new licence.** Measured over the
pool as it stood BEFORE the cure: eight of twenty-seven faces already turned on a universal
