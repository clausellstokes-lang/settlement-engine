## F. THE SHARED-SEAT PASS, AND THE JUDGMENT CALLS

Both packets were written at Opus 5, which is my own seat. Per the brief I read them as my own hand and went at the tells harder, not softer. What that pass found:

1. **A GENDERED PRONOUN IN THREE OF PACKET 2'S STRANGER FACES** (*he could not find out*, *he was told here… telling him*, *readier to tell him*) and in one of its lord's-man candidates. Under ruling 25 the page prints a ROLE drawn by seed from the town's own rows — a pedlar, a traveller, dock workers — and the face must be written so that ANY role of that source could stand in it. `he` breaks on the first role the roll draws that is not a man. It is a systematic tic of one seat, not a slip, and it cost that packet the strongest stranger line in the pool (*could not find out who was out of pocket for it*). Recorded so the writers' next brief can bar it in a clause.
2. **A FLOOR-1 RISK I HAD ALREADY SELECTED AND THEN STRUCK.** Packet 1's variant-2 elders face — *The elders say the keeping falls to the houses that stand behind it. A field stands unworked while the hands that work it are gone.* — was my chosen BILL and my chosen two-sentence stop. *The houses that stand behind it* reads as houses behind a wall; walls stand on 103 of 260 and their denial is barred on the other 157 [F1-07, F1-25]. Struck. This is the cost of a shared seat and the reason a second judgment is applied at all.
3. **A PAIR DROPPED FOR AN ADJACENCY ITS WRITER COULD NOT SEE FROM INSIDE ONE HALF.** Packet 2's variant-2 `view` pair renders whole: *…a house gives what it can toward the burying and that nobody counts it. Those who would turn out say a house gives a body or a tool…*. *Toward the burying* followed by *gives a body* is a black-comic misread at the exact moment a game master is scanning. The pair tag is dropped and BOTH halves are kept as lone faces, three rows apart, where they never co-render and both read clean. That leaves variant 2 without a pair, which is lawful — a pair is not owed per variant — and it is why the pool carries two pairs rather than three.
4. **A SIGNATURE WORD CAUGHT ACROSS TWO PACKETS.** *Never promised* appears on the muster in packet 2's variant 1 and packet 1's variant 2. A source told apart by a repeated word rather than by what it notices is on the veto list, so only the clearer of the two is kept (variant 1). That refusal is what killed packet 1's variant-2 disagree pair, whose muster half carried the phrase.
5. **THE REGISTER'S FOUR BURIAL-IS-ALWAYS-MET LINES.** Packet 2 offered the register the same claim four times in different wording. Three register faces are kept and only two of them make that claim, from two different angles (nobody turned away · never put off); the third is switched to the uncounted giving so the source is told apart by what it notices.
6. **THE COURT AND THE WATCH, honestly.** The court is seated on 3 of 260 and speaks ONCE in this pool, not three times: packet 2's other two court lines paraphrase the kept one, and packet 1's single court line (*nothing about the keeping has ever been brought before it*) sits in tension with it, since quarrels about who owed what toward the work ARE about the keeping. A court town that draws variant 2 or 3 hears no court, and that is organic thinness and not a defect. THE ONE SEATED SOURCE WITH NO CANDIDATE WORTH THE PAGE IN A VARIANT IS THE WATCH AT VARIANT 3: packet 1's offer there spends the variant's physical particular on a lantern for 3 towns when the stranger has earned it for 260, and packet 2's (*nobody has ever had to write it down*) repeats a thesis the gate and the elders already carry in that same variant.

### THE RATIONS, AND ONE READING RECORDED FOR VETO

- **ONE PHYSICAL PARTICULAR — read as one per VARIANT, not one per pool.** My brief's letter says per pool. A render draws ONE variant's spine and ONE of its units, so a particular in variant 1 can never appear on a page with variant 3's; one per variant is exactly what delivers the exemplar's *one per rendered page*, and the strict per-pool reading would leave two of three variants with no concrete thing at all, which is the collapse the first refuter measured as DULL. So the pool carries three and every page carries one: the pails at the water (v1, seen), the bar heard from the far houses (v2, heard), what is put by in cellars and lofts and under the floor (v3, and that one is the card's own W-07 condition rather than invented furniture). **A chair who means the letter can strike two lines and the pool stays lawful.**
- **ONE CONSEQUENCE FACE PER VARIANT — the pool carries none.** Both canonical BILLs offered (packet 1's variant-1 muster, packet 1's variant-2 elders) were refused, the first for clarity and the second for the wall it implies. What is left lands costs inside a clause rather than in a second sentence, which is thinner than the class allows. Recorded because a pool with no bill is a real loss, not an oversight.
- **TWO WEIGHINGS, ONE OBSERVED FACE** — both rations met exactly (§E1, §E3).
