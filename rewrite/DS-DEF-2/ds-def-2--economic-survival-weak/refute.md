  measured, **56 of 260 towns carry a live one** (card lines 828-830).
- **THE SENTENCE THAT DENIES IT:** DS-DEF-8 `Defense › Active military status`, annex line 3513,
  `SECTION-TARGET: defense`, `override active (generic framing)` — its `[ledger]` row says there is
  a crisis on the town and the arrangements have been rebuilt around it; its `[street]` row says
  everything is arranged for the one thing now.
- **WHICH IS THE RECORD:** the stress. It is a stored field with a corpus block of its own on the
  same tab; the spine is prose. This is not the frozen/live seam and not engine prose, so it is a
  contradiction and not a wiring row.
- **WHY IT IS THE HEAVIEST ROW IN THE PACKET:** a spine co-renders with every face of its variant,
  so the whole of variant 3 — eight lawful faces — renders under a false frame on those towns.
- **THE CURE, and it keeps everything the spine is for.** The untested thing is not the EVENT; it
  is the WRITING DOWN, and the writing down is positively measured: `holdersOf` returns `office` 0,
  `tradition` 0, `muster` 0, and no kind reaches a tenth of the preimage (card lines 864-875). A
  crisis on the page cannot make a written rule appear. So move the absence from the calling-on to
  the settling: say that what it would cost, and whose it would be, has never been settled between
  the houses — and drop "has never been called on" entirely. The §9 hook survives intact.

## THE THREE WITHHELD, AND WHY EACH IS WITHHELD RATHER THAN FAILED

1. **Variant 1's spine, "The households make up the difference."** `threatAssessment.js:173` fires
   on 133 of 260 and says the capacity to respond is quickly overwhelmed. Read plainly, "make up
   the difference" says the gap is closed, which that sentence denies. Withheld because the clause
   can also be read as *who bears the cost* rather than *that the cost is met*, and on that reading
   it is exactly the card's own §9 ("the people who would answer a crisis are the able-bodied of
   the place"). The burden is mine and the reading is not forced. **Cure, one clause:** name the
   bearer without claiming sufficiency — the cost falls on the households, or the households carry
   what is left. That reading is denied by neither arm of the 25-point split.

2. **Variant 3, face 1, the stranger's cellars and lofts.** The card's §9 supplies this image
   almost word for word and F1-09 asks for the store and not the building — which this face obeys.
   But it also LOCATES the whole of what is put by, in places that exclude a granary, and 3 towns
   of the preimage carry a REQUIRED granary row with `Grain storage` at p 1. Withheld because the
   card's own flavour section wrote the sentence. **Cure, one word:** make the subject a household's
   store rather than the town's — what a house puts by here — which removes the exhaustiveness at
   no cost to the image and keeps "a house shows its own and never another's" doing its work.

3. **Variant 2, face 6, the muster.** Not a floor. The mechanical voice rules bar a spine phrase
   echoed in any face of that variant, and the spine of variant 2 carries "turns out" and "would
   turn out" while this face opens "Those who would turn out say". They co-render. **Cure:** this
   is the one variant where the muster's own word is spent by the spine, so the face should reach
   for the levy's own noun instead of the spine's verb.

## CRAFT — AT THE POOL GRAIN

### VERDICT: **DULL**. Speakers: 10. The collapse is the SENTENCE, not the sources.

The pool is not a camera. Ten sources speak, each notices something only it would notice, the
disagree pair genuinely disagrees, and the pool is far richer than the three flat abstractions it
replaces (`f2da5a3ee` lines 124-126 are a ledger sentence, a street sentence and an unfolding
sentence with no face under any of them and four clauses that are now false). Nothing here is a
regression. It is DULL on one axis and that axis is load-bearing:

**1. ONE CONSTRUCTION, SIXTEEN TIMES.** The pool's sentence is: *[source] says [a thing], and (that)
[nobody / never / not] [the other thing]*. Counted: eight faces hinge on the exact string "and that"
(V1 F1, F2, F4, F7 · V2 F1, F4, F6 · V3 F5) and ten more on a bare coordinating "and" carrying the
same withheld second beat. **Fifteen of twenty-seven faces close on a negation** — nobody, never,
not, cannot — and the spine of variant 3 closes on two more. Nine of the ten faces of variant 1 are
that one shape end to end. The sources are told apart by what they notice, which is right, and then
say it in the same breath, which is the collapse.

**2. NOT ONE FACE IN THE POOL LANDS ON A CONCRETE NOUN.** Twenty-seven of twenty-seven end on a
pronoun, a particle, a participle or an abstract: *wanted to · anything · to use · them · take it ·
the rest · in it · after · are not · the work · for it · the others · done · counts it · put it
there · counts that · hidden · afford · anything else · another's · here · its own · why they do
not · objected · in writing · everyone else · the rest*. The selector's own §C claims two of these
land on a strong noun; measured against the text, neither does. A pool whose every sentence ends on
a pronoun reads as one voice trailing off, whatever the attribution at the front says.

**3. THE SAME FEW NOUNS.** "house" or "houses" carries twelve of twenty-seven faces. "turn out"
carries four faces and the spine of variant 2 twice. "counts" appears in two faces of variant 2 in
mirrored form (nobody counts it · everybody counts that) — the selector separated them by three
rows on a belief that they never co-render, which is right about the render and does not help the
chair reading the pool as a block.

**4. WHAT IS NOT WRONG, and should not be cured by making it worse.** The stakes are real and in
dispute — who bears the turn, who is asked, who is owed nothing because nothing was promised. The
sources' interests genuinely diverge (the muster's grievance, the tavern's, the market's contrast,
the court's narrow window). Ruling 34's two non-plain closes are present. The archiver's own bare
hand speaks once. The selector's judgment record in NOTES §F is unusually honest and its three
self-caught defects are correctly caught. **The cure is not more faces; it is a third of the
existing ones re-landed.** Take roughly nine faces spread across the three variants, and have them
close on the concrete noun the sentence is actually about — the spears, the pails, the bar, the
field, the stall, the ground — with the withheld beat carried by the ORDER rather than by a final
"nobody". The negation is the pool's signature and a signature repeated sixteen times is wallpaper.
