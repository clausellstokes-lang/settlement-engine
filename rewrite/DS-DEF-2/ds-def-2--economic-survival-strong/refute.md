THE COLLAPSE, NAMED ANYWAY (three things for the cure round, none of them fatal):
1. **The axis is narrow.** About two rows in three land on the wage-against-the-granary opposition. The card
   licenses this — §(9) calls it the pool's own hook — but it means the four faces an unweighted roll draws in
   a variant can easily be four tellings of one grievance. V2 is the tightest: faces 4, 7, 8 and 9 all land on
   who is owed. Worth spreading one of them onto the register's or the gate's subject in the cure round.
2. **Spine 3 repeats spine 1 verbatim.** "fed and supplied through a crisis" stands in both, seven words
   unchanged. The SHAPES differ (bare fact · public perception · two sources joined), which is what ruling 29
   asks, so this is not a floor and not a DULL verdict — but a reader who draws spine 1 and spine 3 across two
   sessions reads the same clause twice. The selector flagged it; I confirm it, and the selector's own cure
   (re-cut the hall's half onto the purse rather than the provision) leaves the tavern's half untouched.
3. **The V2 disagree pair does not meet.** The hall answers on amount ("no more than the trades can carry"),
   the guilds on agency ("the carrying is theirs and the asking is not"). They do not agree, so it is not a
   "disagree that agrees" — but they are not rival accounts of ONE fact either, and that costs the weighing
   above them its object: "the guilds' account is the likelier" is likelier than nothing in particular. Cure:
   move the hall's half onto agency ("the asking is the hall's to make and the hall makes it fairly"), or move
   the weighing off "likelier" onto the dispute form.
