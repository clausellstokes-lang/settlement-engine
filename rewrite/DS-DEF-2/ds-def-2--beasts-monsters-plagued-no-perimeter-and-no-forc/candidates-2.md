WHAT THE CARD FIXED, AND WHAT IT COST THIS PACKET
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The key fixes plagued country (MONSTERS, never disease), the walls bucket empty and both force
buckets empty. So: no wall, line, palisade, earthwork or citadel; no garrison, barracks or
militia; and — because `hasGates` falls with the walls bucket — NO GATE, NO BAR, NO TOLL BOOK
AND NO CHECKPOINT, in either direction, asserted or denied. No face below names one.

The preimage is thorp · hamlet · village · town, plus the ruin-only city. That set three fences
this packet is written inside, and they are the reason several obvious sentences are absent:

  · FLOOR 1 BY INFERENCE. At town the catalogue requires a `Town watch` and a `Town hall`; at
    village and above the engine emits a mandatory Guard Captain and a Mayor. So no candidate
    here says nobody is set up to answer, nobody keeps any kind of guard, or nobody is paid.
    The absence is written ONLY as the absence of a standing force of the town's own — which is
    also what keeps the lines true on the ruined city, where a `Garrison` row and a `City walls`
    row still print. Nothing below denies a history or a fabric.
  · THE SAME-PAGE `Very Safe` TRAP. `safetyProfile.js:276` can print "Visitors can move freely
    at all hours" on this very key. Every face built on a cowed town, a shut-up town or a town
    that stops moving after dark is therefore absent from this packet by design.
  · NO NAMED RECORD. The three shipped spines cite none, so the citation rule bars one outright
    here. Every account attributes through a ROLE and never through a book, a roll or a
    register. `the purse` is money and not a record; it is the only thing counted anywhere here.

TAGS USED, AND WHY THESE. The source vocabulary is closed, and on this pool `muster`, `garrison`
and `gate` are unseatable. What is left, with its seat: `stranger` (every tier, untagged draws
the same) · `elders` (thorp, hamlet, village) · `register` (village and above) · `hall`,
`watch`, `tavern`, `guild`, `market`, `court` (town and above; `court` rides `hasCourtSystem`,
which a required `Town hall` seats). Pairs are only ever built from two sources that CO-RESOLVE
on the same town — `elders` with `register` at village, the town-and-above sources with each
other — since a drawn pair renders whole.

FOUR UNIVERSAL SOURCES THE CARD SEATS AND THE CLOSED VOCABULARY HAS NO TAG FOR — whoever says
the words over the dead (below village), the households, the water, the mill. Their material is
not lost: it is carried by `elders` below town, by `register` at village and above, and by the
