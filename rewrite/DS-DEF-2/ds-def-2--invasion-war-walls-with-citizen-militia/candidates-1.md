## THE FENCES I WROTE AGAINST (card section 8) — so the refuter can check my intent, not guess it

- **No wage, pay, arrears or coin predicated of the muster** (V-09; the row reads "Part-time soldiers with their own tools and no pay"). The grievance on this pool is THE TURN, THE TIME and THE TOOLS. Where a payment appears in my candidates it is a TRADE being paid, or the recorded absence stated plainly, never the militia being owed.
- **No stone, masonry, parapet, wall-walk, stair, battlement, tower or gatehouse** (V-06, F1-32) — the row is "a place that cannot afford stone". My nouns are the page's own: *palisade*, *bank*, *the perimeter*, *the line*, *the gap*, *the way through*. I do not predicate timber of it either, that being half a disjunction the record leaves open, and I have used no word that seats a walk, a height or an inside-and-outside.
- **No named record anywhere** — no roll, no register, no toll book, no accounts, no returns, no "the books". Every record-holder kind resolves to NOBODY on 79/79 and the muster keeps no list of itself. I have spent that absence deliberately, as an absence.
- **THE NIGHT IS NOT SETTLED IN EITHER DIRECTION.** `Night watch` instantiates on 29 of 79. **No candidate in this packet asserts or denies a night patrol, and none is set at night at all.** I cut two good faces for this.
- **No armoury, issued arms, town store or rack by the gap** — the tools are the militia's own, which is the lens's whole opening here.
- **No tier word.** F1-31: hamlet 36 and village 43 draw the same face, so it is "the place", "here", "the houses".
- **No north-European furniture** — eleven culture profiles fire on this preimage. No thatch, no churchyard, no market green, no snow, no brazier, no bell, no horn.
- **No magnitude, count, date, season, duration, rate, age, trend or history.** No headcount of the turnout, no length of the perimeter, and no narration of the bank being raised (F2-03, the single most tempting sentence available here).
- **Floor 3, this pool's likeliest failure:** Guard Captain is mandatory on all 43 villages, Elder and Parish Priest on all 36 hamlets. So `elders` is ALWAYS PLURAL AS A CLASS; nothing here is "whoever calls the muster out", "the one who says where they stand" or "whoever decides who is let through"; where the gap speaks it is a turn-holder or a plural, never a decider.
- **Ruling 35:** every candidate below needs BOTH the perimeter AND the unpaid citizen force to be true. A face about a bare bank belongs to `walls with NO force`; a face about a turnout with nothing to stand behind belongs to `militia only`.
- **The lawful band is the engine's own sentence and nothing wider:** turnout in a crisis, absence in the routine. Nothing here claims reliable patrol and nothing here claims nobody answers.
- **No forecast** (ruling 33): no *will*, no *shall*. The conditionals are deliberate.
- Mechanical: no em dash, no exclamation mark, no digit, no semicolon, no contraction, no `{settlement}` anywhere in this packet.

## WHAT I CUT, AND WHY (so the refuter does not have to reconstruct it)

- Four faces set at night, or turning on who walks the perimeter after dark. `Night watch` fires on 29 of 79: asserting invents it on fifty towns and denying it contradicts twenty-nine, and `safetyDesc` on 54 towns independently says the quiet corners after dark carry risk. The night is this pool's best material and the card refuses it both ways, so I left it out rather than hedge it into mush.
- Every wage face. The walled-garrison shape — the wage comes last and the work comes anyway — is the most natural sentence on this subject and is a floor-1 contradiction here.
- A toll, a book at the gap, a coin changing hands. Nothing is collected at the way through on any of the 79.
- A muster roll, twice. The militia stands on every town of this pool and still keeps no list of itself, which is sharper than the garrison pool's version of the same absence, so I spent it as an absence.
- A bell, a horn and a brazier. None is recorded, and two of the three are north-European furniture on a preimage where eleven culture profiles fire.
- "The stair to the walk", "the parapet", "along the wall-walk". This fabric has no walk and nothing a man stands on top of.
- Anything predicating timber of the fabric. The row is one row covering a palisade OR an earthen bank, so *stakes* and *posts* assert half a disjunction; *the bank*, *the line* and *the gap* do not.
