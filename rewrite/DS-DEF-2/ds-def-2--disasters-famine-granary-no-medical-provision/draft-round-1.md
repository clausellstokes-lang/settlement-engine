1. `[ledger]` {settlement} has a grain store against a failed harvest and nothing at all against disease. Hunger has an address in this town and sickness has none.
   - `[face]` Grain at {settlement} is held in common and the weighing is somebody's charge. A fever belongs to the household it lands in, and no provision of the town's would lift it off them.
   - `[face]` For its grain {settlement} has a store. For the sick it has neither house nor office, and what stands in their place is a neighbour.
   - `[face]` Nothing on the roster at {settlement} answers a sickness. The grain does better, with a building of its own and somebody answerable for the key to it.
2. `[unfolding]` The town's answer to hunger is a building. Its answer to a fever is the house the fever is in, and whoever else is under that roof.
   - `[face]` A sickness that comes into this town finds no building meant for it, and no door to knock at but a private one. Grain that comes in finds a building.
   - `[face]` The dead in this town have ground of their own, and the grain has a roof of its own. Between those provisions, the sick have the house they are in.
   - `[face]` A sick person in this town is a household matter and goes on being one. The town keeps nothing that would come to the door.
3. `[street]` At {settlement} grain is carried to a store. A sick person is carried nowhere, and stays in the room they are in.
   - `[face]` A traveller who takes a fever at {settlement} becomes a householder's business. No office in the town would take that business off the householder.
   - `[face]` Sacks go up into the store at {settlement}. A fever goes into a house and stays there, and the neighbours keep a distance that goes by no name.

### The bars this draft was written against, and how each is met

- **Duration for the store (the pool's one true contradiction, in all three shipped rows).** Gone entirely. No face
  says how long anything lasts. Where the store's purpose is named it is named as a KIND — *against a failed
  harvest*, *the answer to hunger* — never as a span. `foodSecurity.storageMonths` (five months at town,
  `foodGenerator.js:161-165`) is never contradicted because it is never approached.
- **The live badge (§0.7.2).** Every face reports what the town HAS ARRANGED, never how the town is DOING. None
  says the town is provided for, safe, fed, past the worst or in no danger, so none is falsified by a re-graded
  `scores.disaster`, by a famine banner, or by the *relief funding at NN%* note one click below.
- **The store's condition.** No face calls the store full, sound, kept, dry, guarded, locked or well run. The flag
  is a name match and is not ruin-filtered. The faces use only the institution row's own stated function (grain
  held in common, grain carried to it, sacks going up into it) and never its condition. Declared as a deliberate
  reading: the engine's impairment model is *impaired, never inoperable*, so function is treated as the row's own
  description and condition is left alone.
- **The neighbour surfaces.** No face reaches for the supply route, the road, the port, isolation or endurance in
  days — that is DS-DEF-6's `Logistics & Supply`, printing on the same tab. Nothing here answers DS-GEN-17's
  `PROVISIONED` line either; that contradiction is that block's and is left to it.
- **Faith.** Three faces state the absence of a house or an office. None says the town has no faith, no god and
  nobody who prays. `config.faithProfile.piety` and the deity axes live outside the institution roster, and the
  deity doctrine is a wall.
- **Culture.** No thatch, churchyard, market green, hearth-smoke or snow; no *parish*; no lane, square, market day,
  crowd or district. The furniture is a door, a room, water, sacks, a key, ground and a family, which the twelve
  culture profiles all carry.
- **Magnitude, tense, mutability.** No digit, no percent, no spelled count (*one*, *two*, *both* as a tally, *the
  two* were all removed in drafting), no size, no age, no frequency, no rate welded to the habitual. No perfect,
  no durative, no *still*, no *since*, no *no longer*, no comparison against an earlier state. Nothing was built,
  chosen, neglected or let go: the block's FENCE — presence is a STANDING fact with no recorded history — holds in
  all twelve.
- **Scope.** No named person, family, lane, inn or road. No Guard Captain and no second officer of any singular
  tier office. Every person is unnamed and plural or indefinite: somebody, a neighbour, a traveller, a
  householder, the neighbours, a family, whoever else is under that roof.
- **The walls.** No em dash, no exclamation mark, no digit, no `which`-clause, no question, no second person, no
  future indicative. Every face is one or two sentences.
