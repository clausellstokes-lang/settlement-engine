LENS: **THE SOCIAL POSITION** (who pays, who is owed, who is resented; the source's stake shows in
what it calls a fact) and **THE WITHHELD REASON** (a fact set down plainly and the reason not given).

THE POOL'S OWN SUBJECT, as the card puts it: not the wall and not the wage, but **whether anything is
owed** — what the calm is worth, what it would cost to keep something against it, and who would be the
first to know if it stopped. That is this lens' ground exactly, so every face below turns on a claim
somebody does or does not have on somebody else, and on a reason nobody gives.

## THE WRITER'S OWN SCAN

**The words that are not in this packet, and why.** No wall, line, perimeter, ring, rampart, palisade,
bank, ditch or earthwork, in either direction beyond the card's own licensed denial (F1-07). No gate,
bar, checkpoint, toll-bar or closing at dusk, and no denial of one (F1-08, correction (b)). No garrison
(F1-02), no militia (F1-03, F1-26). **No "the watch", "the guard", "no guard", "nobody set up to
respond" or "nothing would justify a watch" in any face whose source draws below town** — the assertion
is false on the hundred and two and the denial is false on the fourteen, which is this sitting's own
named failure mode and the defect the shipped spine committed. The word "watch" appears only inside
`[watch]`-tagged faces, which resolve at town, where the row is required; **nobody is ever said to be
"under arms" or "not under arms"**, since section (2b) files the required Town watch under arms in the
engine's own reading. The safe denial word is **soldier**, and it is the only body word denied anywhere
above. No named record of any kind — no roll, no toll book, no register cited as a record, no accounts,
never "the books" (correction (d), F1-24, and the provenance rule, which is stricter here than the
floors). No named office as a face's subject and none as an attribution: no mayor, no guard captain,
no parish priest, no elder in the singular (F3-06, F1-22).

**Floor 2.** No rate anywhere — no "most nights", "seldom", "every spring", "at any hour", "more often
than not"; the shipped `[visitor]` spine's "at any hour" is gone. No magnitude, headcount or distance;
"the households nobody passes on the way in" and "out past the last houses" are positions, not measures.
No date, duration, age of fabric or trend. No raising and no founding narrated, and no event the record
did not run. The duratives that are here — *has ever been drawn round*, *has never been owed*, *has
never been asked*, *has never been brought*, *has never had him called out*, *has been seen* — all run
over the key's own reads (`config.monsterThreat`, FROZEN; the absence of the perimeter; the absence of
the force) or over a frozen field, which is what ruling 11 licenses. None runs over the live roster:
nothing here says the mill has ground, the ground has been kept, or the doors have stood.

**Floor 1's sharpest trap on this desk, F1-107.** No face settles the town's safety, its order or its
ease in either direction. Nothing says it is safe, quiet, peaceful or untroubled; nothing says the
country sends nothing or that nothing threatens. Every face says only what a source makes of the country
outside, or what is or is not asked of somebody — which is what this key actually fixes. The one
perception that comes near it is the public's, where being mistaken is the licensed half of the form.

**Ruling 35, the sibling rungs.** Each face is keyed against `settled, defenses beyond the need` (there
is nothing to be over-provided with here) and against `plagued, NO perimeter and NO force` (on that rung
a carter is allowed something for the coming, the sexton is called out, the stranger's news is wanted,
and a household is asked). The test applied to every line was: would a source on the plagued rung say
this? Where the answer was yes, the line was recut.

**Shape and variety.** The three spines take three shapes: the bare fact subject-first with a short
second sentence; the place-fronted clause with the obligation after it; two short sentences on a person.
`{settlement}` appears in the first spine and nowhere else in the pool. Attribution verbs are spread —
*says*, *holds*, *the view is*, *by the elders' account*, *at the tavern they say*, *at the tavern a
stranger is asked* — with no verb three times running inside a variant. The physical particulars are
carried by V3's drover (the fields at the doors), V2's observed note (the tools left standing) and V2's
market face (the rain), one per rendered page at most. The bill is landed on rows the roster actually
holds: the stall charge on a stallholder, the common charge on a household, the apprentice's hours on
the trade. No face carries a which-clause closer, a summarising close, an antithesis pair, a
reassurance, a maxim or a clever last beat; no semicolon, no em dash, no exclamation mark, no digit, no
contraction; no `will` or `shall`; no `{settlement}` inside a face; and no face refers to the document
that carries it.

**Pairs.** Six offered, each two faces of one variant, one kind, two different sources, the same N, and
every half a single sentence, so none of them is a two-sentence face. Every pair's two sources co-resolve
on the same tier: pairs 1, 3, 5 and 6 at town; pair 2 below town, where the stranger and the elders both
draw; pair 4 at village, the one tier where the register and the elders both resolve. All twelve halves
are marked `joinable` in their comments and read after *though*, *but* or *and*.
