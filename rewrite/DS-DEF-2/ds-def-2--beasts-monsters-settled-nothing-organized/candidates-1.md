# CANDIDATES — writer 1 (seat: Opus 5) · block DS-DEF-2 · pool `Beasts & Monsters: settled, nothing organized`

**LENS: THE TELLING PARTICULAR.** One object with a weight, an owner or a smell, always inside
somebody's account or the archiver's own observation, and the thing appears only in use or in
dispute. What a SOURCE NOTICES about the thing is the face.

**THE VOICE:** the master archiver's hand. A fact the engine holds stands bare; every account names
its source through a role; the attribution is symmetric; plain words, restrained, the fact and then
the stop.

**WHAT THIS WRITER READ:** the exemplar pack, this pool's card, the pool's shipped and current rows.
Nothing else.

**STANDING SCANS APPLIED TO EVERY LINE BELOW.** No wall, line, perimeter, ring, rampart, palisade,
bank, ditch or earthwork, and none denied but the line itself (the card's own blessing). No gate,
bar, checkpoint, toll-bar or closing at dusk IN EITHER DIRECTION. No garrison, no militia, no
mercenary company, no muster as a body, no crown's assessor, no whoever-holds-the-way-through. No
named record of any kind — no roll, no toll book, no register-as-a-record, no accounts, no books
(correction (d): the provenance rule is stricter here than the floors). No tier word (thorp, hamlet,
village, town, city) anywhere in a spine or a face — the place is *the place*, *here*, *the houses*,
*the households*. No `{settlement}` and no `{defwork}` anywhere, in a spine or a face (this key
fixes no standing wall, so the slot is not offered). No digit, no em dash, no exclamation mark, no
semicolon, no contraction. No *will* and no *shall*. No rate, count, date, duration, distance,
magnitude, age of fabric, trend, raising, founding or narrated event. No named office as a speaker
or a subject (no mayor, no captain, no elder in the singular, no priest — the register speaks as the
sexton). No verdict on the town's safety, order or ease as a FACT in either direction: ease appears
only as what a source makes of the country outside, or as the public's perception, which may be
mistaken. The `compromised` tag is REFUSED on this pool (section (2c)) and no candidate carries it.

**THE DURATIVE, USED DELIBERATELY** (ruling 11): elapsed course runs only over the key's own reads —
`config.monsterThreat` and the two absences — and over the frozen fields, never over the live roster.

## WHAT THIS WRITER DID NOT OFFER, AND WHY

- **No `compromised` candidate.** Section (2c) prints this pool as marked by no covert source; the
  tag is refused here and a reassurance on any face would be the plain tell.
- **No `gate`, `garrison` or `muster` face.** Correction (b) and the force-word map: none of the
  three is a speaker anywhere on this preimage.
- **No register face below village.** Correction (c): a burial ground is not a house of the faith,
  and `access to` is a walk rather than a register.
- **No named record in any attribution.** Correction (d): the shipped spines cite none, so the
  provenance rule spends nothing here. Every account names a role.
- **No face on the calm as a fact about the world.** `settled` multiplies threat down and never to
  zero, and `monster_pressure` can fire on a heartland town and print beside the face. The calm is
  only ever what a source makes of the country, or what the public makes of what it saw.
