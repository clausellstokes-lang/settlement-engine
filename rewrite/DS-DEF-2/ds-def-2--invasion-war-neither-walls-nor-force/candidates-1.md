## THE WRITER'S OWN SCAN

Mechanical: no digit, no em dash, no exclamation mark, no semicolon, no contraction, no `will` or
`shall` before a verb anywhere on any line. `{settlement}` stands in ONE unit only (variant 1's
spine) and in no face. No named record is cited anywhere: not the accounts, not a register, not a
toll book, not a muster roll. No player line says `the survey`, `the record`, `this office` or
`entered as`; `this office` does not appear on a player face, and the notebook notes do not need it.

Floor 1: no face denies a required row or a service at or above the bar. Nothing denies the town
watch's night patrol or gate duty, nothing denies dispute arbitration at the hall, nothing denies
burial. The three absences stated are the three the key fixes. No gate, checkpoint or toll-bar is
asserted OR denied. No body is inferred: no watch below town, no militia, no garrison, no
mercenary, no charter hall, no "the guard", and no rotation or standing manning by another name
(the tavern's "houses that would come out" is explicitly capped at the naming, and the watch faces
bound the round rather than organising anything).

Floor 2: no count, no date, no season, no duration, no rate. The duratives that appear
(`has never once meant standing in a line`, `has always looked to itself`, `has been seen`,
`has ever gone`) run over the key's own reads (walls, garrison, militia) or over the card's frozen
list, which is where ruling 11 licenses them. No event the record did not run: nothing came down,
nothing left, nothing was dismissed, nothing was founded.

The rung test (2d): every face turns on there being nothing raised AND nothing under arms of the
town's own. A face that would sit on `walls with NO force` would have to keep a wall, and none of
these does; a face that would sit on `force with NO walls` or `militia only` would have to seat a
body, and none of these does.

Tier: no tier word, no spelled band, no north-European furniture (no thatch, no hearth-smoke, no
churchyard, no market green, no snow). The objects are ones the roster holds at the tier the face
is seated at: a door and what leans behind it, a spade and turf, a lantern and a stick, a lock on
the granary door, workshop shutters, the long table, trestles and carts, mud and a road.

Shapes: the three spines take three different shapes (the bare fact in the archiver's hand · a
source's account · the public's collective seeing). Within each variant no two faces share a
subject, an opening or a landing noun, the attribution frames rotate, and the same attribution verb
never runs three in a row.
