**Three findings the chair should have, and two of them are about the packets rather than the pool.**

1. **The attribution rate cannot be brought to 0.63 by selection.** Every `[face]` in this corpus is an ACCOUNT and names its source (rulings 13a, 40), so the only unattributed sentences available to a selector are spines and the second sentences of two-sentence faces. The benchmark pools reached 0.63 because their faces carry a bare second beat (*…The watch does not say which street.*). **Neither packet offered such a face — one candidate in twenty-four does it, and it is the one I refused above.** The next writers' brief should ask for them by name.
2. **The close classes are 29 plain by the mechanical count and that count is too narrow.** Four kept faces close on a question left hanging in substance — the hall that says nothing about where the money went instead (V2), the guild that nobody asked (V2), the court that offers no opinion beyond it (V2), and the sexton who does not say which of them has it right (V3) — but the kernel's `closeClassOf` only catches a comma followed by a wh-word, so none of them register. The variety ruling 34 asks for is present; the instrument cannot see it.
3. **The attribution VERB could not be varied and I did not fake it.** Of fifty-one candidate faces across both packets, forty-eight open on `says`, `say`, `holds`, `has it that` or `puts it that`, and forty-three of those are `say`/`says`. With nine faces to a variant a run of three identical verbs is arithmetically unavoidable, so I spent the variety I could actually buy — the POSITION of the attribution: first (most), carried mid-sentence (V1 market, V1 guild), last (V1 court), place-first with no verb of saying (V2 tavern), place-first with one (V1 and V3 tavern). Eighteen of twenty-nine units carry a `, and` join; the field offered nothing shorter that was also worth the page. I record this as a defect of the CANDIDATE FIELD, not of the law, and the cure is in the writers' brief.
