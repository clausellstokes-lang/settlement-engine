### FINDING 1 · v2f7 `[elders · pair 2 · view]` — floor 1

> *"the water is the whole of what this place is careful of"*

**The denier.** `Burial ground` is `required: true` at thorp and hamlet, `Graveyard` at village,
`Parish burial grounds` at town — a place for the dead stands on all 218 towns, and every one of
those rows says in its own words that the town takes deliberate care of it:

- **hamlet (72 of 218, the largest tier)** — `institutionalCatalog.js:311`: *"A walled plot at the
  edge of the settlement, gated against livestock and **kept by the households in turn**."* The
  speakers of this face ARE the households. The required row makes them the keepers of a second
  thing, and the face has them deny it.
- **thorp (68 of 218)** — `:60`: *"The oldest markers have lost their names to the weather and
  **are kept clear anyway**."* Deliberate care, stated as such.
- **village (64 of 218)** — `:788`: consecrated ground on the parish's own plot, ground nearest the
  wall spoken for generations ahead and everyone able to say who holds it.
- **town (14 of 218)** — `Town granary` required, `Grain storage` p 1.0 (`card.md:42`), desc
  *"Communal grain storage. Buffers harvests, prevents famine."* (`:928`); `Town watch` `Night
  patrol` p 1.0.

The card's own §7 names the burial ground's interest as **"the one piece of ground the town keeps
deliberately"** (`card.md:707`). The face says the water is the whole of what it is careful of.

**And it contradicts itself on the page.** Pair 2 is a `view` pair; its two halves are one rendered
unit (ruling 23). The partner reads *"the ground for the dead is marked off and the rest of the place
is not"* — a second thing this place is careful of, printed beside the claim that the water is the
whole of it. Floor 1's first clause is NO SELF-CONTRADICTION ON THE PAGE and the unit here is
literally one unit.

**Which is the record.** The required roster row. A source's opinion is free; *"the whole of"* turns
this into a total factual exclusion over the town's practices, and the brief is explicit that no
source asserts a field's opposite.

**Provenance, and it matters.** This wording is the FIRST SITTING'S OWN SUGGESTED CURE, carried over
almost verbatim from `refute.md` row v2f7. The refuter proposed it to kill a founding-reason claim
(V-12), which it does; it introduced a total exclusion in its place, which nobody re-read. The chair
should note that a refuter's suggested cure is not law and this packet is where it gets tested.

**Cure.** Make it a ranking, which is an opinion, rather than an exclusion, which is a fact claim:
*"The older households say the water is what this place looks to first."* Pair 2 keeps its subject,
its kind and its disagreement, and the partner stops contradicting it.

### FINDING 3 · v1f9 `[watch · pair 1 · disagree]` — floor 1 · 2

> *"the wage he draws is the whole of what the town pays"* against an army

**The denier is the face's own first clause, and then the row.** *"One of the watch"* presupposes a

exactly one member. That is a headcount of one asserted by entailment, on a row whose own word is
plural — and a headcount is DS-DEF-5's cell, not this one.

**The GENERAL FORM runs against this face rather than for it.** The chair's rule rescues a phrase
whose own neighbouring sentence forces the lawful reading. Here the face's own neighbouring clause

whole" then denies.

**The cure packet's claim on this row is testable and fails.** `cure.md` writes: *"No singular
headcount: the subject is a wage, not a man on a street."* The subject is a wage whose POSSESSOR is
singular and whose extent is asserted to be the whole. The first sitting charged the singular as the
secondary hazard inside its v1f9 FAIL; it was not cured, it was relocated from the man to his wage.
The primary charge — "and nothing besides" denying `Gate duty` p 0.8 — IS genuinely cured, and
cleanly: no service is enumerated and §2b's ⛔ has nothing to bite.

**Cure, at zero cost to the pair.** *"One of the watch says the wages they draw are the whole of what
the town pays against an army."* The disagreement with the hall's half is untouched, the one purse
