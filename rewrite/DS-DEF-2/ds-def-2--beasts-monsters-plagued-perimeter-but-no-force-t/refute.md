## THE THREE LONG NOTES

### FAIL · variant 1 face 8 · tavern · FLOOR 1

> "the ones at the way through do it because they live nearest and for no other reason"

**The field that denies it:** `Town watch`, `required: true` at town, whose own asserted services are **Night patrol p 1 and Gate duty p 0.8** — `institutionalCatalog.js:1348-1354`, printed on the card at §2b with the standing ⛔: *a service at or above the bar is a thing the town's own model says this body does; denying it is floor 1, whatever the face is otherwise about.*

**Which is the record:** the roster. The face is not.

**Why it bites here and not elsewhere:** the tavern seats at TOWN ONLY (`Taverns (5-20)` required at town; card §7's tier table prints "the tavern: —" at thorp), so under car 18c's source filter this face draws **exclusively** on towns that carry a required `Town watch` on Gate duty. The exclusivity clause — *and for no other reason* — closes the sentence against the duty: it asserts that proximity is the whole account of why anyone is at the bar. That is the sitting's own named failure mode in its politest dress. It is not the loud form the card warns about ("nobody to man it"); it is the quiet form, and it is the only face in the pool that actually commits it. Faces 3 f2 and 2 f3 walk the same ground and stay lawful because they claim something about a person's instruction or a person's trade instead of about the assignment.

**Cure (one clause):** drop the exclusivity and keep the tavern's account of WHO rather than an account of WHY NOT — *"At the tavern they say the ones at the way through are the ones who live nearest to it."* The flat observation survives; the denial goes.

### FAIL · variant 3 face 9 · elders · FLOOR 3

> "One of the elders says the grain leaves the line to be ground"

**The field that denies it:** `TIER_MANDATORY_ROLES`, `npcGenerator.js:1511-1537` — a thorp emits exactly ONE `Elder`, carrying a personality, a disposition, a behaviour hook and a secret on the next tab. The card says it in one parenthesis at §7: **the elders (plural, always)**, and again under THE NAMED OFFICES A SPEAKER MUST NEVER BE: *"the elder" in the singular … is a person on the next tab.*

**Which is the record:** the NPC generator. The face is not.

The elders source is the below-town family, so this face draws on the thorp half of the preimage — 34 of the 45 towns. The pool's other two elders faces (1 f10, 2 f9) are correctly plural; this is the one slip. ⚠ The draft's own mechanical scan asserts *"no `Mayor`, `Guard Captain`, `High Priest` or singular `Elder` as subject OR attribution"* — that claim is false against the rows as selected.

**Cure (two words):** *"The elders say the grain leaves the line to be ground and comes back through the same gap, and that nobody has put up another way of doing it."* Nothing else in the line changes; `Access to external mill` (required at thorp, *"must travel to the manor mill"*) is affirmed exactly as before, and "the same gap" is not a count of gates.

### WITHHELD · variant 1 face 3 · watch · FLOOR 4 (put to the chair)

> "the works are kept up by the people who live against them and by nobody at the hall"

**The field:** `defenseProfile.economicGates.military` — `milUpkeepMult = min(1, 0.6 + econOutput/50 × 0.4)`, covering *"garrison wages, wall maintenance"* together (`defenseGenerator.js:182`, `:189-192`), **with a floor at 0.6 and `communityMilBase` exempt entirely** (`:186-192`). On this key there is no garrison and no militia, so the only military expense the gate funds is the wall's keeping — and it is funded, on every town of the preimage, at not less than 60 per cent. At town the accounts are the hall's (`Town hall`, required).

**Why I withhold rather than charge.** The sentence has two readings and the record only denies one of them.
- *Labour reading* — the hands that do the keeping are the neighbours', and nobody from the hall turns out. Lawful, and in fact model-true: `communityMilBase` is exactly the unpaid community baseline the generator exempts.
- *Money reading* — the hall's purse pays nothing toward the works. That is F4-04's shape (the licensed extreme is short, late, thin — never none) laid over F4-02's one purse, and it is false against a gate floored at 0.6.

The pair forces the money reading on the reader: its other half is *"kept up at the town's charge"*, which is money, and a `disagree` pair is read as a rebuttal of the half beside it. So the face as placed says the purse pays nothing.

**What is genuinely open on this pool and what is not.** Card §9 puts the open question precisely: whether a kept line with nobody on it is thrift, a bill half paid, or a thing the town keeps because stopping would mean saying so. That is open, and no face may close it. **Who pays is not open** — the engine records it. A pair built on "does the hall pay at all" is not the pool's designed dispute; a pair built on "what the hall's share buys, and whether it is enough" is.

**Cure, either way, one clause:**
- keep the money frame and bound it — *"…says what the hall sends does not reach the far side of it."* (short, not none: F4-04 satisfied, the grievance intact, the pair still genuinely unsettled), or
- keep the labour frame and make it unmistakable — *"…says the works are kept up by the people who live against them, and by nobody the hall sends."*

**The collapse: ONE ATTRIBUTION FRAME.** Not one *sentence* twelve times — the content genuinely varies, and the best of it is very good — but one *construction*, and it is the construction the reader sees first every time.

- 244 distinct words over 749. The three or four nouns *the line · the works · the way through · the country* carry the pool; `the line` alone appears 15 times.

The draft's own NOTES §4 reaches the same finding honestly and calls it a supply problem rather than an ordering one: variants 2 and 3 run 4/4 in one opener class, which exceeds ruling 36, and **no permutation of the candidates offered fixes it.** I agree with that diagnosis, and it is why the verdict is DULL rather than a set of per-face craft notes: the fix is upstream of the selector.

**What is NOT wrong, and should not be touched in the cure:**
- **Ten distinct speakers** (gate · hall · watch · guild · stranger · register · court · tavern · market · elders), every one of them seated by a row the card prints, and each carrying an interest the town's own fields give it.
- **Real stake, genuinely unsettled.** The hall defends its purse, the watch has a grievance, the guilds pay and get nothing, the households owe work, a stranger cannot get a straight answer. Two pairs actually disagree; the disagreement is the session, exactly as ruling 22 asks.
- **The particulars are strong** — the tallow on the bar, the grain leaving the line to be ground and coming back through the same gap, the carrying falling on the finder, the bed inside the line that nobody haggles over, the stalls down while there is light. This pool is decisively **not** duller than the three shipped rows it replaces, which say *"a chokepoint on paper"* and *"long stretches of good work"* and nothing about anybody.

**The cure is a writer's brief, not a re-selection.** Ask by NAME, at a stated rate per variant, for frames the packets did not supply: attribution last (the pool has exactly one, and it is the best line in variant 1); attribution embedded mid-sentence (*"Nobody has come to the hall asking for a company, a clerk there says, and nobody has been refused either"*); object-fronted; a reported custom with no speech verb at all (variant 2's tavern, the pool's one specimen); and the compound pair-joint of ruling 23, which lets the second attribution ride inside the sentence and breaks the subject-first run for free. A synonym for `says` is not the fix — ruling 20 bars the mannered verb, and the collapse is in the frame, not the word.

**Two bare-assertion faults, reported not charged** (ruling 40: the archiver's bare hand is for a fact the engine holds): variant 1 face 4's *"The households that send the hands see nothing back for them"* and variant 2 face 6's *"The carrying falls on the finder, and nobody makes it good"* each attribute sentence one and then state an unrecorded consequence bare.
