**`Beasts & Monsters`: `plagued`, perimeter but NO force to hold it**

1. `[ledger]` The works around {settlement} are kept up. No garrison and no militia stand behind them, and the country outside is thick with creatures.
   - `[face]` `[gate]` A person who holds the way through says the bar is greased with whatever tallow the nearest household can spare, and that the smell of it carries to the first doors.
   - `[face]` `[hall · pair 1 · disagree]` A clerk in the hall says the works are kept up at the town's charge like any other thing the town owns.
   - `[face]` `[watch · pair 1 · disagree]` One of the watch says the works are kept up by the people who live against them and by nobody at the hall.
   - `[face]` `[guild]` The work on the line is let out in the season when the trades have hands to spare, the guilds say. The households that send the hands see nothing back for them.
   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A traveller says the work on the line is better kept than the place looks able to keep it, and that nobody he asked would name who pays.
   - `[face]` `[register]` Whoever buries the dead says the country takes what it takes, and does not say whether the line has changed that.
   - `[face]` `[court]` The court says the line reaches it as a dispute between neighbours over work owed, and in no other form.
   - `[face]` `[tavern]` At the tavern they say the ones at the way through do it because they live nearest and for no other reason.
   - `[face]` `[market]` A stallholder says the stalls come down in the square while there is light left, and that nobody needs telling why.
   - `[face]` `[elders]` The elders say the households behind the line have always kept it, and that which of them keeps which part is settled among themselves.
2. `[visitor]` Travellers report good work along the line here and no soldiers on it. They ask at the way through what keeps the creatures off, and they are given the line for an answer.
   - `[face]` `[gate]` Those who hold the way through say the road is theirs as far as they can see down it, and that what comes off the country comes without notice.
   - `[face]` `[hall]` A clerk in the hall says the works are the town's own and a stranger may walk them by asking at the hall first.
   - `[face]` `[watch]` One of the watch says a traveller is through the bar quicker here than in a town with soldiers, because the person at the bar is wanted back at a trade.
   - `[face]` `[guild]` A guild factor says a stranger haggles over every price in the town and never over the bed inside the line.
   - `[face]` `[tavern]` At the tavern they ask a stranger about the road before they ask his business.
   - `[face]` `[register]` Whoever buries the dead says the ones taken on the road are carried in by whoever came upon them. The carrying falls on the finder, and nobody makes it good.
   - `[face]` `[market]` Stallholders say a stranger's goods are looked over at the way through and priced again once they are inside.
   - `[face]` `[court]` The court says a stranger stopped at the way through can bring the matter before it, and that stopping him is nobody's decision to explain.
   - `[face]` `[elders]` The elders say a stranger who comes in after dark is let through without a question, and the question keeps until morning.
3. `[unfolding]` The country outside the line is not quiet. The line is kept up, the place keeps no company of its own, and nobody here gives a reason.
   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A traveller says he asked here who keeps the line and was answered differently by everyone he asked.
   - `[face]` `[gate]` A person who holds the way through says the bar goes down at dusk, and that nobody has told him what is done if something is already inside it.
   - `[face]` `[watch · pair 5 · disagree]` The watch says it has said what it is short of, and said it to the hall.
   - `[face]` `[hall · pair 5 · disagree]` A clerk in the hall says nothing about the line has come to it in a form it can act on.
   - `[face]` `[tavern · pair 6 · view]` At the tavern they say the ones who go out to the line are owed by everyone who stays in.
   - `[face]` `[court · pair 6 · view]` The court says a household that sends nobody to the line can be brought before it, and that the bringing is where the matter ends.
   - `[face]` `[guild]` The guilds say they would pay for a company if the paying stopped there, and that it never does.
   - `[face]` `[register]` Whoever buries the dead says what the country takes is buried with everyone else, and that no one asks for the ground to be kept apart.
   - `[face]` `[elders]` One of the elders says the grain leaves the line to be ground and comes back through the same gap, and that nobody has put up another way of doing it.

--- NOTES

**THE SPINES**

- spine 1 | `[ledger]` | packet 1 (seat Opus 5) | the bare fact, subject first, and the only unit in the pool carrying `{settlement}`. Won over packet 2's V1 spine, which repeats *around* and *the place* inside two sentences. Two facts and a stop; the arithmetic is the reader's.
- spine 2 | `[visitor]` | packet 2 (seat Opus 5) | the source fronted — a different opener class from spine 1. Packet 1's V2 spine is one sentence on three `and` joins, which is the measured tic in its plainest form. The second sentence answers a question with the thing itself and does not explain itself afterwards.
- spine 3 | `[unfolding]` | packet 2 (seat Opus 5) | opens on the negative (*is not quiet*) and closes on the withheld reason, which leaves the pool's one genuinely open question standing. Packet 1's V3 spine carries *a thing the town keeps because stopping would mean saying so*, which costs a second reading; clarity is the first test in the mix and it fails there.
- the three spines take three shapes (bare fact · account · negative-then-withheld), none names the document, and none permutes another.

**FLAGGED TO THE CHAIR, NOT DECIDED HERE**

1. *the works* against *the line*. Spine 1 and its pair say **the works**; the other two spines and most faces say **the line**. The veto on a subject noun varied for variety's sake is real, and this variation is an artefact of two writers rather than a design. I kept it because the unit a town renders is ONE spine and ONE face, and within variant 1 the spine and its pair share the noun exactly; across the three units where spine 1 draws a *line* face, the two words read as the same thing and cost no second reading. Overturnable in either direction by swapping variant 1's spine and pair to packet 2's *line* wording, at the price of the magnitude claim above.
2. *what it is short of* (variant 3, pair 5, watch). Short OF WHAT is unstated, and the thing the key fixes short is hands, not the purse — but `defenseProfile.economicGates.military` is OPEN on this key and a refuter could read the purse into it (F4-02 / V-17). It is an attributed grievance, and the whole pool exists for it; kept, and named here so it is refuted deliberately or not at all.
3. variant 2 is narrow. Eight of its nine faces have a stranger or a traveller somewhere in them, which is the `[visitor]` tag's own lens and is what both writers wrote to, but the pool's other two variants carry the width. The grammatical subjects do vary, so the assembly veto does not bite; the observation is offered as taste.
4. THE OPENER-CLASS ARITHMETIC, AND A RUN I COULD NOT ORDER AWAY. Ruling 36 vetoes a run of three kept faces in one opener class. Of the twenty-eight faces kept, twenty-four are SUBJECT FIRST, because ruling 25's attributed account puts the role in the subject slot and ruling 20 bars the mannered verb that would move it. The four breaks the packets supplied are variant 1's guild (attribution LAST) and the three tavern faces (PLACE FIRST, and variant 2's carries no speech verb at all). I ordered each variant to spread them as far as the arithmetic allows: variant 1 holds two breaks and runs 3 / 3 / 2, the optimum for eight subject-first faces; variants 2 and 3 hold ONE break each among nine faces and therefore run 4 / 4, which exceeds the rule. **That is a supply problem and not an ordering one** — no permutation of these candidates gets either variant under three. The instrument is a writer's brief that asks by name for attribution-last, attribution-embedded (*Nobody has come to the hall asking for a company, a clerk there says, …* — packet 2 wrote exactly one and its source was already in a pair), object-fronted and bare-observation frames, at a stated rate per variant. A synonym for *says* is not the fix and ruling 20 forbids it.

**THE MECHANICAL SCAN ON THE ROWS AS SELECTED** — no digit, no em dash, no exclamation mark, no semicolon, no contraction in any face or spine. `{settlement}` in ONE unit (spine 1) and in no `[face]` sub-row. No `{defmaterial}` and no wall material, this preimage pooling stakes at thorp against stone at town. No *will* or *shall*; four conditionals and no forecast. NO NAMED RECORD ANYWHERE — the citation ceiling on this pool is zero and nothing here cites a toll book, a muster roll, a register-as-a-record, the accounts, the parish books or *from the road*. No *the survey*, *this office*, *the record*, *entered as*. No minted name; no `Mayor`, `Guard Captain`, `High Priest` or singular `Elder` as subject OR attribution. No muster and no garrison as a speaker. No *nobody to man it*, *nobody stands on it*, *nobody is set up to answer*, *the guard*, *under arms* — the pool's characteristic breach, and the cure is used instead: no garrison, no militia, no soldiers, no company. Nothing calls the watch professional, full-time or soldiers. No decay clock and no permanence on the fabric. No date, founding, raising, age or rate. Duratives used — *have always kept it*, *has been kept*, *never does*, *has been let grow* — all run over the walls, garrison and militia buckets (the key's own reads) or over speech acts; nothing carries a durative over `institutions[bucket=watch]`, which is LIVE and is not a read of this key.
