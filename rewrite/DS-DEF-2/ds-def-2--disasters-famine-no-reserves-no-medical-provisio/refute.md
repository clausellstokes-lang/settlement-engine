## WHAT I HUNTED AND DID NOT FIND (so the chair knows the sweep was whole)

- **No closed-roster body is asserted anywhere.** No hall, chamber or clerk (F1-20, V-29); no guild;
  no watch, garrison, militia, mercenary, charter, walls or gate (F1-01/02/03/07/08, V-23, V-27,
  F4-18) — and equally none is DENIED, so F1-25/F1-30 is clear. The four rosters are untouched.
- **No body inferred into the key's silence** — the sitting's own named failure mode. Not one face
  says nobody here can treat the sick, nobody comes, or nobody is set up to respond; the shipped
  corpus's "has nobody to treat the sick" and "a plague runs until it burns out" (card (8)) are not
  carried forward, and the `Priest (resident)`'s `Healing` p 0.7 and the tab's own "Parish clergy
  provide basic wound care" are denied by nothing. The absence is written as an absence of
  BUILDINGS AND STORES throughout, which is exactly the card's instruction.
- **No cited record.** F1-24 / §R-8 is clear: no book, no register, no accounts, no roll, and never
  "the books" as a generic. "Counted", "weighed" and "a reckoning" are acts, not keepers.
- **No named office.** Never the elder, the priest, the parish priest, the mayor, the guard captain,
  the miller or the healer (F3-06, card (7)); the plural, the trade and the ground carry every
  attribution ("the older households", "whoever grinds it", "those who bury the dead").
- **No deity as subject** (F3-02); nothing beyond the world or the sub-century (F3-04); no named
  character's fate (F3-01).
- **Floor 2 swept clause by clause.** One perfect in fifteen faces (v2 f2, charged). No date, season,
  month or duration (F2-02); no founding or raising (F2-03); no event the record did not run
  (F2-04) — "when the bread stops", "in a bad year" and "on a market day" are generic conditionals,
  not narrated events; no trend (F2-08); no age of fabric (F2-07); no allusion to the town's past
  (F2-09). Magnitudes tested and cleared: "a place this size" (names no value, and W3's spelled band
  is struck), "a sack" / "the sacks" / "by the sack" (containers, no count), "part … and part" (a
  partition with no share).
- **Floor 4 swept.** No decay clock and no permanence over any fabric (F4-01); no split purse and no
  total collapse of pay (F4-02/03/04) — this pool touches no purse at all; `plagued` is never read as
  disease (F4-05); nothing closes the town off, so the port line "No reserves, but sea supply

- **Ruling 35, the neighbouring rung.** Neither sibling rung (`granary AND parish care only`,

## CRAFT — AT THE POOL GRAIN

**VERDICT: PASS.** Five distinct speakers seat across the pool (stranger · elders · register ·
tavern · market) with the public carrying variant 2's spine, which is above the three-speaker bar
with room. Three spines in three shapes (a bare recorded fact · the public's common knowledge · the
stranger's conditional), as ruling 29 asks. The stakes are real and they are different stakes: the
mill's share, the buriers' grievance at being sent for and not thanked, the market's price, the
elders' pride in the arrangement, the stranger's eye on what the town has stopped noticing — and two
of them are in open dispute on the page. The noun set is wide (granary, stone, sack, field, door,
step, lane, plot, roots, market day) against the shipped rows' three flat lines, so this is nowhere
near the 168-distinct-word collapse the licence test produced. No face is a permutation of a sibling
except the one charged at v3 f2.

**THE COLLAPSE RISK, NAMED, SHORT OF DULL:** the pool's one repeated construction is the
**negated counterpoint — "A, and not B."** It closes v1 f3 ("to eat and not to keep"), v1 f4 ("and
not before"), v2 f5 ("and not thanked for coming"), v3 f1 ("counted going and not counted coming
back"), v3 f4 ("part … and part does not") and v3 f5 ("and no place to keep it"), and both negative
spines open on the doubled "no X and no Y". That is the antithesis pair the tell list names, on
roughly half the pool. It does not reach DULL — the subjects, sources, openers and landing nouns all
genuinely vary, and the selector's frame rules did real work — but it is the one move a second pass
should break in two or three places, and it is where the pool will read as itself if it is ever
widened.

## WIRING ROWS (the record is the engine, so no face is charged)

- **R-1 · the no-row granary READING.** `foodStockpile.js:190-193` prints a granary capacity of
  1.5/2.0 months on a town with no granary row (= §1.4 **W-07**), and under `famine` the same page
  prints "Those with food stores face targeted theft or worse". Faces that describe a town keeping
  nothing back therefore sit beside an engine number saying it keeps something. The seam is the
  engine's, not the writer's; v1 f3 stands.
- **R-2 · the village tavern is mute.** `faceSources.js` `TAVERN_NAMES = ['tavern', 'alehouse']`
  (substring), while `institutionalCatalog.js:492` spells the village row **`Ale house`** with a
  space and `:391` spells the hamlet row `Alehouse`. `'ale house'.includes('alehouse')` is false, so
  the `tavern` source seats on hamlets only and NEVER on a village — three of this pool's fifteen
  faces are silent on 97 of the 353 preimage towns for a spelling. Either the list gains `'ale house'`
  or the catalogue row is renamed; both are the chair's call, and neither is a face fault.
- **R-3 · the card's register seat and the kernel's disagree.** The card's mechanical (7) seats
  "the register (parish)" on `Burial ground` at thorp; `faceSources.js` `REGISTER_NAMES`
  (church · cathedral · temple · abbey · monastery · friary) with `REGISTER_EXCLUDE_PREFIX =
  'access to'` seats it on **village only**. The kernel is what renders. The instrument
  (`scripts/prose-mark-card.mjs`) prints a seat the product does not have, and every card of this
  block inherits it.
- **R-4 · the brief's "four faces per variant" is stale.** The derived pin is
  `FACE_PIN = 1 + FACE_SOURCES.length` = 15, and its own comment records that four "is not a floor
  anywhere in the grammar". The refuter's and writers' briefs still carry the old number as a rule
  that "reds the build"; a writer obeying it drops the register and the market on quality-blind
  grounds, which is the exact defect the pin's comment was written to cure.
- **R-5 · hand-written ROLES ahead of car 18l.** The stranger's three faces are attributed as
  "A drover says", "a traveller reports" and "by a carter's reckoning", and the register's three as
  "Those who bury the dead", in place of the class word the brief says to keep until 18l lands. When
  the role slot lands and the page draws a ROLE from the town's own rows for each source, each of
  these faces will carry two roles. The lines read well today; the hazard is at the car, and the
  chair should decide whether to re-spell to the class word now or to pin these faces as
  role-bearing.
