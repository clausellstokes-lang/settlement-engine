### 0.3 THE TIER ARITHMETIC, AND THE TWO ENGINE CONTRADICTIONS THIS POOL SITS ON

**Where this key can fire.** `Town granary` and `City granaries` are `required: true, baseChance: 1`; `assembleInstitutions.js:279-282` seats a required row unless a DM toggle sets `forceExclude`. Two escapes exist and are recorded, not ruled: a DM's `forceExclude` toggle on a town's required granary, and custom content naming a matching row. Recorded as open row 2.

**ENGINE CONTRADICTION 1 — the key's own name is false of the world.** `hasGranary === false` does NOT mean the town holds no food. `foodGenerator.js:161-165` gives a granary-less settlement `1.5` months at thorp/hamlet and `1.0` at village-and-up; `foodStockpile.js:190-193` gives the same settlement `1.5` at thorp/hamlet and `2.0` otherwise (the two producers **disagree**, which is itself recorded here). And `isolationSupport.js:47`'s `RESERVE_INSTITUTION` regex counts `root cellar`, `warehouse`, `storehouse`, `silo`, `cistern`, `cold storage` and `preserv` as RESERVES — so the thorp `Communal root cellar` (`institutionalCatalog.js:111`, described in the catalogue as a store for grain and preserved food and a buffer against a bad harvest) is a reserve to one system and invisible to this flag. The ratified entailment table says this in its own words at L-20, and the defense refuter names the SIBLING cell's shipped line "holds no food buffer at all" (annex `:3046-3049`) as the breach this creates. **This pool's v1 "keeps no food against a bad harvest" is that same breach, one cell over.** A face asserting an empty store is not merely unlicensed; it is contradicted by the engine on the very towns that draw it.
