## VARIANT 1 · `[ledger]` — the entry as an office would set it down

Grammar: **V2 (PRESENT → CONSEQUENCE(structural))** on the row, and V1/V4/V5 across the faces. The discriminating variant: every one of its four renderings states **both** reads, so a reader can always tell this cell from `force without a perimeter` and from `settled, defenses beyond the need`. That is the duty the skeleton names as unearned by the shipped set.

**Row (face 1, canonical at index zero).** *An active frontier lies outside {settlement}, and what the town keeps against it is works with a force to stand on them. What the country holds is not entered at all.* — **31 words.**
- RECORD: the frontier country (C-read-1) · the works (C-read-2) · the force (C-read-3) · the pairing of the two against the country, which is the predicate's own conjunction.
- SILENCE: *"what the country holds is not entered at all"* is the ABSENCE move in its GAP class — a gap in the RECORD, never in the world (R-DA-08; F1-70's positive move, "say the town does not know, and the not-knowing is the hook"). No field enumerates what stands in a monster-pressure country; `config.monsterThreat` is a tier and nothing under it is a bestiary. Floor 1: asserts no body, building, keeper, force or faith-house. Floor 2: no count, no date, no elapsed course, no rate. The absence does not open the unit and sits beside no second absence.
- Carried from the shipped row: *sits on an active frontier* survives as **an active frontier lies outside**, off the `{settlement}` opener (T-F8 bars it, and the shipped row cannot keep it). *a force behind it* survives as **a force to stand on them**. The two-beat shape survives; the shipped second beat's content (*most … will not … most … does not*) is gone entire — F2-01, F2-05, F2-06.

**Face 2.** *The country outside {settlement} is entered as an active frontier, and beside that entry stands the town's own: works, and a force.* — **22 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-1, C-read-2, C-read-3.
- SILENCE: the record-keeping frame (*entered*, *entry*) is the `[ledger]` stance's own; W24's record-word bar is struck by name, and the passive keeps it off a holder citation. Construction: passive record-frame plus a colon list, against the row's active *what the town keeps*.

**Face 3.** *Works stand at {settlement}, a force goes up on them, and past both lies an active frontier.* — **17 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-2 first, C-read-3 second, C-read-1 third — the reads in the reverse order of the row, which is the point of the face.
- SILENCE: none added. **The short line exists** (R-DA-05; the pack's "length follows load"). This is the pool's plainest rendering and it is deliberate: it is why the others land.

**Face 4.** *Keeping the ground outside the works clear is a standing duty at {settlement}, shared out among the force, and the frontier is what the entry gives as its reason.* — **29 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-2 (the works), C-read-3 (the force, as the duty's bearers), C-read-1 (the frontier, as the entered reason). The reason clause is a **capability** clause, which this block's header licenses in terms, and never a historical one.
- SILENCE: the clearing of ground outside a work is labour nothing in the record denies. It names no terrain and no vegetation — *clear* takes no object — so F1-102 and F3-05 are untouched and the face prints as well on a steppe or desert town as on any other. It states no distance, so a5's magnitude bar is untouched: the ground is kept clear, not kept clear *for so far*. Floor 4: it asserts no funding state in either direction (c6) and no permanence.
- Construction: a gerund duty as the subject, the force in apposition, the cause entered last.

## VARIANT 2 · `[street]` — the town at its own grain

Grammar: **V1 / V2 / V6** across the four. **No slot in any of the four**, matching the parent row, which is the shipped row's own shape. The shipped row's elapsed frame (*has taken it seriously long enough that*) is gone entire — F2-02 and F2-05, the perfect plus a duration, against `ageBands.js`'s `HISTORICIZE_BAND` pin. What replaces it reaches *ordinary rather than anxious* from a STANDING condition instead of from time, which is the conversion §F2 asks for.

**Row (face 1, canonical).** *The frontier is spoken of in the town as a duty and not as a danger, and a night on the works is grumbled at like any other night's work.* — **30 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-1 (the frontier) · C-read-2 (the works) · C-read-3 implicitly, in the people who stand the nights.
- SILENCE: how a town speaks of its own standing arrangement is nowhere banded. The grumbling is the COMPLAINT door — a complaint carries the fact, the stake and a voice at once and invents nothing. Floor 2: *a night on the works* is a unit of duty, not a magnitude, a rate or a duration; no count is spoken, and the verb is the simple habitual present, which the addendum names as the writer's main instrument. The contrast *a duty and not a danger* is a posture, never a magnitude — it is the shipped row's *ordinary rather than anxious* re-seated without the elapsed frame.

**Face 2.** *Children play on the works. At dusk whoever is up there calls the children in, and the town finds neither half of that worth a remark.* — **26 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-2 (the works) · C-read-3 (*whoever is up there* — a person on the works, the only wording true across both ends of the garrison∨militia disjunction).
- SILENCE: **F1-34's own rider licenses this by name** — "ease at the town's own grain is free … children on the earthwork." Floor 3: the person is unnamed, is a *whoever*, and is emphatically **not** the singular office the tier emits (F3-06 — no captain, no keeper of a key). Floor 2: *at dusk* is a time of day inside a habitual present, not a date, a duration or a rate; nothing here says *most nights*. Thread: sentence two carries *the works* and *the children* forward from sentence one.

**Face 3.** *Argument in the town runs on the rota rather than on the frontier itself: whose name comes round on the works, and who contrives to be elsewhere.* — **27 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-1, C-read-2, C-read-3 (the rota is the force's own turn-taking).
- SILENCE: a rota, and somebody dodging it, is a plot hook nothing in the record denies. **No proper name is minted** — *whose name* names nobody (F1-126). Floor 2: no count of turns, no frequency, no elapsed shirking (*who contrives to be elsewhere*, not *who has been dodging*). Construction: the subject is *Argument*, not the town, the works or the country — the one face in the pool whose grammatical subject is an abstraction, and it earns it by landing on people.

**Face 4.** *One corner of the works is left alone after dark, and nobody who stands the rest of them will say why, only that the country out there is frontier.* — **29 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-2 · C-read-3 (*nobody who stands the rest of them*) · C-read-1.
- SILENCE: the AVOIDED door, and the WITHHOLDING second clause. *One corner* asserts no circuit and no extent — a corner is a point, not a line (F1-07 untouched). Nothing is denied of the works: they are not said to be unheld, unbuilt or absent, only that one part of them is let alone, which no flag contradicts (F1-25 and F1-30 untouched). Floor 2: *after dark* is habitual, not a rate. The matter is **left standing open** — the face raises a question and refuses to close it, which is the pool's one deliberate open matter.

## VARIANT 3 · `[counterforce]` — what is holding the pressure back, in the subjunctive

Grammar: **V2 / V3 / V1** across the four. The shipped row's IDEA — that what deters is what would be met, not what has been used — is kept and re-seated; both of its cashed terms go. *Very little reaches* is F2-01 plus F2-06 and is contradicted outright wherever `monster_pressure` is live on the same dossier; *visible from a long way off* is a magnitude of the works no field bands (a5, the same fault as the already-struck *substantial works*). **The subjunctive is this angle's licensed instrument and every face uses it or an absence in its place.**

**Row (face 1, canonical).** *Coming at {settlement} out of the country means coming at works, and at whoever is put on them, and the town would rather that were understood than tested.* — **28 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-1 (*out of the country*, the shipped row's own preposition and noun, kept) · C-read-2 · C-read-3.
- SILENCE: the closing clause is a **collective disposition in the subjunctive**. It states no outcome over creature events, no rate, no forecast: nothing is said to have come, to turn back, or to be likely to. *Would rather … than tested* also leaves the deterrence **untested** on the page, which is the honest reading of a key that prints beside a badge reachable at WEAK, ADEQUATE, STRONG and, under `monster_pressure`, CRITICAL (c5, F1-40). No intensity word is tuned to any band.

**Face 2.** *No rule at {settlement} forbids sleeping outside the works, and nobody does. The country is frontier and the works are held, and a rule would be a formality.* — **28 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-1 · C-read-2 · C-read-3 (*the works are held* is exactly the conjunction the key resolves).
- SILENCE: negative narration — the absence opening the face, which the re-cut restored by name (R-ii / W10, the absence-opener cap, STRUCK) and which the pack aims at. The absence is of a **rule**, a thing the record holds none of either way, so no rostered body, building or keeper is denied (floor 1 untouched). Floor 4: *the works are held* asserts no permanence of the row and no material condition; *a rule would be a formality* is the dry note, subjunctive, with no verdict on the town.

**Face 3.** *A bounty stands at {settlement} for what is carried in out of the country, and the paying and the standing fall to the same hands.* — **25 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-1 · C-read-3 (*the standing*, and the hands that do it) · C-read-2 by the works the standing is on.
- SILENCE: the bounty is the pool's own subject and the skeleton names it — the monster purse's own uses in the generator are patrol provisioning, bounty purses and retainers. **No sum, share or count is spoken**, so F2-01 is untouched: *a bounty stands* states terms, not a price. **No funding state is stated in either direction** (c6), and nothing splits the four gates' direction (F4-02 / F4-03) or claims a total collapse of pay (F4-04). The second clause is a CONSEQUENCE landed on people — the same hands do the paying and the standing — which is the pack's "land the great fact on the small household".
- ⚠ **The one face nearest a line, declared.** *what is carried in out of the country* could be read as an outcome over creature events (c3). It is written as a **standing arrangement in the habitual present** — the bounty stands *for* a class of thing, and no event, count, rate or occasion is asserted — and the habitual present is the instrument the addendum explicitly preserves. Recorded here so a refuter does not have to find it.

**Face 4.** *Patrols go out of {settlement} into the country and come back. The works cannot go anywhere, and on a frontier that is the difference between them and the force.* — **29 words.**
- RECORD: C-read-1 · C-read-2 · C-read-3. **`patrol` is a `force`-class token and is free anywhere** (F1-01's own rider), so this face seats no body the roster may deny.
- SILENCE: a country worth patrolling is the pool's own subject. Floor 2: habitual present, no count of patrols, no frequency, no distance, no duration. The second sentence is a **capability** clause of exactly the class this block's header licenses — a work without people cannot be held, and a work cannot go out — and never a historical one. Thread: *The works* is carried forward from sentence one.
