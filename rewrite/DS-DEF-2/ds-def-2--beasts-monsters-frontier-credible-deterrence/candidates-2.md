## HOW THIS PACKET IS BUILT, AND THE FOUR CALLS I MADE

1. **The seat of a source is a fence that protects its claims.** A face draws only on towns where its source resolves. So a claim false at village but true at town and above is safe inside a face tagged to a source the village does not seat. Every claim about a **wage, a purse, a toll, a drill watched, the garrison** sits inside a `hall` · `tavern` · `guild` · `watch` · `garrison` · `market` · `court` face (town and above), never in a spine and never in a `stranger` · `gate` · `muster` · `register` face, which draw on the village too. The village's currency in this packet is LABOUR and CUSTOM, never coin — which is the same lens in the other half of the preimage, and is the `elders`' whole interest per the card.
2. **No `{defwork}` and no `{settlement}` anywhere.** `{defwork}` is offered on this pool by the STATE-KEY line (ruling 10) but the card does not show it FILLED at this block's call sites, and an unfilled slot prints raw. The plain word **the works** is the rulings' own safe generic and costs nothing. Two `{defwork}`-carrying alternates are offered under NOTES so the selector may spend the slot if the chair confirms the fill. `{settlement}` appears nowhere, in a spine or a face (ruling 12, and the owner's word).
3. **No `compromised` tag anywhere.** Card section (2c) prints that none of this block's three covert fields mark this pool; the tag would be refused on every source here.
4. **The material is never named and neither is a circuit.** The preimage pools a timber-or-earth village ring, a stone town wall and a masonry city circuit into one draw, so "the works" and "the way in" carry the substance and the fill may sharpen it later. I also avoid **line** and **perimeter** as a thing running around the town, since the walls bucket admits a point and an inner keep.

**The mechanical bars observed throughout:** no digit, no em dash, no exclamation mark, no semicolon, no contraction, no `{settlement}` in any face, no future indicative (`will` / `shall`), no named tiered office as subject or attribution, no count, date, rate, season, duration or magnitude, no material or its source, no history, no rot and no permanence, no denial of a required row's service, no exemption from a duty and no totality over persons (the refused columns), no self-citation, no which-clause closer, no summarising close, no antithesis pair, no reassurance, no clever last beat.
