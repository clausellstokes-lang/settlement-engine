Cut to `The town's parish stands.` the passage gains a short line the pool otherwise lacks and loses nothing a reader was entitled to. Faces 1 and 2 both run three `and`s or a doubled `stands` through one breath, and face 3's cleft promises a single thing found and then produces a second one in the next sentence, which is audible as well as unlawful. Re-landing these three on the granary would give the variant the varied close its angle wants.
