### THE SELECTOR'S RULINGS AND VETOES

I have read the three-times-running veto as binding the attribution FRAME, not the verb *say*, and have varied the frame instead: attribution first, attribution inside, place first, time first, object fronted. Recorded as a reading, not a finding.
