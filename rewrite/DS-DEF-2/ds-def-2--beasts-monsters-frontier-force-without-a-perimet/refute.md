**The collapse: one construction repeated — the attribution.** Twenty-seven of the thirty-three faces carry
their source on the verb *say/says*, and the selector's own veto ("the same attribution verb three times
running") is breached in every variant: variant 1 runs five straight (f1–f5), variant 2 runs five straight
(f4–f8), variant 3 runs six straight (f6–f11) and four before them (f1–f4). The draft's own scan claims
"no run of three" — that claim is true only of the narrower string *The X says* fronted, and the brief's
veto is on the VERB. The result is that a reader taking the pool as a body hears one frame, and the mix
ruling 29 wants is carried by only six departures (three place-fronted, two attribution-mid, one
"By the guilds' account", plus *calls* / *hears* / *an account is kept*).

**Speakers: eleven**, and that is the pool's strength — well past the three-speaker floor, with every seated
source carrying a face in every variant and no source left without one. This is NOT a camera with no
speaker and NOT a pool without stake: the guilds are not in the room, the watch has no say in either place
it is wanted, the garrison has a roof and no ground, the court has a custom written down nowhere, the
elders' last house feeds strangers for nothing. The nouns are various, the faces are not permutations of
each other, and the pool is plainly richer than the three shipped rows it replaces (which are abstract, and
one of which carries a rate — "costs it something every season" — that this rewrite would refuse).

**So the DULL is narrow and cheap to cure**: vary the attribution verbs and frames without touching a
single claim — *the hall holds that*, *the guilds put it that*, *at the tavern the account is*, *the
register's view is*, the compound joins ruling 23 licenses, and the object-fronted opener the pool already
uses twice. No content needs to move.
