### R-1 · v2f9 (register) — the prior WITHHELD is LIFTED

> "a stranger is owed a roof and a hearing, and the town gives the roof"

Withheld last round on the implicature that the hearing is NOT given, against the required `Town hall`'s
`Dispute arbitration` (p 0.8). Under the re-cut the test is what would have to be FALSE for the sentence to
be true, and the answer is nothing: the sentence asserts only that the roof is given. The shortfall is a

outright that a stranger "may bring a complaint and be heard", so the page resolves the implicature on the
page; and the register's grievance with the town is exactly the stake the voice ruling asks a source to
carry. **PASS.**
