## A. THE CARD'S CLAUSES, LETTERED (every licence cited in section C is one of these)

- **K1** — `reads: beastsRowSituation(family, perimeter, force)` × `predicate: === frontier country, force without a perimeter` × `may claim: that the reader selects the row ... as a STANDING fact of the record`. ONE read whose value is a row with three components, selected only where all three hold. The three components are **R1** the family `frontier` (the country's middle monster-and-raider tier, the corpus's own word, measured never defaulted), **R2** the force `garrison || militia` present on the live roster, **R3** the perimeter absent (the `walls` bucket empty). Under ADDENDUM 8 (1) and ADDENDUM 12 R-v the reads of a key are ONE keyed condition, never "a second fact", so stating all three is not an added claim (A6); under the SKELETON RULE every face states all three.
- **K2** — `bag: {band: RESERVED, route: proper, settlement: proper}` · `FILLED at this block's call sites: {settlement}`. Licenses the `{settlement}` token and nothing else; `{band}` and `{route}` are written nowhere.
- **K3** — `angle: ledger street visitor`. Licenses the three stances the block's own palette defines: `[ledger]` the clerk's view; `[visitor]` what a stranger notices first, without being told; `[street]` the town's own talk about its condition.
- **K4** — `audience: player (no mark)`; `covert: no`. No face carries a `dm-only` mark.
- **K5** — `source: muster · standing LICENSED`, read against §24's ceiling (one citation per unit, on a licensed holder, only for one of S3's three reasons). The muster holds the FORCE component only; none of S3's three reasons obtains on this read; so **the lawful citation count on every wording of this pool is ZERO**, and W24 bars every record noun besides. No face names a roll, a book, a register or a count.
- **KN** — `may NOT: a count, a cause, a season, a future, a standpoint, a second fact, another civic object of the class wall`, plus the always-refused columns (a totality over persons; an exemption from a duty; a named character and that character's fate; a theological claim).
