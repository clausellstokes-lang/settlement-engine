   - `[face]` `[garrison]` The garrison has it that the town argues about which way to send it and finishes the argument after it has gone.
   - `[face]` `[court]` The court says the quarrels that reach it are over which neighbour was owed a walk and which one owed it, and nobody has yet asked it where the owing stops.

## THE FIVE TARGETS — what was cured, and how

**1. variant 2 face 0, the `[visitor]` SPINE — FAIL, floor 1, the `Barracks` row.** CURED by the first of
the two cures the refuter offered: the second sentence is cut, and the replacement is the refuter's own
named safe line of the same shape. The spine now reads *Nobody arriving here is asked their business, and
nobody here is set to ask.* The lost sentence claimed the force lives among the houses, which the resolved
`Barracks` row denies at the two TOWN members of the preimage; nothing in the new line places the force
anywhere, so the collision is gone at all three tiers. `{settlement}` did not have to move, because the
second cure (taking packet 2's variant-1 spine) was the one that would have moved it, and it was not taken:
spine 1 still carries the pool's one `{settlement}`. The surviving first sentence is the card's own blessed
seeing (section (7): *nobody was asked anything on the way in*), and the added clause is scoped to the
ASSIGNMENT of a task rather than to a gate, which is the same scoping the refuter passed on variant 2's
garrison face (*nobody has ever given it the task of knowing who is in the place*). No gate is asserted and
none is denied, so correction (c) is honoured in both directions.

**2. variant 2 face 3, the guilds — FAIL, floor 1, ruling 35 (the sibling rung).** CURED by hinging the
loss on the absence, which is the refuter's cure verbatim in both of its halves: *the road begins where the
houses stop* and *lost within sight of the last house*. On `frontier, credible deterrence` the houses do not
stop where the road begins, because a kept perimeter stops first, and a loss inside sight of the last house
is a loss inside the walled rung's line. The face can no longer be drawn word for word on the sibling. The
attribution also moves off the fronted *By the guilds' account* onto *By the guilds' reckoning*, which is
the source's own card interest (what going out costs them against what staying in would).

**3. variant 2 face 6, the tavern — FAIL, floor 2, F2-01 (a count in a word).** CURED by the refuter's
second offered form, *told last and told by everybody*: the shape of the disagreement is kept exactly (the
watch says first, the tavern says last) and the number is gone. No read on this tab hands a count of
tellings, and none is now claimed. *Last of anybody in the town* is an ordinal within one propagation,
which the refuter passed at variant 1 face 1 (*the houses furthest out hear it last*), and not a count.

**4. variant 2 face 8, the market — FAIL, floor 1, ruling 35.** CURED by turning the face on the absence,
which is the refuter's cure: *nobody has told the stalls who he is before he reaches them*. On the walled
rung somebody HAS placed him first, at the point this key leaves empty, so the sentence is now unavailable
there. The customer clause survives as the consequence of the absence rather than as the claim itself, and
it keeps the pair's `view` against the hall, whose indifference is now the other side of the same silence.

**5. variant 2 face 11, the court — FAIL, floor 1, ruling 35.** CURED by giving the staying a reason this
key owns, as the cure asks: he would be staying *in a place where nothing stopped him coming in and nothing
would keep him*. The court's own service assertion (`Dispute arbitration`, p 0.8, required at town) is
unchanged and still asserted rather than denied; what changed is that the cost of using the court is now
priced against a town with no hold on anybody, which is this key's fact and is false on the walled rung.
*Would keep* is a conditional, which ruling 33 licenses.

## THE DULL — what was done to the pool, and what was not

**The collapse the refuter named was the attribution frame, not the content**, so no claim moved outside the
five targets above. Twenty-seven of thirty-three faces carried their source on *say/says*; eleven of
thirty-three do now, and the verb appears at most twice running anywhere in the pool.

**The frames now in use**, spread so that no attribution verb and no frame CLASS runs three deep in any
variant: the nominal frames (*the account is*, *the talk is*, *the position is*, *a stranger's report is*,
*the stallholders' account is*), the verbal alternatives (*has it that*, *puts it that*, *holds that*,
*reckon that*, *have it that*, *calls*, *hears*, *reports*), the attribution fronted (*By the guilds'
reckoning*), the place fronted (*At the tavern*, *At the market*, *At the church*), the object fronted with
the attribution mid (three, unchanged from the draft, which is where the pool's existing variety lived), and
the impersonal (*it is held*, *an account is kept*). Run check, variant by variant: variant 1 carries four
*say/says* faces with one adjacent pair (faces 9 and 10), variant 2 three with no two adjacent, variant 3
four with one adjacent pair (faces 2 and 3). Nowhere three, and the veto is on the verb and not on the
fronted string.

**Eleven seated speakers are preserved**, every one of them carrying a face in every variant, which is the
pool's strength and was never the fault. The stakes the refuter named as real are all still on the page: the
guilds not in the room, the watch with no say in either place it is wanted, the garrison with a roof and no
ground, the court with a custom written down nowhere, the elders' last house feeding strangers for nothing.
Thirty-three faces, thirty-three different sentences, no permutations.

**THE TWO NON-VERDICT GAPS, BOTH NOW SPENT.** The refuter and the selector both reported these as gaps
rather than faults, and the whole-pool rewrite was the occasion to fill them at no cost to a claim.

- **A `reinforce` pair now exists**, at variant 1, between the stranger and the muster. It is deliberately
  placed on the pool's two UNIVERSAL sources, so it renders on all twenty-two towns, which answers wiring
  row 3 as well: the other four pairs are town-only on both halves or lose a half to the source filter at
  town, and this one cannot. The two halves are different facts pointing the same way, not one fact twice:
  the traveller was lodged in the outermost house and nobody in it thought the position remarkable, and the
  muster reckons the outermost households are the ones that turn out. NOT marked `joinable` — the
  reinforce conjunction would make one sentence of two long clauses, so this pair stands as two sentences.
  Variant 1's pairs are renumbered accordingly: pair 1 is the new reinforce, pair 2 is the hall and the
  guilds, unchanged in claim.
- **A face now closes on a question left hanging** (ruling 34's unspent class), at variant 3, the court:
  *nobody has yet asked it where the owing stops.* The question is left open on the page without a mark,
  which keeps the notebook's device off the fair copy; the court is not made to answer it, and what the
  custom obliges remains, as variant 1's court face says, written down nowhere.

**What the DULL cure did NOT do**: no face changed source, no face changed variant, no face gained or lost a
claim, no pair changed its kind except by the addition above, no spine changed except the cured one, and the
`{settlement}` count is unchanged at one unit and no face.

## THE CURER'S OWN SCAN

- **The floors.** No line in any costume; no gate asserted and none denied. No body word off its seat: *the
  muster* everywhere, *the elders* below town, *the register* at village and town, the seven town bodies on
  town-tagged faces only, no militia at town, no garrison below town, no watch below town. No named record
  cited anywhere, so the pool stays under the provenance ceiling the shipped rows set. No count, date,
  duration, rate, magnitude or age. No wage, arrears or late purse on the force (V-09). No covert tag, which
  section (2c) refuses on this pool. No named office and no minted name. No tier word.
- **The mechanical rules.** No em dash, no exclamation mark, no digit, no semicolon, no contraction. No
  *will* and no *shall*: the two conditionals in the pool (*if he is still here when the word comes*,
  *nothing would keep him*) are ruling 33's licensed form. `{settlement}` in spine 1 only and in no face.
- **The tells.** No which-clause closer, no summarising close, no antithesis pair, no reassurance, no clever
  last beat explaining the beat before it. No figure of speech and no inanimate thing acting with intent. No
  record vouching for itself and no self-citation: nothing says *the survey*, *this office* or *the record*.
  No source told apart by a signature word, and no clerk speaks in the first person anywhere.
- **The new clauses, checked one at a time.** *nobody here is set to ask* is scoped to an assignment and
  names no facility. *the road begins where the houses stop* is the key's own fact about where the
  settlement ends. *within sight of the last house* is a relation and not a measure, and it is the
  refuter's own wording. *last of anybody in the town* is an ordinal, not a count. *nobody has told them who
  a stranger is before he reaches the stall* denies no required service, since no service on this preimage
  places an arrival. *nothing stopped him coming in and nothing would keep him* states the key's absence
  twice over without naming a work. *nobody has yet asked it where the owing stops* leaves a matter open and
  settles nothing.
- **The archiver's hand.** The three spines still take three shapes and three carriers: the bare fact
  (variant 1), the bare statement of what happens to an arrival (variant 2, now one sentence and shorter
  than what it replaces, which the restraint rule prefers), and the public (variant 3). Every face names its
  source. Every bare statement is a fact the engine holds.
