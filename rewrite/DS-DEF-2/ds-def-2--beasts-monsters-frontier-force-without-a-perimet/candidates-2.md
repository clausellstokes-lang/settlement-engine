# CANDIDATES — writer 2 (Opus 5 seat)

Block DS-DEF-2 · pool `Beasts & Monsters: frontier, force without a perimeter`

**LENS:** THE SOCIAL POSITION (who pays, who is owed, who is resented; a source's stake shows in what it calls a fact — Kay, Martin) and THE WITHHELD REASON (a fact set down plainly and the reason not given — Wolfe).

**VOICE:** the master archiver's hand. A recorded fact stands bare; every account names its source through the class word, written so any role of that source could stand in the attribution when car 18l lands.

## THE WRITER'S OWN SCAN (what this packet does and does not do)

- **No wall, line, perimeter, circuit, rampart, stockade, ditch or berm anywhere** (F1-07). The absence is written as a standing condition, never as a history (F2-03, F2-04).
- **The gate is not written in either direction** (card correction (c)): no gate, gatehouse, bar, checkpoint, toll, entry inspection, closing at dusk — and no denial of one either. The `gate` source is not used; it seats nobody on this pool. The stranger's arrival is carried by *nobody asked him anything*, which the card blesses (section (7), the public bullet), and never by a missing gate.
- **No named record is cited anywhere.** The shipped rows of this pool carry no citation, so no toll book, no muster roll, no parish register, no accounts (F1-24, and the provenance-ceiling hazard). The register speaks; it is never cited.
- **No body word off its seat.** `the muster` and `the guard` are the class words and run everywhere; `hall`, `tavern`, `guild`, `watch`, `garrison`, `market`, `court` faces are tagged and seat only at town; `elders` below town; `register` at village and town. The seat is named on every conditional row below.
- **No money quarrel over the force** (V-09): no wage, pay, arrears or late purse is predicated of the muster on any face. The currency of this packet is WORK, OBLIGATION, DISTANCE and WHOSE GROUND — which is what "who pays" means on a pool whose force is a citizen muster on twenty of its twenty-two towns.
- No count, date, duration, rate or magnitude; no `will` or `shall`; no em dash, exclamation mark, digit, semicolon or contraction; no named office as subject or attribution; no `{settlement}` in any face (it stands in one spine only, variant 2); no face settles the force's competence or makes the town's unsafety a fact (F1-107).
- Duratives used: *no line has ever been drawn round the place* (the key's own read, ruling 11a) and the public's *has seen*. Nothing elapses over the live roster.
