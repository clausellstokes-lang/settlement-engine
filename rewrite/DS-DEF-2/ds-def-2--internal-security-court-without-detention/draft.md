## THE POOL'S CHOSEN ROWS

1. `[ledger]` {settlement} keeps a court and no room to hold anybody in. What the law here can take from a man is his money or his place in the town.
   - `[face]` `[watch · pair 1 · disagree]` The watch says what it comes across after dark is dealt with after dark, there being nowhere to leave a man until morning.
   - `[face]` `[court · pair 1 · disagree]` The court says it hears in the daylight what the watch settled in the dark, and that nobody asks it first.
   - `[face]` `[hall]` The hall says the purse does not run to a room and never has. It does not say who asked for one.
   - `[face]` `[market]` At the market a stallholder says a short weight goes before a magistrate and comes back as a sum, and that the scales it was weighed on stay on the stall.
   - `[face]` `[register]` A sexton holds that the parish has a place for a man once he is dead and the town has none for him while he is alive.
   - `[face]` `[guild]` A guild factor says a sum is paid out of a man's takings and forgotten, and that the mark taken off his work is not.
   - `[face]` `[tavern]` At the tavern they say a man with a house here pays and stays, and a man without one goes.
   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A pedlar says there is nothing here to take from a man who owns nothing in the town, and that the town has only the one thing left to do with him.
   - `[face]` `[gate]` At the gate they say they are given a man and a direction and no reason for either.
   - `[face]` `[garrison]` A soldier says the wall is the garrison's business and the people walked out past it are not.
2. `[street]` Everyone in the town has seen a man brought in at one door and walk out by the same one, and the town takes it that nothing much was decided.
   - `[face]` `[tavern · pair 2 · disagree]` At the tavern they say a man who walks out the same day was never going to be kept, whoever heard him.
   - `[face]` `[court · pair 2 · disagree]` The court says a man walks out because there is nowhere to walk him to, and not because of what was decided.
   - `[face]` `[gate]` At the gate they say they are the last part of the town a man sees and the only part that was told to expect him.
   - `[face]` `[watch]` The watch says it is asked why a man is out again and has no answer to give that anybody takes.
   - `[face]` `[market]` At the market they say a man let go is at the stalls again, and the stall he took from is expected to serve him.
   - `[face]` `[hall]` The hall says a man heard here goes back to his own street afterwards, and that what his street does with him is nobody's business at the hall.
   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A pedlar's word is that the town names a sum he cannot find and then settles for the road, and that the road is the one he came in by.
3. `[unfolding]` A matter is heard here and nothing is built to keep the person it is heard about. A household that wants a man kept away from it does the keeping itself.
   - `[face]` `[watch]` One of the night patrol says the households that want a man kept off do their own keeping, and that the patrol knows which doors those are.
   - `[face]` `[hall]` The hall says the same fine is asked of a man with a shop and a man with a bundle, and that this is what fairness means here.
   - `[face]` `[market]` At the market a stallholder says a stall keeps its own eye on a man it has had trouble with. The eye costs the stall a pair of hands.
   - `[face]` `[court]` The court says it names what is owed and does not ask whether it can be got.
   - `[face]` `[tavern]` The man a household wants kept off drinks at the same table as everybody else, a carter at the tavern says.
   - `[face]` `[guild]` The guilds say a fine on a master is worked off by his journeymen, and that nobody hearing the matter knows it.
   - `[face]` `[garrison · pair 3 · reinforce]` A soldier says the garrison is not asked about any of it until somebody runs. <!-- joinable -->
   - `[face]` `[gate · pair 3 · reinforce]` At the gate they say the ones who run go out with the carts before first light. <!-- joinable -->

### THE SPINES

- spine 1 | `[ledger]` | packet 1 (Opus 5) | Won on the inversion. The lane's already-landed sibling rung opens "{settlement} keeps a watch that takes a person up and a place where the matter is heard. It keeps a room with a lock on the outside of its door." This spine turns that sentence over on the one flag that differs, which is the whole of what the key fixes, and its second sentence is a pseudo-cleft rather than another plain subject. Packet 2's spine 1 put the one purse in the spine, which would have made the hall's face a restatement; the purse is better spent on a source with a stake in it.
- spine 2 | `[street]` | packet 2 (Opus 5) | Both writers carried this spine on the public and agreed almost word for word. Packet 2's won on its second half: "nothing much was decided" is a perception the town can be wrong about, which is ruling 28's whole instrument, where packet 1's "the matter was dealt with" closes it. Packet 1's also spent a time of day the face did not need.
- spine 3 | `[unfolding]` | packet 1 (Opus 5) | Packet 2's spine 3 is the better sentence and was REFUSED for a dangling demonstrative: "no lock has ever been wanted on the outside of **that door**" has no antecedent, and a spine that costs a second reading fails the mix's first test before any variety is bought. Packet 1's also gives the pool a THIRD ground — who does the keeping instead — where packet 2's spine 3 (the fine and the road) repeats variant 1's. Cost of the choice, recorded: packet 2's best face in the whole block, `[watch]` "it can tell by a man's coat which of the two he gets, and that it is not the watch that decides it", dies with it, because "which of the two" has no antecedent under this spine. It was the pool's strongest single line and it is refused on referent alone.

### THE ASSEMBLY — what was decided at the pool grain and not at the face

- **The pair numbering is continuous across the pool, not per variant.** Both writers numbered from 1 inside each variant, which would have put two `pair 1` tags in one pool. The lane's already-landed sibling pool numbers 1 and 2 in variant 2 and 3 in variant 3, so the tags were renumbered to that convention: pair 1 (V1), pair 2 (V2), pair 3 (V3). The SOURCE and the KIND of every kept tag are the writers' own and unchanged.
- **The court can speak three times in the pool and three pairs wanted it.** Watch/court, tavern/court and guild/court were all offered. Two were kept, in different variants; the guild/court disagreement — the card's own "best two-source disagreement" — was dropped AS A PAIR and both its parties still speak, the guild in variant 1 on the mark it can take off a man's work and the court in variant 3 on what it names and does not ask. The disagreement is on the page; the bracket is not.
- **Three grounds, one per variant, and no two variants arguing the same thing.** V1 the purse and the two sanctions; V2 the man who walks back out; V3 who does the keeping instead. That is what decided spine 3 against the better sentence: packet 2's spine 3 (the fine and the road) would have made V3 a second V1.
- **Rations.** ONE physical particular in the pool (ruling 31): the scales on the stall. Everything else that named an object — the basket, the pack, the beast, the family's table, the coat — was refused for it, the coat most painfully. ONE consequence face per variant (ruling 32): V1 none, V2 the stall, V3 the pair of hands; read strictly as a face whose SECOND SENTENCE lands the cost, which is why variant 3's guild face (the bill inside one sentence) is not counted against it. ONE observed face and TWO weighings in the pool, both under the held-back heading below.
- **Verb spread and the DULL measure.** Both packets lean hard on "X says A, and that B"; the first refuter's finding was a pool of good lines that was dull for its assembly, so faces were ordered so that no two adjacent ones share an attribution frame and no three in a row open in the same class, and several otherwise lawful candidates were dropped for being a fifth or sixth join of that shape. The kept pool runs place-first, subject-first and attribution-last with two two-sentence faces.
- **Antithesis closes.** Two in variant 1 (the tavern's pays-and-stays, the register's dead-and-alive). Both have concrete contents on both sides, which is the form the pack licenses, and they are the only two in the pool.
- **Both packets share my seat.** Packet 1 and packet 2 are both Opus 5. The tells they share — the "and that" join, the disclaiming source, the durative reached for without checking the field under it — were judged as my own hand and cut harder for it. Three garrison faces all saying "not our business" and three register faces all saying "the household, not the town" are the clearest instance.
