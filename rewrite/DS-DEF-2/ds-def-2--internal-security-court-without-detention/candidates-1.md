  printed sibling sentence (§R-1); the machinery of either does not.

  `Dispute arbitration`, commercial and civil) and no denial of the criminal court at city or metropolis.
- **No named record anywhere in this packet.** The shipped rows cite none, so under the brief's citation
  rule I spent none: no court book, no accounts, no sexton's register, no toll book, no muster roll. The
  `register` source speaks; it never cites its book. I also avoided every provenance-shaped phrase,
  `from the road` included (the measured batch-3 ceiling trap).
- No elders (the `elders` kind resolves on nothing required at any preimage tier) and no muster/militia
  (F1-26: a citizen militia beside a required watch is a contradiction in the catalogue's own terms).
- No named offices as subject or attribution: no mayor, no guard captain, no city watch chief, no high
  priest, no chief magistrate. "A magistrate" is the `Dispute arbitration` service's own word and stands.
- Generic present throughout: a source's OPINION is free, a source's MEMORY is not, so no face narrates a
  particular past occurrence (F2-04). The duratives that appear run over FROZEN reads only
  (`hasPrison`, `hasCourtSystem`, `economicGates`, ruling 11b) — "has never paid for a lock",
  "no bar has been wanted on one".
- No count, date, term, season or rate; no digit, em dash, semicolon, exclamation mark or contraction;
  no `will` or `shall` (ruling 33); no `{settlement}` in any face sub-row (ruling 12 — it stands in ONE

- `[watch · compromised]` and `[hall · compromised]` are REFUSED here (§2c marks neither on this pool).
