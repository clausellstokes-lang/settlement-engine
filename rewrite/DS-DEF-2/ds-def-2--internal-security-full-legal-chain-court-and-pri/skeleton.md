### 1.5 ⭐ THE PREIMAGE — the range of towns this key selects

A boolean-AND key fires wherever both flags are true and on nothing else about the town. That is:

### 1.8 ⭐ WHERE THE FLAVOUR IS (vid 1)

- **The record prices release, and it says so in a field nobody has used.** `Small prison/stocks` carries the service **`Fine payment`: "Pay fines to secure release from detention"**, on at p 0.9, sitting beside *Holding cells*: "Short-term detention **pending trial or payment of fines**" (`institutionServices.js:1635-1639`). So on the commonest row of this pool's range the gaol is not an end state at all: it is a room a person waits in until either a decision or a sum arrives, and the record holds both doors. **That is the `[ledger]` angle's own material and it has never been written.** On the ground: a sum entered against a name rather than a sentence; two ways out of one room, one of them purchasable; the cheapest offences resolved without anyone deciding anything.
- **Confinement and open shame are ONE institution, and the catalogue put them in one breath.** The vocabulary row reads *"Holding cells and public stocks for the town's offenders. **Confinement and open shame in one.**"* and the services list *Public punishment*: "Stocks and pillory for public shaming and minor offenses" (`institutionVocabulary.js:285`; `institutionServices.js:1637`). So the building has an inside and an outside, and the outside faces whoever walks past. A stranger's first knowledge of the town's law is a fixture in public, not a door. Nothing in the record denies the small facts around that — who stops to look and who does not, whether the fixture is in use or standing empty — and the silence is the writer's.
- **One purse pays the hall, the cells and the watch together, and the engine names it in plain English.** `internalUpkeepMult` runs over *"watch wages, court and gaol funding"* with a floor of 0.65 (`defenseGenerator.js:246-254`). So the clerk's true subject is a single recurring charge that keeps three different things from failing, and it can only ever be short across all three at once. The licensed extreme is short, late or thin, never none. **This is the most inviting F4-03 site on the row and also its best unused fact.**
- **The cells at the top of the range are not the town's, and they hold people who were never accused of anything ordinary.** `Large prison` = "Debtors, criminals, political prisoners"; `Massive prison` = a **state** complex holding "political prisoners, debtors, convicted criminals **held separately**" (`institutionalCatalog.js:2282-2288`, `:2514-2520`). A debt and a dissent and a theft under one roof, and at metropolis three different roofs inside one wall, under somebody else's warrant. That is a STANDING arrangement, not a cause and not a history, and it is licensed wherever the face does not fix the row the town actually holds.

### 1.9 What would make the rewrite of vid 1 a regression

Vid 1 not first, or not `[ledger]`, or its slot set not `{settlement}` exactly once, or fewer than four faces, or no longer the pool's canonical index-zero line. Any face that loses the JOINT — a place where something is decided and a place where it is answered — because that pairing is the pool's discriminating claim and without it the face is claim-identical to `court without detention`'s or `detention without process`'s. Any face asserting a criminal trial, a sentence, a bench, a judge or a gallows; any face asserting an arresting body, a watch or a guard. Any face that calls the law mild, impartial, settled or not a threat as a standing fact. Any face carrying a which-clause, an em dash, a digit, a count, a date, a duration or a rate. Any face that cites a record keeper — the card resolves none. Any face that repeats DS-GEN-17's *"a court and a place to hold people"* or the tab's own *"Arrest, prosecute, detain"*. Any face that opens on the `{settlement}` proper slot, or closes on a set-up rather than a standing fact.
