## E. The archiver's notebook — dm-only candidates (ruling 17)

Third person throughout, two sentences at most, the notebook's devices only. Shades and feelings spread. Each points TOWARD a covert fact the card marks for this pool and never away from it, and each names the thing it circles so a successor picks it up cold.

- *conjecture · curiosity* · The men on the walk after dark are not put there by the hall, or not only by it: whoever does the putting is in no account here, and it would be worth knowing who.
- *accusatory · clarity* · The hearing takes what is brought and does not ask after what was not brought. If somebody is choosing what is brought, then that somebody is the law here and the hall is not it.
- *unsure · unease* · The hall answers about the purse without being asked twice, and the answer does not vary… a thing said the same way every time was decided on before anybody asked.
- *hearsay · worry* · It is said at the tavern that a man with a friend in the hall is not kept waiting, and the tavern would say that. If it holds, the waiting is a measure of standing and of nothing else.
- *conjecture · eagerness* · What the walk brings in and what the hall hears are not the same thing, and nobody here is placed to see both… whoever is placed to see both is worth finding.
- *conjecture · surprise* · The room that locks is kept shut on nobody, and kept shut all the same: somebody is paying to keep an empty room ready, and nothing here says who told them to.

## F. Hazards this writer steered around, named so the selector knows they were not missed

- **No fine, anywhere on a face.** `Fine payment` and `Public punishment` belong to the town's `Small prison/stocks` row and to nothing else; a fine face is structurally the stocks trap (§8 consequence (a)) and draws on the city and the metropolis, where `Large prison` carries no such service.
- **No stocks, pillory or public shaming**, for the same reason.
- **No served sentence, term, or long holding**: the town's `Holding cells` is printed short-term, pending trial or a fine.
- **No cited court record and no gaol book.** The required `Multiple courthouses` carries no service menu and seats no `court` holder kind, and `HOLDER_KINDS` has no prison kind at all. Nothing here says "the court's book", "what was entered", "a warrant", "a filed report", "a list of the held".
- **No parish register cited as a record**, either: `Register of the dead` resolves at town and `Central register` at metropolis, but the city's `Burial grounds and charnel house` carries neither, so the sexton speaks and never produces a book.
- **No gallows, gibbet, hanging or executioner**, which the estate does not carry anywhere. The register faces are written so that "the law is finished with him" cannot be read as a capital sentence.
- **No split of the purse's direction.** Every purse face is about DEGREE — short, late, thin — never the court kept while the gaol starves, and never a total collapse (F4-04). The watch's half of pair 1 says what reaches it is short and late, not that it is paid last.
- **No totality of safety in either direction.** No face says the law holds, that crime is answered or that the chain works (which would contradict a `Dangerous` band printed beside it), and none says the law is a dead letter (which would contradict `Very Safe`). The public spine states the town's PERCEPTION, which is the safe way to carry the key's own content.
- **No named office anywhere**: a clerk in the hall, one of the night patrol, a magistrate, the sexton, the traders, a guild factor, the soldiers, a drover. Never the mayor, the guard captain, the city watch chief, the chief magistrate, the priest. No face makes "whoever holds the keys" its SUBJECT.
- **No elders and no crown's assessor**: the `elders` kind seats nothing at any preimage tier, and the engine has no typed crown collector.
- **No militia, mercenary company, charter hall or hospital building** — the four genuinely open rosters.
- **No "the garrison relieves the watch" at town**, and no denial of the garrison, the walls or the gates at city or metropolis. The garrison faces are about what it is NOT asked to do, which is true at every tier its row resolves on.
- **No north-European court furniture**: no assize, no shire, no borough, no county, no churchyard, no market green, no thatch. The furniture is a bench, a door, a lock, a name written down, a purse.
- **No placement the data states at one tier only**: nothing "in the square", nothing sited inside or outside a wall, and the spine says "somewhere else in the town" rather than joining the two rooms in one building.
- **No neighbouring rung**: every face carries the HOLDING or the join, so none would sit as comfortably on `court without detention` or `detention without process`.
