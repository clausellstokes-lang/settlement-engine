## THE TWO FAILS

### FAIL 1 — variant 1, face 4, `[guild]`

**The face:** "the trade pays for a court it does not use"

**The field that denies it:** `src/data/institutionServices.js:1625` — `Town hall` → `"Dispute arbitration": { on: true, p: 0.8, desc: "Bring commercial and civil disputes before a magistrate." }` — carried onto this card at card.md:43 as a §2b non-deniable ("⛔ A SERVICE AT OR ABOVE THE BAR IS A THING THE TOWN'S OWN MODEL SAYS THIS BODY DOES: denying it is floor 1, whatever the face is otherwise about").

**Which of the two is the record:** the service row. It is a data row in the shipped catalogue, not engine prose and not a generation-time projection, so this is a contradiction and not a wiring seam.

**Why it bites exactly here.** The guild is seated at TOWN and nowhere else (`Craft guilds (5-15)` is required at town; no guild row is required at city or metropolis — card §7). Town is precisely the tier where the court half of the key is satisfied by the required `Town hall` and where the hall's ONE instantiated legal procedure is *commercial and civil* arbitration. So the face draws only on the tier whose printed court procedure is the trade's own business, and has a guild factor say the trade does not use it. Under §V.0 floor 1's quantifier — a face is lawful iff no field in the tab's same-page read set, **at any value the keys admit**, denies it — `Dispute arbitration` on (its default, p 0.8) is a value the keys admit, and there it is the face's flat opposite spoken by the constituency the service names.

**The cure** (keeps the greed, keeps the one purse, lands on the detention this pool actually fixes):
> `[face]` `[guild]` A guild factor says the trade brings the hall its quarrels and the cells nobody, and pays for both.

### FAIL 2 — variant 2, face 10, `[gate]`

**The face:** "a person is stopped there before the watch or the hearing ever sees him"

**The field that denies it:** `src/data/institutionServices.js:1324` — `Town watch` → `"Gate duty": { on: true, p: 0.8, desc: "Check travelers entering and leaving. Note unusual visitors." }` — card.md:44, and transcribed again in card §7's TOWN watch row.

**Which of the two is the record:** the service row.

**Why it bites.** At TOWN the gate is manned by the watch: `Gate duty` is the `Town watch`'s own instantiated service and its text is the very act this face describes. A gate-sourced face draws wherever a gate row seats it, and `hasGates` is OPEN at town (it fires on `gates · town walls · city walls · massive walls · palisade`), so this face draws on town-tier towns — where the required `Town watch` stands with gate duty on. There, "before the watch ever sees him" is the service's negation. At city and metropolis the face is fine, because `City walls and gates` → `Gate control` and `Professional city watch` are two rows and the city watch carries no gate duty; but a face must stand at every value the keys admit.

**The cure** (one word dropped; the precedence claim and the quarrel with the hall both survive):
> `[face]` `[gate]` Those who keep the gate say a person is stopped there before the hearing ever sees him, and that the town's law begins at the gate whatever the hall says.

## THE §2d RULING (recorded so the chair can veto it)

§2d and card §7 make a face that "reads as a neighbouring rung" a floor-1 finding, and warn that `hasCourtSystem` is automatic on every preimage town so any face whose whole content is "there is a court here" is that finding by construction. Read at its widest, that charge would take roughly a third of this pool — every face whose content is the court, the hearing or the waiting, and which would sit as comfortably on `Internal Security: court without detention`.

**I did not charge them, and this is the ground.** The re-cut strikes "the same claim set on every face" by name and rules that a face need not restate its key at all — twelve faces are twelve things a person could notice in a town where the key holds. Applied per-face at its widest, §2d would reinstate exactly the struck contract. I therefore read §2d as the card's own words have it: the finding is the face whose **whole content is the bare existence claim**, and the rung-test otherwise binds at the POOL. No face in this pool is the bare existence claim, and the pool carries the detention in the spine and in nine faces (v1 2, v1 6, v2 1, v2 6→cells implied, v3 5, v3 9, plus the observed and both v1/v2 public candidates), so the pool is not the neighbouring rung. **The chair may take the wider reading; if so, the faces to look at first are v1 5, v2 4, v2 5, v3 3 and v3 4.**

## CRAFT — POOL GRAIN: **DULL**

**The collapse: ONE CONSTRUCTION, measured.** Nineteen of the twenty-eight faces are the identical mould — [attribution] + [claim] + "and (that)" + [second claim]:

> v1 1, 5, 6, 9 · v2 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 · v3 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (and v2 4's compound verb is the same hinge in miniature).

And the mould reaches **every grain of the pool, not only the faces**: spine 1 ("…, and a room with a lock"), spine 2 ("…, and the town takes it"), spine 3 ("…, and where the person he names"), both weighings ("…, and it may be that", "…, and it is not settled here"), all three `[public]` candidates ("…, and takes it that" twice, "…, and the town takes it that"), and two of three notebook notes. There is no grain of this pool at which the "and"-hinge is absent.

**Its consequence: the voice's own signature shape never appears.** The archiver is restrained — the fact, and then the stop; "The works are kept. Nobody is paid to stand on them." — two facts, no explanation, the reader does the arithmetic. **Not one face in this pool is two sentences.** The selector records why ("neither writer offered a lawful two-sentence face") and compensates with length, thirteen to thirty-one words. Length is not the same instrument: a long single sentence with an "and" in the middle is the shape the pool already has too much of, so the compensation deepens the collapse instead of breaking it.

**Second collapse, smaller: one object.** The selector rationed the pool to a single physical particular, the room with the lock on the outside of its door, and refused the straw and the bench as a second and a third. That one object then carries spine 1, v1 6, v2 1, v3 5, the observed candidate and two of three public candidates — seven appearances of one noun-cluster in a pool whose card §9 offers five concrete objects (a lock on the outside of a door, a bench where people wait, a fine paid to secure release, a name taken down at the gate, a name read out somewhere else). Three of those five are unused. The ration was set tighter than the record required.

**What is NOT wrong, and the chair should weigh it.** Ten speakers, not three. The stakes are real and each is drawn from a field: the hall's one purse, the watch's grievance with the wage, the guilds' private settling, the gate's claim of precedence, the garrison's flat indifference, the stranger's two confident answers. Two genuine disagree pairs, one of them (v3 1/2 — one man waiting, two causes) the best unit in the pool. No attribution verb three times running. The F1-24 trap that the card calls this pool's signature finding is stepped around everywhere: not one face cites a court book, a gaol roll or a warrant. Against the shipped corpus — three sentences, no faces, no speakers — this is not duller by any measure, and I record that explicitly so the DULL verdict is not read as a preference for the shipped rows.

**So the verdict is DULL on syntax alone, and the cure is syntactic.** The content need not move. Break the mould at roughly a third of the faces: two-sentence faces that stop and then state a second fact; an object-fronted or attribution-last face ("The stall stays shut while the matter is heard, the traders say"); and at least one face whose second clause is not hinged on "and". Retire the "and takes it that" frame from two of the three `[public]` candidates, and take the object ration from one to three so the bench, the fine and the name read out somewhere else can carry faces the lock is currently carrying.
