## Variant 2

2. `[street]` Everyone here knows who comes out when the call goes and who stays behind. The town takes the knowing of it for a defence.

   - `[face]` `[muster]` Those who turn out say they pass the same shut doors on the way, and that nothing is said about it afterwards.
   - `[face]` `[elders]` One of the elders says the man who stays in is not spoken to about it, and that his name goes nowhere because there is nowhere to put it.
   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A pedlar says that elsewhere he asks who keeps order at night, and here he is told which door to knock at and whose it is.
   - `[face]` `[tavern]` At the alehouse they say the man who stays in buys for the man who came out, and that he does not always buy. <!-- seat: a drinking house -->
   - `[face]` `[register]` A priest says the people who come out are the same people who come to the rites, and that nobody has asked him to say anything over their turning out. <!-- seat: VILLAGE ONLY -->
   - `[face]` `[market]` At the market a stallholder says that when the call goes he minds his own stall and the one whose man went. <!-- seat: a market row; the bill landed on a trade the roster holds -->
   - `[face]` `[tavern · pair 2 · disagree]` At the alehouse they say the man who always comes out is owed a drink by the man who never does. <!-- joinable -->

## Variant 3

3. `[visitor]` A stranger reaches the first houses without being stopped, and nobody comes out to ask him anything. The people he passes are the ones who would come out if he were trouble.

   - `[face]` `[muster]` One of the muster says a stranger is looked over from the field he walks past and asked nothing at all.
   - `[face]` `[elders]` One of the elders says a stranger is asked his business at the table he is fed at and not before it.
   - `[face]` `[stranger]` A drover says the place looked him over and went back to work, and nobody asked where he meant to sleep.
   - `[face]` `[tavern]` At the alehouse they say a stranger's cup is poured before his name is asked, and that the asking is done by whoever sits nearest him. <!-- seat: a drinking house -->

   - `[face]` `[muster · pair 4 · reinforce]` One of the muster says nobody is set to meet a stranger, and nobody ever has been. <!-- joinable: reads after ", and"; the durative runs over the key's own read -->
