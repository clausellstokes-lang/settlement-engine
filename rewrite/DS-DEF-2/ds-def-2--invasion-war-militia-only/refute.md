## CRAFT — POOL GRAIN: **DULL**

SPEAKERS: 6 (muster · market · stranger · elders · tavern/alehouse · register). Well above the
three-speaker floor, and every one of the six speaks at least twice. Stake is real and in
dispute: the turn, who comes out and who does not, a drink owed, a name with nowhere to go, and
two honest disagree pairs. **The pool is decisively NOT duller than the shipped rows it
replaces** — the shipped three are abstract summaries of the threat assessment; these are
particulars a game master can spend (the tool that comes home wanting an edge, the bier, the
half-tended stall, the door to knock at, the table he is fed at). The negation-landing measure
also passes: 6 of 19 units land on a negation (32%), just inside the one-third bar.

THE COLLAPSE IS THE ATTRIBUTION FRAME, and it is measured, not impressionistic:

- **"says / say" is the attribution verb in 17 of the 20 units.** Only two units vary it ("By a
  stallholder's account", "holds that"), and both are in variant 1.
- **The brief's selector veto — "the same attribution verb three times running" — is tripped
  twice.** Variant 2 runs say · says · says · says · says · says (SIX). Variant 3 runs says ·
  says · says · says (FOUR).
- **"One of the" opens four faces, and twice they are siblings** (v2 f2+f4, v3 f1+f3), which is
  a straight violation of the mechanical rule that reds the build: no face may open on the same
  three words as a sibling.
- **"A priest says" opens the register face in all three variants**; "One of the muster says"
  opens a muster face in all three.
- **The spine echo bites.** A spine co-renders with every face of its variant. Spine 1 lands on
  "their own tools" and v1f1 opens on "the tool he works with"; spine 2's phrase "comes out"
  reappears in v2 f1, f2 and f6. "come(s) out" carries three spines and four faces.
- **Variant 3 is three permutations of one sentence**: "a stranger is looked over…", "a stranger
  is asked his business…", "a stranger is counted a guest…", two of them hinged on "until".
- The withheld-reason device lands twice in the same words: "and does not say who set them" /
  "and does not say who decides".

WHY THIS IS DULL AND NOT A PASS WITH NOTES: the pool's particulars are good, but a game master
scanning twelve renderings reads one frame — *an indefinite source, the verb "says", a
that-clause* — nineteen times out of twenty. That is "one construction repeated" at the pool
grain, and it is the one craft collapse the verdict exists to catch. The cure is cheap and
already present in the pool's own hand: variant 1 carries two non-"says" attributions, and the
sibling pool's landed rows show the range ("The older households say", "The sexton says", "those
who sit to hear a matter say", "the stallholders say" in medial position).

CURE (pool grain): vary the frame in at least one face per variant — the medial attribution
("The grain is the one thing worth coming for, the stallholders say"), the possessive account
("By a stallholder's account"), "holds that", "it is said", "it is not recorded that" — and in
doing so clear the two sibling-opener collisions, which the same edit reaches.
