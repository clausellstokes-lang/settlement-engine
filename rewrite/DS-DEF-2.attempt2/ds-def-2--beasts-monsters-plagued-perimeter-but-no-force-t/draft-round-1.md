**R2 — THE `[unfolding]` SLOPE CANNOT BE WRITTEN AT ALL ON THIS POOL, and the angle survives only as aspect.**
The shipped variant 3 is a slope: the works do less *each season*, the watch *thins*, the thinning *is not being reversed*. Every one of those three is refused by name — a season and a future by the card's `may NOT:` line, the thinning by A6 (a standing configuration field licenses a structural clause and never a historical one) and by MOVE-GRAMMAR §1.2 row 2 (a HISTORY move needs event provenance; this block holds none). The variant is still written, in its own number, under its own tag, as the rules require.

**R3 — THE PLAGUED-COUNTRY LIMB IS CARRIED BY ALL THREE VARIANTS, INCLUDING THE TWO WHOSE SHIPPED SENTENCES DID NOT STATE IT. A judgment, vetoable.**
Shipped variant 1 states only the wall and the absence; shipped variant 3 states only the works, the thinning and the reversal. Under the strictest reading of *carry exactly the licensed claim set of the old sentence*, K1 would be an addition to those two. I read it the other way, for three reasons. (a) The card's `may claim:` line licenses ONE composite predicate, not three severable facts, so a variant of this pool asserts the predicate by being the pool's variant. (b) Dropping K1 from variant 1 would make it claim-identical to the shipped `Invasion & War: walls with NO force` row, which renders on the same page for the same town — the restatement arm A11 exists to catch exactly that, and the plagued country is this pool's discriminating claim (the U9 protection of a clause carrying the pool key's discriminating claim). (c) The same reading was applied on this pool at the previous attempt and drew no claim finding from the gate. **If the chair rules the narrow way, variants 1 and 3 lose their K1 limb and each becomes a two-limb sentence**; the wordings are trivially cut back and I would rather be told than guess.

**R4 — FOUR BAND FLOORS ARE STRUCTURALLY UNREACHABLE ON A ONE-OR-TWO-SENTENCE UNIT, and are reported rather than chased.**
