**`Beasts & Monsters`: `frontier`, force without a perimeter**
1. `[ledger]` On an open frontier, {settlement} puts its defense in people under arms and has no wall at all.
   - `[face]` The country at {settlement} is frontier, and the town stands in it without a perimeter, on the strength of the people it keeps under arms.
   - `[face]` Frontier country lies about {settlement} with nothing walled against it, and the town keeps its people under arms in the open.
   - `[face]` Armed people are kept at {settlement} against frontier country, and no wall stands between.
2. `[visitor]` A stranger walks into {settlement} without passing a perimeter and finds armed people keeping the town.
   - `[face]` What a stranger finds at {settlement} is a body of people under arms, and a town that no perimeter encloses.
   - `[face]` Armed people at {settlement} meet a stranger who crosses no perimeter on the way in.
   - `[face]` Coming into {settlement}, a stranger crosses no perimeter. What keeps the town is people under arms.
3. `[street]` The town is defended by people under arms and enclosed by nothing.
   - `[face]` Defense here rests on armed people and on no built line.
   - `[face]` People under arms are what the town has, and nothing walled stands about the place.
   - `[face]` Here the defense is a body of people under arms. The ground they keep is open on every side.

--- NOTES

**THE CARD, as the licence is read here.** The card's `may claim` line licenses ONE compound standing fact — `beastsRowSituation(family, perimeter, force) === frontier country, force without a perimeter` — whose three components are (i) the country is frontier, (ii) the town keeps an organised force under arms, (iii) there is no perimeter. Naming a component is naming the one licensed fact, not a second fact. The card's `may NOT` line (a count, a cause, a season, a future, a standpoint, a second fact, another civic object of the class `wall`) is what the drops below discharge. Bag FILLED at this block's call sites is `{settlement}`; `band` is RESERVED and `route` is unfilled, so no band word and no route noun appears in any face — no face names a road, a gate, a ditch or a tower, because each would be a second civic object of the `wall` class or a spatial fact the read does not hold.

**THE PROVENANCE MOVE — LICENSED, DELIBERATELY NOT SPENT.** The card carries `source: muster · standing LICENSED — a citation of this holder is licensed where the provenance budget allows`. No face cites it, for three reasons taken together. (a) Part B §24 admits a citation only for one of S3's three reasons; there are no two accounts in disagreement here, the card forbids a count so "a count from an interested party" cannot arise, and the fact is the town's own posture rather than a keeper's contested record. (b) §24's rate: the exemplar registers with raw text cite at zero per 786 sentences, so a citation habit is a refuter's finding. (c) A citation is two licensed claims (§20), so it could not sit on one face without breaking claim-equality with its three siblings, and sitting on all four would make every draw of that variant a cited draw. Recorded as a deliberate non-spend, not an oversight.

**BOUNDED CONDITION, not a refusal — the level-1 grammar spread.** MOVE-GRAMMAR §2.1 asks a pool of k variants for min(k, 8) distinct level-1 grammars and at least two in any pool of two or more. Filtered by this block's fields, only two members are drawable: V1 (PRESENT alone) and V3 (PRESENT → LACK); V2 needs a structural-consequence field, V4 a named-object field, V5 an institution row, V6 an unresolved-state field, V7 event provenance and V8 a `not-held` record field, and this block holds none of them. The absent perimeter is a `none-exists` world fact, so it is V3's LACK and not V8's GAP. Every variant of this pool must carry the LACK, because the no-perimeter half is the pool key's discriminating claim and dropping it would drop a licensed claim. The floor of two IS met: the compressed single-assertion faces (variant 1 face 4, variant 3 face 2) read as one PRESENT assertion of the compound state, and the rest read as PRESENT → LACK. Three distinct grammars are not reachable on this block's licensing, and no face is written into a member the block cannot license.
