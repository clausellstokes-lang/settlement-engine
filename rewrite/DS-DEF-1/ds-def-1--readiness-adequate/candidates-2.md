   - `[face]` `[hall · pair 1 · disagree]` A clerk in the hall says the town is covered out of the common purse and out of nothing else.
   - `[face]` `[guild · pair 1 · disagree]` A guild member says the town is covered out of what the guilds pay in, and that the common purse is only the hall's name for it.

3. `[street]` Everyone here can see the arrangements working, one thing at a time, and takes it that one thing at a time is how trouble comes.
