# CANDIDATES — WRITER 2 (Fable 5.1) · block DS-DEF-1 · pool `readiness STRONG`

*Checkpoint file. Written in passes; if this file is found part-written, the header and roster are settled and the variants continue from the last one present.*

LENS: **THE SOCIAL POSITION** (who pays, who is owed, who is resented; the source's stake shows in
what it calls a fact) and **THE WITHHELD REASON** (a fact set down plainly and the reason not given).
VOICE: the master archiver's hand. A recorded fact stands bare. Every account names its source through
a role. The attribution is symmetric. Plain modern words, no contraction, no archaism. The fact, then
the stop. Clear before crafted: every line below was asked first whether it reads once.

THE POOL'S OWN SUBJECT, as the card puts it: the key fixes ONE number and it is a MEAN. The town is in
good order as a defended place and nothing on the roster explains why; the same page can print an
underfunded upkeep, a short larder or a stretched watch under this header. So no face below explains
the good order by any body, asserts a full purse, a stocked store, a well-paid or well-drilled watch,
or a totality of safety. What the pool has instead is the thing the card names as the dispute: **one
purse, three accounts of it** — the hall holds it, the stalls and the trades fill it, the watch is paid
out of it — and the thing the card names as the flavour: **an arrangement nobody has had to use**.
Every face turns on who pays for that arrangement, who is owed for it, who is resented over it, and
on a reason somebody does not give.

THE SOURCES THAT SPEAK HERE (card §7 with correction (7.0) applied — the city's required rows carry
at metropolis, so these are universal on all 193 towns of the preimage):
- **stranger** (always) · **hall** (`Town hall` / `City hall`) · **court** (the same rows seat
  `hasCourtSystem` at every tier; no required row seats a prison, so the court fines and releases and
  never holds) · ⭐ **watch** (`Town watch` / `Professional city watch` — the ONLY force seated on every
  town of this pool; written by what it DOES, never called professional, full-time, part-time, drilled
  or soldiers, F1-27) · **tavern** · **register** (`Parish churches` + the burial rows; the source is
  *whoever keeps the ground* or *the register*, never the priest and never the sexton) · **market**
  (never *in the square*, which is a town-only placement).
- conditional — **guild**: `Craft guilds (5-15)` is required at TOWN ONLY and conditional (`hasGuild`)
  at city and metropolis, the reverse of the usual shape; every guild face below carries its seat.
- the public and the archiver observing, and the archiver's weighing: offered under NOTES per variant,
  in the face syntax, for the selector to carry until cars 18n and 18i land.
- **the households** speak through the tavern's seat (*at the tavern a householder says*), since the
  closed source vocabulary has no word for them; **the granary's keeper** has no seat word either and
  is offered under NOTES for the chair to rule the tag.

NOT SPEAKERS HERE, and no face below reaches for one: **the muster** (does not exist on any town of
this preimage, card (7c)), **the garrison** and **the gate** (both OPEN at town, so unwritable across the
preimage), **the elders** (below town only), the crown's assessor (never). No `compromised` face is
offered: section (2c) prints no covert symptom pool on this block and the tag is refused outright.

WORDS KEPT OUT of every line, for the card's reasons: wall, works, gate, bar, toll, garrison, soldier,
muster, militia, drill, professional, part-time, cell, gaol, sentence, trial, square, market day,
priest, sexton, mayor, captain, the band word, the tier word, *from the road*, any record cited by
name (the shipped four cite none), any digit, date, season, rate, duration or magnitude, *will* and
*shall*, and the culture furniture floor 3 bars. A durative appears only over the readiness state
itself (*nobody has had to find out*), never over a force or a building.

## VARIANT 2 · `[ledger]` — the fact, bare

## THE PACKET'S OWN CHECK, before it is handed over

- Every account names its source through a role; every bare line is a recorded fact or a bare observation; nothing says *the survey*, *the record*, *this office* or *entered as* on a player face; the notebook alone is exempt and does not use them either.
- No line names a wall, a gate, a garrison, a muster, a militia, a toll, a cell, a square, a priest, a sexton, a named office, the band word or the tier word. The watch is written only by what it does. No record is cited by name. *From the road* does not appear.
- No digit, em dash, exclamation mark, semicolon or contraction. No *will* or *shall*. No count, date, season, rate, duration or magnitude. The only duratives are over the readiness state itself (*nobody has had to find out*, spine 4 and the variant 2 public line), which section (6) marks frozen and the card licenses by name.
- Pairs: four, one per variant, two different sources each, halves one sentence each, all marked joinable; disagree in 1, 2 and 4, reinforce in 3. A weighing is offered under each and none of them settles anything. No source is favoured across the block.
- The attribution's first three words repeat nowhere within a variant, and the frame varies (a clerk in the hall says · at the hall they say · asked at the hall · the register holds · they say at the tavern · by a traveller's account); *the account is that* is not used. *The hall*, *the watch*, *the court* and *the register* are kept as class words until car 18l lands, written so any role of that source could stand in the slot.
- Particulars: one heard (the round before it is seen; doors tried and none opening), one seen (doors locked ahead of the round; stores in the hall's passage). The selector keeps one per rendered page.
- The bill lands only on rows every town holds: the households, a journeyman of a seated guild, the hall's own roof, the stallholders.
- No `compromised` face is offered, per section (2c). No `{settlement}` inside any face; the token appears in spine 1 only.
