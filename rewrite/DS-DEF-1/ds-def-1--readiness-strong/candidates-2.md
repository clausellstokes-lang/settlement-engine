LENS: **THE SOCIAL POSITION** (who pays, who is owed, who is resented; the source's stake shows in
what it calls a fact) and **THE WITHHELD REASON** (a fact set down plainly and the reason not given).
VOICE: the master archiver's hand. A recorded fact stands bare. Every account names its source through
a role. The attribution is symmetric. Plain modern words, no contraction, no archaism. The fact, then
the stop. Clear before crafted: every line below was asked first whether it reads once.

THE POOL'S OWN SUBJECT, as the card puts it: the key fixes ONE number and it is a MEAN. The town is in
good order as a defended place and nothing on the roster explains why; the same page can print an
underfunded upkeep, a short larder or a stretched watch under this header. So no face below explains
the good order by any body, asserts a full purse, a stocked store, a well-paid or well-drilled watch,
or a totality of safety. What the pool has instead is the thing the card names as the dispute: **one
purse, three accounts of it** — the hall holds it, the stalls and the trades fill it, the watch is paid
out of it — and the thing the card names as the flavour: **an arrangement nobody has had to use**.
Every face turns on who pays for that arrangement, who is owed for it, who is resented over it, and
on a reason somebody does not give.
