THE ENTAILMENT LAW, RATIFIED (brief ADDENDUM 13 part A, 2026-09-11; read the addendum whole): A. THE LAW, as ratified (ADDENDUM 10's six items re-cut; the numbering is kept) 1. STANDS. A recorded noun licenses what the word MEANS, never what the thing typically HAS. Age, height, condition, manning, prosperity, a cause, a history, a count, a season, what the thing is usually for elsewhere: NOT entailed; each needs its own read. 2. STANDS, SHARPENED. An attribute that varies across the recorded class is spoken only through a DERIVED FILL computed from the recorded name. The {defmaterial} fill is chartered with the value table of O-1: Palisade → timber; Town walls · City walls and gates · Massive walls and fortifications → stone; Citadel · Gates (if walled) · Palisade or earthworks → NO FILL (a variant naming the slot drops). UNTIL it is minted, no face names a wall's material (writer bar W11); a material SOURCE ("cut from its own woods", "the stone of the country") is NEVER licensed, because the fortification chain feeds every wall row quarried stone (supplyChainData.js:873-884). The two {defwork} residuals ("palisade or earthworks" and "gates (if walled)" reach the page as literals) are a wiring row for CAR 8b-W-2; until then a writer treats {defwork} as an opaque recorded name and writes no sentence whose grammar breaks on either literal. 3. THE WRITER BARS W11–W19 (bind every writer, gate, refuter and judge from DEF-2 on; a breach is a FAIL with the bar named; each bar stands until its wiring row lands and the chair lifts it here) W11 THE MATERIAL BAR: no wall material and no material source in any face until {defmaterial} is minted; a shipped "stone" or "timber" clause is the corpus's known breach and is dropped by the rewrite, never kept as a floor. W12 THE GARRISON BAR: "the garrison" only where a Garrison or Multiple garrisons row resolves on the roster (city tier), never from a Barracks (a housing row) or the watch; where an occupation is on the page the face says WHOSE ("{counterpart}'s garrison", "the occupier's men") or uses "the soldiers"; an occupation never entails a garrison present. W13 THE WATCH BAR: as item 5; the pay-gate read never uses "the watch"; a corpus row that asserts a watch off a key that reads no watch row is a breach the rewrite cures. W14 THE LABEL BAR: an engine label is rendered at its ENGINE meaning as section 3 states it, never at its dictionary sense. The defense desk's own ten: plagued (monsters); settled (the low tier); hasGranary (a tier proxy: every town-plus, no village-and-below, whatever it stores); hasChurch (true on every hamlet from a church in another settlement); hasCourtSystem (every town and city, from a meeting hall; a civil arbitration is recorded, a criminal trial is not); hasPort (never the sea; the sea only off a Shipyard row or maritimeSupported); hasGates (fires on a palisade); the Town watch's gate duty (recorded on gateless towns); scores.magical (reads a world, not a town); the readiness label (moves with tier and country while no works move). The other desks' labels bind their blocks when those desks come up. W15 THE ALIAS BAR: a generic English word is used only where the row it names resolves on this town's record; otherwise the always-safe spelling of section 4: "the works" or "what the town has built" (any wall-class member); "the muster" / "the town's force"; "the stores" / "what the town holds back"; "the way in by water"; "the approach" / "the country outside"; "the land" / "what the town raises"; "a house of the faith" / "the parish"; "those tending the sick"; "the {seat}" filled from its own name or "the governing body" (never "the hall", "the council", "the chamber" baked); "the town's own reckoning" (never "the rolls" or "the clerks" as a citation); "the instrument" / "the document" and {term} (never "the tribute" or "the cap on arms" for a family); "a working plurality" (never "a majority"); "the mill" only where the resolved row is a grain mill. W16 THE GATE'S WORD: "upkeep" and "the keeping" are the always-safe words for the military gate (the engine names it the economic-UPKEEP gate over "garrison wages, wall maintenance", defenseGenerator.js:186); "wages" and "pay" of the muster are the engine's own words and stay licensed under R-viii′, but never a sum, a rate, a share, a season, arrears or desertion, and never WHOSE beyond the class word (X-1: the gate fires on a wage-less village with a part-time militia; that is a wiring finding, section D, not a writer's fault). W17 THE CRISIS BAR (cross-desk, from the stressors and war tables): a siege entails a besieger, never walls, gates, a watch or an army at the walls; an occupation entails a foreign writ and taxed revenue, never a garrison present or the town's own hall; wartime never entails hardship (the record's own profit coin) and never a garrison; a famine never entails a harvest failure or a season; a disease outbreak is "illness" or "sickness", never "the plague" and never "not yet a plague" (the two records contradict); an insurgency is never armed on the generation record. W18 THE STRESS-RECORD BAR: a generation-built stress licenses the type's static definition AND the factions, the safety sentence and the deltas the same token minted; an APPLY_STRESSOR entry licenses the static definition only; a world stressor's stage, age and severity are the ORIGIN's; a birth-time origin variant licenses past tense only; unattributed and palace_coup license nothing beyond the type. W19 THE FAITH BAR: the creed by the deity's NAME with nothing predicated of the deity (the deity doctrine, constitutional); rank, standing, legitimacy and contested each at its own engine sense (L-122, L-123), never one for another; live: false is "no measured standing yet", never "newly founded".
