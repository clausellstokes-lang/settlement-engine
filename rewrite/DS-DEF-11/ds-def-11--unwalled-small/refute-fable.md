### Variant 1 · `[street]`

- **Face 0** — `{settlement} is village size or under, and no wall closes the place.` — **PASS.** Band as a ceiling, LACK flat and second, the place as the landing noun, the pool's one settlement opener. Held verbatim from the lawful draft.

Law: the register card (a sentence states a fact the world holds and lands on the civic thing the fact names); A6 (as read, the face does not state C1 of the settlement, so the faces are not claim-equal); §21.4 read against the DRAFT, whose `the place carries no wall` bound the LACK correctly — the refiner's named target (R-DA-10's rationed rate, three of eight after the change) is a pool-level band the draft did not exceed, so there is no law behind a change that costs the binding.
- **Face 2** — `At {settlement} the size keeps to a village and no higher. The place stands unwalled.` — **FAIL.** `and no higher` restates, inside one sentence, the ceiling that `keeps to a village` already states; the refiner's note 3 says so ("restates the SAME read as its ceiling") and gives length as the motive. Law: the register card ("the WORD may recur; the FACT must not"; a sentence states a fact and stops there); MOVE-GRAMMAR §1.3 (no MEANING move; a second beat that adds no field is the summarising beat); the draft's own §4 bar ("not cured by padding") and §21.4 (no law behind the change). The two-sentence shape and the second sentence are lawful; the finding is the doubled beat in the first.
- **Face 3** — `Village size holds at {settlement} and no wall stands.` — **FAIL.** With `or under` dropped, `Village size holds at {settlement}` reads as the tier VALUE `village`, not the band; on the two tiers of three the key fires on (thorp, hamlet) the head record names a different tier on the same page.
