### Variant 1 · `[ledger]` · claims per face

The joint is a plain `and` asserting no edge between the two halves; the ledger's stance is the pair as the office's books hold it **(E)**. No cause, no count, no time **(F)**.
- **Face 2** (12 words) — *A {defwork} stands at {settlement}*: **(A)**, keeping the shipped line's own verb `stands`, the density floor of variant 1 (Part B §21.4). *the pay of its muster is short*: **(B)** and **(D)**. - **Face 3** (15 words) — *In place at {settlement} is a {defwork}*: **(A)**, the licensed presence phrase `is in place`, fronted so the subject arrives last; the inversion is word order, spendable (MOVE-GRAMMAR §3.4). *the upkeep of its muster is unmet*: **(B)** and **(D)**.
- **Face 4** (17 words) — *A {defwork} is entered standing at {settlement}*: **(A)**, with the ledger's own record formula (`entered`, as the sibling marker lists it for this angle); the passive is agentless and re-declares no office (R-DA-01: the office is declared once, at the front). *its muster's funding is entered short of the roll*: **(B)** and **(D)**. **Dropped from the shipped line, and why** — *stands better than* (an observable no field holds: no read measures the wall's repair); *the watch* and *that should man it* (an institution the card does not read, plus a duty absent from the institution table and a standpoint); the manning form of the shortfall (a count in words on a funding field); *stone* (an observable; the name class holds palisade and earthwork); *stone keeps itself, and wages do not* (two maxims, a cause, and an antithesis whose rejected alternative names no sibling key — R-DA-02, R-DA-12, **(F)**).
